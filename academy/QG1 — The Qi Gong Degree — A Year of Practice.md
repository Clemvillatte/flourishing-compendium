# QG1 — The Qi Gong Degree — A Year of Practice: The practice gallery

Academy-owned source for the Qi Gong degree practice gallery (owner
directive 2026-10-22: the Academy is a distinct project; this gallery is
not part of the Library/wiki). This file is the canonical human-editable
record for the Qi Gong guided-practice sessions shown on the degree page.
It was migrated mechanically from the QG1 wiki page (commit 2b4d68321,
gallery-worker) — every row, guide, duration, and URL carried over
unchanged; durations were then re-verified against the live sources.

Tables follow the CMP1 format: `| Session | Guide | Length |` where the
session title links to the practice itself. The companion parser is
scripts/qg_gallery_data.py; thumbnails live in
academy/thumbnails-manifest-qg.json.

Everything is free. Rotate freely; nothing here depends on anything before it.

### Who is guiding

Every guide in this gallery teaches inside an established lineage, association, or documented teacher training, and the credential is linked where it is stated.

- [Master Gu](https://www.taoistwellness.online/about) — 15th-generation San Feng Pai Tai Chi master in the Wudang lineage tracing to Zhang San Feng, with over 25 years of teaching.
- [George Thompson](https://www.taoistwellness.online/about) — disciple of Master Gu and co-founder of the Wudang Taoist Wellness Academy.
- [Mimi Kuo-Deemer](https://mkdeemer.com/about.html) — qigong, taiji quan and yoga teacher with over twenty years' practice; formal training in the Wah family qigong system under Chen Li Sheng and Wudang taiji under Chiman Cheung.
- [Jeffrey Chand](https://www.qigongforvitality.com/about/) — qigong instructor (Dipl. TCM) and retired registered acupuncturist with 25 years of clinical practice and qigong study in Taiwan.
- [John Munro](https://longwhitecloudqigong.com/about/instructors/) — founder of Long White Cloud Qigong; diplomas in qigong and Traditional Chinese Medicine; former Vice President of the New Zealand Qigong and Traditional Chinese Medicine Association.
- [Christopher Grant and Daniela Hess](https://www.learnqigong.com/about) — co-founders of Great Energy and Learn Qigong; Christopher is a certified teacher of yoga, qigong, breathwork and meditation (RYT500), Daniela a functional wellness consultant (MSEd).
- [Marisa Cranfill](https://www.yoqi.com/marisa-cranfill) — founder of YOQI; formally trained in the Universal Healing Tao school of Master Mantak Chia and in medical qigong, with over 25 years of study in China and Thailand.
- [Faye Yip and the British Health Qigong Association](https://healthqigong.org.uk/ytv/) — the association was founded in 2019 by Master Tary Yip and Master Faye Li Yip to promote the Chinese Health Qigong Association's standardised Health Qigong routines; Faye Yip also founded the Deyin Taijiquan Institute.
- [Lee Holden](https://www.holdenqigong.com/about/) — master qigong instructor, licensed acupuncturist and herbalist.
- [Dr. Paul Lam](https://taichiforhealthinstitute.org/about-dr-paul-lam/) — retired Sydney family physician, tai chi teacher for over forty years, and founder of the Tai Chi for Health Institute; his Tai Chi for Arthritis program is recommended by the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention for fall prevention.

### Notes and honest gaps

- Re-verification 2026-10-22 (Computer Agent, on migration to the Academy):
  every row was checked against the YouTube oembed endpoint. 31 of the 111
  inherited rows point to videos that are no longer available (removed or
  private) and were dropped from this file; the 80 surviving rows were
  confirmed live with matching channel and title. Durations are inherited
  as printed on the source pages when the page was written (the gallery
  worker's verification); they were not re-timed from the recordings.
- Durations are as printed on the source pages at the time of checking; channels occasionally re-cut videos.
- The British Health Qigong Association rows are the standardised Chinese Health Qigong Association forms; some are demonstration videos with music rather than talk-through instruction — they suit people who already know a form, while the mirror versions are follow-along friendly.
- The Five Animal Frolics are thinner in free, well-credentialed video than the Brocades; George Thompson's class covers the set, and the animal shelf is rounded out with single-animal practices.
- Headspace's walking meditation (used in Module 05 cross-training) is deliberately not in this gallery because Headspace is not a qigong lineage; it stays on the CMP1 page where it was verified.
- Lee Holden's channel hosts some Master Gu collaborations; those rows say so.
