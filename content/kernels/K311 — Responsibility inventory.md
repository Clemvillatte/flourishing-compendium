> **Kernel K311** — Responsibility inventory

List three areas of your life where you feel stuck. For each, write: what is within my control here? What action, however small, could I take? The inventory is not about blame. It is about locating the space where agency operates. Research on internal locus of control shows that perceiving agency in difficult situations predicts better outcomes.
