> **Kernel K343** — Needs-before-solutions

When someone is struggling, name the need before proposing a solution. "It sounds like you need to be heard, not fixed." The practice prevents the well-intentioned but unhelpful pattern of offering advice before the person has been understood. Research on emotional validation shows that feeling understood reduces distress more effectively than receiving advice.
