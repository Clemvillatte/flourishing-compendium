> **Kernel K357** — Shared attention

With someone, attend to the same thing: a view, a piece of music, a problem, a child playing. Do not talk about it. Just attend together. The practice builds what developmental psychologists call joint attention, which is the foundation of shared reality. Research on shared positive events (Gable and Reis) shows that the experience of sharing attention to something good amplifies the positive effect.
