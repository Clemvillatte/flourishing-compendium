> **Kernel K117** — Graded exposure

Create a hierarchy of avoided situations from least to most difficult. Enter the easiest one and stay until anxiety drops by at least half. Move up the hierarchy gradually. Each exposure teaches the nervous system that the feared outcome does not occur or is survivable.
