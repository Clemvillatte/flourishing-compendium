> **Kernel K329** — Revision loop

Take something you have made and revise it three times. Each revision asks a different question: Is it clearer? Is it more honest? Is it more alive? The loop trains the capacity for sustained creative work, which research on expertise (Ericsson) shows is built through deliberate practice with feedback, not through inspiration.
