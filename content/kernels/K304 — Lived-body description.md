> **Kernel K304** — Lived-body description

Spend five minutes describing your body as you experience it from the inside, not as it appears from outside. Not "my legs are short" but "I feel tension in my calves, warmth in my chest, the weight of my head." The exercise is the phenomenological reduction: suspending the natural attitude that treats the body as an object and attending to it as lived.
