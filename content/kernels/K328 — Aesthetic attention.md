> **Kernel K328** — Aesthetic attention

Spend ten minutes with a work of art, a piece of music, a natural scene, or a crafted object. Attend not to what it means but to how it appears: the composition, the texture, the rhythm, the quality of attention it asks of you. The practice is Dewey's aesthetic experience: the capacity for consummatory experience is a dimension of flourishing, not a luxury. Research on aesthetic engagement shows associations with well-being through the mechanisms of absorption and meaning.
