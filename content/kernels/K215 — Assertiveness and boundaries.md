> **Kernel K215** — Assertiveness and boundaries

A structured way to state a need and hold a limit without going to war over it. The modern script comes from Sharon and Gordon Bower's book Asserting Yourself, which taught the four-step DESC form. Reach for it when a need has been swallowed a few too many times, or when a limit has been crossed and the choice is between silence and an explosion.

### Protocol

The four steps are the Bowers' DESC script, quoted here from an opened institutional guide that reproduces it:

1. Describe: "Describe the behavior/situation as completely and objectively as possible. Just the facts!"
2. Express: "Express your feelings or thoughts about the behavior/situation. Try phrasing your statements using 'I' and not 'You'." Sentences that begin with "You" put people on the defensive, and defensive people stop listening.
3. Specify: "Specify what behavior/outcome you would prefer to happen."
4. Consequences: "Specify the consequences if this happens. (Both positive and negative)"

The seed line of this page holds the spirit of the practice: a boundary is not a wall, it is the condition under which presence stays possible.

### Evidence

The trial base for assertiveness training as a whole:

> "This meta-analysis synthesised data from 12 randomised controlled trials, with a total of 503 participants, investigating the use of AT for SA in a variety of contexts and settings. The significant overall effect size was medium, g = 0.62, 95% CI [0.32, 0.92], p < .001, with no evidence of publication bias." (Abstract, [Hede, Malouff & Meynadier, 2026, International Journal of Applied Positive Psychology 11, Article 28](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41042-026-00297-7))

What this evidence does not test: the four-step script as a boundary tool in ordinary relationships. The meta-analysis covers training programmes for people with social anxiety, against waitlist and no-treatment controls, and the DESC script itself comes from a self-help book rather than a trial. The comparison in the trials is also not silence versus speech but training versus nothing.
