> **Kernel K309** — Intersubjective presence

In your next conversation, attend not only to the words but to the embodied presence of the other person: their posture, their breathing, the micro-expressions that accompany speech. The exercise trains attention to the intersubjective field, which phenomenology treats as the primary dimension of social life. Research on nonverbal synchrony shows that conversational partners unconsciously coordinate posture, breathing, and gesture, and that this synchrony predicts rapport.
