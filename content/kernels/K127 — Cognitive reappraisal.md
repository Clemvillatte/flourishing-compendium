> **Kernel K127** — Cognitive reappraisal

When a situation triggers distress, generate an alternative interpretation. What else could this mean? What would a wise friend say? What is the larger context? Reappraisal changes the emotional impact by changing the meaning.

## Facets

- **as meaning-making** — Gross, 2015
- **among the best-evidenced strategies**
