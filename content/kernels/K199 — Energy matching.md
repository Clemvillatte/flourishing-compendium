> **Kernel K199** — Energy matching

When dull or lethargic, straighten your posture, take a few sharp breaths, or move. When restless or agitated, lengthen your exhale, ground your feet, or slow your movements. Match the intervention to the state.

### Protocol

The match is a prescription filled in one minute; the review of the calming half is quoted below. Intended focus: Emotion and Body.

1. Read the state before choosing the tool: dull and lethargic is one prescription, restless and agitated is the opposite one, and the wrong direction makes the state worse.
2. For dullness, raise the body's energy: straighten the posture, take a few sharp breaths, move, splash the face with cold water. For agitation, lower it: lengthen the exhale, ground the feet, slow every movement by a third.
3. Re-read the state after two minutes; if it has not moved, the tool or the read was wrong, and the other direction is worth trying. The aim is the match, not the tool itself.

### Evidence

From the systematic review of slow breathing:

> "Slow breathing techniques promote autonomic changes increasing Heart Rate Variability and Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia paralleled by Central Nervous System (CNS) activity modifications." (Abstract, [Zaccaro, Piarulli, Laurino, Garbella, Menicucci, Neri & Gemignani, 2018, Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 12, 353](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30245619/))

What this evidence does not test: the match. The review pooled 15 studies of breathing under ten breaths per minute in healthy subjects and found autonomic and central changes, with reported psychological outputs including increased comfort, relaxation, vigor and alertness, and reduced arousal, anxiety and anger. That is the calming half of the kernel's prescription, and the review says nothing about the activation half, about reading a dull state, or about choosing between tools; the abstract also reports nothing about matching an intervention to a state. What it supports is one side of the practice: the exhale-lengthening, slow-breathing tools carry measurable autonomic effects, which is why they are the prescription for agitation rather than a guess.
