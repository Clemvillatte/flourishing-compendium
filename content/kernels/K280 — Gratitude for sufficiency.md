> **Kernel K280** — Gratitude for sufficiency

Each evening, name one thing you already have that is enough. Not a new thing you are grateful for, but a thing you already possess whose sufficiency you normally overlook. The practice is not the same as gratitude journaling (which has mixed evidence). It targets the specific mechanism of desire: the tendency to take what is sufficient for granted while pursuing what is not.
