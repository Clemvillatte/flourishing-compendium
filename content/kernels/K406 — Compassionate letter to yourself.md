> **Kernel K406** — Compassionate letter to yourself

Write a letter to yourself about a difficulty you are carrying — written from the perspective of someone who understands your circumstances and wants to help: someone with kindness, patience, and courage to spare. Acknowledge what hurts. Include why your response makes sense given what you have lived through. Include, plainly, what would help.

Take days if you need them; the letter improves with a few sittings. Write about the difficulty without dramatising or minimising it.

Then keep the letter and reread it in the moments you would normally turn on yourself. Some people write several — one for the voice that says you are lazy, one for the voice that says you are alone — and keep them where the hardest moments find them.

Adapted from Kolts, *CFT Made Simple* (2016), compassionate letter-writing instructions (after Gilbert, 2009, 2010).
