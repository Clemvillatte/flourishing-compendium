> **Kernel K352** — Moral injury audit

Review a decision or action that violated your moral code. Name what was violated. Acknowledge the impact on yourself and others. The audit is not about guilt. It is about the recognition that moral injury, like physical injury, requires attention to heal. Research on moral injury (Litz et al.) shows that unaddressed moral pain predicts PTSD, depression, and suicide risk.
