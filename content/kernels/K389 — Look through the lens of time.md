> **Kernel K389** — Look through the lens of time

Pick something currently bothering you. Write a one-sentence description of it. Then run it through temporal lenses:

1. Describe the event that upset you.
2. Rate how important the event feels now, on a scale of 0 to 10.
3. Rate how important the event will feel in an hour, a day, a week, a month, a year, five years, and ten years.
4. Re-rate how important the event feels after you completed the exercise.
5. Reflect: write your new view under "How You Think About the Event Now."

The ratings don't tell you the problem isn't real — they tell you its scale.

Adapted from Tompkins, *The Cognitive Behavioral Therapy Workbook*, New Harbinger, 2024, ch. 4, printed pp. 90–91.
