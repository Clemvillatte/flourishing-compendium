> **Kernel K317** — Tragic optimism

In a situation of genuine loss or suffering, hold three questions simultaneously: What can I create from this? What meaning can I find? What is still possible? The practice is Viktor Frankl's tragic optimism: the capacity to find meaning despite suffering, guilt, and death. Research on meaning-making shows that the ability to find meaning in adversity predicts post-traumatic growth, though the effect is not universal and depends on the nature of the adversity.
