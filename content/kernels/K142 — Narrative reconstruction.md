> **Kernel K142** — Narrative reconstruction

Take one sentence of your self-narrative that feels fixed and painful. Rewrite it in the direction of growth, not denial. Not "I am broken" but "I am in the process of healing what was broken." The reconstruction must be honest, not a positive thinking exercise.
