> **Kernel K358** — Stakeholder impact scan

Before an institutional decision, list every group affected: employees, customers, community, environment, future generations. For each, write one sentence about the impact. The scan forces institutional decisions out of the frame of efficiency or profit alone and into the frame of practical wisdom, which considers the good of the whole.
