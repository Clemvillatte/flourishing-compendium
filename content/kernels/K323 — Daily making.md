> **Kernel K323** — Daily making

Make something every day. It does not matter what: a meal, a sentence, a drawing, a garden bed, a conversation, a spreadsheet that is genuinely useful. The criterion is that you brought something into being that did not exist before. Research on creative activity and well-being shows that everyday creative engagement predicts positive affect and flourishing, independently of professional creative achievement.
