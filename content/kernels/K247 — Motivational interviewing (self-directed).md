> **Kernel K247** — Motivational interviewing (self-directed)

When ambivalent about a change, ask yourself: "What are the costs of staying the same? What are the benefits of changing? How ready am I, on a scale of 1 to 10? What would move me one point higher?" The questions explore ambivalence rather than forcing resolution.
