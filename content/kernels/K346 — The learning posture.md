> **Kernel K346** — The learning posture

In a cultural context not your own, ask three questions before making any statement: What is the history here? Who has been excluded? What would I need to learn before I could understand? The posture is the operationalisation of cultural humility. Research on intergroup contact (Allport) shows that contact under conditions of equal status, common goals, and cooperation reduces prejudice.
