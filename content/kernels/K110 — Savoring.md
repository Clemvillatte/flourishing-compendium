> **Kernel K110** — Savoring

When a pleasant moment occurs, deliberately extend it. Notice the sensory details. Express it silently or aloud. Resist the urge to move on quickly. Savoring builds the capacity to receive good experience, which many people trained in vigilance find difficult.
