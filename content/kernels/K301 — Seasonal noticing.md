> **Kernel K301** — Seasonal noticing

At the change of each season, spend an hour outdoors noticing what has changed: light, temperature, growth, decay, animal behaviour. The practice trains attention to natural cycles, which counters the timelessness of digital life. Research on nature connectedness shows that it predicts well-being independently of time spent in nature.
