> **Kernel K272** — Catastrophe testing

When a feared outcome looms, ask the Stoic questions: Is this really terrible? Has anyone endured worse? Will this matter in a year? In ten years? The exercise restores proportion. Research on decatastrophising shows that it reduces anxiety by testing the inflated probability and severity of feared outcomes.
