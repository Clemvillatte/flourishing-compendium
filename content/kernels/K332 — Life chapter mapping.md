> **Kernel K332** — Life chapter mapping

Divide your life into chapters: childhood, adolescence, early adulthood, and so on. For each chapter, write one sentence about the turning point and one about what it taught. The map builds narrative coherence, which research (McAdams) shows predicts well-being and generativity. The exercise is not therapy. It is the structured attention to the shape of one's life.
