> **Kernel K403** — Core and satellite values

Take the values you have already sorted by importance. Put the three most important in a pile at the center; scatter the remaining seven around them, roughly the way electrons circle a nucleus. Read each of the three core cards a few times.

Now step back three feet from the arrangement and look at it. Say what you see: these values matter to me; they are part of who I am; they do not exhaust who I am. Step back another three feet and look again.

The step backward is the exercise. A value laid out on the floor can be seen whole — something you carry, rather than something you must obey. Notice whether any of them has quietly become a rule that leaves no room for context.

Adapted (simplified) from Blonna, *Maximize Your Coaching Effectiveness with ACT* (2015), pp. 72–74, where the ranking is done over ten life-domain envelopes or slots before the values within them are ranked; this version assumes a values list you already have.
