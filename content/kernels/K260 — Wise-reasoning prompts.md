> **Kernel K260** — Wise-reasoning prompts

For a situation that is troubling you, work through these questions: What are the ways this situation could unfold? What are the different perspectives on it? How might my view be biased by my interests? What would a wise friend advise? These prompts operationalise the components of wise reasoning identified in Grossmann's research program: dialectical thinking, intellectual humility, perspective-taking, and recognition of uncertainty.
