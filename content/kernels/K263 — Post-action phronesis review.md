> **Kernel K263** — Post-action phronesis review

After a significant action, review: What did I intend? What actually happened? Where did the gap between intention and outcome come from? What would I do differently? The review is not self-criticism. It is the feedback loop through which practical wisdom develops. Without it, experience becomes repetition rather than learning.
