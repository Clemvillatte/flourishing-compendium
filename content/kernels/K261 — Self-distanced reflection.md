> **Kernel K261** — Self-distanced reflection

Write about a difficult situation using third-person language: "David decided to..." rather than "I decided to...". The linguistic shift creates psychological distance, which reduces defensive reasoning and improves the quality of reflection. Research on self-distancing shows it reduces rumination intensity, lowers emotional reactivity, and improves wise reasoning. The effect is not about detachment but about creating enough space to think clearly.
