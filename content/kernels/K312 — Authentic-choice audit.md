> **Kernel K312** — Authentic-choice audit

For a significant decision, ask: Am I choosing this because I want it, or because I fear the alternative? Am I choosing it for myself or for someone else's image of me? The audit tests whether the choice is autonomous or driven by introjected pressure. Research on self-concordance (Sheldon and Elliot) shows that goals pursued for autonomous reasons predict both attainment and well-being.
