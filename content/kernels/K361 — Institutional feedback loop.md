> **Kernel K361** — Institutional feedback loop

In an institution you are part of, create a channel for honest feedback about decisions. The channel does not need to be formal. It needs to be safe enough that people will tell you what you do not want to hear. Research on organisational learning (Argyris) shows that institutions fail not from lack of information but from the suppression of information that challenges existing assumptions.
