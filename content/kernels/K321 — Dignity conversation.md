> **Kernel K321** — Dignity conversation

Write a brief account of what has mattered most in your life, what you have learned, and what you would want someone to know. Then share it with someone you trust. This is distinct from the Volume I legacy letter in that it is spoken, not written for the dead. The exercise draws on dignity therapy research (Chochinov), which shows that structured life review reduces suffering and improves quality of life. The mechanism is the experience of being witnessed in one's full humanity, not the production of a document.
