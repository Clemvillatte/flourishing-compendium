> **Kernel K307** — Body-schema noticing

Throughout the day, notice your body schema: the implicit sense of where your body ends and the world begins. Notice how it shifts in different contexts: larger when you are confident, smaller when you are anxious, blurred when you are absorbed. The body schema is not the body as seen but the body as lived, the pre-reflective sense that structures action.
