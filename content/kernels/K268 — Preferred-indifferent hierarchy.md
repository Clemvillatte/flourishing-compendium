> **Kernel K268** — Preferred-indifferent hierarchy

List the things you are pursuing: wealth, reputation, health, comfort, achievement. Rank them. Then ask: which of these have become false goods, treated as if they were necessary for flourishing when they are merely preferred? The Stoic discipline of desire is not indifference to external goods but the refusal to treat them as conditions of worth. Research on intrinsic versus extrinsic goal pursuit shows that materialistic aspirations predict lower well-being.
