> **Kernel K405** — The matrix sort

Draw a large plus sign on a sheet of paper. The horizontal line divides what you do in the world of the five senses (above the line) from what happens inside you — thoughts, feelings, memories (below the line). The vertical line divides moves away from discomfort (left) from moves toward what matters to you (right).

Take today and sort it. Bottom right: the name of someone or something important to you. Bottom left: one uncomfortable thought or feeling that shows up and stands in the way of acting for that person or thing. Upper left: one thing you can be seen doing when that discomfort is running the show. Upper right: one thing you can be seen doing that moves you toward what matters.

That is the whole practice, and it is repeatable daily. The sorting itself is the training: every action gets half-classified as you take it — toward or away, and done in contact with the world or in the grip of inner weather. Over time, notice which people, places, and sensations reliably hook you into the away side. Then choose one workable toward move and check what happens.

Adapted from Polk, Schoendorff, Webster & Olaz, *The Essential Guide to the ACT Matrix* (2016), chs. 1 and 3.
