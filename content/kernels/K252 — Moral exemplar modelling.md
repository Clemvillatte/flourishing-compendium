> **Kernel K252** — Moral exemplar modelling

Identify two or three people, living or dead, real or fictional, whose character you admire. Write down what specifically you admire. When facing a difficult decision, ask: what would this person do here? The exercise is not imitation but orientation. Social learning research confirms that exposure to models of behaviour shapes what people attempt and what they notice as possible.
