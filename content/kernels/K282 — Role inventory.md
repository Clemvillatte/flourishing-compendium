> **Kernel K282** — Role inventory

List every role you occupy: parent, partner, professional, friend, citizen, mentor, student, neighbour. For each, write one sentence about what this role requires of you. The inventory makes visible the web of obligations that structure your life. Research on role identity shows that clarity about one's roles predicts purpose and well-being.
