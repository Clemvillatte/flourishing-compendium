> **Kernel K314** — Anxiety-as-signal

When anxiety arises, before trying to reduce it, ask: what is this anxiety about? Is it pointing to something that needs attention? The existentialist move is not to pathologise anxiety but to read it as information about what matters. Research on anxiety sensitivity shows that interpreting anxiety as dangerous amplifies it, while interpreting it as a normal signal reduces its impact.
