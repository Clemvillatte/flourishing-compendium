> **Kernel K289** — Amends and restitution

When you have caused harm, however small, make amends. Name what you did. Acknowledge the impact. Offer repair. The practice is not about guilt. It is about restoring the relationship and the balance that your action disrupted. Research on apology and forgiveness shows that sincere apologies predict relationship repair and reduce resentment.
