> **Kernel K310** — Bad-faith detection

When you are about to say "I have no choice" or "I had to," pause. Ask: what am I avoiding by framing this as necessity? The exercise is Sartre's diagnosis of bad faith: the attempt to escape freedom by treating one's choices as obligations. The point is not that constraints are unreal but that within them, a choice remains.
