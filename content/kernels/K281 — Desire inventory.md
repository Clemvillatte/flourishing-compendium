> **Kernel K281** — Desire inventory

List your current desires. For each, ask: Is this desire aligned with my values? Does pursuing it serve my flourishing? What would happen if I let it go? The inventory is the Stoic discipline of desire made concrete. Research on intrinsic versus extrinsic goals shows that the content of what you pursue predicts well-being more than the attainment.
