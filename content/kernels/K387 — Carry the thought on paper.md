> **Kernel K387** — Carry the thought on paper

Write a negative recurring thought on a piece of paper ("I'm going to fail miserably"). Fold it up and put it in your pocket. Carry it around for the rest of the day.

You can struggle against it — try to convince yourself it's not true, try to forget it's there, kick yourself for thinking it. None of that makes the thought go away; it just adds the struggle on top. Or you can say: "OK, I have this thought. No big deal. Let me get on with my day."

Adapted from Tolin, *Doing CBT* (2nd ed.), Guilford, 2024, ch. 15, printed pp. 401–402 (exercise at p. 401).
