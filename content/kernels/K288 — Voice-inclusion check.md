> **Kernel K288** — Voice-inclusion check

Before finalising a decision that affects a group, ask: have all affected parties had a chance to speak? If not, create the opportunity. The check operationalises the relational principle that those affected by a decision should have a voice in it. Research on participatory decision-making shows that inclusion improves both the quality of decisions and the acceptance of outcomes.
