> **Kernel K399** — The kindness pool (unblock the spring)

Picture kindness as a clear pool fed by a small spring. You can ration the pool — give each person you meet a thimbleful as they pass — and watch it shrink. Or you can unblock the spring that feeds it, so the pool is continuously replenished. The practice (Befriending meditation, K396) is the unblocking. When you notice yourself rationing kindness — holding back warmth because you are tired or drained — ask: is the pool actually low, or is the spring blocked?

Adapted from Williams and Penman, *Mindfulness*, 2011, ch. 11, printed p. 195.
