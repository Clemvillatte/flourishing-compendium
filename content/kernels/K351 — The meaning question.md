> **Kernel K351** — The meaning question

Keep the question "What is this life for?" alive as an ongoing inquiry. Do not settle it with a fixed answer. The question, held honestly, is more generative than most answers. Research on meaning in life (Steger) shows that the presence of meaning, not the search for it, predicts well-being, but the search is not harmful when it is honest and does not become rumination.
