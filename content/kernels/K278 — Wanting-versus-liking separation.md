> **Kernel K278** — Wanting-versus-liking separation

When you want something, ask: will I actually enjoy this, or do I just want it? The distinction between wanting (dopaminergic motivation) and liking (hedonic pleasure) is one of the most robust findings in affective neuroscience. Many things we want intensely give little pleasure once obtained. The question interrupts the automatic link between desire and pursuit.
