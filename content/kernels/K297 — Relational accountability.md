> **Kernel K297** — Relational accountability

Choose one commitment you have made to someone. Track whether you followed through. If you did not, name what happened and repair. The practice operationalises the relational principle that accountability is not a burden but the condition of trust. Research on trust repair shows that acknowledgement of failure, followed by changed behaviour, is more effective than explanation or excuse.
