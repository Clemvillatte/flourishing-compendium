> **Kernel K266** — Impression-judgement-action log

Keep a three-column log for one week. Column 1: the impression (what appeared to happen). Column 2: the judgement (the interpretation you gave it). Column 3: the action (what you did). The log makes visible the gap between event, interpretation, and response that Stoic practice trains you to notice. Research on cognitive mediation shows that it is the interpretation, not the event, that drives the emotional response.
