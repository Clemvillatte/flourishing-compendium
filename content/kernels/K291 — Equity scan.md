> **Kernel K291** — Equity scan

In a decision you are making (hiring, resource allocation, task assignment), scan for bias: Am I favouring people like me? Am I overlooking someone whose contribution is less visible? The scan is not a guilt exercise. It is a practical check against the well-documented tendency to favour the familiar. Research on implicit bias shows that awareness alone does not eliminate bias, but structured attention to decision criteria does reduce it.
