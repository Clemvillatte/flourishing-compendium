> **Kernel K271** — The "is this up to me?" check

Throughout the day, when a concern arises, ask: is this up to me? If yes, act. If no, release. The check is portable, invisible, and immediate. It trains the habit of distinguishing between what is within your control and what is not, which is the foundation of Stoic practice and a reliable predictor of well-being in locus of control research.
