> **Kernel K355** — Synchrony practice

In your next conversation, attend to the bodily dimension of interaction: posture, breathing, gesture, the rhythm of turn-taking. Do not try to manipulate it. Notice it. The practice trains attention to the pre-reflective dimension of social life that phenomenology identifies as the foundation of intersubjectivity. Research on nonverbal synchrony (Ramseyer and Tschacher) shows that conversational partners unconsciously coordinate posture, breathing, and gesture, and that this synchrony predicts rapport.
