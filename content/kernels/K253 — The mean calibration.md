> **Kernel K253** — The mean calibration

For a virtue you are working on, track the two extremes: excess and deficiency. If you are working on assertiveness, the deficiency is passivity; the excess is aggression. After each situation, rate where you landed on a scale of 1 (deficient) to 10 (excessive). The target is not the mathematical midpoint but the mean relative to you: what this situation, with this person, at this time, calls for. Calibrate over weeks, not days.
