> **Kernel K265** — The advisor consultation

Before a significant decision, consult at least two people whose judgement you respect. Ask not for their opinion of what to do but for what they would consider if they were in your position. The question elicits their reasoning, not their verdict. Research on collective intelligence shows that groups that consider diverse perspectives outperform individuals on complex judgement tasks.
