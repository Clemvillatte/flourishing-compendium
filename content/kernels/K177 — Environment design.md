> **Kernel K177** — Environment design

Design your physical and digital environment to make desired behaviors easy and undesired behaviors hard. Put the phone in another room. Leave the guitar out. Lay out the running shoes. The strongest self-control strategy is choosing environments, not resisting temptations.
