> **Kernel K306** — Affordance walk

Walk through an environment and notice what each object, surface, and space invites you to do: a chair invites sitting, a path invites walking, a low wall invites leaning. The exercise trains attention to the relational structure of perception: we perceive the world in terms of what it offers for action. This is the core insight of Gibson's ecological psychology and Merleau-Ponty's phenomenology.
