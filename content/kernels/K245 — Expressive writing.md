> **Kernel K245** — Expressive writing

Write continuously for 15 to 20 minutes about your deepest thoughts and feelings regarding an emotional issue. Do not edit. The practice has evidence for reducing stress and improving health outcomes.

### Protocol

1. Find a place where you will not be disturbed for a quarter of an hour. Writing by hand or keyboard, whichever; nobody else will read it.
2. Write about something that is weighing on you, ideally something you have rarely talked about. Explore your deepest thoughts and feelings about it, and connect it to other parts of your life if that happens naturally.
3. Write continuously for 15 to 20 minutes. Ignore spelling, grammar, and structure. If you run dry, repeat yourself until something new comes.
4. Repeat on three or four consecutive days, on the same topic or a different one. If a topic starts feeling overwhelming rather than helpful, stop and switch; the exercise should sting a little, not wound.

### Evidence

A meta-analysis of 146 randomised studies of experimental disclosure found that the practice is effective, with a positive and significant average effect size of r = 0.075. The analysis also identified a number of moderators, meaning the practice helps more in some conditions and populations than in others. Treat it as a modest emotional tool rather than a medical treatment.

### A guided version

The method, explained and demonstrated by its developer, is [The Expressive Writing Method, by James Pennebaker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XsHIV9PxAV4).
