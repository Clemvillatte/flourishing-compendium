> **Kernel K245** — Expressive writing

Write continuously for 15 to 20 minutes about your deepest thoughts and feelings regarding an emotional issue. Do not edit. The practice has evidence for reducing stress and improving health outcomes.

## Facets

- **research** — Pennebaker and Beall, 1986
- **and reflection**
- **and structured self-reflection**
