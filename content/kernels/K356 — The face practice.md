> **Kernel K356** — The face practice

In your next conversation, attend to the face of the other person. Not to read their expression for information, but to receive it as the primary presentation of who they are. The practice is Levinas's: the face of the other is not an object among objects but the original encounter that places a demand on me. The exercise trains the capacity to see the other as a person, not as a role or a function.
