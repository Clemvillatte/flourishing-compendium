> **Kernel K273** — The view from above

Imagine yourself from a great height: your city, then your country, then the planet. See your concerns from that distance. The exercise is a Stoic contemplative practice that research on self-distancing shows reduces the intensity of negative emotions and broadens perspective. The point is not that your concerns do not matter but that they are smaller than they feel.
