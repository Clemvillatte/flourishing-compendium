> **Kernel K322** — Future-self dialogue

Write a letter from your future self, ten years from now, to your current self. What would that person tell you about the decision you are facing? What would they say you are worrying about unnecessarily? What would they say you are missing? The exercise is distinct from the Volume I deathbed test in that it uses dialogue rather than a single question. Research on future self-continuity (Hershfield) shows that people who feel connected to their future self make better long-term decisions. The mechanism is the expansion of the temporal self beyond the present moment.
