> **Kernel K318** — Absurd-revolt practice

When you encounter a situation that is genuinely absurd or unjust, and you cannot change it, choose your response to it. The practice is Camus's: the absurd is not resolved but met with revolt, which is the refusal to be defeated by meaninglessness. The exercise is not about finding silver linings. It is about choosing how to stand in relation to what cannot be fixed.
