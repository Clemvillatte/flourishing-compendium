> **Kernel K259** — Particulars before rules

When facing a decision, resist the urge to jump to the rule first. Spend two minutes describing the particular situation: who is involved, what is at stake, what the context is. Only then ask which principle applies. The Aristotelian point is that practical wisdom reads the particular before applying the universal. A rule applied without attention to the particular is not virtue but mechanism.
