> **Kernel K327** — Creative risk-taking

Once a week, take a creative risk: share work that is unfinished, try a form you have not mastered, voice an idea that might be wrong. The risk trains the tolerance for uncertainty that creative work requires. Research on creative self-efficacy shows that confidence in one's creative capacity predicts creative output, and that confidence is built through repeated exposure to the vulnerability of sharing.
