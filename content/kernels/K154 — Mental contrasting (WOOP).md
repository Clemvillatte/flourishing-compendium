> **Kernel K154** — Mental contrasting (WOOP)

Identify a wish (what you want), the best outcome (how it would feel), the main obstacle (what stands in the way), and a plan (what you will do when the obstacle appears). Mental contrasting links aspiration to the reality of resistance.
