> **Kernel K100** — Breath anchoring

Sit upright, eyes half-open or closed. Rest attention on the physical sensation of breathing at the nostrils or belly. When attention wanders, note where it went ("thinking," "planning"), and gently return to the breath. The return is the practice, not a failure. Start with 10 minutes.
