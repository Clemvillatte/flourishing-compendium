> **Kernel K275** — Morning premeditation

In the morning, spend two minutes anticipating what the day might bring: difficult people, setbacks, temptations, delays. Rehearse your response to each. The Stoic practice of premeditatio malorum is not pessimism. It is preparation. Research on mental simulation and implementation intentions shows that pre-rehearsing responses to obstacles increases the likelihood of acting on intentions.
