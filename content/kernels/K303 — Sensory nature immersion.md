> **Kernel K303** — Sensory nature immersion

Spend 20 to 30 minutes in a natural setting, attending to sensory experience: the feel of air, the sound of water, the texture of bark, the smell of soil. Research on attention restoration theory shows that natural settings restore directed attention and reduce rumination. The mechanism is soft fascination: the environment engages attention without demanding it.
