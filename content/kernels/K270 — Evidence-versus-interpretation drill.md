> **Kernel K270** — Evidence-versus-interpretation drill

Take a thought that is causing distress. Write down the evidence for it. Then write down the evidence against it. Then write an alternative interpretation. The drill is the core of the CBT thought record, which has its historical roots in Stoic practice. The evidence base is robust: cognitive restructuring shows moderate effect sizes for depression and anxiety across hundreds of trials.
