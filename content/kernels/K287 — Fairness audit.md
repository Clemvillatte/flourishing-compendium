> **Kernel K287** — Fairness audit

For a decision that affects others (a workplace allocation, a family rule, a community policy), ask: Is the process fair? Does everyone affected have a voice? Are the criteria transparent? Research on [[Procedural justice|procedural justice]] (Tyler) shows that people accept outcomes they disagree with if they perceive the process as fair. The audit is not about the outcome but about the process.
