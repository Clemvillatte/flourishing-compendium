> **Kernel K347** — Story-before-statistic

When encountering a group different from your own, seek stories before statistics. Statistics describe; stories reveal. The practice counters the tendency to reduce cultural difference to data points. Research on narrative empathy (Mar and Oatley) shows that stories build social cognition in ways that data do not.
