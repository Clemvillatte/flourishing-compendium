> **Kernel K293** — Elder and mentor cycles

Identify one person older or more experienced than you in a domain that matters. Ask them for regular conversation. Identify one person younger or less experienced. Offer the same. The cycle is the Confucian and Indigenous pattern of knowledge transmission: receiving from elders, giving to the next generation. Research on mentoring shows benefits for both mentor and mentee.
