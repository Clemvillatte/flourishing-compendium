> **Kernel K276** — Preference fasting

For one week, abstain from one preferred indifferent: coffee, social media, a comfort food, a streaming service. The fast is not about the substance. It is about testing whether the preference has become a need. Research on desire and craving shows that wants, when examined, often lose their urgency. The fast creates the conditions for noticing.
