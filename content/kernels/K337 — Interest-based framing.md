> **Kernel K337** — Interest-based framing

In a conflict, separate positions from interests. The position is what someone says they want. The interest is what they actually need. Ask: what is the concern behind the demand? The reframe moves the conversation from competing claims to underlying needs. Research on integrative negotiation (Fisher and Ury) shows that interest-based framing produces more durable and satisfying agreements than positional bargaining.
