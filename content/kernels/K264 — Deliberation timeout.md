> **Kernel K264** — Deliberation timeout

For decisions above a defined threshold (career change, relationship commitment, major purchase), impose a mandatory waiting period of 24 to 72 hours. During the wait, do not decide. Use the time to gather information, consult, and let emotions settle. The timeout protects against the pull of immediate affect, which research on decision-making shows can override long-term interests.
