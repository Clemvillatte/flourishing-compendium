> **Kernel K302** — Ecological reciprocity

Each month, do one act of ecological reciprocity: plant something native, reduce a form of consumption, support a conservation effort, compost. The act is not about environmentalism as an abstract cause. It is about the relational principle that you receive from the world and owe something back.
