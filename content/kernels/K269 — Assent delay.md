> **Kernel K269** — Assent delay

When a strong impression hits (an insult, a provocation, a piece of news), pause before giving it assent. Count three breaths. In that gap, ask: is this impression accurate? Is the interpretation I am about to give it the only one possible? The delay is not suppression. It is the Stoic practice of withholding automatic agreement long enough for reason to engage. Research on the gap between stimulus and response shows that even brief delays reduce impulsive behaviour.
