> **Kernel K360** — Professional phronesis

For a recurring professional decision, identify the rule or protocol that governs it. Then ask: when would following the rule produce a worse outcome than using judgement? The exercise trains the capacity for professional discretion, which is the practical application of phronesis in institutional life. Research on professional expertise (Dreyfus and Dreyfus) shows that expert practitioners move beyond rule-following to contextual judgement.
