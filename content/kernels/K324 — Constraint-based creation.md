> **Kernel K324** — Constraint-based creation

Choose a creative task and impose a constraint: write in 100 words, draw in one colour, cook with three ingredients, solve a problem in one hour. The constraint is not a limitation. It is a generative structure. Research on creativity shows that constraints can enhance creative output by forcing novel connections and reducing the paralysis of infinite possibility.
