> **Kernel K342** — The caring response

When someone comes to you with a difficulty, resist the urge to fix, advise, or minimise. Instead, ask: what would help most right now? If they do not know, offer presence. The response trains the disposition that care ethics identifies as the core of moral life: receptive attention to the reality of the other.
