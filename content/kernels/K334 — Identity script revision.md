> **Kernel K334** — Identity script revision

Identify one sentence of your self-narrative that feels fixed and limiting. Rewrite it in the direction of growth, without denial. The revision must be honest: not "I am confident" but "I am learning to speak when I would rather stay silent." Research on narrative reconstruction in therapy shows that changing the story changes the experience.
