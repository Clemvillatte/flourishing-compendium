> **Kernel K334** — Identity script revision

Identify one sentence of your self-narrative that feels fixed and limiting. Rewrite it in the direction of growth, without denial. The revision must be honest: not "I am confident" but "I am learning to speak when I would rather stay silent." The study below is the closest the research comes to that wager: in therapy, changes in the story's themes of agency preceded improvements in mental health.

### Protocol

The revision has rules; the study behind them is quoted below. Intended focus: Self and Narrative.

1. Write down one sentence of your self-narrative that feels fixed and limiting. Say it in your own words, the way it actually runs in your head.
2. Rewrite it once, in the direction of growth and without denial: not "I am confident" but "I am learning to speak when I would rather stay silent." The revision must be checkable against what you actually did this week.
3. Test it in small actions, and let the actions edit the sentence again. A revision that cannot survive contact with Tuesday was not honest; make it smaller and truer, not bigger.

### Evidence

From the longitudinal study of stories and mental health:

> "lagged growth curve models revealed that changes in the theme of agency occurred prior to the associated improvements in mental health." (Abstract, [Adler, 2012, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 102(2), 367-389](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21910554/))

What this evidence does not test: the rewriting exercise. Forty-seven adults wrote personal narratives before psychotherapy and after every session, and the narratives' themes of agency and coherence were coded against mental health; agency rose and its rises preceded improvements in mental health, a pattern the study reports held across demographics, traits and diagnoses. The abstract reports no test of rewriting a single limiting sentence on one's own, and the direction of causation in life outside the study is not settled by it. What it supports is the kernel's core wager: the story is not a report on the self, it is part of the machinery, and edits can move it.
