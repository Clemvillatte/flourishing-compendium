> **Kernel K341** — The apology structure

When apologising, use four components: name what you did, acknowledge the impact, express regret without excuse, and state what you will do differently. The structure prevents the common drift into justification. Research on apology (Lazare) shows that apologies that include all four components are more likely to be accepted and to repair trust.
