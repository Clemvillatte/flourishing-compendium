> **Kernel K285** — Duty-to-person mapping

List your duties. For each duty, name the person or community to whom it is owed. The mapping prevents the abstraction that lets duties slip. "I should be a better parent" becomes "I should be more patient with my daughter when she asks the same question three times." Research on implementation intentions shows that specific, contextualised plans are more likely to be acted on than abstract intentions.
