> **Kernel K285** — Duty-to-person mapping

List your duties. For each duty, name the person or community to whom it is owed. The mapping prevents the abstraction that lets duties slip. "I should be a better parent" becomes "I should be more patient with my daughter when she asks the same question three times." The ground is the if-then plan: "an implementation intention that spells out the when, where, and how of goal striving in advance ('If situation Y is encountered, then I will initiate goal-directed behavior X!')" ([Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006](https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/2e749bfb-8533-437c-8203-7e788c910c5f)), the duty made concrete by naming its recipient and its cue. Kant's imperative gives the other side of the practice, why the person matters: "So act as to treat humanity, whether in thine own person or in that of any other, in every case as an end withal, never as means only" ([Kant, Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals, Abbott trans.](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5682/5682-h/5682-h.htm)). A duty with a face is a duty that treats the person as the end.

### Protocol

1. Generate the duty list, each duty written as you would normally write it, vague, abstract, slippable. Do not polish; the point is to see the abstraction you currently carry.
2. Select the recipient for each duty, the specific person or community to whom it is owed, and rewrite the duty with the recipient in the sentence and the situation in the cue: not "be more patient" but "when she asks the same question the third time, then I will lower my voice and answer again."
3. Retain the mapped version as the working memory of the duty, and read the list back after a week of living: which mappings held, which slipped, which recipient was quietly treated as means only.
4. Rework the slips, not the whole map. The practice is complete when every duty on the list has a recipient and a cue, and the abstraction that let them slip is gone.

### Evidence

The mechanism is measured: across "94 independent tests... implementation intentions had a positive effect of medium-to-large magnitude (d= .65) on goal attainment" (Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006, abstract verbatim, [KOPS Konstanz record](https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/2e749bfb-8533-437c-8203-7e788c910c5f)), and the review reports that "implementation intention formation both enhanced the accessibility of specified opportunities and automated respective goal-directed responses", the cue-spotting and the automatic follow-through the mapping depends on. The duty side is Kant's: the person to whom the duty is owed is the end, never means only, quoted above from the Abbott translation. Scope limits: the d = .65 is goal attainment in general, not duties specifically, and the meta-analysis predates the replication-crisis reforms of the 2010s; the mapping is an application of the mechanism to a domain the studies did not single out. This page follows K282 Role inventory (the list of roles) and K284 the what-does-this-role-require practice (the entry question); the mapping is where their outputs become plans.
