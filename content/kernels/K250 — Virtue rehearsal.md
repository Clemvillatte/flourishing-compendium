> **Kernel K250** — Virtue rehearsal

Choose one virtue you want to strengthen this month (courage, honesty, generosity, patience). Each day, find one small situation that calls for it and act. The situation does not need to be dramatic. If the virtue is courage, speak one true thing you would normally swallow. If it is generosity, give something you would normally keep. Record the act in one sentence. Over thirty days, the disposition begins to settle.
