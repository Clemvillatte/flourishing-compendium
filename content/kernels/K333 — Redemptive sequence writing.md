> **Kernel K333** — Redemptive sequence writing

Take one difficult period and write it as a story with three parts: the suffering, the turning point, and what grew from it. The exercise does not deny the pain. It locates it within a narrative arc that makes it intelligible. Research on redemptive sequences (McAdams) shows that the capacity to construct such narratives predicts well-being, though the effect depends on the story being honest, not formulaic.
