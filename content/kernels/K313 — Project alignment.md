> **Kernel K313** — Project alignment

List the projects that currently structure your life (career, relationship, creative work, community involvement). For each, ask: does this project express who I want to be? The question is Heidegger's: our projects are not things we do but the way we are. Research on eudaimonic well-being (Ryff) identifies purposeful engagement as a core dimension of flourishing.
