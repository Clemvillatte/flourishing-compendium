> **Kernel K331** — Play practice

Once a week, do something with no purpose beyond the doing: a game, a doodle, an improvisation, a walk with no destination. Play is not the absence of seriousness. It is a different mode of engagement, one that research shows predicts creativity, stress recovery, and well-being. The mechanism is the suspension of goal-directed striving, which allows new possibilities to emerge.

### Protocol

The appointment is with purposelessness; the trial of stimulating playfulness is quoted below. Intended focus: Emotion and Motivation.

1. Once a week, do something with no purpose beyond the doing: a game, a doodle, an improvisation, a walk with no destination. The rule is the suspension of the outcome, so nothing that will be evaluated, scored, or shown counts.
2. When the striving reflex arrives mid-play, and it will, notice it and return to the doing; the noticing is part of the practice, not a failure of it.
3. End without an inventory of what the play produced; the practice's yield is the mode of engagement itself, and a one-line note on what it felt like to be in that mode is all the record it needs.

### Evidence

From the randomized trial of a playfulness intervention:

> "All interventions increased expressions in all facets of playfulness, had short-term effects on well-being, and ameliorated depression." (Abstract, [Proyer, Gander, Brauer & Chick, 2021, Applied Psychology: Health and Well-Being 13(1), 129-151](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32844597/))

What this evidence does not test: the weekly appointment. Five hundred thirty-three participants were randomized to one of three one-week online conditions, doing three playful things, using playfulness in a new way, or counting playfulness, against a placebo; all three raised playfulness, with short-term well-being effects and reduced depression at follow-ups out to twelve weeks. The interventions were exercises in noticing and deploying playfulness, not a standing weekly practice of purposeless activity, and the abstract reports nothing about creativity or stress recovery, the outcomes the kernel also gestures at. What it supports is the kernel's core wager: playfulness is not a fixed allotment, and deliberately doing playful things moves it.
