> **Kernel K397** — Approach, don't avoid (the mouse in the maze)

When you notice yourself bracing against a task, a conversation, or a feeling, ask which direction you are facing. Are you moving *toward* something (helping the mouse find the cheese) or *away* from something (fleeing the owl)? In a real experiment, students who solved a maze by avoiding an owl did 50% worse on a later creativity test than students who solved the same maze by helping a mouse find cheese. The same act, different spirit, different aftermath: avoidance closes the mind; approach opens it.

Adapted from Williams and Penman, *Mindfulness*, 2011, ch. 7, printed pp. 110–111 (citing Friedman & Förster, 2001).
