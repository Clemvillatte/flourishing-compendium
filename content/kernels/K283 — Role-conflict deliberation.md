> **Kernel K283** — Role-conflict deliberation

When two roles conflict (work and family, friend and judge, self and community), write down what each role demands. Then ask: what would a person of practical wisdom do here? The exercise is not about finding a compromise. It is about finding the action that best honours both roles without betraying either. Research on role conflict shows that chronic, unresolved role conflict predicts burnout.
