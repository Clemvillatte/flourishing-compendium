> **Kernel K359** — The institutional mirror

Ask whether the institution you are part of practices what it preaches. Where does the gap between stated values and actual practice lie? The mirror is not about cynicism. It is about the Aristotelian insight that institutions, like persons, have characters, and that character is shown in action, not in mission statements.
