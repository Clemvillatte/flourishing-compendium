> **Kernel K340** — Repair-before-resolve

In a conflict, prioritise repair over resolution. You may not resolve the disagreement, but you can repair the relationship. Say: "We may not agree, and I want to make sure we are okay." Research on relationship repair (Gottman) shows that the capacity to repair predicts stability more than the absence of conflict.
