> **Kernel K279** — Sufficiency testing

For one domain (income, achievements, possessions, social media followers), define in advance what would be enough. Write the number down. When you reach it, stop. The exercise tests whether the goalpost has moved. Research on the hedonic treadmill shows that people adapt to improvements and raise their expectations, which undermines the well-being gains from achievement. Defining sufficiency in advance creates a brake on the escalation.
