> **Kernel K277** — Comfort recalibration

Deliberately introduce small discomforts into your routine: a cold shower, a skipped meal, a day without entertainment, a walk in the rain. The practice is the Stoic exercise of voluntary hardship (askesis). Research on stress inoculation shows that controlled exposure to mild stressors builds tolerance for future stress. The discomfort is the training, not the goal.
