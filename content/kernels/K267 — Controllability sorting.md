> **Kernel K267** — Controllability sorting

Take a current concern. Sort it into two columns: what is within your control (judgements, intentions, efforts) and what is not (outcomes, others' opinions, external events). Spend your energy on the first column. The exercise operationalises the Stoic dichotomy of control, which research on locus of control shows predicts well-being, health behaviours, and resilience.
