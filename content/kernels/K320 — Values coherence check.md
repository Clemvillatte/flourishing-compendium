> **Kernel K320** — Values coherence check

Monthly, review whether the way you are spending your time aligns with what you say you value. Where does your calendar contradict your stated priorities? The check is distinct from the Volume I meaning audit in that it targets the gap between stated values and lived values, not the felt quality of activities. Research on value congruence shows that alignment between stated and lived values predicts well-being more strongly than the content of the values themselves.
