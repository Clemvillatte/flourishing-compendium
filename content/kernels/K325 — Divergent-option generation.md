> **Kernel K325** — Divergent-option generation

When facing a problem, generate at least five possible solutions before evaluating any. The rule is: no judgement during generation. The practice trains divergent thinking, which research shows is a distinct cognitive process from convergent problem-solving. The quantity-first principle is supported by evidence that the first ideas are typically the most conventional and that novelty emerges later in the generation process.

### Protocol

The five are a floor, not a target; the study of what happens across a series of ideas is quoted below. Intended focus: Self and Motivation.

1. State the problem in one sentence, then generate at least five possible solutions, in writing, with the rule held deliberately: no judgement during generation, not even the quiet kind that edits as you go.
2. When the ideas run dry, stay at the page a little longer; the early ideas are the conventional ones arriving first, and the odd ones tend to come after the obvious ones are spent.
3. Only after the list reaches five, switch modes and evaluate, keeping the two phases visibly separate. Note where the chosen option sat in the sequence; the record teaches the rule faster than the rule does.

### Evidence

From the study of the serial order effect in divergent thinking:

> "During the course of divergent thinking (DT), the number of generated ideas decreases while the originality of ideas increases." (Abstract, [Wang, Hao, Ku, Grabner & Fink, 2017, Neuropsychologia 99, 92-100](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28259772/))

What this evidence does not test: the rule. The study measured executive functions and EEG while people generated uses for objects, and found the well-known trade across a series: fluency falls while originality rises, because the obvious ideas come first and more original ones appear as the generator suppresses the obvious and switches categories. The effect was tied to shifting and inhibition abilities in that sample, and the abstract reports nothing about the number five, about deferring evaluation, or about solving real problems. What it supports is the kernel's premise: novelty lives later in the list, which is why the floor of five exists and why judging early cuts off exactly the ideas the practice is for.
