> **Kernel K363** — The deliberative practice

In a group decision you are part of, ensure that every voice is heard before the verdict. Ask the quietest person what they think. Invite dissent. The practice is the operationalisation of deliberative democracy, which research shows produces more legitimate and more durable decisions than majority rule alone.
