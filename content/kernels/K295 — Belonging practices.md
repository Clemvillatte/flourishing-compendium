> **Kernel K295** — Belonging practices

Once a week, engage in a practice that creates belonging: attend a community event, join a shared activity, participate in a ritual. Belonging is not a feeling you wait for. It is a practice you engage in. Research on social connection (Holt-Lunstad) shows that social integration predicts mortality as strongly as smoking. The effect is not about quantity of contact but about perceived belonging.
