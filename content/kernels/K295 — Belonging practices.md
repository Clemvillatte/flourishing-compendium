> **Kernel K295** — Belonging practices

Once a week, engage in a practice that creates belonging: attend a community event, join a shared activity, participate in a ritual. The Confucian picture is harmony without crowd-following ([Analects 13:23, Muller translation](http://www.acmuller.net/con-dao/analects.html)), and the research grounding is the mortality meta-analysis of social relationships ([Holt-Lunstad, Smith & Layton, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20668659/)). Reach for it when belonging has become a feeling waited for rather than a practice engaged in.

### What it rests on

Confucius's line, in Muller's translation:

> "The noble man is in harmony but does not follow the crowd. The inferior man follows the crowd, but is not in harmony." — [Confucius, Analects 13:23, Muller translation](http://www.acmuller.net/con-dao/analects.html)

The weekly menu of practices is my own design; the harmony-without-conformity is Confucius's ([Analects 13:23, Muller translation](http://www.acmuller.net/con-dao/analects.html)).

### Protocol

The menu is editorial; the weekly cadence is the point.

1. Once a week, engage in a practice that creates belonging.
2. Attend a community event, join a shared activity, or participate in a ritual.
3. Choose practices that are genuine participation, not crowd-following.

### Evidence

The nearest research is the meta-analytic review of social relationships and mortality risk:

> "Across 148 studies (308,849 participants), the random effects weighted average effect size was OR = 1.50 (95% CI 1.42 to 1.59), indicating a 50% increased likelihood of survival for participants with stronger social relationships." — [Holt-Lunstad, Smith & Layton, 2010, PLoS Medicine 7(7), Abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20668659/)

The abstract also reports that "the association was strongest for complex measures of social integration (OR = 1.91; 95% CI 1.63 to 2.23)" and lowest for a binary living-alone indicator ([Holt-Lunstad et al., 2010, Abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20668659/)).

What this evidence does not test: the cited research pooled studies of social relationships and mortality; it does not establish the effectiveness of this editorial protocol, and it does not establish that perceived belonging rather than quantity of contact is the operative ingredient ([Holt-Lunstad et al., 2010, Abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20668659/)).

### A guided version

Not applicable: the practice is attendance and participation, not a recording.
