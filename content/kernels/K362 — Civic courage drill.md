> **Kernel K362** — Civic courage drill

Once a month, act on a civic concern you would normally avoid: attend a council meeting, write to a representative, join a community advocacy group, speak at a public forum. The drill trains the virtue of courage in its civic form. Research on civic engagement shows that participation predicts well-being through the mechanisms of social connection, purpose, and perceived efficacy.
