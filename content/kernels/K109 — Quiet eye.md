> **Kernel K109** — Quiet eye

Before executing a skilled action, hold a steady final gaze on the target and begin the movement from inside that stillness. The term is Joan Vickers', from her gaze studies of expert athletes, and it has a formal definition: the final fixation before the critical movement.

### Protocol

*Steps distilled from the quiet-eye literature; the one-to-two-second figure in the old lead was too loose — the formal minimum is 100 ms, and expert durations run longer.*

1. Fix your gaze on the single location or object that matters for the action, within about 3 degrees of visual angle.
2. Hold that fixation through the start of the critical movement: by Vickers' definition, "the onset of the QE occurs prior to a critical movement in the task, and the offset occurs when the gaze deviates of the object or location by more than 3° degrees of visual angle (or less) for a minimum of 100 ms" ([Vickers, 2007](http://www.quieteyesolutions.com/content/Vickers_2007/Introduction.htm)).
3. Do not rush the fixation away when the movement begins; the fixation may carry through and beyond the final movement.

### Evidence

The core empirical finding, in Vickers' own summary: "The duration of the quiet eye is longer in experts than non-experts, and also longer during successful versus unsuccessful trials (Vickers, 1996, 2007)" ([Vickers, 2007](http://www.quieteyesolutions.com/content/Vickers_2007/Introduction.htm)). In the original free-throw study, experts held their final fixation on the target for around 970 ms on made shots and about 810 ms on missed shots, while near-experts stayed under 400 ms either way (Vickers, 1996, as tabulated in the Sirnik et al. 2022 systematic review of visual attention in basketball shooting). Quiet-eye training studies (e.g., Harle & Vickers, 2001) report performance gains in aimed skills. What this literature does not test: quiet eye as a general attention practice outside aimed motor tasks, or any effect on well-being; the mechanism Vickers proposes is visuomotor programming, not the quieting of self-referential thought as such.

### A guided version

Not applicable as a recording: the practice is a fixation held inside a live action.
