> **Kernel K336** — Contamination sequence audit

Review a recent disappointment. Did you frame it as a contamination sequence, where a good beginning was ruined by a bad ending? Try reframing: what was good in it that the bad ending did not erase? The audit tests whether your narrative habit amplifies loss. Research shows that contamination sequences predict lower well-being.
