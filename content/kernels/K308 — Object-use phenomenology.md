> **Kernel K308** — Object-use phenomenology

Take an everyday object (a cup, a pen, a door handle). Use it with full attention to the experience: the weight, the texture, the temperature, the way your hand shapes itself to receive it. The exercise reveals that perception is not passive reception but active, embodied engagement. Research on embodied cognition shows that tool use extends the body schema to include the tool.
