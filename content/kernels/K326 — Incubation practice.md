> **Kernel K326** — Incubation practice

When stuck on a problem, step away from it. Walk, shower, garden, sleep. The incubation effect is one of the most robust findings in creativity research: solutions to problems often emerge after a period of non-focused engagement. The mechanism is not magic. It is the continued unconscious processing of the problem while conscious attention is occupied elsewhere.
