> **Kernel K348** — Name the loss

When you notice grief about environmental change, name it. "I am grieving the river I grew up near, which now runs dry in summer." The naming is not about despair. It is the first step of processing loss. Research on ecological grief (Cunsolo and Landman) shows that naming environmental grief, far from paralysing, can motivate stewardship.
