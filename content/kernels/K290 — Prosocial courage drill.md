> **Kernel K290** — Prosocial courage drill

Once a week, act on a situation where you see unfairness but would normally stay silent: speak up in a meeting when someone is talked over, challenge a biased comment, report a safety concern. The drill trains the virtue of courage in its social form. Research on bystander intervention shows that the presence of others reduces the likelihood of intervention, but that this diffusion can be overcome by deliberate practice.
