> **Kernel K305** — Bracketing interpretation

When a sensation or emotion arises, describe it before interpreting it. "Tightness in the chest, quickened breath, warmth in the face" before "I am anxious." The practice suspends the automatic leap from sensation to meaning, which is the core move of phenomenological method. Research on [[Affect labeling|affect labelling]] shows that naming the components of experience reduces amygdala reactivity.
