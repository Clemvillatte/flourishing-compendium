> **Kernel K350** — Seasonal mourning

At the change of each season, notice what has been lost: a species that no longer appears, a waterway that has changed, a temperature that has shifted. Allow the mourning without rushing to action. The practice trains the capacity to hold grief without being consumed by it, which is the precondition for sustained engagement.
