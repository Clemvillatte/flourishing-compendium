> **Kernel K254** — Character feedback loop

Ask three people who know you well: "What is one virtue you see in me? What is one area where I fall short?" Listen without defending. Write down what they say. The exercise tests whether your self-assessment matches how others experience you. Research on self-other agreement in personality shows that others' ratings predict behaviour better than self-ratings on several traits.
