> **Kernel K364** — Cosmopolitan reflection

Once a month, spend time in a community not your own. Listen. Do not advise. The practice is the Stoic exercise of cosmopolitanism: the recognition that you are a citizen of the world, not just of your own group. Research on intergroup contact (Pettigrew and Tropp) shows that cross-group contact reduces prejudice when it occurs under conditions of equal status and cooperation.
