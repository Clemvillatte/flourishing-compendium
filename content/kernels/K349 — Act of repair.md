> **Kernel K349** — Act of repair

Each month, take one concrete action of ecological repair: plant something native, remove invasive species from a local area, support a restoration project. The act is not about saving the planet. It is about the relational principle that you are part of the land you inhabit, and that care is not an abstract virtue but a concrete practice.
