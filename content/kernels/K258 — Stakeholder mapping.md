> **Kernel K258** — Stakeholder mapping

Before a significant decision, list every person affected by it. For each, write one sentence about how they would be affected and what they would care about. The exercise forces you out of your own perspective and into the relational field where the decision lands. Wise reasoning research shows that considering multiple viewpoints and recognising context-dependence predicts better life outcomes.
