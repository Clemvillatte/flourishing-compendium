> **Kernel K257** — Habituation by design

Choose one virtue and design a thirty-day habituation plan. Break the virtue into daily micro-actions so small that skipping feels absurd. If the virtue is justice, the action might be: notice one unfairness each day and do one small thing about it. Aristotle's insight is that we become just by doing just acts, not by reading about justice. The design makes the doing inevitable.
