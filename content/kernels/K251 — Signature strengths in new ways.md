> **Kernel K251** — Signature strengths in new ways

Take the VIA Inventory of Character Strengths (free online). Identify your top five. Each week, use one signature strength in a new way: creativity in a spreadsheet, curiosity in a conversation, kindness to a stranger. The meta-analytic evidence for strengths use shows modest but reliable effects on well-being, with the strongest associations for hope, zest, gratitude, love, and curiosity. The mechanism is not "feeling good about yourself" but the alignment of action with deeply valued traits.
