> **Kernel K262** — Pre-decision review

Before acting on a significant decision, write: What am I assuming? What would change if the assumption is wrong? What is the worst plausible outcome, and how would I handle it? What is the best plausible outcome? The review does not replace deliberation with a checklist. It slows the leap from judgement to action long enough for second thoughts to surface.
