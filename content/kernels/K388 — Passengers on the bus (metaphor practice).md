> **Kernel K388** — Passengers on the bus

Imagine you're driving a bus. Your negative thoughts are passengers — nasty, scary-looking ones barking orders and being obnoxious. You could stop the bus and argue with them, but that defeats the whole purpose of the trip. You could follow them around making sure they don't misbehave, but then you're not driving.

The practice: when negative thoughts show up, notice them as passengers. Don't stop driving. Don't take them too seriously. They're just thoughts. Keep going where you're going.

Adapted from Hayes, Strosahl, & Wilson (2012), *Acceptance and Commitment Therapy* (2nd ed.); retold in Tolin, *Doing CBT* (2nd ed.), ch. 15, printed pp. 397–398 (metaphor box at p. 397).
