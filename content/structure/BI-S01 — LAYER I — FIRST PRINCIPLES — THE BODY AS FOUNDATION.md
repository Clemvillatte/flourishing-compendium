These are the axioms: understanding that the body is the foundation of mental life, and that physiology and psychology are inseparable. Every downstream principle assumes this.


## Principles

- [[BI001 — Understand that the body is the foundation of the mind — physiology shapes psychology]]
- [[BI002 — Recognise that the autonomic nervous system has two branches — arousal and restoration]]
- [[BI003 — Know that heart rate variability (HRV) is a marker of autonomic flexibility and health]]
- [[BI004 — Understand the stress response — cortisol, adrenaline, and the HPA axis]]
- [[BI005 — Recognise that inflammation affects mood — the immune system and depression]]
- [[BI006 — Understand that neurotransmitters are not simple cause-and-effect — complexity matters]]
- [[BI007 — Recognise that the gut is connected to the brain — the gut-brain axis]]
- [[BI008 — Know that circadian rhythms regulate everything — light, sleep, mood, hormones]]
- [[BI009 — Understand that interoception is the foundation of emotional awareness]]
- [[BI010 — Recognise that the body can be used to regulate the mind — bottom-up regulation]]
- [[BI011 — Know that polyvagal theory is popular but contested — practices may work without the theory]]
- [[BI012 — Understand that the body has limits — health, illness, and medication affect everything]]
