Ask: What dimension is primarily affected? Attention, cognition, affect, self, motivation, or overt behavior? What level? Biophysiological (body, breath, sleep), psychological (thoughts, emotions, habits), or sociocultural (relationships, community, environment)?

The targeting question matters because a problem of attention will not yield to a motivation intervention, and a sociocultural pattern will not shift through breathwork alone. If the dimension is unclear, start with the body (biophysiological level) and work upward.
