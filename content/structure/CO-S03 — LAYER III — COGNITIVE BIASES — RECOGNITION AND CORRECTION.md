These principles cover specific biases and how to counteract them.


## Principles

- [[CO027 — Learn debiasing — it is possible but hard]]
- [[CO028 — Use the outside view — base rates beat inside views]]
- [[CO029 — Practise consider the opposite — deliberately generate counterarguments]]
- [[CO030 — Use pre-mortems — imagine failure before it happens]]
- [[CO031 — Recognise loss aversion — losses hurt more than gains feel good]]
- [[CO032 — Recognise the endowment effect — you value what you own more]]
- [[CO033 — Recognise the status quo bias — you prefer things as they are]]
- [[CO034 — Understand the affect heuristic — feelings substitute for reasoning]]
- [[CO035 — Recognise the representativeness heuristic — similarity is mistaken for probability]]
- [[CO036 — Recognise the halo effect — one trait colours the whole judgment]]
- [[CO037 — Recognise the fundamental attribution error — you blame persons, not situations]]
- [[CO038 — Understand the better-than-average effect — you think you are above average]]
- [[CO039 — Recognise optimism bias — you underestimate your personal risk]]
- [[CO040 — Know the curse of knowledge — you cannot un-know what you know]]
