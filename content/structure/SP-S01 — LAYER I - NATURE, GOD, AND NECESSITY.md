The axioms. Everything in this book depends on these: the single system of nature, the necessity of all things, the rejection of purposes in nature, and the double unity of mind and body.


## Principles

- [[SP001 — Understand rather than condemn]]
- [[SP002 — See everything as one system]]
- [[SP003 — Accept that nothing is contingent]]
- [[SP004 — Stop reading purposes into events]]
- [[SP005 — Treat yourself as part of nature]]
- [[SP006 — Treat mind and body as one thing]]
- [[SP007 — Keep thought and world in parallel]]
- [[SP008 — Treat good and evil as relations, not properties]]
- [[SP009 — Measure perfection as power, not as judgment]]
- [[SP010 — Know yourself as striving]]
