The culmination: eternity, the third kind of knowledge, the intellectual love of God, and blessedness as the mind's greatest strength.


## Principles

- [[SP082 — Practice seeing things under the aspect of eternity]]
- [[SP083 — Understand that the more the mind knows, the more of it remains]]
- [[SP084 — Seek the third kind of knowledge directly]]
- [[SP085 — Cultivate the intellectual love of God]]
- [[SP086 — Trust the love that cannot become hate]]
- [[SP087 — Accept that blessedness is virtue itself, not its reward]]
- [[SP088 — Expect the excellent to be difficult and rare]]
- [[SP089 — Rest in the peace that understanding brings]]
