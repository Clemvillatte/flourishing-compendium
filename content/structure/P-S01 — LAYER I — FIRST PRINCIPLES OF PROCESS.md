The axioms. Everything in the lineage — and everything in this list — depends on these.


## Principles

- [[P001 — Build your life on becoming, not being]]
- [[P002 — Treat the world as events and doings, not things and substances]]
- [[P003 — Relation is primary]]
- [[P004 — Start from experience, not from theory]]
- [[P005 — You are an organism in transaction with an environment]]
- [[P006 — Creativity is the ultimate principle]]
- [[P007 — Expect emergence — wholes exceed their parts]]
- [[P008 — Take perspectives seriously — the human measure is real]]
- [[P009 — Truth is made, not merely found]]
- [[P010 — Hold every belief as provisional — fallibilism]]
- [[P011 — Refuse essences — things have no fixed inner nature]]
- [[P012 — An idea's meaning is its practical effects]]
- [[P013 — Judge by workability]]
- [[P014 — The good life is a process, not a state]]
- [[P015 — Make growth your criterion of value]]
- [[P016 — Treat identity as a verb]]
- [[P017 — Suspend dogma — practice systematic open-mindedness]]
- [[P018 — Practice practical wisdom — judgment over rules]]
