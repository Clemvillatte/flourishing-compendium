These kernels target the cultivation of settled dispositions (hexeis) through repeated, deliberate practice. They draw on Aristotelian habituation theory, VIA character strengths research, and social learning evidence. The core mechanism is that virtues are built the way skills are built: through repetition with feedback, not through reading or intention alone.
