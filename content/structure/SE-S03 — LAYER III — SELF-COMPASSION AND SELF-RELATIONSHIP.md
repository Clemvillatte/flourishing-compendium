These principles cover how to relate to yourself: self-compassion, self-kindness, and the internal relationship.


## Principles

- [[SE025 — Cultivate self-compassion — treat yourself as you would a good friend]]
- [[SE026 — Recognise common humanity — your suffering is not unique or isolating]]
- [[SE027 — Practise self-kindness over self-judgment — kindness is more motivating than cruelty]]
- [[SE028 — Learn mindful self-awareness — hold your experience without over-identifying]]
- [[SE029 — Learn to comfort yourself — develop internal sources of soothing]]
- [[SE030 — Recognise and work with shame — shame is about the self, not the act]]
- [[SE031 — Learn self-forgiveness — release what cannot be changed]]
- [[SE032 — Cultivate an inner ally — develop a supportive internal voice]]
- [[SE033 — Learn to hold your imperfections with humour — lightness is a virtue]]
- [[SE034 — Recognise that self-relationship is the foundation of all relationships]]
- [[SE082 — The seal of attained liberty is to no longer be ashamed of oneself]]
