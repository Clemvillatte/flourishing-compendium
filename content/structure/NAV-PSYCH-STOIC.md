> **Stoic Practice** — an approach within the Psychology track.

The Stoic kernels gather the practices drawn from the Stoic tradition of Hellenistic-Roman philosophy. They centre on the distinction between what is and is not up to the practitioner, the disciplined examination of impressions, and the acceptance of what cannot be controlled. The cards here reference Stoic authors and modern scholarly work on the tradition; the therapeutic approach is not a clinical modality but a philosophy of life with therapeutic effects, and the red modality line on each card notes "Stoic" for this reason.
