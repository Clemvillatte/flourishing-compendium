These principles cover how beliefs form, how they resist change, and how to update them effectively.


## Principles

- [[CO015 — Recognise confirmation bias — you seek evidence that supports what you already believe]]
- [[CO016 — Understand belief perseverance — beliefs persist after their evidence is discredited]]
- [[CO017 — Recognise the availability heuristic — vividness is mistaken for frequency]]
- [[CO018 — Understand anchoring — initial values distort subsequent judgments]]
- [[CO019 — Recognise framing effects — how something is presented changes how you evaluate it]]
- [[CO020 — Know the base rate neglect — you ignore background frequencies]]
- [[CO021 — Understand the sunk cost fallacy — past investment distorts present decisions]]
- [[CO022 — Recognise the planning fallacy — you underestimate time, cost, and risk]]
- [[CO023 — Understand survivorship bias — you see winners, not the full distribution]]
- [[CO024 — Recognise hindsight bias — I knew it all along.]]
- [[CO025 — Know the Dunning-Kruger effect — incompetence is invisible to itself]]
- [[CO026 — Recognise the illusion of explanatory depth — you understand less than you think]]
