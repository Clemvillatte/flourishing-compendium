The third of Epictetus's three disciplines. It concerns how to relate to your own thoughts and impressions.


## Principles

- [[S045 — Examine every impression]]
- [[S046 — Do not add to the impression]]
- [[S047 — See impressions as what they are — appearances, not realities]]
- [[S048 — Withhold assent when the impression is uncertain]]
- [[S049 — Replace first movements with considered responses]]
- [[S050 — Test your impressions against reality]]
- [[S051 — Recognise that your judgements are the source of your distress]]
- [[S052 — Practise the view from above]]
- [[S053 — Distinguish between what you control and what you influence]]
- [[S054 — Distinguish between facts and interpretations]]
- [[S055 — Do not argue with impressions]]
- [[S056 — Recognise that you can choose your response]]
- [[S057 — Recognise that your thoughts are not you]]
- [[S058 — Practise reframing]]
