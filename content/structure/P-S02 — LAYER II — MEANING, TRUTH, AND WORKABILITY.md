How to hold ideas: as instruments, tested by use.


## Principles

- [[P019 — Read an idea through its consequences]]
- [[P020 — Ask for the cash value of your beliefs]]
- [[P021 — Treat ideas as tools, not mirrors]]
- [[P022 — Verify in experience — test beliefs by living them]]
- [[P023 — Treat beliefs as habits of action]]
- [[P024 — Let doubt be genuine]]
- [[P025 — Fix belief by experiment, not tenacity, authority, or taste]]
- [[P026 — Make inquiry communal]]
- [[P027 — Guess well — practice abduction]]
- [[P028 — Expect truth to be what works in the long run]]
- [[P029 — Watch how words work, not what they name]]
- [[P030 — Accept many versions of the world]]
- [[P031 — Choose vocabularies by what they let you do]]
- [[P032 — Walk the middle path between objectivism and relativism]]
- [[P033 — Let truth change its face]]
- [[P034 — Know that every view is from somewhere]]
- [[P035 — Retire obsolete concepts]]
- [[P036 — Notice that language shapes what you can see]]
