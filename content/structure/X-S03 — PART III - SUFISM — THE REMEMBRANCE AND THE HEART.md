The distinct instruments of the Sufi path: dhikr (remembrance practice), the nafs (the commanding self's training), the paired sabr-shukr meter, intention purification, and the mirror of the heart. What is distinct here against Books I-IV: the repetition-based remembrance practice, the explicit technology for purifying intention from display, and the heart-as-mirror tradition of moral self-examination.
