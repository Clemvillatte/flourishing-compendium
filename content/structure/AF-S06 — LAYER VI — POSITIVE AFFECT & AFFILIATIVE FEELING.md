These principles cover positive affect: how to cultivate it, how to savour it, and how to avoid its traps.


## Principles

- [[AF061 — Cultivate positive affect deliberately — it broadens and builds]]
- [[AF062 — Savour positive experiences — deliberately attend to and amplify them]]
- [[AF063 — Practise gratitude — it recalibrates attention toward what is good]]
- [[AF064 — Cultivate awe — it expands the self by diminishing it]]
- [[AF065 — Cultivate compassion — it is sustainable empathy plus concern]]
- [[AF066 — Cultivate self-compassion — treat yourself as you would a good friend]]
- [[AF067 — Cultivate hope — it is not wishful thinking; it is agency plus pathway]]
- [[AF068 — Cultivate pride (authentic, not hubristic) — it consolidates achievement]]
- [[AF069 — Recognise the hedonic treadmill — adaptation is inevitable; plan for it]]
- [[AF070 — Seek variety and novelty to slow hedonic adaptation]]
- [[AF071 — Recognise the peak-end rule — memory of experiences is shaped by peaks and endings]]
- [[AF072 — Recognise that positive affect alone is not flourishing; it is one component]]
