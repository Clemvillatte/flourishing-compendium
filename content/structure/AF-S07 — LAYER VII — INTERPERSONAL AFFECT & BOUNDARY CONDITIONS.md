These principles cover affect in relationships and the boundary conditions — clinical, cultural, and individual — that determine when self-directed practice is insufficient.


## Principles

- [[AF073 — Learn co-regulation — affect is regulated with others, not just within]]
- [[AF074 — Recognise co-rumination — sharing distress can amplify it]]
- [[AF075 — Learn emotional validation — acknowledging a feeling reduces its intensity]]
- [[AF076 — Recognise expressive suppression in close relationships — it reduces intimacy]]
- [[AF077 — Learn emotional disclosure — the capacity to share feelings in a way that helps]]
- [[AF078 — Recognise when affect requires professional help — clinical boundaries]]
- [[AF079 — Recognise trauma's specific effects on affect — it is not the same as ordinary dysregulation]]
- [[AF080 — Recognise bipolar disorder's specific affective pattern — it is not mood variability]]
- [[AF081 — Recognise neurodivergent affect — it is not disordered]]
- [[AF082 — Recognise affect's biophysiological interfaces — sleep, pain, and illness are not separate]]
- [[AF083 — Recognise affect's sociocultural interfaces — culture shapes what you feel and how]]
- [[AF084 — Build a personal affect-maintenance system — learn, adapt, retain]]
