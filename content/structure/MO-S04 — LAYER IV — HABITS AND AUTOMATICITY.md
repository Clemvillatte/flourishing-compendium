These principles cover habit formation: how behaviours become automatic and sustainable.


## Principles

- [[MO035 — Understand that habits are the foundation of sustainable motivation]]
- [[MO036 — Learn to use context-dependent repetition — same context, same behaviour]]
- [[MO037 — Learn to stack habits — link new behaviours to existing ones]]
- [[MO038 — Recognise that habits take time — do not expect immediate automaticity]]
- [[MO039 — Learn to make habits small — start tiny]]
- [[MO040 — Learn to design habit environment — context is the trigger]]
- [[MO041 — Learn to disrupt bad habits — change the context]]
- [[MO042 — Learn to replace habits — substitute, do not just eliminate]]
- [[MO043 — Recognise the role of reward in habit formation — immediate rewards work]]
- [[MO044 — Learn to track habits — monitoring sustains]]
