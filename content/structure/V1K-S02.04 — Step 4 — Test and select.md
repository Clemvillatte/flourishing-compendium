Try the kernels. Track what changes. The selection criterion is workability: does this kernel, in this context, move your life toward your values? A kernel that works beautifully on Monday may fall flat on Wednesday. That is not failure; it is information about context.

Track with simple measures: a 1-to-10 rating, a count (how many times did I catch mind-wandering?), or a subjective note ("felt calmer," "still avoidant"). You are not running a clinical trial; you are calibrating.
