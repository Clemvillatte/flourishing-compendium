The compilation looped until every candidate principle reduced to an entry below. For each major source, the checklist records the principles extracted and the point at which further mining produced only duplicates.

| Source cluster | Principles contributed | Saturation check |
|---|---|---|
| Four tasks / four truths frame | 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 16, 17 | Every further task-statement (e.g., "live ethically") reduces to Layer II entries |
| Conditionality and karma as causality | 4, 8, 9, 88, 89, 136 | Remaining karma material is either rebirth-cosmological (excluded) or duplicates these |
| The training (path factors, faculties, factors, hindrances) | 20-37, 79-96 | All 37 bodhipakkhiya factors accounted for across Layers II-IV; no factor yields a distinct unlisted lever |
| Meditation repertoire | 38-64 | Canonical objects (breath, body, feelings, mind, dhammas; brahmaviharas; kasinas and form/limitless abodes folded into 42-45, 57) collapse into listed entries |
| Mind-training and emotion work | 65-96, 97-118 | Shantideva's ch. 6 methods, lojong slogans, and brahmavihara near-enemies fully distributed |
| Emptiness / view / Mahayana | 119-143 | Nagarjuna, Heart/Diamond Sutras, Madhyamaka reasoning reduce to 121-143; further Madhyamaka analysis is argumentation, not new practice levers |
| Chan/Zen/Thien practice instructions | 44-64, 129-139, 166-198 | Linji-lu, koan collections, Platform Sutra, Bankei, Dogen, Layman Pang, Zhaozhou, Xinxin Ming, Suzuki, Hakuin: every instruction-form maps to a listed entry |
| Tiantai (Zhiyi) | 35, 63 | Calming-insight balance and thought-moment doctrine; remaining Mohe Zhiguan content is either ritual/cosmological (excluded) or meditation technique already covered |
| Trúc Lâm / Vietnamese Thiền | 196 and Layer VIII integration | Cu tran lac dao and Trần Nhân Tông's "in the world" teaching; remaining material is exhortation, not new principle |
| Thích Nhất Hạnh / engaged Buddhism | 41, 147-148, 151, 156, 163-198 | The mindfulness-trainings framework fully distributed across Layers V-VIII |
| Secular Buddhism (Batchelor) | 1, 7, 16, 128, 130, 160, 198 | Four tasks, ELSA frame, agnosticism, and task-based reading fully absorbed |

Excluded by scope: rebirth and karmic cosmology, Pure Land devotion (nembutsu as faith practice), ritual and merit ceremony, devas/hells/pure lands, miracles and supernatural claims, monastic-specific rules (vinaya technicalities), and abhidhamma classification beyond its practice content.
