### Meta-analyses and systematic reviews

Broaden-and-build: Fredrickson, 2001, American Psychologist
Positive psychology interventions: Bolier et al., 2013, Journal of Positive Psychology; White et al., 2019
Character strengths: Casali and Feraco, 2025 (meta-analysis, 130 studies, N=275,007)
Self-determination theory: Vansteenkiste and Ryan, 2013
Social relationships and mortality: Holt-Lunstad et al., 2010, PLOS Medicine
Exercise and depression: Cooney et al., 2013, Cochrane Database
Mindfulness: Goyal et al., 2014, JAMA Internal Medicine
Self-compassion: Wilson et al., 2019
Gratitude: Wood et al., 2010
Forgiveness: Griffin et al., 2015
Flow: meta-analysis, 2025
Awe: Bai et al., 2021
Creativity and constraints: Acar et al., 2019
Psychological flexibility: Gloster et al., 2020
Meaning in life: Steger, 2012
Recovery: Sonnentag, 2018
