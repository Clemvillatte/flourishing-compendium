These principles cover different modes of thinking and how to use them well.


## Principles

- [[CO041 — Trust intuition in the right domains — it is not always wrong]]
- [[CO042 — Know when to deliberate — high-stakes and novel situations need slow thinking]]
- [[CO043 — Recognise the limits of analysis — overthinking can be worse than deciding]]
- [[CO044 — Learn to satisfice — good enough is often optimal]]
- [[CO045 — Cultivate cognitive flexibility — the capacity to switch between modes]]
- [[CO046 — Recognise rumination as a distinct and maladaptive cognitive mode]]
- [[CO047 — Learn concrete rumination-stopping techniques — do not just stop ruminating.]]
- [[CO048 — Distinguish worry from problem-solving — worry is not planning]]
- [[CO049 — Cultivate mindfulness of thoughts — observing thinking without being consumed by it]]
- [[CO050 — Learn to question your thoughts — not every thought deserves belief]]
- [[CO051 — Recognise cognitive distortions — learn the common patterns]]
- [[CO052 — Cultivate dialectical thinking — hold contradictions without forcing resolution]]
