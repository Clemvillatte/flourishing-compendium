The first of Epictetus's three disciplines. It concerns what to want and what to avoid.

- [[S013 — Discipline your desires]]
- [[S014 — Want what actually happens]]
- [[S015 — Distinguish preferred from dispreferred indifferents]]
- [[S016 — Do not desire what belongs to others]]
- [[S017 — Refuse to see externals as good or bad]]
- [[S018 — Accept loss without devastation]]
- [[S019 — Prepare for adversity before it arrives]]
- [[S020 — Remember that nothing can harm you except your own judgement]]
- [[S021 — Do not desire externals as if they were permanent]]
- [[S022 — Do not fear poverty]]
- [[S023 — Do not fear pain]]
- [[S024 — Do not fear death]]
- [[S025 — Do not seek pleasure as the highest good]]
- [[S026 — Practise contentment with what you have]]
- [[S027 — Do not crave fame]]
- [[S028 — Distinguish between needing and wanting]]
