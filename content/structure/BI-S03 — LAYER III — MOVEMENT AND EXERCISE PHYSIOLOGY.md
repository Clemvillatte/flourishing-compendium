These principles cover exercise physiology: movement as a foundation for mental health.


## Principles

- [[BI023 — Use exercise as a mental health intervention — it is antidepressant and anxiolytic]]
- [[BI024 — Learn the dose — how much, how hard, how often]]
- [[BI025 — Learn that aerobic exercise is particularly effective for mood]]
- [[BI026 — Learn that strength training is also effective for mental health]]
- [[BI027 — Learn that movement in nature is doubly beneficial]]
- [[BI028 — Learn that exercise timing matters — morning vs evening]]
- [[BI029 — Learn that yoga and tai chi combine movement, breath, and meditation]]
- [[BI030 — Learn that walking is underrated — simple, accessible, effective]]
- [[BI031 — Learn that excessive exercise can be harmful — overtraining]]
- [[BI032 — Learn to build exercise habits — consistency over intensity]]
