- Everyday activities and mindfulness: [Hanley et al., 2015, Mindfulness (the mindful dishwashing study)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4576680/)

- Awe and self-transcendence: [awe RCT line](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12069556/)
- Environmental restoration and attention: [brain drain hypothesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10525686/); [Ward, attention and creative output](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/691462)
