These principles cover the core behavioural choice: approach vs avoidance, and how to build approach behaviours.

- [[BE026 — Learn exposure therapy principles — approach what you fear]]

- [[BE028 — Learn interoceptive exposure — approach bodily sensations]]
- [[BE029 — Learn to use response prevention — block the compulsive behaviour]]

- [[BE032 — Learn approach behaviours for depression — engage, do not withdraw]]
- [[BE033 — Learn approach behaviours for anger — step toward, do not attack]]
- [[BE034 — Learn approach behaviours for shame — show up, do not hide]]
