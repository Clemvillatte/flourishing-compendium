| Source cluster | Principles contributed | Saturation check |
|---|---|---|
| Ubuntu philosophy (Mbiti, Tutu, Ramose, Gyekye, Wiredu) | 263-274 | Every major concept of Ubuntu (umuntu, ubuntu, compassion, hospitality, forgiveness, consensus, ancestors, collective responsibility, elders, generosity, harmony, dignity) is represented; remaining material is restatement |
| Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander SEWB | 275-287 | All seven SEWB connection domains (Country, culture, community, family/kinship, spirituality, mind/emotions, body) plus cultural continuity, historical trauma healing, self-determination, cultural safety, Dreaming, resilience covered; framework is fully distributed |
| Māori tikanga and whakataukī | 288-302 | All core concepts (whanaungatanga, manaakitanga, kaitiakitanga, rangatiratanga, kotahitanga, mana, tapu, utu, tika, pono, aroha, Te Whare Tapa Whā, whakawhanaungatanga, hau, whānau ora) represented; remaining concepts are variations or applications |
| Pacific relational philosophy (vā) | 303-312 | Vā, teu le vā, fa'asamoa, tautua, matai, talanoa, va tapuia, ofo, alofa, ocean connection all represented; framework is comprehensive |
| Confucian ethics (Four Books) | 313-327 | All core virtues (ren, yi, li, zhi, xin) plus zhengming, five relationships, filial piety, Mencian sprouts, self-cultivation, Doctrine of the Mean, harmony, rectification of mind, reciprocity, extension of knowledge covered; the Four Books are fully distributed |
| Ethics of care (Gilligan, Noddings, Tronto, Kittay, Held) | 328-342 | Care orientation, receptive caring, motivational displacement, Tronto's four phases (attentiveness, responsibility, competence, responsiveness), dependency (Kittay), care as political (Held), self-care, relational autonomy, moral emotion, distant care, care for nature all covered; tradition is fully distributed |
| Dialogical philosophy (Buber, Levinas, Marcel) | 343-362 | I-Thou, primacy of relation, face, infinite responsibility, availability, fidelity, hope, problem/mystery, incarnation, participation, the spoken word, listening, ethics before ontology, substitution, trace, the third, ethical demand, I-It, the Between, greeting all covered; primary texts are fully distributed |
| Relational autonomy and communitarianism | 363-377 | Relational autonomy, constitutive relationality, communitarian critique, recognition, narrative identity, situated freedom, common good, deliberative community, social justice, capabilities, recognition of difference, intergenerational justice, solidarity, structural responsibility, digital ethics all covered; tradition is fully distributed |
| Ecological relationality | 378-392 | Ecological embeddedness, land ethic, deep ecology, ecological self, ecological justice, intergenerational responsibility, biophilia, ecological mindfulness, ecological grief, ecological hope, ecological citizenship, ecological humility, ecological gratitude, ecological spirituality, more-than-human self all covered; tradition is fully distributed |

Excluded by scope: technical political theory (beyond what informs practice principles); detailed policy prescriptions (not actionable as personal principles); the internal debates of care ethics (absorbed into listed principles); the technical details of capabilities measurement; the full corpus of Pacific oral traditions (restricted knowledge not included); the full corpus of Aboriginal ceremonial life (restricted knowledge not included).
