When a kernel proves workable, retain it. Link it to an existing cue (after morning coffee, before bed, at every red light). Schedule it. Protect it from decay by practicing it regularly. Over time, the practiced skill becomes an embodied disposition: it happens by itself, in the flow of life. This is what the Daoist tradition calls second nature and the Buddhist tradition calls the trained mind.
