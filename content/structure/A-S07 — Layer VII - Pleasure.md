Aristotle's treatment of pleasure is nuanced and contested. He rejects both hedonism and anti-hedonism.


## Principles

- [[A079 — Do not demonise pleasure]]
- [[A080 — Choose activities for their completeness]]
- [[A081 — Recognise that pleasure completes activity]]
- [[A082 — Distinguish good pleasures from bad pleasures]]
- [[A083 — Recognise that bodily pleasures are not the highest]]
- [[A084 — Understand that pleasure is not the good but accompanies it]]
- [[A085 — Practise moderation in pleasure]]
- [[A086 — Recognise that pain is not always bad]]
- [[A087 — Seek the pleasure of contemplation]]
- [[A088 — Recognise that pleasure varies with the activity]]
