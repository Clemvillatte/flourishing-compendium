These are the axioms: understanding how attention works, what it is, and what it can and cannot do. Every downstream principle assumes this.


## Principles

- [[AT001 — Understand that attention is selective — you cannot attend to everything]]
- [[AT002 — Know that attention has three networks — alerting, orienting, and executive control]]
- [[AT003 — Recognise that attention is a limited resource — it can be depleted and fatigued]]
- [[AT004 — Understand that attention can be captured (bottom-up) or directed (top-down)]]
- [[AT005 — Know that attention and awareness are not the same — you can be aware without attending]]
- [[AT006 — Recognise that attention is trainable — it can be cultivated]]
- [[AT007 — Understand that attention shapes experience — what you attend to grows]]
- [[AT008 — Recognise that attention is embodied — physical state affects attentional capacity]]
- [[AT009 — Know that attention is social — it is influenced by and influences others]]
- [[AT010 — Understand that attention has a temporal structure — it oscillates]]
- [[AT011 — Recognise that attention can be open or closed — both are needed]]
- [[AT012 — Understand that attention is the gateway to experience — it is where well-being is lived]]
