These are the axioms: understanding how behaviour works, what drives it, and what it can and cannot do. Every downstream principle assumes this.


## Principles

- [[BE001 — Understand that behaviour is the foundation — what you do shapes how you feel, think, and are]]
- [[BE002 — Recognise that behaviour is shaped by consequences — reinforcement and punishment]]
- [[BE003 — Know that behaviour is triggered by context — environment drives action]]
- [[BE004 — Understand that behaviour is learned — skills can be acquired]]
- [[BE005 — Recognise that behaviour is specific — general be better does not work]]
- [[BE006 — Know that behaviour has a functional analysis — every behaviour serves a function]]
- [[BE007 — Understand that behaviour change follows stages — precontemplation to maintenance]]
- [[BE008 — Recognise that behaviour is maintained by reinforcement schedules — consistency matters]]
- [[BE009 — Know that behaviour is shaped gradually — small steps build large changes]]
- [[BE010 — Understand that behaviour is social — others shape what you do]]
- [[BE011 — Recognise that behaviour is measurable — tracking improves outcomes]]
- [[BE012 — Understand that behaviour is ultimately about values — action should serve what matters]]
