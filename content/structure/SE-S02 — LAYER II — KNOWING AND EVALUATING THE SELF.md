These principles cover self-knowledge and self-evaluation: how to know and judge yourself accurately and kindly.


## Principles

- [[SE013 — Cultivate accurate self-knowledge — know your traits, values, and patterns]]
- [[SE014 — Know your values — what actually matters to you]]
- [[SE015 — Recognise your strengths and weaknesses — accurate, not inflated]]
- [[SE016 — Learn self-distancing — view yourself from the outside]]
- [[SE017 — Recognise and manage the inner critic — harsh self-talk is not motivation]]
- [[SE018 — Learn accurate self-evaluation — neither inflated nor deflated]]
- [[SE019 — Recognise the spotlight effect — you overestimate how much others notice you]]
- [[SE020 — Learn self-verification — seek feedback that confirms your self-view]]
- [[SE021 — Recognise contingent self-worth — what your self-esteem depends on]]
- [[SE022 — Cultivate self-acceptance — accept who you are without resigning to it]]
- [[SE023 — Learn to distinguish self-worth from performance]]
- [[SE024 — Recognise the self-serving bias — you take credit for success and blame failure on circumstances]]
