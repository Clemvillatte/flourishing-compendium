These principles cover the capacity to allow difficult feelings without amplifying them: the foundation of ACT, DBT distress tolerance, and mindfulness-based approaches.


## Principles

- [[AF039 — Learn acceptance — the willingness to have a feeling without fighting it]]
- [[AF040 — Distinguish accepting a feeling from accepting a circumstance]]
- [[AF041 — Learn distress tolerance — the capacity to bear intense affect without making it worse]]
- [[AF042 — Practise opposite action — do the opposite of what the feeling urges]]
- [[AF043 — Learn to observe feelings without identification — you are not your affect]]
- [[AF044 — Recognise secondary emotions — feelings about feelings]]
- [[AF045 — Learn to let affect pass — feelings are impermanent]]
- [[AF046 — Know that tolerance is not the goal; valued living is]]
