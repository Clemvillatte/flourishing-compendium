### Corpus

Kyoto School: Nishida Kitaro, An Inquiry into the Good (1911) and the late essays on place, action-intuition, and self-contradictory identity; Tanabe Hajime, Philosophy as Metanoetics (1946); Nishitani Keiji, Religion and Nothingness (1961); Abe Masao, Zen and Western Thought; Hisamatsu Shin'ichi, the FAS essays and Zen and the Fine Arts.

Tibetan lineages: Shantideva, Bodhicharyavatara (The Way of the Bodhisattva), especially chapters 5, 6, and 8; the Seven-Point Mind Training with its fifty-nine slogans (the Atisha-Chekawa lineage); Tsongkhapa's graduated path (the lamrim structure as taught in the Great Treatise and in Patrul Rinpoche's Words of My Perfect Teacher); and the Mahamudra lineage instructions (Tilopa's Six Words of Advice, Saraha's songs, Gampopa's Jewel Ornament of Liberation, Milarepa's Hundred Thousand Songs).
