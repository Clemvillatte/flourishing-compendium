These principles cover the relationship between motivation and well-being: how to pursue goals in ways that enhance rather than undermine flourishing.


## Principles

- [[MO055 — Recognise that goal progress predicts well-being — pursuing goals is good for you]]
- [[MO056 — Recognise that goal attainment is not the same as well-being — arrival is not fulfilment]]
- [[MO057 — Recognise the importance of self-concordant goals — your goals, not others']]
- [[MO058 — Learn to disengage from unattainable goals — letting go is adaptive]]
- [[MO059 — Learn to balance striving and contentment — ambition without suffering]]
- [[MO060 — Recognise the role of flow in motivated well-being]]
- [[MO061 — Learn to cultivate meaning — purpose sustains motivation]]
- [[MO062 — Learn to use rewards wisely — reinforcement, not bribery]]
- [[MO063 — Recognise burnout — motivation can be exhausted]]
- [[MO064 — Learn to rest and recover — motivation requires recovery]]
