These principles cover the architecture of regulation: how to regulate, when, and with what trade-offs.


## Principles

- [[AF025 — Learn the full regulation toolkit — it is larger than calm down.]]
- [[AF026 — Prefer cognitive reappraisal over suppression when possible]]
- [[AF027 — But do not reappraise everything; reappraisal has limits]]
- [[AF028 — Master emotional regulation flexibility — the skill is matching strategy to context]]
- [[AF029 — Build a regulation repertoire — the number of strategies you can deploy matters]]
- [[AF030 — Learn to read feedback from your regulation attempts]]
- [[AF031 — Regulate early, not late — timing matters more than intensity]]
- [[AF032 — Recognise that suppression is not always maladaptive; it depends on context and culture]]
- [[AF033 — Learn self-distancing — the ability to step back from your own experience]]
- [[AF034 — Cultivate equanimity — the capacity to remain steady amid affect]]
- [[AF035 — Recognise that avoidance is negatively reinforcing — it works short-term and maintains suffering long-term]]
- [[AF036 — Learn to regulate through action — affect follows behaviour]]
- [[AF037 — Recognise the costs of over-regulation — you can try too hard]]
- [[AF038 — Know when to stop regulating and start acting]]
