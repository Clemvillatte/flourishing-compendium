|---|---|---|
| Ancient process sources (Heraclitus, Protagoras, Pyrrho/Sextus, Zhuangzi, Nagarjuna, Aristotle) | 1, 3, 8, 10, 11, 17, 18, 82 | Further ancient material either duplicates these or has no practice lever |
| Precursors (Vico, Hume, Hegel, Goethe, Darwin, Nietzsche, Wright) | 9, 16, 23, 36, 43, 54, 88, 112 | Hegel's dialectic and Goethe's morphology survive only where they yield action (16, 43, 112); the rest reduces to later, more workable formulations |
| Classical pragmatism (Peirce, James, Schiller, Dewey, Addams, Mead) | 4, 5, 10-13, 19-32, 37-45, 48-52, 55-70, 73-91, 94, 99-100, 103-131, 133-138, 140, 144, 174, 177-180 | The corpus's practice content is fully distributed across Layers II-VIII; no further Peirce/James/Dewey text yields a distinct unlisted lever |
| Process philosophy proper (Bergson, Alexander, Morgan, Whitehead, Rescher) | 1-3, 6-7, 72, 91-97, 102, 104-106, 113, 148-149, 153-154 | Technical metaphysics (prehension detail, extensive continuum, dipolarity) reduces to 2, 3, 96, 147; the rest is argumentation, not practice |
| Pluralist turns (Wittgenstein, Quine, Goodman, Rorty, Putnam, Bernstein, Brandom) | 11, 29-36, 101, 124, 135-137 | Brandom's inferentialism and Quine's technical papers add no distinct practice lever beyond 29-36 |
| Continental/scientific resonances (Simondon, Deleuze, Prigogine/Stengers, Latour, Barad, Bennett, Ingold) | 59, 101, 145-152 | Remaining material is ontological argument without a distinct practice instruction |
| Science of the flowing mind (Varela/Thompson/Rosch, Juarrero, Deacon, 4E) | 77, 139-144, 150-151 | Enactivist research programs reduce to 139-144; 4E mental health applications are operationalized in Book IV |
| Applied flourishing (Rogers, Maslow, Csikszentmihalyi, Seligman, Hayes) | 13, 14, 61, 63, 115-120, 155-180 | Each thinker's practice content fully absorbed; measurement and protocol detail deferred to Book IV |
| The 2020s wave (Nooteboom, Palgrave series, pragmatism renaissance) | 103, and the framing of the whole | Current work confirms rather than extends the principle set |

Excluded by scope: purely technical metaphysics without a practice lever; theological process doctrine (retained only in secular form); Schiller's and Brandom's contributions where they duplicate James and Peirce in less workable form; and historical commentary with no normative content.
