The ground of the Kyoto School: experience before the split into subject and object, and the Tibetan tradition's basic map of the mind that appears within experience.

- [[K002 — Know that the split is made, not found]]
- [[K003 — Act from unity; reflect from unity]]
- [[K004 — Take the mind itself as the laboratory]]
- [[K005 — See that all fear and suffering are mind-made at the root]]
- [[K006 — Guard the mind as the root of everything downstream]]
- [[K007 — Learn the mind's two registers — appearance and awareness]]
