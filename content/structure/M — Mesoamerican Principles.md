### Corpus

Nahua: the Florentine Codex, Book VI (the Huehuetlatolli, the "words of the elders"); the Cantares Mexicanos and the poems attributed to Nezahualcoyotl and the lords of Tetzcoco; the tlamatinime teachings as reconstructed in Maffie, Aztec Philosophy (2014), and Leon-Portilla, Aztec Thought and Culture (1963).

Maya: the Popol Vuh (Dennis Tedlock's translation, with his notes); Barbara Tedlock, Time and the Highland Maya (the daykeeping ethnography); the modern Maya-identified formulations as labeled.

Shared substrate: the calendar tradition (the 260-day count and its daykeepers), the milpa (corn-field) cycle as a relational practice, the communal work tradition, and the New Fire renewal.
