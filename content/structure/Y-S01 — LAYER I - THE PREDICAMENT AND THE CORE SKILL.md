The axioms. Everything below depends on these: the diagnosis of where suffering begins, the master skill that treats it, and the twin method of steady practice and released grip.


## Principles

- [[Y001 — See experience as constructed before reacting to it]]
- [[Y002 — Settle the mind rather than suppress it]]
- [[Y003 — Recognize misperception as the root error]]
- [[Y004 — Make discrimination your master skill]]
- [[Y005 — Practice steadiness and let go of grip together]]
- [[Y006 — Understand dispassion as release, not numbness]]
- [[Y007 — Grade your effort honestly]]
- [[Y008 — Measure progress by stability, not intensity of experience]]
- [[Y009 — Treat identity as a working hypothesis]]
- [[Y010 — Locate the problem in misidentification, not in weakness]]
- [[Y011 — Prevent suffering before it arrives]]
- [[Y012 — Read the fragility of chasing as signal]]
- [[Y013 — Work with the field and the witness]]
- [[Y014 — Hold the metaphysics lightly]]
