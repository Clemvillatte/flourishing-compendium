These principles cover self-growth: self-efficacy, self-development, and self-actualization.


## Principles

- [[SE059 — Cultivate self-efficacy — belief in your capacity to act]]
- [[SE060 — Learn mastery experiences — build self-efficacy through doing]]
- [[SE061 — Recognise self-efficacy is domain-specific — not general confidence]]
- [[SE062 — Cultivate growth orientation — see self as developable]]
- [[SE063 — Learn to set and pursue self-concordant goals — goals aligned with values]]
- [[SE064 — Recognise intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation — intrinsic predicts well-being]]
- [[SE065 — Learn self-actualization — become who you are capable of being]]
- [[SE066 — Recognise the role of challenge in growth — difficulty develops self]]
- [[SE067 — Learn to use self-monitoring and feedback — track your growth]]
- [[SE068 — Recognise the limits of self-development — not everything is fixable]]
