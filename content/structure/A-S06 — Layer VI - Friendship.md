Aristotle's treatment of friendship (philia) is the longest sustained discussion in the Ethics. He considers it necessary for flourishing and the context in which most virtue is exercised.

- [[A066 — Be a friend to yourself first]]
- [[A067 — Practise equality in friendship]]
- [[A068 — Maintain friendships through presence in misfortune]]
- [[A069 — Seek concord (homonoia)]]
- [[A070 — Understand the asymmetry of benefaction]]
- [[A071 — Recognise that self-love is the foundation]]
- [[A072 — Practise living together (suzēn)]]
- [[A073 — Distinguish friendship from partnership]]
- [[A074 — Be generous in interpreting friends' actions]]
- [[A075 — Recognise that friendships change over time]]
- [[A076 — Practise friendship across difference]]
- [[A077 — Honour friendship in prosperity as well as adversity]]
- [[A078 — Recognise that the best friendship is between the good]]
