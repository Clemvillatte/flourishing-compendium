These are the axioms: understanding how thinking actually works, and what it can and cannot do. Every downstream principle assumes this.


## Principles

- [[CO001 — Understand that cognition is predictive, not passive]]
- [[CO002 — Recognise the two systems (or modes) of thinking — fast intuition and slow analysis]]
- [[CO003 — Know that thinking has limited capacity — cognitive load is real]]
- [[CO004 — Recognise that thinking serves action, not truth alone]]
- [[CO005 — Understand that thinking is embodied — the body shapes the mind]]
- [[CO006 — Recognise that thinking is socially distributed — you think with others]]
- [[CO007 — Know that thinking is trainable — cognitive capacities can be built]]
- [[CO008 — Understand that thinking is constructivist — you build your understanding]]
- [[CO009 — Recognise that thinking has a default mode — mind-wandering]]
- [[CO010 — Understand that thinking is narrative — you story your experience]]
- [[CO011 — Recognise that thinking can be fused with its content — you can believe your thoughts]]
- [[CO012 — Know that thinking is both individual and collective — schemas are shared]]
- [[CO013 — Recognise that thinking has a direction — it can be expansive or contracted]]
- [[CO014 — Understand that thinking is fallible and that wisdom includes knowing this]]
