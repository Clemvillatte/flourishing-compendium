### Corpus

Yoga Sutra of Patanjali (YS), all four padas, with the Yoga-bhashya attributed to Vyasa.

Bhagavad Gita (BG), all eighteen chapters.

Principal Upanishads: Isha, Kena, Katha, Prashna, Mundaka, Mandukya, Taittiriya, Aitareya, Chandogya, Brihadaranyaka.

Shankara, Upadesasahasri (securely attributed); Vivekachudamani, Atma Bodha, Drig-Drisya-Viveka (attributed, authorship debated).

Vivekananda, Raja Yoga (1896) and Karma Yoga (1896), as modern adaptations.
