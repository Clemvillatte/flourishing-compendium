These principles cover specific cognitive challenges (decision-making under uncertainty, creative thinking, moral reasoning) and the boundary conditions where self-directed practice is insufficient.


## Principles

- [[CO073 — Learn to make decisions under uncertainty — you will never have complete information]]
- [[CO074 — Cultivate creative thinking — divergent and convergent modes]]
- [[CO075 — Recognise moral cognition — intuition comes first, reasoning second]]
- [[CO076 — Learn to recognise cognitive dissonance — inconsistency is uncomfortable and drives change]]
- [[CO077 — Recognise the limits of introspection — you do not have privileged access to your own mind]]
- [[CO078 — Understand the role of language in thinking — words shape thought]]
- [[CO079 — Recognise the impact of cognitive ageing — some capacities decline, others improve]]
- [[CO080 — Recognise neurodivergent cognition — different, not disordered]]
- [[CO081 — Recognise cognitive symptoms of clinical conditions — depression and anxiety affect thinking]]
- [[CO082 — Build a cognitive-maintenance system — learn, adapt, retain]]
