Representative state-of-the-art sources behind the psychology parallels. Parallels are bridges, not proof; individual findings vary in strength, and several are explicitly flagged in-text as mixed or emerging.

- Experiential acceptance and psychological flexibility: [ACT outcome evidence](https://contextualscience.org/article/effects_workplace_acceptance_commitment_therapy_act_programs_psychological_flexibilitys) and [flexibility as a core process](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.2147/PRBM.S596070)
- Mindfulness mechanisms and decentering: [Hölzel et al. (2011)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3124168/); [Bernstein et al. (2015)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4645085/); [mindfulness meta-analysis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00223980.2025.2575309)
- Slow breathing and vagal tone: Balban et al. (2023); Zaccaro et al. (2018)
- Craving as a learned loop and urge work: [Brewer (2013)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3748979/); [Bowen et al. (2011)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3284634/)
- Loving-kindness and positive affect: [Fredrickson et al. (2008)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2603570/); [Hofmann et al. (2011)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3152767/)
- Compassion training and empathic distress: [Klimecki et al. (2013)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3757644/); [Singer and Klimecki (2014)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4183915/); [equanimity and contemplative training](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4350240/)
- Nonattachment: [Sahdra et al. (2010)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3468438/); [nonattachment review](https://non-attachment.com/downloads/ciarrochi%20et%20al%20nonattachment-review.pdf)
- Rumination and mind-wandering: [Nolen-Hoeksema (2000)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3177306/); [Killingsworth and Gilbert (2010)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3086756/)
- Nondual awareness: [Josipovic (2014)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4042691/); ego dissolution and self-transcendence: Letheby and Griffiths (2017); Yaden et al. (2017)
- Affect labeling: [Lieberman et al. (2007)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1868416/)
- Gratitude: [Emmons and McCullough (2003)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2756833/)
- Prosocial spending and helping: [Dunn, Aknin and Norton (2008)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2647163/); Post (2005)
- Forgiveness: [Worthington (2001)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2945983/); revenge: [Carlsmith et al. (2008)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2603199/)
- Chronic pain acceptance: [McCracken (1998)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1360255/); [Vowles et al. (2014)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4138373/)
- Mindful everyday activities: [Hanley et al. (2015)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4576680/); walking and creativity: [Oppezzo and Schwartz (2014)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3993970/)
- Habit and repetition: Lally et al. (2010); [Wood and Neal (2007)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1955486/)
- Implementation intentions: Gollwitzer (1999)
- Death reflection: [Cozzolino (2006)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3177306/); stress mindset: [Crum et al. (2013)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5056693/)
- Grief: dual-process model (Stroebe and Schut, 1999); resilience (Bonanno, 2004)
- Well-being trends and income: [Killingsworth, Kahneman and Mellers (2023)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41340495/)
