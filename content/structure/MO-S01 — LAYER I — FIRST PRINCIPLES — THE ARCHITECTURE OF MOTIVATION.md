These are the axioms: understanding how motivation works, what generates it, and what it can and cannot do. Every downstream principle assumes this.


## Principles

- [[MO001 — Understand that motivation is a system, not a feeling]]
- [[MO002 — Know that motivation serves three basic psychological needs — autonomy, competence, and relatedness]]
- [[MO003 — Recognise the difference between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation — quality matters]]
- [[MO004 — Understand that motivation is generated by expectancy and value]]
- [[MO005 — Recognise that motivation is temporal — delay reduces motivation]]
- [[MO006 — Understand that wanting and liking are different systems]]
- [[MO007 — Recognise that motivation is influenced by reward prediction error — surprise drives learning]]
- [[MO008 — Know that motivation is trainable — it can be built and shaped]]
- [[MO009 — Recognise that motivation is social — it is influenced by others]]
- [[MO010 — Understand that motivation has a physiological substrate — energy and health matter]]
- [[MO011 — Recognise that motivation can be autonomous or controlled — the source matters]]
- [[MO012 — Understand that motivation is a resource to be managed, not just summoned]]
