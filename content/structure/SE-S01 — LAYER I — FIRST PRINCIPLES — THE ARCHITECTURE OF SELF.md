These are the axioms: understanding what the self is, how it forms, and what it can and cannot do. Every downstream principle assumes this.


## Principles

- [[SE001 — Understand that the self is constructed, not discovered]]
- [[SE002 — Recognise the observing self — you are not only the contents of awareness]]
- [[SE003 — Know that the self is multilayered — actual, ideal, and ought selves]]
- [[SE004 — Understand that self-esteem is not the same as self-worth]]
- [[SE005 — Recognise that the self is partly social — it is formed in relation]]
- [[SE006 — Know that the self is embodied — the body is part of who you are]]
- [[SE007 — Understand that the self has temporal depth — past, present, and future selves]]
- [[SE008 — Recognise that the self is not unitary — you have multiple self-aspects]]
- [[SE009 — Know that self-knowledge is limited — introspection is fallible]]
- [[SE010 — Understand that the self is a process, not a thing]]
- [[SE011 — Recognise the narrative structure of self — you are a story]]
- [[SE012 — Understand that the self is not the enemy — self-relation is a skill]]
