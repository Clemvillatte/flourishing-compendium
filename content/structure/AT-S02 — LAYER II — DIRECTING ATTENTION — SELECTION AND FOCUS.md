These principles cover how to direct attention: what to select, how to filter, and how to focus.


## Principles

- [[AT013 — Learn to set attentional intentions — decide what to attend to in advance]]
- [[AT014 — Recognise and reduce attentional capture by salient distractors]]
- [[AT015 — Learn to filter — not all information deserves attention]]
- [[AT016 — Cultivate single-tasking — do one thing at a time]]
- [[AT017 — Learn to use attention as a spotlight — narrow for focus, broad for context]]
- [[AT018 — Practise deliberate distraction management — know what pulls your attention]]
- [[AT019 — Learn to redirect attention after distraction — the return, not the absence of distraction, is the skill]]
- [[AT020 — Recognise attentional bias — anxiety and depression distort what you notice]]
- [[AT021 — Learn to attend to the positive deliberately — attention training for well-being]]
- [[AT022 — Recognise the spotlight effect — you attend to what you think others notice about you]]
- [[AT023 — Learn to attend to process, not just outcome]]
- [[AT024 — Cultivate attentional presence — fully attend to what you are doing]]
