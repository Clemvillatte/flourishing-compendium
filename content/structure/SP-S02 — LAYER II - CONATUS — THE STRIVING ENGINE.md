The affect theory: joy and sadness as transitions of power, desire as conscious striving, and the elementary social affects.


## Principles

- [[SP011 — Read joy and sadness as power signals]]
- [[SP012 — Name your desire precisely]]
- [[SP013 — Understand love and hate as attached affects]]
- [[SP014 — Expect to imagine others as like yourself]]
- [[SP015 — Guard against affect contagion]]
- [[SP016 — Watch the desires you copy]]
- [[SP017 — Treat hope and fear as one system]]
- [[SP018 — Calibrate confidence against evidence]]
- [[SP019 — Diagnose ambition and the hunger for esteem]]
- [[SP020 — Convert pity into compassion and equity]]
- [[SP021 — Take envy seriously as hatred]]
- [[SP022 — Practice gratitude as repayment of love]]
- [[SP023 — Separate self-esteem from vanity and self-abasement]]
- [[SP024 — Expect fluctuation, and read it as structure]]
- [[SP025 — Treat greed, luxury, and drunkenness as species of madness]]
- [[SP026 — Study the drunkard, and keep your seat]]
- [[SP027 — Drop the illusion of free will]]
- [[SP028 — See decisions as victories of the strongest appetite]]
