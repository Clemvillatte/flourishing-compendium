These are the axioms: reading feelings correctly, and knowing what they do and do not tell you. Every downstream principle assumes this.


## Principles

- [[AF001 — Treat affect as information, not as verdict]]
- [[AF002 — Distinguish the feeling from its interpretation]]
- [[AF003 — Learn the structure of your affect — valence and arousal]]
- [[AF004 — Cultivate emotional granularity — name feelings precisely]]
- [[AF005 — Learn to read your own body — interoception]]
- [[AF006 — Accept mixed feelings; do not force them to resolve]]
- [[AF007 — Recognise affective forecasting errors; expect hedonic adaptation]]
- [[AF008 — Know the negativity bias; correct for it deliberately]]
- [[AF009 — Understand that affect is constructed, not discovered]]
- [[AF010 — Recognise that affect has temporal structure — intensity, duration, variability, recovery]]
- [[AF011 — Know that affect has a biological substrate; do not moralise it]]
- [[AF012 — Recognise that affect is socially and culturally shaped; do not universalise it]]
