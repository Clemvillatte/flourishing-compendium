## About this volume

# The Principles of Genuine Flourishing — Volume III

# Stories of Genuine Flourishing

This volume is the storytelling companion to the principle volumes. 

Where the earlier books set out the principles of genuine flourishing in numbered, argued form, this one gathers the narratives: parables, biographies, films, novels, myths, historical episodes, and scientific stories in which those principles are lived rather than stated. The aim is not to write the stories out in full, but to index and contextualise them. Each entry gives you the narrative arc, what it shows, and where to go to experience the original.

**How to read an entry.** Every entry follows the same shape. A meta line identifies the kind of story, where and when it comes from, and links a source where the story can be read, watched, or verified. Two short paragraphs tell the story and draw out its significance. A closing line names the principles it illustrates (by their Volume II identifiers) and the cross-tradition principles it resonates with.

**The identifiers.** D marks Daoist principles, B Buddhist, P Pragmatist and Process, A Aristotelian, S Stoic, R Relational, E Existential and Phenomenological, and PP Positive Psychology and wellbeing science. The numbers refer to the principle lists in Volume II, *The Principles of Genuine Flourishing II*. "Resonates" entries are looser echoes across traditions rather than direct mappings.

**Organisation.** Book I collects the stories, organised in eight Parts by tradition, each Part subdivided into the Layers used throughout the project. Book II, on comparative and narrative devices (analogy, metaphor, allegory, parable, simile), follows separately. Layer headings are numbered so that each one has a unique anchor: I.1 is Part I, Layer 1, and so on.
