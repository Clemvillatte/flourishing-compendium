### Corpus

| Source | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Nicomachean Ethics I-X | Eudaimonia, function argument, virtue as hexis, doctrine of the mean, specific virtues, phronesis, akrasia, friendship, pleasure, contemplation, education |
| Eudemian Ethics | Complementary virtue theory, kalokagathia, continuity of the ethical life, fortune and nobility |
| Politics I-VIII | The polis as condition of flourishing, education, distributive and corrective justice, citizenship, household management, leisure |
| Rhetoric I-III | Emotion theory, practical reasoning in public life, ethos, pathos, civic judgment |
| De Anima | Functional psychology, perception and desire, the unity of soul and body |
| Magna Moralia | Practical extensions of virtue theory, friendship taxonomy, self-love |
| Poetics | Catharsis, narrative structure, the function of tragedy in moral life |

The Nicomachean Ethics is the classical core; the other works are weighted for their distinctive practice content.
