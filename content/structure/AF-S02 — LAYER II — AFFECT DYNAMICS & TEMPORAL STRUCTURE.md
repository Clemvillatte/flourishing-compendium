These principles govern how affect unfolds over time: how to recover, how to avoid getting stuck, and how to use variability well.


## Principles

- [[AF013 — Train emotional recovery — the ability to return to baseline]]
- [[AF014 — Recognise emotional inertia — the difference between feeling and getting stuck]]
- [[AF015 — Do not fear emotional variability; fear its absence]]
- [[AF016 — Cultivate emodiversity — the range and evenness of your emotional life]]
- [[AF017 — Recognise that positive and negative affect are not opposite ends of one axis]]
- [[AF018 — Understand that affect is regulated by reward prediction error, not by outcomes alone]]
- [[AF019 — Recognise the paradox of hedonic pursuit — chasing positive affect can reduce it]]
- [[AF020 — Distinguish pleasure from satisfaction; they are different systems]]
- [[AF021 — Understand affective presence — your feelings influence others' feelings]]
- [[AF022 — Recognise affective contagion in groups; do not mistake it for your own feeling]]
- [[AF023 — Know that affect changes memory; do not trust emotional memory as a record]]
- [[AF024 — Recognise affect's relationship to motivation; use it, do not obey it]]
