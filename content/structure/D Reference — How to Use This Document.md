- Read top-down for understanding; there is no required order of practice — start anywhere that stings.
- Pick one principle per week; the numbering is by leverage, not by difficulty.
- Treat the parallels as flashlights, not as proof: a psychological construct or a philosophical concept illuminates a principle's mechanism without exhausting it.
- Treat the list as a mirror, not a checklist: the principle you skip is often the one asking for attention.
- Remember the meta-principle implicit in all 192: stop adding. The list's own advice is to need less of it.
