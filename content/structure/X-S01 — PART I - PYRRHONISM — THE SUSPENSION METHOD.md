The method of the ancient skeptics, as systematized by Sextus Empiricus: the deliberate production of suspension (epochē) by balanced argument, and the quiet that follows. What is distinct here, against every book in this compendium: the other traditions train a stance (acceptance, understanding, awareness); Pyrrhonism trains the deliberate equalizing of opposed stances, and treats the resulting non-assertion as the practice itself.
