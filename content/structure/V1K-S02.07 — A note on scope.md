This list is comprehensive for the purpose of operationalising the 570 principles in Books I through III. It is not a claim to exhaust all of psychological science. The kernels draw from process-based therapy, acceptance and commitment therapy, prevention science, contemplative science, behavioral activation, emotion regulation research, positive psychology, and the well-being literature. These are science-backed and PBT-compatible kernels; evidence strength varies by kernel. Some rest on decades of experimental and meta-analytic support; others are tradition-derived operational variants with adjacent evidence from related processes. The criterion for inclusion is not that every kernel has a direct RCT behind it, but that each is a small, teachable, repeatable unit consistent with the processes of change that the PBT/EEMM framework organises.
