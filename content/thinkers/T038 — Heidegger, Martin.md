Martin Heidegger (born 26 September 1889, Messkirch; died 26 May 1976, Freiburg) reopened the oldest question in philosophy, the question of being, by asking what kind of being the asker is. He named that being Dasein, and in his words "the word 'Dasein' does not designate a way of being at all, but rather a particular entity, that we are ourselves, the human Dasein. We are each respectively a Dasein" ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/), GA24: 36). His record is stained: he joined the Nazi Party in 1933 and, as the [SEP entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/) documents, never publicly renounced his support.

## Personal story / context

Heidegger grew up in a small German town where his father was a master cooper and church sexton; the entry credits his thought to that provincial craft life ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). He entered Freiburg in 1909 for Catholic theology before turning to philosophy, took his doctorate in 1913, habilitated under Heinrich Rickert in 1915, and became Edmund Husserl's assistant in 1919 ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). *Being and Time* appeared in 1927, dedicated to Husserl "in friendship and admiration" ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). At Marburg his students included Gadamer, Löwith, Hans Jonas and Hannah Arendt, with whom he had a complicated love affair stretching from 1925 until her death six months before his ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). Elected Rector of Freiburg in April 1933, he joined the Nazi Party on 1 May, oversaw the university's alignment with party aims, resigned the rectorship in 1934 but kept his membership; after the war the French authorities barred him from teaching until 1949 ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). His antisemitism is, in the entry's words, "now well-documented" ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)).

## Their why

The question that drives him is why being itself fell out of Western philosophy's view, leaving only beings to study. His route back was the being who asks: human existence, whose every action carries an understanding of being. Philosophy had to be rebuilt from the everyday, from tools and moods and mortality, rather than from detached theory.

## Their position

Dasein is being-in-the-world, a unitary structure in which world, self and involvement cannot be separated; the world is "that 'wherein' a factical Dasein as such 'lives'" (SZ 65) ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). Everyday Dasein lives as *das Man*, the anyone-self, disburdened of choice by social norms. Authenticity is owning that situation: Heidegger calls it "choosing to choose to be a self" (SZ 270) ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). The door to it runs through death, "the possibility of the absolute impossibility of Dasein" (SZ 250); its anticipation "individualizes Dasein down to itself" (SZ 263) ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). Later he turned to technology, which orders everything as resource, and to dwelling: "To be a human being" means "to dwell" (GA7: 149), poetically, within the fourfold of earth, sky, mortals and divinities ([SEP, Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)).

## Core ideas

- **Dasein and being-in-the-world** — the human being as the entity that understands being, always already involved. See [[Dasein]], [[Being-in-the-world]].
- **Das Man and authenticity** — the conformist anyone-self, and the owned self that chooses to choose. See [[The they (das Man)]], [[Authenticity]].
- **Being-toward-death** — death as one's ownmost possibility, the individualizer. See [[Being-toward-death]], [[Finitude]].
- **Care and thrownness** — Dasein as thrown project, always ahead of itself in a world it must look after. See [[Sorge]], [[Thrownness]], [[Projection (Entwurf)]].
- **Technology and dwelling** — enframing as the danger, poetic dwelling and the fourfold as the counter. See [[The enframing (Gestell)]], [[Dwelling]], [[Gelassenheit]].
