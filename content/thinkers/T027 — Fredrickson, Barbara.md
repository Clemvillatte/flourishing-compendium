Barbara Lee Fredrickson (born June 15, 1964) is the Kenan Distinguished Professor of Psychology at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and principal investigator of its Positive Emotions and Psychophysiology Laboratory ([Wikipedia, Barbara Fredrickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Fredrickson)). She built the broaden-and-build account of positive emotions, which holds that "A key proposition is that these positive emotions broaden an individual's momentary thought-action repertoire" ([Fredrickson, Phil Trans R Soc B 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1693418/)).

## Personal story / context

Fredrickson's 1990 doctoral thesis studied anticipated endings in social interaction, supervised by Laura L. Carstensen ([Wikipedia, Barbara Fredrickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Fredrickson)). Her early publications include objectification theory, written with Tomi-Ann Roberts in 1997, on how the cultural gaze shapes women's lived experiences ([Wikipedia, Barbara Fredrickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Fredrickson)). The question that changed her direction was the title of her 1998 paper: "What good are positive emotions?" ([Wikipedia, Barbara Fredrickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Fredrickson)). She was there at the field's founding: at the first Akumal Conference in 1999, "in the living room of a Mexican villa said to have been once owned by a band member of the Grateful Dead... Fredrickson made one of her initial presentations of the broaden-and-build theory" ([Fredrickson & Joiner, 2019 reflections](https://peplab.web.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/18901/2019/06/FredricksonJoiner2019PPSupwardspirals.pdf)). Two years earlier she had received the APA's inaugural Templeton Prize in Positive Psychology ([Wikipedia, Barbara Fredrickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Fredrickson)).

## Their why

The theory answers a puzzle: why do people feel good at all? Fear and anger have obvious jobs: escape and attack. Fredrickson's answer is that positive emotions have a job too: they widen what a person notices and tries, and the widening accumulates over time. As she and Thomas Joiner put it, "beyond making people feel good in the present moment, positive emotions also appear to increase the odds – through dynamic broaden-and-build processes – that people will feel good in the future" ([Fredrickson & Joiner, 2019 reflections](https://peplab.web.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/18901/2019/06/FredricksonJoiner2019PPSupwardspirals.pdf)).

## Their position

Broaden-and-build has two moving parts. Positive emotions first widen the momentary repertoire of thought and action: "joy sparks the urge to play, interest sparks the urge to explore, contentment sparks the urge to savour and integrate, and love sparks a recurring cycle of each of these urges within safe, close relationships" ([Fredrickson, Phil Trans R Soc B 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1693418/)). The widened repertoire then builds durable resources, in her words "ranging from physical and intellectual resources, to social and psychological resources," and "these resources function as reserves that can be drawn on later to improve the odds of successful coping and survival" ([Fredrickson, Phil Trans R Soc B 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1693418/)). She is candid about evidence, writing of the 2002 upward-spiral findings: "A clear weakness, however, was that it only offered prospective correlations. No causal claims were appropriate at that time" ([Fredrickson & Joiner, 2019 reflections](https://peplab.web.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/18901/2019/06/FredricksonJoiner2019PPSupwardspirals.pdf)). The same candor marked the 2005 positivity-ratio paper with Marcial Losada: critics showed its nonlinear-dynamics mathematics to be without foundation, Fredrickson agreed the modeling was "questionable", and the paper carries a 2013 correction, though she stood by the general idea that a high positivity-to-negativity ratio helps ([Wikipedia, Barbara Fredrickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Fredrickson)).

## Core ideas

- **Broaden-and-build** — positive emotions widen the momentary thought-action repertoire and thereby build enduring personal resources. See [[Broaden-and-build]].
- **Upward spirals** — everyday positive emotions and broad-minded coping feed each other over time, so well-being compounds. See [[Positive emotions research]].
- **Positivity portfolios** — her practical method of assembling reminders of past positive feelings to cultivate them on purpose. See [[Fredrickson's positivity portfolios]].
- **Love as micro-moments of connection** — in her later work, love is a physiological synchrony shared in brief moments between people, not a lasting bond alone. See [[Micro-moments of connection]], [[Metta (loving-kindness)]], [[Savoring]].
- **Objectification theory** — her earlier work with Tomi-Ann Roberts on how cultural gaze shapes women's mental health. See [[Objectification Theory]].
