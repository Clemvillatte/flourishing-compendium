Emmanuel Levinas (born 12 January 1905, Kaunas, Lithuania; died 25 December 1995, Paris) was the philosopher who put the other person first: ethics, he argued, is first philosophy, arising from the face-to-face encounter before any theory ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). His name for what the encounter carries: "Naked and defenseless, the face signifies, with or without words, 'Do not kill me'" ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)).

## Personal story / context

Levinas went from Lithuania to study philosophy at Strasbourg in 1923, then to Freiburg in 1928-29 to study with Husserl and attend Heidegger's seminar; his 1930 French thesis was the first book-length introduction to Husserl in French ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). He enlisted in the French officer corps in 1939, was captured in 1940 and held in the Fallingbostel labor camp; his Lithuanian family was murdered, while his wife Raïssa and daughter Simone were hidden by nuns in Orléans ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). After the war he directed the École Normale Israélite Orientale in Paris and delivered twenty-three Talmudic readings for the Colloque des Intellectuels Juifs de Langue française, the first in 1957 ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). Professorships followed at Poitiers (1961), Nanterre (1967) and the Sorbonne (1973) ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). His major works are *Totality and Infinity* (1961) and *Otherwise than Being* (1974) ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)).

## Their why

Levinas watched the century's catastrophes and concluded that Western thought had made the same mistake twice: it put being, knowledge and the state before the human being who stands in front of you. His counter-move was to start from the encounter itself, where another person's presence addresses and commands before any concept arrives. The critique lands on his own teacher's tradition: Heidegger's account, he held, underestimates what it means to encounter another person ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)).

## Their position

The face-to-face is where ethics begins: the other's expression interrupts the subject's free activity and calls the subject to account ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). Responsibility is not a decision: "Above all, we do *not choose* to be responsible. Responsibility arises as if elicited, before we begin to think about it, by the approach of the other person" ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). The self answers with "here I am," and inwardness itself takes the form of being-for-another: "All my inwardness is invested [*s'investit*] in the form of a despite-me, for-another" ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). The later work radicalizes this into substitution, the one-for-the-other ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)). The third party then brings justice: institutions, courts and comparison enter when more than two people are present ([SEP, Emmanuel Levinas](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/)).

## Core ideas

- **The face of the Other** — the encounter that commands before it is understood. See [[The face]], [[R083 — Practise the face of the Other|Practise the face of the Other]].
- **Ethics before ontology** — responsibility preceding theory of being. See [[The primacy of the ethical]], [[R093 — Practise the primacy of ethics over ontology|Practise the primacy of ethics over ontology]].
- **Infinite responsibility** — responsibility without contract or limit, elicited rather than chosen. See [[Infinite responsibility]], [[Responsibility]].
- **Substitution** — the self as hostage, one-for-the-other. See [[Substitution]].
- **Alterity and the third** — the other's transcendence, and justice when the third arrives. See [[Alterity]], [[A097 — Practise corrective justice|Practise corrective justice]].
