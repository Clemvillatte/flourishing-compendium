Karl Jaspers (born 23 February 1883, Oldenburg; died 26 February 1969, Basel) started out as a psychiatrist, moved through psychology to philosophy, and became the philosopher of the situations a person cannot escape. His own list of them reads: "I must die, I must suffer, I must struggle, I am subject to chance, I involve myself inexorably in guilt" ([Jaspers, *Way to Wisdom*, trans. Manheim, pp. 19-20](https://archive.org/stream/waytowisdomintro00jasp/waytowisdomintro00jasp_djvu.txt)).

## Personal story / context

Jaspers studied law briefly at Heidelberg, then medicine in Berlin, Göttingen and Heidelberg, took his M.D. in 1909 and worked in the Heidelberg psychiatric clinic ([SEP, Karl Jaspers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/jaspers/)). *General Psychopathology* (1913) made his name and carries his clinical creed: "in psychopathology it is dangerous merely to learn the matter, our task is not to 'learn psychopathology' but to learn to observe, ask questions, analyze, and think in psychopathological terms" ([SEP, Karl Jaspers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/jaspers/)). He took the chair of philosophy at Heidelberg in 1922 and published his three-volume *Philosophie* in 1932 ([SEP, Karl Jaspers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/jaspers/)). The Nazi regime dismissed him in 1937 and banned his publications in 1938; his wife Gertrud came from a German-Jewish family, and only her marriage to him allowed her to remain in Germany ([SEP, Karl Jaspers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/jaspers/)). After the war he wrote *The Question of German Guilt* (1946), moved to Basel in 1948 and became a Swiss citizen ([SEP, Karl Jaspers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/jaspers/)).

## Their why

Medicine taught him that science maps everything except the one doing the mapping. The human being who chooses, suffers and feels guilty never shows up as an object in any science. Jaspers wanted philosophy to be the path on which this non-object, the self he called Existenz, becomes real to itself. The door is what ordinary life tries to forget: "Along with wonder and doubt, awareness of these ultimate situations is the most profound source of philosophy" ([Jaspers, *Way to Wisdom*, p. 20](https://archive.org/stream/waytowisdomintro00jasp/waytowisdomintro00jasp_djvu.txt)).

## Their position

The limit situations cannot be solved, only acknowledged. His description of what they do: "to ultimate situations we react either by obfuscation or, if we really apprehend them, by despair and rebirth: we become ourselves by a change in our consciousness of being" ([Jaspers, *Way to Wisdom*, p. 20](https://archive.org/stream/waytowisdomintro00jasp/waytowisdomintro00jasp_djvu.txt)). Behind all objects stands what he called the Comprehensive: "being as a whole is neither subject nor object but must be the Comprehensive, which is manifested in this dichotomy," and "The Comprehensive does not itself become an object but is manifested in the dichotomy of I and object" ([Jaspers, *Way to Wisdom*, pp. 30-31](https://archive.org/stream/waytowisdomintro00jasp/waytowisdomintro00jasp_djvu.txt)). Transcendence, if it shows at all, shows in ciphers, never in doctrines; hence his "philosophical faith," which keeps reason and openness together and refuses final answers ([SEP, Karl Jaspers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/jaspers/)). In *The Origin and Goal of History* (1949) he named the Axial Age, the span around 800-200 BCE in which Confucius, the Buddha, the Hebrew prophets and the Greek philosophers appeared at once ([SEP, Karl Jaspers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/jaspers/)).

## Core ideas

- **Limit situations (Grenzsituationen)** — death, suffering, struggle, chance and guilt as the unchangeable wall that wakes the self. See [[Limit situations (Grenzsituationen)]], [[Boundary situations]], [[Finitude]].
- **Existenz** — the self that is never an object, realized only in freedom and decision. See [[Existenz]], [[Choice of self]], [[Decision]].
- **Existential communication** — the self becomes real only in dialogue with another self. See [[Existential communication]], [[Dialogue]].
- **The Comprehensive (das Umgreifende)** — being as a whole, which never becomes an object. See [[The encompassing (das Umgreifende)]], [[Transcendence]].
- **Ciphers of transcendence** — signs in nature, art and myth that point beyond without proving. See [[Ciphers of transcendence]].
- **Shipwreck (Scheitern)** — failure as the moment the mask drops. See [[Shipwreck (Scheitern)]], [[Breakdown as breakthrough]].
- **The Axial Age** — the simultaneous birth of the great traditions in one historical window. See [[The Axial Age]].
- **Philosophy as questioning** — philosophizing as an activity of the whole person, not a body of results. See [[Philosophy as questioning]].
