> **Thinker T016** — Steven C. Hayes (born 1948), American clinical psychologist, Nevada Foundation Professor at the University of Nevada, Reno; co-developer of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, developer of Relational Frame Theory, and a principal architect of process-based therapy and contextual behavioral science.

## Personal story / context

Hayes received his B.A. in psychology from Loyola Marymount University and his M.A. and Ph.D. in clinical psychology from West Virginia University, then taught at the University of North Carolina at Greensboro before moving to the University of Nevada, Reno in 1986, where he is Nevada Foundation Professor in the Department of Psychology ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_C._Hayes)).

## Their why

Hayes's psychotherapy program begins from a philosophical position rather than a clinical one: functional contextualism, "a type of psychological pragmatism that extends Skinner's radical behaviorism" ([Hayes, Pistorello and Levin 2012, p. 979](https://www.apa.org/education-career/ce/acceptance-commitment.pdf)). The Behavior Therapy review that frames the model puts the analytic goal as "the prediction-and-influence of psychological events" (same source). Therapy, on this view, is not about correcting the content of thought; it is about changing the workability of behaviour relative to a stated goal.

## Their position

ACT, the clinical application, is built around psychological flexibility rather than symptom removal. The Behavior Therapy review that frames it puts the goal as "the prediction-and-influence of psychological events" and treats chosen values as the necessary precursor to assessing workability. RFT, the research program under the clinical one, gives an account of human language and higher cognition as learned, arbitrarily applicable relational responding. Hayes is also a co-developer of process-based therapy, an idiographic approach that combines insights from evolution theory and complex network theory to target the processes underlying effective treatments ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_C._Hayes)).

## Core ideas

- **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy** — acceptance, defusion, present moment, self-as-context, values, committed action. See [[Acceptance and Commitment Therapy]], [[Acceptance alongside values-based action]].
- **Relational Frame Theory** — language and cognition as relational operants. See [[Relational Frame Theory]].
- **Psychological flexibility** — the six-process ACT model. See [[Psychological flexibility]].
- **Workability** — a pragmatic truth criterion; "truth" is a matter of "successful working". See [[Workability]], [[Cash value]].
- **Functional contextualism** — the philosophical position. See [[Functional contextualism]], [[Context sensitivity]].
- **Process-based therapy / CBS** — the idiographic, process-targeting successor framework. See [[Process-based therapy]], [[Extended Evolutionary Meta-Model]], [[Variation-selection-retention]].
