Gestalt perception is Max Wertheimer's account of how relations are perceived directly: the visual field arrives already organized into wholes, not as a heap of sensations to be assembled. The 1923 paper that founded the movement opens at a window.

> "I stand at the window and see a house, trees, sky. Theoretically I might say there were 327 brightnesses and nuances of colour. Do I have '327'? No. I have sky, house, and trees. It is impossible to achieve '327' as such." (Wertheimer, [Laws of Organization in Perceptual Forms](http://psychclassics.yorku.ca/Wertheimer/Forms/forms), 1923, Ellis translation)

### The laws of organization

The paper's question is whether the arrangements and divisions of experience follow definite principles, and it answers with the grouping factors: proximity, in which the form of grouping involving the smallest interval is most natural, and similarity, the tendency of like parts to band together, among others. When we are presented with a number of stimuli we do not as a rule experience a number of individual things; instead larger wholes separated from and related to one another are given in experience, their arrangement and division concrete and definite.
