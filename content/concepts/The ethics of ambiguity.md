Beauvoir's title for her existentialist ethics: freedom is ambiguous because it must will both itself and the freedom of others. To will one without the other is bad faith ([Wikipedia Simone de Beauvoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_de_Beauvoir)).
