The Aspiration Index assesses people's life goals ([Self-Determination Theory site](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/aspirations-index/)). Ryan and Deci summarize research in which greater relative emphasis on extrinsic aspirations was associated with poorer scores on the well-being indicators examined ([Ryan & Deci 2000, p. 75](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf)). The theory's own site describes the construct and the measure:

> "Aspirations refer to people’s life goals, and SDT research on aspirations has focused on the relative strength of intrinsic aspirations (viz., meaningful relationships, personal growth, and community contributions) versus extrinsic aspirations (viz., wealth, fame, and image)... The Aspiration Index was developed to assess people’s aspirations. There are 7 categories of aspirations with five specific items within each category. The seven categories include: the extrinsic aspirations of wealth, fame, and image; the intrinsic aspirations of meaningful relationships, personal growth, and community contributions; and the aspiration of good health which turned out not to be clearly either extrinsic or intrinsic."
> — [Self-Determination Theory site, "Aspirations Index (AI)" page (theory’s official instrument description, quotation abridged)](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/aspirations-index/)

Respondents rate each aspiration for "(1) the importance to themselves of each aspiration, (2) their beliefs about the likelihood of attaining each, and (3) the degree to which they have already attained each" ([SDT site](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/aspirations-index/)). The correlate finding is summarized in Ryan and Deci's review: "They found, first, that placing strong relative importance on intrinsic aspirations was positively associated with well-being indicators such as self-esteem, self-actualization, and the inverse of depression and anxiety, whereas placing strong relative importance on extrinsic aspirations was negatively related to these well-being indicators" (p. 75) ([Ryan & Deci 2000](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf)).
