“Conscience (Gewissen)” — Heidegger, Being and Time.

In Being and Time, conscience is not a moral faculty that stores rules and issues verdicts. It is an event in which the self is called back to itself. For the most part, Heidegger argues, a person drifts along in the "they" (das Man), taking over whatever the public interpretation of things hands out, and in that drifting does not hear itself at all. Conscience interrupts:

> "The call of conscience has the character of an appeal to Dasein by calling it to its ownmost potentiality-for-Being-its-Self; and this is done by way of summoning it to its ownmost Being-guilty." ([Heidegger, Being and Time, Macquarrie and Robinson translation, p. 314](https://www.beyng.com/pages/en/BeingandTimeMR/BeingandTimeMR.314.html))

The call is strange in two ways. First, it is silent. It comes as no message:

> "Conscience discourses solely and constantly in the mode of keeping silent."

> "Taken strictly, nothing. The call asserts nothing, gives no information about world-events, has nothing to tell." (same book, p. 318)

Second, the caller is not someone else. It is not God, not a moral law within, not a parent's voice internalized:

> "In its 'who', the caller is definable in a 'worldly' way by nothing at all. The caller is Dasein in its uncanniness: primordial, thrown Being-in-the-world as the 'not-at-home' — the bare 'that-it-is' in the 'nothing' of the world. The caller is unfamiliar to the everyday they-self; it is something like an alien voice." (same book, p. 321)

The appeal thus travels from the self to the self: it summons the they-self to its own possibility of being itself, and the summoning is toward Being-guilty, which here is not a list of misdeeds but the ground-character of a being whose basis it never chose and must own. What conscience makes possible is [[Resoluteness (Entschlossenheit)]]: hearing the call and taking over one's situation as one's own. The compendium's principle [[E045 — Practise conscience (Gewissen)]] works with this account rather than the rule-storage picture, and [[Law written on the heart]] gives the older tradition the call echoes.
