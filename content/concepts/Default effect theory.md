The default effect is the finding that people are more likely to pick an option that is already selected for them. The most thorough quantitative review opens with the statement: "When people make decisions with a pre-selected choice option – a 'default' – they are more likely to select that option" ([Jachimowicz, Duncan, Weber and Johnson, 2019](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioural-public-policy/article/when-and-why-defaults-influence-decisions-a-metaanalysis-of-default-effects/67AF6972CFB52698A60B6BD94B70C2C0)).

### How large, and how variable

The authors meta-analysed 58 default studies (pooled n = 73,675) and report a considerable influence of defaults (d = 0.68, 95% confidence interval 0.53 to 0.83). They also report "substantial variation: the majority of default studies find positive effects, but several do not find a significant effect, and two even demonstrate negative effects." Defaults were more effective in consumer domains and less effective in environmental ones ([Jachimowicz et al., 2019](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioural-public-policy/article/when-and-why-defaults-influence-decisions-a-metaanalysis-of-default-effects/67AF6972CFB52698A60B6BD94B70C2C0)).

### Why defaults work

The review names three channels: endorsement, ease and endowment. People may choose the pre-selected option because they believe the intentions of the choice architect are beneficial to them, because staying put takes less effort, or because they judge other options against the one they are already endowed with. In the meta-analysis, defaults were more effective when seen as endorsement or as the status quo (endowment) ([Jachimowicz et al., 2019](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioural-public-policy/article/when-and-why-defaults-influence-decisions-a-metaanalysis-of-default-effects/67AF6972CFB52698A60B6BD94B70C2C0)). A later paper reports that the same meta-analysis found no effect of ease of decision making ([White and colleagues, 2021](https://socialactionlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/White_THE-LIMITS-OF-DEFAULTS-THE-INFLUENCE-OF-DECISION-TIME-ON-DEFAULT_2021.pdf)).

### Scope

The compendium's earlier summary says defaults act as implicit recommendations. That is one of the three channels, the endorsement one, and not the whole account. The studies concern choices such as donating or buying. They do not test contemplative practice or rest.
