Mattering, in the psychological research tradition, is the need to feel significant to other people ([Flett and Zangeneh, Mattering as a Vital Support for People](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/6c58/ddb9d4765e08ba66de217a89c88f4edd1933.pdf)). Flett and Zangeneh trace this account to Rosenberg and McCullough (1981), who focused on three components ([Flett and Zangeneh, Mattering as a Vital Support for People](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/6c58/ddb9d4765e08ba66de217a89c88f4edd1933.pdf)):

> "Rosenberg and McCullough (1981) focused on three components: (1) the sense that other people depend on us; (2) the perception that other people regard us as important; and (3) the realization that other people are actively paying attention to us. They suggested that being a focus of attention is the most central mattering component." (Flett and Zangeneh, [Mattering as a Vital Support for People During the COVID-19 Pandemic](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/6c58/ddb9d4765e08ba66de217a89c88f4edd1933.pdf))

In the meaning-in-life literature, mattering appears as one of three facets of meaning, alongside coherence and purpose ([Costin and Vignoles, Meaning Is About Mattering](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/6d5a/00c00957155add5b4e8853ed7cbcdd78ae68.pdf)):

> "Existential mattering (henceforth, mattering: George & Park, 2014; also known as \"significance\": Martela & Steger, 2016), describes experiences of value, worth, and transcending \"the trivial or momentary\" conditions of our lives (George & Park, 2016a; Heintzelman & King, 2014b; King et al., 2006)." (Costin and Vignoles, [Meaning Is About Mattering](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/6d5a/00c00957155add5b4e8853ed7cbcdd78ae68.pdf))

Costin and Vignoles connect existential mattering with the perceived value of one's life and the difference one's actions make ([Costin and Vignoles, Meaning Is About Mattering](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/6d5a/00c00957155add5b4e8853ed7cbcdd78ae68.pdf)). Deficits in mattering have established links with mental health problems across life contexts ([Flett and Zangeneh, Mattering as a Vital Support for People](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/6c58/ddb9d4765e08ba66de217a89c88f4edd1933.pdf)).
