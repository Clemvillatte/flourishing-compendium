Tracy and Robins's two-facet model splits pride into two components with different attributional roots and different company. [[Authentic pride]] is the achievement-oriented facet, associated with accomplishment and confidence, and follows success attributed to effort; [[Hubristic pride]] is the narcissistic facet, associated with arrogance and conceit, and follows success attributed to stable traits such as intelligence. The comparison matters because the two facets travel differently: authentic pride with genuine self-esteem, successful social relationships and mental health; hubristic pride with narcissistic self-aggrandizement and aggression.

> "Authentic pride is the more socially desirable, achievement-oriented facet, associated with accomplishment and confidence. Hubristic pride is the more narcissistic facet, associated with arrogance and conceit." (Tracy, Cheng, Robins and Trzesniewski, [Authentic and Hubristic Pride: The Affective Core of Self-esteem and Narcissism](https://scispace.com/pdf/authentic-and-hubristic-pride-the-affective-core-of-self-4b4lave9ch.pdf), Self and Identity 8(2):196-213, 2009)
