The many become one is Whitehead's one-line formula for how anything comes to exist. In Process and Reality it is the Category of the Ultimate, the replacement he offers for Aristotle's category of primary substance: "The many become one, and are increased by one. In their natures, entities are disjunctively 'many' in process of passage into conjunctive unity. This Category of the Ultimate replaces Aristotle's category of 'primary substance.' Thus the 'production of novel togetherness' is the ultimate notion embodied in the term 'concrescence'" ([Whitehead, Process and Reality, pp. 21-22, quoted in Open Horizons](https://www.openhorizons.org/the-many-become-one-and-are-increased-by-one.html)).

### How the formula works

The sentence is dense but decomposes. The "many" are the settled results of the past, everything that has already become. The "one" is the new actual occasion that takes them as its data. The "increased by one" is the payoff: once the new occasion exists, it joins the many for whatever comes next. The passage continues: "The novel entity is at once the togetherness of the 'many' which it finds, and also it is one among the disjunctive 'many' which it leaves; it is a novel entity, disjunctively among the many entities which it synthesizes" ([Process and Reality 21-22](https://www.openhorizons.org/the-many-become-one-and-are-increased-by-one.html)). And the engine is named: "'Creativity' is the principle of novelty. An actual occasion is a novel entity diverse from any entity in the 'many' which it unifies" ([Process and Reality 21-22](https://www.openhorizons.org/the-many-become-one-and-are-increased-by-one.html)). The SEP's gloss on the system: "The basic unit of reality in Whitehead's system is an event-like entity called 'actual occasion,' which is the procedural integration or 'concrescence' of processes of data transfer ('prehensions') into unities that become new data" ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Process Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/process-philosophy/)).

### What it replaces

The classical ontology the formula argues against takes substances as fundamental and change as secondary. Process philosophy reverses the order: "being is dynamic and that the dynamic nature of being should be the primary focus of any comprehensive philosophical account of reality and our place within it" ([SEP, Process Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/process-philosophy/)). On this view a thing is not a substrate that changes; it is a repetition of becomings, and the repetition is what gives it whatever stability it has. Whitehead's own summary of the ultimate notions closes with a shrug that philosophers of substance rarely permit themselves: "These ultimate notions of 'production of novelty' and of 'concrete togetherness' are inexplicable either in terms of higher universals or in terms of the components participating in the concrescence. The analysis of the components abstracts from the concrescence. The sole appeal is to intuition" ([Process and Reality 21-22](https://www.openhorizons.org/the-many-become-one-and-are-increased-by-one.html)).
