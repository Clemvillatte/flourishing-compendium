Galinsky et al (2008) is the compendium's shorthand for Adam Galinsky, William Maddux, Debra Gilin and Judith White's study of perspective-taking in negotiation, "Why It Pays to Get Inside the Head of Your Opponent", in Psychological Science. Its finding: the cognitive capacity to consider the world from another individual's viewpoint beats emotional connection at the bargaining table.

> "Across three studies, using both individual difference measures and experimental manipulations, we found that perspective taking increased individuals' ability to discover hidden agreements and to both create and claim resources at the bargaining table." (Galinsky, Maddux, Gilin and White 2008, [Psychological Science 19(4)](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.2008.02096.x/))

### What the study found

Empathy did not prove nearly as advantageous, and at times was detrimental to discovering a possible deal and achieving individual profit; the results held both in negotiations where no prima facie solution was possible and in multiple-issue negotiations requiring mutually beneficial trade-offs. The authors' closing line keeps the scope: empathy is an essential tool in many aspects of social life, while perspective taking is a particularly critical ability in negotiations.
