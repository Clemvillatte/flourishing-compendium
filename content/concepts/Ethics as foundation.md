In Charles Johnston's interpretation of Yoga Sutras II.29, the Commandments and the Rules appear before right Poise and the later practices. His commentary treats the sequence as preparation for subsequent practice.

> "The eight means of Yoga are: the Commandments, the Rules, right Poise, right Control of the life-force, Withdrawal, Attention, Meditation, Contemplation." (Patanjali, Yoga Sutras II.29, tr. Charles Johnston, [Project Gutenberg ebook 2526](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2526/2526-h/2526-h.htm))

### Johnston's reading of the sequence

The Commandments (II.30) are non-injury, truthfulness, abstention from stealing, from impurity, from covetousness; the Rules (II.32) are purity, serenity, fervent aspiration, spiritual reading and obedience to the Master. Johnston's commentary on the ordering is explicit: until the Commandments are fulfilled, concerning oneself with the Rules is futile, and so with all the means of yoga.

> "From steadfastly following after the means of Yoga, until impurity is worn away, there comes the illumination of thought up to full discernment." (Patanjali, Yoga Sutras II.28, tr. Charles Johnston, [Project Gutenberg ebook 2526](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2526/2526-h/2526-h.htm))
