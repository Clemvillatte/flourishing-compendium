### The Svenson driving study

Svenson's 1981 paper asked participants in the United States and Sweden to rate their own driving skill and safety relative to other drivers. The finding is often summarised in the literature as that most participants rated themselves as more skilled and safe drivers than others in the group. As one review of the study records:

> "70–90% of drivers reported that their driving was better and less risky than that of the average driver (Svenson, 1981)." ([PMC, The hazards of perception](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6529486/))

The numbers are described as impossible at the group level: not everyone can be above average.
