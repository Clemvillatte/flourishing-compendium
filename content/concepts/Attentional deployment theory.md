**Attentional deployment theory** — attention can be moved within a situation toward or away from emotional stimuli as a regulation strategy.

Attentional deployment is one of the five families in James Gross's process model of emotion regulation, sitting between situation modification and cognitive change. The definition, from Gross and Barrett's own review:

> "Attentional deployment refers to influencing emotional responding by re-directing attention within a given situation." (Gross & Barrett, [Emotion Generation and Emotion Regulation: One or Two Depends on Your Point of View, Emotion Review 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3072688/))

### Its place in the model

The same article presents the process model as "five families of emotion regulation strategies" (from Gross & Thompson, 2007). The definition's wording carries the seed line's point: the situation stays, the attention moves.
