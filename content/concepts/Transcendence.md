Transcendence is the movement past the self's own boundary, and I hold the two traditions that use the word: the philosophical, where Jaspers makes transcendence the individual's relation to what no object can hold, and the psychological, where Maslow placed it above self-actualization late in his life.

### Jaspers

In Jaspers's account, transcendence is what the encompassing (das Umgreifende) is when it is approached not as the whole of being but as the source beyond every object. Whatever we think of is "always something other than ourselves," and being as a whole "is neither subject nor object but must be the Comprehensive, which is manifested in this dichotomy" ([Jaspers, Way to Wisdom, pp. 28-29, as quoted in Practise the encompassing](content/principles/E094%20—%20Practise%20the%20encompassing%20(das%20Umgreifende).md)). Transcendence, in his terms, is the name for the direction of that relation: never an object of knowledge, approached only through the modes in which we already are.

### Maslow

The psychological version is Maslow's late amendment. In "Theory Z" (1969) he distinguished two kinds of self-actualizing people, "those who were clearly healthy, but with little or no experiences of transcendence, and those in whom transcendent experiencing was important and even central" ([Maslow, Theory Z, in The Farther Reaches of Human Nature, p. 277, quoted at sloww.co](https://www.sloww.co/maslow-theory-z-transcender/)). His definition of the term:

> "Transcendence refers to the very highest and most inclusive or holistic levels of human consciousness, behaving and relating, as ends rather than means, to oneself, to significant others, to human beings in general, to other species, to nature, and to the cosmos."

([Maslow, The Farther Reaches of Human Nature, 1971, p. 269, quoted at reasonandmeaning.com](https://reasonandmeaning.com/2017/01/18/summary-of-maslow-on-self-transcendence/).)

The revision was cut short by his death in 1970, but it is in print under his own name ([Koltko-Rivera 2006, Review of General Psychology](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1037/1089-2680.10.4.302)), and it matters because it moves the top of the model off the self entirely.
