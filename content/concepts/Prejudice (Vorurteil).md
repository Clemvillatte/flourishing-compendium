"Prejudice (Vorurteil)" — Gadamer, Truth and Method.

Gadamer's rehabilitation of prejudice begins with the word itself: a Vor-urteil is a pre-judgment, the judgment one brings before the examination. The Enlightenment's demand that understanding start from nowhere he reads as itself a prejudice, the prejudice against prejudice. What understanding works with is the inheritance that arrived before self-examination did.

> "In fact history does not belong to us but rather we to it. Long before we understand ourselves through the process of self-examination, we understand ourselves in a self-evident way in the family, society, and state in which we live. The focus of subjectivity is a distorting mirror. The self awareness of the Individual is only a flickering in the closed circuit of historical life. That is why the prejudices of an individual are — much more than that individual's judgments — the historical reality of his being." (Gadamer, Truth and Method, p. 289, [Weinsheimer and Marshall translation, Wikiquote](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Hans-Georg_Gadamer))

### The anticipation of meaning

The rehabilitation has a working side. Understanding does not wait for a full reconstruction of another horizon before it begins; it starts from what the reader already brings:

> "We cannot understand without wanting to understand, that is, without wanting to let something be said. It would be an inadmissible abstraction to contend that we must first have achieved a contemporaneousness with the author or the original reader by means of a reconstruction of his historical horizon before we could begin to grasp the meaning of what is said. A kind of anticipation of meaning guides the effort to understand from the very beginning." (Gadamer, Truth and Method, p. 101, [Wikiquote](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Hans-Georg_Gadamer))

The anticipation is not a defect to be cleared away before real understanding starts. It is the material understanding works through, and the correction, when it comes, comes from the matter itself: a text that resists the expectation revises it. The practical takeaway runs against both the Enlightenment's ideal of a view from nowhere and the lazy use of the word: prejudice here is a claim one is answerable for, not a permission slip.
