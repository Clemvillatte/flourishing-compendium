Craftsmanship research is the interdisciplinary line of work that takes making things well as its subject, and Richard Sennett's The Craftsman (2008) is its center of gravity. Its charter definition is one sentence:

> "Craftsmanship names an enduring, basic human impulse, the desire to do a job well for its own sake." ([Sennett 2008, p. 9, quoted in Jones, Van Assche and Parkins 2021, "Reimagining craft for community development", open access](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13549839.2021.1939289))

The research program's first move is to widen the term beyond the workshop:

> "Craftsmanship cuts a far wider swath than skilled manual labour; it serves the computer programmer, the doctor, and the artist; parenting improves when it is practices as a skilled craft, as does citizenship." (Sennett 2008, p. 9, quoted in the same article)

> "In all these domains, craftsmanship focuses on objective standards, on the thing itself." (Sennett 2008, quoted in the same article)

And its two empirical theses give the research its shape:

> "first, that all skills, even the most abstract, begin as bodily practices; second, that technical understanding develops through the powers of imagination" (Sennett 2008, p. 10, quoted in the same article).

The psychological wing of the research line connects to the entry's opening claim about material feedback. Work on skill from [[T001 — Csikszentmihalyi, Mihaly|Csikszentmihalyi]] treats engagement as something the material manufactures: the worker gets continuous feedback from wood, code or clay, and the feedback carries the flow that holds attention. The craftsmanship researchers add the moral reading: doing a job well for its own sake is not an escape from standards but a commitment to "objective standards, on the thing itself", and the standards are reached through the body's dialogue with resistant material. Errors, on this line of thought, are not failures of the plan but the material's way of teaching.

My principle [[P112 — Respect the material — work with its grain]] is this research in counsel form, and the companion pages [[Skillful responsiveness]] and [[The quiet craftsman's life]] carry its psychological and biographical findings.
