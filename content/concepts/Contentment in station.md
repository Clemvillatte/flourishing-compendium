“Contentment in station” — Epictetus, the role-playing counsels of the Discourses.

Contentment in station is Epictetus' answer to the question of what to do with a life one did not choose. His most famous counsel is theatrical:

> "Remember that you are an actor in a play, of such a kind as the director chooses: if short, of a short one; if long, of a long one. If he wishes you to act a beggar, act even this well; and so too a cripple, a ruler, or a private citizen. For this is your business: to act well the part that is given to you; but to choose it belongs to another." ([Epictetus, Encheiridion, chapter 17](https://epictetus-enchiridion.org/en/read/))

The counsel rests on the division the whole handbook opens with:

> "Of things, some are within our power and some are not. Within our power are judgment, impulse, desire, aversion — in a word, whatever is our own doing. Not within our power are the body, property, reputation, office — in a word, whatever is not our own doing." (chapter 1)

The station one is in, like the role, is largely not one's own doing; what one makes of it is. That is why Epictetus can treat the beggar and the ruler as the same assignment, and why his version of progress asks a person to stop managing appearances:

> "If you wish to make progress, be content to be thought foolish and silly in outward things; do not wish to seem to know anything." (chapter 13)

Note what the counsel is not. It is not passivity: chapter 17 says to act the part well, and the training for hardship is active ("if hardship comes, you will find endurance", chapter 10). It is contentment with the part, not with every event inside it. The stance matters for flourishing because it removes a whole class of suffering, the suffering of comparing one's casting with someone else's, and redirects the effort to performance. The compendium's principle [[M034 — Carry yourself with dignity in every station]] is this counsel in practice, and its emotional neighbors [[Gratitude]] and [[Delight in the ordinary]] are what the settled version of it feels like from inside.
