**Reinforcer Pathology Model** — Bickel, Jarmolowicz — addiction is the pathological steep discounting of future rewards against immediate ones.

The Reinforcer Pathology Model is Warren Bickel's behavioral-economic account of addiction. The founding statement:

> "Reinforcer pathology refers to the interaction between two processes: 1) excessive valuation of a drug or other preferred commodity and 2) excessive preference for immediate rewards (1)." (Bickel, Snider, Quisenberry & Stein, [Reinforcer Pathology: The Behavioral Economics of Abuse Liability Testing, 2016](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9812002/))

### The second process

The discounting side is measured directly: "The second process, excessive preference for immediate rewards, is measured by delay discounting, a task that ascertains the extent of the decrease in value of a reinforcer as a function of the delay to its receipt". Later versions of the model treat that discounting as a temporal window over which reinforcer value is integrated, so that a narrow window raises the value of brief intense reinforcers and lowers that of extended ones.
