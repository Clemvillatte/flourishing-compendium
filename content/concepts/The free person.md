Spinoza's exemplar: one who lives by reason, avoids the mob's favors and hatreds, keeps promises from strength, and thinks least of death. The Ethics' Part IV paints this portrait ([SEP Spinoza](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/spinoza/)).
