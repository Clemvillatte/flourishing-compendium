### Definition

Positive freedom is freedom as self-mastery: not the absence of interference but the presence of a self in charge of its own life. Berlin's famous paragraph opens the concept:

> The 'positive' sense of the word 'liberty' derives from the wish on the part of the individual to be his own master. I wish my life and decisions to depend on myself, not on external forces, of whatever kind. I wish to be a subject, to be the instrument of my own, not of other men's, acts of will. ([Berlin, Two Concepts of Liberty](https://berlin.wolf.ox.ac.uk/published_works/tcl/tcl-e.pdf))

He immediately flags the danger the concept carries. Self-mastery tempts the thought that some impulses are the "real" me and others are not, and from there it is a short step to someone else deciding which is which:

> The freedom which consists in being one's own master, and the freedom which consists in not being prevented from choosing as I do by other men, may, on the face of it, seem concepts at no great logical distance from each other – no more than a negative and positive way of saying the same thing. Yet the 'positive' and 'negative' notions of freedom developed in divergent directions until, in the end, they came into direct conflict with each other. ([Berlin, Two Concepts of Liberty](https://berlin.wolf.ox.ac.uk/published_works/tcl/tcl-e.pdf))

### Origin

Berlin delivered "Two Concepts of Liberty" as his 1958 Oxford inaugural lecture, drawing the negative-positive distinction that has organised debates since. Negative liberty asks how many doors are open; positive liberty asks who is doing the choosing. Berlin valued both and warned against each one's characteristic abuse: negative liberty can defend neglect, positive liberty can license coercion "for your own good".

### A real-life example

A woman may be unobstructed in her choices, doors everywhere, and still feel unfree because the voice inside the choosing is not hers: borrowed ambitions, inherited fears, a schedule set by other people's expectations. Her problem is not interference but authorship. That is the problem positive freedom names.
