Competence motivation is Robert White's proposal for the motive behind exploration and mastery. In his 1959 paper, competence names an organism's capacity to interact effectively with its environment, and effectance is his name for the motivation behind the activity that builds and exercises that capacity.

> "As used here, competence will refer to an organism's capacity to interact effectively with its environment." (White, [Motivation Reconsidered: The Concept of Competence](https://greatquestion.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/White-Motivation-reconsidered-the-concept-of-competence-1959.pdf), Psychological Review 66(5):297-333, 1959, p. 297)

### Effectance

White called the motivational aspect of competence "effectance": "It needs its own name, and in view of the foregoing analysis I propose that this name be effectance" ([p. 320](https://greatquestion.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/White-Motivation-reconsidered-the-concept-of-competence-1959.pdf)). "It is in no sense a deficit motive" ([p. 321](https://greatquestion.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/White-Motivation-reconsidered-the-concept-of-competence-1959.pdf)); it is "aroused by stimulus conditions which offer, as Hebb (1949) puts it, difference-in-sameness" ([p. 322](https://greatquestion.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/White-Motivation-reconsidered-the-concept-of-competence-1959.pdf)), and its activity is "selective, directed, and persistent" ([p. 323](https://greatquestion.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/White-Motivation-reconsidered-the-concept-of-competence-1959.pdf)). Its satisfaction is a feeling of efficacy during exploratory activity: "Effectance motivation must be conceived to involve satisfaction—a feeling of efficacy—in transactions in which behavior has an exploratory, varying, experimental character and produces changes in the stimulus field" ([p. 329](https://greatquestion.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/White-Motivation-reconsidered-the-concept-of-competence-1959.pdf)). The paper's summary states: "It is proposed to designate this motivation by the term effectance, and to characterize the experience produced as a feeling of efficacy" ([p. 329](https://greatquestion.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/White-Motivation-reconsidered-the-concept-of-competence-1959.pdf)).
