The beginningless cycle of birth, death, and rebirth driven by karma and ignorance. The Indic traditions agree on the diagnosis and offer distinct remedies: knowledge (Vedānta), practice (Yoga), cessation (Buddhism) ([Wikipedia Saṃsāra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa%E1%B9%83s%C4%81ra)).
