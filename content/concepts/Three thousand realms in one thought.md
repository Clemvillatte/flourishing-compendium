Three thousand realms in one thought (yi nian san qian) is the Tiantai school's contemplation method. The Digital Dictionary of Buddhism glossary records it as "one thought contains the three thousandfold world system", a concept of Zhi Yi (538-597), founder of the Tiantai school. ([Wisdom Library, "Yi nian san qian"](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/yi-nian-san-qian))

> "In one thought to survey or embrace the 3,000 worlds, or a chiliocosmos with all its forms of existence; to see the universe as a thought; it is a Tiantai mode of meditation." (A Dictionary of Chinese Buddhist Terms, quoted at [Wisdom Library](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/yi-nian-san-qian))

The same page carries a Mohe Zhiguan vol. 5 passage stating that the three thousand exist within a single thought, but marks that English rendering as an unverified translation, so it is not quoted here. ([Wisdom Library](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/yi-nian-san-qian))
