Apatheia is the Stoic ideal of freedom from passion — not numbness. The Stoics' definition of a passion (pathos): "impulse which is excessive and disobedient to the dictates of reason, or a movement of soul which is irrational and contrary to nature" ([SEP, "Stoicism"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/stoicism/)). The virtuous agent, they argued, "feels no passions (pathê)" and so lives "entirely passion-free (apathês)" ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/stoicism/)).

### Not the unfeeling robot

The caricature of the sage as an unfeeling robot is belied by the Stoics' own account of good feelings (eupatheiai): "the Sage's good feelings are emotional responses to genuine goods, i.e. to virtue, virtuous actions, and virtuous friends" ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/stoicism/)). The four generic passions — distress, fear, appetite, delight — each rest on a false judgment, "an ascription of value to an object that is present or in prospect" ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/stoicism/)). Apatheia is not the absence of emotion but the retirement of false judgments from emotion.

### The discipline

Epictetus gives the practice: "Men are disturbed not by things, but by the views which they take of things. Thus death is nothing terrible, else it would have appeared so to Socrates. But the terror consists in our notion of death, that it is terrible" ([Enchiridion 5, Higginson translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/45109/45109-h/45109-h.htm)). The training ground is [[The dichotomy of control|the dichotomy of control]]: "There are things which are within our power, and there are things which are beyond our power" ([Enchiridion 1](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/45109/45109-h/45109-h.htm)).

### In the compendium

S071's emotions are judgments and S059's not being carried away by passion are the direct carries; AF001's affect as information rather than verdict is the clinical cousin; AF034's equanimity as a capacity is a modern neighbour to set beside the ideal. The compendium sets other vocabularies beside it as well: B007's fires cooling here and now, D041's purity and stillness (qingjing), R063's rectification of the mind (zhengxin), and B096's even-mindedness.
