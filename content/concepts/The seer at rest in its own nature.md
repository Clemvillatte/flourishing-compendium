Yoga Sutra 1.3 states what remains when yoga stills the mind's turnings: then the seer stands firm in its own form. The sutra follows the definition of yoga as the restraining of the mind's turnings (1.2) and precedes the counterfactual, that otherwise the form of the mind's turnings will be assumed (1.4). ([Yoga Sutra 1.2-1.4, Goswami translation](https://krishnavillage-retreat.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Patanjali-Yoga-Sutras-Krishna-Village.pdf))

> "Then the seer stands firm in his own form." (Yoga Sutra 1.3, tada drashtuh svarupe avasthanam, H.D. Goswami translation, [Krishna Village PDF](https://krishnavillage-retreat.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Patanjali-Yoga-Sutras-Krishna-Village.pdf))

The edition's word glosses give "seer" as the soul, witness, Atman, Self, and note that Patanjali does not try to define the nature of the seer: that is left to be resolved in direct experience. ([Yoga Sutra 1.3 word glosses](https://krishnavillage-retreat.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Patanjali-Yoga-Sutras-Krishna-Village.pdf))
