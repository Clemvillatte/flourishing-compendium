The Epicurean "fourfold cure": don't fear god, don't worry about death, what is good is easy to get, what is terrible is easy to endure ([Wikipedia Tetrapharmakos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrapharmakos)).

The tetrapharmakos is Epicureanism compressed into four lines, a four-part remedy for the four great fears. As expressed by Philodemos and preserved in Herculaneum Papyrus 1005 (5.9-14):

> "Don't fear god, Don't worry about death; What is good is easy to get, What is terrible is easy to endure" — Greek: "Ἄφοβον ὁ θεός, ἀνύποπτον ὁ θάνατος καὶ τἀγαθὸν μὲν εὔκτητον, τὸ δὲ δεινὸν εὐεκκαρτέρητον" ([Wikipedia Tetrapharmakos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrapharmakos))

### The four doctrines behind the cure

Each line of the cure carries a doctrine from Epicurus's Principal Doctrines. On the gods:

> "A happy and eternal being has no trouble himself and brings no trouble upon any other being; hence he is exempt from movements of anger and partiality, for every such movement implies weakness" ([Principal Doctrines 1, via Wikipedia Tetrapharmakos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrapharmakos))

On death:

> "Death is nothing to us; for the body, when it has been resolved into its elements, has no feeling, and that which has no feeling is nothing to us." ([Principal Doctrines 2, via Wikipedia Tetrapharmakos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrapharmakos))

On limits of pleasure:

> "The magnitude of pleasure reaches its limit in the removal of all pain. When pleasure is present, so long as it is uninterrupted, there is no pain either of body or of mind or of both together." ([Principal Doctrines 3, via Wikipedia Tetrapharmakos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrapharmakos))

And on the duration of pain:

> "Continuous pain does not last long in the body; on the contrary, pain, if extreme, is present a short time, and even that degree of pain which barely outweighs pleasure in the body does not last for many days together." ([Principal Doctrines 4, via Wikipedia Tetrapharmakos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrapharmakos))
