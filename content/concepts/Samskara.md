Saṃskāras are the latent impressions left by past action and experience — the deep layer that resurfaces when practice deepens. Yoga works on them; Buddhism's counterpart is saṅkhāra among the [[Skandha|aggregates]] ([Wikipedia Saṃskāra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa%E1%B9%83sk%C4%81ra)).
