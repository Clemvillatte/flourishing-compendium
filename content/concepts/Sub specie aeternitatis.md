"Under the aspect of eternity" — Spinoza's name for the mind's highest perspective: understanding things as necessary, as a mode of the one substance, rather than as contingent objects of hope and fear. It is the viewpoint of the third kind of knowledge and the ground of [[Blessedness]] ([Wikipedia Sub specie aeternitatis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_specie_aeternitatis)).
