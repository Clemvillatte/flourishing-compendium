A horizon, in Husserl's phenomenology, is what is implicitly co-given with any perceived thing beyond what is given explicitly. In Ideas I, objects are not perceived in isolation but against a background (Hintergrund) and in the midst of a surrounding world (Umwelt) of other objects ([Moran, 2019](https://dermotmoran.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/A_2018_Husserl-and-Gurwitsch-on-Horizonal-Intentionality_-The-Gurwitch-Memorial-Lecture-.pdf)). The lecture's worked example is a house: in the Amsterdam Lectures, Husserl describes the noema of a house in a house-perception as opening onto an infinite horizon of other possible profiles of the house ([Moran, 2019](https://dermotmoran.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/A_2018_Husserl-and-Gurwitsch-on-Horizonal-Intentionality_-The-Gurwitch-Memorial-Lecture-.pdf)). His rule for it is that the indeterminacy never fully clears:

> "A horizon of determinable indeterminacy always remains in principle, regardless of how much progress we make experientially, regardless of how large the continua of current perceptions of the same thing that we have run through are." (Husserl, Ideas I § 44, p. 78, as quoted in [Moran, Husserl and Gurwitsch on Horizonal Intentionality](https://dermotmoran.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/A_2018_Husserl-and-Gurwitsch-on-Horizonal-Intentionality_-The-Gurwitch-Memorial-Lecture-.pdf), p. 32)

The quote is taken from a published lecture that cites Ideas I section 44 directly. The same lecture records that in Ideas I § 83 Husserl says "'horizon' has here the same value as did the terms 'halo' and 'background'" (Ideas I, p. 160), and that every experience influences the bright or dim halo of further experiences ([Moran, 2019](https://dermotmoran.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/A_2018_Husserl-and-Gurwitsch-on-Horizonal-Intentionality_-The-Gurwitch-Memorial-Lecture-.pdf)). The horizon is what keeps perception from being a series of snapshots: the lecture quotes Husserl's later lectures on passive synthesis, where everything that genuinely appears is "an appearing thing only by virtue of being intertwined and permeated with an intentional empty horizon", an emptiness "that is not a nothingness, but an emptiness to be filled out" ([Moran, 2019](https://dermotmoran.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/A_2018_Husserl-and-Gurwitsch-on-Horizonal-Intentionality_-The-Gurwitch-Memorial-Lecture-.pdf)).
