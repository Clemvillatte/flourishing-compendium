Heidegger's "releasement" — a letting-be of things that neither manipulates them (the technological stance) nor passively abandons them. It is the late-Heideggerian counterpart to the Daoist [[Wu wei]] and the Eckhartian letting-go of the will ([Wikipedia Gelassenheit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelassenheit)).
