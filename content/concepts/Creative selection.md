Creative selection is this wiki's label for a family of ideas around creation and keeping in Henri Bergson's Creative Evolution (1907). The term does not appear in the book; the research note below records what the opened text does say. Flagged for a namespace disposition (rename or merge into [[Creative evolution]]).

Bergson on existence as self-remaking, in Arthur Mitchell's translation:

> "for a conscious being, to exist is to change, to change is to mature, to mature is to go on creating oneself endlessly." (Bergson, Creative Evolution, ch. 1, trans. Mitchell, [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/26163/26163-8.txt))

He extends the thought to nature: "Their novelty arises from an internal impetus", so the works of nature cannot be foreseen from a snapshot of the material universe, any more than the concrete solution of a painting can be read off its elements.
