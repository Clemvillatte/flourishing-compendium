Ren (仁, humaneness) is Confucius's cardinal virtue — the humane disposition that "wishing to establish oneself, establishes others" (*Analects* 6.30), operating through [[Li: ritual propriety]] (ritual propriety) and expressed first in [[Filial piety]] (filial piety). Its golden-rule form, shu ("do not impose on others what you do not want yourself"), is the Confucian counterpart to Ubuntu and to care ethics ([IEP Confucius](https://iep.utm.edu/confucius/)).
