Cultural dimensions theory is Geert Hofstede's model of national culture: six dimensions on which societies can be compared, each scaled roughly from 0 to 100. The six are power distance, individualism versus collectivism, masculinity versus femininity, uncertainty avoidance, long-term orientation, and indulgence versus restraint.

> "Geert Hofstede, assisted by others, came up with six basic issues that society needs to come to term with in order to organize itself. These are called dimensions of culture. Each of them has been expressed on a scale that runs roughly from 0 to 100." ([The 6 dimensions model of national culture](https://geerthofstede.com/culture-geert-hofstede-gert-jan-hofstede/6d-model-of-national-culture/), geerthofstede.com)

### What the dimensions measure

On the site's account, power distance concerns acceptance of unequal power, while individualism concerns independence versus membership in larger groups. Masculinity-femininity concerns expected emotional gender roles, toughness and competition.

Uncertainty avoidance concerns tolerance for uncertainty and ambiguity. Long-term orientation contrasts preparing for change with looking to the past for moral guidance; indulgence-restraint contrasts freedom to follow impulses with an emphasis on duty. The model's level of comparison is the society, not the individual: the site's masculinity section notes it is "NOT about individuals, but about expected emotional gender roles".
