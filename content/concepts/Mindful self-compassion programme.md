“Mindful self-compassion programme” — Neff and Germer, 2013.

The Mindful Self-Compassion (MSC) program is an 8-week workshop designed to train people to be more self-compassionate. It was evaluated in two studies: a pilot among community adults, and a randomized controlled trial comparing the treatment group with a waitlist control. Compared with controls, intervention participants reported significantly larger increases in self-compassion, mindfulness and wellbeing, with gains maintained at 6-month and 1-year follow-ups.

> "The aim of these two studies was to evaluate the effectiveness of the Mindful Self-Compassion (MSC) program, an 8-week workshop designed to train people to be more self-compassionate." (Neff & Germer, 2013, Journal of Clinical Psychology 69(1), 28–44, [abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23070875/))
