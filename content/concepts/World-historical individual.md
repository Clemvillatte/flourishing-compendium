“World-historical individual” — Hegel, Lectures on the Philosophy of History.

A world-historical individual, in Hegel's lectures, is the person whose private aim happens to carry the next stage of the World Spirit. They are not sages of history; they are doers whose wants coincide with what the age requires:

> "The historical men, world-historical individuals, are those who grasp just such a higher universal, make it their own purpose, and realize this purpose in accordance with the higher law of the spirit." (Hegel, [Lectures on the Philosophy of History](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hi/introduction.htm), trans. Hartman)

### Caesar, and the price

Hegel's worked example is Caesar, who fought to keep his position, honor and safety, and whose victory over his enemies "became at the same time conquest of the entire empire", so that in accomplishing his own purpose "he at the same time fulfilled the necessary historical destiny of Rome and the world": "it is the same with all great historical individuals: their own particular purposes contain the substantial will of the World Spirit" ([Lectures](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hi/introduction.htm)). The price is paid by others, and by them: a world-historical individual "is devoted, come what may, to one purpose", may treat sacred interests inconsiderately, and "so mighty a figure must trample down many an innocent flower, crush to pieces many things in its path" ([Lectures](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hi/introduction.htm)). The engine that lets this happen Hegel names the cunning of reason: the Idea "sets the passions to work for itself, while that through which it develops itself pays the penalty and suffers the loss" ([Lectures](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hi/introduction.htm)).

The compendium's kernel K022 turns the idea to the reader's own scale: locate yourself in history rather than above it, since the age you can grasp is the material you have.
