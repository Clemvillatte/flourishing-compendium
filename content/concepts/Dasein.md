Dasein is Heidegger's name for the kind of being that people have: beings for whom the question of what it means to be is a live one. In ordinary German the word means existence. Heidegger takes it over to name human beings, with a stress on how we exist rather than on what we are made of.

> "In ordinary German, the noun 'Dasein' means existence, but Heidegger appropriates the word to designate human beings." ([Wrathall, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Martin Heidegger", §2.1.3](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/))

### Why not just say "human"

Wrathall reads the choice of word as deliberate: by calling us Dasein instead of Mensch, Heidegger signals that he is "inquiring into our kind of being, and not engaged in an anthropological, psychological, or biological study" ([SEP, §2.1.3](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). The term picks us out "insofar as we have an understanding of being". It is also a count noun: each of us is "respectively a Dasein", in Heidegger's own words quoted by Wrathall.

### A being defined by possibilities

The key claim is modal. Existence is "a kind of being that is essentially related to possibilities", so Dasein can never be defined by a list of facts about it ([SEP, §2.1.3](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). Wrathall draws out three ways a Dasein's being is at stake: it can be who it is in several ways (mother, poet, conservative), it can own responsibility for itself or not (authenticity and inauthenticity), and it can decide not to be at all.

> "To be a Dasein thus means: I always understand myself (at some level) in terms of the contingency of my current existence." ([SEP, §2.1.3](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/))

Wrathall calls "being-in-the-world" the "fundamental structure" of Dasein ([SEP, §2.2](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/)). That is the point at which this concept meets my own project.

### An everyday angle

One reading of the idea (an interpretation, not a claim from the sources above): because no list of facts settles who a Dasein is, a person is never only their job title, diagnosis or habit, and which of their possible ways of being to take up stays an open question.
