The return of the repressed pattern is the compendium's name for the way buried material resurfaces. The underlying idea has two parents. Nietzsche supplies the mechanism in the Genealogy of Morals: drives that cannot discharge outwardly turn inward, and the pressure builds. Freud supplies the clinical observation and the name, "the return of the repressed," for the reappearance of that material in symptoms, slips and dreams.

### Nietzsche's mechanism

The key passage is Second Essay, section 16, where Nietzsche describes the internalization that produces bad conscience:

> "All instincts which do not find a vent without, turn inwards — this is what I mean by the growing 'internalisation' of man: consequently we have the first growth in man, of what subsequently was called his soul."

> "Enmity, cruelty, the delight in persecution, in surprises, change, destruction — the turning all these instincts against their own possessors: this is the origin of the 'bad conscience.'"

> "I regard the bad conscience as the serious illness which man was bound to contract under the stress of the most radical change which he has ever experienced — that change, when he found himself finally imprisoned within the pale of society and of peace."

([Nietzsche, On the Genealogy of Morals, Essay II §16, trans. Horace B. Samuel, Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/52319/52319-h/52319-h.htm).)

Freud later named what happens to the stored material. In his 1896 essay "Further Remarks on the Neuro-Psychoses of Defence" he introduced the concept, and in "Repression" (1915) he wrote that "it is not the repression itself which produces substitutive formations and symptoms, but that these latter are indications of a return of the repressed" ([Freud, quoted in Kihlstrom, Suffering from Reminiscences](https://www.ocf.berkeley.edu/~jfkihlstrom/return.htm)).

### Why it matters to practice

The compendium keeps the concept because deepening practice destabilizes defenses, and the material that surfaces is often old. This is the point of Expect latent material to surface when practice deepens (Y024): the surfacing is not a failure of practice but evidence that something was being held down. The concept also explains the puzzling regularity that patterns people most want gone tend to return at the moments they are least resourced, since the defense that held them down is exactly what is being relaxed.
