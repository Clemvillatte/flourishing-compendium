Original enlightenment (Chinese: benjue, 本覺; Japanese: hongaku) is an East Asian Buddhist doctrine which holds that all sentient beings are already enlightened or awakened in some way ([definition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongaku)). The doctrine's locus is the Awakening of Faith tradition and, in Japan, the medieval Tendai school; the compendium's source for the teaching is the Platform Sutra, the Chan scripture of Huineng, where the mind's original purity is the premise and practice the removal of what covers it. Jacqueline Stone's summary of the doctrine:

> "In essence, original enlightenment doctrine asserts that buddhahood is neither a potential to be realized nor a goal to be achieved but the true status of all things. Ordinary deluded people do not transform and become buddhas; we are buddha already, from the outset, and have only to realize it." ([Stone, "From Buddha Nature to Original Enlightenment"](https://buddhajones.net/papers/stone_buddha-nature-original-enlightenment_2022.pdf))

### The Platform Sutra's version

The sutra renders the doctrine as a fact about the mind: "our natures are fundamentally pure, it is through false thoughts that suchness is covered up" ([Platform Sutra, as rendered on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platform_Sutra)). Practice on this reading does not produce the pure mind; it clears the false thoughts that hide what is already there. Huineng's verse makes the same point with the mirror image: "Fundamentally there is not a single thing. / Where could dust arise?" ([Platform Sutra, verse rendering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platform_Sutra)).

The compendium carries this in two principles: B061 (Trust original purity: nothing to attain) and B087 (Trust the luminous mind: defilements are visitors), which reads it beside the Pali canon's teaching that the mind is luminous and defiled only by incoming defilements.
