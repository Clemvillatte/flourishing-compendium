In their 2001 paper, Demerouti, Bakker, Nachreiner and Schaufeli propose two broad categories of working conditions: job demands and job resources. ([Demerouti et al. 2001, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11419809/))

> "Job demands are primarily related to the exhaustion component of burnout, whereas (lack of) job resources are primarily related to disengagement."

— Demerouti et al. (2001), Abstract, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11419809/)

The study used self-reports and observer ratings of working conditions across human services, industry and transport, with 374 participants altogether. ([Demerouti et al. 2001, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11419809/)) The authors reported highly similar patterns across those three occupational groups. ([Demerouti et al. 2001, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11419809/))
