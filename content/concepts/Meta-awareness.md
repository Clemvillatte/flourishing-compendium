knowing what one is experiencing while experiencing it (Schooler et al., 2011).

The 2011 review frames mind wandering as the cyclic activity of two core processes: perceptual decoupling, the capacity to disengage attention from perception, and meta-awareness, the ability to take explicit note of the current contents of consciousness. The two can come apart in experience: a mind decoupled from perception can drift for stretches before meta-awareness registers the drift.

> "Findings regarding meta-awareness reveal that the mind is only intermittently aware of engaging in mind wandering." (Schooler et al., 2011, Trends in Cognitive Sciences 15(7), 319–326, [abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21684189/))
