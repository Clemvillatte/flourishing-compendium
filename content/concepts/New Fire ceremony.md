The Mexica (Aztec) ritual at each 52-year cycle's end: all fires extinguished, the hearth relit on a captive's chest — periodic cleansing and renewal of the world ([Wikipedia New Fire ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Fire_ceremony)).
