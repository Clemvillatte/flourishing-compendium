The call of conscience is Heidegger's account in Being and Time of how Dasein is summoned out of its lostness in the "they." His statement of what the call is:

> "The call of conscience has the character of an appeal to Dasein by calling it to its ownmost potentiality-for-Being-its-Self; and this is done by way of summoning it to its ownmost Being-guilty."

([Heidegger, Being and Time, trans. Macquarrie and Robinson, §56, beyng.com](https://www.beyng.com/pages/en/BeingandTimeMR/BeingandTimeMR.314.html).)

### The silence of the call

The call does not speak. Heidegger is emphatic on the mode: "Conscience discourses solely and constantly in the mode of keeping silent. [...] [it] forces the Dasein which has been appealed to and summoned, into the reticence of itself" ([§56, Macquarrie-Robinson text at dhspriory.org](https://dhspriory.org/kenny/PhilTexts/Heidegger/BeingTime08.doc)). The fuller formulation: "Only in keeping silent does the conscience call; that is to say, the call comes from the soundlessness of uncanniness, and the Dasein which it summons is called back into the stillness of itself, and called back as something that is to become still" ([Being and Time, beyng.com](https://www.beyng.com/pages/en/BeingandTimeMR/BeingandTimeMR.343.html)).

### What the guilt is

The guilt the call summons Dasein to is ontological, not moral: "Dasein need not first load a 'guilt' upon itself through its failures or omissions; it must only" own up to the nullity that is already its basis ([§58, dhspriory text](https://dhspriory.org/kenny/PhilTexts/Heidegger/BeingTime08.doc)). The Hegel-Heidegger comparison paper states the point plainly: "Dasein is guilty before it has incurred any debt. [...] Dasein is always already guilty, not because of some particular deed" ([Cambridge Core PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/FA1C30246011F2585F41DBC7723E452C/S2051536724000490a.pdf/div-class-title-hegel-and-heidegger-on-the-phenomenology-of-conscience-div.pdf)). And the response: "In understanding the call, Dasein lets its ownmost Self take action in itself in terms of that potentiality-for-Being which it has chosen" ([§58, beyng.com](https://www.beyng.com/pages/en/BeingandTimeMR/BeingandTimeMR.334.html)).
