ownership increases valuation (Kahneman et al., 1990; Thaler, 1980).

The endowment effect is the finding that owning a thing raises what it is worth to its owner. Richard Thaler named it in 1980, and the 1990 Journal of Political Economy paper by Kahneman, Knetsch and Thaler gave it its decisive experimental test. The paper's abstract states the gap in one line:

> "Contrary to theoretical expectations, measures of willingness to accept greatly exceed measures of willingness to pay." (Kahneman, Knetsch & Thaler, [1990](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_et_al_1990_Experimental_tests.pdf), abstract)

The paper's own summary of the naming puts the label on the record:

> "Thaler (1980) labeled the increased value of a good to an individual when the good becomes part of the individual's endowment the "endowment effect."" ([1990](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_et_al_1990_Experimental_tests.pdf), opening pages)

The paper classifies the effect as a manifestation of loss aversion, the generalization that losses are weighted substantially more than objectively commensurate gains ([1990](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_et_al_1990_Experimental_tests.pdf), opening pages).

The mug experiment made the point with objects. College students were given coffee mugs; owners and non-owners then stated prices. "The median selling price of $5.75 was over twice the median buying price of $2.25, and the means were $5.78 and $2.21, respectively" ([1990](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_et_al_1990_Experimental_tests.pdf)). What differed between owners and buyers was possession alone. Nor did the gap shrink away with market experience: "the findings support an alternative view of endowment effects and loss aversion as fundamental characteristics of preferences" ([1990](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_et_al_1990_Experimental_tests.pdf)).
