The aha moment is the colloquial name for insight, and Kounios and Beeman's 2014 review gives the field's definition:

> "Insight occurs when a person suddenly reinterprets a stimulus, situation, or event to produce a nonobvious, nondominant interpretation."

([Kounios and Beeman 2014, The Cognitive Neuroscience of Insight, Annual Review of Psychology 65, Northwestern preprint](https://psychology.northwestern.edu/people/faculty/core/profiles/ann_rvw_psy_2014.pdf).) Their fuller statement: "we define insight as any sudden comprehension, realization, or problem solution that involves a reorganization of the elements of a person's mental representation of a stimulus, situation, or event to yield a nonobvious or nondominant interpretation" ([same](https://psychology.northwestern.edu/people/faculty/core/profiles/ann_rvw_psy_2014.pdf)).

### The suddenness is real and prepared

The review's own point about the timing: "Insight is sudden, but it is preceded by substantial unconscious processing" ([same](https://psychology.northwestern.edu/people/faculty/core/profiles/ann_rvw_psy_2014.pdf)). The EEG signature makes this visible: at the moment of an insight solution, gamma-band activity bursts over the right temporal lobe, and immediately before that, a burst of alpha-band activity over right occipital cortex — a "brain blink" that suppresses visual input so the weak solution can surface ([same](https://psychology.northwestern.edu/people/faculty/core/profiles/ann_rvw_psy_2014.pdf)). As the authors put it in the 2009 version, "the experience of insight is sudden and can seem disconnected from the immediately preceding thought," but it is "the culmination of a series of brain states and processes operating at different time scales" ([Kounios and Beeman 2009, PDF at bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.northwestern.edu/dist/a/699/files/2015/11/Aha-The-cognitive-neuroscience-of-insight-2895xqe.pdf)).

### The honest limits

The PMC review of the field notes the two caveats I hold: "a successful restructuring does not necessarily imply a correct conceptualization of the full nature of the problem, and an answer obtained by insight need not necessarily be correct" ([Current Understanding of the Insight Phenomenon, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8715918/)). The aha is a felt restructuring, and the feeling is evidence of the restructuring, not of the truth of what arrived.
