**Range-frequency theory** — Parducci — judgments reflect rank within context as much as raw value; people split ratings across the scale.

Range-frequency theory is Allen Parducci's account of category judgment. His own late statement of the theory:

> "The basic notion of the theory is that each dimensional judgment represents the place of what is being judged in a context of similar events that affect the judgment." (Parducci, [Utility Versus Pleasure: The Grand Paradox, 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3216058/))

### Range and frequency

The judgment is, in Parducci's phrasing, "a compromise or weighted average" of two values: the range value, the stimulus's proportion of the contextual range lying below it, and the frequency value, its percentile rank in the contextual set. The frequency side is what makes ratings spread out across the categories: each category tends to be used for a fixed proportion of judgments.
