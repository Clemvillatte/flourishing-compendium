The body scan is the first formal practice taught in mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR), the eight-week course Jon Kabat-Zinn developed at the University of Massachusetts Medical Center from 1979 ([Kabat-Zinn 2003](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1093/clipsy.bpg016)). The practitioner lies on the back and moves attention slowly through the body region by region, starting with the toes of the left foot, feeling whatever sensations are present or noticing their absence ([Full Catastrophe Living, ch. 5](https://osher.ucsf.edu/sites/osher.ucsf.edu/files/inline-files/Full-Catastrophe-Living.pdf)).

### The practice

Kabat-Zinn's instructions ask for about 45 minutes a day, six days a week, in the first two weeks ([Full Catastrophe Living, ch. 5](https://osher.ucsf.edu/sites/osher.ucsf.edu/files/inline-files/Full-Catastrophe-Living.pdf)). Attention rests in each region while the breath is imagined moving into and out of it; where nothing is felt, the absence is taken as that region's experience ([Full Catastrophe Living, ch. 5](https://osher.ucsf.edu/sites/osher.ucsf.edu/files/inline-files/Full-Catastrophe-Living.pdf)). The stated aim is to develop concentration and flexibility of attention simultaneously and to reestablish contact with the body, not to force relaxation or remove tension ([Full Catastrophe Living, ch. 5](https://osher.ucsf.edu/sites/osher.ucsf.edu/files/inline-files/Full-Catastrophe-Living.pdf)). Falling asleep is a common initial difficulty; the intention is to "fall awake" ([Full Catastrophe Living, ch. 5](https://osher.ucsf.edu/sites/osher.ucsf.edu/files/inline-files/Full-Catastrophe-Living.pdf)).

### The chronic pain program

The practice's clinical career began with chronic pain. Kabat-Zinn's 1982 report described a 10-week Stress Reduction and Relaxation Program for 51 chronic pain patients who had not improved with traditional medical care ([Kabat-Zinn 1982](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7042457/)). The meditation trained an attentional stance the paper calls detached observation of proprioception, which appeared to "uncouple" the sensory dimension of the pain experience from the affective and evaluative alarm reaction ([Kabat-Zinn 1982](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7042457/)). At 10 weeks, 65 percent of the patients showed a reduction of at least 33 percent on the Pain Rating Index and 50 percent a reduction of at least 50 percent ([Kabat-Zinn 1982](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7042457/)). The study was uncontrolled, as the paper itself notes ([Kabat-Zinn 1982](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7042457/)).

### Evidence and limits

A 2025 meta-analysis of 29 randomized controlled trials with 2,191 participants found that mindfulness training improved self-reported interoception with a small-to-medium effect (Hedges' g = 0.31) ([Treves et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-22661-4)). On objective measures the picture is weaker: earlier meta-analytic work found small, heterogeneous effects with no differences on proximal measures such as heartbeat perception ([Treves et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-22661-4)). The body scan sits inside this pattern. Increased bodily awareness is not automatically calming: some practitioners find the scan relaxing, others uncomfortable, and a non-evaluative attentional style appears to matter for which it is ([Gibson 2019](https://www.frontiersin.org/article/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02012/full)).

### In the compendium

BI054 "Learn body scan meditation: systematic body awareness" carries the practice and adds the compendium's own note that boredom is part of the training. AT050 "Learn body scan: systematic attention through the body" places it among attention practices, and AF005 and BI053 connect it to interoception. B011 "Make mindfulness the master tool (sati)" names the underlying faculty and B043 "Develop insight (vipassana)" the Buddhist practice whose systematic survey of sensation it resembles, a comparison rather than a lineage claim. D053 "Balance alertness and ease" and P139 "Know that cognition is embodied sense-making" round out the compendium's uses.
