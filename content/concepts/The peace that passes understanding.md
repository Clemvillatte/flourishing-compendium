The compendium's heading "the peace that passes understanding" refers to Philippians 4:7. The King James Version describes the peace of God as passing all understanding and keeping hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

### The verse

> "And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus." (Philippians 4:7, KJV, [Biblehub](https://www.biblehub.com/philippians/4-7.htm))
