## Cited in philosophy parallels

- [[E109 — Practise the overman (Übermensch)]]

Nietzsche's Übermensch: the figure who, facing the death of God and [[Nihilism]], creates values rather than receiving them — "a rope stretched between animal and man." Not a biological type but a task of self-overcoming ([Wikipedia Übermensch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Cbermensch)).
