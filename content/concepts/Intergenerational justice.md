Jonas's ground for intergenerational justice is that modern action reaches further than the ethics built for it. His essay states the new imperative directly:

> "An imperative responding to the new type of human action and addressed to the new type of agency that operates it might run thus: 'Act so that the effects of your action are compatible with the permanence of genuine human life'; or expressed negatively: 'Act so that the effects of your action are not destructive of the future possibility of such life'; or simply: 'Do not compromise the conditions for an indefinite continuation of humanity on earth'; or most generally: 'In your present choices, include the future wholeness of Man among the objects of your will.'" (Jonas, "Technology and Responsibility: Reflections on the New Tasks of Ethics", [inters.org](https://inters.org/jonas-technology-responsability); the essay was later incorporated into The Imperative of Responsibility)

Two features make it a justice between generations rather than a general benevolence, on the long horizon that [[Future time perspective]] describes. First, the duty is asymmetric: "I can will the present good with sacrifice of the future good" without contradiction, so nothing in ordinary reasoning stops us, and the imperative has to do the stopping. Second, the duty's holder is the collective: "the new imperative addresses itself to public policy rather than private conduct, which is not in the causal dimension to which that imperative applies." The people owed the duty cannot be present to claim it, which is the structural reason it must be carried by policy and by a duty-shaped will rather than by contract, and why Jonas spends the surrounding pages on a predictive science of long-range effects as a technical precondition of any such duty being dischargeable at all.

**Plain words** Ethics used to assume your actions died with your neighbours. Once actions can reach generations ahead, a new rule is needed: treat the indefinite survival of genuine human life as something you are holding, and include the people who come after you among the people you are acting for.
