In the Zhuangzi's sixth chapter, the great Mass of nature is the body's support through the stages of a life: toil in youth, ease in old age, rest at death. ([Zhuangzi, ch. 6, Legge translation](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

> "There is the great Mass (of nature) - I find the support of my body on it; my life is spent in toil on it; my old age seeks ease on it; at death I find rest in it - what makes my life a good makes my death also a good." (Zhuangzi, chapter 6, section 2, Legge translation, [ETH Zurich PDF](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

A later section of the chapter returns to the image through the dying Zi-lai: rest in what the time requires, and neither joy nor sorrow can find entrance. The chapter closes the scene with birth and death as one sleep: "We are born as from a quiet sleep, and we die to a calm awaking." ([Zhuangzi, ch. 6, Legge translation](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))
