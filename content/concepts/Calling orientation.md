Calling orientation is one of the three orientations to work distinguished by Wrzesniewski, McCauley, Rozin and Schwartz (1997): seeing work as a Calling, focused on the enjoyment of fulfilling, socially useful work, alongside seeing it as a Job (focused on financial rewards and necessity) or a Career (focused on advancement) ([Wrzesniewski et al. 1997, Journal of Research in Personality 31(1):21-33, abstract](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Jobs,-Careers,-and-Callings:-People's-Relations-to-Wrzesniewski-McCauley/eb983573d298ba1b837e5fb2a2867b51b464e8ff)). In the study, employees at two work sites (n=196) with a wide range of occupations from clerical to professional were unambiguous in seeing their work primarily in terms of one of the three orientations ([Wrzesniewski et al. 1997, abstract](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Jobs,-Careers,-and-Callings:-People's-Relations-to-Wrzesniewski-McCauley/eb983573d298ba1b837e5fb2a2867b51b464e8ff)).

> "We present evidence suggesting that most people see their work as either a Job (focus on financial rewards and necessity rather than pleasure or fulfillment; not a major positive part of life), a Career (focus on advancement), or a Calling (focus on enjoyment of fulfilling, socially useful work)." — [Wrzesniewski, McCauley, Rozin & Schwartz 1997, abstract](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Jobs,-Careers,-and-Callings:-People's-Relations-to-Wrzesniewski-McCauley/eb983573d298ba1b837e5fb2a2867b51b464e8ff)

### Not the occupation

The orientations did not reduce to demographics or job type: a homogeneous subset of 24 college administrative assistants were, like the total sample, distributed evenly across Job, Career and Calling ([Wrzesniewski et al. 1997, abstract](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Jobs,-Careers,-and-Callings:-People's-Relations-to-Wrzesniewski-McCauley/eb983573d298ba1b837e5fb2a2867b51b464e8ff)). The same work can be a job to one person and a calling to another ([Wrzesniewski et al. 1997, abstract](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Jobs,-Careers,-and-Callings:-People's-Relations-to-Wrzesniewski-McCauley/eb983573d298ba1b837e5fb2a2867b51b464e8ff)).
