The compendium uses Following nature's rhythms as an editorial label connecting Daoist language with its pages on daily routines. Daodejing 25 describes human beings taking their law from Earth, Earth from Heaven, and Heaven from the Tao.

> "Man takes his law from the Earth; the Earth takes its law from Heaven; Heaven takes its law from the Tao. The law of the Tao is its being what it is." (Daodejing 25, [Legge translation, Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm))

### The compendium's application

The connection with light exposure, sleep and exercise timing is the compendium's application. Chapter 25 does not itself recommend schedules or establish findings about circadian physiology. The same chapter does describe a movement of flow and return, which the label draws on: "Great, it passes on (in constant flow). Passing on, it becomes remote. Having become remote, it returns."
