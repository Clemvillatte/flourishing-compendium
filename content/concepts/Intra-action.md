Barad's coinage, from Meeting the Universe Halfway, is designed to replace one assumption in the word "interaction":

> "The notion of intra-action is a key element of my agential realist framework. The neologism 'intra-action' signifies the mutual constitution of entangled agencies. That is, in contrast to the usual 'interaction,' which assumes that there are separate individual agencies that precede their interaction, the notion of intra-action recognizes that distinct agencies do not precede, but rather emerge through, their intra-action. It is important to note that the 'distinct' agencies are only distinct in a relational, not an absolute, sense, that is, agencies are only distinct in relation to their mutual entanglement; they don't exist as individual elements." (Barad 2007, Meeting the Universe Halfway, p. 33, [as quoted in Herskin 2012](https://tidsskrift.dk/KKF/article/download/28152/24779/65404))

The book's preface states the human-scale consequence plainly: "Individuals do not preexist their interactions; rather, individuals emerge through and as part of their entangled intra-relating" (Barad 2007, p. xi). Her worked case is physical: in measurement, the object and the measuring agencies emerge from the intra-action that produces them, rather than preceding it.

For the compendium the payoff is grammatical before it is metaphysical, and it lands on practice: if the parties are made by the relation, then the question of what you bring to your relationships is already the wrong order of events. If the parties are made by the relation, then asking what a person brings to their relationships already assumes the wrong order of events, and P147's notice — that you and the world co-constitute each other — is the observation intra-action names.

**Plain words** "Interaction" pictures two finished things bumping. "Intra-action" says there were no two finished things: the bump is what finishes them. You and what you are dealing with make each other in the dealing.
