“Being-with (Mitsein)” — Heidegger, Being and Time.

Being-with (Mitsein) is Heidegger's term for the ontological characteristic of the human being. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy's entry on Heidegger summarises the reasoning:

> "Because my response to the world is intrinsically shaped by relations to others, Heidegger argues that 'being-with' is an ontological structure of being-in-the-world." (Wrathall, [SEP: Martin Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/))

### What the term means

The encyclopaedia article on Heideggerian terminology gives the definition:

> "The term 'Being-with' refers to an ontological characteristic of the human being, that it is always already with others of its kind." ([Wikipedia, Heideggerian terminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heideggerian_terminology))

The same article clarifies that the claim is not a statement about spatial proximity:

> "This assertion is to be understood not as a factual statement about an individual, that they are at the moment in spatial proximity to one or more other individuals, but rather a statement about the being of every human, that in the structures of its being-in-the-world one finds an implicit reference to other humans, as one could not live without others." ([Wikipedia, Heideggerian terminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heideggerian_terminology))

### Authentic and inauthentic

The same article records the distinction Heidegger draws:

> "Heidegger, from his phenomenological perspective, calls this feature of human life 'Being-with' (Mitsein), and says it is essential to being human, classifying it as inauthentic when a person fails to recognize how much, and in what ways, someone thinks of themself, and how they habitually behave as influenced by our social surroundings." ([Wikipedia, Heideggerian terminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heideggerian_terminology))

> "Heidegger classifies it as authentic when someone pays attention to that influence and decides independently whether to go along with it or not." ([Wikipedia, Heideggerian terminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heideggerian_terminology))

### Why the structure matters for practice

Because being-with is a structure rather than a choice, the practice cannot be to become related, only to notice how the relations are already running. The authentic mode the article describes is attention plus decision, which is what the kernels below train: the noticing of how the crowd's reading has become one's own, and the independent ruling on whether to keep it. The inauthentic mode is not villainy but inattention, which is why the practice is a listening exercise rather than a rebellion. The reward of the exercise is not detachment from others, since the structure guarantees that others are always part of the equipment through which the world shows up.
