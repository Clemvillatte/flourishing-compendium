Acedia is the state of listless apathy the desert monk Evagrius Ponticus placed sixth among the eight evil thoughts. His Praktikos gives the classic description:

> "The demon of acedia, which is also called the noonday demon, is the most burdensome of all the demons. It besets the monk at about the fourth hour (10 am) of the morning, encircling his soul until about the eighth hour (2 pm)." (Evagrius Ponticus, Praktikos 12, translated by Luke Dysinger, [Guide to Evagrius Ponticus](https://evagriusponticus.net/cpg2430/cpg2430.eng.1990.dysinger.html))

The chapter's symptoms read as a psychology of restlessness: the sun seems to stop, the monk keeps looking out of the window, hates his place and his work, and the demon "employs, as they say, every [possible] means to move the monk to abandon his cell and give up the race" ([Praktikos 12, Dysinger translation](https://evagriusponticus.net/cpg2430/cpg2430.eng.1990.dysinger.html)).
