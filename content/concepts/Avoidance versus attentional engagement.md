Avoidance versus attentional engagement names the behavioural activation (BA) model of what keeps depression going: withdrawal brings short-term relief while slowly removing the experiences that could counter the low mood ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). The learning-theory version comes from Ferster's 1973 model of depression, the clinical tradition from Martell, Addis and Jacobson onward ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9); [Jenness et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12978239/)). BA addresses behavioural avoidance; the compendium extends the comparison to attentional habits, related but not interchangeable senses.

### The withdrawal cycle

Ferster's model holds that when people become depressed, many of their activities function as avoidance or escape from aversive thoughts, feelings or situations ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). The person develops a narrow repertoire of passive behaviour, engages less in satisfying activities, and obtains less positive reinforcement ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). Withdrawal from normal activities brings deeper depression, more rumination, and missed satisfying experiences ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). Examples include avoiding friends, staying in bed, and distraction through television or games ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)).

### What engagement does

Therapist and client schedule specific activities at specific times and act according to plan rather than waiting to feel motivated ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). BA examines whether rumination functions as avoidance rather than a thinking problem to argue with ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). Veale reports Jacobson and colleagues' 1996 study found no statistically or clinically significant differences between activation-only and fuller cognitive-therapy conditions ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)).

### Evidence and limits

A 2014 meta-analysis of 26 randomised trials (1,524 participants) found BA superior to control conditions at post-treatment (SMD -0.74 across 25 studies and 1,088 participants), with a medium effect at six to nine months (-0.35 across 5 studies and 273 participants) ([Ekers et al. 2014](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0100100)). The apparent superiority over medication (-0.42 across four studies) became non-significant once low-quality studies were removed, and most studies were small, of low quality, with short follow-ups ([Ekers et al. 2014](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0100100)). A 2020 Cochrane review of 53 studies found BA may be better than usual care, no evidence of an efficacy difference from CBT, probably lower acceptability than usual care, and mostly short-term findings with limited confidence ([Cochrane summary 2020](https://www.cochrane.org/about-us/news/featured-reviews-behavioural-activation-therapy-depression)). These trials evaluate BA as a treatment; they do not establish an antidepressant effect of attentional engagement alone.

### In the compendium

BE014 "Learn to use activity scheduling: plan specific activities at specific times" is the BA practice itself, countering avoidance with calendar entries. AF057 "Work with boredom: it is a signal, not a void to fill" names the attentional version, where the reflexive scroll replaces contact with the signal. SO060 applies the contrast to digital environments shaped by design for capture.

K017 "Find the still point that is not a withdrawal" frames a test in the compendium's own terms: measure letting go by whether engagements become more available afterward. AT046 "Cultivate digital sabbath" draws the boundary from the other side: deliberate disconnection is recovery, not avoidance. AF027 marks another boundary: for genuine harm, the adaptive move is action, not reframing.
