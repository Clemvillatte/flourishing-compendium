Avoidance versus attentional engagement names the behavioural activation (BA) account of what keeps depression going: withdrawal from activities and people brings short-term relief while slowly removing the experiences that could counter the low mood ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). The learning-theory version comes from Ferster's 1973 model of depression, the clinical tradition from Martell, Addis and Jacobson onward ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9); [Jenness et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12978239/)).

### The withdrawal cycle

Ferster's model holds that when people become depressed, many of their activities function as avoidance or escape from aversive thoughts, feelings or situations ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). The person develops a narrow repertoire of passive behaviour, engages less in satisfying activities, and obtains less positive reinforcement ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). The consequences feed the cycle: withdrawal from normal activities and social interaction brings deeper depression, more rumination, and missed experiences that normally bring satisfaction ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). Examples include not answering the telephone, avoiding friends, staying around the house, spending excessive time in bed, and distraction through television or games ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)).

### What engagement does

Behavioural activation works from the other end. Therapist and client identify avoided activities and valued directions, schedule specific activities at specific times, and act according to plan rather than waiting to feel motivated ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). Rumination is analysed as another form of avoidance rather than a thinking problem to argue with ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). Jacobson and colleagues' 1996 component analysis found the activation component alone matched the full cognitive therapy package across 150 participants ([Veale 2008](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-psychiatric-treatment/article/behavioural-activation-for-depression/EBC5FC0D9D550FFBE6F982B33BF91EE9)). Current formulations treat avoidance as the primary mechanism for onset and maintenance and replace it with approach-focused goal setting ([Jenness et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12978239/)).

### Evidence and limits

A 2014 meta-analysis of 26 randomised trials (1,524 participants) found behavioural activation superior to control conditions at post-treatment (SMD -0.74), with a medium effect at six to nine months (-0.35) ([Ekers et al. 2014](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0100100)). The apparent superiority over antidepressant medication (-0.42 across four studies) became non-significant once low-quality studies were removed, and the authors describe most studies as small and of low quality with short follow-ups ([Ekers et al. 2014](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0100100)). A 2020 Cochrane review of 53 studies found behavioural activation may be better than usual care and no different from CBT, and noted it was probably less acceptable than usual care, with mostly short-term findings and limited confidence in the evidence ([Cochrane summary 2020](https://www.cochrane.org/about-us/news/featured-reviews-behavioural-activation-therapy-depression)).

### In the compendium

BE014 "Learn to use activity scheduling: plan specific activities at specific times" is the BA practice itself, countering avoidance with calendar entries. K017 "Find the still point that is not a withdrawal" carries the Kyoto School's test: measure a practice of letting go by whether engagements become more available afterward. AF057 "Work with boredom: it is a signal, not a void to fill" names the attentional version, where the reflexive scroll replaces contact with the signal. SO060 and AT046 apply the contrast to digital environments, where feeds are engineered for capture and deliberate disconnection counters it. AF027 marks the boundary: for genuine harm, the adaptive move is action, not reframing.
