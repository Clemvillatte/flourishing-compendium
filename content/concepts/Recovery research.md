“Recovery research” — Sonnentag, 2018.

Sabine Sonnentag's research program asks how people unwind from work stress, and its centerpiece is an uncomfortable finding she named the recovery paradox.

> "Although the experience of job stressors calls for effective recovery processes, empirical research shows that recovery processes actually are impaired when job stressors are high (recovery paradox)." (Sonnentag 2018, [paper PDF, University of Helsinki](https://www.helsinki.fi/assets/drupal/2024-04/Sonnentag.pdf))

The core recovery experience she studies is detachment. She defines it with a borrowed phrase, "an individual's sense of being away from the work situation" (Etzion, Eden, and Lapidot 1998, p. 579), and reads it as mentally disconnecting from the job during nonwork time, beyond merely leaving the tasks aside (Sonnentag 2018, [paper PDF, University of Helsinki](https://www.helsinki.fi/assets/drupal/2024-04/Sonnentag.pdf)).
