Context sensitivity is one of the four evolutionary principles the Extended Evolutionary Meta-Model applies to every dimension and level of change processes: behaviour is shaped by the context that selects it, so noticing context is the precondition of changing behaviour. The PBT manual defines it clinically:

> "Increasing a client’s context sensitivity means making them more aware of the shifting demands of their context so they can flexibly adjust their response to it in an adaptive way." (Hofmann, Hayes & Lorscheid, 2021, Learning Process-Based Therapy, Chapter 7)

The principle has an ancestor in functional analysis: behaviour makes sense relative to the situation that occasions and reinforces it. PBT generalises that insight into a whole-grid principle: a stuck pattern can be maintained by an unexamined context, and the definition above makes awareness of that context the clinical target.
