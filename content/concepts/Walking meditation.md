Walking meditation brings deliberate awareness to walking itself: the contact of feet with the ground, the movement of the body and the flow of breathing ([Plum Village, Walking Meditation](https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/mindfulness-practice)). In Thich Nhat Hanh's tradition, the practice invites a leisurely pace and enjoyment of each step ([Plum Village](https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/mindfulness-practice)).

> "Walking in meditation means to walk in such a way that we know we are walking." (Plum Village, The Art of Mindful Living, [Walking Meditation](https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/mindfulness-practice).)

### Steps and breathing

Plum Village suggests taking two or three steps during an in-breath, then three or four during an out-breath; the rhythm depends on the person's lungs and natural pace ([Walking Meditation](https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/mindfulness-practice)). Its instructions widen attention from feet and breath to the whole walking body and surrounding sounds and sights ([Walking Meditation](https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/mindfulness-practice)). These are contemplative instructions, not clinical outcome evidence.

### A canonical counterpart

In MN 10, awareness of walking appears among the bodily postures a monk knows while practising mindfulness of the body ([Satipatthana Sutta, section A.2, translated by Thanissaro Bhikkhu](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.010.than.html)). That passage gives a canonical counterpart to the modern instructions, without specifying Plum Village's breathing-to-step counts ([MN 10, A.2](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.010.than.html)).

## Related concepts

- [[Mindful attention]]: awareness of the activity while it is happening ([Plum Village, Walking Meditation](https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/mindfulness-practice)).
- [[Anapanasati (mindfulness of breathing)]]: the breath awareness that accompanies the steps in Plum Village's instructions ([Mindful Breathing and Walking Meditation](https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/mindfulness-practice)).
