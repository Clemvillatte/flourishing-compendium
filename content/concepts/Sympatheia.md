Sympatheia is the Stoic doctrine of cosmic interdependence, the one [[T014 — Marcus Aurelius|Marcus Aurelius]] gives his most direct instruction to keep in mind. In Meditations 6.38, George Long's translation:

> "Frequently consider the connexion of all things in the universe and their relation to one another. For in a manner all things are implicated with one another, and all in this way are friendly to one another; for one thing comes in order after another, and this is by virtue of the active movement and mutual conspiration and the unity of the substance."

([Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 6.38, trans. George Long, MIT Internet Classics](https://classics.mit.edu/Antoninus/meditations.6.six.html).)

### The doctrine's reach

The concept is not an image of a web as decoration; it is the cosmology. The readaurelius academic edition of 4.40 notes the doctrine behind it: the world as "a single organism," "one substance, one world-soul, one sentience, one impulse," with all things as "joint-causes of all that comes to be," and names the weave "συμπάθεια: no thing is isolated, every event is woven into the common fabric" ([readaurelius.org, Meditations 4.40](https://readaurelius.org/passage/4/40)). Marcus's own statement of the unity: "there is both one Universe, made up of all things, and one Substance, and one Law, one Reason common to all intelligent creatures, and one Truth" ([Meditations 7.9, quoted in the HAL paper on Others as matter of indifference](https://shs.hal.science/halshs-01559067/document)).

### What it grounds

The social corollary is Marcus's standard move from the unity to the duty. He gives it in the imperative register throughout: "We are made for co-operation, like feet, like hands, like eyelids, like the rows of the upper and lower teeth. To act against one another then is contrary to nature" ([Meditations 2.1, Genius annotated text](https://genius.com/Marcus-aurelius-meditations-book-2-annotated)). The HAL analysis makes the same point in the scholarly register: reason is "the permanent link between individuals that makes humanity possible," and the interaction of individuals is always "an expression of the reasonableness and sociability" ([same](https://shs.hal.science/halshs-01559067/document)). My principle on acting as a member of the whole rests on this ground, and the modernized Daily Stoic rendering of the 6.38 instruction gives its use in a sentence: "Meditate often on the interconnectedness and mutual interdependence of all things in the universe" ([Daily Stoic](https://www.dailystoic.com/what-is-sympatheia/)).
