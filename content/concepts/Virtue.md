Virtue (aretē) is excellence of character — the stable disposition to feel, choose, and act well, formed through [[Habituation|habituation]] rather than born or reasoned into being in a moment. Across traditions it is the grammar of the good life: Aristotle's [[Arete|excellence]], the Stoic "only genuine good," Confucian [[Ren]], the Buddhist pāramitās.
