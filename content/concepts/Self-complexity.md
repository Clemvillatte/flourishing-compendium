Self-complexity is Patricia Linville's measure of how many distinct aspects a person's self-concept holds, and how separate those aspects are. Her 1987 study in the Journal of Personality and Social Psychology proposed that a complex self-concept works as a cognitive buffer: when life goes badly in one aspect, the damage stays contained. Her abstract gives the definition in one line:

> "greater self-complexity involves representing the self in terms of a greater number of cognitive self-aspects and maintaining greater distinctions among self-aspects." (Linville, [Self-complexity as a cognitive buffer against stress-related illness and depression](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3572732/), JPSP 52(4), 1987)

### The buffer and its limits

In the study itself, students completed measures of stress, self-complexity, depression and illness in two sessions two weeks apart, and the Stress X Self-Complexity interaction "provided strong support for the buffering hypothesis": subjects higher in self-complexity "were less prone to depression, perceived stress, physical symptoms, and occurrence of the flu and other illnesses following high levels of stressful events." The mechanism Linville proposed is containment: a setback to one aspect spreads less when the aspects share fewer attributes.

The finding has aged with qualifications. McConnell's later review of the self-complexity literature describes it as having focused on "the overall complexity of one's self-concept to produce an individual difference variable," and a 2003 replication concluded that "with regard to depressive symptoms, reports of a stress-buffering effect for self-complexity are premature." The compendium takes the practical core, distinct aspects containing setbacks, as a design principle for identity rather than a settled law of mental health.

### In everyday life

Consider a student whose week runs through lecture halls, a part-time job and a band. A failed exam lands on the student aspect, and how far it colors the whole week depends on how separate the band and the job are from it. That is the buffering claim in miniature, and it is also the model's practical handle: a life built of genuinely distinct aspects can take a hit in one of them without the hit becoming the story of the whole.
