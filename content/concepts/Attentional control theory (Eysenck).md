Attentional control theory is Michael Eysenck, Nazanin Derakshan, Rita Santos and Manuel Calvo's 2007 account of what anxiety does to thinking ([Eysenck et al., 2007](https://tu-dresden.de/die_tu_dresden/fakultaeten/fakultaet_mathematik_und_naturwissenschaften/fachrichtung_psychologie/i1/allgpsy/lehre/pruefungsliteratur_KN_2013/Eysenck-2007.pdf), p. 336). It develops Eysenck and Calvo's (1992) processing efficiency theory ([Eysenck et al., 2007](https://tu-dresden.de/die_tu_dresden/fakultaeten/fakultaet_mathematik_und_naturwissenschaften/fachrichtung_psychologie/i1/allgpsy/lehre/pruefungsliteratur_KN_2013/Eysenck-2007.pdf), abstract, p. 336), and its central assumption is stated in the abstract:

> "It is assumed that anxiety impairs efficient functioning of the goal-directed attentional system and increases the extent to which processing is influenced by the stimulus-driven attentional system. In addition to decreasing attentional control, anxiety increases attention to threat-related stimuli." ([Eysenck, Derakshan, Santos and Calvo, 2007](https://tu-dresden.de/die_tu_dresden/fakultaeten/fakultaet_mathematik_und_naturwissenschaften/fachrichtung_psychologie/i1/allgpsy/lehre/pruefungsliteratur_KN_2013/Eysenck-2007.pdf), abstract, p. 336)

### The two systems

The theory builds on Corbetta and Shulman's distinction between "a goal-directed attentional system influenced by expectation, knowledge, and current goals and a stimulus-driven attentional system responding maximally to salient or conspicuous stimuli" ([Eysenck et al., 2007](https://tu-dresden.de/die_tu_dresden/fakultaeten/fakultaet_mathematik_und_naturwissenschaften/fachrichtung_psychologie/i1/allgpsy/lehre/pruefungsliteratur_KN_2013/Eysenck-2007.pdf), p. 338). Anxiety, in this account, disrupts the balance between the two: "It is associated with an increased influence of the stimulus-driven attentional system and a decreased influence of the goal-directed attentional system" ([Eysenck et al., 2007](https://tu-dresden.de/die_tu_dresden/fakultaeten/fakultaet_mathematik_und_naturwissenschaften/fachrichtung_psychologie/i1/allgpsy/lehre/pruefungsliteratur_KN_2013/Eysenck-2007.pdf), p. 338). The practical reading: under anxiety, attention is harder to aim and easier to capture ([Eysenck et al., 2007](https://tu-dresden.de/die_tu_dresden/fakultaeten/fakultaet_mathematik_und_naturwissenschaften/fachrichtung_psychologie/i1/allgpsy/lehre/pruefungsliteratur_KN_2013/Eysenck-2007.pdf), p. 338).

### Efficiency versus effectiveness

The theory's most useful distinction concerns what anxiety costs. "Adverse effects of anxiety on processing efficiency depend on two central executive functions involving attentional control: inhibition and shifting. However, anxiety may not impair performance effectiveness (quality of performance) when it leads to the use of compensatory strategies (e.g., enhanced effort; increased use of processing resources)" ([Eysenck et al., 2007](https://tu-dresden.de/die_tu_dresden/fakultaeten/fakultaet_mathematik_und_naturwissenschaften/fachrichtung_psychologie/i1/allgpsy/lehre/pruefungsliteratur_KN_2013/Eysenck-2007.pdf), abstract, p. 336). The theory allows performance quality to be maintained through enhanced effort or greater use of processing resources ([Eysenck et al., 2007](https://tu-dresden.de/die_tu_dresden/fakultaeten_fakultaet_mathematik_und_naturwissenschaften/fachrichtung_psychologie/i1/allgpsy/lehre/pruefungsliteratur_KN_2013/Eysenck-2007.pdf), abstract, p. 336).
