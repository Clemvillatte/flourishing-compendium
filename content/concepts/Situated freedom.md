Situated freedom names a philosophical position in Sartre's Being and Nothingness and Merleau-Ponty's Phenomenology of Perception: freedom is always exercised somewhere, within concrete circumstances ([Helsinki thesis on early Sartre](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/da456c81-9afa-4efc-a7e6-d3f7c904a3f9/content)).

Sartre's formulation of the paradox:

> "Thus I am absolutely free and absolutely responsible for my situation. But I am never free except in situation." (Sartre, Being and Nothingness, trans. Barnes, 1992, p. 653, quoted in [Helsinki thesis on early Sartre](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/da456c81-9afa-4efc-a7e6-d3f7c904a3f9/content))

The same book states the dependence from both sides: "there is freedom only in a situation, and there is a situation only through freedom." Human-reality "everywhere encounters resistance and obstacles which it has not created, but these resistances and obstacles have meaning only in and through the free choice which human-reality is" (Being and Nothingness, p. 629, quoted in [the same thesis](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/da456c81-9afa-4efc-a7e6-d3f7c904a3f9/content)). The givens are real; their meaning as obstacles comes from what one is trying to do.

Merleau-Ponty pressed the point against Sartre. In [the thesis's account](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/da456c81-9afa-4efc-a7e6-d3f7c904a3f9/content), which quotes Detmer (1986, p. 86) giving a Phenomenology of Perception locator (p. 508), freedom demands that decisions carry forward:

> "The very notion of freedom demands that our decision should plunge into the future, that something should have been done by it" (Merleau-Ponty, Phenomenology of Perception, p. 508, as quoted in Detmer 1986, p. 86, via [the same thesis](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/da456c81-9afa-4efc-a7e6-d3f7c904a3f9/content))

Decisions have consequences for subsequent choices ([the thesis](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/da456c81-9afa-4efc-a7e6-d3f7c904a3f9/content)).
