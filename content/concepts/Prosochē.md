The Stoic discipline of attention — the continuous monitoring of impressions (phantasiai) as they arise, withholding assent until they are tested. It is the practical core of [[The discipline of assent]] and the Stoic counterpart to mindfulness ([Wikipedia Prosoche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prosoche)).
