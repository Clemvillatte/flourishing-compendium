The compendium uses "Discipline of rest" for the monastic practice of regulating rest by rule. The Rule of St. Benedict schedules rest at fixed points in the day:

> "After the sixth hour, having left the table, let them rest on their beds in perfect silence; or if anyone may perhaps want to read, let her read to herself in such a way as not to disturb anyone else." (Rule of St. Benedict, chapter 48, Doyle translation, [OSB archive](https://archive.osb.org/rb/text/rbemjo3.html))

### Sleep arranged by rule

The Rule also regulates sleeping itself: "Let them sleep clothed and girded with belts or cords... and thus be always ready to rise without delay when the signal is given and hasten to be before one another at the Work of God, yet with all gravity and decorum" (Rule of St. Benedict, chapter 22, Doyle translation, [OSB archive](https://archive.osb.org/rb/text/rbefjo3.html), passage abridged). The same chapter specifies separate beds, bedding, a candle burning until morning, and younger members placed among the older ones.
