## In plain words

Chitta-vritti is the "movements of the mind-stuff" of the Yoga Sutras: the ceaseless turnings of thought, image, memory and mood that yoga trains itself to still. The word names the problem the whole system is built to solve.

## Where it comes from

Patanjali's definition of yoga runs through this concept: "Union, spiritual consciousness, is gained through control of the versatile psychic nature" (Yoga Sutra I.2, [Johnston's translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2526/pg2526.txt)). The Sutras then classify what is to be controlled: "The psychic activities are five; they are either subject or not subject to the five hindrances" (I.5), namely "Sound intellection, unsound intellection, predication, sleep, memory" (I.6) ([Johnston's translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2526/pg2526.txt)). The Sanskrit is "yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ": yoga is the restraint of the mind's modifications.

## How it works

The five are given in the source's own terms: sound intellection, unsound intellection, predication, sleep, memory (I.6). The vrittis are not condemned as evil; they are the mind's normal operation, "kinetic, restless, stimulating to new acts": "These pictures do not remain quiescent in the mind; they are kinetic, restless, stimulating to new acts. Thus the mind-image of an indulgence suggests and invites to a new indulgence; the picture of past joy is framed in regrets or hopes" ([Johnston's commentary on I.5](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2526/pg2526.txt)). Yoga's method is restraint, not destruction: the powers "must be extricated, like gems from the matrix, like the pith from the reed, steadily, without destructive violence" ([Johnston's commentary on I.4](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2526/pg2526.txt)).

## What it explains

The concept explains why a settled mind is not a blank mind in this system: stillness is the restraint of turnings, and "then the Seer comes to consciousness in his proper nature" (I.3) ([Johnston's translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2526/pg2526.txt)). It also explains meditation's difficulty: what must be quieted is the mind's own default activity, five kinds deep, including sleep and memory.

## Everyday life

The fivefold list gives a vocabulary for watching the mind, in the source's terms: sound intellection, unsound intellection, predication, sleep, memory.
