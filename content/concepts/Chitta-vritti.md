The "movements of the mind-stuff" — the five fluctuations (valid knowledge, error, imagination, sleep, memory) that yoga stills: "yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ" (Yoga Sūtra 1.2) ([Wikipedia Vritti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vritti)).
