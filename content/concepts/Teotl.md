Teotl is the Nahua concept of a single, sacred, self-generating energy that is the cosmos — "that without which nothing can be" ([IEP Aztec Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/aztec-philosophy/)). The world is teotl's motion; balance ([[Nepantla|in-betweenness]]) and reciprocity with it are the practice.

## Related concepts

- [[M002]] — Nahua metaphysics is processive; teotl is energy-in-motion, so the cosmos is motion (Maffie, IEP).
