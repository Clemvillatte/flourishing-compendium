Care of the self is a theme in Michel Foucault's later studies of ancient ethics. His discussion examines practices through which people form themselves as ethical subjects and their relationship to themselves.

> "In the ancient conception, ethics referred to the practice through which one forms oneself as an ethical subject following the prescriptive elements of morality." ([Gutting and Oksala, "Michel Foucault", section "Sex in the Ancient World"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/foucault/))

Foucault examines this relationship in ancient Greek and Roman sexual ethics, including choices about a style of existence. The SEP entry also emphasizes that people participate in shaping themselves through practices of the self.
