The face — in Levinas, the other person's face is the primary ethical address: vulnerability and command at once, "thou shalt not kill." The encounter precedes theory and grounds responsibility ([Wikipedia Emmanuel Levinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Levinas)).
