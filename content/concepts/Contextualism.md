“Contextualism” — Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct.

Contextualism is a worldview, and its cleanest statement is Stephen Pepper's World Hypotheses (1942), which the Dewey tradition supplies with its favorite example. Pepper's method is to ask what plain, familiar thing each great philosophical system takes the world to be like, its "root metaphor":

> "A man desiring to understand the world looks about for a clue to its comprehension. He pitches upon some area of commonsense fact and tries to understand other areas in terms of this one. This original area becomes his basic analogy or root metaphor." (Pepper, quoted in [Berry 1995, "An introduction to Stephen C. Pepper's philosophical system", Behavior Research Methods 27:447-456](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.3758/BF03333873.pdf))

For mechanism the root metaphor is the machine; for formism, similarity. For contextualism it is the live event, the act as it happens:

> "The real historical event, the event in its actuality, is when it is going on now, the dynamic dramatic active event. We may call it an 'act'... it is an act in and within its setting, an act in its context." (Pepper 1942, p. 232, quoted in the same article)

> "Moreover, such acts or events are all intrinsically complex, composed of interconnected activities with continuously changing patterns. They are like incidents in the plot of a novel or drama. They are literally incidents of life." (Pepper 1942, p. 233, quoted in the same article)

Three commitments follow. Nothing in the world is a static thing to be dissected first and situated later; the unit of understanding is the whole act with its setting. Every event is unique and embedded in a history, so analysis is always for a purpose, never final. And since the event is always changing, the contextualist prizes what works over what is timeless, a truth criterion of successful operation that runs straight back to pragmatism, with James's pragmatism named as the contextualistic system and Dewey's habit-and-situation psychology in Human Nature and Conduct as its working application.

For me the payoff is practical rather than metaphysical: [[P078 — Read action in its context — always]] is the contextualist discipline as a habit of mind, and its psychological descendants [[Context sensitivity]] and [[Functional contextualism]] carry the worldview into research programs.
