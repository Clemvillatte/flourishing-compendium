Nepantla is a Nahuatl word that the sixteenth-century Dominican Diego Durán glossed as "betwixt and between" (en medios) and "neither one nor the other" (neutros) ([Maffie, The Centrality of Nepantla in Conquest-Era Nahua Philosophy](https://nahuanewsletter.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/nahua-no-44.pdf)). Maffie summarizes the process the word names:

> "In sum, nepantla-processes are dialectical, intermediating, transactional, oscillating, betwixting-and-betweening activities, middling, and abundant with mutuality and reciprocity. They suspend people or things in nepantlatli, i.e., in the middle, betwixt and between two endpoints or 'extremities' (Campbell 1985:212). They engage 'fish' and 'fowl' in a transformative process that destroys their original, independent and well-defined status as 'fish' versus 'fowl' while simultaneously creating a tertium quid that is 'neither fish nor fowl.'" (Maffie, [The Centrality of Nepantla in Conquest-Era Nahua Philosophy, Nahua Newsletter 44](https://nahuanewsletter.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/nahua-no-44.pdf))

The same account extends the word to a borderland ([Maffie, The Centrality of Nepantla in Conquest-Era Nahua Philosophy](https://nahuanewsletter.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/nahua-no-44.pdf)):

> "Alternatively, nepantla-processes place people and things within a borderland, i.e., a dynamic zone of mutual transaction, confluence, unstable and diffuse identity, and transformation." (Maffie, [The Centrality of Nepantla in Conquest-Era Nahua Philosophy, Nahua Newsletter 44](https://nahuanewsletter.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/nahua-no-44.pdf))

In Chicana feminist thought (Anzaldúa) it names the borderlands of identity ([Wikipedia Nepantla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepantla)).

The two readings agree on where the interest of the word lies: neither treats being in the middle as a state to be resolved, and both treat it as a place where something is being made. Maffie's tertium quid is the part of the account that does the work, since a process that suspends fish and fowl between endpoints is also a process that produces something which is neither. The borderland reading names the same thing at the level of a person rather than of things: an identity formed in the middle is one whose well-defined status has already been given up.

The practical reading runs against a habit of treating in-between states as provisional. A person between two cultures, two callings or two selves is often read as someone who has not yet decided, and the concept's claim is that the middle is not a waiting room. The mutuality and reciprocity Maffie names are not features of a difficulty being overcome but features of the thing being made, which is why the account does not end with a recommendation to exit the middle.
