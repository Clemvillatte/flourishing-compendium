The Nahua (Aztec) term for the in-between state — the liminal, uncomfortable middle where contradictory realities overlap. In Chicana feminist thought (Anzaldúa) it names the borderlands of identity. The tradition counsels staying in nepantla without prematurely resolving it ([Wikipedia Nepantla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepantla)).
