“Intuition as method” — Bergson, Introduction to Metaphysics.

Bergson's essay "An Introduction to Metaphysics" sets intuition against analysis as two ways of knowing a thing ([An Introduction to Metaphysics, trans. T. E. Hulme, Internet Archive](https://archive.org/stream/anintroductiont00berggoog/anintroductiont00berggoog_djvu.txt)). Analysis moves round an object, taking points of view and using symbols; intuition enters into it ([An Introduction to Metaphysics, trans. T. E. Hulme, Internet Archive](https://archive.org/stream/anintroductiont00berggoog/anintroductiont00berggoog_djvu.txt)). The passage that opens the distinction:

> "The first implies that we move round the object; the second that we enter into it. The first depends on the point of view at which we are placed and on the symbols by which we express ourselves. The second neither depends on a point of view nor relies on any symbol. The first kind of knowledge may be said to stop at the relative; the second, in those cases where it is possible, to attain the absolute." (Bergson, [An Introduction to Metaphysics, trans. T. E. Hulme, Internet Archive](https://archive.org/stream/anintroductiont00berggoog/anintroductiont00berggoog_djvu.txt))

The distinction is not a preference for feeling over reasoning but a claim about what each mode is doing with its object. Analysis is a way of getting at a thing from the outside: it takes positions around the object and translates what is seen into symbols, and the translation is what makes the knowledge portable and relative at the same time. Intuition, as Bergson uses the word, is a way of being with the thing from the inside, and the inside is not available from any of the positions around it.

The method is therefore a discipline rather than a mood. Bergson is describing something that has to be done rather than something that happens to a person, and the contrast with analysis is what makes the discipline visible, since the alternative is a mode a person already knows how to use. The practical reading is that intuition in this sense is not a shortcut around analysis but a different relation to the same thing, and the two are not competing for the same job.
