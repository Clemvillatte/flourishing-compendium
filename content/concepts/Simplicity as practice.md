The Daoist practice of paring life back: Daodejing 19 recommends renouncing cleverness, showy virtue, and scheming for gain, and settling into plain views and plain courses.

> But simple views, and courses plain and true
> Would selfish ends and many lusts eschew.

(Legge's translation of Daodejing 19, closing lines, [Sacred Texts](https://www.sacred-texts.com/tao/taote.htm))

The chapter's opening lines make the renunciations explicit: giving up sageness and wisdom would benefit the people a hundredfold, and giving up benevolence-and-righteousness would let them be filial and kind again. Simplicity here is not deprivation. It is the removal of artful contrivance and scheming for gain, so that what is plain can carry the weight. The compendium's D034 treats it as an ongoing discipline of the ecosystem rather than a one-time purge.
