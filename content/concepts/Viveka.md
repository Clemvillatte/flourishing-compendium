Viveka is discriminative discernment: the power to tell apart what is real from what is not, the self from the not-self, the permanent from the impermanent ([Wikipedia, "Viveka"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viveka)). The term is Sanskrit and Pali:

> "Viveka (Sanskrit: विवेक, romanized: viveka) is a Sanskrit and Pali term translated into English as discernment or discrimination. In the Vedanta, viveka is considered to be the first requirement of the spiritual journey, the next being vairagya (detachment), as a natural extension of viveka." ([Wikipedia, "Viveka"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viveka))

Advaita Vedanta reads viveka as discrimination between the real (satya) and the unreal (asatya) ([Wikipedia, "Viveka"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viveka)).
