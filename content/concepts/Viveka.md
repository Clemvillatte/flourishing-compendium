Viveka is discriminative discernment — the power to distinguish the permanent from the impermanent, the Self from the not-self. In Vedānta it is the first prerequisite for liberation; in Pātañjala Yoga it pairs with [[Vairagya]] (dispassion) as the two wings of practice ([Wikipedia Viveka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viveka)).
