Vairagya is dispassion — not numbness, but the release of grip on outcomes and objects once they are seen as impermanent. Pātañjali names it, with [[Viveka]], as the dual means of steadying the mind ([Wikipedia Vairagya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vairagya)).
