Contentment — the second niyama (observance) of yoga: the trained capacity to be sufficient in what is. It is the Vedantic counterpart to Stoic self-sufficiency and to the Buddhist middle ([Wikipedia Santosha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santosha)).
