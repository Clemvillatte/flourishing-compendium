## Cited in philosophy parallels

- [[E027 — Practise the they (das Man)]]

Heidegger's "the they" — the anonymous public self we drift into when we live by what "one" does, says, and thinks. Authenticity ([[Authenticity|Eigentlichkeit]]) is the retrieval of oneself from this fallenness ([Wikipedia Heideggerian terminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heideggerian_terminology)).
