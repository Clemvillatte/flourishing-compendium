Non-attachment names release from mental fixations in Sahdra, Shaver and Brown's account of a Buddhist notion ([2010, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20155561/)). The Dhammapada gives the teaching in verse form:

> "From attachment springs grief, from attachment springs fear. For one who is wholly free from attachment there is no grief, whence then fear?" (Dhammapada 214, [Buddharakkhita translation, Access to Insight](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/dhp/dhp.16.budd.html))

### A distinct construct

Sahdra, Shaver and Brown distinguish Buddhist nonattachment from secure and insecure interpersonal attachment, while describing the constructs as related. Their account links interpersonal attachment to internal working models of security, and nonattachment to insight into the constructed and impermanent character of mental representations ([2010, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20155561/)). The same construct has a second page at [[Nonattachment]].
