Social constructivist theory holds that knowledge and the self are built through social interaction: development is socially situated, and what one comes to know is constructed with others rather than received ready-made. The Wikipedia article on the theory states it:

> "Social constructivism is a sociological theory of knowledge according to which human development is socially situated, and knowledge is constructed through interaction with others." ([Wikipedia, Social constructivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_constructivism))

### Origin

The article distinguishes social constructivism from social constructionism — constructivism focusing on epistemology, constructionism on ontology — and ties its psychological tools to Lev Vygotsky's sociocultural perspective.

### In everyday life

The article states that social constructivism "states that people work together to actively construct artifacts". Illustrative scenario, not a reported case: a child counting change at a shop counter, talked through the arithmetic by the shopkeeper, is learning arithmetic in the interaction rather than from a page.
