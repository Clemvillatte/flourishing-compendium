The butterfly dream is an episode in the Zhuangzi in which Zhou dreams he is a butterfly and, after waking, questions whether he was Zhou dreaming of being a butterfly or a butterfly dreaming of being Zhou. The episode appears at the end of Inner Chapter 2. ([Zhuangzi, Legge translation](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

> "Formerly, I, Zhuang Zhou, dreamt that I was a butterfly, a butterfly flying about, feeling that it was enjoying itself. I did not know that it was Zhou. Suddenly I awoke, and was myself again, the veritable Zhou. I did not know whether it had formerly been Zhou dreaming that he was a butterfly, or it was now a butterfly dreaming that it was Zhou. But between Zhou and a butterfly there must be a difference. This is a case of what is called the Transformation of Things." (Zhuangzi, Inner Chapter 2, section 14, Legge translation, [ETH Zurich PDF](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

The ending explicitly distinguishes Zhou from the butterfly and calls the episode the Transformation of Things. ([Zhuangzi, Legge translation](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))
