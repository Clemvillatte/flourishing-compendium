Ecological grief is grief felt over ecological loss. The definition, from Cunsolo and Ellis's 2018 paper, as quoted in the review literature:

> "The grief felt in relation to experienced or anticipated ecological losses, including the loss of species, ecosystems and meaningful landscapes due to acute or chronic environmental change." (Cunsolo and Ellis, 2018, p. 275, as quoted in Comtesse et al., [Ecological Grief as a Response to Environmental Change](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7830022/), 2021)

### Where it sits among neighbouring terms

The same review distinguishes it from its neighbours: solastalgia "closely overlaps with ecological grief in terms of triggering conditions, phenomenology and conceptual foundations" and "may be regarded as a sub-concept of ecological grief", while eco-anxiety is future-oriented, so the review expects "few shared phenomenological features" between grief and anxiety.
