Sense-withdrawal — the fifth limb of Pātañjala Yoga: the senses, mastered, withdraw "as a tortoise draws in its limbs." Not suppression but the reclaiming of attention from stimulus ([Wikipedia Pratyahara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratyahara)).
