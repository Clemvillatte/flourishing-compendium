In [Ecclesiastes 3](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8300), the seasons poem puts birth, death, planting, weeping and healing on one list:

> "All things have their season, and in their times all things pass under heaven. A time to be born and a time to die. A time to plant, and a time to pluck up that which is planted. A time to kill, and a time to heal." (Ecclesiastes 3:1-3, Douay-Rheims translation, [Project Gutenberg #8300](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8300))

The compendium applies the poem's pattern to the body: conditions of health and illness come in their times, and what a person needs changes with them ([Ecclesiastes 3](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8300)).
