# Nondual awareness

> "Consciousness is the great underlying fact, the universal witness for whom the world is a field, the senses instruments. To that witness the worlds and their objects appeal for their reality and for the one world or the many, for the physical equally with the supraphysical we have no other evidence that they exist." (Sri Aurobindo, [The Life Divine, ch. III, "The Two Negations — II. The Refusal of the Ascetic"](https://sri-aurobindo.co.in/workings/sa/18-19/the_life_divine_21-22_e.pdf))

Nondual awareness is consciousness that is not divided into a separate observer and an observed world. Where ordinary experience splits reality into "me in here" and "everything out there," nondual traditions describe an awareness in which that seam does not appear: experience is one field, and the witness is not a private self but something like the field itself. The concept is central to Advaita Vedanta, to Aurobindo's integral philosophy, and to contemporary study of contemplative experience.

> "Not organised mind, but that which, calm and eternal, broods equally in the living earth and the living human body and to which mind and senses are dispensable instruments, is the Witness of cosmic existence and its Lord." (Sri Aurobindo, [The Life Divine, ch. III](https://sri-aurobindo.co.in/workings/sa/18-19/the_life_divine_21-22_e.pdf))

## The key point

Aurobindo's claim is precise: the witness is not the individual embodied mind. The mind is an instrument, useful but not the self doing the witnessing. What observes is described as "cosmic Consciousness embracing the universe," present in earth and body alike. Nondual awareness, on this account, is not an exotic altered state but the discovery of what was doing the seeing all along once the "I" that we ordinarily appoint to the post is stood down.

## Plain words

Imagine watching a film and completely forgetting you are in a cinema. That is ordinary life for most of us; we are so inside our story that the storytelling goes unnoticed. Nondual awareness is the moment the lights come up and you notice the screen was never the same thing as the movie. The world still plays, but it is seen as one continuous show rather than a "me" watching an "other."

## For example

A meditator sits with a racing thought. Instead of fighting it, she stops assuming the thinker of the thought and the awareness of the thought are different things. The thought plays like weather in a sky, and the sky, she notices, has no edge. What remains is not blankness; sights and sounds continue, but the seam between observer and observed has dropped out of experience.

## Summary

Nondual awareness names consciousness without the subject–object split. Aurobindo's Life Divine grounds it in the witness that is cosmic rather than personal, not the organised mind but that to which mind and senses are instruments.
