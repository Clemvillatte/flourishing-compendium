“Meekness” — Gospel of Matthew.

The Beatitudes give meekness its paradox: the word that sounds like weakness is set against the whole world's picture of who ends up owning it.

> "Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted. Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth." ([Matthew 5:3-5, KJV, Sacred Texts](https://www.sacred-texts.com/bib/kjv/mat005.htm))

### What the word carried

The Beatitudes set meekness among the dispositions the world does not expect to win, and the entry's crosslinks in this compendium treat it as a live virtue rather than a synonym for weakness: the Daoist principle on yielding in conflict to remain whole is its nearest neighbor.
