The sacred names what is set apart: the register of things, places, and times marked off from ordinary use and treated as carrying more than use. The seed names five sources; this page is carried by the opened Durkheim thread below, and the others (Marcel's mystery of being, Otto's idea of the holy, and the two nature writers) are cited without being opened here.

### Durkheim's ground

Durkheim made the distinction the center of his definition of religion:

> "religion is a unified system of beliefs and practices relative to sacred things, that is to say, things set apart and forbidden." (Durkheim, The Elementary Forms of Religious Life, quoted in [Wikipedia, "Sacred"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred), citing Elementary Forms, p. 47)

In his theory the sacred represented the interests of the group, especially unity, embodied in group symbols; the profane was the sphere of the mundane and individual. What marks the sacred off is not a property things have on their own but a boundary a community draws and keeps.
