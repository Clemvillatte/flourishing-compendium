Temporal discounting is the devaluing of rewards as their receipt moves further into the future. The standard review is Frederick, Loewenstein, and O'Donoghue (2002), whose terminological distinction is the field's own:

> "We use the term time discounting broadly to encompass any reason for caring less about a future consequence, including factors that diminish the expected utility generated by a future consequence, such as uncertainty or changing tastes. We use the term time preference to refer, more specifically, to the preference for immediate utility over delayed utility."

([Frederick, Loewenstein, and O'Donoghue 2002, JEL 40(2), quoted at stafforini.com PDF](https://pdf.stafforini.com/frederick-2002-time-discounting-time.pdf).)

### The hyperbolic pattern

The best-documented anomaly is hyperbolic discounting: the implicit discount rate is lower over longer horizons than shorter ones. The review's summary: "when subjects are asked to compare a smaller-sooner reward to a larger-later reward [...] the implicit discount rate over longer time horizons is lower than the implicit discount rate over shorter time horizons" ([same](https://pdf.stafforini.com/frederick-2002-time-discounting-time.pdf)). The preference-reversal form: someone may prefer $110 in 31 days over $100 in 30 days, and also prefer $100 now over $110 tomorrow ([Kopp copy of the review](https://sorbonne.pierrekopp.com/downloads/2002%20Discounting%20critical%20rveiw.pdf)). The key prediction of hyperbolic discounting is that "where a smaller-sooner reward is preferred over a larger-later reward, adding sufficient delay to both sooner and later options ought to shift preference toward the larger-later reward" ([PMC review of temporal discounting and health behavior](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3950931/)).

### What the discounting is for

The compendium's principles on temporal motivation and commitment devices run on this mechanism. The reason a pre-commitment works is that the preference the commitment is designed to defeat is state-dependent: the future self's choice of the smaller-sooner reward is not an error to be corrected by information but a valuation that shifts with the horizon. The hyperbolic shape is what makes the present self able to bind the future one, since the two selves' rankings disagree in a predictable direction.
