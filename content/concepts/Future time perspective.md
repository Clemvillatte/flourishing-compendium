Future time perspective is the habit of mind that orients attention toward the future: planning, goal setting and the willingness to delay gratification for later rewards. The construct comes from Philip Zimbardo and John Boyd's 1999 paper, which introduced the Zimbardo Time Perspective Inventory and defined temporal orientations as "the manner by which experienced events are encoded, stored, and recalled," shaping "expectations, goals, contingencies, and imaginative scenarios" ([Zimbardo and Boyd, 1999, JPSP 77](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s12144-019-00536-y.pdf)).

### Five orientations, one balance

The ZTPI is a 56-item scale with five subscales: Past-Positive, Past-Negative, Present-Hedonistic, Present-Fatalistic and Future, with internal consistencies from .74 to .82 ([Psychologica Belgica](https://psychologicabelgica.com/articles/10.5334/pb.487)). The Future subscale captures forward thinking and goal setting, "while often neglecting present enjoyment" ([Frontiers in Psychology](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01781/full)). High Future scores predict greater success in goal achievement ([Springer, time perspectives at work](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s12144-019-00536-y.pdf)), and the authors of that review read the orientation as likely to promote prosocial and fewer antisocial work behaviours.

The model's own caveat matters: Zimbardo and Boyd argue that fixation on any single temporal horizon is maladaptive, even the future one, and propose a balanced time perspective, the "mental ability to switch between time perspectives depending on task features, situational considerations, and personal resources rather than be biased toward a specific time perspective" ([Perspectives on Time and Personality, Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jopy.70052)). Balance predicts well-being: a study with adults found the balanced profile positively associated with happiness, positive affect and vitality, with decreased negative affect ([PMC, time perspective and self-esteem](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11748232/)).

### In the compendium

The compendium treats the future orientation as a lever for responsibility across time. R112 practises intergenerational justice and R121 ecological responsibility, both requiring the long horizon the Future subscale measures; BE068 turns the horizon into legacy behaviour, contribution beyond the self; K226's deathbed test works by projecting perspective to the end of life. B133's Great Death and ST121's creative advance stretch the same orientation to its metaphysical limit; ST256's seventh generation is the Haudenosaunee institutional form of it; ST122's Augustine and ST223's Epictetus carry the classical versions; SE007 gives the horizon its self-structure, the self's temporal depth.
