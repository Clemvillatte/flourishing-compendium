**Realistic conflict theory** — Sherif — intergroup hostility arises from competition over scarce resources, not from difference itself.

Realistic conflict theory (also called realistic group conflict theory) is Muzafer Sherif's account of intergroup hostility, demonstrated in the Robbers Cave experiments. An open textbook chapter states the core:

> "This theory holds that prejudice and discrimination result from competition for scarce resources." ([Realistic Group Conflict Theory, Reconciling Divided Nations, BCcampus](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/conflict2contact/chapter/realistic-group-conflict-theory/))

### Compatible and incompatible goals

The same chapter draws the contrast that carries the theory: "If the goals of two groups are similar but compatible then friendly attitudes will characterize the intergroup relations... If, however, the goals of two groups are similar and incompatible, then tensions arise". The word realistic marks the assumption that the conflict tracks real competition, and the studies behind the theory found the hostility reduced when the groups had to work together toward goals neither could reach alone.
