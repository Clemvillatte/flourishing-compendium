Hegel's master–slave insight made social: the self exists only through being recognized by another; struggles for recognition structure love, rights, and solidarity. Contemporary theory (Honneth) grounds social justice in it ([SEP Recognition](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/recognition/)).
