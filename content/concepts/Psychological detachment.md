(Sonnentag and Fritz, 2007).

The Recovery Experience Questionnaire measures how individuals unwind and recuperate from work during leisure time. Psychological detachment from work is the first of four recovery experiences it differentiates, alongside relaxation, mastery and control. In validation studies, the four experiences emerged as distinct factors in calibration and cross-validation samples totaling 930, and in a subsample of 271 the recovery experiences showed moderate relations with job stressors and psychological well-being.

> "Confirmatory factor analyses with a calibration and a cross-validation sample (total N=930) showed that four recovery experiences can be differentiated: psychological detachment from work, relaxation, mastery, and control." (Sonnentag & Fritz, 2007, Journal of Occupational Health Psychology 12(3), 204–221, [abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17638488/))

The authors discuss potential applications of the four-item measures in longitudinal and diary research.
