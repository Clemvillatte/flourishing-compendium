Non-retaliation is [[T014 — Marcus Aurelius|Marcus Aurelius]]'s rule for answering injury: refuse to answer it in kind. The Meditations states it in one line:

> "The best kind of revenge is, not to become like unto them." (Meditations, Book VI, aphorism V, [Long translation, Gutenberg #2680](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2680/pg2680.txt))

The rule works by re-describing the injury rather than returning it. The line carries the whole tactic: to retaliate would be to join the offender in his error, and the only clean answer is to remain what the offender stopped being.

The aphorism's neighbours give the setting the line sits in. Book VI, IV, ends by conceding that people "do wonder at many things, the reasons whereof we cannot comprehend" ([Long translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2680/pg2680.txt)), which is a description of the mind the rule is written for rather than a consolation for it. Book VI, VI, which follows the revenge line, gives the alternative use of the same day: "Let this be thy only joy, and thy only comfort, from one sociable kind action without intermission to pass unto another, God being ever in thy mind" ([Long translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2680/pg2680.txt)). The advice the two aphorisms together supply is not a demand for passivity but a substitution of occupation: the day is to be filled with kind actions rather than with the settling of accounts.

Book II supplies the ground the rule stands on, since the whole passage distinguishes things that make a person better or worse in their own person from things that merely happen to them. "As for life therefore, and death, honour and dishonour, labour and pleasure, riches and poverty, all these things happen unto men indeed, both good and bad, equally; but as things which of themselves are neither good nor bad; because of themselves, neither shameful nor praiseworthy" ([Book II, VIII, Long translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2680/pg2680.txt)). Dishonour is on the list, and the only thing a person's own power extends to is what they do about it.
