The five pillars of practice is this compendium's heading for the conditions under which a practice takes root. Its anchor is Patanjali's Yoga Sutra 1.14, which names three conditions; the expansion to five belongs to the series rather than the sutra.

> "This becomes a firm resting-place, when followed long, persistently, with earnestness." (Patanjali, Yoga Sutras I.14, Charles Johnston's translation, [Sacred Texts Archive](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/ysp/ysp02.htm))

### The three conditions the sutra names

Johnston's rendering gives each condition a word: duration ("followed long"), continuity ("persistently") and attitude ("with earnestness"). The practice in question is abhyasa, which the chapter pairs with dispassion (vairagya), the two together working to still the mind's activity.
