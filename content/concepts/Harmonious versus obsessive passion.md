the compendium's comparison page for Vallerand's two forms of passion (Vallerand et al., 2003).

The distinction turns on how the passionate activity is internalized into identity. In Vallerand's model, passion is a strong inclination toward an activity that people like, that they find important, and in which they invest time and energy. The two forms differ by the internalization process. The granular pages carry the detail:

- [[Harmonious passion]] — the autonomous form: the activity is chosen, stays under the person's control, and coexists with the rest of life
- [[Obsessive passion]] — the controlled form: an uncontrollable urge to engage, with the activity poorly integrated into other life domains

The 2003 paper's abstract states the contrast in outcomes plainly:

> "HP promotes healthy adaptation whereas OP thwarts it by causing negative affect and rigid persistence." (Vallerand et al. 2003, [abstract, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14561128/))
