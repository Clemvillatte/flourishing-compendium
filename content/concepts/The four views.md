The four views are the Diamond Sutra's name for the four ways a mind reifies itself: the views of a self, a person, a being, and a life. The sutra's own formulation, in the British Library's panel translation of section 31:

> "The views of a self, a person, a being, and a life, that the Bhagavān speaks of, are not views of a self, a person, a being, or a life, and are thus called the views of a self, a person, a being, and a life."

([International Dunhuang Programme, text panel 6](https://idp.bl.uk/blog/the-diamond-sutra-on-display-text-panel-6-with-colophon/).)

### The move the sutra makes

The section is titled "Extinction of the Four Views" in some translations: "if someone claims that I teach views of a self, a person, a sentient being, or a life span, what would you say? Has this person understood the meaning of my teaching?" ([Sunnyvale translation, section 31](https://sunnyvale.ctzen.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/diamond-sutra-v1.9.17-201312011.pdf)). The older Conze-style rendering makes the paradox explicit: "That which has been taught by the Tathagata as 'view of self', as a no-view has that been taught by the Tathagata. Therefore is it called 'view of self'" ([Diamond Sutra full text, nyx.net](http://www.nyx.net/~dbachman/texts/Diamond.txt)). The four views are not four errors to be replaced with four truths; they are the mind's default furniture, and the sutra's instruction is to hold the words without buying the ontology. As the Mu Soeng-style translation puts it, one should "not give rise to signs for phenomena" ([Scribd translation](https://www.scribd.com/document/756005528/%E9%87%91%E5%88%9A%E7%BB%8F)).

### Holding them lightly

The practical teaching is a hypothesis discipline rather than a metaphysics lesson. A bodhisattva who "abides in the signs of self, person, sentient being, or life-span... is not a bodhisattva" ([Scribd translation](https://www.scribd.com/document/756005528/%E9%87%91%E5%88%9A%E7%BB%8F)) — the views are usable coordinates that become toxic the moment they are mistaken for the territory. My research-side phrasing of the same move is to hold hypotheses, not positions.
