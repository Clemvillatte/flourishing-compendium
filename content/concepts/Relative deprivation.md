Relative deprivation is the resentment that arises from an unfavorable comparison with what one wants and feels entitled to. A recent study states the working definition:

> "PRD refers to resentment stemming from the belief that one is deprived of desired and deserved outcomes compared to some referent target (Crosby, 1976; Smith, Pettigrew, Pippin, & Bialosiewicz, 2012)." (Callan et al., [Social Class, Personal Relative Deprivation, and Prosociality, 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5641987/))

### The comparison is local

The same article notes that the comparisons behind the feeling are "more local, specific, interpersonal comparisons such as those we make with 'similar others' (e.g., friends, coworkers) because such comparisons provide the most diagnostic information for self-evaluation". The grievance, in other words, tracks the referent, not the objective level of one's outcomes.
