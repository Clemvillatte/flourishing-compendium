The originary choice is Sartre's account, in Being and Nothingness, of what holds a person together: beneath the particular decisions of a life there is one original choice of how to be, from which the particular decisions take their meaning. The grounding claim, as the Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy summarises the doctrine:

> "The individual choice of fundamental project is an original choice (BN, 564). Consequently, an understanding of what it is to be Flaubert for instance, must involve an attempt to decipher his original choice." ([Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/sartre-ex/))

### What the choice is and is not

The original choice is not a decision made at a moment, and that is precisely its structure: "Since that original choice is ontologically prior to value, it follows that it is not 'a deliberate choice. And that is not because it is less conscious or less explicit than a process of deliberation but on the contrary because it is the foundation of any deliberation'" (Sartre, Being and Nothingness, as quoted in [Schmidt, 2020](https://philarchive.org/archive/ALTFAA-3)). The source of the project, as the IEP puts it, "is a spontaneous original choice that depends on the individual's freedom," and it answers the lack of self-identity that the for-itself experiences, taking form as a desire for being (BN, 565). The choice can be revised, since "the modification of the original project is possible at each instant," but the revision is itself made from within the freedom it exercises. My principle E040 makes a practice of it: rather than interrogating each habit separately, ask what whole way of being the habits serve. A compendium exercise drawn from the concept is to take a recurring pattern one resents and ask what self it is a strategy for being. What the concept does not give: a method for reading another's original choice reliably; Sartre's own attempt, existential psychoanalysis, remained a programme more than a working technique.
