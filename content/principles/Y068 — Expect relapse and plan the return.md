> **Principle 68** — Expect relapse and plan the return

The tradition's fine print: in the gaps of practice, other afflictions may arise through the deep habits. Relapse is built into the model, which changes what it means when it happens: not new failure, known terrain. Practically, write a relapse plan while steady (minimum viable practice, first three steps of return), because the relapsing mind cannot be trusted to design one.

(YS 4.27-4.28.)

**Plain words** The tradition's fine print: in the gaps of practice, other afflictions may arise through the deep habits. Relapse is built into the model — which changes what it means when it happens: not new failure, known terrain. Write the relapse plan while steady.

*For example:* Someone writes their own relapse card while calm: "minimum version: 4 minutes, morning, no exceptions" — and when the bad month comes, the card keeps the practice from dying completely. The gap is bridged; nothing is rebuilt from zero.
