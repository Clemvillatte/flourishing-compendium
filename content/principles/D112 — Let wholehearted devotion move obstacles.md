> **Principle 112** — Let wholehearted devotion move obstacles

The Foolish Old Man moved a mountain, and heaven's help arrived: not because the plan was clever, but because the sincerity was total. Depth of commitment does things effort alone cannot.

(Liezi 5.)

**Plain words** The Foolish Old Man moved a mountain because the sincerity was total, not because the plan was clever. Wholeheartedness is a force; ambivalence is a force in reverse.

*For example:* The founder whose obsession attracts the investor, the co-founder, the first customer — sincerity is the business plan that recruits.

- **[[Grit|Grit]]**, passion and perseverance for long-term goals predict achievement (Duckworth et al., 2007)
