> **Principle 46** — Practice fasting of the mind (xinzhai)

Beyond giving up food, give up expectations and mental chatter: listen not with the ears or mind but with qi: a receptive, empty alertness.

(Zhuangzi 4.)

**Plain words** Give up more than food: give up the mental chatter and the expectations. Fasting of the mind is clearing out what you already expect to hear, so something new can actually arrive.

*For example:* In a hard conversation, dropping your rehearsed rebuttal and just listening — you'll hear the actual fear under the words you were too busy countering.
