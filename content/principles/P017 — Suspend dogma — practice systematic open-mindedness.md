> **Principle 17** — Suspend dogma: practice systematic open-mindedness

When beliefs clash, don't rush to verdict: hold the question open, gather experience, let inquiry do its work. Peace of mind bought by dogma is counterfeit.

(Sextus Empiricus, Outlines of Pyrrhonism; Peirce, "The Fixation of Belief," 1877.)

**Plain words** When beliefs clash, don't rush to verdict — hold the question open, gather experience, let inquiry do its work. Peace of mind bought by dogma is counterfeit. The uncomfortable open question is worth more than the comfortable closed one.

*For example:* The hiring decision where both candidates look right — living with the ambiguity for a week beats forcing a verdict on day one. Evidence arrives; dogma doesn't.
