> **Principle 60** — Read the symptoms that ride with obstacles

The obstacles announce themselves somatically: mental distress, despair, tremor in the body, and irregular breathing. The tradition's insight is that the body carries the score before the mind admits it. Practically, treat a tight chest and short breath in the first minutes of a sitting as information about the day, not as failure of the sitting.

(YS 1.31.)

**Plain words** The obstacles announce themselves somatically: mental distress, despair, tremor in the body, irregular breathing. The body carries the score before the mind admits it. Treat a tight chest and short breath in the first hour of practice as a flag, not a coincidence.

*For example:* The meditator who keeps waking at 4 a.m. with shallow breath — the mind says "nothing's wrong," and the body has been filing the report for weeks. The tradition reads symptoms first; the mind's press office comes second.
