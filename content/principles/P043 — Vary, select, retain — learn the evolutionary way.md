> **Principle 43** — Vary, select, retain: learn the evolutionary way

All learning is evolution in miniature: generate variations, let consequences select, retain what works. Generate more variations than you need, and retain only what survives testing.

(Baldwin, Dewey, and the Darwinian pragmatists; formalized in modern process therapy — Hayes et al., 2020.)

**Plain words** All learning is evolution in miniature — generate variations, let consequences select, retain what works. Generate more variations than you need, and retain only what survives testing. Nature's algorithm, running in your kitchen and your career.

*For example:* Trying three different morning routines for a month each — two die, one survives. That's not indecision; that's selection pressure doing your homework.
