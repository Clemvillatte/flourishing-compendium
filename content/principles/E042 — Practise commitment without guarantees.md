> **Principle 434** — Practise commitment without guarantees

Commit to your choices without the guarantee that they are right, without the assurance of success, without the certainty that you will not regret them. Existential commitment is commitment in the face of uncertainty. Do not wait for certainty; it will not come. Act.

(Sartre, 1946/2007, Existentialism Is a Humanism; de Beauvoir, 1947/2018, The Ethics of Ambiguity, Part II; Kierkegaard, 1843/1983, Fear and Trembling.)

**Plain words** Commit without guarantees: without the assurance you're right, the certainty of success, or the promise you won't regret. Existential commitment is commitment in the face of uncertainty. Certainty will not arrive — it isn't late; it isn't coming.

*For example:* The person who can't choose the city, the partner, or the career because "what if it's wrong" — and finally accepts that every path is chosen blindfolded. The commitment they make without guarantees holds better than the waiting ever did, because it's actually a commitment.
