> **Principle 196** — Dwell in the world, joyful in the Way (cư trần lạc đạo)

The Trúc Lâm teaching: you need not leave family, work, or society to practice: live in the world, among its duties, and be joyful in the Way. Practice is compatible with a full life.

(Trần Nhân Tông, Trúc Lâm tradition.)

**Plain words** You need not leave family, work, or society to practice — live in the world, among its duties, and be joyful in the Way. Practice is compatible with a full life. The monastery is optional; the attention is not.

*For example:* The parent practicing while packing lunchboxes — the kitchen, the deadlines, the noise: all of it, the field. Nothing to escape.
