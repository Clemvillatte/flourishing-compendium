> **Principle 196** — Dwell in the world, joyful in the Way (cư trần lạc đạo)

Trieu identifies Trần Nhân Tông as a king, the founder of Trúc Lâm and its first patriarch. The paper reproduces the closing verse of Cư Trần Lạc Đạo Phú, attributed to him.

> Dwelling in the world with the Joy of Practicing the Dharma,
> let it flow with its conditions.
> When hungry then eat! Where tired, so sleep then!
> Treasures in the home, suspending the search,
> Facing situation with no mind! What inquiring Thiền for!
>
> (Trần Nhân Tông, Cư Trần Lạc Đạo Phú, concluding verse; English rendering reproduced in Don Thuong Trieu)

The compendium takes the references to eating and sleeping as a starting point for an everyday exercise: bring attention to an ordinary responsibility rather than reserving practice for time away from it.

(Trần Nhân Tông, Trúc Lâm tradition.)

**Plain words**
Choose one routine task and give it your attention. When you find yourself waiting for a quieter day to practice, try beginning with that task.

*For example:* Imagine packing a child's lunch while thinking ahead to the next task. Return your attention to what you are preparing.
