> **Principle 106** — Let mortality give shape

Because time is finite, choice matters: death is not a morbid fact but the frame that makes each action weighty. Use your finitude: don't flee it.

(James, The Varieties of Religious Experience, 1902; Whitehead, Adventures of Ideas, 1933.)

**Plain words** Because time is finite, choice matters — death is not a morbid fact but the frame that makes each action weighty. Use your finitude; don't flee it. The deadline is what makes the writing happen; the lifespan is the deadline for everything.

*For example:* The diagnosis that reorganizes a calendar in a week — suddenly the pointless meetings are gone and the trip is booked. Death, briefly consulted, turned out to be an excellent secretary.
