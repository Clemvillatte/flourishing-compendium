> **Principle 67** — Engage in aesthetic behaviour: art, music, beauty

Aesthetic behaviour — engaging with art, music, beauty, nature — is associated with positive affect, awe, meaning, and well-being. It is a specific behavioural practice.

**Plain words** Aesthetic behaviour — music, art, beauty, the cared-for detail — is a direct line to positive emotion and awe. Not decoration on life; one of its actual pillars. Put beauty on the schedule like it's medicine, because it is.

*For example:* The commute transformed by one album — same traffic, different world. The beauty wasn't a luxury add-on; it was the difference between a bad hour and a good one.
## Psychology parallels

- **Arts engagement and well-being**, attending arts events and engaging with art is associated with well-being (Davies et al., 2017; Fancourt & Finn, 2019)
- **Awe and aesthetic experience**, aesthetic experiences produce awe and reduce self-focus (Piff et al., 2015; Stellar et al., 2018)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[The sublime|The sublime and beautiful]]** (Burke; Kant, Critique of Judgement)
- **[[Art as revelation|Art as revelation]]** (Heidegger, The Origin of the Work of Art)
- **Beauty as a value** (Plato, Symposium)

## Practice

- Engage in aesthetic behaviour: attend art, listen to music, visit galleries, seek beauty. Aesthetic engagement is a well-being behaviour. Schedule it.
