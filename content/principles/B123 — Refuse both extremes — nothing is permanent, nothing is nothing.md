> **Principle 123** — Refuse both extremes: nothing is permanent, nothing is nothing

Neither eternalism (things last) nor nihilism (nothing matters): things change, and therefore things matter. The middle way of view.

(SN 12.15; Kaccayanagotta Sutta.)

**Plain words** Neither eternalism (things last) nor nihilism (nothing matters): things change, and therefore things matter. The middle way of view. Impermanence isn't the death of meaning — it's the only reason anything can change for the better.

*For example:* The diagnosis is devastating because nothing is permanent — and treatable for the same reason. One fact, read twice.
