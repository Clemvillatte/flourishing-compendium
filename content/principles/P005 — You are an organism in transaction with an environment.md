> **Principle 5** — You are an organism in transaction with an environment

Life is not a self inside a container; it is a continuous transaction between doer and surroundings. Change the environment or the transaction, not just the "inner" self.

(Dewey, Experience and Nature, 1925.)

**Plain words** Life is not a self inside a container — it's a continuous transaction between doer and surroundings. Change the environment or the transaction, not just the 'inner' self. Willpower is the weakest lever; the room is a strong one.

*For example:* Can't stop scrolling in bed? Don't strengthen the self — charge the phone in the kitchen. Same person, different transaction, different outcome.
