> **Principle 473** — Practise the universality of prejudice

Recognise that you always approach the world with prejudices (Vorurteile) — pre-judgements that are not necessarily errors but the conditions of understanding. You cannot think without preconceptions. The task is not to eliminate them but to become aware of them, test them, and let them be revised.

(Gadamer, 1960/2013, Truth and Method, Part II, ch. 1.)

**Plain words** You always approach the world with prejudices — pre-judgements that are not necessarily errors but the conditions of understanding. You cannot think without preconceptions. The task isn't elimination but awareness: know your priors, hold them lightly.

*For example:* Someone meets the new colleague already knowing three facts — wrong ones, it turns out — and the choice isn't "no assumptions" (impossible) but "checking them within the first week." The priors stay; the blindness goes.
