> **Principle 493** — Practise the critique of technology

Recognise the danger of modern technology: not that technology is evil, but that it enframes the world — reduces beings to resources (standing-reserve, Bestand) to be used. The essence of technology is not technological but a mode of revealing. Do not let the technological mode of being dominate your relationship with the world.

(Heidegger, 1954/1977, "The Question Concerning Technology," in The Question Concerning Technology and Other Essays; Heidegger, 1954/1971, "Building Dwelling Thinking," in Poetry, Language, Thought.)

**Plain words** The danger of technology isn't evil but enframing: reducing beings to resources — standing-reserve — to be used. The essence of technology isn't technological; it's a mode of revealing. Don't let the technological mode become the only way things can show up to you.

*For example:* The forest as "timber stock," the river as "hydro capacity," the person as "human resources" — the frame is useful and totalising. The walk where the trees are just trees again isn't sentimentality; it's the recovery of the other mode.
