> **Principle 49** — Use vivid sensory attention as a support

Another classical steadying support: attention fixed on a sensory object, the sutra's "higher sense-experience." The principle is personalization by strength: a mind that cannot yet settle on abstraction settles on the feel of the breath at the nostril or the weight of the body on the seat. Practically, choose the sense channel that holds you most easily, and enter through it.

(YS 1.35.)

**Plain words** Another steadying support: vivid sensory attention — the feel of the breath at the nostril, the weight of the body in the seat. A mind that can't yet settle on abstraction settles on the sensory. Personalize by strength, not by what sounds advanced.

*For example:* The person who failed at mantra practice for a year — and discovers the feel of warm water on the hands at the sink holds their attention perfectly. The support was never the problem; the assignment was.
