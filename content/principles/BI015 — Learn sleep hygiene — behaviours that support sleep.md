> **Principle 15** — Learn sleep hygiene: behaviours that support sleep

Sleep hygiene involves specific behaviours that support good sleep: consistent schedule, dark and cool bedroom, no screens before bed, no caffeine late, limit naps, use bed only for sleep.

**Plain words** Sleep hygiene is boring and works: same schedule, dark cool room, no screens before bed, no late caffeine, bed for sleep only. None of it is exciting; all of it is structural. Treat bedtime like a scheduled appointment with your own foundation.

*For example:* The phone that lives in the kitchen overnight — the single most boring, most effective sleep intervention of the decade. The bedroom became a bedroom again.

- **Stimulus control therapy**, using bed only for sleep and getting up when unable to sleep (Bootzin & Epstein, 2011; Morin et al., 2006)

## Practice

- Practise sleep hygiene: consistent sleep and wake times, dark cool bedroom, no screens 1 hour before bed, no caffeine after noon, use bed only for sleep. If unable to sleep, get up.
