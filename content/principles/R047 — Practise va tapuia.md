> **Principle 309** — Practise va tapuia

Recognise the sacred dimension of the relational space. Va tapuia is the understanding that the space between people is sacred, that it carries a spiritual weight that must be honoured. Do not treat your relationships as merely instrumental or transactional. Recognise their sacred dimension.

(Samoan philosophy; Tuhiwai Smith, 1999, Decolonizing Methodologies; Wendt, 1996.)

**Plain words** The space between people is sacred — va tapuia — it carries a spiritual weight, and treating relationships as transactions is the violation. Honour the dimension: some things are owed to the space itself, not to any outcome.

*For example:* The business partners who refuse to squeeze the last dollar out of a deal because "that's not how we want the space between us to look in ten years" — and are still partners in ten years.
