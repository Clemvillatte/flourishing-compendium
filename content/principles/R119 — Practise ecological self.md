> **Principle 381** — Practise ecological self

Recognise that your self is not confined to your skin but extends into the ecological web of relationships that constitute your life. The ecological self, as Arne Naess proposed, is a wider self that includes the natural world. Expanding your sense of self to include the more-than-human world is not a loss but an enrichment.

(Naess, 1989, Ecology, Community and Lifestyle; Naess, 2008, The Selected Works of Arne Naess.)

**Plain words** Your self doesn't stop at your skin — it extends into the ecological web that constitutes your life. The wider self isn't a loss of boundaries; it's an enrichment of them. Include the living world in "me" and act accordingly.

*For example:* Someone whose morning walk includes knowing the trees, the birds, the season's shift — when the wetland is drained, they feel it like a cut, not like a news item.
