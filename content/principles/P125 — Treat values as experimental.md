> **Principle 125** — Treat values as experimental

Values are not revealed absolutes; they are tested, revised, and grown in experience: hypotheses about what makes life flourish. Hold your values firmly enough to live by, loosely enough to learn from.

(Dewey, Theory of Valuation, 1939.)

**Plain words** Values are not revealed absolutes — they're tested, revised, and grown in experience: hypotheses about what makes life flourish. Hold your values firmly enough to live by, loosely enough to learn from. A value that can't survive new evidence isn't a value; it's a possession being defended.

*For example:* 'Family first' held through the decade where family was toxic — at some point the hypothesis fails the flourishing test. Revising it isn't betrayal; it's the experiment reporting results.
