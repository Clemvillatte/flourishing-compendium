> **Principle 129** — Serve with what you realize

The integration of knowledge and love: realization that does not issue in service is, by the tradition's own test, incomplete, because the realization is precisely that the boundary between self and other was a mis-taking. The bounded formulation closes the loop with action: the fruit of inquiry is a changed way of being among people. Practically, attach each year's practice to one person or project served.

(BG 3.20 with 6.29-6.32; Vivekananda's Karma Yoga, modern.)

**Plain words** Realization that does not issue in service is, by the tradition's own test, incomplete — because the realization was precisely that the boundary between self and other was a mis-taking. Serve with what you realize; the loop closes in action.

*For example:* The practitioner whose insight deepens and whose calendar quietly fills with other people's needs — not as duty, as consequence. The realization and the service turn out to be the same event.
