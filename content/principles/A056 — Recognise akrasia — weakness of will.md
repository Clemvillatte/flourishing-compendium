> **Principle 56** — Recognise akrasia: weakness of will

Akrasia is knowing what is good but doing otherwise. It is not ignorance of the principle but a failure to apply it in the moment, when desire overcomes reason. Name it when it happens. Do not pretend it is rationalisation; it is a real and familiar human experience.

(NE VII.1-10.)

**Plain words** Akrasia is knowing what's good and doing the other thing anyway. It's not ignorance, it's desire beating reason in the moment. Name it when it happens. Don't wave it away. It's real and almost everyone has been there.

*For example:* You know you should leave the party at midnight for tomorrow's early start. You stay until two, knowing it, and feel worse for it.
