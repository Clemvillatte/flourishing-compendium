> **Principle 87** — Seek the pleasure of contemplation

The pleasure of the intellect is superior to bodily pleasure because it is more continuous, more self-sufficient, and less subject to satiety. Cultivate the capacity for intellectual pleasure: reading, thinking, wondering, discussing.

(NE X.7, X.8; EE VIII.3.)

**Plain words** The pleasure of thinking lasts longer and stays satisfying longer than bodily pleasures. It's more self-sufficient and doesn't cloy. So build the capacity for it: read, think, wonder, discuss.

*For example:* Spending a Sunday on a hard book and a long chat about it can leave you more content than a day out spent buying things.
