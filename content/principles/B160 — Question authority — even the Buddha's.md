> **Principle 160** — Question authority: even the Buddha's

Take nothing on the teacher's word: test it. A teacher is a pointer, not a substitute for your own seeing.

(Kalama Sutta, AN 3.65; Dhammapada 74.)

**Plain words** Take nothing on the teacher's word — test it. A teacher is a pointer, not a substitute for your own seeing. The best teachers are delighted to be verified; the worst are threatened by the question. That's the diagnostic.

*For example:* The instructor who says 'try it and tell me what you find' versus the one who says 'how dare you question' — you have your answer.
