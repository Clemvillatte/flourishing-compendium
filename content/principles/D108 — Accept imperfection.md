> **Principle 108** — Accept imperfection

Great fullness seems empty, great straightness seems bent, great skill seems clumsy. What is genuinely whole looks unfinished: stop sanding it.

(DDJ 45.)

**Plain words** What is genuinely whole looks unfinished — stop sanding it. The obsession with polish is how things get thinner, not better.

*For example:* The first draft that feels rough but alive beats the fourth draft that's smooth and dead — the roughness was the life.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Self-compassion|Self-compassion]]**, self-kindness versus harsh self-judgment predicts resilience (Neff, 2003; MacBeth and Gumley, 2012)
- **[[Maladaptive perfectionism|Maladaptive perfectionism]]** (Hewitt and Flett, 1991), predicts distress

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[Wabi-sabi|Wabi-sabi]]**, the Zen aesthetics of imperfection and transience
- **[[The crooked timber of humanity|The crooked timber of humanity]]** (Kant, "Idea for a Universal History," proposition 6)
