> **Principle 58** — Learn somatic experiencing: working with activation in the body

Somatic experiencing (Levine) is a body-based approach to trauma and stress that works with activation through body awareness and movement.

**Plain words** Somatic experiencing works with stress and trauma activation through the body — trembling, warmth, breath — the body's own ways of settling. The approach trusts the animal's process rather than the story about it.

*For example:* A client notices trembling during a session and tells the therapist. They slow down and check how the client is feeling rather than treating the shaking as proof that trauma has been released.
## Psychology parallels

- **[[Somatic experiencing (Levine)|Somatic experiencing (Levine)]]**, a body-based approach to trauma and stress (Levine, 1997; Payne et al., 2015)
- **Note: the evidence base is developing**, and controlled trials are limited, though clinical reports are positive (Payne et al., 2015; Brom et al., 2017)

## Practice

- Consider somatic experiencing: if trauma or chronic stress has left body-based patterns, this approach works with activation in the body (trembling, warmth, breath) as the body's own ways of settling. Seek a qualified practitioner.
