> **Principle 176** — Distinguish between facts and interpretations

A fact is what happened. An interpretation is what you think it means. "It rained" is a fact. "It rained and ruined my day" is an interpretation. The Stoic practises separating facts from interpretations and holding interpretations lightly.

(Disc. I.1, I.11, I.28; Ench. 5; Med. IV.7, VI.13, VIII.47.)

**Plain words** A fact is what happened. An interpretation is what you think it means. "It rained" is a fact. "It rained and ruined my day" is an interpretation. Practise separating them and hold interpretations lightly.

*For example:* "She didn't call" is fact. "She doesn't care" is interpretation.
