> **Principle 350** — Practise the distinction between problem and mystery

Marcel's canonical statement of the distinction, from Being and Having:

> "A problem is something which I meet, which I find completely before me, but which I can therefore lay siege to and reduce. But a mystery is something in which I am myself involved, and it can therefore only be thought of as a sphere where the distinction between what is in me and what is before me loses its meaning and initial validity." (Marcel, Being and Having, p. 117, as quoted in [Treanor and Sweetman, SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/marcel/))

The test is the questioner. With a problem, "the identity of the person asking the question is not an issue": anyone can ask it, and the technique that solves it works for everyone. With a mystery, "the questioner is involved in the question he or she is asking"; to change the questioner would be to alter the question. Marcel's shorthand for the degradation is that a mystery is "a problem that encroaches on its own data" (Marcel, as quoted in SEP, p. 19). His own example of the difference, on evil:

> "Evil which is only stated or observed is no longer evil which is suffered: in fact, it ceases to be evil. In reality, I can only grasp it as evil in the measure in which it touches me—that is to say, in the measure in which I am involved… being 'involved' is the fundamental fact." (Marcel, as quoted in [Treanor and Sweetman, SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/marcel/), p. 19)

And the boundary is not agnosticism: "The mysterious is not the unknowable, the unknowable is only the limiting case of the problematic" (Marcel, Being and Having, p. 118, as quoted in SEP).

(Marcel, Being and Having; as carried in the opened SEP entry.)

**Plain words** Marcel's distinction: problems get solved by technique; mysteries get entered. The meaning of life, love, suffering — you can't fix these, only live inside them. Approaching a mystery with a technician's attitude is the error; participation is the mode.

*For example:* Imagine someone trying to optimise their grief into closure with worksheets and timelines, then sitting shiva with the family and entering it instead.
