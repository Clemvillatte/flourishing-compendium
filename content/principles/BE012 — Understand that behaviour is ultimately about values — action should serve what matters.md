> **Principle 12** — Understand that behaviour is ultimately about values: action should serve what matters

In Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, values sit inside the definition of effective behaviour itself. The model's core target is defined so that behaving well means behaving in the service of what a person has chosen to matter:

> "Psychological flexibility can be defined as contacting the present moment as a conscious human being, fully and without defense, as it is and not as what it says it is, and persisting or changing in behavior in the service of chosen values." (Hayes, Pistorello & Levin, 2012)

The same paper is explicit that the model's other processes are instruments, not goals: "Acceptance in ACT is not an end in itself. Rather acceptance is fostered as a method of increasing values-based action." Committed action, the behaviour-change process, is defined by the same relation: "Committed actions are designed to further values in the context of openness and awareness." A technique that changes behaviour without [[Values clarification|values clarification]] can install any habit, including ones the person never chose.

**Plain words** A technique can make you do almost anything regularly, and regular doing is only worth something when it serves something you picked. Values are the picked destinations; goals are checkpoints on the way; habits are the vehicle. Check the direction before tuning the engine: ask what a behaviour serves, and if the honest answer is "nothing I chose", the behaviour is motion, not progress.

*For example:* A person perfects a 5 a.m. routine, reclaims two hours a day, then spends them on things they never chose to care about. The machine works and it is pointed nowhere. Name the value first, then build the habit that serves it: a morning routine in service of being present for your family has somewhere to go.

## Practice

- Connect behaviours to values: why does this matter? What does this serve? Behavioural change without values is empty; values without behavioural change is impotent. Do both.
