> **Principle 2** — Expect the cosmos itself to be motion

The Nahua name for the present cosmic age is Nahui Ollin, Four-Movement: the fifth sun, whose name itself says that instability is not a flaw in reality but reality's name. The age is understood to be temporary, as the four before it were. The principle is the opposite of both nostalgia and utopia: change at the largest scale is the constitution, not the accident, and the plan that assumes permanence is mispriced. Practically, hold your structures (career, identity, arrangements) with the grip appropriate to something whose name is Movement.

(Maffie's presentation of the five suns; the Codex Chimalpopoca as translated source.)

**Plain words** The present age's very name — Nahui Ollin, Four-Movement — has instability built into it: change at every scale is the constitution, not the accident. The four previous worlds ended; this one is temporary too. So neither nostalgia ("back when things were stable") nor utopia ("once we fix it, it holds") — motion is the name of the reality you're in.

*For example:* The couple who kept planning for the year things "settle down" — and finally accept the tradition's premise: the settling down IS the life, so the plans start fitting the motion instead of waiting for its end.
