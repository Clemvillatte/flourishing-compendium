> **Principle 79** — Do not demonise pleasure

Pleasure is not the same as the good, but it is not an enemy of the good. Pleasure completes activity as a kind of bloom. The question is not whether to enjoy but what activities are worth enjoying. Seek pleasures that complete worthy activities, not pleasures that distract from them.

(NE VII.11-15, X.1-5.)

**Plain words** Pleasure isn't the good, but it isn't the enemy either. Aristotle says it's like a bloom on an activity done well. So the question isn't whether to enjoy things, it's which activities are worth enjoying.

*For example:* Enjoying a long meal with people you like, after a day of work you're proud of, belongs to a good life. Pleasure doesn't need an apology.
