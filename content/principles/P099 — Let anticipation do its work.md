> **Principle 99** — Let anticipation do its work

Experience is shaped by the consequences it anticipates: expectation organizes perception and action. Use anticipation deliberately: rehearse outcomes, prime good expectations, foresee consequences.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922; How We Think, 1910.)

**Plain words** Experience is shaped by the consequences it anticipates — expectation organizes perception and action. Use anticipation deliberately: rehearse outcomes, prime good expectations, foresee consequences. You're running predictions all day anyway; might as well pick them.

*For example:* Dreading the dentist and the whole week tenses up — the anticipation charged interest on a forty-minute appointment. Priming the other direction pays the same interest, positively.
