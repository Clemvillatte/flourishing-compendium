> **Principle 71** — Learn to assess and monitor biophysiological health: regular check-ups

Biophysiological health can be monitored: sleep, energy, HRV, medical markers (thyroid, iron, vitamin D). Regular assessment provides early detection and feedback.

**Plain words** Keep up routine check-ups, and mention mood and energy when you go — mental health problems sometimes have physical contributors, and a clinician is the right person to decide what to test.

*For example:* The therapist who asks "when was your last blood test?" — a question that sometimes finds a physical cause hiding behind a mood. The lab is part of the mental health team.
## Psychology parallels

- **Physiological monitoring**, tracking sleep, energy, and HRV provides feedback (Thayer et al., 2012; Laborde et al., 2017)
- **Medical check-ups**, regular medical evaluation detects treatable conditions (Stover & Stover, 2011)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[The examined life|The examined life includes examined body]]** (Socrates)
- **Know thyself through measurement** (Delphic maxim)
- **[[Prudence|Prudence and prevention]]** (Aristotle)

## Practice

- Monitor biophysiological health: track sleep and energy, keep up routine medical check-ups, and mention mood and energy when you go — let the clinician decide what to test.
