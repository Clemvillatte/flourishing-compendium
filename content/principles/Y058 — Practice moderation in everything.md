> **Principle 58** — Practice moderation in everything

The Gita's rule is famously concrete: yoga is not for the one who eats too much or too little, sleeps too much or too little; for the one who is moderate in food and recreation, moderate in effort, moderate in sleep and waking, discipline destroys sorrow. The tradition treats moderation not as timidity but as the engineering requirement of a trainable nervous system. Practically, check the four moderations (food, sleep, effort, recreation) monthly, because they drift.

(BG 6.16-6.17.)

**Plain words** The Gita's rule, famously concrete: yoga is not for the one who eats too much or too little, sleeps too much or too little. Moderate in food, recreation, effort, sleep and waking — discipline destroys sorrow. Moderation as engineering, not as blandness.

*For example:* The ascetic month of 5 a.m. cold workouts and skipped meals that ends in a week of takeout and 10 a.m. alarms — versus the boring, moderate schedule that's still running, quietly, in month nine.
