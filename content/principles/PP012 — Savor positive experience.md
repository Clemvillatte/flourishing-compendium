> **Principle 12** — Savor positive experience

When something good happens, extend it. Notice the sensory details. Express it. Take a mental photograph. [[Savoring|Savoring]] is the deliberate prolongation of positive experience. It is trainable. Research shows that savouring predicts well-being independently of the frequency of positive events, and that savouring interventions produce small but reliable gains.

(Bryant and Veroff, 2007; Hurley and Kwon, 2012.)

**Plain words** When something good happens, stretch it. Notice details, express it, take a mental photograph. Savouring is trainable, and it predicts well-being independently of how often good things happen.

*For example:* After a good conversation, you sit for a minute and replay it before reaching for your phone.
