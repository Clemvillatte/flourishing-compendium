> **Principle 31** — Cultivate attentional endurance: build capacity gradually

The ability to sustain attention for long periods (1-4 hours of deep engagement) is built gradually, like physical endurance. Do not expect to go from 15 minutes to 3 hours immediately. Build incrementally.

**Plain words** Long-focus endurance is built like running mileage: add a little, consistently, and back off before injury. Nobody trains for a marathon in a weekend, and nobody gets four-hour focus in one heroic sitting.

*For example:* Week one: 30 minutes. Week four: 60. Week twelve: the afternoon vanishes into the work and you surface surprised and full. That's the mileage showing.

## Practice

- Start with 25-30 minutes of focused work. Gradually increase to 45, 60, 90 minutes. Track your capacity. Build slowly. Do not expect marathon concentration immediately.
