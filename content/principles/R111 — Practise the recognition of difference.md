> **Principle 373** — Practise the recognition of difference

Recognise that persons and communities are different, and that justice requires attending to those differences, not pretending they do not exist. Multicultural recognition is not merely tolerance; it is the affirmative acknowledgment of the value of different cultural identities.

(Taylor, 1992, Multiculturalism and the Politics of Recognition; Kymlicka, 1995, Multicultural Citizenship; Honneth, 1995, The Struggle for Recognition.)

**Plain words** Justice isn't pretending differences don't exist — it's attending to them. Multicultural recognition is affirmative acknowledgment that different cultural identities carry value, not mere tolerance with a smile. See the difference; honour it.

*For example:* The workplace holiday calendar that stops being a single tradition decorated with tokens — and actually reflects the whole room's festivals, which costs little and tells everyone they're part of the "we."
