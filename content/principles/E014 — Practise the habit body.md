> **Principle 406** — Practise the habit body

Recognise that your body carries habits — learned patterns of perception, movement, and response that operate below the level of conscious choice. The habit body is not a deficit but a necessity: without it, every action would require deliberation. Attend to your habits, both bodily and psychological, and cultivate those that serve your flourishing.

(Merleau-Ponty, 1945/2012, Phenomenology of Perception, Part II, ch. 3; Husserl, 1952/1989, Ideas II, §§56-57.)

**Plain words** Your body carries habits — learned patterns of perception, movement, and response that operate below conscious choice. The habit body isn't a deficit; without it, every action would require deliberation. Attend to your habits: they're your autopilot, and autopilot flies most of the flight.

*For example:* The driver who arrives home with no memory of the route — the habit body did the driving — and realizes the same autopilot also drives the after-work snacking, the phone-checking, the tone with the kids. The habit body has opinions.
