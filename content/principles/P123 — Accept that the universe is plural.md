> **Principle 123** — Accept that the universe is plural

The world is not One but many: many persons, perspectives, goods, centers of experience that never fuse into a single absolute. Live pluralism: expect irreducible difference, and value it.

(James, A Pluralistic Universe, 1909.)

**Plain words** The world is not One but many — many persons, perspectives, goods, centers of experience that never fuse into a single absolute. Live pluralism: expect irreducible difference, and value it. The dream of the single final answer is a monogamous relationship with a fantasy.

*For example:* The family gathering where four incompatible worldviews share a table and a dessert — nobody converts anybody, and the pie is still excellent. That's pluralism working.
