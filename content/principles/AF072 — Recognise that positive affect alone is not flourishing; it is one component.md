> **Principle 72** — Recognise that positive affect alone is not flourishing; it is one component

Ryan and Deci review hedonic and eudaimonic approaches to well-being. ([Ryan and Deci, 2001](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2001_RyanDeci_Happiness.pdf)) The compendium uses this distinction to examine a life beyond its pleasant feelings.

(Ryan and Deci, "On Happiness and Human Potentials: A Review of Research on Hedonic and Eudaimonic Well-Being," Annual Review of Psychology 52, 2001; Seligman, 2011.)

> "Current research on well-being has been derived from two general perspectives: the hedonic approach, which focuses on happiness and defines well-being in terms of pleasure attainment and pain avoidance; and the eudaimonic approach, which focuses on meaning and self-realization and defines well-being in terms of the degree to which a person is fully functioning." (Ryan and Deci, [On Happiness and Human Potentials](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2001_RyanDeci_Happiness.pdf), Annual Review of Psychology 52, 2001)

In describing the eudaimonic perspective, the review states that "well-being consists of more than just happiness. It lies instead in the actualization of human potentials." ([Ryan and Deci, 2001](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2001_RyanDeci_Happiness.pdf)) Self-determination theory adds its three needs, "autonomy, competence, and relatedness," whose fulfilment it treats as essential for psychological growth. ([Ryan and Deci, 2001](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2001_RyanDeci_Happiness.pdf))

**Plain words**
The review distinguishes pleasure-focused approaches from approaches concerned with meaning, self-realization and full functioning. ([Ryan and Deci, 2001](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2001_RyanDeci_Happiness.pdf)) For this exercise, consider enjoyment alongside relationships, purpose and opportunities to develop.

*For example:* Consider a week in which pleasant feelings came easily, and ask what besides those feelings made it satisfying.

## Psychology parallels

- **Eudaimonic vs hedonic well-being**, positive affect is a component of flourishing, not its totality (Ryan & Deci, 2001; Seligman, 2011)
- **[[PERMA model|PERMA model]]**, flourishing requires positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment (Seligman, 2011)
