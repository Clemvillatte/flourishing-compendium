> **Principle 79** — Question conventional measures of success

Everyone praises the same prizes and pursues the same ladders; the sage finds them worth questioning. Ask whose goal it is before you chase it.

(Zhuangzi 1; DDJ 20.)

**Plain words** Everyone praises the same prizes and climbs the same ladders — the sage finds them worth questioning. Ask whose goal it is before you chase it. Inherited ambitions are the most expensive things we own, because we pay for them without ever choosing them.

*For example:* Halfway through law school, someone realizes they're there because it was the family's dream — that's the moment to ask the question.
