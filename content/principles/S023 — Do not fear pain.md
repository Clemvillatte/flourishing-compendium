> **Principle 145** — Do not fear pain

Pain is a dispreferred indifferent. It is unpleasant but not evil. The Stoic faces pain with dignity, knowing that it cannot harm the character. Seneca argues that suffering can even strengthen virtue. Do not flee pain at the cost of integrity.

(Seneca, Letters 24, 67, 78, 107; Med. VII.33, IX.41, X.33; Disc. I.1, I.6, I.14.)

**Plain words** Pain is unpleasant but not evil, and it can't harm your character. The Stoic faces it with dignity, and Seneca argues it can even strengthen virtue. Don't run from pain at the cost of your integrity.

*For example:* Getting through a hard physical therapy session without complaint, because the recovery matters more than the comfort.
