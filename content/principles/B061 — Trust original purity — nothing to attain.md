> **Principle 61** — Trust original purity: nothing to attain

The mind is already luminous from the start; the task is not to acquire awakening but to stop obscuring what is already whole.

(Platform Sutra; Bankei.)

**Plain words** The mind is already luminous from the start — the task is not to acquire awakening but to stop obscuring what is already whole. You're not building a lighthouse; you're cleaning the windows of one that's been on the whole time.

*For example:* The way kids naturally pay attention — nothing was installed; the work of childhood was keeping it, not creating it.
