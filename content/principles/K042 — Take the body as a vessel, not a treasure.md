> **Principle 42** — Take the body as a vessel, not a treasure

Shantideva's instruction on embodiment: the body is a tool for accomplishing good, to be maintained the way any instrument is maintained, and neither pampered into softness nor punished into austerity. The vessel framing dissolves both of the modern extremes (the body as project for display, the body as machine to be overridden). Practically, care for the body the way a craftsperson cares for tools: for what it lets you do, not for what it looks like in the case.

(Shantideva, Bodhicharyavatara, ch. 5, the vessel verses.)

**Plain words** The body is a vessel for doing good, to be maintained like any instrument. It's not a treasure to pamper or an enemy to punish. That framing cuts through two modern extremes: the body as display and the body as a machine to override. Look after it so it can carry you.

*For example:* Sleeping properly before a big week, not for vanity and not as a grudging chore, just because the vessel has work to do.
