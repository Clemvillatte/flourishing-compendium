> **Principle 66** — Practise patience with the timeline

Well-being practices do not produce instant results. The effects are typically small, accumulate over time, and are subject to the same variability as any behavioural change. The expectation of rapid transformation is itself a barrier. The principle is to practise with realistic expectations: small gains, sustained over time, compound into meaningful change.

(Sheldon et al., 2013; Lyubomirsky, 2008.)

**Plain words** These practices don't work quickly. Effects are small and accumulate. Expecting rapid transformation is itself a barrier, because the disappointment makes you quit. Practise with realistic expectations.

*For example:* Meditating for two weeks and expecting calm is like going to the gym twice and expecting muscle.
