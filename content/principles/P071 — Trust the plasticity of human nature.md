> **Principle 71** — Trust the plasticity of human nature

Human nature is not fixed: impulses and habits are raw material, reshaped by conditions and education. No one is finished; anyone can be changed: including you.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922.)

**Plain words** Human nature is not fixed — impulses and habits are raw material, reshaped by conditions and education. No one is finished; anyone can be changed, including you. The forty-year-old who learns to swim, code, or apologize — evidence arrives yearly.

*For example:* 'You can't teach an old dog new tricks' — the old dog in question just learned video editing to make grandkids laugh. Dogs, apparently, were never the problem.
