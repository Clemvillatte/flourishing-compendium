> **Principle 383** — Practise intergenerational ecological responsibility

Act as a trustee for the ecological conditions that future generations will inherit. Climate change, biodiversity loss, and ecosystem degradation are not merely present problems; they are debts incurred against the future. Do not leave your children and grandchildren a degraded world.

(Jonas, 1979, The Imperative of Responsibility; Gardiner, 2011, A Perfect Moral Storm.)

**Plain words** Climate change, biodiversity loss, ecosystem degradation are debts charged against the future. Act as trustee of the ecological conditions your grandchildren inherit. Don't leave them the degraded world because it was convenient.

*For example:* The couple deciding on the extension vs. the solar retrofit — and runs the decision by the test: which purchase would the kids' kids thank us for?
