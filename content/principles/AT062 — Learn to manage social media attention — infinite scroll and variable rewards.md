> **Principle 62** — Learn to manage social media attention: infinite scroll and variable rewards

Social media is engineered for maximum engagement: infinite scroll (no natural stopping point), variable rewards (likes, comments arrive unpredictably), and social comparison triggers. Managing social media attention requires deliberate boundaries.

**Plain words** Infinite scroll and variable rewards are slot-machine engineering — no stopping point, random payouts. You're not weak for being hooked; you're up against a team of engineers whose KPI is your surrender. Play with the house rules in your favour.

*For example:* The feed with no bottom — there's never a natural end, which is the design. Give it an artificial one: 20 minutes, a timer, done. The timer is the bottom the feed refuses to give you.
## Psychology parallels

- **Social media and attention**, heavy social media use is associated with poorer attentional control and higher distractibility (Ophir et al., 2009; Uncapher et al., 2016)
- **Infinite scroll removes natural stopping cues**, the absence of stopping points increases usage time (Harris, 2016; Eyal, 2014)
- **Variable rewards create compulsive checking**, unpredictable social feedback creates compulsive engagement (Rosen et al., 2013; Andreassen et al., 2016)

## Practice

- Set deliberate social media boundaries: specific times (not all day), time limits, no infinite scroll apps (use tools with natural ends), and periods of complete disconnection.
