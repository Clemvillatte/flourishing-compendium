> **Principle 159** — Treat your body as a loan, not a possession

Body and life are entrusted to you by heaven-and-earth: a lodging, not property. Care for the lodging; don't cling to the deed.

(Zhuangzi.)

**Plain words** Body and life are entrusted to you by heaven-and-earth — a lodging, not property. Care for the lodging; don't cling to the deed. Stewardship is a lighter way to hold something you love: it invites care without desperation.

*For example:* Renters who treat the house better than owners treat their own — stewardship is a posture, not a contract term.
