> **Principle 308** — Practise talanoa

Engage in open, inclusive dialogue. Talanoa is a Pacific method of dialogue and storytelling that values open, honest, and inclusive conversation. It is not debate or negotiation but the sharing of stories and perspectives in a way that builds relationship and understanding. Practise talanoa in your own conversations.

(Pacific philosophy; Vaioleti, 2006, "Talanoa Research Methodology," Waikato Journal of Education, 12, 21-34.)

**Plain words** Talanoa: dialogue as storytelling, not combat. Open, honest, inclusive — people share their actual perspectives and experiences, and relationship gets built in the telling. It's not a negotiation with a winner; it's a fire everyone sits around.

*For example:* The team that replaces its blame-laden post-mortems with "tell the story of what you saw" — and the room that was ready for a fight starts, unexpectedly, learning.
