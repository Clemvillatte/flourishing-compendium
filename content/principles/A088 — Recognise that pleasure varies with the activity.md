> **Principle 88** — Recognise that pleasure varies with the activity

Each activity has its own proper pleasure. The pleasure of eating is different from the pleasure of thinking, which is different from the pleasure of friendship. Do not judge all pleasures by the same standard. Honour the distinctive pleasure of each worthy activity.

(NE X.5, X.6.)

**Plain words** Each activity has its own pleasure. Eating, thinking and friendship feel different, and each is a different quality. Don't measure them all on one scale. Respect what is distinctive about each good activity.

*For example:* The satisfaction of solving a puzzle isn't comparable to the warmth of a long dinner, and neither needs to be ranked against the other.
