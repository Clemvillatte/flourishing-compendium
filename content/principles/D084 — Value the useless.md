> **Principle 84** — Value the useless

The gnarled tree lives long precisely because no carpenter wants it. Cultivate what has no market value: idle time, unmarketable interests, unimpressive friendships.

(Zhuangzi 1, 4.)

**Plain words** Cultivate what has no market value — idle time, unmarketable interests, unimpressive friendships. Uselessness is a shelter; the useful things get used up.

*For example:* The hobby that will never make money and never becomes a side hustle is often the thing keeping the person who has it sane.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Intrinsic motivation|Intrinsic motivation]]**, curiosity-driven activity is its own reward (Kashdan et al., 2018)
- **Leisure and idle time predict creativity and recovery**
