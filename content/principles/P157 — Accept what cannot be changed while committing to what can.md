> **Principle 157** — Accept what cannot be changed while committing to what can

Hold pain and limits willingly: fighting them doubles them — and invest that conserved energy in valued action. Acceptance and commitment are one movement.

(Hayes, Strosahl and Wilson, 2011.)

**Plain words** Hold pain and limits willingly — fighting them doubles them — and invest that conserved energy in valued action. Acceptance and commitment are one movement. The struggle against the rain makes you wetter and keeps you from the thing you were outside for.

*For example:* The chronic pain patient who stops the war with the pain — the pain remains, the suffering halves, and the garden gets planted. Acceptance wasn't defeat; it was a transfer of troops.
