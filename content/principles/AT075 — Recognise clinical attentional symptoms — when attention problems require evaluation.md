> **Principle 75** — Recognise clinical attentional symptoms: when attention problems require evaluation

Persistent, impairing attentional problems — difficulty concentrating, mind always wandering, inability to focus, attentional fatigue that does not resolve — can be symptoms of depression, anxiety, ADHD, sleep disorders, or medical conditions. These warrant professional evaluation.

**Plain words** There's a line between everyday distractibility and something needing a professional: persistent, impairing, everywhere — work, home, relationships. If focus problems are running your life rather than annoying your day, that's an appointment, not an app.

*For example:* Everyone misplaces keys. The person who loses jobs, relationships, and afternoons to the same pattern for years isn't scattered — they're due an evaluation.
## Psychology parallels

- **Attentional symptoms as clinical indicators**, concentration problems are symptoms of depression, anxiety, ADHD, sleep disorders, thyroid conditions, and other medical conditions (APA, 2022)
- **Evidence-based treatments for attentional disorders**, ADHD medication, CBT for anxiety/depression, sleep treatment all improve attention (Faraone et al., 2021; Hofmann et al., 2010)

## Practice

- If attentional problems are persistent, impairing, or unexplained, seek professional evaluation. Self-directed practices are complements, not substitutes, for treatment of underlying conditions.
