> **Principle 104** — Love without possessing

Give birth, don't own; act, don't presume; lead, don't dominate. Love as giving conditions, not as holding captive.

(DDJ 2, 10, 51.)

**Plain words** Love as giving conditions, not as holding captive. Possessive love is a cage with compliments — the affection is real, but so are the bars.

*For example:* Parents who let their kid quit the violin they themselves love — and discover the kid flourishes at something else — give birth without owning.
