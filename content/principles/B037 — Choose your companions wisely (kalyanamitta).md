> **Principle 37** — Choose your companions wisely (kalyanamitta)

Admirable friendship is, in practical terms, the whole of the holy life: the people you keep shape your views, energy, and ethics more than any text. Choose environments, not just intentions.

(Upaddha Sutta, SN 45.2.)

**Plain words** Admirable friendship is, in practical terms, the whole of the holy life — the people you keep shape your views, energy, and ethics more than any text. Choose environments, not just intentions. You will become the average of the rooms you sit in.

*For example:* The friend group where everyone trains, reads, and builds — the new person levels up within a year without ever being lectured.
