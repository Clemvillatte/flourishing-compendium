> **Principle 73** — Hold experience loosely (non-attachment)

Neither clutch what you love nor banish what you hate: hold both lightly, as a bird held in the hand.

(Dhammapada 212; Shantideva.)

**Plain words** Neither clutch what you love nor banish what you hate — hold both lightly, as a bird held in the hand. Too tight and you crush it, too loose and it flies. Non-attachment is the calibrated grip, and it's the only grip love survives.

*For example:* The parent holding the teenager's independence — the bird is leaving; the grip decides whether it visits.
