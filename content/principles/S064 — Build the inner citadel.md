> **Principle 186** — Build the inner citadel

Marcus uses the image of the inner citadel: a fortress within that cannot be taken by external force. Your judgements, your values, your character: these are the citadel. Everything else is the territory outside the walls. Build the citadel strong, and let the territory be what it will.

(Med. III.5, IV.3, V.29, VII.48, VIII.48; Disc. I.1, I.18.)

**Plain words** Marcus imagines an inner citadel no outside force can storm: your judgements, values and character. Everything else is territory outside the walls. Build the citadel strong, and let the rest be what it will.

*For example:* Losing your job shakes you, but your sense of who you are holds because it never lived there.
