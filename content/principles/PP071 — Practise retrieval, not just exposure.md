> **Principle 71** — Practise retrieval, not just exposure

Learning from experience requires retrieval, not just exposure. After reading, attending a workshop, or having a significant conversation, retrieve the key points: what did I learn? What will I do differently? The retrieval consolidates the learning in a way that passive exposure does not. Research on the testing effect (Roediger and Karpicke) shows that retrieval practice produces more durable learning than re-reading or re-exposure.

(Roediger and Karpicke, 2006; Karpicke and Blunt, 2011.)

**Plain words** Learning needs retrieval, not just exposure. After a book, a workshop or a hard conversation, ask what you learned and what you'll do differently. Retrieval is what consolidates knowledge.

*For example:* Closing the book and writing three sentences about it teaches you more than a fourth re-read.
