> **Principle 35** — Practice the five strengths

The fifth point's curriculum for the whole of life: strong determination (deciding once, clearly, what the practice is); the white seed (accumulating the virtuous, day by day); reproach of the ego (meeting each flare of self-centeredness with a standing, wry acknowledgment: "there you are again"); aspiration (dedicating the practice's fruits beyond yourself); and steady familiarization (the long, boring middle of practice, trusted). Practically, name your current five strengths in one line each and revisit them monthly.

(Seven-Point Mind Training, Point Five.)

**Plain words** Five strengths for the whole of life: strong determination, deciding once what the practice is; the white seed, accumulating the good day by day; reproach of the ego, meeting each flare of self-centredness with "there you are again"; aspiration, dedicating the fruits beyond yourself; steady familiarisation, the long boring middle.

*For example:* A dull Tuesday, no inspiration at all, and you sit anyway. That's the fifth strength doing its work.
