> **Principle 30** — Use weak ties as a bridge

Use weak ties — acquaintances, colleagues, neighbors, casual contacts — as a bridge to information, opportunity, and novelty. Mark Granovetter (1973) showed that job opportunities come disproportionately through weak ties, not strong ones. The adaptive stance is to maintain a portfolio of weak ties alongside your close relationships.

**Plain words** Weak ties — acquaintances, neighbours, the person at the coffee shop — carry disproportionate opportunity. Jobs, information, novelty flow through them precisely because they're not your inner circle. Tend the periphery.

*For example:* The job that came through the guy you chat with at the gym — never through your five closest friends, who all know what you know. The periphery sees what the core can't.

## Practice

- Maintain casual contacts through regular, low-cost interactions (greetings, brief conversations). Use weak ties for information and opportunity; use strong ties for support and intimacy.
