> **Principle 67** — Notice feeling tone first (vedana)

Before any reaction, experience arrives as pleasant, unpleasant, or neutral: a quick flash of feeling tone that triggers the whole machinery of liking and disliking. Catch the tone before the reaction.

(MN 10; MN 118.)

**Plain words** Before any reaction, experience arrives as pleasant, unpleasant, or neutral — a quick flash of feeling tone that triggers the whole machinery of liking and disliking. Catch the tone before the reaction; the tone is the spark, the reaction is just the fire.

*For example:* The slight flinch at the boss's email tone — noticing the flinch (unpleasant!) gives you a beat before the defensiveness ignites.
