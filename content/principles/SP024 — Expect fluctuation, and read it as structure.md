> **Principle 24** — Expect fluctuation, and read it as structure

Spinoza names a specific misfortune of the passionate life: animi fluctuatio, the fluctuation of the soul, being drawn now by this affect, now by its opposite, like a boat on choppy water. Ambition pulls one way, fear another, love another. The condition is not a character flaw but the predictable result of multiple partial causes without a coordinating one. Practically, when you notice inner weather swinging, do not treat each swing as the truth; treat the swing itself as the datum, and look for the competing strivings underneath.

(Ethics III, P17 scholium, the fluctuatio analysis.)

**Plain words** Animi fluctuatio: the fluctuation of the soul — drawn now by this affect, now by its opposite, like a boat on choppy water. Ambition pulls one way, fear another, love a third. Expect the fluctuation and read it as structure: multiple forces, unresolved, all pulling.

*For example:* The person who wants the promotion AND dreads it, wants the relationship AND wants out — not confusion, chop. The Spinozist move isn't to pick a side but to map the forces: name each pull, and the water starts to show which one is deepest.
