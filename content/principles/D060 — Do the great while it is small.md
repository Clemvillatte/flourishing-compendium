> **Principle 60** — Do the great while it is small

The giant tree was a two-sprout seedling; the tower rose from a handful of earth. Start now, small.

(DDJ 63, 64.)

**Plain words** Everything impressive was once unimpressive — the tree was a two-sprout seedling, the tower a handful of earth. The gap between your sprout and the tree is just seasons, not talent. Start now, small.

*For example:* Every long marriage started with one nervous first date; the tree is just the sprout that kept going.
