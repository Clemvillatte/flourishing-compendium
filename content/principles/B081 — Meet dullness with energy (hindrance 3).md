> **Principle 81** — Meet dullness with energy (hindrance 3)

When sloth and torpor settle in: heaviness, sleepiness, mental fog — rouse energy: straighten the posture, open the eyes, brighten the attention, stand up if needed.

(MN 10; SN 46.)

**Plain words** When sloth and torpor settle in — heaviness, sleepiness, mental fog — rouse energy: straighten the posture, open the eyes, brighten the attention, stand up if needed. Dullness isn't treated with willpower shame; it's treated with posture and oxygen.

*For example:* The meditation nap ends when you sit up, open eyes, and face slightly upward — the fog lifts on the new posture.
