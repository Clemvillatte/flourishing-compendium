> **Principle 20** — Treat secular meaning as legitimate

Treat secular meaning — meaning found in family, work, art, nature, community, and projects, without religious framing — as fully legitimate. The adaptive stance is not to require religion for meaning; it is to respect both religious and secular forms. This is the meaning-level version of the principle (emotional pluralism).

**Plain words** Secular meaning is fully legitimate — meaning found in family, work, art, nature, and projects, no religion required. Neither needs to diminish the other. The stance is respect for both routes up the mountain.

*For example:* The believer and the non-believer both moved to tears by the same forest — one calls it cathedral, one calls it complex systems. Both are having the meaning. Both are right.

## Practice

- In meaning work, identify your own sources of meaning without assuming a religious form. In community work, respect both religious and secular participants.
