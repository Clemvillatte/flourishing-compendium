> **Principle 104** — Give up the doer's claim

The deeper layer of non-attachment: not only the fruits but the authorship is released; the Gita says the wise see that the strands of nature do their work while the seer watches, and the perfected one acts without the inner announcement of "I do this." The bounded formulation: attribute outcomes to the whole system (causes, collaborators, conditions) that produced them, including your own body-mind. Practically, strike "I" from one accomplishment story per week and watch what remains true.

(BG 14.19; BG 5.8-5.9.)

**Plain words** The deeper layer: not only the fruits but the authorship is released. The wise see the strands of nature do their work while the seer watches — the perfected one acts without the inner announcement of "I do this." Attention shifts from the self's claim to the work's needs.

*For example:* The jazz musician mid-solo — the training did the work, the self is not announcing anything, and the phrase arrives. Everyone who's been in that flow knows the announcement would have killed it.
