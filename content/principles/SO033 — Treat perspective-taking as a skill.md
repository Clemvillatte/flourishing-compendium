> **Principle 33** — Treat perspective-taking as a skill

Treat perspective-taking — the deliberate attempt to see the world from another's point of view — as a learnable skill. Perspective-taking improves empathy, reduces prejudice, and improves negotiation (Galinsky et al., 2008; Todd et al., 2011; Ku et al., 2015). It is not a panacea: it can backfire when the other's perspective is hostile or when it leads to projection. The adaptive stance is deliberate, disciplined perspective-taking.

**Plain words** Perspective-taking is a learnable skill, not a personality gift — the deliberate act of seeing from another's position. It improves empathy, negotiation, and reduces prejudice. Like any skill: reps, feedback, gradual gains.

*For example:* The negotiation that unlocks when one side finally says "what does a good outcome look like for you?" — one question, and the whole geometry changes. That's the skill, applied once.
