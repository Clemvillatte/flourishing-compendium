> **Principle 101** — Rejoice in others' happiness (mudita as practice)

When someone succeeds, delights, or receives: practice feeling glad. Start with the easy cases and train up to the rivals.

(Brahmavihara tradition; Shantideva 8.86.)

**Plain words** When someone succeeds, delights, or receives — practice feeling glad. Start with the easy cases and train up to the rivals. Sympathetic joy is a muscle: everyone can be happy for a friend; the training starts when it's the competitor.

*For example:* Genuinely liking the ex's wedding photos — that's the black-belt version of this practice, and it's achievable.
