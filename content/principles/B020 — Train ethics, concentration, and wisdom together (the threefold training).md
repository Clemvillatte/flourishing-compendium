> **Principle 20** — Train ethics, concentration, and wisdom together (the threefold training)

Dhammapada 183 gives the trainings in a single verse: avoid evil, do the skillful, cleanse the mind, one teaching, not three. Verse 185 runs the conduct items and the mind items together the same way: "Not disparaging, not injuring, restraint in line with the Patimokkha, moderation in food, dwelling in seclusion, commitment to the heightened mind: this is the teaching of the Awakened."

> "The non-doing of any evil,
> the performance of what's skillful,
> the cleansing of one's own mind:
> this is the teaching
> of the Awakened." (Dhammapada 183, [Thanissaro, accesstoinsight](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/dhp/dhp.14.than.html))

The compendium reads the verses as showing the trainings arriving together in the teaching, rather than as separate electives.

**Plain words**
The verse does not rank the three; it lists them in one breath. Ask of any self-improvement plan which of the three it trains, and whether the plan treats them as needing each other.

*For example:* Suppose a meditation practice keeps getting wrecked by late nights and unpaid bills. As a modern application, the verse's question is whether the plan includes the conduct items at all, not only the sitting.
