> **Principle 12** — Model yourself on water

Chapter 8 gives the model and the reason attached to it:

> "The highest excellence is like (that of) water. The excellence of water appears in its benefiting all things, and in its occupying, without striving (to the contrary), the low place which all men dislike. Hence (its way) is near to (that of) the Tao." (Daodejing 8, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm))

Chapters 61 and 66 extend the low-place geometry. The rivers and seas are able to receive the homage and tribute of all the valley streams because they are lower than they; the sage ruler wishing to be above men puts himself by his words below them ([DDJ 66, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)). A great state is like a low-lying, down-flowing stream to which the small states tend ([DDJ 61, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)).

Chapter 78 adds the payoff: nothing in the world is softer or weaker than water, yet for attacking things firm and strong nothing takes precedence of it; everyone knows the soft overcomes the hard, though no one carries it out in practice ([DDJ 78, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)).

Read as practical advice, these passages connect benefiting others with accepting a low position and acting without contention. Chapter 8 ends with the text's account of non-contention: the person of highest excellence does not wrangle about a low position, so no one finds fault with him ([DDJ 8, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)).

(DDJ 8, 61, 66, 78.)

**Plain words**
Imagine a group where everyone wants the visible role. Ask what useful work is being left undone. These passages suggest helping without insisting on the highest position. Chapter 66 presents a ruler who puts himself below others in speech and behind them in person; accepting the lower position is compatible with taking responsibility.

*For example:*
Suppose a meeting has ended without a clear record of its decisions. You offer to prepare the summary without asking to be named the project lead.
