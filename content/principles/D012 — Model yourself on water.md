> **Principle 12** — Model yourself on water

Benefit others without contending, seek the low places others avoid, take any shape the situation requires, and keep moving gently toward your destination.

(DDJ 8, 61, 66, 78.)

**Plain words** Water benefits everything without competing for credit, seeks the low places others avoid, takes any shape the situation requires, and still gets where it's going. That's the whole strategy: useful, humble, adaptable, persistent.

*For example:* The colleague who does unglamorous work well, doesn't jockey for spotlight, and adapts to whatever the project needs — ends up indispensable.
