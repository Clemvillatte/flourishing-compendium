> **Principle 73** — Bind attention to one place

Concentration (dharana) is defined as the binding of attention to a single place or object. The definition is deliberately narrow: not thinking about the object, not visualizing it, but returning attention to it. Practically, one object, one place of attention on it, and the practice is the return.

(YS 3.1.)

**Plain words** Concentration is defined narrowly: the binding of attention to a single place or object. Not thinking about it, not visualizing it — returning attention to it. One object, one place on it, and the practice is the returning.

*For example:* The person who thinks concentration means thinking hard about the breath — versus the one who just returns, again, to the feel of it, forty times, each return a rep. Only one of them is doing the sutra.
