> **Principle 56** — Train ethics as the second perfection, at the floor first

The perfection of ethics begins with the floor (the non-harm list: not killing, not stealing, not lying, not dividing, not cruel speech) before it aspires to the ceiling. The tradition's reasoning is the same rule of thumb: a mind at war with its conduct cannot settle. Practically, keep the floor unconditionally for one season before working on the ceiling, and notice what happens to your sitting practice as the accounts clear.

(The six perfections; Shantideva's ethics chapters; Tsongkhapa's lamrim.)

**Plain words** Start the perfection of ethics at the floor: not killing, stealing, lying, dividing people or speaking cruelly. A mind at war with its own conduct can't settle. Keep the floor unconditionally for one season before aspiring to the ceiling.

*For example:* For three months you tell no lies, not even white ones, and notice how quiet your head becomes.
