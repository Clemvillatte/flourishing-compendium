> **Principle 44** — Learn to track habits: monitoring sustains

Tracking habits (marking, measuring, logging) provides feedback, increases awareness, and sustains motivation. Monitoring is a habit-maintenance practice.

**Plain words** Track the habit — a mark on a calendar is embarrassingly powerful. It creates feedback, awareness, and a small streak to protect. The chain of X's becomes its own motivation.

*For example:* The calendar by the desk with a growing chain of red marks — breaking the chain hurts more than the workout. The tracker quietly became the trainer.
## Psychology parallels

- **Self-monitoring and habit maintenance**, tracking behaviour improves maintenance (Michie et al., 2011; Gardner, 2015)
- **Habit tracking increases success**, monitoring is a key component of habit interventions (Michie et al., 2011; Clear, 2018)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[The examined life|The examined life requires data]]** (Socrates, Apology)
- **Know thyself through tracking** (Delphic maxim)
- **[[Fallibilism|Fallibilism and feedback]]** (Peirce)

## Practice

- Track habits: use a habit tracker, mark an X, use an app. Make the chain visible. Do not break the chain (Seinfeld strategy). Monitoring sustains.
