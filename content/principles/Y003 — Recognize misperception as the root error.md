> **Principle 3** — Recognize misperception as the root error

The root affliction (avidya) is not stupidity; it is a systematic mis-taking: the impermanent taken as stable, the impure as pure, pain as pleasure, and what is not self as self. Every downstream affliction (attachment, aversion, clinging to continued existence) grows from this root. Practically, treat your strongest certainties as the first place to check for mis-taking, especially where something you want is involved.

(YS 2.3-2.5.)

**Plain words** The root error isn't stupidity — it's systematic mis-taking: the impermanent taken as stable, pain as pleasure, the not-self as self. Every downstream affliction grows from this root. Learn the four flips and check your current suffering against them.

*For example:* Someone treats the job title as permanent and the weekend as happiness — both mis-takings, and both on the list. The suffering that follows isn't bad luck; it's the root error doing exactly what it does.
