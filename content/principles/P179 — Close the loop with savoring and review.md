> **Principle 179** — Close the loop with savoring and review

Experience is completed twice: once in the living, once in the digesting: savor the good, review the day, extract the learning. Reflection is the second half of experience.

(Dewey, How We Think, 1910; savoring research.)

**Plain words** Experience is completed twice — once in the living, once in the digesting: savor the good, review the day, extract the learning. Reflection is the second half of experience. The un-savored good meal and the un-reviewed bad month both end half-lived.

*For example:* The vacation that gets properly looked at on the flight home — photos, stories, laughter — versus the one that evaporates on Monday. Same trip. One got lived twice.
