> **Principle 95** — Let knowledge purify action

Bhagavad Gita 4.38 calls knowledge an unequalled purifier. It describes a person perfected in Yoga finding that knowledge within the Self, in due time. In the preceding verse, the effect of knowledge on actions is compared to a blazing fire reducing fuel to ashes.

> "Verily, there is no purifier in this world like knowledge. He who is perfected in Yoga finds it within the Self in due time." ([Bhagavad Gita 4.38](https://bhagavadgita.com/chapter/4/verse/38))

> "As the blazing fire reduces fuel to ashes, O Arjuna, so does the fire of knowledge reduce all actions to ash." ([Bhagavad Gita 4.37](https://bhagavadgita.com/chapter/4/verse/37))

**Plain words** The understanding described here is found inwardly by someone accomplished in yoga. A suggested application is to give practice time, without demanding a new insight after every session.

*For example:* (Imagined application.) After a yoga session, a practitioner notices disappointment at having no new insight and chooses to return to their practice without setting a deadline for one.
