> **Principle 54** — Retrain association deliberately

Part V's most practical proposition: because affects follow the laws of imagination, they can be re-linked. The mind can arrange its associations so that the images of things are connected to thoughts that empower rather than enslave; Spinoza even prescribes the method, by repeatedly pairing the affect-triggering image with reflections that put it in a larger frame. Two and a half centuries before behavior therapy, the prescription is written out. Practically, take one trigger image that governs you and deliberately re-pair it, daily, with a truthful larger context.

(Ethics V, P10's condition, so long as we are not assailed by contrary emotions; the training regimen is an adaptation.)

**Plain words** Because affects follow the laws of imagination, they can be re-linked: the mind can arrange its associations so that images connect to thoughts that empower rather than enslave. Retrain the association deliberately. The Spinozist version of exposure and reconsolidation, three centuries early.

*For example:* The office that triggers the anxiety — deliberately re-linked, visit by visit, with the competency of the work itself — until the building's lobby means "focus" instead of "threat." The association was trained; it retrains.
