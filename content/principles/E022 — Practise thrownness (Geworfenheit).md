> **Principle 414** — Practise thrownness (Geworfenheit)

Recognise that you are thrown into a world you did not choose. Your historical period, your culture, your language, your family — these are not chosen but given. You cannot control the conditions of your thrownness, but you can choose how to respond to them. Do not fantasise about being unencumbered; own your thrownness.

(Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §§27, 38, 41.)

**Plain words** Thrownness: you're thrown into a world you didn't choose — period, culture, language, family, all given, none selected. You can't control the conditions of the throw, but you can choose your response to them. The dice were never yours; the move is.

*For example:* Someone spends a decade resenting the throw — the country, the accent, the debt — and finally switches to the only available game: given this throw, what's the best move? Same facts, entirely different decade.
