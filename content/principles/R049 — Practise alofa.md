> **Principle 311** — Practise alofa

Love with generosity and care. Alofa is the Samoan concept of love that encompasses affection, compassion, and generosity. It is not merely a feeling but a practice of caring for others through concrete action.

(Samoan philosophy; Tuhiwai Smith, 1999, Decolonizing Methodologies.)

**Plain words** Alofa: love as affection, compassion, and generosity in one — a practice expressed through concrete care, not a feeling waiting to strike. You love by showing up with something useful.

*For example:* The auntie whose love looks like the freezer always stocked for whoever turns up, the ride to the airport at 5 a.m., the exact-right amount in the card — nobody doubts they're loved; they can eat the evidence.
