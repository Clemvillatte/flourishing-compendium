> **Principle 131** — Practice the same principle at every scale

The tradition's fractal design: the training of attention on the breath and the training of a life are the same operation at different scales: bind, steady, release the claim. The Gita says the same of seeing the self in all beings. Practically, take one day per month as a scaled practice: run the day as one sitting, with the same beginning, the same return-when-wandering, and the same dedication at the end.

(YS 3.4 applied; BG 6.29-6.32 applied.)

**Plain words** The fractal design: training attention on the breath and training a life are the same operation at different scales — bind, steady, release the claim. Take one day per month and run the operation at life-scale deliberately.

*For example:* Someone realises their whole year is a sit: bind attention to the chosen work, steady through the distractions, release the claim on the results — the breath practice and the annual plan turn out to be one instruction at two zoom levels.
