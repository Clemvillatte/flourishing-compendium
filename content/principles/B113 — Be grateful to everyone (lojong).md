> **Principle 113** — Be grateful to everyone (lojong)

Every person: helper, stranger, irritant — is showing you something about your mind. Train the reflex of gratitude toward all of them.

(Lojong slogans, e.g., "Be grateful to everyone"; Shantideva 8.)

**Plain words** Every person — helper, stranger, irritant — is showing you something about your mind. Train the reflex of gratitude toward all of them. The lojong move: the difficult person is the free coach; the kind one is the free massage.

*For example:* The client from hell taught you boundaries — you now charge properly because of them. Thank them, internally, and raise your rates.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Benefit finding|Benefit finding]]**, even in adversity, finding benefit predicts growth (Affleck and Tennen, 1996)
- **[[Post-traumatic growth|Post-traumatic growth]]** (Tedeschi and Calhoun, 2004)
