> **Principle 27** — Learn that movement in nature is doubly beneficial

Exercising in nature (green exercise) combines the benefits of exercise and nature contact. It reduces stress, improves mood, and restores attention more than indoor exercise.

**Plain words** Movement in nature is a double hit — exercise plus green space, each good, together better. The park run beats the treadmill run; the outdoor walk beats the mall walk. If you can take the workout outside, take it.

*For example:* The same 30-minute route on a busy road versus through the park — the body gets similar exercise, the mind gets a different world. The trees are a co-therapist.
## Psychology parallels

- **[[Green exercise (Barton, Pretty)|Green exercise (Barton, Pretty)]]**, exercise in nature produces greater improvements in mood and self-esteem than indoor exercise (Barton & Pretty, 2010; Pretty et al., 2005)
- **Nature and exercise combined**, the combination produces additive or synergistic benefits (Barton & Pretty, 2010; Bratman et al., 2015)

## Practice

- Exercise in nature: walk, run, or cycle in parks, forests, or by water. The combination of movement and nature is more restorative than either alone.
