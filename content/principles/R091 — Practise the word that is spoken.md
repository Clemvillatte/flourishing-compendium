> **Principle 353** — Practise the word that is spoken

Recognise that genuine communication is not the transmission of information but the creation of a shared reality. The word that is spoken between persons creates the Between, the shared space of meaning. Do not use words as tools or weapons. Let your speech create relation.

(Buber, 1965, The Knowledge of Man, "The Word That Is Spoken"; Buber, 1923/1970, I and Thou.)

**Plain words** Real communication isn't information transfer — it creates a shared reality between people. Words used as tools or weapons build nothing; words spoken to create relation build the between. Let your speech be constructive in the literal sense.

*For example:* Two ex-partners who could exchange all the "correct" texts in the world — and what actually changes things is the one conversation where each says the true thing and hears the other.
