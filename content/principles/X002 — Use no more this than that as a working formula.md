> **Principle 2** — Use "no more this than that" as a working formula

The skeptic's formula ou mallon ("no more this than that") applied to any disputed matter: no more pleasure than pain in the thing, no more natural than conventional in the claim. The formula is a portable instrument, applied in the moment of dispute, not only in the study. Its function is immediate de-escalation of the stakes. Practically, when two options have argued you to exhaustion, say the formula ("no more this than that"), act if action is needed, and let the formula carry the residual weight.

(Sextus Empiricus, Outlines, Book I, the formulas.)

**Plain words** When two options have argued you to a standstill, the old skeptic's formula finishes the job: "no more this than that." Say it, pick if picking is needed, and let the formula carry what's left of the weight. It's a pocket-sized de-escalator.

*For example:* Torn for a week between two identical job offers, someone mutters "no more this than that," takes the one with the shorter commute, and the jaw finally unclenches.
