> **Principle 21** — Learn to use exercise for sleep: timing matters

Exercise improves sleep quality and can help insomnia. However, vigorous exercise too close to bedtime can be stimulating. Timing matters.

**Plain words** Exercise improves sleep, but timing matters — vigorous exercise right before bed revs some people up. Morning or afternoon is safest. The workout is a sleep investment; don't spend it at 9 p.m.

*For example:* The evening HIIT class that leaves one person sleepy and another wired — the experiment is cheap: move it to morning for a week and watch the sleep log. The body votes.
## Psychology parallels

- **Exercise and sleep**, regular exercise improves sleep quality (Buman et al., 2014; Yang et al., 2012)
- **Timing of exercise**, vigorous exercise close to bedtime can disrupt sleep in some people (Buman et al., 2014; Dolezal et al., 2017)

## Practice

- Use exercise for sleep: regular exercise improves sleep, but finish vigorous exercise at least 3 hours before bed. Gentle movement (walking, stretching, yoga) close to bedtime is fine.
