> **Principle 192** — Life is freedom inserting itself into necessity

Matter, for Bergson, is inertia, geometry, necessity. Consciousness is memory, anticipation, and choice. The two are antagonistic; life is what reconciles them. Life is precisely freedom inserting itself into necessity and turning it to its profit. Freedom does not abolish necessity — it works through it, like water finding a crack in rock.

This is a metaphysical framing of agency, not a claim that every difficulty is freely chosen or freely redeemable.

**Plain words** Matter, for Bergson, is inertia, geometry, necessity; consciousness is memory, anticipation, and choice. Life is precisely freedom inserting itself into necessity and turning it to its profit. Freedom doesn't abolish necessity — it works through it, like water finding a crack in rock.

*For example:* The pianist bound by the piano's mechanism, the score's structure, the hand's anatomy — and somehow Chopin. Necessity everywhere; freedom running through every crack in it. That's the model: not escaping the rock, finding the fissure.

## Philosophy parallels

- **(Daoism)** The Daoist water metaphor: yielding, non-forceful, yet formative. Freedom works through necessity, not against it.
- **(Buddhism)** Karma as conditions plus response; responses are also conditioned, but not fully determined.

## Practice

- When facing a constraint you did not choose, ask: where is the smallest place where freedom can be inserted? Not a grand liberation — a crack.

## Limits

- Not a claim that suffering is freely chosen. Not a claim that all constraints can be turned to profit.
