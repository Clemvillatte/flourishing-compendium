> **Principle 89** — Ride the rhythm of activity and receptivity

Life is not constant doing: it breathes between effort and surrender, assertion and openness. Honor the passive phases: rest, silence, receptivity — as half of intelligent action.

(Dewey, Art as Experience, 1934; Bergson, Time and Free Will, 1889.)

**Plain words** Life is not constant doing — it breathes between effort and surrender, assertion and openness. Honor the passive phases: rest, silence, receptivity — as half of intelligent action. The inhale is not the absence of breathing.

*For example:* The writer who walks after drafting — the walking is not slacking; it's where the next paragraph is being assembled. Receptivity is a work mode.
