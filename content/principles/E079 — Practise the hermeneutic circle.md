> **Principle 471** — Practise the hermeneutic circle

Recognise that understanding is circular: you understand the whole through the parts and the parts through the whole. You cannot understand a text, a person, or yourself without moving between the particular and the general. Practise the hermeneutic circle in your own self-understanding: revise your understanding of the whole in light of new particulars, and revise your understanding of particulars in light of the whole.

(Gadamer, 1960/2013, Truth and Method; Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §32; Ricoeur, 1976, Interpretation Theory.)

**Plain words** The hermeneutic circle: you understand the whole through the parts and the parts through the whole — you can't read a sentence without the book, or the book without sentences. Practise the circle on texts, people, and yourself: keep moving between particular and general until they click.

*For example:* Someone tries to understand their parents through individual incidents, then through the whole family story — each incident re-read in light of the whole, the whole rebuilt from the incidents. Neither reading alone is understanding; the circle is.
