> **Principle 319** — Practise the five relationships

Honour the five fundamental relationships: parent-child, ruler-subject, husband-wife, elder-younger sibling, and friend-friend. Each relationship carries specific obligations. In each, there is a role to fulfil with virtue.

(Confucius, The Analects 12.11; Mencius, The Mencius 3A4; [[The doctrine of the mean|The Doctrine of the Mean]], ch. 20.)

**Plain words** Five relationships — parent-child, ruler-ruled, spouses, siblings, friends — each with its own obligations and its own virtues. Not all bonds are the same, and pretending they are is a category error. Learn what each of yours asks of you.

*For example:* Someone who shows up very differently for their friend's crisis than their parent's — not coldness, just the correct recognition that "being there" means different acts in different bonds.
