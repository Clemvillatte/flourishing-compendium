> **Principle 175** — To receive, first give

What you want to diminish, first let flourish; what you want to receive, first offer. Work deliberately with reversal instead of being ambushed by it.

(DDJ 36.)

**Plain words** What you want to receive, first offer. The world runs on exchange — hoarders get bypassed by the circulation itself.

*For example:* The mentor who gives away their best advice freely — and finds the universe repays it in opportunity, with interest.
