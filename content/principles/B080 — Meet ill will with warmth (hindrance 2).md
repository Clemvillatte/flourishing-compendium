> **Principle 80** — Meet ill will with warmth (hindrance 2)

When aversion arises: anger, irritation, contempt — meet it with the warmth of loving-kindness rather than fighting it. Fire doesn't put out fire.

(MN 10; Dhammapada 3-5.)

**Plain words** When aversion arises — anger, irritation, contempt — meet it with the warmth of loving-kindness rather than fighting it. Fire doesn't put out fire. The counterintuitive move that actually works: heat met with warmth, not with more heat.

*For example:* Silently wishing the difficult coworker well — not for them, at first; for the acid it neutralizes in you.

- **[[Metta (loving-kindness)|Loving-kindness]]**, reduces anger and increases positive affect (Fredrickson et al., 2008)
