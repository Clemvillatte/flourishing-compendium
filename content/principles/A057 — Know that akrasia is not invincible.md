> **Principle 57** — Know that akrasia is not invincible

The person who knows the good but fails to do it is in a state that can be changed. Habituation, practice, and the cultivation of phronesis gradually close the gap between knowing and doing. The gap is not a metaphysical fact; it is a developmental stage.

(NE VII.3, VII.8-10.)

**Plain words** The gap between knowing and doing isn't permanent. Habit, practice, and growing wisdom gradually narrow it. Aristotle sees it as a stage of development, not a built-in flaw. So don't read your slips as final.

*For example:* A new runner who skips training days gets more reliable after a season of showing up, even without any new motivation.
