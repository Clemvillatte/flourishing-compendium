> **Principle 12** — Practice only happens now

The present moment is the only place where attention, choice, and experience exist. The past is a memory arising now; the future is an image arising now. Return, always, to now.

(Bahiya Sutta, Ud 1.10; Thích Nhất Hạnh, The Miracle of Mindfulness.)

**Plain words** The present moment is the only place where attention, choice, and experience exist. The past is a memory arising now; the future is an image arising now. Return, always, to now — the meeting you're dreading and the vacation you're craving are both just thoughts in this room.

*For example:* Eating lunch while mentally rehearsing the afternoon meeting — you were at neither. The sandwich was the only real appointment.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Mind wandering|Mind-wandering]]**, nearly half of waking life is spent elsewhere, and "being present" predicts happiness (Killingsworth and Gilbert, 2010)
- **[[Rumination|Rumination research]]** (Nolen-Hoeksema, 2000)

## Philosophy parallels

- **The present as the only reality** (Augustine, Confessions XI)
- **[[Nunc stans|Nunc stans]]** (Boethius, The Consolation of Philosophy)
