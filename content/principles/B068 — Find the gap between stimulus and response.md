> **Principle 68** — Find the gap between stimulus and response

Between contact and craving there is a moment: the feeling tone — where you can intervene. Practice lives in that gap.

(SN 12; dependent origination applied.)

**Plain words** Between contact and craving there is a moment — the feeling tone — where you can intervene. Practice lives in that gap. It's small, but it's the only real estate in the whole chain that's actually for sale.

*For example:* The notification buzzes (contact), the hand reaches (craving) — noticing the half-second between is the entire game.
