> **Principle 57** — Learn grounding techniques: present-moment body awareness

Grounding techniques involve attending to the body and present-moment sensory experience to reduce anxiety, dissociation, and overwhelm. They are specific body-based interventions.

**Plain words** Grounding: attention to the body and the present senses — five things you see, the feet on the floor — to cut anxiety and dissociation. Simple, portable, fast-acting. The senses are anchors; use them.

*For example:* The panic defused by naming five objects in the room and feeling the chair — the mind was time-travelling, the senses stayed in the room. Meet back with your feet.
## Psychology parallels

- **Grounding techniques for anxiety and dissociation**, 5-4-3-2-1 and body-focused attention reduce acute distress (Najavits, 2007; Linehan, 1993)
- **Grounding for trauma**, body-based grounding is a core trauma intervention (Najavits, 2007; van der Kolk, 2014)

## Practice

- Use grounding: "5 things I see, 4 I hear, 3 I touch, 2 I smell, 1 I taste." Or feel your feet on the floor, the weight of your body. Use for anxiety, dissociation, overwhelm.
