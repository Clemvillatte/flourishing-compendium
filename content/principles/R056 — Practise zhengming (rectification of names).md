> **Principle 318** — Practise zhengming (rectification of names)

Use language accurately and call things by their proper names. When names are not correct, language does not correspond to reality; when language does not correspond to reality, action goes wrong. In your own life, be precise in your speech, honest in your self-descriptions, and clear about what you mean.

(Confucius, The Analects 13.3.)

**Plain words** Zhengming, rectification of names: call things what they actually are. When names slip, language stops matching reality, and action goes wrong. In your own life: precise speech, honest self-descriptions, no euphemisms doing your dirty work.

*For example:* The manager who stops calling layoffs "right-sizing" and starts calling them layoffs — and finds the team can finally plan around the truth instead of the fog.
