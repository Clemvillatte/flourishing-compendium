> **Principle 20** — Ask for the cash value of your beliefs

A belief earns its keep by what it lets you do: true ideas are those we can assimilate, validate, corroborate, and act on. Audit your beliefs by their yield.

(James, Pragmatism, 1907.)

**Plain words** A belief earns its keep by what it lets you do — true ideas are those we can assimilate, validate, corroborate, and act on. Audit your beliefs by their yield. A portfolio of beliefs is a toolbox; check which tools actually turn bolts.

*For example:* 'People are basically trustworthy' — in practice this gets you burned twice a year and a hundred friendships. The yield is net positive; keep the belief.
