> **Principle 69** — Build social accountability

Share a practice with someone who will ask about it. The social commitment increases follow-through. The principle is not about peer pressure but about the well-documented effect of social accountability on behaviour change. The accountability partner does not need to practise the same thing. They need to ask.

(Leahey et al., 2012; Best et al., 2016.)

**Plain words** Share a practice with someone who will ask you about it. Social commitment increases follow-through. The partner doesn't need to do the same practice; they just need to ask.

*For example:* Texting a friend every Sunday: "Did you write this week?" and answering honestly.
