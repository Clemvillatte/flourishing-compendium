> **Principle 53** — Connect affects to ordered thought

The mind can bring it about that the images of things are ordered and connected according to the order of the intellect, so that the affect-laden imagination is re-filed inside a structure of understanding. This is the Spinozist version of narrative work: the raw memory does not change, its position in the system does. Practically, write the affective event into a longer account: what preceded it, what it meant at the time, what it means now; the writing itself performs the re-ordering.

(Ethics V, P10, with P6's necessity reading.)

**Plain words** The mind can re-file the affect-laden imagination inside a structure of understanding — images ordered and connected according to the order of the intellect. The Spinozist version of narrative therapy: the same memories, re-organised inside understanding, stop being wounds and become data.

*For example:* The failed marriage re-told inside a structure — the economics, the ages, the forces — and the same events, filed under understanding, lose their sting without being denied. Nothing changed in 2009; everything changed in the filing.
