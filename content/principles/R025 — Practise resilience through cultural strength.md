> **Principle 287** — Practise resilience through cultural strength

Draw on your cultural traditions as a source of resilience in the face of adversity. Cultural strength is not a return to a romanticised past but the living, adaptive practice of cultural values, stories, and ceremonies in the present.

(Aboriginal SEWB framework; Dudgeon et al., 2014, Working Together.)

**Plain words** When adversity hits, draw on the tradition — the stories, values, and ceremonies — as the strength reservoir. Not as retreat to a romanticised past, but as the living practice of who you already are. Culture is load-bearing.

*For example:* The family that goes through a brutal year and holds it together with the ceremonies, the songs, the gatherings — outsiders call it resilience, they just call it how we do things.
