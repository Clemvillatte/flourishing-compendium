> **Principle 427** — Practise the gaze of the Other (le regard)

Recognise that the gaze of the Other fundamentally transforms your experience. Under the gaze of the Other, you become an object for them, and your world is reorganised around their look. Do not be paralysed by the gaze; but do not ignore it. Recognise its power and choose how to respond.

(Sartre, 1943/2003, Being and Nothingness, Part III, ch. 1; Sartre, 1943/2003, Being and Nothingness, Part IV, ch. 3.)

**Plain words** The gaze of the Other transforms your experience: under it, you become an object for them, and your world reorganises around their look. Don't be paralysed by the gaze; don't ignore it. Recognise its power — and then decide how much of your world it gets to organise.

*For example:* The dancer who is free alone in the studio and frozen the moment the door opens — the work is learning to keep the studio's freedom in the room with the audience. The gaze is real; the paralysis is negotiable.
