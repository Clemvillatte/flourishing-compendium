> **Principle 31** — Work steadily; idleness is the root disorder

Book VI of the Florentine Codex preserves counsel to the young about work and sleep. One passage tells a youth: "thou art to be one who riseth from sleep, one who holdeth vigil through the night. Thou art not to give thyself excessively to sleep, lest it will be said of thee… a heavy sleeper". (Sahagún, Florentine Codex Book VI, trans. Anderson & Dibble, 1969, [scanned text](https://archive.org/stream/volume-11-book-11-earthly-things-florentine-codex-general-history-of-the-things-of-new-spain/Volume%206,%20Book%206%20-%20Rhetoric%20and%20Moral%20Philosophy%20-%20%5BFlorentine%20Codex%20General%20History%20of%20the%20Things%20of%20New%20Spain%5D_djvu.txt)). There, rising at night is tied to prayer and sweeping, a devotional practice more than a productivity rule.

The Codex also holds the other half. A speech to a daughter says that the earth "is not a place of joy" but that "in order that we may not go weeping forever, may not die of sorrow, it is our merit that our lord gave us laughter, sleep, and our sustenance" (same text; the chapter number is not confirmed from the scan). The Codex passage does not frame overwork as a failure of the same virtue; that step is an interpretation.

Modern evidence supports structured activity as a treatment for depression, not as a general ethic. In a trial of 440 adults with major depression, behavioural activation delivered by junior workers was "non-inferior to CBT" delivered by trained therapists, with 12-month PHQ-9 scores of 8.4 in each arm (Richards et al. 2016, Lancet 388:871-880, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27461440/)).

**Plain words**
The Nahua elders told the young to be diligent and to avoid heavy sleeping. They also said rest and laughter are gifts. Behavioural activation, which schedules meaningful activity, performed as well as CBT for depression in one large trial. That does not show that more work makes anyone well. For a week, give each day its work, done steadily, and take the rest as planned.

*For example:* A retiree who thought rest was the reward finds the days blurring, and takes a part-time craft job for the structure. This is an imagined case.
