> **Principle 57** — Design workplaces for flourishing

Design workplaces for adaptive flourishing. The evidence is substantial: autonomy-supportive management, psychological safety, work-life balance, and fair pay predict well-being, productivity, and retention (Deci and Ryan, 2000; Edmondson, 1999; Warr, 1987; Karasek, 1979). The demand-control-support model (Karasek and Theorell, 1990) shows that high demand with low control and low support is the harmful combination.

**Plain words** Workplaces can be designed for flourishing: autonomy-supportive management, psychological safety, balance, fair pay. These predict well-being and productivity — the business case and the human case point the same way. Culture isn't a poster; it's a design decision.

*For example:* The team where you can say "I don't know" without a penalty — versus the one where admitting error is career damage. One of these teams learns faster and burns out slower. The design did that.

## Practice

- In management, provide autonomy, clear expectations, and support. In job design, match demands to resources. In career choice, weigh autonomy and purpose alongside pay.
