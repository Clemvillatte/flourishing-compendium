> **Principle 157** — Protecting others, you protect yourself

The harm you do to others becomes your own atmosphere; the good you do them becomes yours. Practice kindness as enlightened self-interest at minimum.

(Dhammapada 157; Sedaka Sutta, SN 47.19.)

**Plain words** The harm you do to others becomes your own atmosphere; the good you do them becomes yours. Practice kindness as enlightened self-interest at minimum. There's no version of poisoning the well where you don't drink.

*For example:* The office where one person starts genuinely thanking others — within a month, everyone's day is measurably better, including theirs.
