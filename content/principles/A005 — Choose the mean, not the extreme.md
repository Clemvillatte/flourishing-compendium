> **Principle 5** — Choose the mean, not the extreme

Virtue finds the intermediate between excess and deficiency: courage between cowardice and recklessness, generosity between miserliness and prodigality. The mean is not a mathematical midpoint but what is appropriate to the situation, the person, and the purpose. Seek the fitting, not the average.

(NE II.2, II.6.)

**Plain words** Too much and too little both miss. Courage sits between cowardice and recklessness, generosity between stinginess and waste. But the middle isn't an average you can calculate. It shifts with the situation, the person, and the point. The target is whatever fits.

*For example:* Being honest with a grieving friend doesn't mean listing every flaw in the eulogy. It also doesn't mean saying nothing. The right amount depends on who's in the room.
