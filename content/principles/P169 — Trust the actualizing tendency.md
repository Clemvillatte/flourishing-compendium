> **Principle 169** — Trust the actualizing tendency

There is a directional tendency in every organism toward growth and fulfillment: not a perfect force, but a reliable current. Create conditions (safety, openness, feedback) and trust the current to do its work — in yourself and others.

(Rogers, On Becoming a Person, 1961.)

**Plain words** You don't push the plant; you water it and get out of the way. There's a directional current in every organism toward growth — not perfect, but reliable. Create the conditions: safety, openness, feedback.

*For example:* The team that stops being micromanaged and starts doing surprising good work — the current was always there. The management was the dam. Conditions change; the growth was on schedule.
