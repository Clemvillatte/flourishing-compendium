> **Principle 106** — Live at your own scale

The quail laughs at the giant Peng bird: wrongly, but the point stands: the Peng's journey would kill the quail. Don't run another creature's race.

(Zhuangzi 1, 2.)

**Plain words** The quail laughs at the giant Peng bird — wrongly, but the point stands: the Peng's journey would kill the quail. Don't run another creature's race. Ambition that isn't yours doesn't just fail; it exhausts you on the way to failing.

*For example:* Some people are built for startups and some for deep thirty-year institutions — swapping their jobs wrecks both, and neither is lesser.
