> **Principle 59** — Distinguish incontinence from vice

The incontinent person knows the good but fails to do it; the vicious person does not know the good or does not care. Incontinence is curable; vice is harder. Do not confuse weakness with corruption.

(NE VII.1-10, VII.8.)

**Plain words** A person with weak will knows the good and fails at it. A vicious person doesn't know the good or doesn't care. Weak will can be cured. Vice is far harder. So don't confuse a lapse with a rotten character.

*For example:* A person who lies once under pressure and feels awful is in a different place than someone who lies routinely and feels nothing.
