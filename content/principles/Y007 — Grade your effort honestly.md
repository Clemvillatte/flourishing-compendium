> **Principle 7** — Grade your effort honestly

The tradition explicitly grades practice: mild, moderate, and ardent, and states that results follow the grade. This is not perfectionism; it is accurate self-assessment as a practice. Practically, name your current grade without flattery or harshness, and let the intensity of your practice match the difficulty of your current mind.

(YS 1.21-1.22.)

**Plain words** The tradition grades practice explicitly — mild, moderate, ardent — and says results follow the grade. Not perfectionism: accurate self-assessment as practice. Name your current grade without flattery or harshness, and let the honest name set the intensity.

*For example:* Someone who tells themselves they're "basically meditating daily" — then actually counts: twice last month, mild grade. Naming it accurately is the moment the ardent version becomes possible.
