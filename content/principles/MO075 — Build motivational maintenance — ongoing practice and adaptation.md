> **Principle 75** — Build motivational maintenance: ongoing practice and adaptation

Motivation is not a one-time achievement; it requires ongoing maintenance, adaptation to changing circumstances, and retention of what works. This is the variation-selection-retention loop applied to motivation.

**Plain words** Motivation is a practice, not a trophy — life changes and the system needs re-tuning: new job, new baby, new season. The structures that worked last year won't next year. Maintenance isn't failure; it's the actual work.

*For example:* The productivity system that survived two job changes by being rebuilt each time — not the same system, but the same gardener. The re-tuning was the skill.
## Psychology parallels

- **Motivation requires ongoing maintenance**, like fitness, motivation requires practice (Ericsson et al., 1993; Ryan & Deci, 2017)
- **Goal review and adjustment**, regular review improves goal attainment (Wrosch et al., 2007)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[The examined life|The examined life]] as ongoing practice** (Socrates, Apology 38a)
- **[[Bhavana (cultivation)|Bhavana (ongoing cultivation)]]** (Buddhism)
- **[[The unity of the virtues|Virtue as maintained habit]]** (Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics II)

## Practice

- Build a motivational maintenance routine: regular goal review, values check, environment design, habit maintenance. Retain what works; discard what does not.
