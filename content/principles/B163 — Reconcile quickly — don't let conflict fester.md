> **Principle 163** — Reconcile quickly: don't let conflict fester

Repair soon: a small rift addressed today is a conversation; the same rift next year is a wall.

(Dhammapada 6; MN 61.)

**Plain words** Repair soon — a small rift addressed today is a conversation; the same rift next year is a wall. Reconciliation compounds like interest, in the wrong direction. The awkward five-minute talk now versus the estranged decade later.

*For example:* The apology text sent the same evening versus the silence that hardens — same rift, two futures, one hour of difference.
