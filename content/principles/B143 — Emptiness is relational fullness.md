> **Principle 143** — Emptiness is relational fullness

To be empty of self is to be full of relation: nothing is anything alone. The emptier the grasp, the richer the connection.

(Nagarjuna; Thích Nhất Hạnh, Interbeing.)

**Plain words** To be empty of self is to be full of relation — nothing is anything alone. The emptier the grasp, the richer the connection. It sounds like a paradox and works like a fact: loosening the grip is what lets things actually touch you.

*For example:* The couple that stops being two fortresses negotiating — the walls come down and, strangely, so does the loneliness.
