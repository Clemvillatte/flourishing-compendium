> **Principle 49** — Treat intersectionality as an analytic lens

Crenshaw introduced the term in a 1989 law review article about antidiscrimination law. She argued that a "single axis" approach tended "to treat race and gender as mutually exclusive categories of experience and analysis", and that its paradigms were based on the experiences of white women for sex discrimination and Black men for race discrimination, as quoted in the NYU Press keywords essay ([NYU Press Keywords](https://keywords.nyupress.org/american-cultural-studies/essay/intersectionality/)). Her central case was DeGraffenreid v. General Motors, where five Black women alleged that a seniority system perpetuated past discrimination against Black women, and the court did not treat their claim as one of combined race and sex discrimination ([Crenshaw 1989, PDF copy](https://www.allisonbolah.com/site_resources/reading_list/Demarginalizing_Crenshaw.pdf); [Legal Theory Lexicon](https://legaltheorylexicon.com/2026/07/legal-theory-lexicon-121-intersectionality/)). So the concept began as a critique of how law reads harm, not as a general rule about identity.

In psychology, Cole proposed three questions: "Who is included within this category? What role does inequality play? Where are there similarities?" ([Cole 2009, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19348518/)). Bowleg argued that additive thinking, "Black + Lesbian + Woman", contradicts the central tenet that identities and inequality are "interdependent", which makes measurement hard ([Bowleg 2008, Sex Roles](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11199-008-9400-z)).

### Scope

I did not open McCall 2005 or Jones 2000. The seed's phrase "the person at the intersection" is the compendium's; the sources focus on categories and inequality in law and research.

**Plain words**
Crenshaw showed that a Black woman's experience of discrimination cannot always be broken into race plus gender. Researchers still struggle to measure that.

**For example**
When you read a statistic on one group, ask who within the group it leaves out.
