> **Principle 7** — Expect emergence: wholes exceed their parts

New levels of organization arise whose properties are not present in their parts: molecules, minds, communities. Your growth can be emergent — a new level of functioning, not just more of the same.

(Alexander, Space, Time, and Deity, 1920; Morgan, Emergent Evolution, 1923.)

**Plain words** New levels of organization arise whose properties aren't present in their parts — molecules, minds, communities. Your growth can be emergent: a new level of functioning, not just more of the same. Wetness isn't in the hydrogen; the team's morale isn't in any single member.

*For example:* Twelve okay musicians and a garage — and some nights, a band. The thing that appears is real and is nowhere in the parts.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Dynamic systems theory|Dynamic systems theory]]**, new behavioral patterns emerge from reorganization, not accumulation (Thelen and Smith, 1994)
