> **Principle 469** — Practise the liberation of others

Work for the liberation of others as a condition of your own liberation. De Beauvoir insists that to will oneself free is to will others free. Your freedom is not complete if it is achieved at the expense of others. Practise solidarity in freedom.

(de Beauvoir, 1947/2018, The Ethics of Ambiguity, Part III; de Beauvoir, 1949/2010, The Second Sex, Conclusion.)

**Plain words** Work for the liberation of others as a condition of your own: your freedom isn't complete if it's achieved at others' expense. Practise solidarity in freedom. The project of self-liberation and the project of others' liberation are one project.

*For example:* The senior woman in the firm who spends real political capital opening the path behind her — not charity, arithmetic: her freedom as "the exception" is a gilded cage. The next three hires make her normal, which was the actual liberation.
