> **Principle 364** — Practise constitutive relationality

Sandel's argument is that some attachments do not merely influence you; they partly define you (Sandel, [Liberalism and the Limits of Justice, opened text](https://is.cuni.cz/studium/eng/predmety/index.php?do=download&did=330749&kod=JPB595)). Against the picture of a self whose identity is never tied to its aims, he lists the ways people actually understand themselves: "as members of this family or community or nation or people, as bearers of this history, as sons and daughters of that revolution, as citizens of this republic" (Sandel, [Liberalism and the Limits of Justice, opened text](https://is.cuni.cz/studium/eng/predmety/index.php?do=download&did=330749&kod=JPB595)):

> Allegiances such as these are more than values I happen to have or aims I 'espouse at any given time'. They go beyond the obligations I voluntarily incur and the 'natural duties' I owe to human beings as such. They allow that to some I owe more than justice requires or even permits, not by reason of agreements I have made but instead in virtue of those more or less enduring attachments and commitments which taken together partly define the person I am.
>
> To imagine a person incapable of constitutive attachments such as these is not to conceive an ideally free and rational agent, but to imagine a person wholly without character, without moral depth.

(Sandel, Liberalism and the Limits of Justice; [opened text, Charles University repository](https://is.cuni.cz/studium/eng/predmety/index.php?do=download&did=330749&kod=JPB595).)

The practice follows from the definition. When you audit your commitments, some are not objects you hold; they are part of the holder. The question for those is not whether you chose them, but how you are living them.

### Plain words

Some of your ties are not items you own; they are part of what you are made of. You can revise your relationship to them, and you choose how to live them. The check is not whether you picked them, but whether you are living them well.

### For example

Suppose someone emigrates to start fresh. Some of what they carry may be revisable; some may turn out to be part of what they are. The exercise of finding which is which is the practice, and it does not end in one sitting.
