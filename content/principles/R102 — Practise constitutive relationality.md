> **Principle 364** — Practise constitutive relationality

Recognise that your identity is constituted by your relationships, not merely influenced by them. The communities you belong to, the traditions you inherit, the relationships you maintain — these are not external to your selfhood but constitutive of it. You cannot be fully yourself without your relational context.

(Sandel, 1982, Liberalism and the Limits of Justice; MacIntyre, 1981, After Virtue; Taylor, 1989, Sources of the Self.)

**Plain words** Your relationships don't just influence your identity — they constitute it. The communities, traditions, and bonds you maintain are not outside your selfhood; they're its material. You can't be fully yourself in a relational vacuum.

*For example:* Someone who emigrates and "starts fresh" — and discovers they're still made of the language, the table manners, the humour they thought they'd left; the self came along because it was never separable.
