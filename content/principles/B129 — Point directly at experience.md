> **Principle 129** — Point directly at experience

The teachings point at the moon; don't confuse the finger for the moon. Verify everything against immediate experience.

(Zen's direct pointing; Bahiya Sutta, Ud 1.10.)

**Plain words** The teachings point at the moon; don't confuse the finger for the moon. Verify everything against immediate experience. The map is not the territory, the menu is not the meal, and the commentary track is not the film.

*For example:* Reading about the taste of mangoes for years versus one bite — the teaching points; only the eating verifies.
