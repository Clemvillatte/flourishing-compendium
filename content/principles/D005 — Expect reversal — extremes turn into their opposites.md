> **Principle 5** — Expect reversal: extremes turn into their opposites

The Dao moves by reversal: what goes up comes down, what is stretched snaps back, what is full empties, what is over-protected is lost. Never build your life on one extreme.

(DDJ 9, 40, 58.)

**Plain words** The Dao moves by reversal: whatever gets stretched too far snaps back, whatever is over-protected gets lost, whatever is full empties. Extremes are unstable by nature. If you build your life on one extreme — all work, all intensity, all certainty — you're building on a hinge.

*For example:* The dieter who swings between strict restriction and total abandon keeps ending up back at the start; the person who builds modest habits keeps the weight off.
