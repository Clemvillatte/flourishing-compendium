> **Principle 5** — Expect reversal: extremes turn into their opposites

Chapter 40 of the Daodejing gives the rule as a property of the Dao itself:

> "The movement of the Tao / By contraries proceeds; / And weakness marks the course / Of Tao's mighty deeds." (Daodejing 40, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm))

Chapter 58 applies it to human categories: "Misery!—happiness is to be found by its side! Happiness!—misery lurks beneath it! Who knows what either will come to in the end?" Even correction, pushed as policy, "shall by a turn become distortion, and the good in it shall by a turn become evil" ([DDJ 58, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)).

Chapter 9 supplies the case studies. It is better to leave a vessel unfilled than to attempt to carry it when it is full; if you keep feeling a point that has been sharpened, the point cannot long preserve its sharpness; "when gold and jade fill the hall, their possessor cannot keep them safe"; "when wealth and honours lead to arrogancy, this brings its evil on itself" ([DDJ 9, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)). In each image the extreme fails from its own condition, not from outside attack.

The practical reading: reversal is a default expectation, not a surprise. Where you find a maximum, look for the opposite forming; where you find a rigid extreme, expect the flip. Chapter 9 ends with the sage's move: "when the work is done, and one's name is becoming distinguished, to withdraw into obscurity is the way of Heaven" ([DDJ 9, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)).

(DDJ 9, 40, 58.)

**Plain words**
Each extreme builds the pressure that undoes it. Total certainty stops you checking, so it leaves the one mistake that catches you out. Total indulgence creates the sickness that turns you puritan. The advice is not bland middle-of-the-road caution. Reversal is built into the situation, so plan for the swing instead of being surprised by it.

*For example:* The dieter who cycles between strict restriction and giving up entirely never meets the condition that would end the cycle: an ordinary amount, kept up. Each extreme produces the swing back.
