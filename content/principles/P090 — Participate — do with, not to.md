> **Principle 90** — Participate: do with, not to

The highest forms of agency are participatory: cooking with, not consuming; conversing, not lecturing; co-creating, not controlling. Aim at participation in every domain.

(Dewey, Democracy and Education, 1916; Addams, Twenty Years at Hull-House, 1910.)

**Plain words** The highest forms of agency are participatory — cooking with, not consuming; conversing, not lecturing; co-creating, not controlling. Aim at participation in every domain. The audience seat is comfortable and grows nothing.

*For example:* The parent who cooks with the kid versus the one who cooks for the kid — one dinner produces dinner and a cook. Participation compounds.

- **[[Cooperative learning|Cooperative learning]]**, structured interdependence raises performance and relations (Johnson and Johnson, 1989)
