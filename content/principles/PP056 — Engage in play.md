> **Principle 56** — Engage in play

Play is not the absence of seriousness. It is a different mode of engagement: voluntary, intrinsically motivated, and unconcerned with outcome. Research on play in adults shows associations with creativity, stress recovery, and well-being. The mechanism is the suspension of goal-directed striving, which allows new connections and possibilities to emerge.

(Brown, 2009; Magnuson and Barnett, 2013.)

**Plain words** Play is voluntary, intrinsically motivated and unconcerned with outcome. It isn't the opposite of seriousness; it's a different mode. In adults it's linked with creativity, recovery and well-being.

*For example:* Adults building an absurd sandcastle, no goal, laughing, fully absorbed.
