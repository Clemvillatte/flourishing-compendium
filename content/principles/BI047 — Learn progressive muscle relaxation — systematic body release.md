> **Principle 47** — Learn progressive muscle relaxation: systematic body release

Progressive muscle relaxation (PMR) involves systematically tensing and releasing muscle groups. It reduces physiological arousal, anxiety, and tension. It is a specific relaxation behaviour.

**Plain words** Progressive muscle relaxation: tense each muscle group for a few seconds, release, notice. The body relearns the difference between holding and letting go — a difference most adults have forgotten at the shoulders. Systematic, dull, effective.

*For example:* The first PMR session where the shoulders drop an inch on release and the shock is that they were up there at all — the body had been holding a meeting you weren't invited to.

## Practice

- Practise PMR: systematically tense and release muscle groups (feet → legs → abdomen → hands → shoulders → face). Hold tension 5 seconds, release for 15. Practise daily.
