> **Principle 500** — Practise the will to power

Recognise that the will to power is not domination over others but the drive toward self-overcoming, toward the enhancement of life. Nietzsche's will to power is the fundamental drive of all living things: not the will to survive but the will to grow, to expand, to overcome. Channel your will to power not toward domination but toward self-transcendence.

(Nietzsche, 1883/1961, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "Of Self-Overcoming"; Nietzsche, 1886/2001, Beyond Good and Evil, §§13, 259, 367; Nietzsche, 1885/1968, The Will to Power.)

**Plain words** The will to power is not domination over others — it's the drive toward self-overcoming, toward the enhancement of life: not the will to survive but the will to grow, expand, overcome. The fundamental drive of living things. Channel it upward, not sideways.

*For example:* Two ambitious people: one's will runs at other people (rank, wins, zero-sums), one's will runs at the self and the work (mastery, creation, growth). The tradition's point: the second one is what the drive actually is; the first is the drive, misdirected and stunted.
