> **Principle 65** — Cultivate compassion: it is sustainable empathy plus concern

Compassion is the warm-hearted wish that others be free from suffering, combined with the motivation to help. It is distinct from empathic distress (which is unsustainable) and from mere pity (which is hierarchical). Compassion can be trained and is associated with well-being and prosocial behaviour.

**Plain words** Compassion is the warm-hearted wish that others be free of suffering, plus the motivation to help — distinct from empathic distress (which burns out) and pity (which looks down). Sustainable empathy: feel with, and move toward help.

*For example:* The hospice volunteer who lasts twenty years — not because they feel less, but because their empathy converts: warmth plus the wish, then the practical act. The ones who burn out in six months feel equally; they feel without the off-ramp.
## Psychology parallels

- **Compassion training increases well-being and reduces empathic distress**, loving-kindness and compassion meditation shift from empathic distress to sustainable concern (Klimecki et al., 2013; Fredrickson et al., 2008)
- **Compassion is associated with better relationship outcomes**, [[Self-compassion|self-compassion]] and other-compassion predict better relationships (Neff & Beretvas, 2013)

## Practice

- Practise loving-kindness meditation: silently wish "may you be well, may you be free from suffering" toward yourself, a loved one, a neutral person, a difficult person, and all beings.
