> **Principle 35** — Balance calming and insight (samatha-vipassana; zhiguan)

Calming steadies the mind; insight sees clearly. Neither alone suffices: a calm mind that does not see is a still pond with no clarity; insight without calm scatters. Train both together.

(Zhiyi, Mohe Zhiguan; MN 149.)

**Plain words** Calming steadies the mind; insight sees clearly. Neither alone suffices — a calm mind that doesn't see is a still pond with no clarity; insight without calm scatters. Train both together: the pond needs to be still AND you need to look into it.

*For example:* Meditators who get very calm but see nothing, and analysts who see everything but can't stop shaking — both halves, one practice.
