> **Principle 72** — Practice letting go (vossagga)

Whatever arises: thought, mood, tension — release it. Not pushing away; allowing it to complete and dissolve, like a wave reaching shore.

(MN 118; Dhammapada 348.)

**Plain words** Whatever arises — thought, mood, tension — release it. Not pushing away; allowing it to complete and dissolve, like a wave reaching shore. Letting go isn't dropping; it's unclenching your grip on something that was leaving anyway.

*For example:* The exhale that ends the held breath — the body's own proof that release is just the completion of a cycle.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Nonattachment|Nonattachment]]**, a flexible, non-grasping relation to experience predicts well-being (Sahdra et al., 2010)
