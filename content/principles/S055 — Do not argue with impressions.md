> **Principle 177** — Do not argue with impressions

When an impression arises, do not fight it. Fighting it gives it energy. Simply note it: "this is an impression." Let it pass. The impression is not you; it is a visitor.

(Disc. I.1, I.20, III.3; Ench. 1; Med. VII.35.)

**Plain words** When an impression arises, don't wrestle it. Fighting gives it energy. Just note "this is an impression" and let it pass. It isn't you, it's a visitor.

*For example:* An anxious thought comes during a meeting. You label it and carry on with the agenda.
