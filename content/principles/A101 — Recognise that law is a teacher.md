> **Principle 101** — Recognise that law is a teacher

Law habituates citizens to virtue by requiring just action before they understand why. Good laws make good people. Respect the law not as a constraint but as an educator of character. Where laws are unjust, work to change them; do not simply break them.

(NE V.1, V.3, X.9; Politics III.1.)

**Plain words** Laws teach virtue by making you act justly before you fully understand why. Good laws help make good people. Respect them as teachers of character. Where they're unjust, work to change them instead of simply breaking them.

*For example:* Following a seat-belt law until it becomes a habit turns a rule into your own care.
