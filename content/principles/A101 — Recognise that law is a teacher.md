> **Principle 101** — Recognise that law is a teacher

Aristotle connects legislation with habituation:

> "legislators make the citizens good by forming habits in them"
> (NE II.1, W. D. Ross translation.)

In V.1, he describes law as commanding and forbidding acts associated with virtues, distinguishing rightly framed laws from hastily conceived ones. In X.9, he argues that practice, habituation and legislation remain necessary during adult life.

**Plain words** When judging a rule, ask what it repeatedly requires people to do and what habits it is intended to encourage.

*For example:* Imagined compendium analogy: a club requires members to declare conflicts before voting. A new member follows the procedure and considers whom it protects.
