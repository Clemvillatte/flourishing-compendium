> **Principle 7** — Treat social change as a normal condition, not a crisis

Treat rapid social change — migration, technological change, economic disruption, cultural pluralization — as a normal condition of modern life, not an aberration to be resisted. The adaptive stance is not to idealize a static past but to build the capacity to flourish amid change.

**Plain words** Social change is a constant, not an emergency — migration, technology, upheaval are the normal weather of modern life. Longing for a static past is nostalgia, not strategy. The skill is adaptation capacity, not time-freezing.

*For example:* The industry that "waited for things to go back to normal" — normal had left, permanently, and was never coming back. The ones who adapted had the same information. Different relationship to change.
