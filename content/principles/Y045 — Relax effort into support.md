> **Principle 45** — Relax effort into support

The method of the posture is given in the next sutra: relax the effort, and let attention rest on something boundless (the tradition reads "ananta" as the serpent-support of the cosmos, the infinite, or a steady support without end). The bounded formulation: after finding the position, spend as much effort relaxing into it as you spent getting there. Practically, once in position, exhale and deliberately release the jaw, shoulders, and hands.

(YS 2.47-2.48.)

**Plain words** The method of the posture, in the next sutra: relax the effort, and let attention rest on something boundless — the infinite, or simply a steady support without end. After finding the position, release the effort into support. The pose is held by letting go, not by gripping.

*For example:* In the held stretch or the held seat, someone finds the exact moment to stop trying — and the posture, suddenly supported rather than performed, becomes sustainable. Effort got you there; release keeps you there.
