> **Principle 66** — Rest in pure experience before the splits

Before experience is sorted into "subject" and "object" or "thought" and "thing," there is just experience: one flowing stuff. Return to that pre-dual layer regularly.

James's definition, in Essays in Radical Empiricism:

> "'Pure experience' is the name which I gave to the immediate flux of life which furnishes the material to our later reflection with its conceptual categories. Only new-born babes, or men in semi-coma from sleep, drugs, illnesses, or blows, may be assumed to have an experience pure in the literal sense of a that which is not yet any definite what, tho' ready to be all sorts of whats; full both of oneness and of manyness, but in respects that don't appear; changing throughout, yet so confusedly that its phases interpenetrate and no points, either of distinction or of identity, can be caught." (James, Essays in Radical Empiricism, [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/32547/32547-h/32547-h.htm))

The subject/object split, on James's account, is retrospective work: "The instant field of the present is at all times what I call the 'pure' experience. It is only virtually or potentially either object or subject as yet. For the time being, it is plain, unqualified actuality, or existence, a simple that." The doubling of a moment into a state of mind and a reality intended by it happens in retrospection.

(James, Essays in Radical Empiricism, 1912.)

**Plain words**
The sorting of a moment into what I thought and what happened is a second act; the moment itself arrives unsorted. The instruction to return to the pre-split layer is a return to what is already there before the filing starts. A practical handle: catch any moment before you name it, the light on the desk, the sound arriving, and stay a beat with it before the labels land.

**For example**
Totally absorbed in the swim — no swimmer, no water, just swimming. Then the mind files it into 'me' and 'pool.' The swimming was first.
