> **Principle 66** — Rest in pure experience before the splits

Before experience is sorted into "subject" and "object" or "thought" and "thing," there is just experience: one flowing stuff. Return to that pre-dual layer regularly.

(James, Essays in Radical Empiricism, 1912.)

**Plain words** Before experience is sorted into 'subject' and 'object' or 'thought' and 'thing,' there's just experience — one flowing stuff. Return to that pre-dual layer regularly. The fork in the road comes later; the road itself is first.

*For example:* Totally absorbed in the swim — no swimmer, no water, just swimming. Then the mind files it into 'me' and 'pool.' The swimming was first.
