> **Principle 131** — Treat weapons as a last resort

Fine weapons are inauspicious instruments: things detested; use them only when unavoidable. This applies to lawsuits, ultimatums, and burned bridges too.

(DDJ 31.)

**Plain words** Fine weapons are inauspicious instruments — things detested; use them only when unavoidable. This applies to lawsuits, ultimatums, and burned bridges too: every weapon you use in a relationship stays in the relationship forever.

*For example:* The spouse who invokes divorce in every argument has taught the marriage to live with a loaded gun in the room.
