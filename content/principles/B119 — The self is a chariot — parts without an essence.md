> **Principle 119** — The self is a chariot: parts without an essence

The "chariot" is just parts in working relation; the "self" is just body, feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness in working relation. Look for the owner and find only parts.

(Milindapanha; Anatta-lakkhana Sutta.)

**Plain words** The 'chariot' is just parts in working relation; the 'self' is just body, feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness in working relation. Look for the owner and find only parts. You've been searching the car for the driver — the driving is the driver.

*For example:* Dismantle the word 'team' and find no single thing — just people, plays, and a scoreboard in relation. Same with 'you'.
