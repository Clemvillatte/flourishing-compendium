> **Principle 114** — Create as growth: creation is the evidence of growth

Maslow's self-actualizing people are spontaneous creators: not necessarily artists, but people who approach life creatively. Treat each new situation as material for creation.

(Maslow, Toward a Psychology of Being, 1962.)

**Plain words** The self-actualizing people are spontaneous creators — not necessarily artists, but people who approach life creatively. Treat each new situation as material for creation. The dinner made from what's in the fridge is the same act as the symphony, at a different scale.

*For example:* The uncle who turns every family crisis into a bit, a plan, a production — nobody calls him an artist, and that's what he is. The situation was the medium.
