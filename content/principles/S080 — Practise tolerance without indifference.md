> **Principle 202** — Practise tolerance without indifference

The Stoic tolerates what cannot be changed but does not become indifferent to what should be changed. Tolerance of persons is not tolerance of injustice. Distinguish between accepting what is outside your control and working to change what is within it.

(Med. II.1, II.13, VII.22, IX.4, IX.11; Disc. I.12, I.18; Seneca, Letters 95, 107.)

**Plain words** The Stoic tolerates what can't be changed but isn't indifferent to what should be. Tolerating people isn't tolerating injustice. Distinguish accepting what's outside your control from working to change what's within it.

*For example:* Accepting that your father won't change, while still campaigning to improve the care home.
