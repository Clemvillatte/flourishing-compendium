> **Principle 167** — Practice congruence: align experience, awareness, and expression

Suffering grows in the gaps between what you feel, what you admit, and what you show: close the gaps. Be what you are, in awareness and expression.

(Rogers, On Becoming a Person, 1961.)

**Plain words** The feeling, the knowing, and the showing line up — and the psychic rent of pretending drops to zero. Suffering grows in the gaps between what you feel, what you admit, and what you show. Close the gaps.

*For example:* The exhaustion hidden for three months until the body starts showing it anyway — the gaps don't eliminate the truth, they just charge interest on it. Saying 'I'm burned out' in week one was always cheaper.
