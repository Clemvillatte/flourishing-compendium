> **Principle 62** — Let go of what exceeds your grasp

Whoever wants to grab the world and manage it: there's nothing to be done. Some things can't be taken hold of; release them.

(DDJ 29.)

**Plain words** Whoever wants to grab the world and manage it will find there's nothing to take hold of. Some things can't be gripped — release them. Knowing what exceeds your grasp isn't defeat; it's the information that frees your hands for what doesn't.

*For example:* You can't control whether someone loves you back; you can control whether you show up worth loving. One of these is your job.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Goal disengagement|Goal disengagement]]**, withdrawing effort from unattainable goals predicts lower cortisol and better well-being (Wrosch et al., 2003)
- **[[Sunk cost effect|Sunk-cost bias]]** (Arkes and Blumer, 1985)
