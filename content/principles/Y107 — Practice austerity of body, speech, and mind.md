> **Principle 107** — Practice austerity of body, speech, and mind

The Gita's triple austerity: of body (reverence, cleanliness, non-harm), of speech (true, beneficial, non-agitating speech), and of mind (serenity, gentleness, silence, self-mastery). The grading by intention matters: austerity performed for display is named as demonic. Practically, adopt one austerity per month, one per domain, and keep it private per principle 40.

(BG 17.14-17.19.)

**Plain words** Triple austerity: of body (reverence, cleanliness, non-harm), of speech (true, beneficial, non-agitating), of mind (serenity, gentleness, silence, self-mastery). The grading by intention matters: austerity performed for display is named as vanity. Check the audience of your discipline.

*For example:* The person doing the 5 a.m. cold-plunge routine who makes sure everyone at work hears about it — same practice as the silent one, graded entirely differently. The intention is the third austerity.
