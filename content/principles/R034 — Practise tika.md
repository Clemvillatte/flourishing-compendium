> **Principle 296** — Practise tika

Act with righteousness and justice. Tika means doing what is right, what is just, what is proper. It is the Māori equivalent of moral integrity. In every decision, ask: is this tika? Is this right?

(Māori tikanga; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori; Durie, 1994, Whaiora.)

**Plain words** Tika is the question "is this right?" applied to every decision — moral integrity as a habit of asking. Not "is this allowed?" or "is this normal?" — right. Justice, propriety, straightness.

*For example:* The builder who tells the client the cheaper option is worse, losing the upsell, because the question they actually work by is tika, not margin.
