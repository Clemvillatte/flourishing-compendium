> **Principle 212** — See that life is short and do not waste it

Seneca argues that much of life is wasted (ch. 1):

> "It is not that we have a short space of time, but that we waste much of it. Life is long enough, and it has been given in sufficiently generous measure to allow the accomplishment of the very greatest things if the whole of it is well invested." (Seneca, On the Shortness of Life 1, trans. John W. Basore, [Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/On_the_shortness_of_life/Chapter_I))

In chapter 2, Seneca cites a poet: "The part of life we really live is small." He then adds: "For all the rest of existence is not life, but merely time."

(Seneca, On the Shortness of Life 1-2.)

**Plain words**
The hours you have and the way you use them are different questions. Review an ordinary day and identify one optional activity you would choose differently.

*For example (imagined):* Tracking a week in half-hour blocks and finding that three hours a day vanish into scrolling.
