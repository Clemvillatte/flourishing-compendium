> **Principle 135** — Teach without words

The sage practices the teaching that uses no words. Model the behavior; skip the lecture.

(DDJ 2, 43.)

**Plain words** Model the behavior; skip the lecture. People learn what they watch you do, and almost nothing from what you tell them to do. Your example is the syllabus.

*For example:* The parent who reads books raises readers; the one who mandates reading raises kids who hide comic books inside textbooks.

- **[[Observational learning|Observational learning]]**, modeling is the primary channel of behavior transmission (Bandura, 1977)
