> **Principle 15** — Classify the mind's movements into five types

The tradition offers a compact taxonomy of all mental activity: right knowledge, error, imagination, sleep, and memory. The use is diagnostic. Practically, when the mind churns, name which of the five is operating before deciding what to do with it; naming the type selects the tool.

(YS 1.5-1.6.)

**Plain words** The five movements of mind: right knowledge, error, imagination, sleep, memory. The use is diagnostic — when the mind churns, name which of the five is operating before deciding what to do with it. Naming the type is half the handling.

*For example:* 2 a.m.: is this real knowledge, error, imagination, or memory? Someone runs the checklist and finds it's imagination — a rehearsal of a meeting that hasn't happened. Different type, different tool: not solving, just labeling fiction as fiction.
