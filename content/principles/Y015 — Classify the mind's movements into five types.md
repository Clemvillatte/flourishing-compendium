> **Principle 15** — Classify the mind's movements into five types

Patanjali's inventory, in the Johnston (1912) rendering:

> "The psychic activities are five; they are either subject or not subject to the five hindrances (Book II, 3)." (Yoga Sutras 1.5, [Johnston translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2526/pg2526.txt))
>
> "These activities are: Sound intellection, unsound intellection, predication, sleep, memory." (1.6)

An interpretive translation gives the same five with the Sanskrit terms attached:

> "The five varieties of thought patterns to witness are: 1) knowing correctly (pramana), 2) incorrect knowing (viparyaya), 3) fantasy or imagination (vikalpa), 4) the object of void-ness that is deep sleep (nidra), and 5) recollection or memory (smriti)." (1.6)

The third item is the one the 2 a.m. checklist turns on most, and the Sutras define it by its objectlessness:

> "Fantasy or imagination (vikalpa) is a thought pattern that has verbal expression and knowledge, but for which there is no such object or reality in existence." (1.9)

Johnston's rendering of the same sutra: "Predication is carried on through words or thoughts not resting on an object perceived." Each of the five is defined in the Sutras by its relation to its object, so naming the type already says something about where the thought gets its content.

(YS 1.5-1.6.)

**Plain words**
The taxonomy earns its keep at 2 a.m. A thought arrives; before arguing with it, ask where it got its content. Right knowledge rests on what was actually perceived; error rests on a misperception; imagination has words and pictures but no object; sleep rests on mind states; memory holds a past perception unmodified. The type names the relation to reality, and the relation selects the response: imagination does not get solved, it gets recognized as fiction.

**For example**
The 2 a.m. rehearsal of a meeting that has not happened runs the checklist: it has verbal expression and pictures, but no object in existence, the imagination type. Recognizing the type is the handling; there is nothing in it to solve.
