> **Principle 16** — Practice proper ambition (philotimia)

Honour is valued by the magnanimous person, but not as the highest good. Seek recognition for the right things and in the right measure. Do not chase honour as if it were the point; do not refuse it when it comes deservedly.

(NE IV.4.)

**Plain words** Honour matters to the person of good character, but it isn't the top goal. Seek recognition for the right things, in proportion. Don't chase it for itself, and don't pretend you don't want it when you've earned it.

*For example:* Accepting a promotion you worked for, with thanks and without false modesty, is proper ambition. Angling for a title just to be seen with it isn't.
