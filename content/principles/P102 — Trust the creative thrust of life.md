> **Principle 102** — Trust the creative thrust of life

Life is not a mechanism running down but a current that pushes toward creation: in you as everywhere. When you feel stuck, remember the current: small creative acts reconnect you to it.

(Bergson, Creative Evolution, 1907 — secularized as the tendency of living systems to create.)

**Plain words** Stuckness is rarely the absence of energy; it's blocked direction. Life is a current that pushes toward creation — small creative acts reconnect you to it; the current is still there, waiting for a channel.

*For example:* The dead month that ends with rearranging one room — one small act, and suddenly you're cooking again, calling people, alive. The current needed a crack, not a dam removal.
