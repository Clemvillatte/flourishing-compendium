> **Principle 127** — No one is worthless

The good person is the bad person's teacher; the bad person is the good person's work. Don't abandon people; don't discard things.

(DDJ 27.)

**Plain words** Nobody is worthless — everyone is either teaching you something or asking something of you, often both at once. Don't abandon people; don't discard things.

*For example:* The colleague whose mistakes taught you more than the manuals — the 'bad' one was curriculum in disguise.
