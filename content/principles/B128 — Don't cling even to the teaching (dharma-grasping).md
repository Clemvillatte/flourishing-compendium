> **Principle 128** — Don't cling even to the teaching (dharma-grasping)

Making "being Buddhist" an identity is just a new costume for the self. Practice, don't perform.

(MN 22; Dhammapada 97.)

**Plain words** Making 'being Buddhist' an identity is just a new costume for the self. Practice, don't perform. The tradition's own warning label: the practice can become the most sophisticated hiding place the ego ever finds.

*For example:* The person who mentions their meditation practice in every conversation — the practice has become the identity it was meant to dissolve.
