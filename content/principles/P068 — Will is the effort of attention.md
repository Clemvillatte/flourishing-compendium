> **Principle 68** — Will is the effort of attention

The essential phenomenon of will is attending to a difficult but chosen object and holding it there. Strengthen attention, and you strengthen will.

(James, The Principles of Psychology, 1890; The Will to Believe, 1896.)

**Plain words** Willpower isn't a muscle of grit; it's the skill of keeping your eyes on the chosen thing. Will is attention held on a difficult but chosen object — strengthen one and you strengthen the other.

*For example:* The open book, the buzzing phone — the whole of 'willpower' is returning the eyes to the page, again, and again, and again. That's the entire mechanism.
