> **Principle 37** — Use epistemic diversity as a resource

Use epistemic diversity — the inclusion of different perspectives, backgrounds, and cognitive styles in knowledge communities — as a resource. Diverse groups outperform homogeneous groups on complex problems (Hong and Page, 2004; Page, 2007); the mechanism is that diversity brings different heuristics, information, and perspectives to bear.

**Plain words** Epistemic diversity is a resource — different perspectives and cognitive styles make groups better on complex problems. Homogeneous groups are faster and wronger. Comfort is the price of the better answer.

*For example:* The team of five identical thinkers that solves the problem in ten minutes — and the mixed team that argues for an hour and finds the flaw the fast team shipped. The discomfort was the work.
