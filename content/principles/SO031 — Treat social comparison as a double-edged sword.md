> **Principle 31** — Treat social comparison as a double-edged sword

Treat social comparison — the tendency to evaluate oneself against others — as a double-edged sword. Upward comparison can inspire; it can also deflate. Downward comparison can comfort; it can also breed complacency or schadenfreude. Social media has industrialized comparison (see principle 76). The adaptive stance is deliberate comparison management.

**Plain words** Comparison is a double-edged tool — upward can inspire or deflate; downward can comfort or corrode. Social media has industrialised the comparison engine and set it to "upward, curated." Know the tool; handle with intention.

*For example:* Scrolling a feed of promotions and beaches — the comparison is with a highlight reel edited by professionals. You're comparing your whole life to a trailer. That's not information; that's a beating.

## Practice

- In comparison, ask: is this inspiring or deflating? If deflating, either reframe or limit the comparison (see principle 76). In social media use, curate feeds to reduce upward comparison.
