> **Principle 53** — Practice periodic cleansing and renewal

The Mesoamerican tradition of limpias (cleansings), sweatbaths (the temazcal), and threshold rituals survives in living practice; they are kept here as psychological and communal technologies, with the specific theology excluded. The principle is the deliberate, periodic clearing of accumulated residue (physical, emotional, domestic, relational), on a schedule rather than on a crisis. Practically, adopt one cleansing practice per scale: daily (a wash with intention), seasonal (a house clearing), annual (a retreat or confession-shaped review).

(The temazcal and limpia traditions as presented in the ethnographic literature; marked as kept-for-practice.)

**Plain words** The tradition of cleansings, sweatbaths, and threshold rituals — kept here as psychology and communal technology. The principle: the deliberate, periodic clearing of accumulated residue (physical, emotional, domestic, relational) on a schedule, rather than when the residue becomes a crisis.

*For example:* The couple with the standing quarterly "clearing" dinner — every three months, the resentments and the logistics both get emptied onto the table — so nothing accumulates into the fifth year's explosion.
