> **Principle 53** — Practice periodic cleansing and renewal

The sweat bath has a documented history in Mesoamerica, mainly as a healing space. A chapter in an Oxford history of North American steam baths reports that "Pregnant women were cared for inside steam baths before, during, and after childbirth", and that afterbirth was often buried inside a family's steam bath ([First Medicine, OUP](https://academic.oup.com/book/44925/chapter/384843056?guestAccessKey=)). The Florentine Codex, as quoted in a study of Mayan midwifery, describes an Aztec midwife who "heated the sweat bath, and she put the maiden in the sweat bath, where she massaged the pregnant woman's abdomen" (Sahagún, 1969b, p. 155, quoted in [Developing Destinies, OUP](https://academic.oup.com/book/26650/chapter/195394068)).

(The temazcal and limpia traditions as presented in the ethnographic literature; marked as kept-for-practice.)

### Health cautions and the limits of this page

A study of Mayan families in highland Guatemala found that traditional wood-fired temazcales, which are small and poorly ventilated, were associated with higher excretion of mutagenic metabolites from wood smoke ([Mutagenesis, 2014](https://academic.oup.com/mutage/article/29/5/367/2763469)). The temazcal also appears in sources as a postpartum practice believed to "normalise, heal, and warm the uterus" ([PMC study of Guatemalan postpartum practice](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3799972/)).

I did not find evidence that periodic cleansing rituals clear emotional or relational "residue", and I did not open sources on limpias. The principle is the compendium's modern translation, kept for practice.

**Plain words**
Plan a regular reset, in the body, the home or a relationship, instead of waiting for a crisis. If you try a sweat bath, treat the smoke and the heat as real risks.

**For example**
Pick one reset for each scale: a ten-minute tidy at the end of each day, a monthly clear-out of one cupboard, and a yearly honest conversation with a partner or friend. Put them in the calendar now.
