> **Principle 53** — Practice recognizing the kindness you have received

The lamrim's gratitude step, systematized: every capacity you have was given (language, food, roads, the people who raised you), and the tradition trains a deliberate meditation on this, not as etiquette but as an accurate correction of the mind's default self-authorship. Practically, once per week, trace one current capability back through the hands that gave it to you, by name where you know them.

(Tsongkhapa's lamrim, the kindness-of-others meditation.)

**Plain words** Everything you can do was given: language, food, roads, the people who raised you. The tradition trains a deliberate meditation on that, as an accurate correction to the mind's default belief that you made yourself. Once a week, trace one capability back through the hands that gave it.

*For example:* Tracing how you came to cook: your grandmother, the market vendor, the farmer, the person who wrote the recipe.
