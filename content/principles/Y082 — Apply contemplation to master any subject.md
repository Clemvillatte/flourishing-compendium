> **Principle 82** — Apply contemplation to master any subject

The tradition's own application layer: samyama directed at a subject yields knowledge of it, and the sutras run through a catalogue (the body, the sequences of change, the mind itself). The bounded formulation is the learning principle: sustained, absorbed, multi-pass attention to a subject produces knowledge that scattered study cannot. Practically, apply the three-part attention skill to one domain of life per quarter.

(YS 3.16-3.29, the applications.)

**Plain words** The tradition's own application layer: sustained, absorbed, multi-pass attention directed at a subject yields knowledge of it — the sutras run through a whole catalogue. The bounded formulation is the learning principle: mastery is samyama pointed at a field.

*For example:* The diagnostician who has absorbed ten thousand ECGs — not memorized, absorbed, multi-pass — and now "just sees" the arrhythmia. The tradition would recognize the mechanism: contemplation applied to a subject.
