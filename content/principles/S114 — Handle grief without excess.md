> **Principle 236** — Handle grief without excess

Grief is natural. The Stoic feels it but does not let it become a passion. Seneca's consolation letters model the movement from grief to acceptance: feel, acknowledge, and then return to life.

(Seneca, Letters 63, 99, 107; Med. IV.1, IV.41; Disc. I.11, III.24; Ench. 16.)

**Plain words** Grief is natural. The Stoic feels it without letting it become a passion. Seneca's consolations move from grief toward acceptance: feel it, acknowledge it, then return to life.

*For example:* You cry at your friend's funeral, and next week you cook for his family.
