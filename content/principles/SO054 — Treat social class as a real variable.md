> **Principle 54** — Treat social class as a real variable

Treat social class — one's position in the economic and cultural hierarchy — as a real variable, not a taboo. Class shapes health, education, opportunity, and culture (Bourdieu, 1984; Lareau, 2011). The adaptive stance is to see class clearly, without shame or contempt.

**Plain words** Class is real — it shapes health, education, opportunity, and culture. Neither taboo nor destiny. See it clearly: the habitus you inherited, the capital you have or lack. Not shame, not pride — accuracy.

*For example:* "We don't talk about money" and "the right schools" — class is a set of inherited instructions nobody hands you the manual for. Naming the class effects out loud is how they stop running your life silently.
