> **Principle 320** — Practise filial piety (xiao)

Honour your parents and ancestors. Filial piety is not mere obedience; it is the recognition of the debt you owe to those who gave you life, nurtured you, and made you who you are. Honour them through care, respect, and the continuation of their values.

(Confucius, The Analects 1.2, 1.11, 2.5, 2.7, 2.8; Mencius, The Mencius 4A18, 4A19.)

**Plain words** Xiao is filial piety: honouring parents and ancestors — not blind obedience, but recognising the debt to the ones who gave, nurtured, and made you. Care, respect, and carrying forward what was worth carrying.

*For example:* The son who drives his mother to every appointment and listens to the same stories — not because he always agrees with her, but because he knows exactly what he's repaying.
