> **Principle 26** — Practice patience with others' faults

The patient person bears minor wrongs without complaint. Not every slight requires a response. Not every injustice needs correction. Distinguish what must be addressed from what should be let go.

(NE IV.5; EE III.7; MM II.7.)

**Plain words** Not every slight deserves a reply. The patient person lets small wrongs pass and saves effort for what matters. The skill is sorting. Some things must be addressed, some should be let go, and the difference is a judgement you make each time.

*For example:* A neighbour's bin left out for two days isn't worth a note. A neighbour's dog attacking yours is.
