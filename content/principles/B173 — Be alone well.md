> **Principle 173** — Be alone well

Cultivate the capacity to be alone without collapse or distraction: solitude is where you find out who you are when no one is watching.

(MN 122; Dhammapada 61-62.)

**Plain words** Cultivate the capacity to be alone without collapse or distraction — solitude is where you find out who you are when no one is watching. If every quiet moment gets filled with a screen, the appointment with yourself keeps getting rescheduled.

*For example:* The solo dinner at a restaurant, phone in the pocket — forty minutes of discomfort, then a strange, sturdy calm.
