> **Principle 166** — Act as a member of the whole

The Stoic sees themselves as a part of a larger whole: the cosmos, the human community, the natural order. No action is purely private. Act with awareness of your place in the whole.

(Med. II.3, IV.10, V.8, V.30, VI.42, VII.9, VII.19, VII.47, VIII.7, VIII.34, VIII.50, VIII.52, X.1, X.6, X.33, XI.1, XI.18, XII.36; Disc. I.12, I.14, I.25, III.10, III.24; Seneca, Letters 71, 95, 107; Ench. 31, 53.)

**Plain words** You're a part of larger wholes: the cosmos, the human community, the natural order. No action is purely private. Act aware of your place in the whole.

*For example:* Throwing away a plastic bottle becomes a slightly different act when you remember where it ends up.
