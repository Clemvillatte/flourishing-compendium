> **Principle 21** — Value the process over the outcome

Engagement that is oriented to the process, not the outcome, is more sustainable and more satisfying. The athlete who runs for the running, not the medal, is more likely to persist and to experience flow. The artist who paints for the painting, not the exhibition, is more likely to produce work that matters. Outcome orientation, when it becomes primary, undermines the intrinsic motivation that produces the best work.

(Ryan and Deci, 2000; Vansteenkiste et al., 2010.)

**Plain words** Engagement focused on the process lasts longer and satisfies more. The runner who loves running is likelier to keep going and find flow than one chasing a medal. [[Process versus outcome focus|Outcome focus]] can still matter, but it's fragile when results disappoint.

*For example:* Writing every morning because you like it, whether or not the book sells.
