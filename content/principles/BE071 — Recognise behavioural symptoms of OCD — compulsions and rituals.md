> **Principle 71** — Recognise behavioural symptoms of OCD: compulsions and rituals

OCD produces specific behavioural symptoms: compulsions (checking, washing, ordering, counting) and rituals. These are symptoms, not choices. They are treatable with exposure and response prevention (ERP).

**Plain words** OCD's prints are the rituals — checking, washing, counting, the exact right order — driven by intrusive thoughts. The compulsion isn't quirk; it's the fear's tax collection. It's treatable, specifically and well.

*For example:* The twenty-minute shower that "just feels right" — not fastidiousness, but the disorder's schedule. That's a referral-shaped symptom, not a hygiene preference.
## Psychology parallels

- **OCD and compulsions**, compulsive behaviours are core symptoms (APA, 2022)
- **ERP is the gold standard**, exposure and response prevention is effective for OCD (Foa et al., 2007; Abramowitz et al., 2019)

## Practice

- If you have compulsive behaviours, recognise them as symptoms, not choices. Treatment is ERP: approach the obsession without doing the compulsion. Seek professional help; OCD is treatable.
