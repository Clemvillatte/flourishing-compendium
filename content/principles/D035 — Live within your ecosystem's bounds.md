> **Principle 35** — Live within your ecosystem's bounds

Be close enough to what you need: neighbors in sight, no compulsion to chase the distant. Boundaries and self-sufficiency over dependence on far-away things.

(DDJ 80.)

**Plain words** Be close enough to what you need: neighbours in sight, no compulsion to chase the distant. The longer your supply lines, the more fragile your life.

*For example:* The person whose groceries, friends, gym, and work are all within a walk has a sturdier week than the one commuting across a city for each.
