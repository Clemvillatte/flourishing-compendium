> **Principle 48** — Think reflectively: turn experience over

In the first chapter of *How We Think*, Dewey uses Columbus to separate two kinds of thinking. "Men *thought* the world was flat until Columbus *thought* it to be round," he writes. The earlier belief was held "because men had not the energy or the courage to question what those about them accepted and taught". Columbus, in contrast, "did not accept unhesitatingly the current traditional theory", and "went on thinking until he could produce evidence for both his confidence and his disbelief." Dewey adds that even if Columbus had been wrong, "it would have been a different sort of belief from those it antagonized, because it was reached by a different method" ([Dewey, *How We Think*, 1910, ch. 1 §1, Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/37423/pg37423.txt)).

> "Active, persistent, and careful consideration of any belief or supposed form of knowledge in the light of the grounds that support it, and the further conclusions to which it tends, constitutes reflective thought." (John Dewey, *How We Think*, 1910, ch. 1 §1, [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/37423/pg37423.txt))

At the end of the same chapter he says what this takes: "To maintain the state of doubt and to carry on systematic and protracted inquiry--these are the essentials of thinking." Experience gives you material for that work, and the work is done on purpose.

**Plain words** Having a hard week and learning from a hard week are different things. To learn from it, write down what happened, what you believed at the time, and what the evidence was for and against that belief. Then ask what that suggests doing next. Dewey says this takes persistence, so a single quick look will not do it.

*For example:* After a difficult month at work, someone sets aside fifteen minutes one Friday. They write down what went wrong, which assumption each problem rested on, and one thing to try differently next month. This is my illustration of the method, not Dewey's own example.
