> **Principle 40** — Learn to manage caffeine: it affects anxiety and sleep

Caffeine is a stimulant that can worsen anxiety and panic and disrupt sleep. It has a half-life of ~5-6 hours, meaning afternoon caffeine affects night sleep.

**Plain words** Caffeine is a real anxiogenic — it can trigger and worsen anxiety, panic, and sleep loss. The jittery, wound, heart-racing feeling may be your coffee, not your life. Cut it for two weeks and see what's actually yours.

*For example:* The person medicating anxiety while drinking five coffees a day — one of these is treatable in a fortnight. The anxiety that remains after the caffeine trial is the real one.
## Psychology parallels

- **Caffeine and anxiety**, caffeine can worsen anxiety and panic symptoms (Lara, 2010; Broderick & Benjamin, 2004)
- **Caffeine and sleep**, afternoon caffeine disrupts night sleep (Drake et al., 2013; Clark & Landolt, 2017)

## Practice

- Manage caffeine: limit to 1-2 cups per day, none after noon. If prone to anxiety or insomnia, reduce or eliminate. Notice the effect on your body and mood.
