> **Principle 42** — Develop calm (samatha)

Let the mind settle into stillness, concentration deepening until the body is tranquil and the mind unified. Calm is the platform on which insight sees.

(MN 118; Dhammapada 372.)

**Plain words** Let the mind settle into stillness, concentration deepening until the body is tranquil and the mind unified. Calm is the platform on which insight sees. You can't photograph the bottom of a lake while stomping through it.

*For example:* The difference between re-reading the same paragraph five times agitated, and once, settled — same text, different water.
