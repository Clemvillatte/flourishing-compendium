> **Principle 118** — Understand shame (aischynē)

Shame is a pain or disturbance concerning evils that seem to bring us into disrepute. Shame involves a judgment about reputation: that what we have done would diminish us in the eyes of others. Distinguish healthy shame (which guides behaviour) from toxic shame (which attacks the self).

(Rhetoric II.6; NE IV.9.)

**Plain words** Shame is distress about things that would bring disrepute. It judges that what you did would diminish you in others' eyes. Healthy shame guides behaviour. Toxic shame attacks the person. Learn to tell them apart.

*For example:* Feeling uneasy about padding an expense claim is healthy. Replaying an awkward comment from years ago and feeling worthless isn't.
