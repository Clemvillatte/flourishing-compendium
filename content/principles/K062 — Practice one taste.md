> **Principle 62** — Practice one taste

The Kagyu presentation names one taste (ro-gcig) as the third of the four yogas of mahamudra. The Ninth Karmapa's root text, in Alexander Berzin's translation: "When you have the stable realization that, whether atop appearances or voidness, whichever one, on each, all phenomena of the pathway minds, as many as there might be, are complete in terms of having the single taste of the essential nature that you have stably realized as the (ultimate) Dharma, (this is) the lesser (stage of) the single taste" ([Mahamudra: Eliminating the Darkness of Ignorance](https://studybuddhism.com/en/tibetan-buddhism/original-texts/tantra-texts/mahamudra-eliminating-the-darkness-of-ignorance/enhancing-mahamudra-meditation)). The middling stage widens the pair: "when compulsive existence and the tranquil peace (of nirvana) dawn, straight as an arrow, as equal; (in other words, when) uncontrollably recurring samsara and released (nirvana dawn, straight as an arrow,) as inseparable, (this is) the middling (stage) of the single taste." The differences are not erased — the taste is of the essence, across them. The commentary glosses: "The abiding foundation of the here and now is the single taste of both appearances and voidness in the pristine awareness of the inseparability of these two. Thus whether you are aware of an appearance or its voidness, you see the single taste of both" ([Beru Khyentse Rinpoche's commentary](https://studybuddhism.com/en/advanced-studies/vajrayana/mahamudra-advanced/commentary-on-mahamudra-eliminating-the-darkness-of-ignorance-beru-khyentse-rinpoche/karma-kagyu-mahamudra-enhancing-the-meditation)). And the stage's work runs between sitting and the rest of the day: "your total absorptions and subsequent attainments were mixed (in a single taste)."

This entry's translation of the test is transfer: what you can access on the cushion you can access at the sink. Practically, take your best moment of the morning sitting and deliberately locate the same register during your worst task of the afternoon.

**Plain words** One taste is the third yoga of mahamudra. The claim is not that pleasant and unpleasant become the same, but that the nature you saw is present in both: the taste of the essence is one whether you are aware of an appearance or of its voidness, whether the period is formal meditation or the rest of the day. So the test is transfer: the register you can reach sitting should also be findable at the sink, in traffic, in the awkward phone call.

For example: The calm you find on the cushion holds, a little, when your phone rings at an awkward moment.
