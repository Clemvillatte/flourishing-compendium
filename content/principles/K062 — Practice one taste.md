> **Principle 62** — Practice one taste

The lineage's instruction on the equality of experience: the meditator who has seen the nature of mind finds the same taste in meditation and post-meditation, in pleasant and unpleasant, in sacred and ordinary, not because the differences vanish but because the underlying field does not change. The practical test is transfer: what you can access on the cushion you can access at the sink. Practically, take your best moment of the morning sitting and deliberately locate the same register during your worst task of the afternoon.

(The Mahamudra one-taste instruction; Gampopa's presentation.)

**Plain words** One taste means that someone who has seen the nature of mind tastes the same in meditation and out of it, in pleasant and unpleasant, in sacred and ordinary. The differences remain; the underlying field doesn't change. The test is transfer: what you can reach on the cushion should be there in traffic.

*For example:* The calm you find on the cushion holds, a little, when your phone rings at an awkward moment.
