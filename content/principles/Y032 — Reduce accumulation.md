> **Principle 32** — Reduce accumulation

Non-grasping (aparigraha) is defined as taking only what is needed, and the tradition ties excess accumulation directly to mental turbulence: every possession is an appointment for attention. Practically, apply the subtraction test: before acquiring, name what you will remove; before keeping, name what it costs you to store, guard, and think about.

(YS 2.39; BG 13.7-11, non-attachment and non-possessiveness in the virtue list.)

**Plain words** Non-grasping: take only what is needed — because every possession is an appointment for attention, and excess accumulation is tied directly to mental turbulence. Apply the subtraction test: before acquiring, name what you'll remove.

*For example:* Someone buys the fourth jacket and feels the flicker of turbulence — nothing to wear it to, nowhere to put it, one more appointment on a crowded calendar. The tradition's diagnosis predates the closet by two millennia.
