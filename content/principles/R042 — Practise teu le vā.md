> **Principle 304** — Practise teu le vā

Beautify and care for the relational space. Teu le vā is the active practice of maintaining, nurturing, and beautifying the space between people. It includes being courteous, thoughtful, and intentional in how you relate to others.

(Samoan philosophy; Tuhiwai Smith, 1999, Decolonizing Methodologies; Wendt, 1996.)

**Plain words** Teu le vā: actively beautify the relational space. Courtesy, thoughtfulness, the deliberate small gesture — the space between people is kept the way a garden is kept, by tending. Neglect is also a choice, just a worse one.

*For example:* The colleague who always leaves the shared kitchen better than they found it, remembers birthdays, sends the thank-you text — not sainthood, just someone who gardens the between.
