> **Principle 113** — Rest deeply

The True Person sleeps without dreams and wakes without sorrow: quality rest is a spiritual practice, not downtime.

(Zhuangzi 6.)

**Plain words** Quality rest is a spiritual practice, not downtime. The day is built on the night before it — treating sleep as an inconvenience is treating the foundation as decoration.

*For example:* Two identical Mondays: one after eight hours' sleep, one after five and coffee. The difference isn't effort — it's the night.

## Psychology parallels

- **Sleep as overnight emotional therapy**, REM sleep recalibrates affective memory (Walker and van der Helm, 2009)
- **[[Sleep deprivation|Sleep deprivation]]** (Yoo et al., 2007), increases amygdala reactivity
