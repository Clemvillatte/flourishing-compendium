> **Principle 127** — Take no experience as final

The neti neti procedure applied to attainments: every state, however luminous, is a state, and the tradition's counsel is to use it and pass through. The Buddhist raft parable makes the same point: the teaching that carries you across is the first thing to release on arrival. Practically, when a breakthrough arrives, write it down, thank it, and schedule the return to basics within the week.

(Brihadaranyaka 2.3.6 applied; Buddhist tradition, Alagaddupama Sutta, MN 22.)

**Plain words** Neti neti applied to attainments: every state, however luminous, is a state — use it and pass through. The Buddhist raft parable says the same: the teaching that carries you across is the first thing to release on arrival. Take no experience as final.

*For example:* The retreat's transcendent week — treated as a room passed through, not a home. The practitioner who moves on carries the benefit; the one who camps there carries the memory.
