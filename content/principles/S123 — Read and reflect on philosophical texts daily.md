> **Principle 245** — Read and reflect on philosophical texts daily

The Stoics recommend daily reading as part of the philosophical life. Not for accumulation of knowledge but for transformation of character. Read a passage, sit with it, apply it. One passage, deeply absorbed, is worth more than a book skimmed.

(Seneca, Letters 2, 16, 108; Med. I.1, I.6; Disc. I.4, II.17, IV.9; Musonius Rufus, Lectures.)

**Plain words** Read philosophy the way you'd eat a good meal — one bite at a time, chewed properly. The Stoics wanted daily reading that changes you, not trivia that fills you. One passage genuinely absorbed and applied beats a whole book skimmed.

*For example:* Someone who reads two pages of the Meditations with their morning coffee and carries one line around all day like a stone in their pocket.
