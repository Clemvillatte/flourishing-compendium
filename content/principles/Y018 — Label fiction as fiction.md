> **Principle 18** — Label fiction as fiction

Imagination (vikalpa) is defined with precision: cognition that follows from words alone, empty of corresponding substance. It is not useless; planning and empathy are vikalpa. The practice is labeling: knowing when you are in construction rather than perception, so the construction can serve you instead of steering you. Practically, in rumination, find the sentence that is pure word-spin ("everyone thinks...") and mark it as fiction.

(YS 1.9.)

**Plain words** Imagination is cognition from words alone — empty of corresponding substance. Not useless: planning and empathy are imagination. The practice is labeling — knowing when you're in construction rather than perception — so the construction doesn't get mistaken for the world.

*For example:* Someone spends a full anxious week inside the imagined version of the difficult conversation — every retort, every catastrophic reply — and the actual conversation, when it finally happens, matches none of it. The week was spent in fiction, unlabeled.
