> **Principle 169** — Eat mindfully

Be present for the meal: the food, the many hands it passed through, the body receiving it. Eating is a daily appointment with gratitude.

(Thích Nhất Hạnh; MN 10.)

**Plain words** Be present for the meal — the food, the many hands it passed through, the body receiving it. Eating is a daily appointment with gratitude. Three times a day, a free gratitude practice with snacks included.

*For example:* The first mindful bite of the day — the warmth, the hands that grew it — versus the meal inhaled over a keyboard.
