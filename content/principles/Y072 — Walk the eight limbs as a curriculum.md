> **Principle 72** — Walk the eight limbs as a curriculum

The limbs are given as a sequence: restraint, observance, posture, breath regulation, sense withdrawal, concentration, absorption, and integration. The sequence is a dependency map: each limb creates the conditions for the next, and when a later limb fails, the fault is usually in an earlier one. Practically, treat the sequence as a diagnostic grid rather than a ladder to climb once.

(YS 2.28-2.29; YS 2.54-3.3.)

**Plain words** The eight limbs as a sequence: restraint, observance, posture, breath, withdrawal, concentration, absorption, integration. It's a dependency map — each limb creates the conditions for the next. When a later limb fails, the fault is usually upstream. Walk the curriculum in order.

*For example:* Someone struggling with concentration (limb six) for years — and the audit shows the failure upstream: no ethics (limb one) in a conduct war, no steady seat (limb three), no withdrawal (limb five). The concentration was never the problem; it was the symptom.
