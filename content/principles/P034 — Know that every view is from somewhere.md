> **Principle 34** — Know that every view is from somewhere

There is no view from nowhere: all knowing is positioned, interested, partial: and can still be disciplined and honest. State your position; don't pretend you don't have one.

(Nietzsche, The Gay Science, 1882; Bernstein, The Pragmatic Turn, 2010.)

**Plain words** There's no view from nowhere — all knowing is positioned, interested, partial, and can still be disciplined and honest. State your position; don't pretend you don't have one. The claimed neutrality is the most suspect position on the field.

*For example:* The report commissioned by the company that profits from its findings — the data may be fine, but the position statement should be the first page.
