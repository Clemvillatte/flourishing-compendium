> **Principle 103** — Accept restlessness: it is the human condition

We are the animals that cannot stop striving, questioning, wanting to be otherwise. Don't pathologize restlessness; harness it — it is the energy of growth.

(James, The Varieties of Religious Experience, 1902; Nooteboom, Process Philosophy: A Synthesis, 2025 — the "restless" condition.)

**Plain words** The itch that never fully scratches is not a design flaw; it's the engine spec. We are the animals that cannot stop striving — don't pathologize restlessness, harness it.

*For example:* Retirement that lasts eleven days before the volunteer project starts — that's not failure to relax. That's the human condition, showing up on time.
