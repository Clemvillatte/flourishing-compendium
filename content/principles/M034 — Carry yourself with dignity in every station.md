> **Principle 34** — Carry yourself with dignity in every station

The huehuetlatolli's counsel on rank and bearing: whatever your station (noble or commoner), carry it with dignity, fulfill its duties, and do not envy the other stations. The tradition's stratified society is named here honestly, and the principle extracted from it is the transportable one: dignity is available in every station and is distinct from status; envy of other stations corrupts the one you hold. Practically, in your current station, practice its duties excellently and its dignities fully, and notice the envy that argues otherwise.

(Florentine Codex, Book VI, the station counsels; marked as the book's transport of the tradition's stratified context.)

**Plain words** Whatever your station, carry it with dignity, fulfil its duties, and don't envy the other stations. Dignity is available in every station, and it's distinct from status — the tradition's stratified society is named honestly, and the transportable part is the bearing.

*For example:* The airport cleaner who does the floor of the business lounge like a craft, nook by nook — and the frequent flyers who set their watch by her standard. Different stations; only one of them was enviable that morning.
