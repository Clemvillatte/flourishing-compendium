> **Principle 60** — Admit there is no spectator self

You are always a participant, never a pure observer: your looking changes the scene, your interests shape what shows up. Drop the fantasy of the detached judge; own your involvement.

(James, The Principles of Psychology, 1890; Dewey, The Quest for Certainty, 1929.)

**Plain words** You are always a participant, never a pure observer — your looking changes the scene, your interests shape what shows up. Drop the fantasy of the detached judge; own your involvement. The anthropologist changes the village by being in it.

*For example:* The manager 'quietly observing' the team meeting — the team performs for the manager. There is no observation deck; there's only the field.

## Psychology parallels

- **Observer effects and expectancy**, observation and expectation alter the observed (Rosenthal, 1966)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[Radical empiricism|Radical empiricism]]** (James, Essays in Radical Empiricism)
- **[[Enactivism|Enactivism]]** (Varela, Thompson and Rosch, The Embodied Mind)
