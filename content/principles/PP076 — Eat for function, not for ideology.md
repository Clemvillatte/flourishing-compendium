> **Principle 76** — Eat for function, not for ideology

Diet affects mood, energy, and cognitive function. The evidence for specific diets (Mediterranean, whole-food plant-based) is stronger than for individual nutrients or supplements. The principle is not about a particular diet. It is about the recognition that what you eat affects how you feel, and that dietary patterns that support physical health generally support mental health.

(Sarris et al., 2015; Lopresti et al., 2013.)

**Plain words** What you eat affects how you feel and think. The evidence favours overall patterns, Mediterranean-style and whole-food diets, over any single nutrient or supplement. It's not about ideology but about noticing the connection.

*For example:* A week of late takeaway and skipped meals shows up as low mood and fog, not just weight.
