> **Principle 32** — Keep the body and the house clean

Book VI of the Florentine Codex gives washing and sweeping as plain instruction, joined to worship. One speech tells a young person: "wash thy face, wash thy hands, wash thy mouth. Seize the broom: be diligent with the sweeping; be not tepid, be not lukewarm." The next lines turn to washing "the mouths [of the gods]" and the offering of incense. A table instruction says the youth should wash before eating, wash others' hands, "sweep the place where there has been eating", and after the meal wash again and "cleanse thy teeth" (Sahagún, Florentine Codex Book VI, trans. Anderson & Dibble, 1969, [scanned text](https://archive.org/stream/volume-11-book-11-earthly-things-florentine-codex-general-history-of-the-things-of-new-spain/Volume%206,%20Book%206%20-%20Rhetoric%20and%20Moral%20Philosophy%20-%20%5BFlorentine%20Codex%20General%20History%20of%20the%20Things%20of%20New%20Spain%5D_djvu.txt)). Here cleanliness is courtesy, worship and discipline together.

The modern evidence on the psychological side is more limited than the old page claimed. In a small study of 30 dual-income couples, wives who described their homes with words such as clutter or unfinished showed "flatter diurnal slopes of cortisol" and "increased depressed mood over the course of the day"; the results for husbands were "largely null" (Saxbe & Repetti 2010, Pers Soc Psychol Bull 36(1):71-81, [abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0146167209352864)). This is a correlation based on how people talk about their homes, so it does not show that tidying improves mood. I did not find a study of tidying interventions in the sources I opened.

**Plain words**
The Nahua elders told the young to wash, sweep and keep order as part of daily life, and linked it to courtesy and worship. Modern research finds that people who describe their homes as cluttered or unfinished tend to report a worse mood, at least among women in one small study. It does not show that cleaning causes the better mood. Treat daily washing and sweeping as a small routine, and judge for yourself whether it helps.

*For example:* In a hard month, someone who cannot fix the job or the news washes the dishes and sweeps the kitchen. This is an imagined case, and the effect on how they feel is theirs to observe.
