> **Principle 85** — Hear the teaching first

The classical method begins with shravana, listening: receiving the teaching from a source that has walked it, before the mind's commentary begins. The modern translation is learning from models: wisdom is caught through exposure before it is tested. Practically, when entering a new domain, listen twice as long as you speak.

(Shankara, Upadesasahasri, the teacher-student opening; BG 4.34.)

**Plain words** The classical method begins with shravana, listening: receiving the teaching from a source that has walked it, before the mind's commentary begins. The modern translation is learning from models — wisdom caught through exposure before it's tested.

*For example:* The newcomer to any craft who spends the first months mostly watching the masters work — absorbing before arguing — versus the one who arrives with opinions. The listening phase is not passivity; it's the method's first step.
