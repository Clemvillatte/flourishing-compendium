> **Principle 33** — Let truth change its face

Truth is neither wholly invented nor wholly discovered: it is a matter of fit within a conceptual scheme, and schemes evolve. Expect your truths to be revised, refined, occasionally overturned.

(Putnam, Reason, Truth and History, 1981.)

**Plain words** Truth is neither wholly invented nor wholly discovered — it's a matter of fit within a conceptual scheme, and schemes evolve. Expect your truths to be revised, refined, occasionally overturned. Buy truth like you buy a phone: excellent today, upgradeable tomorrow.

*For example:* Newton wasn't wrong so much as fit for his era's measurements — then Einstein, then whatever's next. The fit improved; the inquiry continues.
