> **Principle 160** — Be patient with your own maturation

The greatest vessel is completed last. Deep development looks slow from inside; give it the years it needs.

(DDJ 41.)

**Plain words** Comparing your chapter three to someone's chapter thirty is the amateur error that quits good books. Deep development looks slow from inside — give it the years it needs.

*For example:* The 'late bloomer' who built quietly for a decade — the overnight success that took ten years is the rule, not the exception.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Identity development|Identity development]]** (Erikson, 1968), a decades-long process
- **[[Deliberate practice|Deliberate practice]]**, expertise requires years, not months (Ericsson et al., 1993)
