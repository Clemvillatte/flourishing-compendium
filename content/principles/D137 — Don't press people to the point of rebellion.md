> **Principle 137** — Don't press people to the point of rebellion

When people are hemmed in, they lose respect and then break through the fence. Leave doors open, always.

(DDJ 72.)

**Plain words** The cornered animal is the dangerous one — and you built the corner. Leave doors open, always. Openness is a safety feature.

*For example:* The teen with zero privacy gets sneakier, not better — the kid with reasonable freedom tells their parents more, not less.
