> **Principle 219** — Recognise that fame after death is meaningless

Marcus tells himself that the fame of the dead is as worthless as the fame of the living. The person who seeks posthumous fame seeks the approval of people who do not exist yet and who will themselves die. Seek virtue, not reputation.

(Med. I.19, II.6, III.4, IV.3, IV.19, V.33, VI.16, VII.6, IX.30, X.8, XI.1, XI.27, XII.21, XII.27.)

**Plain words** Marcus reminds himself that posthumous fame is as worthless as living fame. Seeking it means seeking the approval of people who don't exist yet and who will die too. Aim at virtue, not reputation.

*For example:* Declining to polish a legacy project and spending the time with your grandchildren.
