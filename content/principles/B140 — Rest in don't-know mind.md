> **Principle 140** — Rest in don't-know mind

Before each verdict, return to "don't know": a clean, open not-knowing that is not confusion but spaciousness.

(Korean Seon: "don't know"; Xinxin Ming.)

**Plain words** Before each verdict, return to 'don't know' — a clean, open not-knowing that is not confusion but spaciousness. Don't-know mind is the beginner's mind's older sibling: quieter, less cute, more available.

*For example:* The mediator who enters every meeting with 'I don't know this family yet' — versus the one with the theory. Only one of them hears anything.
