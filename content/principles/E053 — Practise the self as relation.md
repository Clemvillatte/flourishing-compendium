> **Principle 445** — Practise the self as relation

Recognise that the self is not a substance but a relation — a relation that relates itself to itself. You are not a thing that exists; you are the activity of relating to yourself. This self-relation is dynamic, ongoing, and never complete. Do not seek a fixed self; practise the ongoing relation.

(Kierkegaard, 1849/1983, The Sickness unto Death, Part I.)

**Plain words** The self is not a substance but a relation — a relation that relates itself to itself. You are not a thing that exists; you are the activity of relating to yourself, ongoing, never complete. Don't seek a fixed self; practise the relating.

*For example:* The person hunting their "true self" like a hidden room — versus the one who notices the self is more like a marriage: a daily relation, renegotiated, never finished being chosen. One searches; the other relates.
