> **Principle 50** — Trust trained intuition, but test it

Expertise makes intuition fast and mostly reliable: and systematically wrong at the edges. Use intuition as hypothesis, not verdict.

(Aristotle's phronesis; James on habit; modern naturalistic decision research.)

**Plain words** Expertise makes intuition fast and mostly reliable — and systematically wrong at the edges. Use intuition as hypothesis, not verdict. The veteran's snap judgment is gold in the middle of the field and a trap at the boundary.

*For example:* The trader whose instincts carried a decade of bull markets — the edges changed, the instincts didn't, and the account noticed first. Test the snap.
