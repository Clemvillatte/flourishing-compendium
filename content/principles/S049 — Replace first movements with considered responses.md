> **Principle 171** — Replace first movements with considered responses

The Stoics recognised "first movements": automatic emotional reactions that arise before reason can intervene. You cannot prevent the first movement, but you can prevent the second. Do not let the first reaction become the final response.

(Seneca, De Ira II.1-4; Med. V.26, VI.6; Disc. I.18, III.2.)

**Plain words** The Stoics noticed first movements: reactions that fire before reason can step in. You can't stop the first, but you can stop the second. Don't let the first reaction be the final response.

*For example:* You flinch at an angry email, which is fine. You don't reply for an hour, which is the practice.
