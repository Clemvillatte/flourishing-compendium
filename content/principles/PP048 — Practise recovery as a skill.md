> **Principle 48** — Practise recovery as a skill

Recovery from stress is not the absence of stress. It is the active process of returning to baseline: sleep, exercise, social connection, time in nature, and deliberate disengagement from work. Recovery that is passive (collapsing in front of a screen) is less effective than recovery that is active (walking, socialising, pursuing a hobby). Research on recovery (Sonnentag) shows that recovery experiences predict well-being and engagement at work.

(Sonnentag, 2018; Demerouti et al., 2009.)

**Plain words** Recovering from stress is an active skill, not collapsing. Sleep, movement, time outside, real conversation and deliberate disengagement from work all help. Passive recovery, scrolling on the sofa, restores much less.

*For example:* After a hard week, a long walk with a friend leaves you genuinely restored; four hours of television leaves you flat.
