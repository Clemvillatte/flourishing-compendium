> **Principle 33** — Do not mock the vulnerable

The huehuetlatolli's specific and repeated counsel: do not mock the old, the sick, the deformed, the poor, the deaf, or the simple; the mocker is warned that they invite the same. The counsel names the cruelty that ordinary social life tolerates and forbids it categorically. The principle is the tradition's floor of dignity. Practically, audit your humor: what does your laughter usually cost, and whom?

(Florentine Codex, Book VI, the counsel on mockery.)

**Plain words** Do not mock the old, the sick, the deformed, the poor, the deaf, the simple — the mocker is warned they invite the same. This names the cruelty ordinary social life tolerates and forbids it categorically. Audit your humor: what does your laughter feed on?

*For example:* The group chat where the harmless impression of the new hire turns out, on inspection, to be exactly this — and one person says "not this one," which is the whole tradition in three words.
