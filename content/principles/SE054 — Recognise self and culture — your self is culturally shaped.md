> **Principle 54** — Recognise self and culture: your self is culturally shaped

The self is shaped by culture — what counts as a good self, what is valued, what identities are available. This is not a limitation; it is the human condition. Understanding it prevents both arrogance (assuming your self is universal) and confusion (not knowing which self is yours).

**Plain words** Your very idea of what a good self is — how independent, how loud, how ambitious — was handed to you by a culture. That's not a flaw; that's the factory setting. But it means "just being myself" is always partly "being my culture's version of a self." Worth knowing which parts you actually chose.

*For example:* The son of immigrants caught between "stand on your own two feet" and "family comes before everything" isn't confused. He's bilingual in selves. The work isn't picking a winner — it's learning to speak both without lying in either.

## Practice

- Recognise that your self is partly cultural. Do not assume your way of being a self is universal. If you are bicultural, work on integration rather than choosing.
