> **Principle 158** — Don't tax yourself dry

People starve because those above eat too much; you burn out because you extract from yourself without limit. Lower your self-imposed taxes.

(DDJ 75.)

**Plain words** You burn out because you extract from yourself without limit. Lower your self-imposed taxes — the inner ruler demanding constant output is a government nobody elected.

*For example:* The self-imposed rule that every hour must be 'productive' — the tax collector lives in your own head.
