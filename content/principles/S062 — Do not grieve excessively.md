> **Principle 184** — Do not grieve excessively

Grief is natural, but excessive grief is a judgement that loss is unbearable. The Stoic feels the loss, acknowledges it, and then returns to what can be done. Seneca's consolation letters model this: face the loss, see it in proportion, and continue.

(Seneca, Letters 63, 99; Med. IV.1, IV.41; Disc. I.11, III.24.)

**Plain words** Grief is natural, but excessive grief is a judgement that the loss is unbearable. The Stoic feels it, acknowledges it, and returns to what can be done. Seneca's consolations show the way: face it, see its proportion, carry on.

*For example:* After a parent's death, you mourn fully and also call the funeral home the next morning.
