> **Principle 298** — Practise aroha

Love with compassion and generosity. Aroha is not merely romantic love; it is a deep, compassionate love that motivates action on behalf of others. It is the affective dimension of tikanga, the emotional ground of right relationship.

(Māori tikanga; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori; Durie, 1994, Whaiora.)

**Plain words** Aroha is love with legs: compassion and generosity that motivate action on behalf of others — the emotional ground of right relationship. It's not the romantic kind only, and it's never just a feeling; it's a felt commitment that reaches outward.

*For example:* When the flood hits the marae, the aunties are already cooking — nobody organised them, nobody asked; aroha is what the kitchen runs on.
