> **Principle 35** — Retire obsolete concepts

Ideas have working lives: when a concept no longer earns its keep: when it blocks inquiry or distorts experience — replace it with a better instrument. Keep a clean conceptual toolkit.

(Dewey, The Quest for Certainty, 1929.)

**Plain words** Ideas have working lives — when a concept no longer earns its keep, when it blocks inquiry or distorts experience, replace it with a better instrument. Keep a clean conceptual toolkit. Retire the tool that only fits problems you no longer have.

*For example:* 'Boss/employee' did real work for a century; for your three-person collaborative studio it just gets in the way. Shelf it.
