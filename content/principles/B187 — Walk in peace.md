> **Principle 187** — Walk in peace

Wherever you walk, walk as a walking meditation: even between meetings, even in a parking lot. Every walk can be a peace walk.

(Thích Nhất Hạnh, Peace Is Every Step.)

**Plain words** Wherever you walk, walk as a walking meditation — even between meetings, even in a parking lot. Every walk can be a peace walk. The car park is an underrated cathedral.

*For example:* The eighty meters from the car to the office door — done with attention, it's a reset button; done on the phone, it's nothing.

## Psychology parallels

- **Walking**, increases creativity (Oppezzo and Schwartz, 2014)

## Philosophy parallels

- **Walking as thinking** (Thoreau, "Walking")
- **[[Walking off one's thoughts|Walking off one's thoughts]]** (Kierkegaard, letters)
