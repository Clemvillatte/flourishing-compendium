> **Principle 62** — Savour positive experiences: deliberately attend to and amplify them

Savouring is the capacity to attend to, appreciate, and enhance positive experiences in the present, past, or future. The scale Fred Bryant built for the construct spells out the three temporal orientations:

> "First, before an upcoming good event occurs, people may look forward to it in ways that generate positive feelings in the present (i.e. savouring through anticipation). Next, while a good event is happening, people may intensify or prolong their positive feelings through specific thoughts and behaviors (i.e. savouring the moment). And finally, after a good event is over, people may look back on it in ways that prolong or rekindle their positive feelings (i.e. savouring through reminiscence)." (Bryant, 2003 — [Savoring Beliefs Inventory](https://www.carolinemiller.com/info/Savoring_Belief_Inventory_Bryant.pdf) — Introduction)

The same paper locates the construct on the control side of feeling: savouring is "a form of perceived control over positive emotions", largely independent of beliefs about coping with negative ones. The evidence base for training it has since been pooled: a meta-analysis of 20 randomized controlled trials with 4,805 participants found a moderate pooled effect of savoring interventions on emotional outcomes (g = 0.51) (Chen et al., 2026).

**Plain words**
The good thing happens; the practice is where the attention goes while it happens. The three moves the definition names are all things you can do on purpose: look forward to something coming, be in something while it happens, look back at something past. The compendium's suggestion: pick one of the three today.

**For example**
Imagine someone who gets a genuinely good piece of feedback and is already drafting the next task in their head. The compendium's suggestion: reread the feedback once, let the sentences land, and tell one person about it at lunch.

## Practice

- When something good happens, slow down. Name it. Attend to the bodily sensation of it. Share it with someone. Do not rush past it.
