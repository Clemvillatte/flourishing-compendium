> **Principle 15** — Nothing exists alone (interbeing)

A sheet of paper contains the cloud, the tree, the logger; you contain your food, your ancestors, your language. See the web in every object and act accordingly.

(Thích Nhất Hạnh, Interbeing; SN 12.)

**Plain words** A sheet of paper contains the cloud, the tree, the logger; you contain your food, your ancestors, your language. See the web in every object and act accordingly. Nothing exists alone — the delusion of separateness is the original accounting error.

*For example:* Your morning coffee contains the rain, the farmer, the trucker, and the barista — spill some of it and the whole web gets notified.
