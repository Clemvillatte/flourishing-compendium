> **Principle 292** — Practise kotahitanga

Seek unity and collective purpose. Kotahitanga is the practice of coming together as one, not by erasing difference but by finding common ground. In relationships, in community, and in work, seek unity of purpose without demanding uniformity of thought.

(Māori tikanga; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori.)

**Plain words** Kotahitanga: coming together as one — by finding common ground, never by erasing difference. Unity of purpose without uniformity of thought. The many voices are the material of the unity, not the obstacle to it.

*For example:* The village committee where the elders, the youth, and the skeptics all want different things — and find the one project all three will build, each for their own reasons.
