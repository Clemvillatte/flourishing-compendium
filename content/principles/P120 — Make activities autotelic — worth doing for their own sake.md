> **Principle 120** — Make activities autotelic: worth doing for their own sake

The deepest engagements are ends in themselves: the doing is the reward. Convert forced activities into chosen ones, and protect the ones that are already autotelic.

(Csikszentmihalyi, Flow, 1990.)

**Plain words** The deepest engagements are ends in themselves — the doing is the reward. Convert forced activities into chosen ones, and protect the ones that are already autotelic. The things you'd do unpaid are the map of your actual life; the rest is overhead.

*For example:* The weekend spent fixing a friend's bike — nobody paid, nobody asked, and Monday's exhaustion feels clean. That's autotelic. The same hours in a pointless meeting feel like theft.
