> **Principle 155** — Do not retreat from the world

The Stoic is not a recluse. Marcus was an emperor; Seneca was a politician and investor; Epictetus was a teacher. The philosophy is lived in the marketplace, the senate, and the household. Do not use philosophy as an excuse to withdraw from your obligations.

(Med. IV.3, V.1, VI.7, IX.6; Seneca, Letters 5, 20; Disc. III.7, III.21; Musonius Rufus, Lectures.)

**Plain words** The Stoic isn't a recluse. Marcus was an emperor, Seneca a politician and investor, Epictetus a teacher. The philosophy gets lived in the marketplace, the senate and the household. Don't use it as an excuse to withdraw.

*For example:* Taking on a difficult committee role rather than retreating to quiet reading, because that's where the practice is tested.
