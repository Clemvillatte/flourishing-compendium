> **Principle 178** — Practice without gaining ideas

Sit without waiting for anything: no enlightenment to earn, no calm to achieve, no report card. The gaining idea is itself the noise.

(Suzuki, Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind.)

**Plain words** Sit without waiting for anything — no enlightenment to earn, no calm to achieve, no report card. The gaining idea is itself the noise. You can't grab water; you can only make the bucket still and let it settle clear.

*For example:* Meditating to 'get somewhere' — months of frustration. Meditating to sit — weeks of quiet. The goal was the static.

- **[[Process versus outcome focus|Outcome-focus vs process-focus]]**, excessive outcome focus degrades performance and enjoyment (Vallerand, 2010)
