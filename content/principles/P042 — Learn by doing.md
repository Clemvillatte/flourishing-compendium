> **Principle 42** — Learn by doing

Knowledge is not poured in; it is grown through transaction: trying, suffering consequences, adjusting. Choose doing-with-reflection over knowing-without-trying.

(Dewey, Democracy and Education, 1916.)

**Plain words** Knowledge is not poured in — it's grown through transaction: trying, suffering consequences, adjusting. Choose doing-with-reflection over knowing-without-trying. The scar teaches what the manual gestures at.

*For example:* Reading about negotiation versus negotiating the used car badly once — the second lesson has a permanent seat in your memory.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Experiential learning|Experiential learning cycle]]**, concrete experience, reflection, conceptualization, experimentation (Kolb, 1984)
- **[[The generation effect|The generation effect]]**, self-produced knowledge is retained better (Slamecka and Graf, 1978)
