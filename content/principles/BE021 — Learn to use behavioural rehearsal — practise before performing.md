> **Principle 21** — Learn to use behavioural rehearsal: practise before performing

Behavioural rehearsal involves practising a behaviour (social, assertive, performance) in a safe context before doing it in real life. This builds skill and reduces anxiety.

**Plain words** Rehearse before the performance — the hard conversation, the interview, the assertive no. Practise in the shower, with a friend, in the mirror. Performance under pressure is built in rehearsal, when there's nothing on the line.

*For example:* The salary negotiation rehearsed three times over coffee with a friend — the actual meeting feels oddly familiar. Your nervous system already did this. It's a rerun.

## Practice

- Rehearse behaviours: before a difficult conversation, presentation, or social situation, practise it (alone, with a friend, in front of a mirror). Build the skill in safety before performance.
