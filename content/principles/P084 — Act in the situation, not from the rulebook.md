> **Principle 84** — Act in the situation, not from the rulebook

Moral life happens in concrete situations; general rules are instruments that the situation may require bending. Meet the situation first, consult rules second.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922; Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics.)

**Plain words** Moral life happens in concrete situations — general rules are instruments that the situation may require bending. Meet the situation first, consult rules second. The rulebook is a consultant, not the boss; the situation signs the checks.

*For example:* 'Never lie' meets the hidden Jews in the attic — the rule was a good servant and would have been a catastrophic master. Meet the situation; it's the one actually there.
