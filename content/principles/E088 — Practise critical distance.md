> **Principle 480** — Practise critical distance

Maintain a critical distance from your own tradition, your own culture, and your own self-understanding. Gadamer emphasises that understanding requires openness, but Ricoeur adds that it also requires distance — the ability to see your own context from the outside, to question what you have inherited. Practise both belonging and distance.

(Ricoeur, 1973, "The Task of Hermeneutics," in Hermeneutics and the Human Sciences; Gadamer, 1960/2013, Truth and Method.)

**Plain words** Maintain critical distance from your own tradition, culture, and self-understanding. Understanding requires openness, but also the ability to see your own context from outside, to question what you've never questioned. Visit your own beliefs like a traveller.

*For example:* The patriot who's never left the country versus the one who's lived abroad and returned — the second one's patriotism is worth more, not less, because it's now held with distance. The unexamined version is just inheritance wearing a flag.
