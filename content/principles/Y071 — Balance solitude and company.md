> **Principle 71** — Balance solitude and company

The tradition asks for both: solitude as practice environment, and companionship, teaching, and the fourfold stance as practice content. Neither is safe alone; unmixed solitude drifts into avoidance, unmixed company into diffusion. Practically, treat the ratio as a dial to adjust monthly, not a fixed rule.

(BG 13.10-11 with 12.13-14; YS 1.33.)

**Plain words** The tradition asks for both: solitude as practice environment, and companionship, teaching, and the fourfold stance as practice content. Neither is safe alone — unmixed solitude drifts into avoidance, unmixed company into diffusion. Treat the ratio as a dial, not a doctrine.

*For example:* The hermit year that was actually avoidance wearing robes — and the social year that was diffusion wearing a calendar. The correction was the same both times: turn the dial one notch, not to the end.
