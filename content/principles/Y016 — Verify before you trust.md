> **Principle 16** — Verify before you trust

Right knowledge comes, says the sutra, through three channels: direct perception, inference, and trustworthy testimony. Each has its own failure mode, and the practice is to know which channel a given belief arrived by and to check it accordingly. Practically, before acting on an important belief, ask: did I see this, infer it, or absorb it?

(YS 1.7.)

**Plain words** Right knowledge arrives through three channels: direct perception, inference, and trustworthy testimony — each with its own failure mode. Know which channel a belief came in by, and check it accordingly: verify perception, test inference, check the source.

*For example:* Someone is certain the new neighbour is unfriendly — checks the channel: inference from one curt hello. Test it: the next hello, with actual data. The inference fails the test, and the whole story dissolves.
