> **Principle 44** — Learn to recognise chronic stress: signs and symptoms

Chronic stress has signs: persistent fatigue, sleep problems, irritability, cognitive difficulties, physical symptoms (headaches, digestive, tension), and reduced immune function. Recognising them is the first step to addressing them.

**Plain words** Chronic stress has a signature: persistent fatigue, wrecked sleep, irritability, brain fog, headaches, gut trouble, every cold that comes to town. Learn your own signature — the body files complaints in your name. Read them.

*For example:* The "I'm just busy" quarter that features three colds, a jaw ache, and crying at an advert — that's the signature, filled out in triplicate. The body submitted the paperwork.
## Psychology parallels

- **Chronic stress symptoms**, fatigue, sleep disturbance, irritability, cognitive impairment, physical symptoms (McEwen, 2007; Sapolsky, 2004)
- **Allostatic load markers**, chronic stress is measurable in physiological markers (McEwen, 2007; McEwen & Wingfield, 2003)

## Practice

- Recognise chronic stress: persistent fatigue, sleep problems, irritability, tension, cognitive fog, frequent illness. These are signs of chronic stress, not personal weakness. Address them.
