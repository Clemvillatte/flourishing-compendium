> **Principle 112** — Practice gratitude: recognize your conditions

Your abilities, food, safety, and understanding arrive through countless others' labor. Acknowledge it — explicitly, often.

(Katannuta Suttas, AN 3.31-32; Thích Nhất Hạnh.)

**Plain words** Your abilities, food, safety, and understanding arrive through countless others' labor. Acknowledge it — explicitly, often. Gratitude isn't a nicety; it's the accurate reading of your actual supply chain.

*For example:* The morning coffee: the farmer, the roaster, the driver, the person who fixed the road. Naming three of them changes the taste of the sip.
