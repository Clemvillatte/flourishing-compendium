> **Principle 345** — Practise the face of the Other

Levinas's term for the encounter is the face, and he defines it against the idea:

> "The way in which the other presents himself, exceeding the idea of the other in me, we here name face." (Levinas, Totality and Infinity, [Lingis translation, p. 50](https://wuecampus.uni-wuerzburg.de/moodle/pluginfile.php/2896578/mod_folder/content/0/Levinas,%20Totality%20%20Infinity,%20S.%2048-51,110-114,117-119,197-201.pdf))

What the face does, in his most-quoted pages, is resist, not physically but ethically:

> "This infinity, stronger than murder, already resists us in his face, is his face, is the primordial expression, is the first word: 'you shall not commit murder.'" (Levinas, Totality and Infinity, [Lingis translation, p. 199](https://wuecampus.uni-wuerzburg.de/moodle/pluginfile.php/2896578/mod_folder/content/0/Levinas,%20Totality%20%20Infinity,%20S.%2048-51,110-114,117-119,197-201.pdf))

His argument for why the face is not a physical feature runs through the analysis of murder: "The Other is the sole being I can wish to kill" (p. 198), because only what exceeds my powers infinitely could be a candidate for total negation, and the infinity in the face already paralyzes that power. The discourse the face opens is obligation:

> "The face opens the primordial discourse whose first word is obligation, which no 'interiority' permits avoiding." (Levinas, Totality and Infinity, [Lingis translation, p. 201](https://wuecampus.uni-wuerzburg.de/moodle/pluginfile.php/2896578/mod_folder/content/0/Levinas,%20Totality%20%20Infinity,%20S.%2048-51,110-114,117-119,197-201.pdf))

(Levinas, 1961/1969, Totality and Infinity.)

**Plain words** Before any reasoning about justice, the other person's face arrives as a command: this one matters, do not harm. That's Levinas — the moral summons is prior to the argument. You can reason after; the face gets first refusal.

*For example:* Imagine someone who has walked past a hundred statistics about a crisis, unmoved; what finally makes them stop is one face on one screen.
