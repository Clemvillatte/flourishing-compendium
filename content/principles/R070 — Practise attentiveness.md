> **Principle 332** — Practise attentiveness

Be attentive to the needs of those who depend on your care. Attentiveness is the first element of care as identified by Joan Tronto. It is the disposition to notice what others need, even when they do not or cannot articulate it.

(Tronto, 1993, Moral Boundaries, pp. 127-130.)

**Plain words** Attentiveness: notice what others need, even when they can't or won't say it. The first element of care, and the one that fails silently — you can't respond to what never got noticed. Needs mostly arrive in code.

*For example:* The teacher who notices the kid whose uniform stopped fitting and quietly connects the family with the uniform bank — nobody asked, which is the point.
