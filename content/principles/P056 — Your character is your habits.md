> **Principle 56** — Your character is your habits

The empirical self is the sum of what you own and do: your body, clothes, house, achievements, relationships. You are what you repeatedly do: so choose your repetitions.

(James, The Principles of Psychology, 1890.)

**Plain words** The empirical self is the sum of what you own and do — your body, clothes, house, achievements, relationships. You are what you repeatedly do: so choose your repetitions. Character isn't declared; it's compiled.

*For example:* The person who 'values fitness' and owns a dusty bike versus the one who rides it Tuesdays — the second one values fitness. Check the schedule, not the statement.
