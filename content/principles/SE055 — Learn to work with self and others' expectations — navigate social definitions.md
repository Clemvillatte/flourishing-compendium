> **Principle 55** — Learn to work with self and others' expectations: navigate social definitions

Others define you — they have expectations, labels, and categories for who you are. Navigating these (accepting some, resisting others) is a self-relational skill. Being defined by others is unavoidable; being determined by them is not.

**Plain words** People will hand you definitions of yourself — labels, expectations, roles — all your life. Some fit. Some are cages. The skill is processing incoming definitions instead of swallowing them whole: keep the ones that fit, refuse the ones that shrink you.

*For example:* The whole family calls her "the flaky one" because she was late twice at nineteen. At forty, she's the most organised person at her firm, and still flinches at the nickname. At some point you have to hand the label back: "that was one decade, not a diagnosis."

## Practice

- Notice which others' definitions you have internalised. Ask: "is this my view of myself, or someone else's view that I have absorbed?" Retain what fits; release what does not.
