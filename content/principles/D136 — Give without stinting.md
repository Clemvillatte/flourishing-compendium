> **Principle 136** — Give without stinting

The Way of heaven gives without stinting to all; give your knowledge, help, and resources the same way: giving is how you finish rich.

(DDJ 81.)

**Plain words** The Way of heaven gives without stinting to all. Give your knowledge, help, and resources the same way — giving is how you finish rich. Hoarded knowledge rots; shared knowledge compounds, and most of the interest comes back to you.

*For example:* The engineer who documents everything for the next person ends up with the network, the reputation, and the referrals.

- **[[Prosocial spending|Prosocial spending]]**, spending on others raises happiness more than spending on self (Dunn, Aknin and Norton, 2008)
