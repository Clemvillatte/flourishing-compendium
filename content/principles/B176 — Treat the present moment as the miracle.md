> **Principle 176** — Treat the present moment as the miracle

The miracle is not elsewhere: to be alive, breathing, seeing: this is the miracle. The urgent question is not "how to live" but "are you here?"

(Thích Nhất Hạnh, The Miracle of Mindfulness.)

**Plain words** The miracle is not elsewhere — to be alive, breathing, seeing: this is the miracle. The urgent question is not 'how to live' but 'are you here?' Everyone's waiting for the once-in-a-lifetime event and missing the once-in-a-lifetime breath.

*For example:* The sunset watched from the waiting room window — no ticket, no travel, no planning. Just the ordinary miracle, on schedule.
