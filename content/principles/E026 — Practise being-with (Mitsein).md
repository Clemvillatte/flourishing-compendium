> **Principle 418** — Practise being-with (Mitsein)

Recognise that your being is always being-with others. You do not exist as a solitary subject and then encounter others; your existence is fundamentally co-existence. The world you inhabit is a shared world. Do not treat others as obstacles or instruments; recognise them as co-dwellers in the shared world.

(Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §§25-27, 47; Merleau-Ponty, 1945/2012, Phenomenology of Perception, Part II, ch. 4.)

**Plain words** Your being is always being-with others — you don't exist as a solitary subject who then encounters people; your existence is fundamentally co-existence. The world you inhabit is shared. Don't treat others as obstacles or instruments; they're co-inhabitants.

*For example:* The driver who experiences every other car as an obstacle versus the one who experiences traffic as "all of us, going somewhere" — same road, and only one of them arrives without having fought a war.
