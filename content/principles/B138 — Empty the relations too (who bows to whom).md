> **Principle 138** — Empty the relations too (who bows to whom?)

The one who bows and the one who is bowed to are both empty: no fixed bower, no fixed bowed. Hold relationships lightly, too, not just things.

(Diamond Sutra; Zen tradition.)

**Plain words** The one who bows and the one who is bowed to are both empty — no fixed bower, no fixed bowed. Hold relationships lightly too, not just things. Love the verb, not the frozen nouns you've made of each other.

*For example:* The couple who stop being 'the anxious one' and 'the avoidant one' — the labels dissolved, the love got roomier.
