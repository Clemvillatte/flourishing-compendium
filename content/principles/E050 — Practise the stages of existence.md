> **Principle 442** — Practise the stages of existence

Recognise that existence unfolds in stages: the aesthetic (the pursuit of pleasure and novelty), the ethical (the commitment to duty and relationship), and the religious (the leap beyond reason). These are not merely historical categories but modes of living. Examine your own life: in which stage do you primarily dwell? What would it mean to move to the next?

(Kierkegaard, 1843/1987, Either/Or; Kierkegaard, 1843/1986, Fear and Trembling; Kierkegaard, 1846/1992, Concluding Unscientific Postscript.)

**Plain words** Existence unfolds in stages: the aesthetic (pleasure and novelty), the ethical (duty and relationship), the religious (the leap beyond reason). Not historical categories — modes of living. Examine your current stage honestly; they're climbed, not skipped.

*For example:* Someone lives the aesthetic stage brilliantly into their forties — the experiences, the novelty, the rotation — and notices the returns thinning. The ethical stage isn't a defeat; it's the next rung, and it holds weight the first one never could.
