> **Principle 472** — Practise the fusion of horizons

Recognise that understanding another person, text, or tradition requires the fusion of horizons — the meeting of your perspective with theirs. You do not abandon your horizon; you let it be challenged and expanded by the other. Practise openness to the other's horizon.

(Gadamer, 1960/2013, Truth and Method, Part II, ch. 2; Gadamer, 1960/2013, Truth and Method, Part III.)

**Plain words** Understanding another requires the fusion of horizons — your perspective meeting theirs. You don't abandon your horizon; you let it be challenged and expanded by the other. Practise openness to the other's horizon — the meeting is the understanding.

*For example:* The engineer and the artist arguing about the project — until each can state the other's position well enough to be corrected by them. Nothing was surrendered; both horizons got bigger. That's the fusion.
