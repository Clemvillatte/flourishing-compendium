> **Principle 15** — Treat opposites as co-creating

High depends on low; before depends on after; difficult and easy complete each other. Stop seeking one side while cursing the other: both are needed.

(DDJ 2.)

**Plain words** High exists because low exists; difficult creates easy; before depends on after. Opposites co-create each other. So stop seeking one side while cursing the other — you can't have the summit without the valley, and pretending otherwise just makes the valley worse.

*For example:* Nobody appreciates a good week without having survived the brutal one; the hard sprint is what makes the rest feel earned.
