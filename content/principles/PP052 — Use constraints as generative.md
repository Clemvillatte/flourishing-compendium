> **Principle 52** — Use constraints as generative

Constraints do not inhibit creativity; they structure it. The blank page produces paralysis; the constraint produces focus. The constraint can be formal (a sonnet, a time limit, a budget), material (three ingredients, one colour), or procedural (no revisions during the first draft). Research on creativity and constraints shows that moderate constraints enhance creative output more than complete freedom.

(Stokes, 2006; Acar et al., 2019.)

**Plain words** Constraints don't kill creativity; they focus it. A blank page paralyses, a tight brief directs. Formal limits, tight budgets or strict rules often produce better work than total freedom.

*For example:* Writing a whole story in exactly fifty words forces decisions that a thousand words would let you dodge.
