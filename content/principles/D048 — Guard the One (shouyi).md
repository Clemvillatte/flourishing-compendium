> **Principle 48** — Guard the One (shouyi)

Gather your scattered attention into a single whole: one body, one breath, one task at a time. A unified mind is the platform of all skill and calm.

(Neiye; DDJ 10, 22.)

**Plain words** One body, one breath, one task at a time — gather the scattered attention into a single whole. Fragmentation is why everything feels harder than it should.

*For example:* Closing the seventeen browser tabs and doing the one thing is not just tidier — the work takes half the time.
