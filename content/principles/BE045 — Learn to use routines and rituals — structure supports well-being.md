> **Principle 45** — Learn to use routines and rituals: structure supports well-being

Routines and rituals — regular, structured patterns of behaviour — provide predictability, reduce decision fatigue, and support well-being. They are behavioural anchors.

**Plain words** Routines and rituals are scaffolding for a mind — predictability lowers the baseline load. The morning sequence, the Friday call, the Sunday reset: not monotony, but handrails. Structure is what freedom walks on.

*For example:* The chaotic week that starts badly and gets worse — versus the same week with a Wednesday-night swim locked in. One anchor changes the whole drift of the sea around it.
## Psychology parallels

- **Routines and well-being**, having regular routines is associated with better mental health (Lyons et al., 2016; Zisberg et al., 2007)
- **Rituals reduce anxiety and improve self-control**, rituals reduce anxiety and improve performance (Norton & Gino, 2014; Hobson et al., 2018)

## Practice

- Build routines and rituals: morning routine, evening routine, weekly rituals, seasonal practices. Structure supports well-being. Do not leave life to chance.
