> **Principle 24** — Learn the dose: how much, how hard, how often

The dose of exercise for mental health benefits is moderate: roughly 30 minutes of moderate-intensity activity, 3-5 times per week. More is not necessarily better; too little is ineffective. The "sweet spot" is moderate and consistent.

**Plain words** The dose for mental health: ~30 minutes moderate, 3–5 times a week. More isn't proportionally better, and too little doesn't fire. You don't need a marathon; you need a regular walk. Consistency is the active ingredient.

*For example:* The person waiting to "get in shape first" before exercising — the walk IS the getting in shape. Start at the dose, not the dream.
## Psychology parallels

- **Exercise dose and mental health**, moderate dose (30 min, 3-5x/week) is effective; more is not necessarily better (Schuch et al., 2018; Chekroud et al., 2018)
- **The "U-shaped" relationship**, both too little and too much exercise are associated with worse mental health (Chekroud et al., 2018; Kim et al., 2019)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[The doctrine of the mean|The golden mean]]** (Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics II)
- **[[Nothing in excess|Nothing in excess]]** (Delphic maxim)
- **Wu wei: moderate effort** (Daoism)

## Practice

- Target moderate dose: 30 minutes of moderate-intensity activity (brisk walking, cycling, swimming), 3-5 times per week. Do not over-exercise (which can be harmful) or under-exercise (ineffective).
