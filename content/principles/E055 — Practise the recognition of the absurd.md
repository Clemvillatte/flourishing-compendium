> **Principle 447** — Practise the recognition of the absurd

Confront the absurd — the confrontation between the human demand for meaning and the universe's silent indifference. The absurd is not a problem to be solved but a condition to be lived. Do not flee from it through religion, philosophy, or distraction. Face it.

(Camus, 1942/1955, The Myth of Sisyphus; Camus, 1951/1956, The Rebel.)

**Plain words** The absurd: the confrontation between the human demand for meaning and the universe's silent indifference. Not a problem to be solved — a condition to be lived. Don't flee it through religion, philosophy, or distraction. Face it; the facing is the practice.

*For example:* Someone does everything right — the kindness, the diligence, the health kick — and the universe replies with randomness anyway. The absurd isn't the punishment; it's the realisation that the deal was never structured the way the fairness instinct assumed.
