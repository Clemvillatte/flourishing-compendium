> **Principle 110** — Use music and the arts for moral education

Aristotle argues that music and the arts are not merely entertainment but tools of moral education. They shape character by cultivating the right pleasures and aversions. Engage with art that elevates: music, poetry, drama that teaches you to feel the right things.

(Politics VIII.5-7; NE II.2, X.9; Poetics 4-6.)

**Plain words** Music and the arts aren't just entertainment. They shape what you enjoy and dislike. Aristotle recommends art that lifts: music, poetry and drama that teach you to feel the right things.

*For example:* A play that makes you feel real sympathy for someone you'd normally dismiss trains something in you.
