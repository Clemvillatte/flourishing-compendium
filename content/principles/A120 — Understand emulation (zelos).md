> **Principle 120** — Understand emulation (zelos)

Emulation is a distress caused by the presence of good things that are highly valued and not possessed by oneself, combined with a desire to obtain them. Unlike envy, emulation does not wish to deprive the other but to rise to their level. Convert envy into emulation.

(Rhetoric II.11; NE II.7.)

**Plain words** Emulation is distress at lacking something valued that others have, with a drive to get it. Unlike envy, it doesn't want to take it from them. It wants to rise. Turn envy into emulation.

*For example:* A junior designer who sees a colleague's polished portfolio and starts studying how it's built is emulating.
