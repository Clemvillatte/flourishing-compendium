> **Principle 25** — Clarify purpose as a direction

Purpose is not a goal to be achieved but a direction to be pursued. It answers the question: what am I doing with my life? The clarification of purpose does not require a single answer. It requires that the direction be chosen, that it be large enough to organise daily action, and that it be honest. Research on purpose in life shows that it predicts well-being, physical health, and longevity.

(Steger, 2012; Hill and Turiano, 2014.)

**Plain words** Purpose isn't a finish line but a direction. It answers "what am I doing with my life?" It needn't be one answer. It needs to be chosen, large enough to organise daily action, and honest. Purpose is linked with health and longevity.

*For example:* Someone whose direction is "help people understand their bodies" can be a nurse, a coach or a writer at different times.
