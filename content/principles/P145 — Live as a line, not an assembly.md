> **Principle 145** — Live as a line, not an assembly

Life is not built from parts assembled; it is a line of growth along which things happen: a path of correspondence with the world. Follow your lines — the crafts, relations, places you have grown along.

(Ingold, Correspondences, 2020; Lines, 2007.)

**Plain words** Life is not built from parts assembled — it's a line of growth along which things happen: a path of correspondence with the world. Follow your lines: the crafts, relations, places you've grown along. You're not an assemblage; you're a trail.

*For example:* The thirty years from hobbyist coder to accidental systems architect — no plan, one line followed through a hundred jobs and forks. The résumé shows parts; the life is the line.
