> **Principle 72** — Use cultural formulation in clinical practice

The Cultural Formulation Interview (CFI) in DSM-5 is "a set of 16 questions that clinicians may use to obtain information during a mental health assessment about the impact of culture on key aspects of an individual's clinical presentation and care" ([Lewis-Fernández, DSM-5 Handbook appendix](https://www.vumc.org/psychiatry/sites/default/files/GRED%20pdfs/GRED%20Feb%2012%20LewisFernandezR_2016_AppendixACulturalForm_DSM5HandbookOnTheCult.pdf)). Its four domains are the cultural definition of the problem (questions 1 to 3), perceptions of cause, context and support (4 to 10), factors affecting self-coping and past help seeking (11 to 13) and factors affecting current help seeking (14 to 16) ([Lewis-Fernández, World Cultural Psychiatry](https://www.worldculturalpsychiatry.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/2-Lewis-Final-V11S1.pdf)). The first question asks "What brings you here today?", and the form tells clinicians to use the person's own term for the problem in later questions ([APA CFI form](https://www.psychiatry.org/getmedia/5cc5329d-3bd4-4c6a-bae1-dfd0d6496f44/APA-DSM5TR-CulturalFormulationInterview.pdf)).

In the international field trial, "mixed-methods data from 318 patients and 75 clinicians found the CFI feasible, acceptable and useful", though clinician feasibility ratings were significantly lower than patient ratings ([British Journal of Psychiatry](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/the-british-journal-of-psychiatry/article/feasibility-acceptability-and-clinical-utility-of-the-cultural-formulation-interview-mixedmethods-results-from-the-dsm5-international-field-trial/FD28CDEA89A308AA90C4BF7BAF335D4E)). Transcripts indicated that clinicians in busy outpatient services could cover all four sections within a standard one-hour assessment ([Focus](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.focus.18103)).

### Scope

The seed says ten questions; the sources say 16. A field-trial version had 14 and the final DSM-5 version has 16. Those sources report feasibility, acceptability and perceived utility. I did not find evidence here that the CFI improves clinical outcomes. I did not open Sue 2009 or Hook 2013.

**Plain words**
The interview gives clinicians a short list of open questions about how a person understands their problem, what they think caused it, and what help they want.

**For example**
Ask "What brings you here today?" and then use the person's own words for the problem in the next questions, as the form instructs.

## Practice

- In clinical assessment, use the CFI to elicit the client's cultural model. In treatment planning, adapt interventions to the cultural context (see principle 64). In supervision, attend to cultural dynamics.
