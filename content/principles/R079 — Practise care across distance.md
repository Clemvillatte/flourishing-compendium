> **Principle 341** — Practise care across distance

Extend your care beyond your immediate circle. The ethics of care does not deny the special obligations of close relationship, but it does insist that care must extend to strangers, to distant others, and to the natural world.

(Held, 2006, The Ethics of Care; Tronto, 2013, Caring Democracy.)

**Plain words** Extend care past your immediate circle: strangers, distant others, the natural world. Care ethics doesn't deny special bonds — it just refuses to let them be the border of the moral map. Far-away people are people too.

*For example:* Someone whose care has always stopped at the family gate — and takes the shift cooking at the shelter once a month, discovering the distance was mostly a habit of attention.
