> **Principle 151** — Fulfil your roles well

You are a son or daughter, a parent, a citizen, a friend, a worker. Each role carries duties. The Stoic does not reject roles but fulfils them excellently. Ask: what does this role require of me, right now? Then do it.

(Disc. II.10, III.7, III.23; Med. VI.30; Seneca, Letters 44, 71, 95.)

**Plain words** You're a child, parent, citizen, friend, worker. Each role has duties. The Stoic doesn't reject roles but does them well. Ask what this role requires of you right now, then do it.

*For example:* Dragging yourself to your daughter's recital after a long day, because being her father right now means being there.
