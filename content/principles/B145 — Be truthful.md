> **Principle 145** — Be truthful

Commit to honesty: with others and with yourself. Deception fragments the mind that practices; truthfulness is the ground of clarity.

(Dhammapada 10; MN 61.)

**Plain words** Commit to honesty — with others and with yourself. Deception fragments the mind that practices; truthfulness is the ground of clarity. Every lie requires a maintenance crew, and the crew is you, working nights.

*For example:* The secret that costs more energy to keep than it would to confess — the confession is a one-time fee; the keeping is a subscription.
