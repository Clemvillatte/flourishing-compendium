> **Principle 81** — Hold powers and peak experiences lightly

The tradition is blunt about attainments: the extraordinary capacities (siddhis) that arise with deep practice are obstacles to integration, though the world counts them as powers. The principle generalizes to every peak experience: states that flatter the self re-inflate the self the practice was thinning. Practically, when something impressive happens in practice, tell one grounded person and move on.

(YS 3.37; YS 4.1.)

**Plain words** The tradition is blunt about attainments: the extraordinary capacities that arise with deep practice are obstacles to integration, though the world counts them as powers. The principle generalizes to every peak experience: states that flatter the self re-inflate it.

*For example:* The retreat participant who comes back with the aura-readings and the cosmic dreams — collecting them like trophies — and the quiet one who noticed the same things and let them pass. The tradition says which one was practicing.
