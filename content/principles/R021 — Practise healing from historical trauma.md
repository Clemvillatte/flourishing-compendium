> **Principle 283** — Practise healing from historical trauma

Acknowledge the impact of colonisation, dispossession, and forced removal on your community and yourself. Healing from historical trauma is not a one-time event but an ongoing process. Seek culturally appropriate healing practices and support.

(Aboriginal SEWB framework; Atkinson, 2002, Trauma Trails, Recreating Song Lines: The Transgenerational Effects of Trauma in Indigenous Australia.)

**Plain words** Name what colonisation, dispossession, and removal actually did — to the community and to you — because trauma that's unnamed gets inherited. Healing isn't an event with a date; it's an ongoing practice, and it deserves culturally grounded support.

*For example:* The family that starts going to the healing camp together — three generations — and the teenagers' grandparents' silences finally start making sense to everyone at once.
