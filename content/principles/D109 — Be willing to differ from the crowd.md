> **Principle 109** — Be willing to differ from the crowd

"Everyone else is busy and smug; I alone am dull, like an infant before it smiles." Comfort with not-belonging is a superpower.

(DDJ 20.)

**Plain words** 'Everyone else is busy and smug; I alone am dull, like an infant before it smiles.' Comfort with not-belonging is a superpower — the crowd's direction is a rumor, not a compass. Being okay alone in a room is the skill that keeps your judgment yours.

*For example:* The one person at the party not performing happiness — just watching, at ease — is usually the most interesting conversation in the room.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Conformity|Conformity]]**, majority pressure sways judgments even against perception (Asch, 1951)
