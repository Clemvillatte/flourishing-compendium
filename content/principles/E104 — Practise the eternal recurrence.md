> **Principle 496** — Practise the eternal recurrence

Live as if every moment of your life will recur eternally. The thought of eternal recurrence is Nietzsche's test of affirmation: if a demon told you that you would live this life again and again, in every detail, would you affirm it? Practise living in a way that you would will the eternal recurrence of every moment.

(Nietzsche, 1882/1974, The Gay Science, §341; Nietzsche, 1883/1961, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "The Convalescent.")

**Plain words** The eternal recurrence, as a test: live so that if a demon told you this exact life would repeat eternally, in every detail, you'd affirm it. Not a doctrine — a mirror held up to each choice. Would you sign for the eternal repeat of this evening?

*For example:* Someone runs the demon-test on the Tuesday: the commute, the tone at dinner, the hour of scrolling — and the parts they wouldn't sign for become visible for the first time. The recurrence is fictional; the audit is real.
