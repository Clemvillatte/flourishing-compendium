> **Principle 59** — Learn yoga nidra: deep rest practice

Yoga nidra ("yogic sleep") is a guided deep relaxation practice that produces a state between waking and sleep. It is deeply restorative and is used for stress, trauma, and sleep.

**Plain words** Yoga nidra — guided deep rest between waking and sleep — restores like nothing else that involves lying still. Used for stress, trauma, sleep. The nap's sophisticated cousin. Twenty minutes, no skill required.

*For example:* The 20-minute nidra audio that does what the three-hour doomscroll claimed to do — rest. You rise different. The practice is older than the excuses.
## Psychology parallels

- **Yoga nidra and relaxation**, yoga nidra produces deep relaxation and reduces stress (Moszeik et al., 2020; Datta et al., 2017)
- **iRest (integrative restoration)**, a yoga nidra-based intervention for PTSD and stress (Moszeik et al., 2020; Pence et al., 2018)

## Philosophy parallels

- **The state between waking and sleep** (Yoga tradition)
- **Deep rest as practice** (Yoga nidra)
- **[[Wu wei|Wu wei: deep rest]]** (Daoism)

## Practice

- Practise yoga nidra: 20-45 minutes of guided deep relaxation (lying down, following a guided audio). This is deeply restorative. Use for stress, sleep, and trauma.
