> **Principle 356** — Practise substitution

Recognise that in the deepest ethical relation, you are responsible for the Other as if you were responsible for their very being. Substitution is Levinas's most radical concept: the idea that the self is constituted by its responsibility for the Other, that I am "hostage" to the Other before I am free. Do not flee from this responsibility; let it ground your selfhood.

(Levinas, 1969, Otherwise Than Being; Levinas, 1961/1969, Totality and Infinity.)

**Plain words** Levinas's most radical move: the self is constituted by responsibility for the other — you're "hostage" before you're free. You don't opt into it; it's the origin of you. Don't flee it — let it ground you.

*For example:* The eldest child who resents being "the responsible one" for years — and one day stops fighting it and realises the responsibility was never a role assigned; it's who they actually are.
