> **Principle 333** — Practise responsibility

Take responsibility for responding to the needs you perceive. Responsibility is the second element of care. It is not merely seeing a need but taking ownership of the response.

(Tronto, 1993, Moral Boundaries, pp. 127-130.)

**Plain words** Responsibility: seeing a need isn't enough — take ownership of the response. The second element of care, and the difference between "somebody should do something" and "I'll be there at three."

*For example:* In every apartment block, someone notices the elderly neighbour's curtains stay drawn. Responsibility is the one who knocks instead of mentioning it at the next body-corp meeting.
