> **Principle 21** — Treat ideas as tools, not mirrors

Concepts are instruments for coping with the world, like knives and forks: keep them sharp, use them, replace them when better ones exist. Stop asking whether your worldview is the true copy and start asking whether it serves.

(Dewey, The Quest for Certainty, 1929.)

**Plain words** A worldview is a toolkit, not a painting to be authenticated. Keep the concepts sharp, use them, and swap them out when better ones exist — the question is whether they serve, not whether they are the true copy.

*For example:* The five-year plan: a great fork in a stable decade, a terrible one in a volatile one. Not a moral issue. Just cutlery.
