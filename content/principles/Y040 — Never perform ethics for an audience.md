> **Principle 40** — Never perform ethics for an audience

The Gita is unsparing about the person who restrains action while mentally rehearsing the objects of sense, and calls that state hypocrisy. The instruction cuts both ways: ethics performed for reputation destabilizes the mind it was meant to steady. Practically, keep at least one observance entirely private, known to no one, as the control condition of your practice.

(BG 3.6; BG 17.19 context, austerity done for display.)

**Plain words** The Gita is unsparing about restraining action while mentally rehearsing the sense-objects — it calls that hypocrisy. Ethics performed for reputation destabilizes the very mind it was meant to steady. Keep at least one good act secret from everyone, forever.

*For example:* The generous donor who needs the plaque, and the one whose charity appears in no record — the tradition says only the second one is practicing anything; the first is just shopping for reputation.
