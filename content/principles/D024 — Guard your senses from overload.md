> **Principle 24** — Guard your senses from overload

Too many colors blind, too many flavors deaden, too much excitement maddens, rare goods make people covet and steal. Curate what you let in.

(DDJ 12; Yinfu Jing.)

**Plain words** Too many colors blind, too many flavors deaden, too much stimulation maddens. Your senses have a budget, and the modern world spends it for you. Curate what you let in — that's not prudishness, it's input hygiene.

*For example:* After an hour of scrolling, everything looks gray — then you step outside and one actual tree is more vivid than the whole feed was.
