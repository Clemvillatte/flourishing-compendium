> **Principle 354** — Practise listening as a moral act

Listen to the other person not as a means to your own ends but as an end in itself. Listening is the primary act of I-Thou relation. When you truly listen, you create the space for the other to become who they are.

(Buber, 1965, The Knowledge of Man; Rogers, 1961, On Becoming a Person.)

**Plain words** Listening is a moral act, not a data-collection method. When you truly listen — not to reply, not to fix — you create the space the other person needs to become themselves. It's the primary act of taking someone seriously.

*For example:* The friend who said "I'm not going to say anything for a while, just tell me all of it" — and forty minutes later the speaker finds, in their own words, the answer they'd been asking everyone else for.
