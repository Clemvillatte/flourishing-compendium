> **Principle 96** — Let the many become one: and be increased by one

Each moment gathers the many influences of the past into a new unity, which then becomes one more fact for the next moment. Every day, integrate what life has given you — and add yourself to it.

(Whitehead, Process and Reality, 1929.)

**Plain words** You're a meeting that convenes daily: everything you've been, plus one new voice — yours. Each moment gathers the many influences of the past into a new unity, which becomes one more fact for the next moment.

*For example:* The morning that absorbs yesterday's argument, the book you finished, the bad sleep — and produces a slightly wiser Tuesday. That's the many becoming one. It happens whether you attend or not; attending makes it yours.
