> **Principle 141** — Let emptiness itself be empty

Don't reify emptiness into a new doctrine to hold: emptiness is also empty, a tool for seeing, not a metaphysical trophy.

(Nagarjuna, MMK 13.7-8.)

**Plain words** Don't reify emptiness into a new doctrine to hold — emptiness is also empty, a tool for seeing, not a metaphysical trophy. The moment you defend emptiness in an argument, it has become a fullness of the wrong kind.

*For example:* The person who lectures about non-attachment for an hour — attached to the lecturing. The tool has become the trophy.
