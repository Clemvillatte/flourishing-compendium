> **Principle 391** — Practise ecological relationality as spiritual practice

Let your relationship with the natural world be a dimension of your spiritual life. Spend time in nature not as recreation but as contemplation. Let the more-than-human world speak to you, teach you, heal you. The ecological relationship is not merely practical; it is spiritual.

(Macy and Brown, 2014, Coming Back to Life; Berry, 1999, The Great Work; Snyder, 1990, The Practice of the Wild.)

**Plain words** Let nature be part of your spiritual life, not just your recreation: time in the wild as contemplation, letting the more-than-human world speak, teach, heal. The ecological relationship is practical, yes — and it's also devotion.

*For example:* Someone who stopped going to the gym and started walking the same hill daily in all weathers — and calls it, only half-joking, "the only church that never cancels."
