> **Principle 4** — Expect the quiet to arrive unsought, like a shadow following a body

Sextus tells the story as an accident: the painter Apelles, unable to render the foam on a horse's mouth, "flung at the picture the sponge on which he used to wipe the paints off his brush, and the mark of the sponge produced the effect of a horse's foam" ([Outlines of Pyrrhonism I.12, Bury](https://users.manchester.edu/Facstaff/SSNaragon/Online/texts/316/Sextus%20Empiricus,%20Pyrrhonism.pdf)). The Sceptics had the same experience: hoping to gain quietude by deciding the questions of sense and thought, "being unable to effect this they suspended judgment; and they found that quietude, as if by chance, followed upon their suspense, even as a shadow follows its substance" ([Outlines of Pyrrhonism I.12, Bury](https://users.manchester.edu/Facstaff/SSNaragon/Online/texts/316/Sextus%20Empiricus,%20Pyrrhonism.pdf)). He adds the limit: the Sceptic "is troubled by things unavoidable," thirst and age among them, but escapes the added belief that these conditions are evil, and with it a large part of the discomfort ([Outlines of Pyrrhonism I.12, Bury](https://users.manchester.edu/Facstaff/SSNaragon/Online/texts/316/Sextus%20Empiricus,%20Pyrrhonism.pdf)).

> "they found that quietude, as if by chance, followed upon their suspense, even as a shadow follows its substance." (Sextus Empiricus, Outlines of Pyrrhonism I.12, [Bury translation](https://users.manchester.edu/Facstaff/SSNaragon/Online/texts/316/Sextus%20Empiricus,%20Pyrrhonism.pdf))

**Plain words**
The Sceptics hoped that settling the questions would bring quietude; Sextus names that hope the originating cause of the whole inquiry ([Outlines of Pyrrhonism I.6, Bury](https://users.manchester.edu/Facstaff/SSNaragon/Online/texts/316/Sextus%20Empiricus,%20Pyrrhonism.pdf)). He reports that, unable to settle them, they suspended judgment, and quietude followed "as if by chance" ([Outlines of Pyrrhonism I.12, Bury](https://users.manchester.edu/Facstaff/SSNaragon/Online/texts/316/Sextus%20Empiricus,%20Pyrrhonism.pdf)).

**For example**
Reading example: compare the Sceptics' hope of attaining quietude with the Apelles story. Distinguish their intended route from the unexpected result of suspending judgment.

(Sextus Empiricus, Outlines of Pyrrhonism, Book I, chapter 12, the Apelles passage and the shadow image.)
