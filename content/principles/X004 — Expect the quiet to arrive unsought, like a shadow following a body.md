> **Principle 4** — Expect the quiet to arrive unsought, like a shadow following a body

The famous image: ataraxia (undisturbedness) follows upon suspension the way a shadow follows the body, without any further act of will. The quiet is not pursued; it is the residue of the balanced state. The instruction's practical genius is its anti-striving: striving for calm is itself the agitation. Practically, when practicing any balancing method, do not check whether the calm has arrived; the checking is the door slamming on it.

(Sextus Empiricus, Outlines, Book I, the shadow image.)

**Plain words** The quiet you want arrives the way a shadow follows a body — automatically, not by chasing. In fact, checking whether you've calmed down yet is exactly the noise that keeps the calm out. Balance the arguments and let the stillness be the residue.

*For example:* Someone practising balance techniques who keeps asking "am I calm yet?" — and only gets calm the afternoon they forget to ask.
