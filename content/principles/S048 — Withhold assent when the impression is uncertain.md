> **Principle 170** — Withhold assent when the impression is uncertain

If you are not sure whether something is true, do not act as if it were. The discipline of withholding assent is the capacity to live with uncertainty without rushing to judgement. This is not scepticism for its own sake; it is the protection of your peace of mind.

(Disc. I.28, II.18, III.2-3; Ench. 45.)

**Plain words** If you aren't sure something is true, don't act as if it were. Withholding assent is the ability to live with uncertainty without rushing to judgement. It isn't scepticism for its own sake, it protects your peace.

*For example:* You don't reply to a worrying message until you've heard the full story.
