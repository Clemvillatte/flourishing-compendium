> **Principle 491** — Practise the question of being

Heidegger opens Being and Time by saying we have lost the question and do not feel the loss: "Do we in our time have an answer to the question of what we really mean by the word 'being'? Not at all. [...] But are we nowadays even perplexed at our inability to understand the expression 'Being'? Not at all. So first of all we must reawaken an understanding for the meaning of this question" (Heidegger, 1927/1962, Being and Time, Introduction, §1, trans. Macquarrie and Robinson, [full text](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5e265eb50aee2d7e8a81ae69/t/5ff90abe346f087732825582/1610156759396/Heidegger-Martin-Being-and-Time+-+Complete+Text.pdf)).

The principle's question, "why is there something rather than nothing", closes his 1929 lecture: "Why are there beings at all, and why not rather nothing?" (Heidegger, "What Is Metaphysics?", trans. in [Basic Writings](https://archive.org/stream/heidegger-what-is-metaphysics-1928/Heidegger%20-%20WHAT%20IS%20METAPHYSICS%20%5B1928%5D_djvu.txt), ed. Krell, p. 110 in this printing). The lecture ties it to a mood: "In the clear night of the nothing of anxiety the original openness of beings as such arises: that they are beings—and not nothing" (p. 103).

Everyday life works against the question. In Being and Time, curiosity "does not seek the leisure of tarrying observantly, but rather seeks restlessness and the excitement of continual novelty and changing encounters". It "has nothing to do with observing entities and marvelling at them. To be amazed to the point of not understanding is something in which it has no interest" (§36, M&R pp. 216-217).

**Plain words**
Most days you deal with particular things, and the plain fact that anything exists at all slips out of sight. Heidegger asks you to keep the question alive, and he offers no solution. Make time for stillness and wonder.

**For example**
Once a week, switch off every screen and sit with one ordinary object, a cup or a tree. Ask what it means that it is here at all.
