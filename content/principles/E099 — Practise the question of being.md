> **Principle 491** — Practise the question of being

Do not lose sight of the question of being. The question "why is there something rather than nothing?" is not a puzzle to be solved but a mystery to be inhabited. Let the question of being orient your existence. Do not let the busyness of life crowd out this fundamental question.

(Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, Introduction I; Heidegger, 1953, Introduction to Metaphysics; Jaspers, 1932/1971, Philosophy, vol. 1.)

**Plain words** Don't lose the question of being: "why is there something rather than nothing?" — not a puzzle to be solved but a mystery to be inhabited. Let it orient your existence; don't let the busyness crowd out the fundamental wonder. Keep the question alive on purpose.

*For example:* Someone schedules it — the walk where the question is allowed to actually arise, away from the feeds and the deadlines — and finds the ten minutes of unanswerable wonder does more for their week than the other ten thousand minutes combined.
