> **Principle 93** — Know your field and its knower

The Gita's distinction: the body-and-mind is the field (kshetra), and the awareness of it is the field-knower (kshetrajna); the chapter then says the knower in all fields is the same in kind. The bounded formulation: in every experience there is the content and the knowing of it, and training includes telling them apart under pressure. Practically, in conflict, ask "what is the field here (facts, feelings) and what knows it?" before speaking.

(BG 13.1-3.)

**Plain words** The Gita's distinction: the body-and-mind is the field; the awareness of it is the field-knower. In every experience there's the content and the knowing of it — learn to tell them apart, because everything practical follows from the sorting.

*For example:* The athlete who learns to read the field (the body's fear) without mistaking it for the knower — "the field is reporting danger; the knowing of it is steady" — performs differently in the final minutes.
