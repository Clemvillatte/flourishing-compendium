> **Principle 60** — Practice without seeking

Do not make awakening a gaining idea: a future prize to clutch. Seeking makes it farther. Sit without reaching for any result.

(Dogen, Genjokoan; Suzuki.)

**Plain words** Don't make awakening a gaining idea — a future prize to clutch. Seeking makes it farther; sit without reaching for any result. The grasping mind can turn even peace into a commodity to acquire, which is the problem wearing a solution's uniform.

*For example:* Meditating to 'achieve calm' and getting tense about progress — the measurement was the agitation.
