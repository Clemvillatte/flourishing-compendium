> **Principle 118** — Bear the opposites

The instruction comes early in Krishna's teaching, in chapter 2:

> O son of Kunti, the contact between the senses and the sense objects gives rise to fleeting perceptions of happiness and distress. These are non-permanent, and come and go like the winter and summer seasons. O descendent of Bharat, one must learn to tolerate them without being disturbed.

(BG 2.14, trans. Swami Mukundananda, [holy-bhagavad-gita.org](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/verse/14/).)

The verb is titikshasva, "tolerate". Mukundananda's commentary says that "a person of discrimination should practice to tolerate" both pleasant and unpleasant feelings, and that anyone who lets them in will "sway like a pendulum from side to side". The reason given is impermanence: the same cool water pleases in summer and distresses in winter, so the feeling belongs to the season and not to the water.

Shankara's Vivekachudamani defines the same capacity:

> The bearing of all afflictions without caring to redress them, being free (at the same time) from anxiety or lament on their score, is called Titiksha or forbearance.

(Vivekachudamani 24, trans. Swami Madhavananda, [Wisdom Library](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/vivekachudamani/d/doc144472.html).)

Read strictly, this is a definition of how to hold what comes, with no call to look for hardship. Practising on purpose with small discomforts, such as the last thirty seconds of a cold shower, is this page's modern training idea. It does not mean refusing to treat a real illness or injury.

**Plain words**

Discomfort comes and goes whether you like it or not. If every bout of cold, boredom or pain sends you into a panic, you will spend your life reacting. The verse says to let the feeling be there without being thrown by it, and says it is something you can get better at. Small, harmless discomforts give you practice at it.

**For example**

Someone gets used to waiting out the urge to check their phone and to walking in the rain. Months later, a bad week of illness is still hard, but they notice they can sit with the feeling and not add dread on top of it.
