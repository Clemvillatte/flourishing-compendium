> **Principle 6** — Guard the mind as the root of everything downstream

Shantideva opens the vigilance chapter with the root claim:

> "Those who wish to keep a rule of life / Must guard their minds in perfect self-possession. / Without this guard upon the mind, / No discipline can ever be maintained." (Shantideva, Bodhicharyavatara 5.1, [Padmakara Translation Group, chapter text](https://nalandaolywa.org/docs/Shantideva-Ch5.pdf))

The next verses set the stakes with an elephant: the untethered "elephant of mind" brings worse calamities than any worldly beast, while a mind "tethered all around" by "mindfulness' rope" ends the fears and hands over the virtues ([5.2-3, ibid.](https://nalandaolywa.org/docs/Shantideva-Ch5.pdf)). The principle's own gloss is that a guarded mind is a watched one rather than a repressed one, and what you let reach the mind belongs to that watch. The modern parallel is one early option among several. In Gross's process model, situation selection is the first of five points at which emotion can be regulated: "Situation selection refers to approaching or avoiding certain people, places, or things so as to regulate emotion" ([Gross 2002](https://www.psychiatry.wisc.edu/courses/Nitschke/seminar/gross_2002_psychophys.pdf)), and "the first four of these are antecedent focused, the fifth is response focused" ([ibid.](https://www.psychiatry.wisc.edu/courses/Nitschke/seminar/gross_2002_psychophys.pdf)). Choosing inputs acts early in the generative process; the compendium's account is [[Situation selection]].

**Plain words**
Everything you say and do flows out of the state your mind is in, the way a harvest comes from water. Choosing the water is the earliest move available, and often the cheapest; it is not the only one. Notice which situations leave you worse, and choose differently when you can. When you cannot, the other forms of regulation are still there. And guarding the mind means watching it, not gagging it; you can only choose inputs you notice.

*For example:* Noticing that every time you read certain news before bed you wake up irritable, and quietly stopping. Not suppressing anything, just choosing the water.
