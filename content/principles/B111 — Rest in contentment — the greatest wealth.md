> **Principle 111** — Rest in contentment: the greatest wealth

Health is the greatest gain, contentment the greatest wealth: know what you have, and let it be enough.

(Dhammapada 204; SN 1.10.)

**Plain words** Health is the greatest gain, contentment the greatest wealth — know what you have, and let it be enough. Not resignation; arithmetic. The richest person in the room is whichever one has stopped needing the next thing to enjoy this one.

*For example:* Two neighbors with identical lawns: one is planning the upgrade, one is barefoot in it. Only one is wealthy today.
