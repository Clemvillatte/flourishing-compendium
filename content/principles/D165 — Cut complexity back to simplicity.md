> **Principle 165** — Cut complexity back to simplicity

Complexity is the disease of civilization and of the psyche: trim systems, conversations, and rules to their plainest working form.

(DDJ 19, 57.)

**Plain words** Complexity is the disease of civilization and of the psyche. Trim systems, conversations, and rules to their plainest working form — complexity feels like sophistication but functions as friction. The plain version that works is the masterpiece.

*For example:* The onboarding process cut from nine steps to three — adoption doubles, and nobody misses the six steps.
