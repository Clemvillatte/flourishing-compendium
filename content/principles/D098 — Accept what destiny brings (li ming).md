> **Principle 98** — Accept what destiny brings (li ming)

Endeavor has its domain and fate has its own; the skilled still meet bad hands. Do your best, then accept the outcome as weather, not verdict.

(Liezi, "Endeavor and Destiny.")

**Plain words** The skilled still meet bad hands — do your best, then take the outcome as weather, not verdict. That's the difference between effort and attachment: you keep the first, you're owned by the second.

*For example:* The surgeon who does everything right and still loses the patient — skill was theirs; outcome wasn't. Grief, yes; guilt, no.
