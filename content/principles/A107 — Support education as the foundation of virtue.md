> **Principle 107** — Support education as the foundation of virtue

The polis exists for the sake of the good life, and the good life requires good citizens. Education is the process by which the polis creates good citizens. Support educational practices that cultivate virtue, not merely skills.

(NE X.9; Politics VIII.1, VIII.3.)

**Plain words** The city exists for the good life, and that needs good citizens. Education is how it makes them. Aristotle asks us to support schooling that builds virtue, not merely skills.

*For example:* A parent-teacher group that argues for time on cooperation and honesty, not just exam results, is acting on this.
