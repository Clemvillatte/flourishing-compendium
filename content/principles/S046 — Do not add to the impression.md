> **Principle 168** — Do not add to the impression

When something happens, the impression is: "this happened." The mind immediately adds: "and this is terrible." The addition is yours, not the event's. Practise catching the addition and withholding it.

(Disc. I.11, I.28; Ench. 5; Med. IV.7, VIII.47.)

**Plain words** Something happens, and the mind says "this happened," then adds "and it's terrible." The addition is yours. Practise catching it and not passing it on.

*For example:* Your flight is delayed. "The flight is delayed" is the fact. "This ruins everything" is the add-on.
