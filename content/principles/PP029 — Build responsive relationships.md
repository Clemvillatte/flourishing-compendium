> **Principle 29** — Build responsive relationships

The quality of responsiveness, the capacity to be present to another person's experience and to respond to it accurately, predicts relationship quality, attachment security, and well-being. Responsiveness is not agreement or advice. It is the experience of being understood, validated, and cared for.

(Reis and Clark, 2013; Reis and Shaver, 1988.)

**Plain words** Responsiveness is being present to someone's experience and answering it accurately. It predicts relationship quality, secure attachment and well-being. It isn't agreement or advice. It's feeling understood, validated and cared for.

*For example:* A friend says "that sounds exhausting," instead of telling you to take a holiday.
