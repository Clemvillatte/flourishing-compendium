> **Principle 388** — Practise ecological citizenship

Exercise your citizenship not only in relation to your political community but in relation to the ecological community. You are a citizen of the Earth, with rights and responsibilities toward the more-than-human world. Vote, advocate, and act for ecological flourishing.

(Dobson, 2003, Citizenship and the Environment; Eckersley, 2004, The Green State.)

**Plain words** You're a citizen not just of your country but of the ecological community — rights and responsibilities toward the more-than-human world. So vote, advocate, and act for ecological flourishing as part of your citizenship, not as a side hobby.

*For example:* The voter who adds the candidates' environmental records to their actual checklist — not as a single-issue vote, but as part of what citizenship now means.
