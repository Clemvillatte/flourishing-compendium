> **Principle 48** — Treat discrimination as a health determinant

Paradies and colleagues' meta-analysis of 293 studies reported in 333 articles found racism associated with poorer health across outcomes: "Racism was associated with poorer mental health... including depression, anxiety, psychological stress and various other outcomes", and "also associated with poorer general health... and poorer physical health" (2015). The authors' summary is measured: the relationship is "stronger for poor mental health and weaker for poor physical health", and the effect sizes "were stronger in cross-sectional compared with longitudinal data", so the associations are associations. The compendium's point sits upstream of the statistics: if reported discrimination reliably travels with worse health, then the social level is a place health work has to reach.

> "Racism was associated with poorer mental health (negative mental health: r = -.23, 95% CI [-.24,-.21], k = 227; positive mental health: r = -.13, 95% CI [-.16,-.10], k = 113), including depression, anxiety, psychological stress and various other outcomes. Racism was also associated with poorer general health (r = -.13 (95% CI [-.18,-.09], k = 30), and poorer physical health (r = -.09, 95% CI [-.12,-.06], k = 50)." (Paradies et al., 2015, abstract)

**Plain words** Discrimination is a health determinant — unfair treatment based on group membership shows up in cardiovascular, metabolic, and mental-health outcomes. This is physiology, not just feelings. When a group has worse health, look at the treatment before the lifestyle.

*For example:* The stress of always being the one watched in the shop, always the one asked to justify your presence — that vigilance has a medical bill attached. The health disparity starts upstream of the clinic.

## Practice

- In health care, screen for discrimination as part of the social history. In policy, treat discrimination reduction as health policy (see principle 75). In personal life, recognize the health impact of discrimination and seek support.
