> **Principle 48** — Treat discrimination as a health determinant

Treat discrimination — unfair treatment based on group membership — as a health determinant. The evidence is substantial: perceived discrimination predicts poorer mental and physical health, including cardiovascular and metabolic outcomes (Pascoe and Smart Richman, 2009; Paradies et al., 2015; Williams et al., 2019; Major et al., 2018). The mechanisms include stress physiology, health behaviors, and access to care.

**Plain words** Discrimination is a health determinant — unfair treatment based on group membership shows up in cardiovascular, metabolic, and mental-health outcomes. This is physiology, not just feelings. When a group has worse health, look at the treatment before the lifestyle.

*For example:* The stress of always being the one watched in the shop, always the one asked to justify your presence — that vigilance has a medical bill attached. The health disparity starts upstream of the clinic.
