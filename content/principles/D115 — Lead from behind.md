> **Principle 115** — Lead from behind

The best leader: people barely know she exists; the next best, they love and praise; the worst, they despise. Power over is failure mode; power through is the work.

(DDJ 17.)

**Plain words** The best leader is one people barely know exists. If the team's success depends on your constant presence, that's not leadership — it's a bottleneck.

*For example:* The manager who builds systems and quietly leaves early — the team runs fine — versus the hero-manager whose vacation is everyone's crisis.
