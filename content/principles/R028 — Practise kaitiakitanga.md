> **Principle 290** — Practise kaitiakitanga

Care for the environment as a guardian, not as a consumer. Kaitiakitanga is the practice of stewardship over the natural world, rooted in the understanding that you are in a relationship of reciprocity with the land, waters, and all living things.

(Māori tikanga; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori; Durie, 1994, Whaiora.)

**Plain words** Kaitiakitanga is guardianship: care for the environment as a guardian, not a consumer — because you're in a live relationship of reciprocity with the land, waters, and everything living. You're not the owner; you're the one on duty.

*For example:* The family that clears the invasive weed from the creek bank every season — no sign thanks them, no one pays them, and the creek runs clear because somebody holds the duty.
