> **Principle 85** — Let obstruction do its work

Growth is stimulated by resistance: a smooth life produces no strength. When you meet friction, don't ask only "how do I remove this": ask "what capacity is this developing?"

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922; Experience and Nature, 1925.)

**Plain words** Growth is stimulated by resistance — a smooth life produces no strength. When you meet friction, don't ask only 'how do I remove this' — ask 'what capacity is this developing?' The gym doesn't apologize for the weights.

*For example:* The difficult client nobody wants — the one who taught you contracts, boundaries, and patience. The friction was the tuition; the invoice was metaphorical.
