> **Principle 61** — Treat economic inequality as a health determinant

Treat economic inequality — the gap between rich and poor — as a health determinant. The Spirit Level literature (Wilkinson and Pickett, 2009) argues that more unequal societies have worse health and social outcomes; the evidence is debated but substantial (Pickett and Wilkinson, 2015; for critiques, see Snowdon, 2010).

**Plain words** Inequality is a health determinant in itself — more unequal societies have worse outcomes across the board, including for the people near the top. The gap does biology, not just economics. Care about the ratio, not just your own number.

*For example:* The wealthier-but-unequal society with worse health than its poorer-but-equal neighbour — the average income was higher; the trust and health were lower. The gap was the exposure.
