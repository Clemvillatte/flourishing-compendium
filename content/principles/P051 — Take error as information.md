> **Principle 51** — Take error as information

Error is not a verdict on your worth; it is data about the fit between habit and world. Treat mistakes as feedback in an ongoing experiment.

(Peirce, "The Fixation of Belief," 1877.)

**Plain words** Error is not a verdict on your worth — it's data about the fit between habit and world. Treat mistakes as feedback in an ongoing experiment. The failure isn't the falling; it's leaving the information on the floor.

*For example:* The failed product launch dissected in a blameless post-mortem — the failure becomes next quarter's road map. Same event, two very different companies.
