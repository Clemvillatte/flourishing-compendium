> **Principle 8** — Know that motivation is trainable: it can be built and shaped

Motivation is not a fixed trait. It can be built through practice, environment design, and skill development. The belief that you are "just not a motivated person" is both wrong and self-defeating.

**Plain words** Motivation is trainable — not a fixed allotment issued at birth. It grows with skill (competence), design (environment), and body (sleep, food, movement). "I'm just not a motivated person" is usually an untrained person describing their training gap.

*For example:* The "unmotivated" employee who becomes tireless after a role change — same person, different setup. The motivation was always there, waiting for conditions.
## Psychology parallels

- **Motivation as trainable capacity**, motivation can be built through practice and design (Locke & Latham, 2002; Ryan & Deci, 2017)
- **Growth mindset effects are modest but real**, believing abilities are developable supports persistence (Dweck, 2006; Yeager et al., 2019)

## Practice

- Do not accept "I am just not motivated" as a fixed trait. Motivation is built through: clear goals, self-efficacy, environment design, and connection to values. Start small and build.
