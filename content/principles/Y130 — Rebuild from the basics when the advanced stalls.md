> **Principle 130** — Rebuild from the basics when the advanced stalls

The tradition's own practice instruction, and the companion to principle 125's diagnostic grid: when integration fails, return to the restraints and observances; when the cathedral cracks, inspect the foundation. The return is not regression but the tradition's designed maintenance cycle. Practically, schedule an annual "foundations month": restraints, observances, posture, breath, moderation audit.

(YS 2.28-2.32 revisited; BG 6.16-6.17.)

**Plain words** When integration fails, return to the restraints and observances — when the cathedral cracks, inspect the foundation. The return is not regression; it's the tradition's own practice instruction. Rebuild from the basics when the advanced stalls.

*For example:* The advanced student whose year falls apart — sleep, diet, speech — and whose teacher's prescription is the beginner's course: yamas and niyamas, five daily. The tradition never treated the basics as the thing you graduate from.
