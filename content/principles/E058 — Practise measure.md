> **Principle 450** — Practise measure

Recognise the need for measure — the Camusian virtue of refusing both total affirmation and total negation. Measure is the wisdom to accept limits, to resist the temptation of totality, to leave room for what exceeds human comprehension. Do not seek to possess the whole; practise measure.

(Camus, 1951/1956, The Rebel, Part Three; Camus, 1939/1940, "L'Étranger" and the essays in Lyrical and Critical Essays.)

**Plain words** Measure: the Camusian virtue of refusing both total affirmation and total negation — accepting limits, resisting the temptation of totality, leaving room for what exceeds comprehension. Don't seek to possess the whole. Practise measure.

*For example:* The political era tempts everyone into totalisms — the system is everything, the collapse is certain, the cause is absolute — and the measured voice in the room, the one saying "some of it, within limits," is doing the hardest intellectual work there.
