> **Principle 34** — Learn to review and adjust goals: flexibility matters

Goals are not set-and-forget. They need regular review and adjustment. Circumstances change, priorities shift, and goals that were right may become wrong. Regular review is a motivational practice.

**Plain words** Goals need review like plants need water — circumstances change, and a goal set in April may be wrong by August. Quarterly: keep, adjust, or kill. The kill is a legitimate outcome. Zombie goals drain everything.

*For example:* The "learn piano" goal that survives three years of guilt — one honest review would have either revived it with a better plan or freed the energy for something real. Either answer was better than the zombie.
## Psychology parallels

- **Goal review and adjustment**, regular review improves goal attainment and well-being (Neubach et al., 2007; Wrosch et al., 2007)
- **Goal disengagement**, the capacity to disengage from unattainable goals is associated with better well-being (Wrosch et al., 2003; Wrosch & Miller, 2009)

## Practice

- Review goals regularly (weekly, monthly, quarterly). Ask: is this still the right goal? Is progress happening? Do I need to adjust, persist, or disengage? Flexibility is a motivational virtue.
