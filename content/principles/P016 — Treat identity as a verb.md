> **Principle 16** — Treat identity as a verb

You are not a fixed being with a fixed nature; you are a becoming: a process that takes itself as its own next project. Say "I am becoming" rather than "I am."

(Nietzsche, The Gay Science, 1882; Simondon, L'individuation, 1958.)

**Plain words** You're not a fixed being with a fixed nature — you're a becoming: a process that takes itself as its own next project. Say 'I am becoming' rather than 'I am.' The noun traps you; the verb hires you.

*For example:* 'I'm not a math person' versus 'I haven't learned this yet' — the first is a life sentence, the second a schedule.
