> **Principle 100** — Redefine skill as evenness

The Gita names yoga "skill in action" and defines it in context as the evenness just described (the parallel with choking research is a process comparison, not a validation): the skill of acting is the skill of remaining unperturbed while acting fully. This inverts the usual reading of skill as speed or brilliance. Practically, in your craft, measure sessions not by output alone but by output minus turbulence (how much inner weather accompanied the work).

(BG 2.50.)

**Plain words** The Gita names yoga "skill in action" and defines it as evenness: the skill of acting is the skill of remaining unperturbed while acting fully. This inverts the usual reading — the skill isn't the outcome, it's the levelness carried through the act.

*For example:* Watch two surgeons in the same crisis: same hands, same speed — one white-knuckled and contracted, one level and full. The tradition's definition of skill picks the second one every time, regardless of who wins the round.
