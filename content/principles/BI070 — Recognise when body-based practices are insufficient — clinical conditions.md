> **Principle 70** — Recognise when body-based practices are insufficient: clinical conditions

MedlinePlus lists medicines, psychotherapy, or both as the main treatments for bipolar disorder, while describing exercise and a consistent sleep schedule as lifestyle changes that can also help ([MedlinePlus, Bipolar disorder](https://medlineplus.gov/bipolardisorder.html)). Its PTSD guidance names talk therapy, medicines, or both and advises working with a mental health professional to find the best treatment for your symptoms ([MedlinePlus, PTSD](https://medlineplus.gov/posttraumaticstressdisorder.html)). The federal schizophrenia page describes a serious mental illness and says treatment can help people with schizophrenia engage in school or work ([NIMH, schizophrenia](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/schizophrenia)).

> "If you, or someone you love is depressed, see your provider or a mental health provider. It's important to get treatment at the earliest sign of a problem." (MedlinePlus, Depression, [medlineplus.gov](https://medlineplus.gov/depression.html))

**Plain words**
Feeling calmer after an exercise does not, by itself, establish that a clinical condition has been adequately treated. Tell the professional helping with your care what you have been trying and what symptoms remain.

*For example:* An imagined person with bipolar disorder keeps their usual exercise routine, records changes in sleep, and brings those notes to a treatment appointment.

## Psychology parallels

- **Clinical conditions require professional treatment**, severe depression, bipolar disorder, psychosis, and severe trauma require evidence-based treatments (APA, 2022)
- **Body-based practices as adjuncts**, breathing, exercise, and relaxation are complements, not substitutes, for treatment (Schuch et al., 2018; Hofmann et al., 2010)

- **Know thy limits** (Delphic maxim)

## Practice

- If you have a clinical condition, seek professional treatment. Use body-based practices as complements (supports), not substitutes. Do not self-treat severe conditions with lifestyle alone.
