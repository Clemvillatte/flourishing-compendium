> **Principle 140** — Accept loss without devastation

What you have was given; what is given can be taken. When you lose something, do not say "I have lost what is mine." Say: "it has returned to where it came from." Nothing is truly yours except your character.

(Ench. 11, 15; Med. II.11, IV.1, VI.14; Seneca, Letters 24, 91, 99.)

**Plain words** What you have was given, and what's given can be taken. When you lose something, don't say "I lost what was mine." Say "it went back to where it came from." Nothing is truly yours except your character.

*For example:* When a friend moves abroad, you can resent the loss, or you can be grateful she was in your life at all.
