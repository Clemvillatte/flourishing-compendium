> **Principle 198** — Freedom is now: nothing to wait for

Dhammapada 239 describes removing impurities little by little and moment by moment.

> One by one, little by little, moment by moment, a wise man should remove
> his own impurities, as a smith removes his dross from silver.
>
> (Dhammapada 239, Buddharakkhita)

The Platform Sutra offers a different emphasis: "You should enlighten yourself and not seek outside. If you hear the Sudden Teaching you may become enlightened right away."

The compendium draws an exercise from these passages while preserving their different emphases: begin an available practice without assuming its completion is immediate.

(Dhammapada 239; Platform Sutra.)

**Plain words**
Name one condition you have been waiting for before beginning a practice. Identify a small part of that practice you can attempt today. Beginning now does not require assuming you have already reached the goal.

*For example:* Imagine standing at the dishwasher and noticing you have been rushing. Pause and attend to the task for a few breaths.
