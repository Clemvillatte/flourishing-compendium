> **Principle 198** — Freedom is now: nothing to wait for

The final principle: don't postpone. The freedom the path points to is available in this breath, this attention, this choice. The waiting mind is the only thing missing.

(Dhammapada 239-240; Platform Sutra; Batchelor.)

**Plain words** The final principle: don't postpone. The freedom the path points to is available in this breath, this attention, this choice. The waiting mind is the only thing missing. Everything you're waiting for is waiting for you to stop waiting.

*For example:* The person who decides, mid-Tuesday, mid-dishwasher, that this is the life — and it is. It always was.
