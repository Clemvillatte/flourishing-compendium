> **Principle 234** — Handle fame without vanity

Fame is a preferred indifferent. The Stoic may be famous but must not seek or depend on fame. Marcus was emperor and still wrote that fame is worthless. Do not let the approval of others become your standard.

(Med. I.6, I.9, III.4, IV.3, IV.18, V.3, VI.16, VII.31, VIII.45, IX.30; Seneca, Letters 20, 29, 41, 44, 73, 75, 79, 91, 94, 103, 123.)

**Plain words** Fame is a preferred indifferent. A Stoic may be famous but mustn't seek or rely on it. Marcus was emperor and still wrote that fame is worthless. Don't let others' approval become your standard.

*For example:* A teacher with a big following still checks with herself, not the numbers, whether the lesson was good.
