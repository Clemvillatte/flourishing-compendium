> **Principle 79** — Build financial security, not financial excess

Financial security (meeting basic needs, having a buffer, freedom from financial stress) predicts well-being. Financial excess (accumulating wealth beyond the point of security) does not. The income-well-being relationship flattens at a level that varies by country and cost of living. Beyond that point, additional income produces diminishing returns and can become a source of stress in itself.

(Kahneman and Deaton, 2010; Killingsworth et al., 2023, with the caveat that the exact plateau is contested.)

**Plain words** Financial security, meeting needs with a buffer, supports well-being. Excess beyond that point adds little. The relationship between income and well-being flattens at a level that varies by place. Past security, the returns are mostly elsewhere.

*For example:* The second car and the bigger house change the week very little; the end of money stress changes it completely.
