> **Principle 119** — Keep to the center: excess words exhaust

More words soon exhaust the breath; better keep to the middle. Talking past the point costs energy and credibility.

(DDJ 5.)

**Plain words** Every sentence after the point is made is a withdrawal from the same account. Talking past the point costs energy and credibility.

*For example:* The apology that lands in three lines versus the one that circles the block for ten minutes and re-opens the wound.

## Psychology parallels

- **Worry as verbal-linguistic activity**, the avoidance theory of worry (Borkovec et al., 1983)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[Apophatic theology|Apophatic theology]]** (Pseudo-Dionysius, Mystical Theology), the highest things elude speech
