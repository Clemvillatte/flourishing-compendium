> **Principle 156** — Cultivate psychological flexibility: the master skill

The core of applied pragmatist psychology: be open, aware, and actively engaged — contact the present moment, hold thoughts lightly, and act on values. Every other principle here bends toward this one.

(Hayes, Strosahl and Wilson, 2011.)

**Plain words** Flexibility isn't one skill among many; it's the skill that installs the others. Be open, aware, and actively engaged — contact the present moment, hold thoughts lightly, act on values. Every other principle here bends toward this one.

*For example:* The person who meditates, hates it, quits, tries walking meditation, keeps it — open to the moment, light on the thought 'I'm bad at meditating,' moving toward the value. That's the whole master skill, in one small story.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Psychological flexibility|Psychological flexibility]]**, predicts mental health and performance across hundreds of studies (Kashdan and Rottenberg, 2010; Hayes et al., 2011 meta-analyses)
