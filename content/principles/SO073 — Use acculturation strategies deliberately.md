> **Principle 73** — Use acculturation strategies deliberately

Use acculturation strategies — Berry's integration, assimilation, separation, and marginalization (1997) — deliberately, especially in migration and cultural transition. Integration (maintaining heritage culture while adopting the host culture) predicts the best outcomes; marginalization (losing both) predicts the worst. The choice is not automatic; it is a decision.

**Plain words** Acculturation strategies can be chosen — integration, assimilation, separation, marginalisation. Integration (heritage plus the new) does best on the outcomes. Most people drift; you can steer. Knowing the menu is the first move.

*For example:* The migrant who deliberately keeps the mother tongue at home and the local language at work — that's not divided loyalty, it's the integration strategy, chosen. The ones who choose do better than the ones who drift.
