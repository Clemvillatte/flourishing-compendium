> **Principle 148** — Let stillness heal

When the mind is calm and the qi ordered, the body's own healing activates: vitality itself is the great physician. Rest is treatment, not laziness.

(Neiye.)

**Plain words** Rest is treatment, not laziness. Make room for rest and stillness. Persistent exhaustion deserves appropriate assessment; stillness is not a substitute for care.

*For example:* The week you actually take off — sleep without an alarm, walks, no laptop — and by Friday the body feels like yours again. Rest did what willpower couldn't.
