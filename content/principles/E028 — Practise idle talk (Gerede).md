> **Principle 420** — Practise idle talk (Gerede)

Recognise the temptation of idle talk — the repetition of what others have said without genuine understanding. Idle talk is not merely gossip; it is the uncritical circulation of received opinions that substitutes for authentic engagement with the world. When you speak, ask: am I saying what I mean, or am I repeating what "they" say?

(Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §35.)

**Plain words** Idle talk (Gerede): the repetition of what others have said without genuine understanding — not merely gossip, but the uncritical circulation of received opinions that substitutes for authentic engagement. When you speak, ask whether you're saying something or relaying it.

*For example:* The meeting where everyone "knows" the reorg is a disaster — and one person asks who has actually read the document. Two hands. The room's certainty was Gerede doing what Gerede does.
