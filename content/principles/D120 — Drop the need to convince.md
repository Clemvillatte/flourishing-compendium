> **Principle 120** — Drop the need to convince

Those who know don't speak; those who speak don't know. Stop trying to win every conversation: persuasion is often just noise.

(DDJ 56.)

**Plain words** Stop trying to win every conversation. The need to convince is a need, and it's yours, not the listener's.

*For example:* The person who actually changed your mind did it with one question, not a hundred arguments.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Motivated reasoning|Motivated reasoning]]**, people evaluate arguments by desired conclusions (Kunda, 1990)
- **Backfire and resistance effects**
- **[[Motivational interviewing|Motivational interviewing as the alternative]]** (Miller and Rollnick, 2013)

## Philosophy parallels

- **Truth emerges from free discussion, not force** (Mill, On Liberty)
