> **Principle 77** — Avoid unnecessary danger

The one skilled at preserving life walks where there are no tigers and no enemy lines: the rhino finds no place to horn him. Don't go looking for battles you don't need.

(DDJ 50.)

**Plain words** Don't go looking for battles you don't need — the cheapest fight is the one your route never passes. The one skilled at preserving life walks where there are no tigers.

*For example:* Taking the long way around the known sketchy block at 2am isn't cowardice — it's route planning by someone who wants tomorrow.
