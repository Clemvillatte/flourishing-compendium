> **Principle 167** — Be sick of your sickness

Only when you recognize your faults as faults can you cure them. Self-honesty is the entrance to every improvement.

(DDJ 71.)

**Plain words** The flaw you refuse to name is the flaw you've married. Diagnosis isn't self-criticism; it's the beginning of treatment.

*For example:* The alcoholic who says 'I don't have a problem' versus the one who says 'I have a problem' — only one of them is now treatable.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Cognitive restructuring|Cognitive restructuring]]** (Beck, 1976), begins with identifying one's own distortions
- **Insight as a mechanism of change**

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[The unexamined life|The unexamined life]]** (Socrates, in Plato, Apology 38a)
- **Knowing the passions is mastering them** (Spinoza, Ethics IV-V)
