> **Principle 76** — See through perception

Perception is not a window but a construction: what you "see" is heavily shaped by expectation and history. Test your seeing; hold it as hypothesis.

(MN 18; Bahiya Sutta.)

**Plain words** Perception is not a window but a construction — what you 'see' is heavily shaped by expectation and history. Test your seeing; hold it as hypothesis. The world you're reacting to is partly a draft your mind is still writing.

*For example:* The neutral email read as hostile because you were already bruised — the hostility was manufactured in-house.
