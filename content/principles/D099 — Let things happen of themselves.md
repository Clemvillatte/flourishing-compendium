> **Principle 99** — Let things happen of themselves

The Liezi is full of events that occur "of themselves": ziran again: don't micromanage outcomes you only influence.

(Liezi; DDJ 37.)

**Plain words** Events occur 'of themselves' — ziran again. Don't micromanage outcomes you merely influence. There's a category of things where your job is to show up, not to control — and peace is knowing which category you're in.

*For example:* You can send the perfect application; you can't send the acceptance letter. Water the seed, let the sky decide the rest.
