> **Principle 15** — Make growth your criterion of value

The one question to keep asking of any course of action: does it enable further growth: of yourself and others? Growth is the only end that is never finished and never self-defeating.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922; Experience and Nature, 1925.)

**Plain words** Money, status, comfort all max out; growth compounds. Keep asking of any course of action: does it enable further growth, in yourself and others? It's the only end that's never finished and never self-defeating.

*For example:* The job offer decision: higher pay, zero learning, versus lower pay, steep curve. The growth criterion votes, and it's usually right within two years.
