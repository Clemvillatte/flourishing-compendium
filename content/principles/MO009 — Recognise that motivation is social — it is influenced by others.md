> **Principle 9** — Recognise that motivation is social: it is influenced by others

Motivation is not purely individual; it is influenced by social context. Others' goals, expectations, and support shape your motivation. Social norms, social support, and accountability all affect motivation.

**Plain words** Motivation is contagious — other people's goals, standards, and energy set yours more than you think. That's why gyms, studios, and offices have "atmosphere." Pick your exposure like it's fuel, because it is.

*For example:* Join a running club and suddenly you're a person with a 6 a.m. problem — nobody had to motivate you. The group's normal did the motivating.

## Practice

- Recognise that motivation is social. Surround yourself with people who support your goals. Use accountability (tell someone, work with others). Do not rely solely on willpower.
