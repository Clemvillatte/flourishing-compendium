> **Principle 128** — Begin in conversation

Democracy and growth start as conversation across difference: the social practice of testing ideas and enlarging experience. Protect the conditions of real talk: turn-taking, listening, honest disagreement.

(Addams, Twenty Years at Hull-House, 1910; Dewey, The Public and Its Problems, 1927.)

**Plain words** Kill real talk — turn-taking, listening, honest disagreement — and you don't get silence; you get politics by other means. Democracy and growth start as conversation across difference.

*For example:* The team meeting with a talking rule — everyone speaks once before anyone speaks twice. Silly, cheap, and it changed who got heard. That's institution-building at conversation scale.
