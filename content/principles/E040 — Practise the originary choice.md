> **Principle 432** — Practise the originary choice

Recognize that your fundamental orientation toward the world — your basic stance — is a choice that underlies all your particular choices. This originary choice is not deliberated but lived. It structures how you encounter the world, others, and yourself. Reflect on the deep structure of your orientation: is it toward engagement or withdrawal, openness or closure, affirmation or negation?

(Sartre, 1943/2003, Being and Nothingness, Part IV, ch. 2.)

**Plain words** Your fundamental orientation toward the world — your basic stance — is a choice underneath all your particular choices. Not deliberated, but lived: it structures how you encounter everything. Reflect on yours: is the world a hostile place, a task, a gift? That stance was chosen; it can be re-chosen.

*For example:* Two people get the same diagnosis: one's originary stance says "the world tests you," the other's says "the world gives lessons." Same hospital, same news — two entirely different years, from a choice neither remembers making.
