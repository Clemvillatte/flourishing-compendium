> **Principle 95** — Collect the mind (samadhi as gathering)

Gathering scattered attention into one place: the breath, the step, the task — builds a mind that can see clearly.

(Dhammapada 23, 372; MN 118.)

**Plain words** Gathering scattered attention into one place — the breath, the step, the task — builds a mind that can see clearly. Samadhi is collection, not levitation. A scattered mind is a bag of spilled marbles; a gathered one is a lens.

*For example:* The difference between 'working' with eleven tabs open and working with one — the marbles, gathered.
