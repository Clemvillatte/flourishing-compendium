> **Principle 19** — Read an idea through its consequences

Before adopting or rejecting a concept, spell out what it would mean in practice: what you would do differently, expect differently, feel differently. An idea you cannot cash out in consequences is not yet understood.

(Peirce, "How to Make Our Ideas Clear," 1878.)

**Plain words** An idea you can't cash out in consequences is not yet understood. Before adopting or rejecting a concept, spell out what you'd do differently, expect differently, feel differently. No consequences, no comprehension.

*For example:* 'I believe in sustainability' — okay: what changes on Monday? Nothing? Then you admire the idea; you don't hold it.
