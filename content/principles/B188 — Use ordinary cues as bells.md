> **Principle 188** — Use ordinary cues as bells

A red light, a doorbell, a phone chime: let each be a bell of mindfulness: stop, one breath, return. Cue by cue, the day becomes a practice.

(Thích Nhất Hạnh, The Miracle of Mindfulness.)

**Plain words** A red light, a doorbell, a phone chime — let each be a bell of mindfulness: stop, one breath, return. Cue by cue, the day becomes a practice. You don't need the temple bells; the traffic is ringing constantly.

*For example:* Every red light on the commute: one conscious breath instead of one curse — twenty free sessions, twice daily.
