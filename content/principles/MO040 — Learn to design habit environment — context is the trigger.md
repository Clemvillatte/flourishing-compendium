> **Principle 40** — Learn to design habit environment: context is the trigger

Habits are triggered by context (time, place, preceding action, people). Designing the environment to support desired habits (and undermine undesired ones) is more effective than willpower.

**Plain words** Habits are triggered by context — the time, the place, the chair, the people. Design the context and the habit half-forms itself. The environment is the training programme; you're just living in it.

*For example:* The meditation cushion that lives in the corner, visible, used — versus the app buried on page three of the phone. The cushion asks daily; the app never does.
## Psychology parallels

- **Environment and habit (Wood & Neal)**, environmental cues trigger habits automatically (Wood & Neal, 2007; Gardner, 2015)
- **Context change disrupts habits**, changing environment disrupts existing habits (Wood & Neal, 2007; Gardner, 2015)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[Designing for virtue|Designing for virtue]]** (Aristotle, Politics)
- **[[Wu wei|Wu wei through environment]]** (Daoism, Daodejing 19)
- **The environment shapes behaviour** (Dewey)

## Practice

- Design the environment: put the running shoes by the door, keep the book on the pillow, remove the phone from the bedroom. Make the environment trigger the desired behaviour.
