> **Principle 41** — Learn to disrupt bad habits: change the context

Bad habits are triggered by context. Changing the context (time, place, cues) disrupts the habit. Removing the trigger is more effective than resisting the urge.

**Plain words** Bad habits run on context — change the context and the habit loses its cue. New route to work, different chair, phone charger in another room. Disruption isn't willpower; it's demolition of the trigger.

*For example:* The afternoon biscuit that stopped when the desk moved away from the kitchen — the craving was real, but the cue was the kitchen. No kitchen, no craving loop.
## Psychology parallels

- **Habit disruption through context change**, changing environment disrupts habits (Wood & Neal, 2007; Gardner, 2015)
- **Cue removal**, removing the environmental trigger is more effective than willpower (Wood & Neal, 2007; Marteau et al., 2012)

## Practice

- Disrupt bad habits by changing the context: do not buy the junk food, remove the app, change the room, change the time. Do not rely on willpower; remove the trigger.
