> **Principle 2** — See everything as one system

God and Nature are one and the same thing, Spinoza says, using his famous formula deus sive natura: God, or in other words, Nature. Whatever exists is in Nature, and nothing exists outside it. The practical effect of this axiom is a change of frame: your failure, your enemy, your illness, and your mood are not foreign intrusions into reality but movements of the same single process that carries the stars. This does not mean approval; it means you stop splitting the world into the way things should be and the way things are, which is the split that generates most preventable suffering.

(Ethics I, especially P14 and P15.)

**Plain words** Deus sive natura: God, or in other words, Nature — whatever exists is in Nature, nothing outside it. The practical effect is a frame change: no outside. Your life, your body, your city, your problem — all one system, no enemies from beyond the walls.

*For example:* The person who frames their health, their mood, and their finances as separate problems with separate villains — and starts seeing the one system: the same stress is running the sleep, the spending, and the temper. One system, one fix point.
