> **Principle 73** — Recognise behavioural symptoms of ADHD: impulsivity and inattention

ADHD produces specific behavioural symptoms: impulsivity (acting without thinking), inattention, hyperactivity, difficulty with sustained tasks. These are symptoms, not choices. They are treatable.

**Plain words** ADHD's prints are impulsivity and inattention — the blurting, the lost keys, the half-finished projects, the astonishing hyperfocus on the wrong thing. A different engine, not a worse one. Behavioural scaffolding works.

*For example:* The person who can't file expenses but can rebuild an engine from memory — attention isn't missing; it's interest-gated. Design for the engine you have.
## Psychology parallels

- **ADHD as behavioural disorder**, impulsivity and inattention are core symptoms (APA, 2022; Barkley, 1997)
- **Evidence-based treatments**, medication and behavioural strategies are effective (Faraone et al., 2021; Brown, 2013)

## Practice

- If you have ADHD behavioural patterns (impulsivity, inattention), recognise them as symptoms, not choices. Treatment combines medication and behavioural strategies (external structure, routines, habit design). Seek evaluation.
