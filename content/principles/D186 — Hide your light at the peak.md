> **Principle 186** — Hide your light at the peak

At the top of your powers, dim down: retire the crown while the crowd still wants more: nothing survives being maximized.

(DDJ 4, 9, 36.)

**Plain words** At the top of your powers, dim down — retire the crown while the crowd still wants more. Nothing survives being maximized: the band that plays the encore until everyone's tired of them. Leaving at the peak isn't vanity; it's preservation — of the thing and the memory.

*For example:* The comedian who ends the set early with the room wanting more — versus the one who encores themselves into legend-destroying.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Goal disengagement|Goal disengagement]]**, stepping back at the right time protects health (Wrosch et al., 2003)
- **[[Peak-end rule|Peak-end research]]**, how things end shapes the memory of the whole (Kahneman)

- **A time for everything** (Ecclesiastes 3)
