> **Principle 78** — Connect to the natural world

Time in natural settings reduces rumination, restores attention, and lowers stress physiology. The effect is not about wilderness. Urban green space, a garden, a tree on a street corner, all produce measurable benefits. The mechanism is soft fascination: the environment engages attention without demanding it, which allows directed attention to recover.

(Bratman et al., 2015; two nature and attention restoration studies.)

**Plain words** Time in nature reduces rumination and restores attention. It doesn't require wilderness: a garden, a street tree and a park all do measurable good. The mechanism is soft fascination, attention caught without being demanded.

*For example:* A twenty-minute walk without a phone through a park breaks an afternoon of spiralling.
