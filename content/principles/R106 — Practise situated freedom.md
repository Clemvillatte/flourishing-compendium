> **Principle 368** — Practise situated freedom

Recognise that your freedom is always situated within a historical, social, and relational context. You are not free to be anything; you are free to choose how to respond to the situation in which you find yourself. Do not fantasise about unencumbered freedom. Exercise the freedom you have, where you are, with the resources available to you.

(Sartre, 1943/2003, Being and Nothingness; Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time; Merleau-Ponty, 1945/2012, Phenomenology of Perception.)

**Plain words** Freedom is always situated: you're not free to be anything, you're free to choose your response to the situation you're actually in. Drop the fantasy of unencumbered freedom; exercise the real freedom where you stand, with what you have.

*For example:* The caregiver who can't change the diagnosis, the job, the family — but spends the year perfecting what's genuinely theirs: the tone of every one of their own responses.
