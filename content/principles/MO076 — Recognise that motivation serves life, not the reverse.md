> **Principle 76** — Recognise that motivation serves life, not the reverse

Motivation is not an end in itself. It is a capacity that serves a life well-lived. The goal is not maximal motivation for its own sake but motivation that supports meaning, connection, engagement, and flourishing.

**Plain words** Motivation serves life, not the other way around — the point of all this engine-building is to go somewhere worth going. Perfect motivation with nothing loved is a sports car in a garage. Build the engine, then drive it somewhere that matters.

*For example:* The person optimising every habit, ritual, and routine — and the calendar underneath is empty of things they actually care about. The engine's tuned. Put it in the car and drive somewhere.

## Practice

- Ask: "does my motivation serve the life I want to live?" If motivation becomes compulsive striving or burnout, redirect toward sustainable, values-based motivation. Motivation serves life.
