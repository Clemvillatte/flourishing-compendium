> **Principle 98** — Recognise natural justice (physikon dikaion)

Aristotle distinguishes natural justice (what is just by nature, the same everywhere) from legal justice (what is just by convention, varying from place to place). Some things are unjust regardless of what any law says. Learn to distinguish what is merely legal from what is genuinely just.

(NE V.7, V.10; Politics I.5.)

**Plain words** Some things are just by nature, the same everywhere. Others only by convention, and vary. Some acts are wrong whatever a law says. Learn to tell what's merely legal from what's actually just.

*For example:* A company may legally pay a worker nothing for an hour of required setup. Many would still call that unjust.
