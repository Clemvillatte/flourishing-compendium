> **Principle 21** — Trust the law of compensation

Emerson's essay states the law in two registers: "Every act rewards itself, or, in other words integrates itself, in a twofold manner; first in the thing, or in real nature; and secondly in the circumstance, or in apparent nature." "Men call the circumstance the retribution," but "the causal retribution is in the thing and is seen by the soul" ([Emerson, Compensation](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2944/2944-h/2944-h.htm)). The reward inside the act is the actor's own change: "in labor as in life there can be no cheating. The thief steals from himself. The swindler swindles himself," for "the real price of labor is knowledge and virtue, whereof wealth and credit are signs" ([Emerson, Compensation](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2944/2944-h/2944-h.htm)). The mechanism in one sentence: "Crime and punishment grow out of one stem" ([Emerson, Compensation](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2944/2944-h/2944-h.htm)).

> "Cause and effect, means and ends, seed and fruit, cannot be severed; for the effect already blooms in the cause, the end preexists in the means, the fruit in the seed." (Emerson, Compensation, [Essays — First Series, Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2944/2944-h/2944-h.htm))

**Plain words**
Emerson's law has two registers, and both are his: the act shapes the actor, and the circumstance answers in time. The register you can use daily is the first: the knowledge and virtue that only the actual work builds cannot be counterfeited or stolen, so the shortcut and the honest route build different people even when they end at the same title ([Emerson, Compensation](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2944/2944-h/2944-h.htm)).

**For example**
Hypothetical illustration: two colleagues can take the same shortcut to the promotion; five years on, the difference is not the title but who each of them has become at meetings.

(Emerson, Compensation, Essays — First Series.)
