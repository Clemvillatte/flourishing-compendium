> **Principle 13** — Prioritise sleep as the foundation of mental health

Sleep is not optional; it is the foundation of mental health, emotional regulation, cognitive function, and well-being. Chronic sleep deprivation is associated with depression, anxiety, impaired cognition, and reduced well-being. Sleep is the master variable.

**Plain words** Sleep is the foundation, not the luxury — mood, thinking, regulation all stand on it. Chronic short sleep is a depression risk wearing a productivity costume. Guard the night like it's load-bearing, because it is.

*For example:* The "I'll sleep when I'm dead" crowd, dying slowly of everything except sleep — and the week that proves it: the fight, the tears, the spiral, all following four bad nights. The foundation was the whole problem.
## Psychology parallels

- **Sleep and mental health**, sleep deprivation is associated with depression, anxiety, and impaired emotion regulation (Walker, 2017; Alhola & Polo-Kantola, 2007)
- **Sleep and emotional reactivity**, sleep loss increases amygdala reactivity by ~60% and reduces prefrontal regulation (Yoo et al., 2007)
- **Sleep is a treatment target**, improving sleep improves mental health (Morin et al., 2006; CBT-I)

## Practice

- Prioritise sleep above almost everything else: 7-9 hours for adults, consistent schedule, dark and cool bedroom, no screens before bed. Sleep is the foundation, not a luxury.
