> **Principle 78** — Read action in its context: always

No behavior means anything out of context: the same act is kindness or intrusion depending on the situation. Judge and plan actions contextually, never abstractly.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922; functional contextualism — Hayes et al., 2011.)

**Plain words** No behavior means anything out of context — the same act is kindness or intrusion depending on the situation. Judge and plan actions contextually, never abstractly. 'Is texting during dinner rude?' has no answer; it has a situation.

*For example:* The same question — 'you okay?' — from a stranger, a boss, a friend on the day of the funeral. One is care, one is surveillance, one is love.
