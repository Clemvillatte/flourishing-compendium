> **Principle 278** — Practise connection to family and kinship

Know your kinship system. In Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander traditions, kinship defines your responsibilities, obligations, and relationships. It is not merely biological but extends through skin groups, totems, and ceremonial connections. Honour your kinship obligations.

(Aboriginal SEWB framework; Dudgeon et al., 2014, Working Together.)

**Plain words** Kinship is a system that tells you what you owe and to whom — wider than biology, running through skin groups, totems, and ceremony. Learn your system, and honour its obligations. It's not paperwork; it's the load-bearing structure of a life.

*For example:* The young woman who knows exactly which auntie to call, which cousin she's responsible for, whose country she's visiting — and is never stranded, because the map has never failed her.
