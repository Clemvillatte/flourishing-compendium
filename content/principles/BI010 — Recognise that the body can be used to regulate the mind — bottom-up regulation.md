> **Principle 10** — Recognise that the body can be used to regulate the mind: bottom-up regulation

Regulation is not only top-down (cognitive, thinking your way to calm); it is also bottom-up (using the body to change the state). Breath, posture, movement, temperature, and touch all regulate the nervous system directly.

**Plain words** You can regulate from the bottom up: breath, posture, movement, temperature, touch — the body changes the mind. Everyone tries to think their way to calm; fewer try breathing their way there. The back door is often unlocked.

*For example:* The anxious loop that no amount of reasoning fixed, dissolved by ten slow breaths and a walk — the mind was stuck, the body wasn't. Change the state, and the thoughts follow.
## Psychology parallels

- **Bottom-up regulation**, breathing, movement, posture, and temperature regulate the nervous system directly (Lehrer & Gevirtz, 2014; Zaccaro et al., 2018)
- **Breath as direct autonomic regulator**, slow breathing activates parasympathetic and reduces arousal (Zaccaro et al., 2018; Lehrer & Gevirtz, 2014)

## Practice

- Use the body to regulate: when distressed, use bottom-up tools — slow breathing (4-6 breaths per minute), gentle movement, cold water, upright posture, or touch. Do not rely only on thinking.
