> **Principle 192** — Do not let grief become your identity

Grief is natural. But when grief becomes your identity, it has become a passion. The Stoic feels grief, allows it, and then returns to life. Seneca's consolation letters model this movement: feel, acknowledge, and then re-engage.

(Seneca, Letters 63, 99, 107; Med. IV.1, IV.41; Disc. I.11, III.24.)

**Plain words** Grief is natural. But when it becomes your identity, it's turned into a passion. The Stoic feels it, allows it, and returns to life. Seneca's letters model the movement: feel, acknowledge, re-engage.

*For example:* A widow who never stops being "the one who lost her husband" has lost something else too.
