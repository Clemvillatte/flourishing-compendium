> **Principle 310** — Practise ofo

Feel compassion for those who suffer. Ofo is the Samoan concept of compassion that arises when you witness the suffering of others. Do not suppress this feeling; let it motivate you to act.

(Samoan philosophy; Tuhiwai Smith, 1999, Decolonizing Methodologies.)

**Plain words** Ofo: the pang you feel at another's suffering. Don't suppress it or argue with it — it's the signal, and its job is to move you. Feeling it is the start of acting on it.

*For example:* Someone who sees the flood footage and doesn't scroll past — the pang itself decides the evening, and the donation and the drives follow.
