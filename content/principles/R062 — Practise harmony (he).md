> **Principle 324** — Practise harmony (he)

Seek harmony in your relationships and in your community. Harmony is not sameness; it is the creative integration of difference. The Confucian concept of he contrasts with tong (mere agreement). True harmony requires diverse voices.

(Confucius, The Analects 12.23, 13.23, 13.27; The Analects 1.12.)

**Plain words** He is harmony as the creative integration of difference — explicitly not tong, mere sameness. True harmony needs diverse voices; a room of agreement is a quieter, poorer thing. Build the ensemble, not the choir of one note.

*For example:* The project team deliberately staffed with people who'll fight — and led so the fights compose. The result is better than any same-minded team produces.
