> **Principle 77** — Climb the subtlety ladder

Absorption is graded: with reasoning (vitarka), beyond reasoning (vichara), with joy (ananda), and with self-sense alone (asmita). The ladder teaches that objects differ in subtlety, and that the practice naturally refines from coarse to fine. Practically, when a coarse object (breath, sensation) becomes easy, do not celebrate; refine.

(YS 1.17.)

**Plain words** Absorption is graded: with reasoning, beyond reasoning, with joy, with self-sense alone — a ladder of subtlety. Objects differ in fineness, and the practice naturally refines from coarse to fine. When a coarse object stops yielding, move one rung subtler.

*For example:* Someone works the breath (coarse) until it's fully familiar, then notices the practice wants the subtler object — the feeling of the noticing itself. The ladder was always meant to be climbed, not camped on.
