> **Principle 43** — Develop insight (vipassana)

Once the mind is steady, look directly into the nature of experience: its impermanence, its unsatisfactoriness, its ownerlessness. See things as they actually are.

(MN 10; Dhammapada 277-279.)

**Plain words** Once the mind is steady, look directly into the nature of experience — its impermanence, its unsatisfactoriness, its ownerlessness. See things as they actually are. Insight isn't a belief you adopt; it's what becomes visible once the water stops moving.

*For example:* Watching a craving fully — it swells, peaks, and dies without being fed — the observation IS the insight, no belief needed.
