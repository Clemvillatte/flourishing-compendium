> **Principle 152** — Act for the common good

The Stoic is not a solitary sage. Every action should serve the community. You are a part of a larger whole, and your actions affect it. Ask of any action: does this serve the common good? If not, revise it.

(Med. II.1, VII.5, VII.55, IX.6; Disc. II.10, III.7, III.24; Seneca, Letters 48, 95.)

**Plain words** The Stoic isn't a solitary sage. Every action should serve the community, because you're part of a larger whole and your actions affect it. Ask of any plan: does this serve the common good? If not, revise it.

*For example:* Before taking a shortcut at work that benefits you, you consider who has to clean up after it.
