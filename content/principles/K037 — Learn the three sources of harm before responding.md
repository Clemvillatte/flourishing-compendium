> **Principle 37** — Learn the three sources of harm before responding

Shantideva's analysis: harm comes from people, from events, and from your own past actions catching up. Each source calls for a different first response: from people, patience; from events, problem-solving; from consequences, ownership. Most conflict escalates because the responses are swapped (you problem-solve a person, own an event, blame the person for the consequence). Practically, in the next injury you receive, classify the source before speaking.

(Shantideva, Bodhicharyavatara, ch. 6.)

**Plain words** Harm comes from three places: people, events, and your own past actions catching up. Each needs a different first response: patience for people, problem-solving for events, ownership for consequences. Most conflict escalates because the responses are swapped.

*For example:* A leaking roof isn't someone's fault. Treating it as an insult wastes a week. Treating a colleague's rudeness as a plumbing problem fails too.
