> **Principle 37** — Learn the three sources of harm before responding

Shantideva's chapter on patience works by tracing harm to its sources, and the analysis dissolves the anger at each step. Harm arrives from things without minds and from beings with them: "Since injury is inflicted on embodied beings by both those with a mind and things having no mind, why single out and begrudge (only) those with a mind? Therefore, be patient with harm!" (Bodhicharyavatara 6.66). From beings, the analysis runs further: the person who wields the staff "was incited by anger, so he's secondary (too). It would be more fitting to get enraged with his anger" (6.41). And some of the harm is older than this moment: "Previously, I must have inflicted such pain on limited beings, therefore, it's fitting that harm comes to me, who've been a cause of violence toward limited ones" (6.42). The whole variety of harmful acts "arise from the force of conditions: there aren't any under their own power" (6.25) (translation carried on the opened [redzambala page](https://buddhism.redzambala.com/shantideva/bodhisattva-behaviour-by-shantideva-part-6.html)).

This entry's application: each source calls for a different first response — patience for people, problem-solving for events, ownership for consequences. Most conflict escalates because the responses are swapped (you problem-solve a person, own an event, blame the person for the consequence). Practically, in the next injury you receive, classify the source before speaking.

**Plain words** Shantideva's move is to ask where harm comes from before reacting. Three answers cover it: a thing without a mind (the weather, the leaking roof), a person driven by their own anger, and your own past actions coming back. None of these is a fit target for rage, and each asks for something different from you: a repair, a patience, an ownership. The practice: classify before responding.

For example: A leaking roof isn't someone's fault. Treating it as an insult wastes a week. Treating a colleague's rudeness as a plumbing problem fails too.
