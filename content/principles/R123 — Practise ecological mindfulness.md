> **Principle 385** — Practise ecological mindfulness

Be mindful of your ecological impact. Pay attention to what you consume, what you waste, what you destroy. Mindfulness is not merely an inner practice; it extends to your relationship with the material world. Practise ecological mindfulness in your daily choices.

(Macy and Brown, 2014, Coming Back to Life: Practices to Reconnect Our Lives, Our World; Hooker et al., 2020, "Everyday Engagement with Nature," Environment and Behavior.)

**Plain words** Ecological mindfulness: pay attention to what you consume, waste, and destroy. Mindfulness isn't just an inner practice — it extends to your relationship with the material world. See your supply chain; it's part of your mind's environment too.

*For example:* Someone who actually reads where their clothes are made, what's in the cleaner, how the food traveled — and finds that just seeing it changes what they reach for.
