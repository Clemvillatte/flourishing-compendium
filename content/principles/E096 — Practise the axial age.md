> **Principle 488** — Practise the axial age

Recognise that you stand within a historical transformation. Jaspers described the Axial Age (800-200 BCE) as the period in which the major philosophical and religious traditions of the world emerged — Confucianism, Buddhism, Zoroastrianism, Judaism, Greek philosophy. You are heir to this transformation. Recognise your place in the history of consciousness.

(Jaspers, 1949/1953, The Origin and Goal of History; Jaspers, 1948/1971, "On the Concept of the Axial Age," in The Origin and Goal of History.)

**Plain words** You stand within a historical transformation: the Axial Age (800–200 BCE) — Confucianism, Buddhism, Zoroastrianism, Judaism, Greek philosophy — produced the traditions that still structure your possibilities. You inherited the questions; the answers are your generation's shift. Study the tradition you're standing in.

*For example:* The modern person arguing about justice, the good life, and suffering is having a Confucian-Buddhist-Greek argument with new examples — and knows it once they've read the originals. The dialogue is 2,500 years old; you're the newest speaker in it.
