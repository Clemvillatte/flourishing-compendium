> **Principle 56** — Interrupt the chain from stimulus to ruin

The Gita gives the tradition's clearest causal chain: dwelling on an object breeds attachment; attachment breeds craving; craving breeds anger; anger breeds delusion; delusion destroys memory; lost memory destroys wisdom; and lost wisdom destroys the person. The practice is interception at the earliest link, and the chain is taught so you can find your link. Practically, identify your usual entry link (most people enter at dwelling), and post your watch there.

(BG 2.62-2.63.)

**Plain words** The Gita's clearest causal chain: dwelling breeds attachment; attachment breeds craving; craving breeds anger; anger breeds delusion; delusion destroys memory; lost memory destroys wisdom; lost wisdom destroys the person. The practice point: the chain is interruptible at every link — earliest is cheapest.

*For example:* Someone catches the chain at "dwelling" — the third hour of refreshing the rival's profile — and steps out for a walk. The same chain, interrupted at link one, never produces the anger it would have produced by link four.
