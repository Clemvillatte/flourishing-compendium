> **Principle 140** — Respect others' nature: don't cage what you love

The seabird died from being honored with human ceremonies. Don't love others by your own recipe; love them by theirs.

(Zhuangzi 18.)

**Plain words** What feels like care to the giver can be captivity to the receiver. Don't love others by your own recipe; love them by theirs — the menu isn't yours.

*For example:* Grandparents who feed a vegetarian grandchild their 'favorite' roast — love, delivered in the wrong language, reads as disrespect.
