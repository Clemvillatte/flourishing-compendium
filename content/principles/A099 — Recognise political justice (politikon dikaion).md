> **Principle 99** — Recognise political justice (politikon dikaion)

Political justice is justice within a polis: the framework of laws, institutions, and customs that govern the community. It includes both natural and conventional elements. Do not treat politics as separate from ethics; the conditions of flourishing are partly political.

(NE V.6, V.7; Politics III.1.)

**Plain words** Political justice is the framework of laws and customs that shape a community. Part natural, part conventional. Aristotle says politics isn't separate from ethics. Some of the conditions for flourishing are political.

*For example:* A neighbourhood with good public transport makes it easier for everyone to live well, whatever their personal virtue.
