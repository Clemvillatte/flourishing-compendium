> **Principle 61** — Learn to cultivate meaning: purpose sustains motivation

Meaning — the sense that your life and actions matter — is a powerful and sustainable motivator. Goals connected to purpose (contribution, growth, connection) are more motivating than goals without it.

**Plain words** Meaning is the deep fuel — the sense that this matters, to someone, for something. It sustains when reward, novelty, and mood have all quit. Connect the task to the point; motivation follows the connection.

*For example:* The data entry that becomes bearable when tied to the patients it serves — same keystrokes, different story. The work didn't change; the meaning did.
## Psychology parallels

- **Meaning and motivation**, sense of purpose predicts motivation and goal attainment (Steger, 2012; Steger et al., 2006)
- **Meaning in life and well-being**, presence of meaning is associated with higher well-being (Steger, 2012; Frankl, 1959)

## Practice

- Connect goals to meaning: how does this serve something larger than myself? What is my purpose? Goals tied to purpose are more sustainable than goals without.
