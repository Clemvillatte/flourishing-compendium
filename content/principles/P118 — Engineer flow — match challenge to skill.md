> **Principle 118** — Engineer flow: match challenge to skill

Flow lives at the meeting of high challenge and high skill: too easy is boredom, too hard is anxiety. Adjust challenge: not always down: raise skill.

(Csikszentmihalyi, Flow, 1990.)

**Plain words** Flow lives at the meeting of high challenge and high skill — too easy is boredom, too hard is anxiety. Adjust challenge; not always down: raise skill. The dial has two ends, and people forget the second one.

*For example:* The job that got boring after year two — the fix isn't an easier job or a vacation. It's a harder problem. Boredom is a skills surplus announcing itself.
