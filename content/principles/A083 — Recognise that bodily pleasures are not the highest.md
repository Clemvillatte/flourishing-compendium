> **Principle 83** — Recognise that bodily pleasures are not the highest

Bodily pleasures (food, drink, sex) are necessary but not the highest. The highest pleasures are those of the intellect: the pleasure of understanding, of contemplation, of friendship. Do not arrange your life around bodily pleasure; include it but do not make it the centre.

(NE X.3, X.5, VII.14.)

**Plain words** Eating, drinking and sex are necessary, not the top of the ladder. Aristotle ranks the pleasures of understanding, contemplation and friendship higher. Include the bodily ones, just don't build your life around them.

*For example:* A holiday planned entirely around meals can be lovely. A life planned that way gets thin.
