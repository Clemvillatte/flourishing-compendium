> **Principle 33** — Cultivate the seven factors of awakening

Mindfulness, investigation of states, energy, joy, tranquility, concentration, and equanimity: these are the seven factors that, developed and balanced, mature the mind.

(SN 46; Dhammapada 372.)

**Plain words** Mindfulness, investigation, energy, joy, tranquility, concentration, and equanimity — the seven factors that, developed and balanced, mature the mind. Not seven separate projects but one recipe: notice which ingredient the moment is missing and add it.

*For example:* A stale practice gets revived by deliberately cultivating joy — sometimes the missing factor isn't discipline, it's delight.
