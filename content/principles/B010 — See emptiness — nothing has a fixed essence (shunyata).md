> **Principle 10** — See emptiness: nothing has a fixed essence (shunyata)

Things: including you, your problems, and your enemies — are not solid, self-contained realities but shifting networks of conditions. What is empty can change.

(Nagarjuna, MMK; Heart Sutra.)

**Plain words** Things — including you, your problems, and your enemies — aren't solid, self-contained realities but shifting networks of conditions. What is empty can change. This isn't nihilism; it's the good news that nothing is locked in place, including the situation you're sure is permanent.

*For example:* The 'impossible' boss retires, and the whole job transforms — the solidity was a special effect of circumstances.
