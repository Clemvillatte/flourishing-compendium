> **Principle 4** — Treat flourishing as multi-dimensional

Both leading frameworks refuse the single dial. Seligman's has five measurable elements, positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning and accomplishment, each pursued by many people for its own sake and measurable independently. Huppert and So build theirs from the other end: they take the internationally agreed criteria for depression and anxiety, define the opposite of each symptom, and land on ten features that combine feeling and functioning. In both, someone who is high on one dimension and low on another is not a rounding error; the profile is the finding.

> In 2011, I suggested that PERMA (Positive Emotion, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning and Accomplishment) are five measurable elements that make up well-being (Seligman, 2011, pp 16–25).
> (Seligman 2018, The Journal of Positive Psychology)

> Flourishing refers to the experience of life going well. It is a combination of feeling good and functioning effectively.
> (Huppert and So 2013, Social Indicators Research 110)

Aristotle made the same refusal twenty-three centuries earlier: "human good turns out to be activity of soul in accordance with virtue, and if there are more than one virtue, in accordance with the best and most complete" (Nicomachean Ethics I.7). Happiness, for him, is an activity and not a state, since a state of mind can exist without producing any good result, "as in a man who is asleep". The compendium's application: when you assess your life, do not average it. Score the dimensions separately, notice which one is lowest, and let the low one set the agenda; a single number hides exactly the thing that needs work.

**Plain words** When someone asks how your life is going, the honest answer is usually a list, not a number. You can be absorbed in work, sleeping badly, close to your family, and unsure what it is all for, all at once. The multi-dimensional view is the refusal to add those up. Ask instead which dimension is lowest, and what would improve it this month. The number you would compute is not stable; the profile is where the information is.

*For example:* Imagine a surgeon with a high salary and a full operating list who cannot name a person to call on a bad weekend. On a single-dial measure of success the life scores well; on the five elements, or on Huppert and So's ten, it has an obvious hole.
