> **Principle 4** — Treat flourishing as multi-dimensional

Flourishing is not a single variable. The PERMA model identifies five dimensions: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment. Other models add health, financial security, and psychological stability. No single dimension is sufficient. A person high in accomplishment but low in relationships is not flourishing, however productive. The multi-dimensional view prevents the reduction of well-being to happiness or productivity.

(Seligman, 2011; Huppert and So, 2013.)

**Plain words** Flourishing isn't one variable. PERMA names five parts: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning and accomplishment. Other models add health, money and stability. No single dimension is enough, and someone high in achievement but low in relationships isn't flourishing.

*For example:* A successful surgeon with no close friends and a good salary would score high on one side and low on another.
