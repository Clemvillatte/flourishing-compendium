> **Principle 173** — Accept the world's dirt

To receive the state's dirt is to be the lord of the valley: take blame, criticism, and the mess without defensiveness: it's the price and proof of standing.

(DDJ 78.)

**Plain words** To receive the state's dirt is to be lord of the valley — take blame, criticism, and the mess without defensiveness. It's the price and proof of standing. Leaders who deflect every criticism eventually have no one willing to tell them anything.

*For example:* The captain who takes the blame publicly and fixes privately — the team would follow that person into weather.

## Psychology parallels

- **Growth from negative feedback**, defensiveness blocks learning
- **[[Self-verification theory (Swann)|Self-verification versus self-enhancement]]** (Swann, 1983)

## Philosophy parallels

- **"the best revenge is not to be like your enemy"** (Marcus Aurelius, Meditations VI)
- **[[Humility|Humility under reproach]]** (Augustine, The City of God)
