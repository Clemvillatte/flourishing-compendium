> **Principle 64** — Begin with faith, energy, and memory

The path's early supports are named as a sequence: faith (shraddha) that the practice is worth doing, which generates energy (virya), which stabilizes memory (smriti), which ripens into settledness and insight. The sequence is a motivation design: faith is not required as belief, only as working trust sufficient to fuel effort. Practically, when energy is missing, do not will harder; revisit the trust (talk to someone for whom the practice works).

(YS 1.20.)

**Plain words** The early supports, as a sequence: faith that the practice is worth doing, which generates energy, which stabilizes memory, which ripens into settledness and insight. Faith isn't required as belief in results — just enough trust to generate the next day's energy. Start the sequence anywhere it's honest.

*For example:* The beginner who can't honestly claim faith — but can claim curiosity, which funds one week of practice, which produces one small result, which produces actual faith. The sequence builds itself; you just have to start it somewhere.
