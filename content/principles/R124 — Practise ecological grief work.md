> **Principle 386** — Practise ecological grief work

Acknowledge your grief for the loss of species, ecosystems, and places. Ecological grief — the mourning of ecological loss — is not a pathology but a legitimate response to real loss. Do not suppress it. Let it move you to action.

(Cunsolo and Landman, 2017, "Mourning Nature," in Mourning Nature; Albrecht, 2019, Earth Emotions: New Words for a New World.)

**Plain words** Grief for lost species, ecosystems, places is not pathology — it's the correct response to real loss. Ecological grief work: don't suppress it; let it move you to action. The mourning is part of the functioning.

*For example:* Someone weeps for the burnt valley they grew up hiking — and the grief doesn't get medicated away; it becomes the volunteer seed-collecting Saturdays.
