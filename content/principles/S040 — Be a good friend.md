> **Principle 162** — Be a good friend

Friendship is a Stoic practice. The Stoic friend is reliable, honest, present in adversity, and not dependent on the friend for happiness. Be the friend you would want to have. Do not use friendship as a means to an end.

(Med. I.1, I.5, I.11, I.15; Seneca, Letters 3, 6, 9, 35, 63, 109; Disc. II.10, III.7, III.24; Musonius Rufus, Lectures.)

**Plain words** Friendship is a Stoic practice. The Stoic friend is reliable, honest, present when things go wrong, and doesn't depend on the friend for happiness. Be the friend you'd want, and don't use people.

*For example:* Calling a friend who has just lost a job, not to cheer him up but to listen.
