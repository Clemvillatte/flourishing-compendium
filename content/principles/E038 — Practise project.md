> **Principle 430** — Practise project

Recognise that your existence is a project — not a plan you have made but the fundamental orientation of your being. You are always projecting toward possibilities, always ahead of yourself. The question is not whether you have a project but whether you own the project you have. Examine your fundamental project: what are you living for?

(Sartre, 1943/2003, Being and Nothingness, Part IV, ch. 2; Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §§31-34.)

**Plain words** Your existence is a project — not a plan you made but the fundamental orientation of your being: always projecting toward possibilities, always ahead of yourself. The question isn't whether you have a project; it's whether you own the one you have. Audit it.

*For example:* Someone's project turns out, on inspection, to be "remain comfortable and be approved of" — a project they never chose, running for years. The audit doesn't change the project overnight; it just ends the deniability.
