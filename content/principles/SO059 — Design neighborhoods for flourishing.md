> **Principle 59** — Design neighborhoods for flourishing

Design neighborhoods — the physical and social environment of daily life — for adaptive flourishing. The evidence: walkable neighborhoods, green space, and social cohesion predict physical activity, mental health, and well-being (Diez Roux and Mair, 2010; Branas et al., 2011; Sampson et al., 1997).

**Plain words** Neighbourhoods are health infrastructure — walkability, green space, and social cohesion predict activity, mental health, and connection. Your postcode does more daily biology than your GP. Invest in the design of where you live.

*For example:* The same person, two addresses — one with a park and a corner shop, one with neither — two different activity levels, two different social lives. The address was prescribing.
