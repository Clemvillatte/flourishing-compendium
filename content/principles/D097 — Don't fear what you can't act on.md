> **Principle 97** — Don't fear what you can't act on

The man of Qi feared the sky would fall and couldn't eat or sleep: a life spent dying of an imaginary event. Sort worries into actionable and unactionable; drop the second pile.

(Liezi.)

**Plain words** Sort worries into actionable and un-actionable, and drop the second pile. Most fear is tuition paid for a course that never runs.

*For example:* Losing a week of sleep over a rumor of layoffs — then the rumor dissolves. The sky billed you either way.
