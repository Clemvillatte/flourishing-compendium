> **Principle 97** — Don't fear what you can't act on

The Liezi's most famous story runs on two sentences and one symptom. A man of Qi worries that heaven and earth will collapse, leaving his body nowhere to rest — the worry becomes so consuming he stops eating and sleeping ([Liezi 1, Graham translation, Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%9D%9E%E4%BA%BA%E6%86%82%E5%A4%A9)). Note the cost: he is not struck by a falling sky, he is starved by the fear of one. The story is still known in Chinese as 杞人憂天, "the man of Qi fears the sky" — groundless fear.

The friend's cure is not reassurance; it is physics. Sky is accumulated air, everywhere, so it cannot fall; the stars are luminous patches of that air, so even if they fell they could not strike anyone; the earth fills every void, so it cannot give way. But the point the compendium carries is not the old cosmology — it is the sorting move. The friend separates the fear into pieces and asks what each piece actually implies. When a piece implies nothing you can do, the worry has nothing to chew on and loses its grip.

> "There was a man in Qi who worried that Heaven and Earth would collapse, leaving him nowhere to exist. He couldn't eat or sleep." (Liezi 1, A. C. Graham translation, [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%9D%9E%E4%BA%BA%E6%86%82%E5%A4%A9))

**Plain words** Two people can have the same imagined disaster on their minds. One sorts it: is there anything I can actually do today, or not? If not, the worry stays but has no handle to bite. The other does not sort — the worry runs, and the body pays rent on a room it is not using. The man of Qi never needed a doctor or a stronger argument; he needed someone to ask what, specifically, would happen next. The sky is fine. The sleep is the thing being destroyed.

*For example:* A colleague hears a rumor of layoffs and loses a week of sleep. Another hears the same rumor, spends ten minutes updating a resume and sending two emails, then stops. Both faced the same uncertainty. Only one of them was still functional when the rumor dissolved.
