> **Principle 152** — Know yourself ecologically

You are constituted by your relations to food, place, people, organisms, and atmosphere: an ecological self, not a skin-bounded one. Act for the web that acts for you.

(Cobb and process ecology — secularized; Whitehead's communal cosmos.)

**Plain words** "Environment" is not what surrounds you; it's what continues you. You are constituted by your relations to food, place, people, organisms, and atmosphere — act for the web that acts for you.

*For example:* The gut microbiome rewriting your moods, the street trees cooling your sleep — the skin is an administrative border, not a real boundary. You are larger than you, and you have staff.
