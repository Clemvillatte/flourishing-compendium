> **Principle 74** — Let absorption come from continuity

Meditation proper (dhyana) is defined as the continuous flow of cognition toward the object: the state where attention stops returning and begins resting. It is not a different skill from concentration; it is concentration after enough returns. Practically, do not chase absorption; count returns, and let the continuity accumulate on its own schedule.

(YS 3.2.)

**Plain words** Meditation proper is the continuous flow of cognition toward the object — attention stops returning and begins resting. Not a different skill from concentration; it's concentration after enough returns. Don't chase absorption; fund the returns.

*For example:* Every sit that "failed to be deep" but logged two hundred returns — that's the account the absorption is drawn on. The one who sits waiting to feel absorbed gets nothing; the one who returns gets everything.
