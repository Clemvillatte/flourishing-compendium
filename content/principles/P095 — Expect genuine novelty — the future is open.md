> **Principle 95** — Expect genuine novelty: the future is open

Whitehead's process cosmology makes the openness of the future structural rather than hopeful. Becoming, on his first category of explanation, "is a creative advance into novelty", and the creative advance is "the application of this ultimate principle of creativity to each novel situation which it originates" ([Process and Reality, 1929](http://www.palmyreoomen.nl/uploads/pdf's/A.N.Whitehead_Process-and-Reality.pdf), Part I, Ch. II). Novelty is not noise in the system; it is how the system runs.

> The future is merely real, without being actual; whereas the past is a nexus of actualities.
>
> (Whitehead, Process and Reality, 1929, Part V)

What exists settled is the past; the future is real as potential, "the reality of what is potential, in its character of a real component of what is actual". The compendium's application: plans are made from the settled and the likely, and they live inside a future that keeps arriving as genuinely new situations. Expecting novelty is not pessimism about planning; it is the reason plans have checkpoints.

**Plain words**
The past is finished and the future is not; that is the whole idea. The settled part of the world is its past, and the next situation is where the deciding happens. Keep the plan, and treat being surprised as the plan working rather than as the plan failing. The compendium's exercise: take one fixed expectation about the next year and write what you would do if it turned out wrong.

*For example:*
A plan written in 2019 met 2020, and the interesting question became what the new situation asked for rather than what the plan said. On Whitehead's terms the surprise is the novel situation that the creative advance keeps originating.
