> **Principle 95** — Expect genuine novelty: the future is open

The universe is not a film already shot: the future is genuinely undecided, and creative advance into novelty is real. Plan, but expect to be surprised — and to create.

(Whitehead, Process and Reality, 1929.)

**Plain words** The universe is not a film already shot — the future is genuinely undecided, and creative advance into novelty is real. Plan, but expect to be surprised — and to create. Determinism is a mood, not a finding; tomorrow is unwritten and you hold a pen.

*For example:* Nobody's 2019 plan included 2020 — and the people who created new things inside the surprise did better than the ones waiting for the film to resume.
