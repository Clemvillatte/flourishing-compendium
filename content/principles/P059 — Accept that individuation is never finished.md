> **Principle 59** — Accept that individuation is never finished

You are not a finished individual who then enters relations; you are an ongoing individuation: becoming yourself through and through your relations and activities. Never say "this is who I am" in the past tense.

(Simondon, L'individuation, 1958.)

**Plain words** You're not a finished individual who then enters relations — you're an ongoing individuation, becoming yourself through and through your relations and activities. Never say 'this is who I am' in the past tense. The carving isn't done; the chisel is moving right now.

*For example:* 'I'm just not a morning person' — a claim of finished essence from someone who has never once tried the experiment. The jury is still out, permanently.
