> **Principle 160** — Be a good citizen

The Stoic does not withdraw from political life. Marcus was emperor; Seneca was advisor to Nero; Musonius Rufus was exiled for his political stance. The Stoic participates in the political community, not for personal gain but for the common good. Engage with your community politically.

(Med. I.1, I.6, IV.3, V.1, VI.7, IX.6; Seneca, Letters 14, 73, 73, 91; Disc. III.7, III.24.)

**Plain words** The Stoic doesn't withdraw from public life. Marcus ruled, Seneca advised an emperor, Musonius was exiled for his stance. Take part in your community's politics for the common good, not for gain.

*For example:* Showing up to a local planning meeting about the clinic's funding, when you'd rather stay home with a book.
