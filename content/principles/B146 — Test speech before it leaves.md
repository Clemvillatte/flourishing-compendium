> **Principle 146** — Test speech before it leaves

Is it true? Is it timely? Is it beneficial? Is it gentle? If it fails any of these, refine or hold it.

(Sutta Nipata 3.3; Abhaya Sutta, MN 58.)

**Plain words** Is it true? Is it timely? Is it beneficial? Is it gentle? Test speech before it leaves — if it fails any of these, refine or hold it. Four gates, half a second each. Most of what we regret saying failed at least two of them in transit.

*For example:* The perfect comeback that was true but failed 'timely' and 'gentle' — held at the gate, it aged into wisdom instead of a wound.
