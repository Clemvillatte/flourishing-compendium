> **Principle 73** — Don't over-plan: act from presence

The true person doesn't scheme ahead. Plan enough to start; then respond to reality rather than to the plan.

(Zhuangzi 6; DDJ 15.)

**Plain words** Don't scheme ahead. Plan enough to start; then respond to reality rather than to the plan. The plan is a hypothesis — reality is the experiment. People who marry the plan end up defending it against their own eyes.

*For example:* The startup that treats its business plan as a living document survives; the one that executes it blindly is famously a case study now.
