> **Principle 12** — Use cultural values as a lens, not a cage

Treat cultural values — Schwartz's autonomy, embeddedness, hierarchy, mastery, and harmony; Hofstede's individualism, power distance, uncertainty avoidance, masculinity, and long-term orientation; Inglehart and Welzel's survival vs. self-expression values — as a lens through which to understand your own and others' behavior, not a cage that determines it. Values are statistical tendencies, not individual destinies.

**Plain words** Cultural values are a lens, not a cage — they're real and they shape what you see, but you can take the lens off and examine it. Schwartz, Hofstede: the frameworks are maps, and maps are for reading, not living on.

*For example:* "In our family we don't discuss money" — a value-lens that feels like physics until you look at it. It's a lens. Lenses can be cleaned, upgraded, occasionally replaced.
