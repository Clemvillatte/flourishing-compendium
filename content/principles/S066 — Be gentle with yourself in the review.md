> **Principle 188** — Be gentle with yourself in the review

Marcus tells himself not to be angry with the person who fails to live up to their principles. That person is himself. The self-examination is honest but not harsh. You are training, not judging. Return to the practice without guilt.

(Med. II.1, V.9, VI.6, VI.21, X.1, XI.9, XI.18; Disc. III.25.)

**Plain words** Marcus tells himself not to be angry with the person who falls short of their principles, who is himself. The review is honest but not harsh. You're training, not judging. Return to the practice without guilt.

*For example:* You miss a week of practice and begin again on Monday, with no speech about it.
