> **Principle 45** — Cultivate cognitive flexibility: the capacity to switch between modes

Cognitive flexibility is the ability to adapt thinking to changing demands, switch between tasks or perspectives, and update strategies when the environment changes. It is a core executive function and a strong predictor of well-being and resilience.

**Plain words** Cognitive flexibility: the ability to adapt thinking to changing demands, switch perspectives, and update strategies when the environment shifts. A core executive function. Train it — the rigid mind, however smart, breaks against a moving world.

*For example:* The strategist whose 2020 plan was excellent for 2019 — and the difference between the ones who re-planned in March and the ones who defended the dead plan. Same intelligence; different flexibility. The flexible ones kept the company.
## Psychology parallels

- **[[Cognitive flexibility|Cognitive flexibility as a core executive function]]**, the ability to shift between mental sets and adapt to new rules (Miyake et al., 2000; Diamond, 2013)
- **Cognitive flexibility is associated with better mental health and resilience**, inflexibility (rigidity, perseveration) is associated with depression, anxiety, and OCD (Gillie & Broadbent, 1989; Murray et al., 2018)

## Practice

- Practise deliberately switching: when you notice yourself stuck on one approach, deliberately generate a different one. Play games that require rule-switching. Learn new skills that demand new strategies.
