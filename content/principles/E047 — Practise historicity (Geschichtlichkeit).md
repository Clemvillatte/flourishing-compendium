> **Principle 439** — Practise historicity (Geschichtlichkeit)

Heidegger's historicality (Geschichtlichkeit) is not an interest in history. It is the way Dasein is stretched along between birth and death. "Factical Dasein exists as born; and, as born, it is already dying, in the sense of Being-towards-death" (Being and Time §72, H. 374, trans. Macquarrie and Robinson). Hence "As care, Dasein is the 'between'" (§72).

### Dasein is its past

The past does not trail behind you like baggage: "Dasein 'is' its past in the way of its own Being, which, to put it roughly, 'historizes' out of its future on each occasion" (§6). It has "grown up both into and in a traditional way of interpreting itself", and its own past "is not something which follows along after Dasein, but something which already goes ahead of it" (§6).

### Owning the inheritance

Authentic historicality takes the inheritance on knowingly. "The resoluteness in which Dasein comes back to itself, discloses current factical possibilities of authentic existing, and discloses them in terms of the heritage which that resoluteness, as thrown, takes over" (§74). He ties it to a shared world: "Only in communicating and in struggling does the power of destiny become free" (§74). Fate, for him, is "that powerless superior power, which puts itself in readiness for adversities" (§74).

**Plain words** Your life is not a point in the present with a past behind and a future ahead. You are already moving between where you came from and what you are headed toward, and each shapes the other. The inheritance includes your language, your family and your culture. You do not escape it, but you can find out what you were given and take up some of it on purpose.

*For example:* Imagined example: you notice that you apologise whenever a meal goes wrong, the way your mother always did. You see the habit as inherited, and you choose to keep the generosity and drop the apology.

(Heidegger, Being and Time §§6, 72-74, trans. Macquarrie and Robinson (1962), Internet Archive; opened 2026-10-21. Passages from §§75-77 were not used.)
