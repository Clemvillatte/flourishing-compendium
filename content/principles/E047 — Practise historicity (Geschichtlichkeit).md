> **Principle 439** — Practise historicity (Geschichtlichkeit)

Recognise that your existence is historical — not merely that you live in history, but that historicity is a structure of your being. You inherit a past, you project a future, and you live in a present that is shaped by both. Own your historicity: acknowledge the tradition you have received, the future you project, and the responsibility of living in the present.

(Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §§72-77.)

**Plain words** Your existence is historical — not merely that you live in history, but that historicity is a structure of your being: you inherit a past, project a future, and live in a present shaped by both. Own it: acknowledge the inheritance, then author the projection.

*For example:* The family patterns that arrive like weather — the silence about money, the praise reserved for suffering — someone finally reads the inheritance instead of just transmitting it. The past gets a different vote once it's been seen.
