> **Principle 71** — Treat loneliness in older adults as a public-health issue

Treat loneliness in older adults — the social disconnection of ageing — as a public-health issue. Social isolation in older adults predicts mortality, dementia, and depression (Holt-Lunstad et al., 2010, 2017; Wilson et al., 2007).

**Plain words** Loneliness in older adults is a public-health issue — isolation predicts mortality, dementia, and depression. The treatment is community infrastructure, not just individual effort. Visit your older neighbours; it's not charity, it's medicine.

*For example:* The regular visitor programme that measurably reduces hospital admissions — the visitors aren't bringing medical care, they're bringing the thing the body was missing: company. The prescription was social.
