> **Principle 489** — Practise transcendence

Recognise that human existence is oriented toward transcendence — toward that which exceeds the empirical, the given, the known. Transcendence, for Jaspers, is not a flight from the world but the horizon within which all worldly experience is situated. Do not reduce your existence to what can be measured or observed. Honour the dimension of transcendence.

(Jaspers, 1932/1971, Philosophy, vol. 3; Marcel, 1951b, The Mystery of Being.)

**Plain words** Human existence is oriented toward transcendence — toward what exceeds the empirical, the given, the known. Not a flight from the world but the horizon within which all worldly experience is situated. Don't reduce your existence to what can be measured; the measuring is inside the horizon too.

*For example:* The scientist who knows every mechanism of the sunset — refraction, scattering, wavelengths — and still stops the car to look. The knowledge is real; the orientation toward what exceeds it is realer. Both are the human position.
