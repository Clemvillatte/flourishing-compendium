> **Principle 422** — Practise dwelling (wohnen)

Cultivate the capacity to dwell — to be at home in the world, to belong somewhere, to be rooted. Dwelling is not merely inhabiting a physical space but the way you belong to the world and the world belongs to you. Practise dwelling by building, cultivating, and caring for the places of your life.

(Heidegger, 1951/1971, "Building Dwelling Thinking," in Poetry, Language, Thought; Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §§40, 70-71.)

**Plain words** Dwelling: being at home in the world, belonging somewhere, rooted — not merely inhabiting a space but the way you belong to the world and it to you. Practise dwelling by building, cultivating, caring. Make a place that knows you.

*For example:* The person who's "lived" in the apartment for five years without hanging a picture — versus the one who, in month three, has the shelf, the plant, the worn spot on the sofa. One has an address; the other has a dwelling.
