> **Principle 151** — Keep the mind clear: sobriety

Don't cloud the instrument: intoxicants promise ease and deliver fog. Practice needs a [[Clarity of mind|clear mind]]; guard it.

(Five precepts, fifth; Dhammapada.)

**Plain words** Don't cloud the instrument — intoxicants promise ease and deliver fog. Practice needs a clear mind; guard it. The glass of wine that takes the edge off also takes the edge off the one tool you own.

*For example:* The 'just to relax' nightly drink that slowly becomes the reason relaxation is impossible without it — the fog, financed.
