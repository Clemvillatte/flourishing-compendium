> **Principle 68** — Maintain friendships through presence in misfortune

The true test of friendship is not shared prosperity but shared adversity. The friend who stays when things go wrong is the friend who matters. Be that friend. Do not abandon people when they become inconvenient or costly.

(NE VIII.4, IX.11.)

**Plain words** Good times show who's around. Bad times show who's a friend. Aristotle says the one who stays when things go wrong matters most. Be that person. Don't drop people when they become costly or awkward.

*For example:* Visiting a friend who has had a divorce and doesn't want to talk, and just sitting with her, is the test.
