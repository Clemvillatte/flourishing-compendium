> **Principle 38** — Recognise that habits take time: do not expect immediate automaticity

Habit formation takes time — a median of 66 days, with wide variation (18-254 days). Expecting immediate automaticity leads to discouragement. Patience is part of habit formation.

**Plain words** Habits take about two months on average — sometimes three weeks, sometimes eight months — and people quit at day 9 assuming failure. Automaticity is a slow-ripening fruit. Judge the habit at 66 days, not 6.

*For example:* The "I've been doing this for a week and it's still hard" despair — of course it's still hard; it's day 7 of 66. The struggle isn't the verdict; it's the schedule.

## Practice

- Be patient with habit formation: expect weeks to months, not days. Do not give up when automaticity does not arrive immediately. Track progress, not perfection.
