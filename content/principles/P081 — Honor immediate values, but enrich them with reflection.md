> **Principle 81** — Honor immediate values, but enrich them with reflection

The immediately enjoyed has its own validity: and it grows when illuminated by thought about its conditions and consequences. Don't choose between enjoyment and reflection; combine them.

(Dewey, Experience and Nature, 1925.)

**Plain words** The immediately enjoyed has its own validity — and it grows when illuminated by thought about its conditions and consequences. Don't choose between enjoyment and reflection; combine them. The best meals are both delicious and understood to be.

*For example:* The beach vacation enjoyed purely, versus enjoyed with a little knowledge of the tides, the birds, the history — same beach, richer experience. Reflection is seasoning.
