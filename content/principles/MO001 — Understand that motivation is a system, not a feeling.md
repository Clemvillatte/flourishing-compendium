> **Principle 1** — Understand that motivation is a system, not a feeling

Ryan and Deci define motivation as a matter of energy and direction rather than a feeling:

> "Motivation concerns energy, direction, persistence and equifinality—all aspects of activation and intention." (Ryan & Deci, 2000, p. 69)

Vallerand's hierarchical model adds the second part of the principle's claim: motivation operates at three levels of generality, from a global orientation at the personality level down to the motivation toward a specific activity at a specific moment, with a contextual level in between covering "broad life contexts such as interpersonal relationships, education, work, religion, and others" (Guay, Mageau & Vallerand, 2003). This is why you can be motivated in one domain and unmotivated in another: the domains are distinct motivational systems rather than a single tank. The compendium's framing: treat motivation as a system to be diagnosed (which domain, which goal, which need) rather than a feeling you either have or lack.

**Plain words** Motivation is not one dial in your head. It is energy aimed at something, and the aim is built from needs, goals and context, each with its own level. A person can be fully driven at work and flat at the gym without contradiction. When you feel unmotivated, the useful question is not "how do I get motivated" but "which part of the system is missing".

*For example (imagined):* A student who cannot start studying checks the parts: no goal that matters to her in the course, no fixed time slot in the week, and a phone on the desk. She sets one concrete goal, moves study to a fixed morning hour and the phone to another room.

## Psychology parallels

- **Motivation as a multi-component system**, motivation involves needs, cognitions, emotions, and habits (Ryan & Deci, 2017; Locke & Latham, 2002)
- **Motivation is domain-specific**, motivation for work, health, and relationships are distinct (Vallerand, 1997; Vallerand et al., 2003)

## Practice

- Do not treat "I am unmotivated" as a global state. Diagnose: which domain, which goal, which need is unmet? Motivation is specific.
