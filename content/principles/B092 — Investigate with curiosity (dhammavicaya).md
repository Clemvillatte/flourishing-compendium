> **Principle 92** — Investigate with curiosity (dhammavicaya)

Curiosity is a factor of awakening: meet experience with the question "what is this?" rather than the verdict "this is bad." Investigation is warm, not clinical.

(SN 46; seven factors.)

**Plain words** Curiosity is a factor of awakening — meet experience with 'what is this?' rather than the verdict 'this is bad.' Investigation is warm, not clinical. The lab coat is optional; the genuine question is not.

*For example:* The insomnia approached with 'huh, what's this?' instead of 'not again' — one of these has a chance of being interesting.
