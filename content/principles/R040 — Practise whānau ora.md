> **Principle 302** — Practise whānau ora

Prioritise the wellbeing of your whole family, not just individuals within it. Whānau ora is a holistic approach to family health that recognises the family as a living system. When one member suffers, the whole family is affected. When the family is well, each member flourishes.

(Māori health framework; Durie, 1994, Whaiora; Taskforce on Whānau-Centred Initiatives, 2010.)

**Plain words** Whānau ora: the family is the patient. Wellbeing isn't an individual scorecard — when one member suffers, the whole system feels it, and the treatment has to reach the system. Ask "how is the family?" not only "how are you?"

*For example:* The clinic that stops treating the asthmatic child and starts treating the house — the mould, the landlord, the stress — and the child's chart improves faster than any inhaler alone.
