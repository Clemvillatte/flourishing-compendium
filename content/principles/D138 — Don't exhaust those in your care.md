> **Principle 138** — Don't exhaust those in your care

People starve because rulers eat too much tax; teams burn out when leaders take too much. Check what you extract from others: and from yourself.

(DDJ 75.)

**Plain words** Check what you extract from others — and from yourself. Extraction is a habit that reads as normal right up until everyone's empty.

*For example:* The manager who keeps adding 'one more quick thing' to the strongest employee — until the strongest employee is a resignation letter.
