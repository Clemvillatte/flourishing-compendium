> **Principle 297** — Practise pono

Be truthful and authentic. Pono means truth, integrity, and genuineness. Do not be false in your dealings with others. Speak truthfully, act authentically, and be genuine in your commitments.

(Māori tikanga; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori; Durie, 1994, Whaiora.)

**Plain words** Pono: truthfulness and authenticity — no falseness in your dealings, speech that matches reality, commitments that match conduct. Not radical candour as a weapon; just the refusal to be a crooked instrument.

*For example:* The manager who says "I don't know, and here's what we'll do to find out" — and whose team, after a year, has stopped double-checking everything they're told.
