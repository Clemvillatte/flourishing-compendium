> **Principle 119** — Give flow clear goals and immediate feedback

Absorption requires knowing what you are doing and how it is going, moment to moment. Build goals and feedback into any activity you want to love.

(Csikszentmihalyi, Flow, 1990.)

**Plain words** Absorption requires knowing what you're doing and how it's going, moment to moment. Build goals and feedback into any activity you want to love. Vague goals plus no feedback equals watching the clock; clear goals plus instant feedback equals forgetting the clock exists.

*For example:* The runner with no watch versus the one with splits — same run, but one of them is playing a game with herself and one is just enduring. The game is the goal plus the feedback.
