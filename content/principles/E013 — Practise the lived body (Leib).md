> **Principle 405** — Practise the lived body (Leib)

Recognise the distinction between your body as a physical object (Körper) and your body as you live it (Leib). The lived body is not a thing you have but the subject you are. It is the medium through which you perceive, act, and exist. Do not treat your body as a mere instrument. Live it as the ground of your being.

(Merleau-Ponty, 1945/2012, Phenomenology of Perception, Parts I and II; Husserl, 1952/1989, Ideas II, §§35-62.)

**Plain words** Distinguish the body as physical object (Körper) from the body as lived (Leib) — the body you are, not the body you have. The lived body is the medium through which you perceive and act. Don't treat it as a machine you're piloting.

*For example:* The same knee injury: as Körper it's a torn meniscus with a protocol; as Leib it's "I can no longer trust the stairs" — the whole world reorganising around it. The lived body is the one that needs the physio's attention too.
