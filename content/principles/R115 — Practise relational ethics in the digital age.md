> **Principle 377** — Practise relational ethics in the digital age

Recognise that digital technologies transform but do not eliminate relational obligations. The ethics of care, recognition, and dialogue apply online as well as offline. Do not treat digital interactions as exempt from moral responsibility. Practise the same care, respect, and presence in your digital relationships as in your face-to-face ones.

(Vallor, 2016, Technology and the Virtues; Floridi, 2013, The Ethics of Information.)

**Plain words** Digital life doesn't exempt you from relational ethics — care, recognition, and presence apply online too. The screen is not a moral vacuum. Bring the same presence to your digital relationships that you'd bring face to face.

*For example:* The person who notices a friend's posts going dark and lonely — and doesn't "send thoughts," but calls, actually calls, because that's what presence means when it's not performative.
