> **Principle 136** — Recognize your blindness: others live in worlds you cannot see

Each of us is blind to the inner meaning of others' lives and ventures: what is dead routine to you is life's center to another. Hold your judgments of others' choices with this humility.

(James, "On a Certain Blindness in Human Beings," 1899.)

**Plain words** The commute you pity carries someone's entire inner world. What is dead routine to you is life's center to another — hold your judgments of others' choices with that humility.

*For example:* The factory job you'd find soul-crushing — the guy there for thirty years, provider and patriarch, master of the line. Your pity says more about your blindness than his life.
