> **Principle 230** — Handle poverty with dignity

Seneca's Letter 17 argues that poverty need not block philosophy: "Riches have shut off many a man from the attainment of wisdom; poverty is unburdened and free from care", and "Poverty is contented with fulfilling pressing needs." He also tells Lucilius to "be a philosopher now, whether you have anything or not" ([Seneca, Letter 17, Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Moral_letters_to_Lucilius/Letter_17)).

The seed says Seneca "practised voluntary poverty". What Letter 18 actually prescribes is a rehearsal: "Set aside a certain number of days, during which you shall be content with the scantiest and cheapest fare, with coarse and rough dress, saying to yourself the while: 'Is this the condition that I feared?'" He asks for "three or four days at a time, sometimes for more, so that it may be a test of yourself instead of a mere hobby" and notes that many poor people live this way without choosing it ([Seneca, Letter 18, Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Moral_letters_to_Lucilius/Letter_18)).

Psychology adds a caution. Mani and colleagues found that financial worries reduced cognitive performance among poor participants, and that farmers performed worse before harvest than after ([Mani et al. 2013, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23990553/?dopt=Abstract)). A reanalysis disputed the statistical basis of the lab experiments ([Wicherts and Scholten, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24311665/)).

(Seneca, Letters 17, 18, 87; Epictetus, Disc. I.9, I.25, III.7, III.8; Ench. 15; Med. III.6, VI.13, IX.6; Musonius Rufus, Lectures.)

### Scope

I did not open the other texts in the locator, so I cannot confirm the "dispreferred indifferent" wording or Musonius's teaching. Chosen simplicity is not involuntary poverty.

**Plain words**
Seneca recommends rehearsing a simple life for a few days. Lasting poverty is different, and research suggests it can strain thinking.

**For example**
Follow Letter 18 for three days: plain food and plain clothes, and each day ask whether this is the condition you feared.
