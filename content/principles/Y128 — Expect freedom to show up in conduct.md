> **Principle 128** — Expect freedom to show up in conduct

The tradition's mature position, most developed in the later Vedanta line: liberation while living (jivanmukti), visible not in withdrawal or in claims but in conduct: reduced reactivity, harmless speech, levelness under gain and loss, service without lien. The test offered is behavioral, which makes it checkable. Practically, audit your freedom claims against the last month's conduct, and let conduct be the measure.

(The jivanmukti doctrine, Vidyaranya and the Yoga Vasistha line, labeled as the later tradition's development.)

**Plain words** Liberation while living — visible not in withdrawal or claims but in conduct: reduced reactivity, harmless speech, levelness under gain and loss, service without lien. The test of any realization is the Tuesday after. Expect freedom to show up in conduct.

*For example:* The teacher who claims the big realization — and the one whose realization shows up as the way they handle the cancelled flight. The tradition's scoreboard only prints conduct.
