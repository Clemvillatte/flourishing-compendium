> **Principle 64** — Act with the timing

Heaven and earth advance and retreat, expand and contract, in their seasons. Read the moment: market, mood, season, readiness — and move when the door is open, not when you are merely ready.

(Yinfu Jing; DDJ 8.)

**Plain words** Heaven and earth advance and retreat in their seasons. Read the moment — market, mood, readiness — and move when the door is open, not merely when you're ready. Being ready at the wrong time is a form of not being ready.

*For example:* The same raise request lands completely differently on a record quarter versus a layoff week — the words matter less than the door.
