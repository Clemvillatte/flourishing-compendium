> **Principle 371** — Practise social justice

Work for a society in which every person has access to the conditions of flourishing: education, healthcare, meaningful work, political participation, and cultural recognition. Do not confine your moral concern to your own circle. Recognise that structural injustice — poverty, discrimination, exclusion — is a moral problem that demands your response.

(Rawls, 1971, A Theory of Justice; Nussbaum, 2000, Women and Human Development; Sen, 1999, Development as Freedom.)

**Plain words** Work for a society where everyone actually has the conditions of flourishing — education, healthcare, meaningful work, a say — and take structural injustice (poverty, discrimination, exclusion) as a moral problem that lands on you, not just "the system." Your circle isn't the border of moral concern.

*For example:* Someone whose charity ends at their postcode — and joins the wider campaign for the housing policy, discovering the food bank needs both the donated cans and the changed law.
