> **Principle 307** — Practise respect for matai

Honour the chiefs and elders who hold responsibility for the wellbeing of the community. In Samoan culture, the matai system is a structure of governance and care. Respect the wisdom and responsibility of those who lead. More broadly, respect those in your community who carry responsibility for the collective.

(Samoan philosophy; Tuhiwai Smith, 1999, Decolonizing Methodologies.)

**Plain words** Honour those who carry responsibility for the collective — the matai, the elders, the ones whose job is the wellbeing of the group. Respect isn't worship of rank; it's recognising that someone is holding weight you're not holding.

*For example:* The meeting where the newest member speaks last, listens to the chiefs first — and learns more in one hour about how this community decides things than in a year of documents.
