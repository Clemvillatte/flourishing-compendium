> **Principle 93** — Take proper pleasure in the right things

Pleasure is good when it completes a worthy activity and bad when it completes a base one. The same pleasure can be good for one person and bad for another, depending on the activity it completes. Be mindful of what your pleasures are completing.

(NE X.5.)

**Plain words** A pleasure is good when it completes something worth doing, and bad when it completes something base. The same pleasure can be fine for one person and not for another, depending on what it's attached to. Check what yours is finishing.

*For example:* Laughing with friends after a hard rehearsal is fine. Laughing at someone's expense to feel big isn't.
