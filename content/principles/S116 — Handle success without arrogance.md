> **Principle 238** — Handle success without arrogance

Marcus describes how his adoptive father handled good fortune:

> "And the things which conduce in any way to the commodity of life, and of which fortune gives an abundant supply, he used without arrogance and without excusing himself; so that when he had them, he enjoyed them without affectation, and when he had them not, he did not want them."

(Med. I.16, George Long translation, [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/15877/pg15877.txt).)

The compendium applies the portrait to success: the man is praised for using fortune's gifts without arrogance and for not missing them when he did not have them. The exercise after a win is to enjoy it without display, and to notice what you begin calling your due.

**Plain words** Use what came in, thank whoever helped, and go back to the plan. Watch whether success changes how you treat other people.

*For example:* Imagined: after a promotion, someone thanks the team and keeps their morning routine unchanged.
