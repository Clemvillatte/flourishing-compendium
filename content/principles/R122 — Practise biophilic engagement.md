> **Principle 384** — Practise biophilic engagement

Biophilia is a hypothesis, not a settled fact. An APA abstract of an ecopsychology chapter describes it as "an inherited tendency to affiliate with other life forms", first proposed by E. O. Wilson. It adds that "biophilia has attracted its share of critics, and the hypothesis has effectively been ignored in most scientific circles" ([APA PsycNET record](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2012-22191-001)). Peter Kahn's book asks the open questions directly: "Do children have a deep connection to the natural world that gets severed by modern society? Or do such connections emerge, if at all, later in life, with increased cognitive and moral maturity?" ([MIT Press, The Human Relationship with Nature](https://direct.mit.edu/books/monograph/3903/The-Human-Relationship-with-NatureDevelopment-and)).

The better-tested part is the link between feeling connected to nature and wellbeing. A meta-analysis of 30 samples (n = 8523) found "a small but significant effect size (r = 0.19)", with the strongest link for vitality (r = 0.24). The authors note that it used correlations "to examine the strength of the relationship but not necessarily if one variable causes the other" ([Capaldi, Dopko and Zelenski 2014](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00976/full)).

(Wilson, 1984, Biophilia; Kahn, 1999, The Human Relationship with Nature.)

### What this supports

Time with living things is worth trying, and the evidence for a small positive association is consistent. It does not show that nature contact causes happiness, or that an "instinct" starves without it. I did not open Wilson's Biophilia.

**Plain words**
Spend regular time among living things and pay attention to them. Treat it as an experiment about your own mood and energy, not as a duty owed to an instinct.

**For example**
Walk a different route for two weeks, past a park or tree line. Each evening rate your energy from 1 to 10 and note one living thing you saw. Compare the two weeks with a fortnight on your usual route.
