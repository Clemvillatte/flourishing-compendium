> **Principle 384** — Practise biophilic engagement

Cultivate an affinity for life and the living world. Biophilia, as E. O. Wilson named it, is the innate human tendency to seek connections with nature and other forms of life. Do not let your life become entirely artificial. Spend time in nature, observe living things, and let your biophilic instinct express itself.

(Wilson, 1984, Biophilia; Kahn, 1999, The Human Relationship with Nature.)

**Plain words** Biophilia: the innate pull toward life and the living world. Don't let your life go fully artificial — spend time in nature, observe living things, feed the instinct. It's not a hobby; it's a hunger with your name on it.

*For example:* The office worker who can't name a single plant on their commute — takes the long route home through the park, and within a month knows the magpies individually.
