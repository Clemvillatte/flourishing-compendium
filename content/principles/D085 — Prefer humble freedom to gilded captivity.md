> **Principle 85** — Prefer humble freedom to gilded captivity

The tortoise drags its tail in the mud rather than being venerated in a temple; the marsh pheasant takes ten steps for one peck rather than live in a cage. Choose the muddy, free option.

(Zhuangzi 17, 20.)

**Plain words** Choose the muddy, free option — gilded captivity is still captivity. The tortoise drags its tail in the mud rather than being venerated in a temple.

*For example:* The corporate job with the title and the golden handcuffs versus the smaller role where you can look at yourself in the mirror — the pheasant knows.
