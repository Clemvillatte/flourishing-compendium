> **Principle 41** — Set ends-in-view, and revise them

Goals are not final destinations but ends-in-view: provisional aims that guide present action and are themselves revised as action proceeds. Hold goals firmly enough to act, loosely enough to learn.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922.)

**Plain words** Goals aren't final destinations but ends-in-view — provisional aims that guide present action and are themselves revised as action proceeds. Hold goals firmly enough to act, loosely enough to learn. The goal is a rudder, not an anchor.

*For example:* The five-year plan revised in year two — not a failure of planning, but planning working exactly as designed: producing information.
