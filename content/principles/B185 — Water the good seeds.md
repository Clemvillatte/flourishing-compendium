> **Principle 185** — Water the good seeds

In yourself and others, deliberately water the seeds you want to grow: attention, appreciation, encouragement to the good — and refrain from watering the seeds of anger and fear.

(Thích Nhất Hạnh, Understanding Our Mind.)

**Plain words** In yourself and others, deliberately water the seeds you want to grow — attention, appreciation, encouragement to the good — and refrain from watering the seeds of anger and fear. Attention is irrigation; you're always watering something.

*For example:* The team meeting that opens with what went right — the whole room's seeds lean toward the light for the next hour.

## Psychology parallels

- **Positive attention and reinforcement**, behavior grows with attention (Skinner; Gottman's culture of appreciation)
- **[[Fredrickson's positivity portfolios|Fredrickson's positivity portfolios]]** (2013)

## Philosophy parallels

- **What you feed grows** (Laozi, Daodejing 27)
