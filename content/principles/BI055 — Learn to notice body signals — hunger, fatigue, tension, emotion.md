> **Principle 55** — Learn to notice body signals: hunger, fatigue, tension, emotion

The body signals its needs: hunger, fatigue, tension, emotion. Noticing these signals — and responding to them — is a foundational self-care skill. Ignoring them (pushing through hunger, fatigue, tension) accumulates problems.

**Plain words** The body sends signals — hunger, fatigue, tension, emotion — and most people have trained themselves to override them. Noticing and responding is a foundational skill, maybe THE foundational skill. The body's memos deserve replies.

*For example:* "I'll eat after this" — said at 2 p.m., with the irritability at 4 p.m. that follows. The hangry was a memo, marked urgent, unopened for hours. Open your mail.
## Psychology parallels

- **Interoceptive signals and self-care**, noticing body signals (hunger, fatigue, tension) enables responsive self-care (Craig, 2009; Barrett et al., 2019)
- **Body signals as information**, body signals are information about needs (Craig, 2009; Khalsa et al., 2018)

## Practice

- Notice body signals: hunger, fatigue, tension, emotion. Ask: "what is my body telling me?" Respond (eat, rest, stretch, regulate). Do not ignore or override body signals.
