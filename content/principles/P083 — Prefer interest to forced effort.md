> **Principle 83** — Prefer interest to forced effort

Effort that fights against interest is wasted: genuine interest is the strongest motive. Build your life around what genuinely interests you: and grow new interests deliberately.

(Dewey, Democracy and Education, 1916; James, Talks to Teachers, 1899.)

**Plain words** Effort that fights against interest is wasted — genuine interest is the strongest motive. Build your life around what genuinely interests you, and grow new interests deliberately. The grind culture is paying full price for a discount fuel.

*For example:* The coder who loses weekends to side projects but can't last an hour on tutorials — the interest was the curriculum all along. Build around it.
