> **Principle 36** — Cultivate theoretical wisdom (sophia)

Sophia is the combination of nous (grasp of first principles) and episteme (demonstrative knowledge). It is the highest intellectual virtue: the capacity to understand the most fundamental truths about reality. Make space for deep, sustained reflection on the largest questions.

(NE VI.6-7, VI.12.)

**Plain words** Sophia is the top of Aristotle's intellectual ladder: grasping first principles and the reasoning built on them, about the biggest questions. It needs room. So set aside real time, uninterrupted, for the large questions you normally brush off.

*For example:* A long walk with no podcast, thinking about what the point of your work is, counts. So does an afternoon with one hard book.
