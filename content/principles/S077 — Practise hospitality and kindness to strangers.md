> **Principle 199** — Practise hospitality and kindness to strangers

The Stoic is a citizen of the world. Every human is a fellow citizen. Treat strangers as you would treat neighbours. Kindness costs little and returns much.

(Med. IV.29, VI.14, VIII.5; Disc. III.24; Seneca, Letters 3, 9, 95; Musonius Rufus, Lectures.)

**Plain words** A Stoic is a citizen of the world, so every human is a fellow citizen. Treat strangers as neighbours. Kindness costs little and returns much.

*For example:* Giving directions to a lost tourist, walking her to the corner.
