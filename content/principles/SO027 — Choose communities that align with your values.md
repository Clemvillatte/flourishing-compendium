> **Principle 27** — Choose communities that align with your values

Choose communities that align with your values, not just any community. Communities are not neutral; they shape your norms, your aspirations, and your behavior. The adaptive stance is to select and shape your communities deliberately, as you select your environment.

**Plain words** Communities aren't neutral — they set your norms, aspirations, and defaults. Choose them like you'd choose a co-author of your life, because that's what they are. Drift is a choice too.

*For example:* The gym friend group, the startup crowd, the church committee — each quietly rewires what "normal" means within a year. Nobody announces it. The community edits you.

## Practice

- Audit your communities for value alignment (see principle 26). Where misaligned, either work to change the community or leave it. In community selection, treat the community's norms as you would treat your own habits.
