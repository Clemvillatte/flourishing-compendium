> **Principle 502** — Practise life affirmation

Nietzsche's yes to life is not optimism. In The Gay Science he sets it out as a New Year's resolution:

> "I want more and more to perceive the necessary characters in things as the beautiful:—I shall thus be one of those who beautify things. Amor fati: let that henceforth be my love! I do not want to wage war with the ugly. I do not want to accuse, I do not want even to accuse the accusers."

He ends the same passage: "I wish to be at any time hereafter only a yea-sayer!" (GS §276).

### Including the hard parts

The yes covers pain. In Twilight of the Idols he describes the Dionysian as "the saying of Yea to life, including even its most strange and most terrible problems, the will to life rejoicing over its own inexhaustibleness" (TI, "What I Owe to the Ancients" §5). The Gay Science puts it more quietly: "No! Life has not deceived me! On the contrary, from year to year I find it richer, more desirable and more mysterious" (GS §324).

Ecce Homo goes further. His "formula for greatness in man" is that "a man wishes nothing to be different, either in front of him or behind him, or for all eternity", and the necessary must be "loved" (EH, "Why I Am So Clever" §10). The [[Will to power|the will to power]] belongs in the same neighbourhood of Nietzsche's thought, though the passages above do not state how the two connect.

(Nietzsche, The Joyful Wisdom §276, §324, trans. Common; Twilight of the Idols, "What I Owe to the Ancients" §5, trans. Ludovici; Ecce Homo, "Why I Am So Clever" §10, trans. Cohn; all as hosted by Project Gutenberg.)

**Plain words** Saying yes to life does not mean calling life good or feeling cheerful. Nietzsche's yes includes the things you cannot change and would rather were different. Practise it by dropping the war on what is ugly or unfair, by not spending the day accusing, and by looking for what, in the unavoidable, you can still make beautiful.

For example: Imagined: after a diagnosis you did not want, you stop asking who or what is to blame, and ask what in the days left you can still do well. That is closer to his resolution than forced positivity is.
