> **Principle 53** — Play seriously

Play is not the opposite of work; it is experimental action freed from immediate stakes: the safest laboratory for variation. Protect play in adult life.

(Dewey, Democracy and Education, 1916; Schiller on the play drive.)

**Plain words** Play is not the opposite of work — it's experimental action freed from immediate stakes, the safest laboratory for variation. Protect play in adult life. The joke that lands, the sketch that fails, the riff that goes nowhere: that's R&D for the soul.

*For example:* The engineers' Friday side project that becomes the company's flagship feature — nobody was brave enough to propose it; somebody was free enough to play with it.
