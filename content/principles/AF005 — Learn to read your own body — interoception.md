> **Principle 5** — Learn to read your own body: interoception

The ability to notice and accurately interpret internal bodily signals (heart rate, breath, gut, muscle tone) is the foundation of affective awareness. Low interoceptive accuracy is associated with alexithymia and poorer emotional regulation. This is a trainable skill.

**Plain words** Interoception: the ability to notice and accurately read internal signals — heart rate, breath, gut, muscle tone. It's the foundation of affective awareness. Low interoceptive accuracy goes with alexithymia and poorer regulation. Learn to read your own body like an instrument panel.

*For example:* The person who never feels "stressed" — just gets phantom chest tightness and eats everything after 9 p.m. — and the training that reconnects them to the panel: the body was filing the stress reports all along, in a language nobody had taught them.
## Psychology parallels

- **Interoception and emotion are linked**, interoceptive accuracy correlates with emotional awareness and regulation (Craig, 2009; Barrett et al., 2019)
- **Body scan and mindfulness improve interoception**, systematic training improves accuracy and reduces distress (Khalsa et al., 2018)

## Practice

- Spend 2–5 minutes a day scanning the body — heart, breath, gut, shoulders, jaw — without judging. Note the raw signal before the interpretation.
