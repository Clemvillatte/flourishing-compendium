> **Principle 68** — Yield in conflict to remain whole

The principle joins two chapters. Chapter 22 opens with reversals: the partial becomes complete; the crooked, straight; the empty, full; the worn out, new. The sage's one possession is humility, and the chapter ends on the strategic point that because he does not strive, no one can strive with him ([Legge, DDJ 22](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt)). Chapter 68 carries the same logic into a fight: in Legge's verse the warrior who has skill assumes no martial port, and the fighter of most good will makes no resort to rage ([Legge, DDJ 68](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt)).

> It is because he is thus free from striving that therefore no one in the world is able to strive with him. (DDJ 22, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt))

> He who in (Tao's) wars has skill
> Assumes no martial port;
> He who fights with most good will
> To rage makes no resort. (DDJ 68, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt))

The reason the chapters give is strategic rather than moral. The refrain of chapter 68 is that the good fighter "ne'er contends", and "therein is his might" ([Legge, DDJ 68](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt)): rage and display hand the opponent something to engage, while composure keeps the fighter and his options whole. The practice kernels attached to this principle train that composure for ordinary arguments.

(DDJ 22, 68.)

**Plain words**
Two people collide in a doorway. The one who plants their feet and shoves has decided a doorway is worth a bruise; the one who steps aside has lost three seconds and kept everything else. That is the trade the principle points at: yielding spends something small and visible, rigidity risks something large. Before a fight, ask what the prize actually is and whether it is priced in temper.

**For example**
A colleague misstates your work in a meeting. The soft move is to correct the record in writing afterwards; correcting him in front of the room wins the moment and spends the working relationship.
