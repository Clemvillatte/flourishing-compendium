> **Principle 68** — Yield in conflict to remain whole

Yield and remain whole; bend and remain straight. The good warrior is not angry, the good fighter not riled: composure, not aggression, wins.

(DDJ 22, 68.)

**Plain words** Composure, not aggression, wins. Yielding here isn't losing — it's refusing to spend yourself on a collision the outcome doesn't need.

*For example:* The martial artist who steps aside uses the attacker's force — the wall that stands rigid is the one that cracks.
