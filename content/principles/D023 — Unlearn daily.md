> **Principle 23** — Unlearn daily

Pursue the student of less: each day drop an opinion, a fear, a should. Expertise in living is measured in what you can let go of.

(DDJ 20, 48.)

**Plain words** Be a student of less: each day, drop an opinion, a fear, a should. Expertise in living is measured in what you can let go of, not what you accumulate. Unlearning is the daily practice that keeps the mind light.

*For example:* Realizing you've outgrown a hot take from five years ago — and actually letting it go instead of defending it — is a workout.
