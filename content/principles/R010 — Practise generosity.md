> **Principle 272** — Practise generosity

Give freely of what you have. In African traditions, wealth is measured not by what you accumulate but by what you distribute. The person who has much and shares is honoured; the person who has much and hoards is shamed.

(Ubuntu philosophy; Mbiti, African Religions and Philosophy; Tutu, No Future Without Forgiveness.)

**Plain words** Wealth is measured by distribution, not accumulation: the one who has much and shares is honoured; the one who has much and hoards is the embarrassment. Give freely of what you have — status is the receipt.

*For example:* The person who quietly pays the deposit for a cousin's course, funds the church roof, covers a colleague's shortfall — and is the first name everyone says when they describe who holds this community together.
