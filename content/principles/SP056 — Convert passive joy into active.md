> **Principle 56** — Convert passive joy into active

There is a joy that happens to you (the promotion, the praise, the luck) and a joy you produce (understanding, competence, creation), and Part V's program is the systematic conversion of the first kind into the second. The passive joys decay by hedonic law; the active ones compound, because they increase the power that produces them. Practically, for every pleasure you receive, build one pleasure you generate, and over a season shift the center of gravity from the first column to the second.

(Ethics V, P4, with Part III's active-passive distinction.)

**Plain words** Two joys: the joy that happens to you (the promotion, the praise, the luck) and the joy you produce (understanding, competence, creation). Part V's program is the systematic conversion of the first kind into the second — passive joys decay by habituation; active ones don't.

*For example:* The person whose happiness is fully outsourced to wins and compliments — needs ever bigger doses — versus the one with a joy factory installed: they can make the understanding, the meal, the repair. Same day, one of them is weather-dependent.
