> **Principle 70** — Understand the asymmetry of benefaction

Aristotle notes that the benefactor seems to love the beneficiary more than the beneficiary loves the benefactor. This is because the benefactor has invested something of themselves in the relationship. Do not keep a ledger of what you have given; give freely and let go.

(NE IX.7.)

**Plain words** The giver tends to care more about the receiver than the reverse, because they've put something of themselves in. Aristotle notes it. So don't keep a ledger of what you've given. Give freely and let it go.

*For example:* A parent who drove their kid to training for years doesn't hold it as a debt. If they start listing it, the giving has gone sour.
