> **Principle 70** — Treat climate change as a sociocultural stressor

Treat climate change — and its social effects (displacement, conflict, economic disruption) — as a sociocultural stressor. The evidence includes climate anxiety (Hickman et al., 2021), displacement (McLeman and Smit, 2006), and the mental-health effects of extreme weather (Berry et al., 2010).

**Plain words** Climate change is a sociocultural stressor — displacement, conflict, anxiety — with mental-health effects already measurable. Not a future problem; a current exposure. The coping is partly collective, which is unusual: this one is processed better together.

*For example:* The young people carrying measurable eco-anxiety — the distress is not pathology; it's an accurate response to real data. What helps is action in company. The anxiety is the alarm working.
