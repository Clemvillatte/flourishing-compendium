> **Principle 133** — Humanize institutions

Institutions: workplaces, schools, bureaucracies — exist for the growth of the people in them, not the reverse. Where you have influence, bend institutions toward human scale and human ends.

(Dewey, The Public and Its Problems, 1927; Addams, Twenty Years at Hull-House, 1910.)

**Plain words** Institutions — workplaces, schools, bureaucracies — exist for the growth of the people in them, not the reverse. Where you have influence, bend institutions toward human scale and human ends. The org chart is supposed to serve the humans; somewhere along the way the humans started serving the chart.

*For example:* The manager who kills the useless weekly report — nobody reads it, everyone writes it. One deletion, forty hours a month returned to actual work. That's an institution bending back.
