> **Principle 139** — Live the question ("what is this?")

Keep an alive question at the center of practice: not an answer to defend, a question to live. The question does the work.

(Zen koan practice; "what is this?" as continuous inquiry.)

**Plain words** Keep an alive question at the center of practice — not an answer to defend, a question to live. The question does the work. 'What is this?' carried honestly through a week will out-perform a hundred settled opinions.

*For example:* The scientist whose best years came from loving the question — the colleagues who needed answers retired early.
