> **Principle 196** — Forgive those who wrong you

The Stoic recognises that no one does wrong on purpose. Wrongdoing is the product of ignorance about what is truly good. When someone wrongs you, see the ignorance behind the act. Do not excuse the wrong, but do not personalise it either.

(Med. II.1, II.13, IV.3, VII.22, VII.63, IX.4, IX.11, XI.18; Disc. I.18.)

**Plain words** No one does wrong on purpose, says the Stoic. Wrongdoing comes from ignorance about what's truly good. When someone wrongs you, see the ignorance behind it. Don't excuse the wrong, but don't take it personally.

*For example:* A friend lets you down, and you ask what they believe is important that led to it.
