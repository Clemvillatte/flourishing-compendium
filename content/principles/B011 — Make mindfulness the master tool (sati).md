> **Principle 11** — Make mindfulness the master tool (sati)

Knowing what is happening while it happens: in body, feeling, mind — is the faculty that powers every other principle. Develop it first and keep developing it.

(Satipatthana Sutta, MN 10.)

**Plain words** Knowing what is happening while it happens — in body, feeling, mind — is the faculty that powers every other principle. Develop it first and keep developing it. Mindfulness isn't incense and pillows; it's the difference between driving and being driven.

*For example:* Noticing you're clenching your jaw during the meeting — that tiny observation is the whole technology.
