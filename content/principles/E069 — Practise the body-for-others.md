> **Principle 461** — Practise the body-for-others

Recognise that your body exists not only as you live it (body-subject) but as it appears to others (body-object). The experience of being seen, judged, or evaluated through your body is not a deviation but a fundamental dimension of embodiment. Do not flee from this dimension; engage with it authentically.

(Sartre, 1943/2003, Being and Nothingness, Part III, ch. 2; Merleau-Ponty, 1945/2012, Phenomenology of Perception, Part II, ch. 4; de Beauvoir, 1949/2010, The Second Sex, Introduction.)

**Plain words** Your body exists not only as you live it (body-subject) but as it appears to others (body-object) — being seen, judged, evaluated through the body is not a deviation but a fundamental dimension of embodiment. Don't flee this dimension; don't be ruled by it.

*For example:* The teenager who experiences their body as pure object-for-others for five years — and the maturing task is the integration: this body as lived (the dancing, the appetite, the strength) AND as seen. The exile to either pole is the suffering.
