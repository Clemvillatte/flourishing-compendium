> **Principle 24** — Practice right action: non-harm

Refrain from taking life, from taking what is not given, and from sexual misconduct. Reduce the footprint of your actions on living beings.

(Dhammapada 124-125; AN 10.176.)

**Plain words** Refrain from taking life, from taking what is not given, and from sexual misconduct — reduce the footprint of your actions on living beings. Non-harm isn't a rule from outside; it's the observation that harm ripples back to the doer first.

*For example:* The person who decides the office gossip is off their menu — nothing dramatic, just a smaller footprint and cleaner evenings.
