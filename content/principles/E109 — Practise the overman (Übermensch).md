> **Principle 501** — Practise the overman (Übermensch)

Recognise the figure of the overman — not as a biological type but as the possibility of self-transcendence that is open to every human being. The overman is the one who has overcome the values of the herd, who has created their own values, who affirms life unconditionally. Do not seek to be the overman; seek to overcome yourself.

(Nietzsche, 1883/1961, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "Zarathustra's Prologue"; Nietzsche, 1882/1974, The Gay Science, §335.)

**Plain words** The overman (Übermensch): not a biological type but a possibility open to everyone — the one who has overcome the herd's values, created their own, and affirms life unconditionally. The figure is a direction, not a person. Face the direction.

*For example:* Anyone who's quietly left the default settings — the inherited opinions, the borrowed ambitions, the fear of the neighbours' judgment — and lives by values they can defend in their own words. The cape and the superman imagery were always the least interesting part.
