> **Principle 501** — Practise the overman (Übermensch)

Zarathustra announces the figure to a crowd that came to see a rope-dancer, and the announcement is earthbound:

> "The Superman is the meaning of the earth. Let your will say: The Superman SHALL BE the meaning of the earth!" (Nietzsche, 1883/1910, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "Zarathustra's Prologue" §3, trans. Thomas Common.)

He ties the figure to staying faithful to the earth: "I conjure you, my brethren, REMAIN TRUE TO THE EARTH, and believe not those who speak unto you of superearthly hopes!" The instruction not to seek to be the overman but to overcome yourself comes from the image the Prologue gives next: man is the crossing, not the arrival.

> "Man is a rope stretched between the animal and the Superman—a rope over an abyss." "What is great in man is that he is a bridge and not a goal: what is lovable in man is that he is an OVER-GOING and a DOWN-GOING." (Nietzsche, 1883/1910, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "Zarathustra's Prologue" §4, trans. Thomas Common.)

The overman names the direction of the crossing, not a person or a biological type. What is asked of anyone is the crossing itself: the leaving-behind and going-over, done again.

(Nietzsche, 1883/1961, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "Zarathustra's Prologue"; Nietzsche, 1882/1974, The Gay Science, §335.)

**Plain words**
Übermensch names a direction of travel, not a rank to hold. Zarathustra's picture is a rope: you are the crossing between the animal and whatever you could become, stretched over an abyss. You cannot arrive at the far end and stop; the figure exists only as the direction of the crossing. The practice is not to win a title but to keep moving: leave the inherited settings you cannot defend, and let the crossing itself be the thing you love.

**For example**
Anyone who has quietly left a default setting they cannot defend in their own words: an inherited opinion, a borrowed ambition, a fear of the neighbours' judgment. They have not become an overman; they have done a bit of over-going, which is the only form the figure takes.
