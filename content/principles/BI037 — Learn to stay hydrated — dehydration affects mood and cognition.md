> **Principle 37** — Learn to stay hydrated: dehydration affects mood and cognition

Dehydration — even mild — affects mood, energy, and cognition. Staying hydrated (water) is a simple physiological foundation for mental health.

**Plain words** Hydration is a mood variable — even mild dehydration degrades mood, energy, and thinking. The cheapest intervention on this list: water. The 3 p.m. fog is sometimes just a drought.

*For example:* The headache and the irritability that vanished with two glasses of water — the cheapest therapy session on record. The body was complaining in its own language.
## Psychology parallels

- **Hydration and mood**, mild dehydration impairs mood, energy, and cognitive performance (Armstrong et al., 2012; Ganio et al., 2011)
- **Dehydration and cognition**, even 1-2% dehydration impairs attention and memory (Ganio et al., 2011; Adan, 2012)
