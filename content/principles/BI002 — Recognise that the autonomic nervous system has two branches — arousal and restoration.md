> **Principle 2** — Recognise that the autonomic nervous system has two branches: arousal and restoration

The autonomic nervous system (ANS) has two branches: the sympathetic (arousal, mobilisation, "fight or flight") and the parasympathetic (restoration, digestion, "rest and digest"). Mental health depends on the balance and flexibility of these systems, not on one being dominant.

**Plain words** You have two nervous systems settings: gas (sympathetic — fight or flight) and brake (parasympathetic — rest and digest). Both are needed; the trouble is being stuck in gas. Every technique in this discipline is a way of finding the brake pedal.

*For example:* The jaw that's been clenched since the morning email — that's gas, applied to the jaw. The long exhale that unlocks it — that's the brake. The body has pedals; most people only know the accelerator.

## Practice

- Understand your autonomic state: activated (sympathetic-dominant) or restored (parasympathetic-dominant). Both are necessary; the problem is imbalance or rigidity. Practise shifting between them deliberately.
