> **Principle 499** — Practise the creation of values

Do not merely inherit your values; create them. Nietzsche's challenge is to become the authors of our own values, to give ourselves the law rather than receive it from tradition or authority. This is not relativism but the highest form of responsibility: the creation of values that serve life, that affirm existence, that enhance flourishing.

(Nietzsche, 1882/1974, The Gay Science, §301, §335; Nietzsche, 1883/1961, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "On the Thousand and One Goals"; Nietzsche, 1886/2001, Beyond Good and Evil, §211.)

**Plain words** Don't merely inherit your values; create them. Nietzsche's challenge: become the authors of our own values, give ourselves the law rather than receive it. Not relativism — the highest form of responsibility. The unchosen values are still values; they're just somebody else's, running your life.

*For example:* The person who keeps every rule they were raised with and calls it integrity — versus the one who tests each rule and either re-issues it in their own name or lets it go. The second one's kept rules hold better, because there's an author behind them.
