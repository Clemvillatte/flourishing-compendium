> **Principle 14** — Use cultural identity as a resource, not a constraint

Treat cultural identity — your identification with a cultural group, tradition, or community — as a resource, not a constraint. Cultural identity supplies belonging, meaning, continuity, and a repertoire of practices; it constrains when it becomes rigid, exclusionary, or imposed. The adaptive stance is to own your cultural identities without being owned by them.

**Plain words** Cultural identity is a resource — belonging, meaning, a repertoire of practices — until it hardens into a wall. Keep it as a garden, not a bunker. Feed from it without being fenced by it.

*For example:* The tradition that gives you the recipes, the songs, and the strong spine — and the moment you realise it also said who you couldn't marry. Keep the songs; renegotiate the fence.
