> **Principle 39** — Deliberate by rehearsal, not by verdict

Deliberation is not a court issuing verdicts; it is a dramatic rehearsal: running competing courses of action in imagination until one organizes the situation. Rehearse options vividly before choosing.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922.)

**Plain words** Deliberation is not a court issuing verdicts — it's a dramatic rehearsal: running competing courses of action in imagination until one organizes the situation. Rehearse options vividly before choosing. You have a simulator; use it before the live show.

*For example:* Mentally walking through quitting in the Monday meeting — the imagined scene collapses in ten seconds. The rehearsal just saved you a career move.
