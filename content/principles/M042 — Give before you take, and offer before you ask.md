> **Principle 42** — Give before you take, and offer before you ask

The Mesoamerican offering structure (kept here as psychology and social form, with the theology excluded): the first fruits, the first portion, the first hour are given before the rest is used. The practice trains the stance that the tradition considers primary: arrival in any system as a debtor to it, rather than as an extractor. Practically, adopt the first-fruits rule in one domain: the first hour of the workday given to the highest thing, the first portion of income given away, the first fruits of any harvest shared.

(The offering traditions; the first-fruits structure in the Mesoamerican calendars.)

**Plain words** The first fruits, the first portion, the first hour — given before the rest is used. The offering structure trains the stance: you arrive in any system as a debtor to it, not as an extractor. Adopt the first-portion rule somewhere in your life.

*For example:* Someone who takes the first hour of the day — before the inbox — for the thing they'd never otherwise get to, and finds the "offering" quietly re-prices the whole day.
