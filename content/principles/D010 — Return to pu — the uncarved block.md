> **Principle 10** — Return to pu: the uncarved block

Beneath your conditioning there is an original, uncut simplicity. Regularly strip back roles, credentials, and noise to that unpolished core and act from it.

(DDJ 15, 19, 28, 32, 37.)

**Plain words** Beneath all your roles, credentials and learned opinions there's an original simplicity — the uncarved block. It's not damaged by experience; it's just buried. Regularly strip back the performance and act from that unpolished core, which is sturdier than any persona.

*For example:* After a layoff strips away the title and the badge, what's left — how you actually treat people — turns out to be the real asset.
