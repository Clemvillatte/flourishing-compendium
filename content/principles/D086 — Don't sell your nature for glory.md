> **Principle 86** — Don't sell your nature for glory

The sacrificial ox is fed and robed for years: then butchered. If a role requires your essence as its price, decline the role.

(Zhuangzi 20, 32.)

**Plain words** The sacrificial ox is fed and robed for years — then butchered. If a role requires your essence as its price, decline the role. Being cultivated for the altar is not being valued; it's being prepared.

*For example:* The star employee being groomed for a role that will consume their weekends, health, and identity — the feeding is the preparation.
