> **Principle 130** — Ground your views of others in their experience

Judgments about classes, groups, or distant lives must answer to the actual experience of those lives: go and see, ask, listen. Abstractions about people are hypotheses, not data.

(Addams, Twenty Years at Hull-House, 1910.)

**Plain words** "The poor," "the rich," "immigrants" — all maps, drawn by people who mostly haven't been to the territory. Judgments about distant lives must answer to the actual experience of those lives: go and see, ask, listen.

*For example:* Volunteering at the shelter for a month and discovering 'the homeless' is forty individual Tuesdays with names — the abstraction never survives contact with the actual.
