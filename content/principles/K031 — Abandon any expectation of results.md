> **Principle 31** — Abandon any expectation of results

The slogan that cuts the practitioner's most subtle attachment: do the practice without the inner ledger that expects a return on it. Expectation converts practice into a transaction, and transactions collapse when the reward lags. The instruction is not against goals; it is against the grip on them. Practically, run one practice period per week with the explicit frame "this is not going anywhere," and notice what changes in the quality of the doing.

(Seven-Point Mind Training, Point Two.)

**Plain words** Do the practice with no inner ledger expecting a return. Expectation turns practice into a transaction, and transactions collapse when the reward is late. This isn't against goals, it's against gripping them. Once a week, practise with the frame "this is not going anywhere."

*For example:* Sitting for ten minutes with no plan to become calmer, and noticing you're calmer than usual.
