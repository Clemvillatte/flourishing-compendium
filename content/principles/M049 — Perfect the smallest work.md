> **Principle 49** — Perfect the smallest work

Florentine Codex Book X, in Anderson and Dibble's translation, describes the good craftsman: "The good craftsman is a willing worker, patient, calm. He works with care, he makes works of skill; he constructs, prepares, arranges, orders, fits, matches [materials]" ([Nahuatl Dictionary, entry for toltecatl](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/content/toltecatl)). The care is described as a trait of the craftsman. The passage I read does not mention an unseen audience, a vessel, a stone or a seam, so the seed's "quality that doesn't depend on an audience" is the compendium's reading, as is the link to the earlier private-observance principle.

Self-determination theory is the nearest research. Ryan and Deci's 2000 review says that satisfying the needs for "competence, autonomy, and relatedness" yields "enhanced self-motivation and mental health" ([Ryan and Deci 2000, American Psychologist](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2000-13324-007)). A later review reports that autonomous motivation "predicts persistence and adherence and is advantageous for effective performance, especially on complex or heuristic tasks" ([Deci and Ryan 2008](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2008_DeciRyan_CanPsy_Eng.pdf)). Neither tests whether people keep up their standards in unobserved work, so the seed's link between unobserved quality and internalized standards is unsupported by what I opened.

(The toltec craft tradition; the huehuetlatolli's counsel on the smallest works.)

### Scope

I did not find a Codex passage on small works specifically. The huehuetlatolli "counsel on the smallest works" cited in the seed was not located.

**Plain words**
The Nahua source praises the craftsman who works with care, patience and calm. The idea that quality kept up in private is the truest kind is the compendium's reading.

**For example**
Pick one small recurring task, such as how you leave a tool or a document at the end of the day. For a month, finish it to the same standard each time, and tick it off on a private list.
