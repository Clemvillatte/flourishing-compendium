> **Principle 49** — Perfect the smallest work

The toltec ideal extends to the smallest artifacts: the finishing of a vessel, the setting of a stone, the closing of a seam. The masters' reputation rested on work whose quality did not depend on audience. The principle is the tradition's version of the private observance (principle 40): excellence with no witnesses is the excellence that is actually yours. Practically, take one recurring small task and perform it to the toltec standard for one month, with no one told.

(The toltec craft tradition; the huehuetlatolli's counsel on the smallest works.)

**Plain words** The toltec ideal extends to the smallest artifacts: the finishing of a vessel, the setting of a stone, the closing of a seam — quality that doesn't depend on an audience. Excellence with no witnesses is the excellence that's actually yours.

*For example:* The bricklayer on the wall no one will ever see inside the roof cavity — who lays it true anyway — and the difference between him and the one who doesn't is the whole difference between a craft and a job.
