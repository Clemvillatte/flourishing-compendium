> **Principle 372** — Practise capabilities

Work to ensure that every person has the capabilities — the real freedoms, not just formal rights — to live a life they have reason to value. The capabilities approach asks not "how satisfied are people?" but "what can people actually do and be?" Advocate for the conditions that expand human capabilities.

(Sen, 1999, Development as Freedom; Nussbaum, 2000, Women and Human Development; Nussbaum, 2011, Creating Capabilities.)

**Plain words** The capabilities approach asks the better question: not "how satisfied are people?" but "what can people actually do and be?" Formal rights on paper mean nothing without the real freedom to live a life you have reason to value. Advocate for the conditions that expand that.

*For example:* Two countries both guarantee education — in one, the girl walks eight unsafe kilometres to the nearest school; in the other, she doesn't. Same right, wildly different capability. Work on the difference.
