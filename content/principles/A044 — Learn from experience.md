> **Principle 44** — Learn from experience

Phronesis cannot be taught from a textbook. It comes from lived experience, attended to and reflected upon. Young people can be clever but not practically wise, because they lack the years of particular cases that build the capacity for judgment. Value your experience as your primary teacher.

(NE VI.8.)

**Plain words** You can't learn practical judgement from a book. It comes from lived cases, paid attention to and thought about. That's why the young can be clever but not wise. They haven't seen enough. Treat your experience as your best teacher.

*For example:* Someone who has run three failed projects often sees the fourth go wrong at the planning stage. The first-time manager can't.
