> **Principle 45** — Use social identity as a resource and a risk

Treat social identity — the part of the self that derives from group memberships (nationality, ethnicity, religion, profession, gender, class) — as both a resource and a risk. Social identity supplies belonging, meaning, and support (Tajfel and Turner, 1979; Haslam et al., 2009); it also supplies in-group bias, out-group derogation, and intergroup conflict (Tajfel, 1981; Sidanius and Pratto, 1999). The adaptive stance is to own your identities while managing their risks.

**Plain words** Your group memberships — nationality, profession, gender, faith — are both a resource and a risk. They supply belonging and backup; they can also supply blind spots and walls. Use the membership, watch the edges.

*For example:* The profession that gave you identity, network, and pride — and quietly taught you that other professions are lesser. Same membership, both effects. The skill is keeping the first and auditing the second.
