> **Principle 97** — Keep the question alive

Katha Upanishad 1.3.14 calls on the seeker to arise, awaken, approach the great, and learn. It describes the path as hard to tread and difficult to cross, using a sharp razor's edge as its image. A suggested practice is to bring a question you cannot resolve to a teacher.

> "Arise! Awake! Approach the great and learn." ([Katha Upanishad 1.3.14](https://shlokam.org/text/kathopanishad.htm))

**Plain words** You can continue studying while acknowledging that you need help. Ask someone knowledgeable to explain the point you cannot yet understand.

*For example:* (Imagined application.) After reading a passage twice, a student writes down what remains unclear and asks their teacher to work through it.
