> **Principle 48** — Use the breath to steady attention

Among the classical supports for steadiness is the breath itself: the sutra names exhalation and the pause of breath as a direct means of mental stability. This is the tradition's own answer to the restless sit: give the mind the breath, which is always available and always moving. Practically, when scattered, extend the exhalation and count breaths before returning to the chosen object.

(YS 1.34.)

**Plain words** Among the classical supports for steadiness is the breath itself — the sutra names exhalation and the pause of breath as direct means of mental stability. The tradition's own answer to the restless sit: give the mind the breath. Always available, always with you.

*For example:* The anxious flyer who can't meditate the turbulence away — but can lengthen the exhale, count the pause, and let the breath do what the reassurances couldn't.
