> **Principle 72** — Grow by incorporating the other

Every experience prehends: takes in — elements of the world and makes them part of itself: you grow by digesting what is not yet you. Feed on the unfamiliar, not only the familiar.

(Whitehead, Process and Reality, 1929; Mead, Mind, Self and Society, 1934.)

**Plain words** Every experience takes in elements of the world and makes them part of itself — you grow by digesting what is not yet you. Feed on the unfamiliar, not only the familiar. You're made of what you've eaten: ideas, people, places, all of it.

*For example:* The year abroad that reorganized your taste in food, friends, and furniture — nothing was added; something was digested. The unfamiliar became structure.
