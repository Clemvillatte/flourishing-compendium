> **Principle 69** — Recognise ADHD: interest-based attention, not a deficit

The interest-based pattern has empirical footing. In one reading-comprehension study, children at risk for ADHD did significantly worse than peers on familiar stories — and indistinguishably from them when the story was novel. Novelty changed the outcome.

> "The study found that the children at risk for ADHD performed significantly worse (strong effect size) than non-ADHD children on attention and recall with reading material that was familiar. However, when presented with a novel story, they performed indistinguishably from their non-ADHD counterparts (Beike & Zentall, 2012)." (DeWitt, "ADHD, Willpower, and Interest: A Positive Approach", University of Pennsylvania MAPP capstone, 2020, p. 17, [full text via UPenn repository](https://repository.upenn.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/e85d5911-7c45-41b2-8377-2bb30cec785a/content))

The neural side of the pattern runs through the reward pathway. PET studies found ADHD subjects with fewer dopamine receptors and less transporter availability in that pathway, associated with lower scores on the motivation trait; the authors suggested considering a "motivation or interest deficit" as part of the core pathology (Volkow et al. 2011, as quoted in DeWitt, 2020, pp. 19-20). DeWitt's synthesis of the evidence:

> "Given the evidence above, we could therefore describe ADHD as a deficit of interest, meaning that when interest is low, ADHD traits tend to be high, but when interest is high, ADHD traits tend to be low (for example: Antrop et al., 2000; Brown, 2005; Giwerc, 2011; Roberts, Milich, & Barkley, 2015)." (DeWitt, 2020, p. 20, [full text via UPenn repository](https://repository.upenn.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/e85d5911-7c45-41b2-8377-2bb30cec785a/content))

So the deficit, where it shows up, sits in the interest and motivation circuitry rather than in the attention machinery itself: the same system that drops a boring page can hold a gripping one. The design implication is to supply the spark rather than shame the engine — "the brain of an individual with ADHD needs stimulation and interest in order to ameliorate some ADHD traits" (DeWitt, 2020, p. 18).

**Plain words**
For this attention system, interest is the on-switch. The machinery is not missing; it starts for some tasks and stays off for others. A boring task and a gripping one are, for this system, two different machines.

**For example**
The report that sits untouched for days, then gets finished in one focused hour once the deadline lights up. The capacity was there the whole time; it was waiting for urgency.

## Psychology parallels

- **ADHD as interest-based attention**, attention is captured by interest and novelty rather than directed by importance (Brown, 2013; Barkley, 1997)
- **ADHD is a neurodevelopmental condition with evidence-based treatments**, medication and behavioural strategies are effective (Faraone et al., 2021; Brown, 2013)
- **Note: ADHD is not simply "poor attention"**, it involves interest-based regulation, time blindness, and executive function differences (Brown, 2013; Barkley, 1997)

## Practice

- If you have ADHD, do not judge your attention by neurotypical standards. Design around interest: make important tasks interesting (novelty, challenge, urgency), use external structure (timers, accountability, visual cues), and consider professional evaluation for treatment.
