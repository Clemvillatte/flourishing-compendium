> **Principle 182** — Separate pain from suffering: daily

In daily life, distinguish the actual discomfort from the mental protest about it. The protest is optional; attend to what's actually wrong.

(SN 36.6; McCracken.)

**Plain words** In daily life, distinguish the actual discomfort from the mental protest about it. The protest is optional; attend to what's actually wrong. Rain is wet; the outrage about rain is a separate, dry event.

*For example:* The cold is real — put on a coat. The twenty-minute internal monologue about the cold is a second, colder weather system.
