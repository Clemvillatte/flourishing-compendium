> **Principle 41** — Cultivate transcendence

The virtue of transcendence includes appreciation of beauty, gratitude, hope, humour, and spirituality. It is the capacity to connect to something larger than the self. Its cultivation is not about religion, though religion is one path. It is about the experience of meaning, awe, and connection that exceeds the boundaries of the individual.

(Peterson and Seligman, 2004; Van Cappellen et al., 2016.)

**Plain words** Transcendence covers appreciation of beauty, gratitude, hope, humour and spirituality: connection to something larger than yourself. It isn't only religion, though that is one path. It's the experience of meaning and awe beyond your own boundaries.

*For example:* Standing in front of a painting for ten minutes and losing track of yourself.
