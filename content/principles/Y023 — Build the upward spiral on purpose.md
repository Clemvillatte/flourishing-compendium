> **Principle 23** — Build the upward spiral on purpose

Sutras 3.9 to 3.12 describe how the mind's subliminal impressions (samskara) shift as concentration deepens. They are about the mechanics of meditation, and the principle takes them as a general model. On this reading, a pattern that is practised grows stronger.

### What the text says

Sutra 3.9 describes "a becoming invisible of the subliminal-impression of emergence and a becoming visible of the subliminal-impression of restriction", which the commentary calls "the mutation of restriction" (trans. Woods, p. 209). Sutra 3.10 adds that the mind-stuff "flows peacefully by reason of the subliminal-impression", and the commentary says that this "requires dexterity in the application of the subliminal-impressions of restriction". If those impressions "become weak", they are "overwhelmed" by the impressions of emergence (p. 210). Sutra 3.11 names a third change: "the dwindling of dispersiveness and the uprisal of singleness-of-intent" (p. 211).

### What it does not say

The commentary speaks of restriction and emergence inside meditation. It does not mention calm responses in a relationship, early wins, or three-step spirals. The upward spiral is a fair practical extension, in the same direction as the sutras, but it is the principle's own reading.

**Plain words** In the text, practice works by tipping a balance. Each time restriction wins, its impression gets stronger and the mind rests more easily. If the impressions weaken, the old ones return. A good everyday rule is to protect the early repetitions, since they are the least stable.

*For example:* Imagined example: someone who has never left a party early does so three times in a month. By the fourth week the question "should I stay?" has changed shape. It is a modern illustration of the pattern, not a claim from the sutras.

(Yoga Sutras 3.9-3.11 with Vyasa's Bhasya and Vacaspati Misra's gloss, trans. James Haughton Woods, The Yoga-System of Patanjali (1914), pp. 209-211, Internet Archive, opened 2026-10-21. Sutra 3.12 not quoted.)
