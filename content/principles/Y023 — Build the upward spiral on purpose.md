> **Principle 23** — Build the upward spiral on purpose

The tradition observes that each mental event reinforces the tendency that produced it: a restraint strengthens restraint, an indulgence strengthens indulgence. This is the samskara loop read optimistically. Practically, engineer early wins: the first three calm responses in a difficult relationship are not just three good moments, they are the first three turns of a new spiral.

(YS 3.9-3.12.)

**Plain words** Each mental event reinforces the tendency that produced it: a restraint strengthens restraint, an indulgence strengthens indulgence — the samskara loop, read optimistically. Engineer early wins: the first three calm responses make the fourth cheaper.

*For example:* Someone who's never once left a party early — does it three times in a month, and finds the fourth week's "should I stay?" question has quietly changed shape. The spiral runs up as easily as down; that's the point.
