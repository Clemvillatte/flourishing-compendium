> **Principle 137** — Expect your seeing to change (the wise see differently)

Perception is trained: what you now take as solid reality is partly a skill of seeing, and the skill can be retrained. Trust the deepening.

(Dhammapada 277-279; SN 12.23.)

**Plain words** Perception is trained — what you now take as solid reality is partly a skill of seeing, and the skill can be retrained. Trust the deepening. The expert wine-taster and the novice are drinking the same grape; the training is the difference.

*For example:* The first year in a new country is noise; the fifth year is language. The world didn't change — the seeing did.
