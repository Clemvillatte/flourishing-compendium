> **Principle 107** — Treat education as growth, not preparation

Learning is not stocking up for a future life; it is living growingly now. Whatever you are learning, learn it as growth, not as deposit.

(Dewey, Democracy and Education, 1916.)

**Plain words** Learning is not stocking up for a future life — it is living growingly now. Whatever you're learning, learn it as growth, not as deposit. The kid taught that school is a waiting room for life learns to wait, which is the one skill that ruins the waiting room years.

*For example:* The degree done 'to get a job' versus the year of night classes done for the love of the subject — the second one still pays dividends at dinner parties, decades later.
