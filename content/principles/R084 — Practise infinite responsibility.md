> **Principle 346** — Practise infinite responsibility

Recognise that your responsibility for the Other is infinite and prior to your freedom. You do not choose to be responsible; you discover that you already are. This does not mean you can meet every need, but it means that the demand of the Other is never exhausted by what you have done.

(Levinas, 1961/1969, Totality and Infinity; Levinas, 1969, Otherwise Than Being.)

**Plain words** Your responsibility for others isn't a contract you signed — you discover you were already in it. And it's never fully discharged: whatever you've done, the demand isn't exhausted. Not a guilt trip; a description of what a human being is.

*For example:* The neighbour who noticed the hoarder's house years ago and "helped once" — and understands, reading the news later, that the once was never going to be enough, and that noticing again is still on them.
