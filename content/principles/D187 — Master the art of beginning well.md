> **Principle 187** — Master the art of beginning well

Whatever you would do, begin at the true beginning: with stillness, alignment, and smallness — and endings tend to themselves.

(DDJ 64; Neiye.)

**Plain words** Whatever you would do, begin at the true beginning — with stillness, alignment, and smallness — and endings tend to themselves. Most failures are beginnings that skipped steps and called it speed. The foundation is the cheap part that everything expensive sits on.

*For example:* The renovation that started with 'let's just see about the wall' — versus the one that checked the pipes first.
