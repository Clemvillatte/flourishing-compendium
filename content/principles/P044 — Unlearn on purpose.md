> **Principle 44** — Unlearn on purpose

Growth is not only acquisition but retirement: drop strategies that once worked and now don't. Keep a "stop-doing" list as serious as your to-do list.

(Dewey, The Quest for Certainty, 1929.)

**Plain words** Growth is not only acquisition but retirement — drop strategies that once worked and now don't. Keep a 'stop-doing' list as serious as your to-do list. Most people's growth stalls not from missing additions but from undischarged subtractions.

*For example:* The micromanagement that saved the startup at five people is strangling it at fifty — the stop-doing list is where the next quarter is won.
