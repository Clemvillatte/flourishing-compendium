> **Principle 389** — Practise ecological humility

Recognise the limits of your knowledge and your power in relation to the natural world. Do not approach nature with the attitude of mastery. Approach it with humility, curiosity, and respect. You are not the master of nature; you are a participant in it.

(Hooker et al., 2020, "Everyday Engagement with Nature," Environment and Behavior; Merchant, 1980, The Death of Nature.)

**Plain words** Approach nature without the mastery attitude — with humility, curiosity, respect. You're not the manager of nature; you're a participant in it, with limited knowledge and limited power. The posture changes what you're able to see.

*For example:* The landowner who stopped "improving" the back paddock for a season, just watched — and learned the ground had been telling him something all along that the dozer never let him hear.
