> **Principle 13** — Practice generosity (eleutheriotes)

Generosity is the mean between miserliness and prodigality regarding material goods. Give the right amount, to the right people, at the right time, from the right sources, and for the right reasons. Giving should cost you something but not ruin you.

(NE IV.1.)

**Plain words** Giving well means the right amount to the right people at the right time, for the right reasons. Aristotle says it should cost you something but not ruin you. Giving too little is hoarding. Giving everything isn't virtue, it's another kind of poor judgement.

*For example:* Donating a steady slice of your income to a cause you've checked is generosity. Handing over your rent to every sad story on social media is something else.
