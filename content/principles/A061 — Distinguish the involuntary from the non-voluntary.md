> **Principle 61** — Distinguish the involuntary from the non-voluntary

An involuntary act is done through ignorance, and the agent feels regret afterward. A non-voluntary act is also done through ignorance, but the agent feels no regret. The distinction matters: regret is a sign that the person understood the wrong.

(NE III.1.)

**Plain words** Both kinds of act are done through ignorance. But in the involuntary case you feel regret when you find out. In the non-voluntary case you don't. Aristotle's point is that regret shows you understood the wrong.

*For example:* Someone who accidentally shares a private message and feels sick about it differs from someone who shrugs. The sick feeling is evidence of care.
