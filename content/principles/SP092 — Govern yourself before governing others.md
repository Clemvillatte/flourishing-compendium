> **Principle 92** — Govern yourself before governing others

The Ethics and the Political Treatise converge on one rule of leadership: the person who would order the lives of others must first have ordered their own, because the un-ordered ruler projects their disorder onto everyone below them. Spinoza's political writings are full of this practical psychology: fear-driven rulers produce fear-driven states. Practically, in every domain where you lead, your first instrument of leadership is your own regulated state.

(Political Treatise, ch. 1 and 6-7; Ethics IV, P37 scholium 1.)

**Plain words** The person who would order the lives of others must first have ordered their own — the un-ordered ruler projects disorder onto everyone below. Govern yourself before governing others; the team's chaos is usually the leader's interior, exported.

*For example:* The manager who "can't understand why everyone's so anxious" — while visibly running on caffeine, deadline panic, and avoidance — and the Spinozist diagnosis: the team inherited the weather. The first fix was never the team's.
