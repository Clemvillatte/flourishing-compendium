> **Principle 143** — Do not desire externals as if they were permanent

Everything external is subject to change: wealth, status, health, relationships. The person who depends on externals for happiness builds on sand. Anchor your well-being in what cannot be taken: your own character.

(Disc. I.25, II.5; Med. II.11, IV.1, VI.14; Seneca, Letters 8, 71, 87.)

**Plain words** Everything external changes: wealth, status, health, relationships. Anyone who depends on them for happiness builds on sand. Anchor your well-being in what can't be taken: your own character.

*For example:* A person whose confidence rests on her job title is shaken at redundancy. One whose confidence rests on how she treats people carries it to the next job.
