> **Principle 72** — Design the environment to support the practice

The strongest self-control strategy is choosing environments, not resisting temptations. Put the phone in another room. Leave the guitar visible. Lay out the running shoes. Design the physical and digital environment so that the desired practice is the easy choice and the undesired habit is the hard one. Research on environmental design (Thaler and Sunstein) shows that choice architecture shapes behaviour more reliably than willpower.

(Wood and Neal, 2007; Thaler and Sunstein, 2008.)

**Plain words** The strongest self-control strategy is choosing environments, not resisting temptation. Phone in another room, guitar left visible, shoes by the door. Make the wanted behaviour easy and the unwanted one hard.

*For example:* Not buying the biscuits beats not eating the biscuits in the cupboard.
