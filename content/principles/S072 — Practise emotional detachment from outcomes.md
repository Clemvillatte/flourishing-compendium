> **Principle 194** — Practise emotional detachment from outcomes

The Stoic does not detach from action but from the outcome of action. Act well, and let the result be what it will. Your responsibility is for the action, not for the result. This is not passivity; it is freedom from the anxiety of outcomes.

(Disc. I.1, I.12, I.25; Ench. 1; Med. II.9, IV.3, VII.8; Seneca, Letters 107, 123.)

**Plain words** The Stoic detaches from outcomes, not from action. Act well and let the result be what it will. You're responsible for the action, not the result. That isn't passivity, it's freedom from outcome anxiety.

*For example:* Preparing carefully for a pitch, then letting go of whether they say yes.
