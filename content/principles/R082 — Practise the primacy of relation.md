> **Principle 344** — Practise the primacy of relation

Recognise that relation is not something added to individual existence but is prior to it. You do not first exist as an isolated individual and then enter into relation; you exist in relation from the start. The Between is not a space between two pre-existing subjects but the ontological ground from which subjects emerge.

(Buber, 1923/1970, I and Thou; Buber, 1965, The Knowledge of Man, "The Word That Is Spoken.")

**Plain words** You don't exist first and then connect — you exist in relation from the very start. The "between" isn't the gap between two finished people; it's the ground the people themselves grow out of. Which means: relation is maintenance, not accessory.

*For example:* The twins' shared language, the old couple's shorthand, the team's unspoken rhythm — none of it lives in either person; it lives in the between, and it needs both of them showing up to survive.
