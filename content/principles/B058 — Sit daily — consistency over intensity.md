> **Principle 58** — Sit daily: consistency over intensity

Brief, regular sittings outperform heroic binges: the mind trains the way a river cuts a gorge, by persistence.

(Dhammapada 239; Suzuki.)

**Plain words** Brief, regular sittings outperform heroic binges — the mind trains the way a river cuts a gorge, by persistence. Five minutes daily for a year will do what three hours on New Year's Day never could. Consistency is the advanced practice.

*For example:* The person who meditates ten minutes every day versus the retreat-weekend warrior — by December, only one of them has a practice.
