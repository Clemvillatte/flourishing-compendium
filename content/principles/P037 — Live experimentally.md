> **Principle 37** — Live experimentally

Treat situations as experiments: formulate a hypothesis, act, observe, learn. A life run as a series of intelligent experiments cannot be a failure: every trial yields data.

(Dewey, The Quest for Certainty, 1929; James, The Will to Believe, 1896.)

**Plain words** Treat situations as experiments — formulate a hypothesis, act, observe, learn. A life run as a series of intelligent experiments cannot be a failure: every trial yields data. The only losing move is not running trials.

*For example:* The month you tried waking at six, the month at eight — two experiments, one clear result, zero failed months. Data either way.
