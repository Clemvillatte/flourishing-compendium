> **Principle 121** — Let relationship sharpen self-knowledge

The Gita's diagnostic: relationships are where practice is proven, because the other person's pleasure and pain mirroring yours is the test of whether your equanimity is real or performed. The tradition treats difficulty in relationships as curriculum, not as interruption. Practically, in your most difficult relationship, treat each flare-up as a scheduled experiment: what did this show about my practice?

(BG 6.32, the parallel of others' states as one's own.)

**Plain words** Relationships are where practice is proven — the other person's pleasure and pain mirroring yours is the test of whether your equanimity is real or performed. Treat difficulty in relationships as curriculum, not interruption.

*For example:* The meditator who is serene everywhere except within fifteen minutes of their mother — and finally takes the hint: the mother is the exam the retreat was studying for.
