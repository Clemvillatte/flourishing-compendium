> **Principle 8** — Recognise that behaviour is maintained by reinforcement schedules: consistency matters

Behaviour is maintained by how it is reinforced. Continuous reinforcement (every time) builds behaviour quickly but extinguishes quickly. Intermittent reinforcement (sometimes) builds behaviour slowly but extinguishes slowly (more resistant to extinction).

**Plain words** Habits live on reinforcement schedules, and intermittent rewards — random, occasional payouts — are the stickiest kind. That's why slots, feeds, and toxic relationships are so hard to leave: the next pull might pay. Consistency in your own habits works the same physics, on purpose.

*For example:* The occasional windfall keeps someone at the machine far longer than a guaranteed small payout would — the unpredictability is the hook. Design your rewards predictable, and the habit boring.

## Practice

- Use continuous reinforcement to build new behaviours (reward every time), then shift to intermittent to maintain. This produces durable behaviour.
