> **Principle 26** — Use community as a resource

Use community — the networks of people, institutions, and practices that surround you — as a resource for adaptive flourishing. Community supplies support, meaning, identity, and practical help; it also makes demands. The adaptive stance is to give and receive within community, not to consume it.

**Plain words** Community is a resource that also bills you — support, meaning, identity, practical help, in exchange for demands. The healthy stance is both giving and receiving. The taker and the martyr both break the same machine.

*For example:* The neighbour who borrows your drill and brings soup when you're sick — the exchange is the community. The one who never asks for anything is as outside it as the one who never gives.
