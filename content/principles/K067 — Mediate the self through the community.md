> **Principle 67** — Mediate the self through the community

Tanabe's "logic of the species": the individual does not exist first and then join groups; the individual is constituted in and through the community (family, nation, craft) that mediates them, and the self that ignores this mediation is abstract. The practical consequence is the opposite of the individualist fear: serving the community is not sacrificing the self, it is where the self is. Practically, name the three communities that constitute you, and give each one deliberate service in the season ahead.

(Tanabe, Philosophy as Metanoetics, the logic of the species chapters.)

**Plain words** Tanabe's logic of the species says you don't exist first and then join groups. You're formed in and through family, nation, craft. The self that ignores that is an abstraction. So serving your community isn't giving yourself up. It's part of how you became yourself.

*For example:* A craftsperson realises her skill is a gift from her teachers and community, and teaching an apprentice feels less like sacrifice and more like repayment.
