> **Principle 214** — Treat adversity as training

What stands in the way becomes the way. Adversity is not an obstacle to flourishing but the material of it. The person who has never faced difficulty has never tested their virtue. When hardship comes, ask: what virtue does this situation call for?

(Med. IV.1, IV.50, V.8, V.10, V.18, V.20, VI.48, VII.8, VII.19, VII.20, VII.43, VII.55, VIII.35, VIII.41, X.33; Disc. I.6, I.24, III.20; Ench. 1, 16, 19, 29, 53; Seneca, On Providence.)

**Plain words** What stands in the way becomes the way. Adversity isn't a blocker of the good life. It's raw material. Someone who has never been tested doesn't know their own virtue. When hardship arrives, ask which virtue it's calling for.

*For example:* A failed launch becomes the occasion to practise honesty with your team and customers.
