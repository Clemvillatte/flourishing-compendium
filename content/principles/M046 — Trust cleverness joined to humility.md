> **Principle 46** — Trust cleverness joined to humility

The Popol Vuh's heroes (the twins Hunahpu and Xbalanque) win through wit, patience, and partnership against forces stronger than they: they lose the games, endure the trials, accept the sacrifice, and out-think the lords of death in death itself. The narrative's teaching is the tradition's practical epistemology: strength is not the only resource; the humble trickster's intelligence is. Practically, when outmatched (by institutions, illness, circumstance), do not resign and do not collide; think, wait, partner, and take the long angle.

(Popol Vuh, Part Two, the hero twins' trials.)

**Plain words** The hero twins win through wit, patience, and partnership against stronger forces: they lose the games, endure the trials, accept the sacrifice — and out-think the lords of death in death itself. Strength is not the only resource; the humble trickster's cleverness, joined to endurance, beats the monsters.

*For example:* The small business outmaneuvering the giant not by out-spending but by out-patienting — smaller, humbler, faster to learn, willing to lose rounds and win the war.
