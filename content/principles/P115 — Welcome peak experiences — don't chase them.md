> **Principle 115** — Welcome peak experiences: don't chase them

Peak experiences — moments of wholeness, awe, or unity — come unbidden to the open and the growing. Prepare the conditions, receive them when they come, and don't turn them into a demand.

(Maslow, Toward a Psychology of Being, 1962; Religions, Values, and Peak-Experiences, 1964.)

**Plain words** The peak you chase flees; the peak you allow arrives on its own schedule. Moments of wholeness and awe come unbidden to the open and the growing — prepare the conditions, receive them, and don't turn them into a demand.

*For example:* The mountaintop moment that happened on a bus, looking at rain on a window — not on the two planned retreats. Peaks aren't scheduled; they're noticed. Conditions, then attention.
