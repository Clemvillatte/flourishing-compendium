> **Principle 62** — Learn to track physiological states: HRV, sleep, energy

Physiological states can be tracked: HRV (autonomic flexibility), sleep (quality and duration), energy (subjective). Tracking provides feedback for adjustment.

**Plain words** Track the physiology — HRV, sleep, energy — and the patterns emerge: the Monday dip, the post-alcohol week, the pre-illness crash. Data beats impressions about your own body. Two weeks of logs outperform a year of self-theory.

*For example:* "I sleep fine" — says the tracker with the 11 p.m. glucose spike and the 2 a.m. wake-ups, every night. The log has no ego, which is why it's honest.
## Psychology parallels

- **HRV as biomarker**, HRV reflects autonomic flexibility and stress resilience (Thayer et al., 2012; Laborde et al., 2017)
- **Sleep tracking**, sleep quality and duration are measurable and affect mental health (Walker, 2017; Morin et al., 2006)

- **[[The examined life|The examined life requires data]]** (Socrates)

## Practice

- Track physiology: use an HRV app or wearable, track sleep (duration, quality), and monitor energy. Use this data to adjust sleep, exercise, and stress management.
