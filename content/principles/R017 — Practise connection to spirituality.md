> **Principle 279** — Practise connection to spirituality

Maintain your connection to the spiritual dimension of life. In Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander traditions, spirituality is not separate from daily life but pervades every aspect of existence. The Dreaming is not a story of the past but a living reality that continues to shape the present.

(Aboriginal SEWB framework; Dudgeon et al., 2014, Working Together.)

**Plain words** The spiritual dimension isn't a separate compartment — it runs through everything, and the Dreaming isn't history but a live current shaping the present. Keep the connection: attend to the sacred in the ordinary, not just on appointed days.

*For example:* The fisherman who won't take from the bay on certain days, tells the story of why to his kids, and finds the rule does something to the fishing that the regulation sign never could.
