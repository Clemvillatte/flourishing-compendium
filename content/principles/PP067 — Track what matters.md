> **Principle 67** — Track what matters

Use simple measures to track the effects of practices: a 1-to-10 rating of well-being, a count of specific behaviours, a brief mood log. The tracking is not for clinical assessment. It is for calibration: does this practice, in this context, at this dose, produce the intended effect? If the data are ambiguous, adjust the practice and track again.

(Park et al., 2014; Bolier et al., 2013.)

**Plain words** Track effects with simple measures: a mood rating, a behaviour count, a short log. Not for clinical assessment but for calibration: does this practice, at this dose, actually do what you intend? If not, adjust.

*For example:* Rating your mood out of ten each evening reveals the evening walk is doing more than the morning meditation.
