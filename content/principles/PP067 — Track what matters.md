> **Principle 67** — Track what matters

Tracking has experimental support when the goal is a behaviour or outcome you are working toward. A meta-analysis of 138 randomised studies (N = 19,951) found that interventions increasing progress monitoring promoted goal attainment (d+ = 0.40), with larger effects "when the outcomes were reported or made public, and when the information was physically recorded" ([Harkin et al. 2016, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26479070/)). The studies focused mostly on health goals such as diet and weight. Monitoring a behaviour changed the behaviour but not necessarily the outcome, and monitoring an outcome changed the outcome but not the behaviour ([APA summary](https://www.apa.org/news/press/releases/2015/10/progress-goals)).

Mood tracking is less clear. A trial in which mood was monitored for two to four weeks found self-monitoring did not directly change depressive symptoms ([JMIR 2012 trial](https://www.jmir.org/2012/3/e67/)). A review of ambulatory mood monitoring in depression found that some people reported worry or rumination about low mood, though quantitative studies have not shown long-term worsening ([Nature review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-02118-8)). A pooled analysis found about 2% reported a lowering of mood, with limited certainty ([JMIR meta-analysis](https://mental.jmir.org/2025/1/e79500/XML)).

(Park et al., 2014; Bolier et al., 2013.)

### Scope

I did not open the seed's citations (Park et al. 2014; Bolier et al. 2013) for this claim, and none of the studies above tested a one-to-ten well-being rating used to adjust practices. The calibration idea is the compendium's.

**Plain words**
Tracking a specific behaviour or goal helps people reach it, especially if it is written down or shared. Daily mood ratings can sometimes make low mood feel worse, so stop if the log becomes a source of worry.

**For example**
Write down each evening whether you did the practice, yes or no. Once a week, share the count with one person and note whether your rating changed.
