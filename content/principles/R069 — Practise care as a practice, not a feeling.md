> **Principle 331** — Practise care as a practice, not a feeling

Care is not a feeling but a practice. It involves attentiveness, responsibility, competence, and responsiveness. Practise these four elements in every caring relationship.

(Tronto, 1993, Moral Boundaries: A Political Argument for an Ethics of Care, pp. 127-130; Tronto, 2013, Caring Democracy.)

**Plain words** Care isn't a feeling — it's a practice with four working parts: attentiveness, responsibility, competence, responsiveness. Run all four or it's not care, whatever the warmth level. The feeling is optional; the elements aren't.

*For example:* The person who loves their ageing parent dearly but keeps missing the meds — warm feelings, three missing elements. The sibling who sets up the pill organiser has all four.
