> **Principle 150** — Accept incompleteness as constitutive

You are an unfinished process, and not by accident: incompleteness is what a process is. Stop waiting to be complete before living; live from your incompleteness.

(Deacon, Incomplete Nature, 2011 — secularized; Dewey's growth.)

**Plain words** You are an unfinished process, and not by accident — incompleteness is what a process is. Stop waiting to be complete before living; live from your incompleteness. The draft is the only version of you that exists, and drafts are the only things that ever get written.

*For example:* 'I'll start dating once I have my life together' — the together life never arrives, because it's a process, not a destination. Live from the draft. Everyone you'd date is also a draft.
