> **Principle 110** — Make life artistic: seek "an experience."

Most of life is loose and unfinished; every so often, an experience completes — a meal, a conversation, a project that has unity, development, and a felt completion. Design for completion: beginnings that develop, endings that fulfil.

(Dewey, Art as Experience, 1934.)

**Plain words** Most days are loose and unfinished — but a meal, a conversation, or a project can complete: a beginning that develops, a middle that turns, an ending that lands. Design for those completions; they are what art delivers, and your calendar can deliver them too.

*For example:* The dinner party that lands — the food, the argument, the laughing departure at the door. It had the shape of a good story. That's not luck; that's design, and you can design more of them.
