> **Principle 11** — Practice courage (andreia)

Courage is the mean between cowardice (too much fear) and recklessness (too little fear). It is not fearlessness. It is acting well in the face of what is genuinely fearful, for the sake of something worthy. Identify what you are afraid of that is worth facing, and face it in measured steps.

(NE III.6-9.)

**Plain words** Courage isn't having no fear. Aristotle puts it between cowardice and recklessness: you act well in the face of what's truly frightening, for something worth it. Pick the fear that's worth facing, and start small enough that you can actually do it.

*For example:* Someone afraid of public speaking who volunteers for a two-minute update at a team meeting is practising courage, far more than the person who jumps off a cliff for a dare.
