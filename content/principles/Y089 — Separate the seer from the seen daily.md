> **Principle 89** — Separate the seer from the seen daily

The Drig-Drisya-Viveka procedure, attributed to Shankara's circle: take any experience and sort it into the seen (qualities, changes, objects) and the seeing (the fact of awareness of them), repeatedly, until the sorting is reflexive. The book's standing caveat applies: this is offered as procedure, with the ontology open. Practically, one sort per day, at a moment of strong feeling: what in this is the seen, what is the seeing?

(Drig-Drisya-Viveka, attributed; YS 2.18-2.20 as the Yogic parallel.)

**Plain words** The seer-seen sorting procedure: take any experience and sort it into the seen (qualities, changes, objects) and the seeing (the fact of awareness of them) — repeatedly, until the sorting is reflexive. A daily discipline, not a doctrine to believe.

*For example:* In the middle of a mood, someone runs the sort: the mood is seen, the seeing of it is not the mood — and the mood, now seen rather than been, loses about a third of its vote. Same mood; different seat.
