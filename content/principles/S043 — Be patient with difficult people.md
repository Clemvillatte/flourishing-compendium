> **Principle 165** — Be patient with difficult people

Marcus reminds himself daily that he will meet difficult people and that they are part of nature. The difficult person is not an obstacle to flourishing but material for the practice of patience, justice, and compassion.

(Med. II.1, II.13, IV.3, VII.13, VII.22, IX.4, IX.11, XI.18.)

**Plain words** Marcus reminds himself each morning that he'll meet difficult people and that they're part of nature. They aren't an obstacle to the good life. They're material for practising patience, justice and compassion.

*For example:* A manager known for being abrasive is the person you practise listening on.
