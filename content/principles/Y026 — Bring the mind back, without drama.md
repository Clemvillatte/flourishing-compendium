> **Principle 26** — Bring the mind back, without drama

The Gita's instruction is exact: wherever the restless and unguarded mind wanders, lead it back under the self's control. The instruction's genius is its tone: no self-attack, no commentary, just return. Practically, count the return itself as the repetition that builds the muscle; a session of fifty returns is fifty reps, not a failure.

(BG 6.26.)

**Plain words** The Gita's instruction is exact: wherever the restless and unguarded mind wanders, lead it back under the self's control — with no self-attack, no commentary, just return. Count the return itself as the repetition that builds the muscle. The return IS the practice.

*For example:* The meditator who berates themselves for every wandering — versus the one who treats each return like a bench-press rep: wandered, returned, one. A hundred unglamorous returns is the workout. Nobody counts the wandering.
