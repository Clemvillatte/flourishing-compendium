> **Principle 82** — Practice seeing things under the aspect of eternity

> It is in the nature of reason to perceive things under a certain form of eternity (sub quâdam æternitatis specie).

(Spinoza, Ethics, Part II, Proposition 44, Corollary 2, trans. R. H. M. Elwes, [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3800).)

Spinoza's proof is that "It is in the nature of reason to regard things, not as contingent, but as necessary," and that this necessity "is the very necessity of the eternal nature of God." In Part V, Proposition 29, "Whatsoever the mind understands under the form of eternity, it does not understand by virtue of conceiving the present actual existence of the body, but by virtue of conceiving the essence of the body under the form of eternity." Proposition 30 adds that the mind so conceiving "has to that extent necessarily a knowledge of God."

So for Spinoza, seeing things under the form of eternity means seeing them as following necessarily from the nature of things, with no reference to time. The entry's practice, holding a troubling event inside the widest frame you can honestly build (a lifetime, a civilisation, nature), is an adaptation of the phrase and is not what these propositions say.

For the modern parallel, Grossmann and Kross (2014) found in three experiments (N = 693) that people reasoned more wisely about another person's problems than their own, and that instructing participants to self-distance rather than self-immerse "eliminated this asymmetry" (Psychological Science 25(8); [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24916084/)). That study is about third-person distance on interpersonal conflicts. It did not test the largest-frame practice.

**Plain words** Spinoza means seeing things as part of how nature has to unfold, not as isolated accidents. A related, easier habit is to step back and look at your own problem the way you would look at a friend's. This entry offers the wide-angle exercise as a way to try the same shift.

*For example:* A career setback viewed up close is a year of humiliation. Viewed as one movement in a longer life and a wider economy, it is smaller and easier to think about. Both views are true.
