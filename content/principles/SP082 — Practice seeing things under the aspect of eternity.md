> **Principle 82** — Practice seeing things under the aspect of eternity

The mind can conceive things sub specie aeternitatis, under the form of eternity: not as fragments of time, here and gone, but as expressions of the whole. The practice is a discipline of attention, not a metaphysical claim you must accept first: take any troubling event and hold it inside the largest frame you can honestly construct (a lifetime, a civilization, nature), and note what survives the reframing. That which survives is what deserves your feeling.

(Ethics V, P29-30, with the eternity of mind analysis.)

**Plain words** Sub specie aeternitatis: things conceived under the form of eternity — not as fragments of time, here and gone, but as expressions of the whole. A discipline of attention, not a metaphysical claim. Practise the wide shot: this event, seen as part of the whole.

*For example:* The career catastrophe zoomed in — the year of collapse, the humiliation — and the same event in the wide shot: one movement of a whole life, a whole generation, a whole economy. Not consolation; perspective. The zoom level is a choice, and both views are true.
