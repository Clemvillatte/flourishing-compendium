> **Principle 5** — Practice steadiness and let go of grip together

Yoga Sūtra 1.12 presents practice and passionlessness together as means of restriction. Sūtra 1.13 describes practice as effort toward steadiness, and 1.14 specifies duration, continuity and earnest attention. Sūtra 1.15 defines passionlessness through mastery over craving.

For a compendium exercise, plan a repeatable study period. Record whether each session took place and separately note the reward you hope the work will bring.

> Passionlessness is the consciousness of being master on the part of one who has rid himself of thirst for either seen or revealed objects.
>
> Patañjali, Yoga Sūtra 1.15, translated by James Haughton Woods (1914), [reprinted text](https://www.theessentialway.org/yoga-sutras/).

**Plain words**
Choose a regular task. Decide when you will do it and note what you hope to get from it. After each session, record the work you did and any urge to judge your progress immediately.

**For example**
In an imagined exercise, a language learner plans a repeatable study period and records after each session whether the planned session took place.
