> **Principle 6** — Creativity is the ultimate principle

The universe is not a machine replaying its script; novelty is real: genuinely new things emerge. Your life can produce genuine novelty: new capacities, new relations, new value.

(Whitehead, Process and Reality, 1929.)

**Plain words** The universe isn't a machine replaying its script — novelty is real: genuinely new things emerge. Your life can produce genuine novelty: new capacities, new relations, new value. You're not a rerun; the next episode genuinely hasn't been written.

*For example:* Nobody had the smartphone, then somebody did — and your grandmother's group chat is proof the universe invents.
