> **Principle 168** — Put yourself last: and be preserved

The sage puts his own body last, and it is found first; he is detached from his body, and it is preserved. Paradoxical, reliable.

(DDJ 7, 13.)

**Plain words** The sage puts his own body last, and it is found first. Paradoxical, reliable — the person obsessively self-protecting loses themselves in the protection, while the one absorbed in the work somehow stays intact. Guarding the candle kills the flame.

*For example:* The performer who thinks about the audience's judgment flops; the one absorbed in the material is unforgettable — and fine afterward.
