> **Principle 152** — Align your work with your values (right livelihood, revisited)

AN 5.177 gives lay followers a concrete restriction on earning a living. It names five kinds of business to avoid ([Thanissaro](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/an/an05/an05.177.than.html)):

> Business in weapons, business in human beings, business in meat, business in intoxicants, and business in poison.

(AN 5.177, [Thanissaro](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/an/an05/an05.177.than.html).)

The instruction concerns what a person trades in, rather than how meaningful the work feels. The compendium extends that question to the tasks a job requires: what are you being paid to help happen? A move beyond the five named trades needs to be argued as an application, not presented as another item in the sutta's list.

**Plain words** Look at a specific part of your work that troubles you. What does it require you to do, who is affected, and what room do you have to change it? You can investigate those questions without deciding that you must immediately resign.

*For example:* Imagine being asked to describe a product with a claim you cannot substantiate. You ask for evidence and propose wording you can support. If the request persists, you consider what options are available before committing to a next step.
