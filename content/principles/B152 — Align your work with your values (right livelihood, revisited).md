> **Principle 152** — Align your work with your values (right livelihood, revisited)

Your livelihood is your largest single practice: what you do all day trains you all day. Choose work you can defend to yourself.

(AN 5.177.)

**Plain words** Your livelihood is your largest single practice — what you do all day trains you all day. Choose work you can defend to yourself. You can meditate for an hour and undo it with eight hours of work you're ashamed of.

*For example:* The person who leaves the manipulative marketing job and discovers their meditation suddenly 'works better' — the eight-hour counterweight, removed.
