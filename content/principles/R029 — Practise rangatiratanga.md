> **Principle 291** — Practise rangatiratanga

Exercise leadership that is grounded in service to the people. A rangatira (chief) is one who weaves the people together. Leadership is not domination or control but the facilitation of collective wellbeing. Lead by serving, not by commanding.

(Māori tikanga; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori; Durie, 1994, Whaiora.)

**Plain words** Rangatiratanga: lead by weaving people together. A rangatira isn't the one who commands from the front but the one whose serving holds the group steady. Leadership as facilitation of collective wellbeing — domination is the failure mode, not the strong version.

*For example:* The team lead whose instinct in every crisis is "who needs to be connected to whom?" — and whose team, after a year, can run without them, which they count as the win.
