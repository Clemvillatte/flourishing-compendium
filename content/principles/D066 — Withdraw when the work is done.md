> **Principle 66** — Withdraw when the work is done

This is heaven's way: complete the task, step back, don't linger for applause. Ending well is half the work.

(DDJ 9.)

**Plain words** Complete the task, step back, don't linger for applause. This is heaven's way: ending well is half the work. Overstaying your moment — milking the win, hovering after the help — is how good work curdles.

*For example:* The guest who leaves at the perfect moment is remembered fondly; the one who stays until the host yawns is remembered too.
