> **Principle 29** — Stop before the cup overflows

Better to halt than to brim: a cup held too full spills, a blade over-sharpened dulls, a hall of gold and jade cannot be kept. Ease off before the peak.

(DDJ 9.)

**Plain words** Better to halt than to brim: the over-filled cup spills, the over-sharpened blade dulls, the hall of gold and jade can't be kept. Ease off before the peak — the last 5% of gain usually costs more than it pays.

*For example:* Negotiating one more concession after the deal is already good is how deals die; the seasoned closer stops at good.
