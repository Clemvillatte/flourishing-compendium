> **Principle 52** — Learn to use deadlines: constraints motivate

Deadlines — external or self-imposed — create urgency that mobilises motivation. Without deadlines, tasks expand and are delayed. Deadlines are a motivational tool.

**Plain words** Deadlines are motivation's cattle prod — tasks expand to fill the time available and shrink when the time is fenced. Self-imposed deadlines work better than none, external ones better still. Build the fence yourself or let the world build it for you.

*For example:* The manuscript unwritten for two years, finished in six weeks when a real editor had a real date — same author, same book. The fence did the motivating.
## Psychology parallels

- **Deadlines and motivation**, deadlines increase motivation and completion (Ariely & Wertenbroch, 2002; Steel, 2007)
- **Precommitment deadlines**, self-imposed deadlines increase goal attainment (Ariely & Wertenbroch, 2002)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[Memento mori|Memento mori]]: time is finite** (Stoics, Marcus Aurelius, Meditations)
- **[[The urgency of time|The urgency of time]]** (Heidegger, Being and Time)
- **[[Carpe diem|Carpe diem]]** (Horace, Odes)

## Practice

- Use deadlines: set specific dates for tasks, use external accountability, create artificial constraints. Do not leave tasks open-ended; deadlines create urgency.
