> **Principle 30** — Learn that walking is underrated: simple, accessible, effective

Walking — simple, accessible, requiring no equipment — is one of the most underrated forms of exercise for mental health. It improves mood, reduces stress, supports creativity, and is sustainable.

**Plain words** Walking is criminally underrated — no gear, no membership, no skill floor. Mood, stress, creativity, health: it moves all of them. The greatest exercise programme ever devised is the one you already know how to do.

*For example:* The walking meeting that solves what the conference room couldn't — the ideas arrive at about the second kilometre. The legs are part of the thinking apparatus.
## Psychology parallels

- **Walking and mental health**, walking reduces stress, improves mood, and supports creativity (Oppezzo & Schwartz, 2014; Hanson & Jones, 2015)
- **Walking and creativity**, walking (especially outdoors) enhances creative thinking (Oppezzo & Schwartz, 2014)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[The walking philosopher|The walking philosopher]]** (Aristotle, peripatetic school)
- **Walking as meditation** (Thich Nhat Hanh)
- **Walking in nature** (Thoreau, Walking)

## Practice

- Walk more: daily walks (especially in nature), walking meetings, walking meditation. Walking is simple, accessible, and effective. Do not underestimate it.
