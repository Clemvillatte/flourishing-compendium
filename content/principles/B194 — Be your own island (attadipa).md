> **Principle 194** — Be your own island (attadipa)

In the end, take refuge in yourself: stand on your own practice, your own seeing, your own ethical ground: everything else is support, not footing.

(Mahaparinibbana Sutta, DN 16.)

**Plain words** In the end, take refuge in yourself — stand on your own practice, your own seeing, your own ethical ground. Everything else is support, not footing. Teachers, books, communities: scaffolding. The building is yours.

*For example:* The crisis that hits when the teacher is unreachable, the book is in storage — and you find your practice actually holds weight.
