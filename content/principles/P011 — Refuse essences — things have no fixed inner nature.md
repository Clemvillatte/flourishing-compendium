> **Principle 11** — Refuse essences: things have no fixed inner nature

Categories are tools, not mirrors of eternal essences: "game," "self," "health" are family-resemblance clusters, not natural kinds with borders. Use concepts loosely and flexibly.

(Later Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations, 1953; Nietzsche, The Gay Science, 1882.)

**Plain words** Categories are tools, not mirrors of eternal essences — 'game,' 'self,' 'health' are family-resemblance clusters, not natural kinds with borders. Use concepts loosely and flexibly. A concept that fits every case like a glove fits no case at all.

*For example:* Arguing whether chess, solitaire, and tag are 'really' games — the argument dissolves when you notice 'game' was never a bordered category.
