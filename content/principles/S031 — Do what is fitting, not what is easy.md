> **Principle 153** — Do what is fitting, not what is easy

The Stoic does not shrink from difficulty when duty calls. The right action is often the hard one: the honest conversation, the refusal to participate in injustice, the work that no one will notice. Do what is fitting because it is fitting, not because it is rewarded.

(Med. II.5, VI.12, VII.15, IX.6; Disc. III.24; Seneca, Letters 20, 71, 104.)

**Plain words** The Stoic doesn't shrink from difficulty when duty calls. The right action is often the hard one: the honest conversation, the refusal to join an injustice, the work no one will notice. Do it because it's fitting, not because it's rewarded.

*For example:* Telling your team the project is behind schedule, rather than letting it slide until someone else finds out.
