> **Principle 124** — Meet suchness: things as they are

In the seen, only the seen; in the heard, only the heard: drop the commentary and meet what is actually presenting itself.

(Bahiya Sutta, Ud 1.10; tathata.)

**Plain words** In the seen, only the seen; in the heard, only the heard — drop the commentary and meet what is actually presenting itself. Suchness: the raw thing before the review board convenes. Most of life is spent reviewing instead of seeing.

*For example:* Actually tasting the food instead of rating the restaurant; actually hearing the friend instead of drafting your reply.
