> **Principle 156** — Engage the world's suffering (engaged Buddhism)

Meditation is not withdrawal from injustice: suffering has structural causes, and practice includes addressing them: nonviolently, together.

(Thích Nhất Hạnh, Interbeing; the Order of Interbeing's fourteen mindfulness trainings.)

**Plain words** Meditation is not withdrawal from injustice — suffering has structural causes, and practice includes addressing them: nonviolently, together. The lotus position is not an exit from history; it's a position from which to enter it, steadier.

*For example:* The practitioners who sit AND organize — calm in the meeting, useful in the world. Both, or it's just a hobby.
