> **Principle 417** — Practise spatiality of Dasein

Attend to the way space is experienced not as a geometric grid but as a field of significance. Your world is not made of neutral points in space but of meaningful places: near, far, here, there, home, away. The spatiality of your existence is existential, not mathematical.

(Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §§22-24; Merleau-Ponty, 1945/2012, Phenomenology of Perception, Part I, ch. 2.)

**Plain words** Space isn't experienced as a geometric grid but as a field of significance: near, far, here, home, away — meaningful places, not neutral points. The spatiality of your existence is existential, not mathematical. You don't live at coordinates; you live in a web of places.

*For example:* The same kilometre to the hospital feels different by the third day — the route has thickened with meaning, the parking spot has become "ours." No map software has ever rendered that layer.
