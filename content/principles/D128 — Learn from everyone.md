> **Principle 128** — Learn from everyone

Every person you meet is either a teacher or a mirror of your own faults: usually both. Waste no encounters.

(DDJ 27.)

**Plain words** The irritating person is free tuition; the impressive one is a map. Everyone you cross is data if you're collecting.

*For example:* The client who triggers you is showing you exactly where your patience is still under construction — useful, if humiliating.
