> **Principle 57** — Learn to use physical touch appropriately: touch is connection

Physical touch (appropriate, consensual) is a connection behaviour. Hugs, handshakes, and appropriate touch release oxytocin, reduce stress, and strengthen bonds.

**Plain words** Appropriate, consensual touch is connection hardware — a hug, a hand on the shoulder. It's regulated by context and consent, and it's a legitimate need. The handshake exists for a reason; so does the hug.

*For example:* The friend who asks "hug or handshake?" — consent handled in three words, connection delivered either way. The asking is part of the respect; the touch is part of the human.
## Psychology parallels

- **Touch and well-being (Field)**, appropriate touch reduces stress and releases oxytocin (Field, 2010; Holt-Lunstad et al., 2008)
- **Hugs and conflict**, hugging reduces conflict-related negative affect (Light et al., 2020; Ditzen et al., 2007)

## Practice

- Use appropriate touch: hug loved ones, shake hands, use appropriate physical contact. Touch is a connection behaviour (when consensual and appropriate).
