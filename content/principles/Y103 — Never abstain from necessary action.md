> **Principle 103** — Never abstain from necessary action

The Gita's hard line: no one remains actionless even for a moment, and the attempt to abstain from action while the body and the world demand it is self-deception; abstention from necessary action is also action, worse. Practically, when in doubt about whether to act, apply the test of necessity (does the situation require this of me?) before the test of desire (do I want to?).

(BG 3.4-3.8.)

**Plain words** The hard line: no one remains actionless even for a moment — and abstention from necessary action is also action, worse. When in doubt about whether to act, apply the necessary-action test before the contemplative one.

*For example:* The "above it all" colleague who abstains from the toxic meeting as a spiritual act — and the toxicity runs the meeting unopposed. The abstention was an action, all right; the tradition just grades it lower.
