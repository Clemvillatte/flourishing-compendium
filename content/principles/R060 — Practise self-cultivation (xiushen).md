> **Principle 322** — Practise self-cultivation (xiushen)

Make the cultivation of your own moral character your primary project. All social and political improvement begins with self-cultivation. As The Great Learning states: from the extension of knowledge, the will becomes sincere; from the sincerity of the will, the mind becomes rectified; from the rectified mind, the person is cultivated; from the cultivated person, the family is regulated; from the regulated family, the state is governed; from the governed state, the world is at peace.

(The Great Learning; Confucius, The Analects 1.4, 12.1; [[The doctrine of the mean|The Doctrine of the Mean]], ch. 1.)

**Plain words** Xiushen: make your own character the primary project. The Great Learning's cascade — sincere will, rectified mind, cultivated self, regulated family, governed state — starts at step one, and step one is you. Every improvement you want out there begins as work in here.

*For example:* The coach who spends the off-season on their own temper — and finds the team's discipline improves without a single new rule being issued.
