> **Principle 92** — Find joy in learning

The exercise of the intellect is naturally pleasant. Learning is not a chore but a form of flourishing. Curiosity, the desire to know, is a basic human drive. Follow it. Do not let it die under the weight of obligation.

(NE IV.7, X.7; Metaphysics I.1.)

**Plain words** Using your mind is naturally pleasant. Aristotle treats learning as part of flourishing, not a chore. Curiosity is a basic drive. Follow it, and don't let it be buried under obligations.

*For example:* A person who spends an evening reading about birdsong, for no reason, is doing something very human.
