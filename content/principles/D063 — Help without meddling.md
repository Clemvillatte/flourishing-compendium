> **Principle 63** — Help without meddling

Assist the ten thousand things but don't dare impose on them. Support people's growth the way rain supports a plant: conditions, not commands.

(DDJ 64.)

**Plain words** Assist the ten thousand things but don't impose on them. Support people's growth the way rain supports a plant: conditions, not commands. Help that comes with instructions isn't help — it's remote control with extra steps.

*For example:* Watering the seedling instead of pulling it upward to make it grow faster — you provide, nature decides.

## Psychology parallels

- **Autonomy support**, support that honors choice yields intrinsic motivation and growth
- **Control yields compliance or rebellion** (Deci and Ryan, 2000)

- **[[I and Thou|I-Thou versus I-It]]** (Buber, I and Thou)
