> **Principle 45** — Let practice and enlightenment be one

Do not treat practice as a means and awakening as a future reward: the very act of wholehearted sitting is itself the expression of awakening. Practice is not in order to arrive: it is arriving.

(Dogen, Genjokoan.)

**Plain words** Don't treat practice as a means and awakening as a future reward — the very act of wholehearted sitting is itself the expression of awakening. Practice is not in order to arrive; it is arriving. The journey IS the destination, which is either nonsense or the whole point.

*For example:* The dancer absorbed in the step isn't dancing toward a certificate — the dancing is the thing, complete in each measure.
