> **Principle 54** — Practice equanimity (upekkha)

Cultivate an even-minded balance toward all experience: pleasure and pain, gain and loss, praise and blame — so that the mind remains steady under the worldly winds.

(Brahmavihara tradition; AN 8.6.)

**Plain words** Cultivate an even-minded balance toward all experience — pleasure and pain, gain and loss, praise and blame — so the mind remains steady under the worldly winds. Equanimity isn't coldness; it's a boat that doesn't capsize in either direction.

*For example:* The trader who's calm on both the green and red days — the winds blew, the boat stayed level.
