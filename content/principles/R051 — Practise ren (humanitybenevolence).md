> **Principle 313** — Practise ren (humanity/benevolence)

Cultivate the virtue of humanity, the disposition to love and care for others. Ren is the supreme Confucian virtue, the ground of all other virtues. Practise it by wishing for the flourishing of others and working to bring it about.

(Confucius, The Analects 12.22; Mencius, The Mencius 2A6; [[The doctrine of the mean|The Doctrine of the Mean]], ch. 20.)

**Plain words** Ren is humanity: the disposition to genuinely wish for others' flourishing and then work for it. It's not niceness — it's the root virtue everything else grows from. Practise it by wanting good things for people, out loud, with your hands.

*For example:* The colleague who hears about a rival's promotion and notices the pang — then deliberately congratulates them properly, and means it. The sprout gets watered either way; only one watering is ren.
