> **Principle 132** — Rest in not one, not two (nonduality)

The seer and the seen are not two: awareness and its contents arise together. Stop looking for a watcher apart from the watching.

(Zen nonduality; Huayan influence.)

**Plain words** The seer and the seen are not two — awareness and its contents arise together. Stop looking for a watcher apart from the watching. The audience you've been seeking a refund for was never separate from the show.

*For example:* The moment you stop trying to observe your own dancing and just dance — the watcher dissolves into the watching.
