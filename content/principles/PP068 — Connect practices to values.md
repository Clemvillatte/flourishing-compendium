> **Principle 68** — Connect practices to values

A practice that is connected to a value is more sustainable than one that is pursued for its own sake. Mindfulness practised as a way of being present for one's children is more durable than mindfulness practised because it is supposed to reduce stress. The connection is not motivational decoration. It is the mechanism through which the practice becomes meaningful.

(Sheldon et al., 2010; Ryan and Deci, 2017.)

**Plain words** A practice tied to a value lasts longer than one pursued for its own sake. Being present for your children will carry you further than "reducing stress" will. The value is the engine, not decoration.

*For example:* The parent who meditates so he can actually listen at dinner keeps going when the novelty dies.
