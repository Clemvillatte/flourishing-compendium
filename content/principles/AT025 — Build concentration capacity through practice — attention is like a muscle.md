> **Principle 25** — Build concentration capacity through practice: attention is like a muscle

Concentration — the ability to sustain focused attention on a single object or task — is a trainable capacity. It improves with deliberate practice, just as physical strength improves with exercise. Short sessions build capacity gradually.

**Plain words** Concentration is a muscle: it grows with load, shrinks with disuse, and can't be borrowed from tomorrow. Small daily sessions of sustained focus, gently extended, do more than heroic all-nighters ever will.

*For example:* Start with 25 focused minutes, like a beginner at the gym — nobody walks in and deadlifts 200kg. The brain's the same gym, same rules.
## Psychology parallels

- **Focused attention meditation improves concentration**, regular practice improves sustained attention and reduces mind-wandering (Lutz et al., 2008; Tang et al., 2015)
- **Concentration improves with practice duration and consistency**, brief daily practice over weeks produces measurable improvements (Zanesco et al., 2016; Mrazek et al., 2013)

## Practice

- Practise sustained attention daily. Start with 10-15 minutes of focused attention on a single object (breath, candle, task). When mind wanders, return. Build gradually.
