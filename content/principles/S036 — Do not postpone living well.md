> **Principle 158** — Do not postpone living well

Seneca warns that life is not short; we waste most of it. The time to live well is now, not tomorrow. Do not wait for retirement, for the project to finish, for the children to grow. Practise the philosophy today, in this hour, with the materials at hand.

(Seneca, On the Shortness of Life 1, 7, 10; Med. II.4, IV.17, VII.29; Disc. I.1, I.4.)

**Plain words** Seneca says life isn't short, we waste most of it. The time to live well is now, not after retirement, the project, or when the children are grown. Practise philosophy today, in this hour, with what's at hand.

*For example:* Calling a friend you've been meaning to call, now, rather than "when things calm down."
