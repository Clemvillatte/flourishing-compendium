> **Principle 423** — Practise radical freedom

Recognise that you are radically free — not free from all constraints, but free in the sense that you are responsible for what you make of your situation. You did not choose to be born, but once you exist, every response to your situation is yours. Do not flee from this freedom; own it.

(Sartre, 1943/2003, Being and Nothingness, Introduction and Part IV; Sartre, 1946/2007, Existentialism Is a Humanism.)

**Plain words** You are radically free — not free from all constraints, but free in this sense: you are responsible for what you make of your situation. You didn't choose to be born; once you exist, every response is yours. Don't flee from that freedom into "I had no choice."

*For example:* The two people from the same sinking department: one spends the year in "what could I do?" and the other spends it retraining, applying, grieving, moving. The situation was identical; the freedom was the difference.
