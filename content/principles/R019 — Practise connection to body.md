> **Principle 281** — Practise connection to body

In the social and emotional wellbeing (SEWB) model, connection to body is one of seven linked domains, alongside mind and emotions, family and kinship, community, culture, Country, and spirituality and ancestors ([AIFS summary of Gee et al. 2014](https://aifs.gov.au/resources/policy-and-practice-papers/aboriginal-and-torres-strait-islander-perspectives-what-works)). Gee and colleagues' chapter defines it this way: "Connection to body is about physical wellbeing and includes all of the normal biological markers and indices that reflect the physical health of a person (i.e. age, weight, nutrition, illness and disability, mortality)" ([Gee et al. chapter PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/67e62754edf59f4f81c47f86/t/6809ba25cead453d0af9a8c3/1745467954129/Gee+SEWB.pdf)).

The 2017 national framework describes the domain as "feeling strong and healthy and able to physically participate as fully as possible in life" and lists exercise, access to healthy food and access to culturally safe health services among protective factors ([National framework 2017, NIAA](https://www.niaa.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/publications/mhsewb-framework_0.pdf); [Queensland Mental Health Commission paper](https://info.qmhc.qld.gov.au/hubfs/PAPER%208_Social%20and%20emotional%20wellbeing%20(SEWB).pdf?hsLang=en-au)). The framework treats SEWB as "the foundation for physical and mental health" (NIAA).

(Aboriginal SEWB framework; Social Health Reference Group, 2004.)

### Scope

The seven-domain wording above comes from Gee et al. and the 2017 framework, which postdate the 2004 framework the seed names, and I did not open the 2004 document itself. I found no source saying that rest or sleep belong to this domain. The domains are interconnected and not meant to be separate checklist items.

**Plain words**
In this framework, caring for the body means feeling strong, healthy and able to take part in life, and it is tied to mind, family, community and Country.

**For example**
Pick one protective factor from the framework, such as regular exercise or access to healthy food, and keep it for a month.
