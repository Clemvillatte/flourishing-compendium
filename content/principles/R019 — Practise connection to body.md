> **Principle 281** — Practise connection to body

Care for your physical body as an essential dimension of wellbeing. Physical health, nutrition, exercise, and rest are not separate from mental and spiritual health.

(Aboriginal SEWB framework; Social Health Reference Group, 2004.)

**Plain words** Care for the body as a dimension of wellbeing, not a separate errand. Sleep, food, movement, rest — these are mental-health and spiritual infrastructure too. The body isn't the vehicle; it's half the passenger.

*For example:* The person whose mood kept being blamed on their job — until a stretch of real sleep and regular meals showed the job was only half the story.
