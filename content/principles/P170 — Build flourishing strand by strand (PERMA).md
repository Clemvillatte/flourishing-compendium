> **Principle 170** — Build flourishing strand by strand (PERMA)

Well-being is a weave of positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment: build each strand deliberately; measure the whole.

(Seligman, Flourish, 2011.)

**Plain words** The good life isn't one thread pulled hard; it's five threads, each getting some intentional weaving time. Well-being is a weave of positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment — build each strand, measure the whole.

*For example:* The life audit: great career, no friendships — one strand at 100 percent, the weave failing. The fix isn't more career. It's a standing Tuesday dinner. Strand repair.
