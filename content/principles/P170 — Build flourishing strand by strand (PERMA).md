> **Principle 170** — Build flourishing strand by strand (PERMA)

In Flourish, Seligman shifts the target from happiness to well-being made of five elements, each valued for its own sake: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment. He names flourishing as the standard for measurement, the thing positive psychology tries to measure and increase. (Seligman, Flourish, chapter 1.)

> "Flourishing rests on five pillars, each of which we value for its own sake, not merely as a means to some other end." (Seligman, Flourish, ch. 1, excerpt at [Authentic Happiness, University of Pennsylvania](https://www.authentichappiness.sas.upenn.edu/newsletters/flourishnewsletters/newtheory))

> "These elements, which we choose for their own sake in our efforts to flourish, are the rock-bottom fundamentals to human well-being. What is the good life? It is pleasant, engaged, meaningful, achieving, and connected." (Seligman, Flourish, ch. 1, excerpt at [Authentic Happiness, University of Pennsylvania](https://www.authentichappiness.sas.upenn.edu/newsletters/flourishnewsletters/newtheory))

> "I believe the gold standard for measuring well-being is flourishing, and that the goal of positive psychology is to increase flourishing." (Seligman, Flourish, ch. 1, excerpt at [Authentic Happiness, University of Pennsylvania](https://www.authentichappiness.sas.upenn.edu/newsletters/flourishnewsletters/newtheory))

The compendium's exercise: rate each of the five strands for the past week, take the lowest one, and choose a single small act for that strand alone.

**Plain words**
Feeling good, being absorbed, people, purpose, and finishing things are the five strands. Find your weakest and give it an hour.

*For example:* Imagine your week is full of absorbing work but you have seen no one outside it. You schedule one dinner with a friend, treating it as a strand of the weave.
