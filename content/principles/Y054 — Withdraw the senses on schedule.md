> **Principle 54** — Withdraw the senses on schedule

Pratyahara is defined as the senses' imitation of the mind: when the mind withdraws, the senses withdraw with it, rather than the mind following the senses out. The practice is a withdrawal of investment, on schedule, before exhaustion enforces it. Practically, schedule one sense-fast per day: twenty minutes with no input, eyes closed or on a blank wall, phone out of reach.

(YS 2.54-2.55.)

**Plain words** Sense withdrawal is defined as the senses' imitation of the mind: when the mind withdraws, the senses follow — not the other way around. The practice is a withdrawal of investment, on schedule, before exhaustion enforces it. Pull out at closing time, not at collapse.

*For example:* Someone whose evenings end with the phone dropping from a numb hand at 1:40 a.m. — versus the one who schedules the withdrawal at 10, lights low, screens parked, while the mind still has a vote.
