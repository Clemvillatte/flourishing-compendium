> **Principle 124** — Practice both-and where you can

Where others see forced choices: freedom or security, self or others, work or life — look for the "and." Duality is often a failure of imagination.

(James, A Pluralistic Universe, 1909; Bernstein, Beyond Objectivism and Relativism, 1983.)

**Plain words** Where others see forced choices — freedom or security, self or others, work or life — look for the 'and.' Duality is often a failure of imagination. Most either/or questions were written by someone who couldn't imagine a both.

*For example:* 'Do I take the stable job or follow my passion?' — the person who does the stable job that funds the pottery studio answers by living. The question was badly drafted.
