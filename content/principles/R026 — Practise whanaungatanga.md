> **Principle 288** — Practise whanaungatanga

Build and maintain relationships of kinship and connection. Whanaungatanga is not merely biological kinship but the active practice of relationship-building through shared experience, mutual obligation, and care. Prioritise relationship-building in every domain of your life.

(Māori tikanga; Durie, 1994, Whaiora: Māori Health Development; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori: Living by Māori Cultural Values.)

**Plain words** Whanaungatanga is relationship as a verb: kinship and connection built through shared experience, mutual obligation, and care — not just the family you're born with. Prioritise building relationship everywhere: work, street, team. The connection is the asset.

*For example:* The new hire who spends their first month learning everyone's names, kids, and stories — and by June is the person others describe as "the reason this floor works."
