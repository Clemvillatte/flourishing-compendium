> **Principle 154** — Care for the community of life

Process thought's ecological conclusion: value is present wherever there is experience, and the human good is continuous with the living world. Treat the biosphere as a stakeholder in your choices.

(Cobb and Griffin, Process Theology, 1976: secularized; Whitehead's value realism.)

**Plain words** Value is present wherever there is experience, and the human good is continuous with the living world. Treat the biosphere as a stakeholder in your choices. The frog in the creek and your grandkid are on the same committee; it just meets slowly.

*For example:* The building decision that weighs the wetland — not as decoration but as a party with standing. Slow committee, long minutes, and your name in them either way.
