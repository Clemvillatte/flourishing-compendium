> **Principle 101** — Work as offering

The Gita's yajna framework: action dedicated as offering rather than performed as acquisition, with the world sustained by the wheel of mutual giving. The bounded formulation removes the ritual: dedicate the work (to a person, a craft, a generation) before beginning it, and let the dedication steady the doing. Practically, open each workday with one sentence naming whom the work serves.

(BG 3.9-3.16.)

**Plain words** Work as offering: action dedicated rather than performed as acquisition — the ritual removed, the dedication kept. Dedicate the work (to a person, a craft, a generation) before beginning it, and let the dedication do what the self's lien used to do.

*For example:* The same shift on the ward, dedicated at the start to the patients in it — and the hours run differently from the same hours run as acquisition. Nothing external changed; the frame did.
