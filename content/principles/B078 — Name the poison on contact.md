> **Principle 78** — Name the poison on contact

Learn to spot greed, hatred, and delusion as they arise: not after they have shaped your actions. The moment of naming is the moment of freedom.

(MN 2; Dhammapada 3.)

**Plain words** Learn to spot greed, hatred, and delusion as they arise — not after they've shaped your actions. The moment of naming is the moment of freedom. A named poison is a managed poison; an unnamed one runs the kitchen.

*For example:* 'That's greed talking' — the half-second it takes to say it is the difference between the impulse and the purchase.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Affect labeling|Affect labeling]]**, reduces amygdala response (Lieberman et al., 2007)
- **[[Emotion awareness|Emotion awareness]]**, predicts regulation (Barrett et al., 2001)
