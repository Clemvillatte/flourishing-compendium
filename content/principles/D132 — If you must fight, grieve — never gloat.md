> **Principle 132** — If you must fight, grieve: never gloat

When forced to conflict, win modestly: the victor who celebrates delights in killing, and one who delights in killing cannot win the world.

(DDJ 31, 30.)

**Plain words** When forced to conflict, win modestly. The victor who celebrates delights in killing, and one who delights in killing cannot win the world — gloating converts a victory into a permanent enemy. Grace in winning is a strategy, not a nicety.

*For example:* The candidate who wins the election and mocks the losers' supporters — the rematch four years later is now personal.
