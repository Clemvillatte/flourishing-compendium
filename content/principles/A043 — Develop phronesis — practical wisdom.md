> **Principle 43** — Develop phronesis: practical wisdom

Phronesis is the capacity to deliberate well about what is good and expedient for a human life, in particular circumstances, with respect to particular facts. It is not theoretical knowledge. It is the trained ability to see what this situation requires and to act accordingly. Cultivate it through experience, reflection, and the study of exemplary lives.

(NE VI.5, VI.7-8.)

**Plain words** Phronesis is knowing what this situation, with these people, actually calls for. It's no textbook, but it can be trained: through experience, reflection, and watching people who do it well. Pay attention to how good judges handle the details.

*For example:* A senior doctor who tells a patient the truth gently, at the right pace, isn't following a script. She's reading the person.
