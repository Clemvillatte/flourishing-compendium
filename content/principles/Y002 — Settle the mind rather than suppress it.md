> **Principle 2** — Settle the mind rather than suppress it

Yoga Sūtra 1.2 defines yoga in terms of restricting the mind's fluctuations. Sūtra 1.12 names practice and passionlessness as its means, while 1.13 describes practice as effort directed toward maintaining the restricted state.

For a compendium exercise, set aside a brief sitting period. Afterwards, record how you responded when attention wandered and which instructions you gave yourself.

> Yoga is the restriction of the fluctuations of mind-stuff.
>
> Patañjali, Yoga Sūtra 1.2, translated by James Haughton Woods (1914), [reprinted text](https://www.theessentialway.org/yoga-sutras/).

**Plain words**
Give yourself a short period to sit. When you notice attention wandering, observe what you do next. Afterwards, write down whether you repeated instructions, returned to your chosen focus, or ended the session.

**For example**
In an imagined exercise, a beginning meditator who has been silently repeating "stop thinking" notices the repetition, drops the command, and returns attention to the breath for the remainder of the sitting.
