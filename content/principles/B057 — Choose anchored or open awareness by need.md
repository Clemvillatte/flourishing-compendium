> **Principle 57** — Choose anchored or open awareness by need

When scattered, anchor attention on breath or body; when settled, open to the whole field. Neither is superior: match the tool to the state.

(MN 118; Dogen.)

**Plain words** When scattered, anchor attention on breath or body; when settled, open to the whole field. Neither is superior — match the tool to the state. The expert isn't the person with one tool; it's the person who knows which tool today needs.

*For example:* Anchoring hard on the breath before the difficult conversation, then letting awareness open wide during it — two tools, one afternoon.
