> **Principle 50** — Practise tolerance of uncertainty

The capacity to tolerate not knowing what will happen, without rushing to certainty, prediction, or distraction, is a trainable skill. Intolerance of uncertainty predicts anxiety, worry, and avoidance. The practice is not about embracing uncertainty as a philosophy but about building the capacity to sit with the discomfort of not knowing.

(Dugas et al., 2004; Carleton, 2012.)

**Plain words** Tolerating uncertainty means sitting with not knowing, without rushing to prediction, certainty or distraction. Intolerance of uncertainty drives worry and avoidance. The skill is built by staying with the discomfort a little longer each time.

*For example:* Waiting for medical results without refreshing the portal every ten minutes.
