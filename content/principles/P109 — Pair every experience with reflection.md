> **Principle 109** — Pair every experience with reflection

Experience alone does not teach; it is experience plus reflection on experience that educates. Close every significant experience with a reflective pass: what happened, what did I do, what follows?

(Dewey, How We Think, 1910; Democracy and Education, 1916.)

**Plain words** Experience alone does not teach — it's experience plus reflection on experience that educates. Close every significant experience with a reflective pass: what happened, what did I do, what follows? The unexamined decade is the repeated year, ten times.

*For example:* The twenty arguments that all ended the same way — until one was autopsied in a journal. Argument twenty-one ended differently. Reflection is the difference between a loop and a spiral.
