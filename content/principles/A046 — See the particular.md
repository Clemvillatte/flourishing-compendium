> **Principle 46** — See the particular

Phronesis concerns particulars, and perception of particulars is the work of a trained eye. Universal rules are necessary but insufficient. The practically wise person sees what is called for here, now, with these people, in this situation. Train your perception of the concrete.

(NE VI.8, VI.11.)

**Plain words** Rules help, but they stop short of the real case. The wise person sees what's called for here, now, with these people. That perception is trained. So practise noticing the concrete details, not just applying the general principle.

*For example:* Telling a nervous new hire "great work" might be right. Noticing she needs a specific, honest correction instead is the trained eye at work.
