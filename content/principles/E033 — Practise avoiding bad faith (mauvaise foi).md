> **Principle 425** — Practise avoiding bad faith (mauvaise foi)

Sartre's bad faith (mauvaise foi) is a lie to oneself rather than a lie to others: a single consciousness hiding something from itself ([Being and Nothingness, Part I, ch. 2, Barnes translation; opened full text](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt)). It is a flight from anguish, and it works by denying one of the two poles of the human condition, treating oneself as a fixed thing or as pure unconstrained freedom ([same opened text](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt)). His examples are the café waiter who exaggerates his waiter-ness into a mechanism, and the woman on a date who postpones the decision about her hand:

> He is playing, he is amusing himself. But what is he playing? We need not watch long before we can explain it: he is playing at being a waiter in a café.

(Sartre, Being and Nothingness, trans. Hazel E. Barnes, 1956, Part I, ch. 2; [opened full text, Internet Archive](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt).)

Sartre calls bad faith a permanent threat rather than an occasional accident ([same opened text](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt)). The practice is to notice which pole you are currently dropping and to hold both: the constraints that are real, and the choices they leave open.

### Plain words

Bad faith is telling yourself a story that removes your choice. "I had no choice" treats habit as a law of nature; "I can become anyone by Friday" treats the past as nothing. Each drops half of the situation. The check is simple to state and hard to run: which half am I dropping right now?

### For example

A person who has said "I'm just not a math person" for twenty years can test the sentence against the actual range of moves left open: the courses not taken, the help not asked for. A person planning a total self-reinvention by Friday can test it against the actual record: the habits, debts and relationships that will still be there. The honest position keeps both lists.
