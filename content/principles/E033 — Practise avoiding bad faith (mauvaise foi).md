> **Principle 425** — Practise avoiding bad faith (mauvaise foi)

Sartre's bad faith (mauvaise foi) is a lie to oneself rather than a lie to others: a single consciousness hiding something from itself ([Being and Nothingness, Part I, ch. 2, Barnes translation; opened full text](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt)). It is a flight from anguish, and it works by denying one of the two poles of the human condition, treating oneself as a fixed thing or as pure unconstrained freedom ([same opened text](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt)). His examples are the café waiter who exaggerates his waiter-ness into a mechanism, and the woman on a date who postpones the decision about her hand ([same opened text](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt)):

> He is playing, he is amusing himself. But what is he playing? We need not watch long before we can explain it: he is playing at being a waiter in a café.

(Sartre, Being and Nothingness, trans. Hazel E. Barnes, 1956, Part I, ch. 2; [opened full text, Internet Archive](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt).)

Sartre calls bad faith a permanent threat rather than an occasional accident ([same opened text](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.6651/2015.6651.Being-And-Nothingness_djvu.txt)). The practice is to notice which pole you are currently dropping and to hold both: the constraints that are real, and the choices they leave open.

### Plain words

Bad faith is a story that ends your choosing. The check to run: does this explanation treat a constraint as my whole identity, or wave off a constraint I actually face? Either move drops half of the situation.

### For example

Suppose someone has said for years that they are not a math person. The sentence could name a real difficulty or could be doing bad-faith work; the difference shows in what they have actually tried, not in how settled the sentence feels. Naming a real limitation is not bad faith.
