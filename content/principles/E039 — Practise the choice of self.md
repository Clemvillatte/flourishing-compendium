> **Principle 431** — Practise the choice of self

Recognise that at the deepest level, you choose who you are. This is not a one-time decision but the ongoing structure of your existence. Every choice you make is a choice of yourself. Do not wait to discover who you are; choose who you will be.

(Sartre, 1943/2003, Being and Nothingness, Part IV, ch. 2; Sartre, 1946/2007, Existentialism Is a Humanism.)

**Plain words** At the deepest level, you choose who you are — not once, but as the ongoing structure of your existence. Every choice is a choice of yourself. Don't wait to discover who you are; choose who you'll be, then keep choosing it.

*For example:* The person waiting for their "real self" to emerge like a photo developing — versus the one who treats each decision as a vote for a self, and watches the votes accumulate. Discovery was never on the menu; there's only election day, every day.
