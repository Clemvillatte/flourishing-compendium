> **Principle 166** — The farther out you go, the less you know

Don't exhaust yourself gathering external data: know the world by knowing yourself; the sage knows without going out the door.

(DDJ 47.)

**Plain words** The farther out you go, the less you know. Don't exhaust yourself gathering external data — know the world by knowing yourself; the sage knows without going out the door. The pattern-recognition machine you already own is you; feed it you.

*For example:* The traveler who's been everywhere and knows themselves nowhere — versus the person who went deep in one place and understood all places.
