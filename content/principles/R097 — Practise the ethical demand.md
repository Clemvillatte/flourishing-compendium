> **Principle 359** — Practise the ethical demand

Recognise that the ethical demand is unconditional and absolute. It does not depend on reciprocity, on the Other's merit, or on the consequences of your action. The demand of the Other is immediate and non-negotiable.

(Levinas, 1961/1969, Totality and Infinity; Løgstrup, 1997, The Ethical Demand.)

**Plain words** The ethical demand is unconditional: it doesn't wait for reciprocity, merit, or good consequences. The other's need arrives as a claim before you've had a chance to negotiate terms. Not a bill — a fact about being human.

*For example:* The stranger whose car is in the ditch at midnight — no time to check whether they "deserve" help; the demand is immediate, and the stopped cars know it.
