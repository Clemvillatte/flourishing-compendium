> **Principle 76** — When habits conflict, let deliberation do its work

Conflicting habits create the hesitation we call deliberation: don't force a snap resolution: let the competing tendencies play out in imagination until one integrates the situation.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922.)

**Plain words** The stall isn't dysfunction; it's the machine doing its work. Conflicting habits create the hesitation we call deliberation — let the competing tendencies play out in imagination until one integrates the situation.

*For example:* The job offer tug-of-war — ambition pulling one way, family the other. Instead of flipping a coin: live each choice vividly for a week. One of them starts gathering the others into it. That's the decision, forming.
