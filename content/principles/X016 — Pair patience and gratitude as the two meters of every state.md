> **Principle 16** — Pair patience and gratitude as the two meters of every state

The tradition's instruction: in hardship, practice sabr (patient endurance without complaint's inner collapse); in ease, practice shukr (gratitude that uses the ease rather than merely consuming it); and check, in every state, that the fitting meter is running. The pairing is the distinct technology: two states, two instruments, no gaps. Practically, at each state change (good news, bad news), name the fitting meter aloud and switch practices.

(The sabr-shukr pairing across the tradition; Quran's own pairing of the two.)

**Plain words** Two meters, one for each weather: hardship calls for sabr — endurance without the inner collapse of complaint; ease calls for shukr — gratitude that uses the good rather than merely consuming it. At every state change, name the fitting meter and switch. No state is left uncovered.

*For example:* The same person in one week gets a diagnosis and a bonus — and runs patience on Monday's news, gratitude on Friday's, instead of grinding the wrong tool through both.
