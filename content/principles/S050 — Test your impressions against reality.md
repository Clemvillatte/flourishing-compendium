> **Principle 172** — Test your impressions against reality

When you have an impression, check it. Is the person really insulting you, or are they having a bad day? Is the situation really hopeless, or are you catastrophising? The test is not introspection but observation.

(Disc. I.20, I.28; Med. IV.7, VI.13, XI.18; Seneca, De Ira III.36.)

**Plain words** When you have an impression, check it against the world. Is the person really insulting you, or having a bad day? Is the situation hopeless, or are you catastrophising? The test is observation, not more introspection.

*For example:* Thinking your boss is angry, you notice she was like that with everyone this morning.
