> **Principle 50** — Rest in ordinary mind: it is the Way

Nothing special is required: the ordinary mind, eating when hungry and sleeping when tired, is already the Way. Stop seeking elsewhere.

(Zhaozhou; Xinxin Ming; Layman Pang.)

**Plain words** Nothing special is required — the ordinary mind, eating when hungry and sleeping when tired, is already the Way. Stop seeking elsewhere. The treasure hunt through exotic states is the mind's way of avoiding the living room.

*For example:* The years spent chasing retreats and techniques, while the deepest peace kept being found in the morning tea.
