> **Principle 48** — Recognise anxiety's core: intolerance of uncertainty

Anxiety is not just fear of specific threats; it is a broader response to uncertainty and unpredictability. Intolerance of uncertainty is a core maintaining factor in generalized anxiety disorder and is a direct target of treatment.

**Plain words** Anxiety's core is often not fear of specific threats but intolerance of uncertainty — a broader response to unpredictability itself. Target the uncertainty tolerance directly: small doses of not-knowing, tolerated on purpose, retrain the system.

*For example:* The person who checks the tracking, the forecast, the reviews — everything — to eliminate the not-knowing, and the paradox that each check feeds the intolerance. The treatment is deliberately letting some things stay unknown: the text unanswered for an hour, survived.
## Psychology parallels

- **Intolerance of uncertainty is a core mechanism of GAD**, difficulty tolerating uncertainty maintains worry (Dugas et al., 1998; Carleton, 2016)
- **Worry as cognitive avoidance**, worry is a verbal-linguistic activity that suppresses imagery and the physiological arousal of fear, negatively reinforcing the worry (Borkovec et al., 1983)

## Practice

- When you notice worry, ask: "am I trying to eliminate uncertainty by thinking?" Recognise that worry is an avoidance strategy, not a problem-solving strategy. Practise tolerating small uncertainties deliberately.
