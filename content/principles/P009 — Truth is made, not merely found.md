> **Principle 9** — Truth is made, not merely found

We know truly what we successfully make and do: verum factum. Understanding is a construction tested in action, not a copy of a ready-made world.

(Vico, Scienza Nuova, 1725/1744.)

**Plain words** We know truly what we successfully make and do — verum factio. Understanding is a construction tested in action, not a copy of a ready-made world. You don't learn the recipe by staring at the dish; you learn it by cooking.

*For example:* The student who can explain photosynthesis and the one who can keep a plant alive — only one of them knows anything yet.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Constructivist learning theory|Constructivist learning theory]]**, understanding is built through active construction, not passive reception (Piaget; Bruner)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[Verum factum|Verum factum]]** (Vico, Scienza Nuova)
- **Truth as verified in experience** (James, Pragmatism)- **[[Pragmatism|Pragmatism]]** (Pragmatism)
