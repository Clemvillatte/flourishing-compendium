> **Principle 136** — Want what actually happens

Do not wish for things to be other than they are. The person who wants reality to conform to their desires is perpetually frustrated. The person who wants reality as it is has no frustration. Practise the reframing: "this is what I wanted," meaning, "this is what nature has produced, and I accept it."

(Disc. I.12, I.17; Med. IV.10, VIII.50; Seneca, On Providence.)

**Plain words** Don't wish things were otherwise. The person who wants reality to match their desires is permanently frustrated, and the one who wants reality as it is has no frustration. The practice is to reframe: "this is what nature has produced, and I'm taking it."

*For example:* Rain on your wedding day. Wanting dry weather ruins it. Wanting this day, rained on, saves it.
