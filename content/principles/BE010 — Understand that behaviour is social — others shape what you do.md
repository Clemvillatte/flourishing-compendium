> **Principle 10** — Understand that behaviour is social: others shape what you do

Behaviour is not purely individual; it is shaped by others. Social norms, modelling, social support, and accountability all influence behaviour. Using social influence is a behaviour-change strategy.

**Plain words** Behaviour is contagious — the people around you are quietly setting your defaults: how much you drink, spend, move, worry. You didn't choose most of it; you absorbed it. Choose your exposure like it matters, because it's editing you.

*For example:* The friend group where everyone hikes — and within a year, you own hiking boots. Nobody ever "decided" anything. The group decided, and you came along.
## Psychology parallels

- **Social norms and behaviour (Cialdini)**, descriptive and injunctive norms shape behaviour (Cialdini et al., 1990; Cialdini, 2001)
- **Social support increases behaviour change**, supportive others increase behaviour change (Wing & Jeffery, 1999; Hogan et al., 2015)

## Practice

- Use social influence: surround yourself with people who do the behaviour you want, use accountability partners, and make commitments public. Do not rely solely on individual willpower.
