> **Principle 465** — Practise avoiding the adventurer

Recognise the temptation of the adventurer — the person who pursues action for its own sake, without commitment to any value or any other person. The adventurer is free but their freedom is empty; it is freedom without content. Do not confuse movement with purpose; let your freedom be directed toward meaning.

(de Beauvoir, 1947/2018, The Ethics of Ambiguity, Part II, ch. 3.)

**Plain words** The adventurer: action for its own sake, without commitment to any value or person. Free, but the freedom is empty — freedom without content. Don't confuse movement with purpose. The adventure is a mood, not a life.

*For example:* The serial starter — jobs, cities, relationships, passions — each abandoned at the exact point where commitment would begin. The résumé sparkles; the absence of anything that held weight for a decade is the tell.
