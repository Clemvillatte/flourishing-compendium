> **Principle 49** — Learn to remove distractions: environment drives procrastination

Procrastination is often driven by distraction (phone, internet, other tasks). Removing distractions (environment design) is more effective than willpower.

**Plain words** Distraction is procrastination's enabler — the phone is never the reason, but always the getaway car. Remove it: other room, other device, other hour. An undistracted mind has to face the task, which is most of the battle.

*For example:* The writer who works with the router unplugged — not because they lack discipline, but because they know the discipline will lose if the internet's in the room. It always does.
## Psychology parallels

- **Distraction and procrastination**, environmental distractions trigger procrastination (Pychyl, 2013; Steel, 2007)
- **Choice architecture**, designing environment to reduce distraction (Thaler & Sunstein, 2008; Milkman et al., 2011)

## Practice

- Remove distractions: phone in another room, notifications off, website blockers, clean workspace. Do not rely on willpower; design the environment.
