> **Principle 62** — Rest in the unborn (Bankei)

The mind that does not chase or fixate: the "unborn" — is already free; let thoughts come and go without following them into birth.

(Bankei; Zen tradition.)

**Plain words** The mind that doesn't chase or fixate — the 'unborn' — is already free; let thoughts come and go without following them into birth. Bankei's point: you don't have to kill thoughts, just decline the invitation to ride each one.

*For example:* Noticing the worry-thought arrive, bow, and leave — you stayed home. The thought went looking for a passenger and found none.
