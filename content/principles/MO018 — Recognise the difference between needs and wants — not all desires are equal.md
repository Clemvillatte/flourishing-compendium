> **Principle 18** — Recognise the difference between needs and wants: not all desires are equal

Needs (autonomy, competence, relatedness, health, safety) are fundamental and non-negotiable. Wants (specific goals, possessions, achievements) are negotiable and often substitute for needs. Confusing wants with needs creates unnecessary suffering.

**Plain words** Needs and wants are different animals — needs (safety, connection, competence, health) starve you when unmet; wants are negotiable. Most motivational chaos is a want being mistaken for a need. Sort the list.

*For example:* "I need this promotion" — usually means "I need security, and I want the promotion." Sorting that lets you find three other routes to security instead of white-knuckling one.

- **[[Distinguishing needs from wants (SDT)|Distinguishing needs from wants (SDT)]]**, psychological needs are universal; specific wants are shaped by culture (Deci & Ryan, 2000; Ryan & Deci, 2017)

## Practice

- When you notice a strong want, ask: "what need is underneath this?" Often a want for achievement, possessions, or approval is a substitute for unmet needs for autonomy, competence, or relatedness. Address the need.
