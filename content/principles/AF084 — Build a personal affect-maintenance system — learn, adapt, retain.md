> **Principle 84** — Build a personal affect-maintenance system: learn, adapt, retain

Affect regulation is not a one-time acquisition; it is an ongoing practice of learning what works for you, adapting as circumstances change, and retaining what works. This is the variation-selection-retention loop applied to your own affective life.

**Plain words** Affect regulation is not a one-time acquisition — it's an ongoing practice: learn what works for you, adapt as circumstances change, retain what works. Build a personal maintenance system: review, revise, keep the keepers.

*For example:* The person's regulation toolkit from their twenties (the gym, the parties) that quietly died in their forties — and the annual audit that rebuilds it: what still works, what's gone, what's new. The system survives by being a system, not a relic.
## Psychology parallels

- **[[Personalised medicine / personalised intervention|Personalised medicine / personalised intervention]]**, what works varies by person; monitoring and adapting is more effective than one-size-fits-all (Cheyne & Poulsen, 2019; Ferrer et al., 2020)
- **Maintenance and relapse prevention**, sustained practice prevents relapse; discontinuing practice predicts recurrence (Kuyken et al., 2015)

## Practice

- Keep a simple affect log. Try a regulation practice for 2 weeks; assess whether it works; retain what works, discard what doesn't. Repeat as circumstances change.
