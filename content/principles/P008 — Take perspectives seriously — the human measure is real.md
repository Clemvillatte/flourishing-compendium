> **Principle 8** — Take perspectives seriously: the human measure is real

Every view is a view from somewhere; the world is always met from a position. Don't seek the view from nowhere — seek more and better positions.

(Protagoras, via Plato, Theaetetus; James, A Pluralistic Universe, 1909.)

**Plain words** Every view is a view from somewhere — there is no camera position outside the world. Don't chase the fantasy of the neutral observer; it has delayed more good thinking than most biases. Collect positions instead.

*For example:* The financial plan that ignores the fact you have a body, a family, and a fear of risk — that's a view from nowhere, and it's worse than a view from somewhere honest.
