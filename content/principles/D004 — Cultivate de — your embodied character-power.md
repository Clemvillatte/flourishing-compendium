> **Principle 4** — Cultivate de: your embodied character-power

Virtue is not rule-following but a built-up inner capacity: the power that flows from being aligned with the Dao. Invest in who you are rather than in policing what you do.

(DDJ 51; Neiye.)

**Plain words** De isn't following rules — it's built-up inner capacity. Character you've actually grown, not behavior you police. Invest in who you are becoming rather than in managing how you appear, because power that comes from alignment doesn't need supervision.

*For example:* Someone who's genuinely practiced honesty for years doesn't agonize over whether to fudge a report — integrity has become their reflex, not their rule.
