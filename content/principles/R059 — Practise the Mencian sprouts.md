> **Principle 321** — Practise the Mencian sprouts

Nurture the four moral sentiments that are natural to human beings: the feeling of compassion (the sprout of ren), the feeling of shame and dislike (the sprout of yi), the feeling of deference and compliance (the sprout of li), and the feeling of right and wrong (the sprout of zhi). These are seeds; they must be cultivated to grow into full virtues.

(Mencius, The Mencius 2A6, 6A6, 6A10.)

**Plain words** Mencius says you were issued four moral sprouts at birth: compassion (grows into ren), shame-and-dislike (yi), deference (li), right-and-wrong (zhi). Seeds, not finished trees — the whole work is cultivation. Water them or they stay sprouts.

*For example:* Everyone at the scene of the accident feels the flinch toward the injured — that flinch is the sprout. The person who kneels down and helps is just the sprout, watered.
