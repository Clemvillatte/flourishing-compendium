> **Principle 89** — Intervene at intention

The karmic moment is not the action but the intention behind it: intervene at the level of wanting, and the actions take care of themselves.

(Dhammapada 1-2; SN 12.)

**Plain words** The karmic moment is not the action but the intention behind it — intervene at the level of wanting, and the actions take care of themselves. Work on the steering, not on rerouting each individual street.

*For example:* The diet that finally works when the intention shifts from 'look better' to 'feel good' — same foods, different engine.
