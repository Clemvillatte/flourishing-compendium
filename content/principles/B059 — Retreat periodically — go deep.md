> **Principle 59** — Retreat periodically: go deep

From time to time, step out of ordinary life into sustained practice: days or weeks of silence let the mind settle to depths daily life cannot reach.

([[Gradual training|Gradual training]]; retreat tradition.)

**Plain words** From time to time, step out of ordinary life into sustained practice — days or weeks of silence let the mind settle to depths daily life cannot reach. A retreat isn't an escape; it's a deep-clean of a house you actually live in.

*For example:* The first silent retreat: terrifying for two days, then a week of noticing the volume knob on your own mind.

## Psychology parallels

- **Intensive meditation training**, produces measurable changes in attention and well-being (MacLean et al., 2010; Davidson and McEwen, 2012)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[Leisure|Leisure as the basis of culture]]** (Pieper, Leisure: The Basis of Culture)
