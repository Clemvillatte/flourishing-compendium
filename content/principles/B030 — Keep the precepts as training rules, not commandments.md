> **Principle 30** — Keep the precepts as training rules, not commandments

The five precepts: non-harm, truthfulness, non-taking, sexual responsibility, sobriety — are rules of training you choose, bright lines that simplify decisions.

(AN 8.41; Dhammapada 248.)

**Plain words** The five precepts — non-harm, truthfulness, non-taking, sexual responsibility, sobriety — are training rules you choose, bright lines that simplify decisions. Not commandments from outside: personal guardrails that save you a thousand small nightly negotiations.

*For example:* The person who simply doesn't drink at weeknight work events anymore — one bright line replaces forty micro-decisions.
