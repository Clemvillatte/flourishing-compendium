> **Principle 156** — Let your actions be consistent with your words

Integrity is the alignment of speech and action. The Stoic does not say one thing and do another. This is not moralism but practical wisdom: inconsistency breeds inner conflict, and inner conflict breeds suffering. Practise saying less and doing more.

(Med. IV.16, V.9; Seneca, Letters 20, 75, 108; Disc. III.21.)

**Plain words** Integrity is when what you say matches what you do. The Stoic doesn't preach one thing and live another. It isn't moralism but practical sense: inconsistency breeds inner conflict and inner conflict breeds suffering. Say less, do more.

*For example:* Not telling your children to be honest, then being honest where they can see you.
