> **Principle 18** — Practice practical wisdom: judgment over rules

No rule or algorithm replaces discernment in context: phronesis is the trained capacity to see what the situation requires. Cultivate judgment; use rules as its instruments, not its substitutes.

(Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics, Book VI.)

**Plain words** No rule or algorithm replaces discernment in context — phronesis is the trained capacity to see what the situation requires. Cultivate judgment; use rules as its instruments, not its substitutes. The recipe is a bicycle; judgment rides it.

*For example:* The veteran nurse who ignores the protocol's letter and catches the sepsis the checklist missed — that's judgment, and it's why she's allowed near the checklist.
