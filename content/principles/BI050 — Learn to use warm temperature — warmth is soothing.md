> **Principle 50** — Learn to use warm temperature: warmth is soothing

Warmth (warm bath, warm drink, warm blanket, sunlight) activates the parasympathetic system and is soothing. Warmth is a specific physiological regulator.

**Plain words** Warmth is a physiological sedative — warm bath, warm drink, blanket, sunlight. It activates the soothing system directly, no thinking required. The cup of tea is not a cliché; it's a technique.

*For example:* The bad day that genuinely improves in the bath — not distraction, biology. Warmth is one of the oldest regulators; the grandmother knew.
## Psychology parallels

- **Warmth and parasympathetic activation**, warm temperature activates the parasympathetic system and reduces arousal (Craig, 2003; Venkatraman et al., 2018)
- **Warm bath for sleep**, warm baths before bed improve sleep (Haghayegh et al., 2019)

## Practice

- Use warmth for soothing: warm bath, warm drink (non-caffeinated), warm blanket, sunlight. Warmth is regulating and soothing. Use it for stress and sleep.
