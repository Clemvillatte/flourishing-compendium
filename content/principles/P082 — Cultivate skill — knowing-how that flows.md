> **Principle 82** — Cultivate skill: knowing-how that flows

Mastery is knowledge in the muscles and timing: the butcher's knife that follows the joints without forcing. Let skill develop until action flows with the grain of the situation.

(Zhuangzi, Inner Chapters; Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922.)

**Plain words** Mastery is knowledge in the muscles and timing — the butcher's knife that follows the joints without forcing. Let skill develop until action flows with the grain of the situation. Expertise isn't thinking faster; it's not needing to think at all.

*For example:* The veteran barista during the morning rush — no decisions, no strain, just the pour. That's years of joints, followed. Grace is accumulated.
