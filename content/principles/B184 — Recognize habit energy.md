> **Principle 184** — Recognize habit energy

Much of what you do is habit energy running its program: notice it: "ah, habit energy" — and smile at it before deciding whether to obey.

(Thích Nhất Hạnh, Understanding Our Mind.)

**Plain words** Much of what you do is habit energy running its program — notice it: 'ah, habit energy' — and smile at it before deciding whether to obey. The smile isn't weakness; it's the gap where choice lives.

*For example:* The hand reaching for the phone for no reason — 'ah, habit energy' — and for once, the hand just goes back to the lap.
