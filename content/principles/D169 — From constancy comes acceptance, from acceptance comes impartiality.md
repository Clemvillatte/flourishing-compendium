> **Principle 169** — From constancy comes acceptance, from acceptance comes impartiality

Daodejing 16 begins with active things returning to their root. Knowing the unchanging rule leads, in Legge's wording, to capacity and forbearance, then to a community of feeling with all things. This principle's title calls those links acceptance and impartiality; those are interpretive terms, not the words used in this translation.

(DDJ 16.)

> "The knowledge of that unchanging rule produces a (grand) capacity and forbearance, and that capacity and forbearance lead to a community (of feeling with all things). From this community of feeling comes a kingliness of character; and he who is king-like goes on to be heaven-like." (DDJ 16, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm))

The personal application follows one part of the chain: giving judgement room before acting on it.

### Plain words

When someone angers you, describe what happened before deciding what it says about them. Add what you know about their situation and what is only a guess. Then make the decision you still need to make.

### For example

If a colleague is late, draft your immediate account and a second that includes the constraints you actually know. Mark guesses in both. Choose a response that fits the facts and your boundary, then reuse the fact-versus-guess check. This is a cognition practice, not mind-reading.
