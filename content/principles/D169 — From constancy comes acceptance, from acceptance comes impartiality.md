> **Principle 169** — From constancy comes acceptance, from acceptance comes impartiality

Knowing the constant, one tolerates; tolerating, one becomes impartial; impartial, one becomes kingly: the chain from calm to fair judgment.

(DDJ 16.)

**Plain words** Steadiness isn't just peace of mind — it's the prerequisite for seeing anything clearly enough to judge it. The chain runs calm, then tolerant, then impartial, then fit to judge.

*For example:* The judge who isn't rattled by the lawyer's theater — composure is what fairness is made of.
