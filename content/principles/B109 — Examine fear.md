> **Principle 109** — Examine fear

Much fear is papanca: stories about what might happen, not what is. Look directly: what is actually here, and what is imagined? Fear examined becomes information.

(MN 20; Dhammapada 212.)

**Plain words** Much fear is papanca — stories about what might happen, not what is. Look directly: what is actually here, and what is imagined? Fear examined becomes information. The monster in the basement is almost always a coat rack.

*For example:* The presentation panic, itemized: real risk is minor embarrassment; imagined risk is career collapse. Itemizing shrinks it back to coat-rack size.
