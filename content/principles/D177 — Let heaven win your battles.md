> **Principle 177** — Let heaven win your battles

The Way of heaven doesn't contend yet excels at winning, doesn't speak yet excels at answering. Set up the conditions, then step back and let them do the winning.

(DDJ 73.)

**Plain words** The Way of heaven doesn't contend yet excels at winning; doesn't speak yet excels at answering. Set up the conditions, then step back and let them do the winning. Strategy is arranging things so the outcome doesn't need your supervision.

*For example:* The gardener who prepares soil, sun, and water doesn't tug at the seedlings — the harvest was set up, not forced.

## Psychology parallels

- **Situational strategies beat willpower**, changing the environment outperforms in-the-moment resistance (Duckworth et al., 2016)

## Philosophy parallels

- **The archer** (Cicero, De Finibus III), the Stoic archer does everything in his power to aim well, then accepts where the arrow strikes
