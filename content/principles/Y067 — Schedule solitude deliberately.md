> **Principle 67** — Schedule solitude deliberately

The Gita's virtue list includes resorting to solitary places and an ease with being alone; the tradition treats solitude as a training environment for the mind, not as misanthropy. Practically, schedule solitude as an appointment with the same status as a meeting, and protect the first fifteen minutes after rejoining people, when the practice is most erodable.

(BG 13.10-11; BG 6.10.)

**Plain words** The Gita's virtue list includes resorting to solitary places and an ease with being alone — solitude as training environment for the mind, not misanthropy. Schedule it as an appointment with the same status as a meeting, and protect it.

*For example:* The parent who hasn't been alone in a room since 2019 — books the monthly solo walk, guards it like a dentist appointment, and comes back a slightly larger person every time.
