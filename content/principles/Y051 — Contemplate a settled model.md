> **Principle 51** — Contemplate a settled model

The tradition names, as another support, the contemplation of a mind free of craving: resting attention on someone who exemplifies the settledness you are training. The support works by modeling, and the tradition is explicit that it is a support, not the destination. Practically, choose one exemplar, living or historical, and use the question "what would stillness look like here?" as an anchor in difficult moments.

(YS 1.37.)

**Plain words** Another support: the contemplation of a mind free of craving — resting attention on someone who exemplifies the settledness you're training. It works by modeling, and the tradition is explicit that it's a support, not the destination. Use the model; don't worship it.

*For example:* The junior surgeon who calms by recalling the senior surgeon's hands in the crisis — not magic, just the nervous system borrowing a template of steadiness until it grows its own.
