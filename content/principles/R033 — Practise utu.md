> **Principle 295** — Practise utu

Maintain balance in relationships through reciprocity. Utu is often translated as revenge, but its deeper meaning is the restoration of balance. When someone gives to you, give back. When someone harms you, seek a proportional response that restores balance rather than escalating conflict.

(Māori tikanga; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori; Durie, 1994, Whaiora.)

**Plain words** Utu is balance, not revenge: when someone gives, give back; when someone harms, respond proportionally enough to restore balance — not to escalate. The ledger of relationship is real, and it's kept in motion in both directions.

*For example:* The neighbour who returns the borrowed ladder with firewood from their pile — no debt is spoken of, and the balance quietly holds for thirty years.
