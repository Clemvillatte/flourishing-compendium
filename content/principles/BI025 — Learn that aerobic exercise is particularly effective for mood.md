> **Principle 25** — Learn that aerobic exercise is particularly effective for mood

Aerobic exercise helps with depression, but it is not clearly the "particularly effective" kind, and "reliably" overstates the certainty. The 2013 Cochrane review pooled 35 trials (1,356 participants) and found a moderate effect of exercise on depression (SMD -0.62), yet in the six trials at low risk of bias the effect was small and not statistically significant (SMD -0.18, 95% CI -0.47 to 0.11) ([Cooney et al. 2013, Cochrane](https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD004366.pub6/full)). A more recent update of that review pooled 57 trials (2,189 participants) with an SMD of -0.67 and rated the evidence low-certainty. Restricted to seven higher-quality trials, the SMD was -0.46 (95% CI -0.88 to -0.04) ([Cochrane update, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41500513/)).

On type, a network meta-analysis found moderate reductions in depression for walking or jogging (g -0.62), yoga (-0.55), strength training (-0.49), mixed aerobic exercise (-0.43) and tai chi or qigong (-0.42), compared with active controls ([Noetel et al. 2024, BMJ](https://www.bmj.com/content/384/bmj-2023-075847)). Several kinds of exercise therefore help, and strength training and yoga are in the same range as walking.

### Scope

These sources are about depression. The claims about anxiety and about BDNF and neuroplasticity are not tested here, and Schuch 2018, Erickson 2015 and Vivar 2013 are listed as further reading. The claim that "the chemistry moved" is not tested here.

**Plain words**
Moving regularly lifts depressive symptoms for many people, and walking works about as well as the other forms. The evidence is moderate in strength.

**For example**
Pick a form of movement you will actually do, such as walking, yoga or lifting, and do it for a month.

## Psychology parallels

- **Aerobic exercise and depression**, aerobic activity is effective for reducing depressive symptoms (Schuch et al., 2018; Cooney et al., 2013)
- **Aerobic exercise and neuroplasticity**, aerobic exercise increases BDNF and promotes neuroplasticity (Erickson et al., 2015; Vivar et al., 2013)

## Practice

- Include aerobic exercise: walking, running, cycling, swimming, dancing — 30 minutes, 3-5 times per week. This is the most evidence-supported type for mood.
