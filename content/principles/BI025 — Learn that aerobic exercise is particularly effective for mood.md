> **Principle 25** — Learn that aerobic exercise is particularly effective for mood

Aerobic exercise (walking, running, cycling, swimming) — sustained activity that elevates heart rate — is particularly effective for improving mood and reducing depression and anxiety.

**Plain words** Aerobic exercise — walking, running, cycling, swimming — is the mood machine: sustained heart-rate elevation reliably lifts depression and anxiety. If you do one kind, do this kind. The lungs are antidepressant equipment.

*For example:* The two-week experiment of daily 30-minute walks that changes a mood more than a month of introspection — not because the problems were solved, but because the chemistry moved. The legs treated the brain.
## Psychology parallels

- **Aerobic exercise and depression**, aerobic activity is effective for reducing depressive symptoms (Schuch et al., 2018; Cooney et al., 2013)
- **Aerobic exercise and neuroplasticity**, aerobic exercise increases BDNF and promotes neuroplasticity (Erickson et al., 2015; Vivar et al., 2013)

## Practice

- Include aerobic exercise: walking, running, cycling, swimming, dancing — 30 minutes, 3-5 times per week. This is the most evidence-supported type for mood.
