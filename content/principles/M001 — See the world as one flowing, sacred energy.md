> **Principle 1** — See the world as one flowing, sacred energy

The tlamatinime's metaphysics, as reconstructed by Maffie, centers on teotl: a single, sacred, self-generating energy whose "waves" and "currents" are everything that exists. There are no things, strictly speaking; there are concentrations and movements of the one process. The practical consequence is a change of relation to your own life: you are not an object in a world of objects but a movement of the whole, and your states (energy, mood, health) are currents rather than possessions. Practically, when you feel stuck, remember that a current is never actually still; the stuckness is a description, not a physics.

(Maffie, Aztec Philosophy, the teotl chapters, as reconstruction; Leon-Portilla's presentation of the tlamatinime.)

**Plain words** Teotl: everything that exists is one sacred energy in motion — waves, currents, concentrations. There are no separate "things," including you: you're a movement of the whole, and your states (energy, mood, health) are currents, not possessions. Stuckness is a description, not a physics — a current is never actually still.

*For example:* Someone in a bad year keeps waiting for their "real life" to resume — then takes the teotl view: the low mood is weather in a system that has no other mode than moving, and their only job is not to dam it.
