> **Principle 54** — Allow incubation

When stuck on a problem, step away. The incubation effect, in which solutions emerge after a period of non-focused engagement, is one of the most robust findings in creativity research. The mechanism is not mystical. It is the continued unconscious processing of the problem while conscious attention is occupied elsewhere. Walking, showering, and sleeping are reliable incubation contexts.

(Wallas, 1926; Sio and Ormerod, 2009.)

**Plain words** When stuck, walk away. Solutions genuinely emerge after time away from the problem. It isn't mystical: the mind keeps processing while your attention is elsewhere. Sleep, walks and other activities are reliable incubators.

*For example:* The answer arrives in the shower the morning after you gave up for the night.
