> **Principle 38** — Cultivate humanity

The virtue of humanity includes love, kindness, and social intelligence. It is the capacity to care for and connect with others. Its cultivation is not about niceness. It is about the genuine capacity to recognise, respond to, and act for the good of others. Research on compassionate love (Underwood) and kindness interventions (Layous et al., 2012) supports the well-being effects of humanity-related strengths.

(Peterson and Seligman, 2004; Layous et al., 2012.)

**Plain words** The virtue of humanity covers love, kindness and social intelligence. It's the real capacity to see and care for others, not niceness. Research on compassionate love and kindness interventions suggests it can be built.

*For example:* Noticing a coworker is struggling and quietly taking a task off her plate.
