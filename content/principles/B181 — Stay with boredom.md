> **Principle 181** — Stay with boredom

When boredom arrives, don't flee into the phone: stay and look at it. Boredom is the surface of a mind that has forgotten how to be with itself.

(Zen tradition; MN 122.)

**Plain words** When boredom arrives, don't flee into the phone — stay and look at it. Boredom is the surface of a mind that has forgotten how to be with itself. The itch is information; the phone is just the scratch that spreads it.

*For example:* The queue without the phone — two minutes of restlessness, then an unexpected pocket of actual thought. The phone would have eaten it.
