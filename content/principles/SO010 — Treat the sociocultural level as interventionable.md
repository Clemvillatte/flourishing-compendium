> **Principle 10** — Treat the sociocultural level as interventionable

Treat the sociocultural level as interventionable, in the same sense. Community interventions (Wandersman and Florin, 2003), policy interventions (Biglan et al., 2012), cultural interventions (Gone, 2011; Mutamba et al., 2021), and institutional design (Ostrom, 1990) are all established forms of sociocultural intervention. The adaptive stance is not fatalism about social conditions but active engagement with them.

**Plain words** Communities and cultures can be intervened on — programmes, policies, institutional design. Not just individuals on couches; whole populations move. Prevention at the level of the village beats treatment at the level of the person.

*For example:* The clean-water effect in mental health: the community that added street lighting and youth programmes saw depression rates move — nobody's individual therapy did that. The intervention was upstream.
