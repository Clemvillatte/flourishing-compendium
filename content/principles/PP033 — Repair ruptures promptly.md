> **Principle 33** — Repair ruptures promptly

When a relationship ruptures, repair it within 48 hours. The longer the delay, the more the rupture calcifies. Repair is not about resolving the disagreement. It is about acknowledging that the relationship is more important than the issue. Research on repair attempts (Gottman) shows that the capacity to repair predicts relationship stability more than the frequency of conflict.

(Gottman, 1999; Driver and Gottman, 2004.)

**Plain words** When a relationship ruptures, repair it quickly, ideally within about 48 hours. The longer the delay, the harder it sets. Repair isn't about settling the argument. It's about saying the relationship matters more than the issue. The ability to repair predicts lasting relationships.

*For example:* After a fight on Tuesday you send a message Wednesday: "I hate this distance. Can we talk tonight?"
