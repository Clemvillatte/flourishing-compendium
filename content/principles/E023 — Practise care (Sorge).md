> **Principle 415** — Practise care (Sorge)

Recognise that your fundamental way of being is care. Care, in Heidegger's sense, is not an emotion but the structural totality of your being: you are always ahead of yourself (projection), already in a world (thrownness), and alongside others (being-with). Your existence is not neutral; you care about your being. Attend to the structure of your caring.

(Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §§39-44.)

**Plain words** Care (Sorge): your fundamental way of being — not an emotion but the structure of you. You are always ahead of yourself (projection), already in a world (thrownness), alongside others (being-with). Your existence is care before it is anything else. You matter about things; that's what you are.

*For example:* Even the cynic who claims to care about nothing is caring about the claiming — the protest took energy, the posture took maintenance. There is no human position outside care; there are only choices about what to care about.
