> **Principle 125** — Use the limbs as a diagnostic grid

The troubleshooting practice that pairs with principle 72's curriculum: when the advanced stalls, walk the grid downward. Meditation failing: check sense withdrawal (inputs). Withdrawal failing: check breath and body (regulation). Regulation failing: check observances (contentment, study, moderation). Conduct failing: check restraints (harm, truth, taking, energy, grasping). An editorial rule of thumb, not an established empirical finding: many stalls appear to resolve at the level of ethics or body rather than technique. Practically, keep the grid as a monthly audit, cross-referencing principle 72's curriculum.

(YS 2.28-2.29, the sequence as diagnostic.)

**Plain words** The troubleshooting grid, paired with the curriculum: when the advanced stalls, walk the grid downward. Meditation failing: check the inputs (withdrawal). Withdrawal failing: check breath and body. Regulation failing: check conduct. The fault is almost always downstream of what you're practising.

*For example:* Someone fights their "concentration problem" for a year — then audits upward and finds the actual fault: 5 a.m. doom-scrolling. The grid reads bottom-up: no withdrawal, no regulation, no meditation. Fix the base.
