> **Principle 455** — Practise meaning through love

Find meaning through loving encounter with another person. Love, in Frankl's framework, is the second path to meaning: the experience of being seen, known, and affirmed by another. Love is not merely a feeling but the deepest form of encounter. Practise love as a path to meaning.

(Frankl, 1946/2006, Man's Search for Meaning; Frankl, 1969, The Will to Meaning; Buber, 1923/1970, I and Thou.)

**Plain words** Love is the second path to meaning: the experience of being seen, known, and affirmed by another — the deepest form of encounter. Not merely a feeling; a path. Practise love as a practice, not as weather that happens to you.

*For example:* The couple whose decades look unremarkable from outside — but inside the ordinary Tuesday dinners, two people keep being truly seen. Frankl's point: that seeing is a meaning-source on par with any achievement.
