> **Principle 176** — Prefer growth goals to validation goals

Goals aimed at growing capacity beat goals aimed at proving worth: the first builds, the second guards. Convert "show them" into "learn this."

(Maslow's growth versus deficiency motivation: Toward a Psychology of Being, 1962.)

**Plain words** The proving goal has a finish line and a hangover; the growing goal has neither. Convert "show them" into "learn this" — capacity goals build, validation goals guard.

*For example:* The speech given to impress versus the one given to get better at speaking — same stage, and only one of them leaves with a skill. 'Show them' ends the moment the applause does. 'Learn this' compounds.

## Psychology parallels

- **Learning versus performance goals** (Dweck and Leggett, 1988)
- **[[Self-concordant goals|Self-concordant goals]]** (Sheldon and Elliot, 1999)

- **Growth versus deficiency** (Maslow, Toward a Psychology of Being)
- **Growth as the only moral end** (Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct)
