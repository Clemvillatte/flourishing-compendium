> **Principle 74** — Use bicultural identity integration as a strength

Use bicultural identity integration — the capacity to hold two cultural identities without conflict, and to switch between them fluently — as a strength. Bicultural integration predicts well-being and creativity (Benet-Martínez et al., 2002; Benet-Martínez and Haritatos, 2005; Saad et al., 2013).

**Plain words** Bicultural identity integration is a strength — holding two cultural identities without conflict, switching fluently. It predicts well-being and creativity. The bilingual of selves. Not confusion; range.

*For example:* The person who is one self at the family table and another at the office, fluent in both, translating constantly — it looks like burden and measures as creativity. The switching itself is the skill.
