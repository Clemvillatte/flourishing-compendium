> **Principle 73** — Build rest into the system

Rest is not the absence of work. It is a component of the system. Without scheduled rest, the practices that produce flourishing become sources of stress. Protect a day, an evening, or an hour that is genuinely unfilled. The rest is not laziness. It is the recovery that makes sustained engagement possible.

(Sonnentag, 2018; Zijlstra et al., 2014.)

**Plain words** Rest is part of the system, not the absence of work. Without protected rest, the practices of flourishing become sources of stress. Keep a genuine hole in the week.

*For example:* An evening with nothing in it, defended like an appointment.
