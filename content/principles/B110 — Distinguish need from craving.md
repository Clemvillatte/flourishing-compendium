> **Principle 110** — Distinguish need from craving

Needs are finite and satisfiable: food, rest, connection; craving is infinite and insatiable — more, newer, better. Serve the first; study the second.

(Dhammapada 186-187; Shantideva 6.10.)

**Plain words** Needs are finite and satisfiable — food, rest, connection. Craving is infinite and insatiable: more, newer, better. Serve the first; study the second. One is a pet, the other is a fire, and only one of them can ever be fed enough.

*For example:* Hungry: a sandwich ends it. Bored: no sandwich ends it — that's the diagnostic difference between lunch and the feed.
