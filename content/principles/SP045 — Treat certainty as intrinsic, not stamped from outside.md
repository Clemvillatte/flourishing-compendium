> **Principle 45** — Treat certainty as intrinsic, not stamped from outside

A true idea carries its own mark, like work well done; it does not need a certificate from another idea. Doubt dissolves not by being doubted in turn but by the presence of adequate understanding, the way light ends darkness without a fight. Practically, notice the difference between feeling certain (fluency, familiarity, confidence) and understanding (being able to explain, derive, and apply); train the second, and be suspicious of the first when it arrives without the second.

(Treatise on the Emendation of the Intellect, the analysis of doubt and certainty; Ethics II, P43-44.)

**Plain words** A true idea carries its own mark, like work well done — it doesn't need a certificate from another idea. Certainty is intrinsic, not stamped from outside. Doubt dissolves not by being doubted in turn but by the presence of adequate understanding — the way light ends darkness.

*For example:* The person who keeps asking others to confirm what they already see clearly — and the moment they stop outsourcing the confirmation, the idea's own structure does the work. The light doesn't need a certificate; it ends the darkness by showing up.
