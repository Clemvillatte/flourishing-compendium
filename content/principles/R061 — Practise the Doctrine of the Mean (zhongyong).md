> **Principle 323** — Practise the Doctrine of the Mean (zhongyong)

Seek the golden mean in all things. Zhongyong is the practice of finding the central, balanced way between extremes. It is not compromise or moderation for its own sake but the practical wisdom to find the right measure in each situation.

(The Doctrine of the Mean; Confucius, The Analects 11.16.)

**Plain words** Zhongyong, the mean: not lukewarm compromise but finding the exact right measure in each situation — between excess and deficiency, per the moment, not per a formula. The mean for courage isn't the average of rashness and cowardice; it's whatever this situation actually requires.

*For example:* The parent who doesn't ban the teenager's phone and doesn't surrender to it either — but finds the actual right measure for this kid, this year, this problem.
