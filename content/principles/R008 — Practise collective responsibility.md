> **Principle 270** — Practise collective responsibility

Do not think of yourself as an isolated individual responsible only for your own actions. You are part of a web of relationships, and what happens to one affects all. Take responsibility for the well-being of your community, not only for your own.

(Ubuntu philosophy; Gyekye, Tradition and Modernity.)

**Plain words** You're a node in a web, not a free-standing unit: what happens to one moves everyone. So take responsibility for the community's wellbeing, not just your own scorecard. The private life and the common life are the same life.

*For example:* Someone who sees the street's kids hanging around with nothing to do and organises the weekend football — not their job, exactly their responsibility.
