> **Principle 189** — Let your yes be small and your no rare

Commit sparingly, refuse gently, and keep your word absolute where given. Reliability is quiet; noise is not.

(DDJ 63, 23.)

**Plain words** A small "yes" honored is worth ten large ones performed — the calendar believes verbs, not intentions.

*For example:* The friend who says 'I can't make it, but I'll see you next month' — and does. The yes is small and solid gold.
