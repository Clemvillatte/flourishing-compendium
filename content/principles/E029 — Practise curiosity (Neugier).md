> **Principle 421** — Practise curiosity (Neugier)

Recognise the mode of curiosity — the restless seeking after novelty for its own sake, the constant distraction that prevents genuine dwelling. Curiosity, in Heidegger's technical sense, is not intellectual openness but the flight from depth. Do not confuse information consumption with understanding. Practise dwelling rather than curiosity.

(Heidegger, 1927/2008, Being and Time, §36.)

**Plain words** Curiosity in Heidegger's technical sense: the restless seeking of novelty for its own sake — the constant distraction that prevents genuine dwelling. Not intellectual openness; the flight from depth. Don't confuse information consumption with understanding.

*For example:* Someone with forty browser tabs and no memory of any of them — the curiosity mode is doing exactly what it's designed to do: keeping everything shallow enough to skim. Depth requires the tab that stays open for months.
