> **Principle 63** — See each thought-moment containing the whole (Tiantai)

A single moment of mind, when seen clearly, holds the entire field of conditions: the whole world in one thought. Rest in the suchness of any moment.

(Zhiyi, Mohe Zhiguan; Tiantai tradition.)

**Plain words** A single moment of mind, when seen clearly, holds the entire field of conditions — the whole world in one thought. Rest in the suchness of any moment. You don't need to understand everything; you need to fully touch anything.

*For example:* Fully tasting one sip of tea — the rain, the picker, your grandmother's kitchen, all somehow present in it.
