> **Principle 28** — Practice resilience in adversity

Adversity tests character but does not create it. It reveals what was already there. The resilient person does not collapse under misfortune but draws on the resources of character built in better times. Build your character when it is easy, so it holds when it is hard.

(NE I.10, I.11; EE VII.14.)

**Plain words** Hard times don't build character, they show it. Aristotle's resilient person draws on what they built when things were easier. So build your character when it's cheap, and it holds when it's expensive.

*For example:* Someone who has spent years keeping promises in small things is more likely to keep one when keeping it costs them dearly.
