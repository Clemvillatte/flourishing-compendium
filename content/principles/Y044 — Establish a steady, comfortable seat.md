> **Principle 44** — Establish a steady, comfortable seat

Asana is defined by exactly two qualities: sthira (steadiness) and sukha (comfort). Not depth, not shape, not photogenic difficulty. The definition is functional: the posture exists to hold a body comfortably still for a long time so attention can work. Practically, when a position needs constant maintenance, it is not your posture yet.

(YS 2.46.)

**Plain words** The posture is defined by exactly two qualities: steady and comfortable. Not depth, not shape, not photogenic difficulty. The posture exists to hold a body comfortably still for a long time so attention can work. When assessing a posture, ask only those two questions.

*For example:* The person who quit yoga because they "couldn't do the poses" — and the teacher's question: can you sit, steady and comfortable, for ten minutes? Then you can do the only pose the tradition actually requires.
