> **Principle 51** — Learn to use touch: self-soothing through contact

Touch (hand on heart, self-hug, gentle touch) activates the parasympathetic system and is soothing. Self-touch is a specific physiological regulator.

**Plain words** Touch is a regulator — a hand on the heart, an arm around your own shoulders. Yes, self-touch counts; the skin doesn't check whose hand it is. The body reads contact as safety.

*For example:* The airport goodbye survived with a hand on your own chest, breathing slowly — nobody saw it, and it worked anyway. The skin doesn't require an audience to soothe.
## Psychology parallels

- **Touch and parasympathetic activation (Field)**, gentle touch reduces arousal and releases oxytocin (Field, 2010; Uvnäs-Moberg et al., 2015)
- **Self-soothing touch**, hand on heart, self-hug, and gentle self-touch activate the soothing system (Gilbert, 2009; Neff, 2011)

## Practice

- Use self-soothing touch: hand on heart, hand on arm, self-hug, gentle touch to face or arms. This activates the soothing system. Use it for distress and [[Self-compassion|self-compassion]].
