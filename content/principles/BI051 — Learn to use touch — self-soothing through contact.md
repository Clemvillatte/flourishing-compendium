> **Principle 51** — Learn to use touch: self-soothing through contact

A review of moderate-pressure massage concludes that the stimulation of pressure receptors leads to increased vagal activity, which in turn seems to mediate the diverse benefits noted for massage therapy ([Field, Diego & Hernandez-Reif 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20402578/)). A study of affective touch, the slow pleasant stroking that activates C-tactile nerve fibres, found it effective in decreasing sympathetic activity and increasing vagal dominance, possibly buffering emotional distress ([Della Longa et al. 2025](https://academic.oup.com/scan/article/20/1/nsaf090/8248583?login=false)).

Self-touch is a subtler case. The brain-and-heart study that compared being stroked on the forearm, stroking one's own forearm, and stroking a pillow found the parasympathetic advantage sat with the social version: for cardiac parasympathetic indices, social touch consistently had higher values compared to self-touch, with no significant differences found between self- and object-touch ([Candia-Rivera et al. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-07448-z)). A self-hug or hand-on-heart is a practice this compendium suggests for moments when no other person is available, but the opened evidence does not certify it as a strong parasympathetic lever on its own ([Candia-Rivera et al. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-07448-z)).

> "The stimulation of pressure receptors leads to increased vagal activity which, in turn, seems to mediate the diverse benefits noted for massage therapy." (Field, Diego & Hernandez-Reif 2010, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20402578/))

**Plain words**
A self-hug and a massage are different activities. In the one opened comparison, the cardiac measures favoured being touched by another person over touching yourself. Findings about massage do not tell you how a self-hug will affect your body, so try it as an optional comfort practice without assuming the same results.

*For example:* An imagined case: someone alone after bad news puts a hand on their chest and breathes slowly, because that is what is available at midnight; the next day they also seek out people who can put an arm around them. The practice scales with availability ([Candia-Rivera et al. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-07448-z)).

## Psychology parallels

- **Touch and parasympathetic activation (Field)**, gentle touch reduces arousal and releases oxytocin (Field, 2010; Uvnäs-Moberg et al., 2015)
- **Self-soothing touch**, hand on heart, self-hug, and gentle self-touch activate the soothing system (Gilbert, 2009; Neff, 2011)

## Practice

- Use self-soothing touch: hand on heart, hand on arm, self-hug, as a [[Self-compassion|self-compassion]] practice. The vagal evidence opened here concerns moderate-pressure or affective touch received from another person ([Field et al. 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20402578/); [Della Longa et al. 2025](https://academic.oup.com/scan/article/20/1/nsaf090/8248583?login=false)); self-touch is a portable option when no other person is available, and the opened comparison found the cardiac advantage with social touch ([Candia-Rivera et al. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-07448-z)).
