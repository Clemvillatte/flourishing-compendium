> **Principle 79** — Use AUM as a portable anchor

The tradition names the syllable AUM as a chosen object of repetition, with the stated effect of removing obstacles and turning attention inward. The bounded formulation: mantra and sound as attentional anchors, portable, usable in noise, and rhythmically self-regulating. Practically, adopt one sound and use it in transit, in waiting rooms, and in the moments before difficult conversations.

(YS 1.27-1.29; Mandukya Upanishad, the syllable analyzed.)

**Plain words** The tradition names AUM as a chosen object of repetition, with the stated effect of removing obstacles and turning attention inward. The bounded formulation: mantra and sound as attentional anchors — portable, usable in noise, rhythmically self-regulating. A pocket-steadier.

*For example:* On the loud bus, in the MRI machine, in the back of the courtroom — where the breath is too subtle to hold, the sound is not. The anchor that works everywhere is the one worth having.
