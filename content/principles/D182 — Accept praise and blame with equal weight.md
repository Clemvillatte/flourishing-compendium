> **Principle 182** — Accept praise and blame with equal weight

Favor and disgrace both cause alarm: the world's verdicts are weather, not measurement. Neither inflate nor collapse.

(DDJ 13; Zhuangzi 20.)

**Plain words** Favor and disgrace both cause alarm — the world's verdicts are weather, not measurement. Neither inflate nor collapse. Praise and blame are the same substance in different temperatures; dress for both.

*For example:* The author who believes the rave reviews AND the savaging — both times the book was exactly the same book.
