> **Principle 6** — Let the skeptics' slogans guard the practice's humility

Sextus (Outlines of Pyrrhonism 1.197, Bury translation) says the Sceptic "determines nothing, not even the very proposition 'I determine nothing'", because it is "not a Dogmatic assumption" but "an expression indicative of our own mental condition". Saying it means "I am now in such a state of mind as neither to affirm dogmatically nor deny any of the matters now in question" ([Wofford College PDF of the Outlines](https://sites.wofford.edu/wp-content/uploads/OUTLINES%20OF%20PYRRHONISM.pdf)). At 1.206 he adds that the slogans "may possibly be confuted by themselves", "just as aperient drugs do not merely eliminate the humours from the body, but also expel themselves along with the humours" (same text). So the seed's point is in the source, and Sextus supplies the purgative image.

The "changed teams" warning also has a source: the Pyrrhonist avoids the "negative dogmatism" Sextus levels at the Academics ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/humanities/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/sextus-empiricus-third-century-ce)). Scholars read the slogans as reports of how things appear ([Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/book/26354/chapter/194665319)). The seed's list of three slogans is accurate: the Stanford Encyclopedia lists "No more this than that", "I determine nothing" and the "all things are non-apprehensible" formula among them ([Stanford Encyclopedia](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sextus-empiricus/)). The practical advice to "hold your own suspension-practices' results lightly", and the ex-believer example, are the page's application.

The psychology parallel, "fixed anti-belief functions as belief", and its link to cognitive fusion, were not opened. Treat it as an analogy only. Wittgenstein (Tractatus 6.54) and the Alagaddupama Sutta (MN 22) were also unread.

**Plain words** In Sextus's account, "I determine nothing" reports a state of mind and is not a claim about reality. The slogans cancel themselves, like a purgative that leaves with what it clears. So if you become certain that certainty is bad, you have moved from the method into a doctrine.

*For example:* This is an imagined case. A former believer becomes the loudest atheist in the room, then notices the pattern and relaxes.
