> **Principle 482** — Practise the hermeneutics of faith

Balance the hermeneutics of suspicion with the hermeneutics of faith — the trust that meaning can be found, that appearances can be trusted, that the world can be read as meaningful. Do not let suspicion consume all trust. Hold both in tension.

(Ricoeur, 1965/1970, Freud and Philosophy; Ricoeur, 1973, "The Task of Hermeneutics," in Hermeneutics and the Human Sciences.)

**Plain words** Balance suspicion with the hermeneutics of faith — the trust that meaning can be found, that appearances can be trusted, that the world can be read as meaningful. Don't let suspicion consume all trust. Hold both: the detective and the believer in the same reader.

*For example:* The fully suspicious person can't read a love letter without deconstructing it — and the fully trusting one signs the fraud. The skilled reader does both: suspicious of the terms, trusting of the friend, in the right proportions.
