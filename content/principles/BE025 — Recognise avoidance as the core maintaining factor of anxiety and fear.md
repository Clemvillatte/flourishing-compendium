> **Principle 25** — Recognise avoidance as the core maintaining factor of anxiety and fear

Avoidance is the primary maintaining factor of anxiety disorders, phobias, and fears. Avoiding a feared situation reduces anxiety immediately (negative reinforcement) but prevents extinction of the fear. The treatment is approach, not avoidance.

**Plain words** Avoidance is anxiety's fuel — every time you flee the thing, the fear learns it was right to flee. Short-term relief, long-term sentence. The loop doesn't break from the sidelines; it breaks by going back in.

*For example:* Skipping every social event to keep the peace — and the peace is a shrinking cell. Each absence confirms the danger. The anxiety isn't being managed; it's being trained.
## Psychology parallels

- **Avoidance maintains fear**, avoidance prevents extinction learning (Craske et al., 2014; Foa et al., 2007)
- **Exposure therapy is the gold standard for anxiety disorders**, systematic approach to feared cues reduces fear (Foa et al., 2007; Craske et al., 2014)
- **Negative reinforcement of avoidance**, avoidance reduces anxiety immediately, reinforcing it (Mowrer, 1960; Craske et al., 2014)

## Practice

- Recognise avoidance as the problem, not the solution. When you avoid something because of anxiety, the avoidance strengthens the fear. The treatment is approach (in graded steps).
