> **Principle 75** — Recognise that friendships change over time

Friendships of utility end when the utility ends. Friendships of pleasure end when the pleasure changes. Friendships of virtue are more durable but even they can end if one party changes. Accept that some friendships are seasonal; hold the durable ones more dearly.

(NE VIII.3, VIII.13.)

**Plain words** Friendships of use end when the use ends. Friendships of pleasure end when the fun changes. Even deep ones can end if one person changes. Accept that some are seasonal, and hold the durable ones more dearly.

*For example:* Two parents who became close while their kids were in the same class often drift apart after the school years. That's a season ending, not a failure.
