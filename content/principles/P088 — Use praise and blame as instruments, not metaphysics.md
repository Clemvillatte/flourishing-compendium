> **Principle 88** — Use praise and blame as instruments, not metaphysics

Responsibility practices exist to shape future behavior, not to reward a metaphysically free will. Praise what you want more of, blame sparingly and specifically: including toward yourself.

(Dewey, Human Nature and Conduct, 1922.)

**Plain words** Guilt that changes nothing is just self-harm with a philosophical cover story. Praise and blame exist to shape future behavior — praise what you want more of, blame sparingly and specifically, including toward yourself.

*For example:* The boss who praises the exact behavior she wants repeated — 'the way you handled that call' — versus the one who yells 'careless!' at everything. Only one of them is managing anything.
