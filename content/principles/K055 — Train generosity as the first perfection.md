> **Principle 55** — Train generosity as the first perfection

The six perfections begin with generosity, and the tradition's instruction is graded: begin with small, real gifts (time, attention, money in amounts that stretch slightly), because the practice is the loosening of the grip, and the grip is loosened by repetitions, not by intentions. The perfection is measured by the inner event (the loosening), not the outer size. Practically, give something small daily for a month, and watch the grip-meter rather than the ledger.

(The six perfections; Gampopa's Jewel Ornament of Liberation, the generosity chapter.)

**Plain words** Generosity is the first perfection, and the instruction is graded. Start with small, real gifts of time, attention, money in amounts that stretch you a little, because the practice is loosening the grip, and the grip loosens through repetition. The measure is the inner event, not the size of the gift.

*For example:* Giving up an evening to help a friend move, when you'd rather rest, and noticing a small release.
