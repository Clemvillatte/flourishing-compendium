> **Principle 22** — Practice gratitude as repayment of love

Spinoza defines gratitude as a desire springing from love: wanting to benefit someone who has benefited us out of love. The definition appears among the Definitions of the Emotions at the end of Ethics Part III, in Elwes's translation. ([Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/3800/pg3800.txt))

> "XXXIV. Thankfulness or Gratitude is the desire or zeal springing from love, whereby we endeavour to benefit him, who with similar feelings of love has conferred a benefit on us."

(Ethics III, Definitions of the Emotions XXXIV, Elwes translation, [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/3800/pg3800.txt).)

**Plain words** Think of a favour given out of care. Do you now want to help that person? Spinoza's definition concerns that wish to benefit the giver, whether or not you have acted on it. The condition it names is the giver's own love: the benefit was conferred with similar feelings of love, and the wish answers in kind.

*For example:* Imagined: a mentor gives you time and advice. Later, wanting to help that mentor, you offer assistance with their project.
