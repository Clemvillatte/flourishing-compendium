> **Principle 26** — Rest in the nature of awareness itself

The slogan that directs the practitioner to the awareness register: rest in the nature of alaya, the ground consciousness, the sheer knowing prior to content. The instruction pairs with the Mahamudra layer below; here it is enough to note that lojong includes, deliberately, a non-conceptual resting practice alongside its slogan-work, and that the two feed each other. Practically, end your sitting with one minute of no technique: just awareness, open, without an object.

(Seven-Point Mind Training, Point Two, the alaya slogans.)

**Plain words** The slogan says to rest in the nature of awareness itself, the plain knowing before any content. Lojong includes a wordless resting practice alongside the slogans, and the two feed each other. End a sitting with a minute of that, without doing anything.

*For example:* After twenty minutes working with slogans, you drop them for sixty seconds and just sit, aware, with nothing to fix.
