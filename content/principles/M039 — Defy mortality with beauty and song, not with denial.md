> **Principle 39** — Defy mortality with beauty and song, not with denial

The songs' answer to death is neither doctrine (a promised afterlife, outside this scope) nor despair: it is the poem itself, the flower and song raised against the fading. The defiance is in the making: the mortal being that makes something beautiful is answering death in the only register death cannot reach. Practically, when mortality stings, do not argue with it; make something, and let the making be the answer.

(The Cantares's defiant songs; the flower-and-song answer to impermanence.)

**Plain words** The songs' answer to death is neither doctrine nor despair — it's the poem itself: the flower and song raised against the fading. The defiance is in the making: the mortal being who makes something beautiful is answering death in the only register death cannot reach. When mortality stings, make something.

*For example:* The woman who learns her diagnosis is terminal and writes the family's recipes into a book, properly, with stories — death will have the body; it doesn't get the book.
