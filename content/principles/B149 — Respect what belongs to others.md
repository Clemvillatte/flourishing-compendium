> **Principle 149** — Respect what belongs to others

Take nothing not given: objects, credit, attention, trust. This is generosity's twin and the floor of trust.

(Five precepts; AN 10.176.)

**Plain words** Take nothing not given — objects, credit, attention, trust. This is generosity's twin and the floor of trust. Stealing credit is still stealing; the inventory is just subtler, and the security guard is your own conscience.

*For example:* The teammate who quietly resubmits your shared work under only their name — the object taken was 'recognition,' and it was not given.
