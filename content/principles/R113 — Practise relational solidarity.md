> **Principle 375** — Practise relational solidarity

Stand in solidarity with those who suffer injustice. Solidarity is not charity from a position of superiority; it is the recognition that your fate is bound up with the fate of others. Act with them, not merely for them.

(Scholz, 2008, Political Solidarity; Rorty, 1989, Contingency, Irony, and Solidarity; Arendt, 1958, The Human Condition.)

**Plain words** Solidarity isn't charity from above — it's the recognition that your fate is bound up with theirs, so you act with people, not merely for them. Stand in it together or don't call it solidarity.

*For example:* The well-off resident who joins (not funds, joins) the tenants' campaign — shows up to the meetings, learns the names, takes the same risk of looking foolish. With, not for.
