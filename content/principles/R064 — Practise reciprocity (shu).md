> **Principle 326** — Practise reciprocity (shu)

Do not impose on others what you yourself do not desire. This is the Confucian golden rule, stated in The Analects 15.24. It is both a negative prohibition (do not do to others what you do not want done to yourself) and a positive injunction (help others achieve what you would want to achieve).

(Confucius, The Analects 12.2, 15.24; [[The doctrine of the mean|The Doctrine of the Mean]], ch. 13.)

**Plain words** Shu, reciprocity: don't impose on others what you yourself don't want. The negative form is a brake; the positive form — help others toward what you'd want — is an engine. Both directions run off one question: how would this land if it landed on me?

*For example:* The person who stops forwarding the cruel joke because they know exactly how it feels to be its subject — and starts forwarding the opportunity because they know how that lands too.
