> **Principle 44** — Just sit (zazen, shikantaza)

Sit upright, eyes open, attending to the whole field of experience without an object: not chasing, not suppressing, just being present as the breath of all things.

(Dogen, Fukanzazengi; Dogen, Genjokoan.)

**Plain words** Sit upright, eyes open, attending to the whole field of experience without an object — not chasing, not suppressing, just being present. Shikantaza isn't concentrating on nothing; it's not concentrating on anything. The room is the meditation.

*For example:* Sitting in the garden with coffee and no task — not relaxing, not planning, just the birds being fully heard.
