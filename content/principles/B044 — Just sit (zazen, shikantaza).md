> **Principle 44** — Just sit (zazen, shikantaza)

Dogen's instructions for zazen in the Fukanzazengi are famously short and famously puzzling. After adjusting the posture, rocking the body right and left, and settling into a steady, immovable sitting position, the entire instruction for the mind is this: "Think of not thinking. How do you think of not-thinking? Nonthinking. This in itself is the essential art of zazen" ([Dogen, Fukanzazengi, Terebess translation](https://terebess.hu/zen/dogen/Fukanzazengi.pdf)). The dialogue is borrowed from Master Yakusan, who was asked what he thinks of when sitting motionlessly in zazen, and replied that he thinks of not-thinking ([Terebess, Fukanzazengi PDF](https://terebess.hu/zen/dogen/Fukanzazengi.pdf)).

The key term is nonthinking (hishiryo), and it does not mean having no thoughts. The Dogen scholar Hee-Jin Kim describes it as radically nondualistic, objectless, non-referential thinking — not a deficiency of thought, but freedom from the coveting and grasping that usually accompanies it ([Brill, Buddhism and the Body](https://brill.com/display/book/9789004544925/BP000012.xml?language=en)). The practice is not to suppress thoughts; it is to stop measuring experience with thoughts, ideas and views, and to let whatever arises arise without comment.

> "Think of not thinking. How do you think of not-thinking? Nonthinking. This in itself is the essential art of zazen." (Dogen, Fukanzazengi, [Terebess translation](https://terebess.hu/zen/dogen/Fukanzazengi.pdf))

**Plain words** The instruction sounds like a paradox until you notice it is not asking you to stop thinking. It is asking you to stop commenting. Thoughts arrive; the practice is to let them arrive without following, fixing, judging or pushing them away. This is the part most people get wrong on day one — they treat zazen as concentration on nothing, which is just concentration with a harder target. The actual move is to be the room the thoughts are passing through.

*For example:* Two people sit for ten minutes. One spends the whole time trying not to think, which is itself a thought, repeated. The other notices thoughts arriving and lets each one pass without a verdict. Same ten minutes; only one of them was actually sitting.
