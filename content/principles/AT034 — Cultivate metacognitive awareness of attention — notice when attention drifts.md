> **Principle 34** — Cultivate metacognitive awareness of attention: notice when attention drifts

The definitional passage from the review that named the process:

> Meta-awareness, also known as meta-consciousness or metacognitive awareness, can be defined as one's explicit knowledge of the current contents of thought.
>
> (Schooler et al., "Meta-awareness, perceptual decoupling and the wandering mind", Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 2011)

The paper's first finding is that this knowledge is intermittent: "Findings regarding meta-awareness reveal that the mind is only intermittently aware of engaging in mind wandering" ([Schooler et al. 2011](https://metalab.psych.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/2025-12/perceptual-decoupling.pdf)). Its theoretical account treats meta-awareness as its own process: "a central premise in recent theorizing about meta-awareness is that it corresponds to an intermittent process whereby individuals periodically notice the current contents of their mind" ([Schooler et al. 2011](https://metalab.psych.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/2025-12/perceptual-decoupling.pdf)).

The account assigns noticing a specific job: "the intermittent explicit assessment of the current state of the mind can enable the detection of lapses that could be missed by more low-level implicit monitoring systems", and "meta-awareness directly aids in the identification of mind-wandering lapses, and the subsequent re-engagement of the primary task" ([Schooler et al. 2011](https://metalab.psych.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/2025-12/perceptual-decoupling.pdf)). The same view "predicts that techniques that facilitate meta-awareness should minimize disruptive mind-wandering lapses" ([Schooler et al. 2011](https://metalab.psych.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/2025-12/perceptual-decoupling.pdf)).

The compendium's reading of the seed's insight: noticing that attention has left is presented in this account as its own intermittent process, distinct from the quality of the attention it notices, and its function is re-engagement rather than self-criticism.

(Schooler et al., Trends in Cognitive Sciences 15(7), 2011.)

**Plain words**
Mind wandering often happens without your knowing it; the paper's phrase is that awareness of the drift is intermittent. Meta-awareness is the process that periodically takes note of what the mind is doing. When the note lands on a drift, the task can be re-engaged. Train the taking-note; the scolding is extra.

*For example:*
Imagined: Midway through a budget review, a reader notices the sentence "I'm planning my holiday in the middle of this budget review" arrive in their mind. The noticing itself, not the scolding that could follow, is what the account treats as the operative event.

## Practice

- Practise noticing your attentional state. Ask: "where is my attention right now?" When you notice it has wandered, gently note "wandering" and return. This noticing itself is the skill.
