> **Principle 248** — Practise the contemplation of exemplary lives

Marcus begins the Meditations by listing the virtues he learned from others: his grandfather, his father, his mother, his teachers. The contemplation of exemplary lives is a Stoic practice. Study the lives of people you admire. Let their example guide you.

(Med. I.1, I.3, I.5, I.11, I.15, I.16, I.17; Disc. I.2, I.9, I.14; Seneca, Letters 7, 9, 52, 94, 104, 108.)

**Plain words** Marcus opened his journal by thanking people — his grandfather's seriousness, his mother's kindness, a teacher's patience. Keep a mental shelf of people who do things well, and take them down when you need direction. Example is a faster teacher than theory.

*For example:* The new head teacher who, before a hard parent meeting, asks herself "what would my old mentor do here?" — and instantly sits up straighter.
