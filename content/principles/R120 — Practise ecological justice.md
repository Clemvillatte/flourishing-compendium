> **Principle 382** — Practise ecological justice

Work for a society in which environmental benefits and burdens are distributed fairly. Environmental injustice — the disproportionate exposure of marginalised communities to pollution, climate change, and environmental degradation — is a moral failure. Advocate for environmental justice for all communities.

(Schlosberg, 2007, Defining Environmental Justice; Bullard, 1990, Dumping in Dixie.)

**Plain words** Environmental harms and benefits don't fall fairly — marginalised communities get the pollution, the dumps, the climate hits first and worst. That's a moral failure, not an accident of geography. Advocate for environmental justice for all communities.

*For example:* The city that twice approves the incinerator for the same low-income block — and the third time, the whole city shows up, because the pattern finally showed itself.
