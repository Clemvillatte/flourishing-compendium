> **Principle 14** — Preserve your childlike vitality

The child's power is in softness, undivided attention, and not knowing too much. Protect your capacity for wonder, play, and whole-body presence.

(DDJ 10, 28, 55.)

**Plain words** A child's power is softness, undivided attention, and not knowing too much. Adults lose vitality by hardening, fragmenting, and knowing everything in advance. Protect your capacity for wonder and play — that's not childishness, that's the battery.

*For example:* Watch a kid fully absorbed in puddles for forty minutes — that total, undivided absorption is the state adults pay retreats to recover.
