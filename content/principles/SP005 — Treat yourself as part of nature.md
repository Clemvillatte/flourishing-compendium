> **Principle 5** — Treat yourself as part of nature

The preface to Part III says it directly: people are not outside nature, and human affect is not a defect in the universe but a natural fact like hot and cold. You are a mode of Nature, not its judge. The practical consequence for self-relations is large: self-hatred treats your jealousy or fear as a personal scandal, which both intensifies it and hides its cause; treating it as nature treats it as a mechanism you can study and steer.

(Ethics III, Preface; I, P15.)

**Plain words** People are not outside nature, and human affect is not a defect in the universe but a natural fact like hot and cold. You are a mode of Nature, not its judge. For self-relations this is huge: your anxiety and your appetite are weather, not sins.

*For example:* The person who hates themselves for being "so emotional" — and finally treats the emotion like temperature: a fact of the system, to be managed, not a moral failing to be confessed. The thermostat was never a sinner.
