> **Principle 23** — Root yourself in a people

The rooted life, in the tradition, is rooted in a community of mutual obligation: the family, the calpulli (the local kin-neighborhood unit), the work collective. The individual without roots is the tradition's picture of the lost person, not its ideal of freedom. The modern translation keeps the insight while dropping the specific institutions: your well-being is carried by a specific set of people who know you, and you by them. Practically, name your calpulli-equivalent (the people who would show up if you called at 3 a.m.) and give them deliberate time.

(The calpulli structure.)

**Plain words** The rooted life is rooted in a community of mutual obligation — family, neighbourhood, work collective. The individual without roots is the tradition's picture of the lost person, not its ideal of freedom. Your wellbeing is carried by a specific set of people; make sure the set is real and named.

*For example:* The freelancer who has "total freedom" and no one who'd notice if they vanished for a week — and joins the co-op, the club, the rota, discovering the obligations were the missing structure, not the enemy of it.
