> **Principle 300** — Practise whakawhanaungatanga

Actively build relationships across difference. Whakawhanaungatanga is the process of establishing relationship where none existed before, of finding connection across cultural, social, or personal difference. It is the active, intentional version of whanaungatanga.

(Māori tikanga; Mead, 2003, Tikanga Māori; Durie, 1994, Whaiora.)

**Plain words** Whakawhanaungatanga is relationship-building as an active craft: establishing connection where none existed, across cultural, social, or personal difference. Not waiting for common ground — digging for it.

*For example:* The nurse who learns three words of greeting in each language of her ward's patients — small effort, and every patient's whole posture changes in the first thirty seconds.
