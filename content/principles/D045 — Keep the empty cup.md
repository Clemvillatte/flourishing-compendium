> **Principle 45** — Keep the empty cup

Approach everything: a person, a day, an old skill — as if for the first time. Full cups receive nothing.

(DDJ 11, 71; Zhuangzi 4.)

**Plain words** Approach everything — a person, a day, an old skill — as if for the first time. Full cups receive nothing. Expertise is useful until it becomes a wall between you and what's actually happening. Beginner's mind isn't ignorance; it's availability.

*For example:* The veteran teacher who still watches students with fresh eyes catches what the 'seen it all' teacher stops noticing.
