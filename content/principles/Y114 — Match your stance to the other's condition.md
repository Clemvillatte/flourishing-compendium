> **Principle 114** — Match your stance to the other's condition

The social application of the fourfold stance taught in principle 65: friendliness to the happy (against envy), compassion to the unhappy (against pity), delight in the virtuous (against competitive comparison), and even-minded disregard toward the hostile (against entanglement). Each stance is a discipline, and the discipline is chosen by the other's state, not by your mood. Practically, in your hardest relationship, identify their current state and practice the prescribed stance for a week before judging whether the relationship can work.

(YS 1.33, social application; BG 12.13-14.)

**Plain words** The social application of the fourfold stance: friendliness to the happy (against envy), compassion to the unhappy (against pity), delight in the virtuous (against competitive comparison), even-minded disregard toward the hostile (against engagement). Match the stance to the condition.

*For example:* At the reunion: the friend with the dream job gets warmth, the friend in the rough year gets compassion, the humble one gets delight, and the provocateur gets nothing at all. Four doors, four stances, no leakage.
