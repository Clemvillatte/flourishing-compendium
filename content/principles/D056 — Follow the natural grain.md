> **Principle 56** — Follow the natural grain

Cook Ding's blade never wears out because it slides through the natural spaces in the joint. Find the existing seams in people, projects, and problems: work there, not through the bone.

(Zhuangzi 3.)

**Plain words** Cook Ding's blade never dulls because it slides through the natural spaces in the joint instead of hacking through bone. Find the existing seams in people, projects, and problems — work there. There is always a seam; hacking is what you do when you haven't found it.

*For example:* Selling to a customer's actual problem instead of your pitch — the conversation slides because you found the joint.
