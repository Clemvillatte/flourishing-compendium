> **Principle 74** — Make freedom of thought the safety valve

Spinoza's political conclusion, argued across the Theological-Political Treatise and stated with complete clarity in its final chapter: the purpose of the state is really freedom, and it can never be safer than when it allows each person to think and say what they like. The argument is practical, not utopian: minds cannot be compelled, so enforced uniformity produces hypocrisy and conspiracy, while freedom produces loyalty. Practically, in every group you lead or belong to, protect the person who says the unwelcome true thing; they are your early-warning system.

(Theological-Political Treatise, ch. 20; Political Treatise, ch. 1-2.)

**Plain words** The purpose of the state is really freedom, Spinoza concludes — never safer than when each person is allowed to think and say what they will. Make freedom of thought your own safety valve too: the household, the team, the head that can't voice dissent will erupt it.

*For example:* The manager who "doesn't want complaints" — and gets the sabotage, the quiet quitting, the resignation letter with the real news in it. The valve was always cheaper than the explosion. Open it on purpose.
