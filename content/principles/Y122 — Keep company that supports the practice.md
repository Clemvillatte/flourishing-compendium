> **Principle 122** — Keep company that supports the practice

The Gita's counsel on association appears both positively (the wise are met through inquiry) and negatively (the virtue list includes disengagement from fault-finding company), and the Upanishadic method itself assumes a protected learning environment. The bounded formulation: curate your closest five the way you curate your inputs. Practically, name your five most-contacted people, and ask which direction each pulls the practice.

(BG 13.11 context, the company of the settled; Chandogya 6 context, the protected teaching.)

**Plain words** Keep company that supports the practice: the wise met through inquiry, and disengagement from fault-finding company — the tradition is explicit in both directions. The bounded formulation: audit your five closest voices; are they practice or friction?

*For example:* Someone notices the group chat has become a two-hour nightly complaint ritual — and redirects a third of that energy to the friend who's actually building something. The audit doesn't require drama; it requires re-weighting.
