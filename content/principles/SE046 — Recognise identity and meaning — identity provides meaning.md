> **Principle 46** — Recognise identity and meaning: identity provides meaning

Identity is not just descriptive (who I am) but evaluative (what makes my life meaningful). A coherent identity — a life story that makes sense — is a foundation of meaning and purpose. Identity crises are often meaning crises.

**Plain words** Identity isn't just who you are — it's what makes your life feel like it adds up to something. A coherent story gives you a why. When the story fragments, life can be full of events and empty of meaning. You need the narrative, not just the ingredients.

*For example:* Two people retire with identical pensions and health. One says "forty years in the same plant." The other says "I built things with my hands, raised two kids, and outlasted three managers I outworked." Same facts. One of them has a life.
## Psychology parallels

- **Identity and meaning**, coherent identity is associated with meaning and purpose in life (McAdams, 2001; Steger, 2012)
- **Meaning in life and well-being**, presence of meaning predicts well-being (Steger, 2012; Steger et al., 2006)

## Practice

- Attend to the meaning dimension of identity: what makes my life meaningful? What is my story about? If identity feels empty, the issue may be meaning, not identity per se.
