> **Principle 113** — Express by shaping, not venting

Expression is not a pressure valve: it is the shaping of raw feeling into form until the form carries the feeling. When you need to express, don't dump: shape — write it, sing it, build it.

(Dewey, Art as Experience, 1934.)

**Plain words** Expression is not a pressure valve — it's the shaping of raw feeling into form until the form carries the feeling. When you need to express, don't dump: shape. Write it, sing it, build it. The scream relieves for a minute; the shaped thing relieves for years and helps strangers.

*For example:* The grief that becomes a garden — the same energy, given a form. Vented, it would have been three bad weeks. Shaped, it's a place the whole family visits.
