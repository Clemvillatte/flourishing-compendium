> **Principle 122** — Turn maintenance into art

Routine tasks are neither dead time nor necessary evil: done with attention and form, they become small arts: the dish washed well, the email written with grace. Let craft redeem the ordinary.

(Dewey, Art as Experience, 1934.)

**Plain words** Routine tasks are neither dead time nor necessary evil — done with attention and form, they become small arts: the dish washed well, the email written with grace. Let craft redeem the ordinary. You will do the dishes roughly ten thousand more times; that's ten thousand chances for either art or resentment.

*For example:* The barista who cleans the machine like it matters — because it does, to him. Same closing shift, but one person is doing a craft and one is doing time.

## Psychology parallels

- **Mindful activity studies**, attending to routine tasks raises positive affect (Hanley et al., 2015)
