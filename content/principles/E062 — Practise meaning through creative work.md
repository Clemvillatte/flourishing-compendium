> **Principle 454** — Practise meaning through creative work

Find meaning through creative engagement with the world. Creative work, in Frankl's framework, is one of the three paths to meaning: through doing, through experiencing, or through the attitude taken toward unavoidable suffering. Engage in work that is meaningful, not merely instrumental.

(Frankl, 1946/2006, Man's Search for Meaning; Frankl, 1969, The Will to Meaning.)

**Plain words** Creative work is one of the three paths to meaning: doing, experiencing, or the attitude toward unavoidable suffering. Engage in work that means something — creation, not just consumption. The making is the meaning; the product is the receipt.

*For example:* The retired executive who decays in eighteen months of pure leisure — and revives within weeks of taking up the woodworking, the teaching, the garden. The organism needs to make things; the leisure was missing an ingredient.
