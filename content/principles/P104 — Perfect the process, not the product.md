> **Principle 104** — Perfect the process, not the product

Process has priority over product: any achievement is a by-product of the process that made it, and dead once detached from it. Put your pride in how you do things, not only in what is done.

(Rescher, Process Metaphysics, 1996.)

**Plain words** Process has priority over product — any achievement is a by-product of the process that made it, and dead once detached from it. Put your pride in how you do things, not only in what is done. The trophy on the shelf is a receipt; the game is the transaction.

*For example:* The athlete who medals once and keeps training for a decade — the medal was a souvenir. The training was the life. The ones who only wanted the medal are gone by year two.
