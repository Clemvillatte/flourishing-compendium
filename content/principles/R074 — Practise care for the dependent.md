> **Principle 336** — Practise care for the dependent

Recognise that dependency is not a deviation from the human norm but a fundamental feature of human life. Every human being is dependent at the beginning and end of life, and at many points in between. Do not stigmatise dependency; structure your life and your community to meet it.

(Kittay, 1999, Love's Labor: Essays on Women, Equality, and Dependency; Tronto, 2013, Caring Democracy.)

**Plain words** Dependency isn't a deviation from the human norm — it IS the norm: everyone starts and ends life dependent, with stretches in between. So don't stigmatise it, and don't build a life or community that pretends otherwise. Structure for meeting it.

*For example:* The workplace that designs the return-to-work plan for the parent and the post-surgery case with the same seriousness as the budget — because "independence" is a phase, not an identity.
