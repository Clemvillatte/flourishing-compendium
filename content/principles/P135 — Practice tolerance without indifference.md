> **Principle 135** — Practice tolerance without indifference

Pluralism means tolerating other ways of life: not ceasing to care or to argue. Tolerate persons and practices; keep engaging views.

(James, "On a Certain Blindness in Human Beings," 1899; Bernstein, 1983.)

**Plain words** Pluralism means tolerating other ways of life — not ceasing to care or to argue. Tolerate persons and practices; keep engaging views. Tolerance is not agreement and it is not surrender; it's the continued conversation across a real difference.

*For example:* The neighbor with the opposing lawn sign — you argue over the fence twice a year, borrow each other's tools monthly. Toleration with the argument intact. That's the whole thing working.
