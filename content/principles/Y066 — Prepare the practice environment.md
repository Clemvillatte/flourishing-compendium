> **Principle 66** — Prepare the practice environment

The Gita is exact about the setup: a firm seat, neither too high nor too low, in a clean place, with the senses and mind gathered, and the body held as one piece. The instructions look liturgical but read as environmental design for attention. Practically, designate and prepare one physical spot, and let its constancy do part of the work.

(BG 6.11-6.13; YS 2.46.)

**Plain words** The Gita's setup is exact: a firm seat, neither too high nor too low, in a clean place, senses and mind gathered, body held as one piece. It reads liturgical but functions as environmental design for attention. Prepare the practice environment on purpose.

*For example:* The person who "just can't get into it" at home — designates the corner, the cushion, the same hour, the clean surface — and discovers the mind starts dropping into practice on approach, like a dog at the park.
