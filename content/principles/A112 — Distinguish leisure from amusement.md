> **Principle 112** — Distinguish leisure from amusement

Amusement is a form of rest, not an end in itself. Leisure (scholē) is higher than amusement: it is activity for its own sake, not for recovery from work. Do not arrange your life around amusement. Arrange it around leisure in the Aristotelian sense.

(Politics VII.14-15; NE X.6.)

**Plain words** Amusement is a rest from work, not a goal. Leisure is higher: activity for its own sake. Aristotle says to arrange life around leisure, not just amusement.

*For example:* Binge-watching to recover from work is amusement. Spending the same hours learning the guitar is closer to leisure.
