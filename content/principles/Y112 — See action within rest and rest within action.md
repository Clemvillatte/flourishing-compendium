> **Principle 112** — See action within rest and rest within action

The Gita's paradox: the wise see inaction in action and action in inaction; the one who sits in activity (whose actions are consumed by knowledge) rests, and the one who sits still while craving is busy. The bounded formulation: rest is defined by inner weather, not by posture, and activity can be restful when unclaimed. Practically, schedule one restful action per week (an act done with full attention and no claim) and treat it as rest.

(BG 4.18; BG 4.19.)

**Plain words** The paradox: the wise see inaction in action and action in inaction — the one whose actions are consumed by knowledge rests even while working, and the one who sits still while craving is busy. Rest is defined by inner weather, not by furniture.

*For example:* Two people in the same deck chair on the same beach: one is on holiday, one is rehearsing a war. Only one of them is resting — and it's not the one who "did nothing" all week.
