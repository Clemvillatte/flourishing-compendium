> **Principle 347** — Practise availability (disponibilité)

Make yourself available to the other person and to the mystery of being. Availability is Gabriel Marcel's concept of the disposition to be present, open, and receptive to the other. It is the opposite of being "unavailable" — absorbed, distracted, enclosed in oneself. Practise availability by being truly present when someone speaks to you.

(Marcel, 1951a, Being and Having; Marcel, 1967, The Philosophy of Existentialism.)

**Plain words** Disponibilité: availability — the disposition to be actually present and open, versus the absorbed, distracted, enclosed-in-oneself default. When someone speaks to you, be there for it. The opposite of availability isn't rudeness; it's absence wearing your face.

*For example:* The parent who puts the phone face-down in another room during the bedtime story — and the kid notices, and the story lands completely differently than the version told over a screen.
