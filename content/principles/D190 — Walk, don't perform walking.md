> **Principle 190** — Walk, don't perform walking

The Way is walked, not discussed: one hour of practice outweighs a library of commentary.

(Zhuangzi 13; DDJ 1.)

**Plain words** The Way is walked, not discussed — one hour of practice outweighs a library of commentary. The person reading their tenth book on meditation is procrastinating at altitude. At some point the map must touch the territory.

*For example:* The person with the full home gym and the app subscription — versus the one who just walks. Only one of them is exercising.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Behavioral activation|Behavioral activation]]**, doing, not discussing, is the active ingredient against depression (Jacobson et al., 1996; Martell et al., 2001)

## Philosophy parallels


- **[[Praxis|Praxis]]** (Marx, Theses on Feuerbach XI), "the philosophers have only interpreted the world; the point is to change it"
- **[[Dao|The Dao — the way things work]]** (Daoism, Daodejing 25)
