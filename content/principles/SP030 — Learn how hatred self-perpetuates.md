> **Principle 30** — Learn how hatred self-perpetuates

Hatred is increased by returned hatred, and destroyed by love, so that hatred is returned for hatred, and the cycle Spinoza describes is the exact blueprint of every feud: each injury justifies the counter-injury, which becomes the next injury's justification. His prescription is counterintuitive and structural: meet hatred with kindness, not as a moral ornament but as the only intervention that removes the fuel. Practically, in one running conflict, run the experiment Spinoza prescribes: answer coldness with one act of genuine benefit, and watch the system rather than the moment.

(Ethics III, P43; the note to IV, P73: hatred overcome with love.)

**Plain words** Hatred is increased by returned hatred and destroyed by love: the cycle is the blueprint of every feud — each injury justifies the counter-injury, which becomes the next injury's justification. Spinoza states the engineering plainly: you cannot hate your way out of a hatred cycle. Someone has to stop returning it.

*For example:* The family feud in its fortieth year — every funeral re-litigating 1984 — and the one member who stops returning the hatred, not from weakness but from having read the schematic. The cycle doesn't end with the last hater; it ends with the first non-returner.
