> **Principle 1** — See experience as constructed before reacting to it

The suffering you know does not arrive raw from the world; it arrives through the activity of your own mind, which selects, colors, and narrates before you are aware it has done so. Yoga begins here: the movements of the mind (chitta-vritti) are the medium of your life, and they can be examined rather than obeyed. Practically, when a strong reaction arises, ask what the mind added to the event.

(YS 1.2-1.4.)

**Plain words** Your suffering doesn't arrive raw from the world — it arrives through your mind, which has already selected, colored, and narrated it before you noticed. Yoga starts here: the mind's movements are the medium of your life, and they can be examined rather than obeyed. When a strong reaction arises, ask what the mind added.

*For example:* Someone gets a curt email and feels the day is ruined — then re-reads the same words as rushed rather than hostile, and the "ruin" turns out to have been ninety percent added in transit.
