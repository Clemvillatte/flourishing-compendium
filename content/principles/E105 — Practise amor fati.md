> **Principle 497** — Practise amor fati

Love your fate. Do not merely endure what happens to you; affirm it. Amor fati is not passive resignation but the active, joyful affirmation of everything that constitutes your life — including suffering, loss, and failure. Practise the love of fate.

(Nietzsche, 1882/1974, The Gay Science, §276; Nietzsche, 1882/1974, The Gay Science, §277; Nietzsche, 1888/1992, Ecce Homo, "Why I Am So Clever," §10.)

**Plain words** Amor fati: love your fate — not merely endure but affirm what happens to you, including the suffering, loss, and failure. Not resignation; the active, joyful affirmation of everything that constitutes your life. Practise the love of fate — hardest at the exact moments it matters.

*For example:* Someone's worst year — the diagnosis, the loss, the collapse — and somewhere in it, the strange turn: not approval of the events, but the refusal to wish the life that contains them away. The affirmation doesn't make it hurt less; it makes it yours.
