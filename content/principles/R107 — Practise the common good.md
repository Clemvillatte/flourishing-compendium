> **Principle 369** — Practise the common good

Seek the common good, not merely your private good or the aggregate of private goods. The common good is the set of conditions that enable all members of a community to flourish. It is not achieved by maximising individual utility but by creating the social, political, and cultural conditions for shared flourishing.

(Aristotle, Politics III; MacIntyre, 1981, After Virtue; Sandel, 2009, Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do?)

**Plain words** The common good isn't your private good, and it isn't private goods added up — it's the set of conditions that let everyone in the community flourish. Create those conditions: social, political, cultural. A rising tide is not the same thing as a healthy sea.

*For example:* The neighbourhood that could each buy generators — or together fix the drainage that floods every one of them; the aggregate purchase loses to the common good.
