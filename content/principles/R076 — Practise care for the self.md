> **Principle 338** — Practise care for the self

Do not neglect your own care in the process of caring for others. Self-care is not selfish; it is a prerequisite for sustainable caring. You cannot pour from an empty cup.

(Noddings, 2002, Starting at Home; Tronto, 2013, Caring Democracy.)

**Plain words** Care for yourself isn't selfish — it's the prerequisite for sustainable caring. You can't pour from an empty cup, and pretending otherwise just moves the emptiness onto the people depending on you. Self-care is part of the care.

*For example:* The carer who finally takes the offered respite weekend — and returns patient enough to keep going for another year, which is exactly what the person they love needs.
