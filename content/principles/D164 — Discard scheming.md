> **Principle 164** — Discard scheming

Cut out cleverness and profit-scheming, abandon contrived benevolence: the machinations, not the kindness. Simple beats cunning, long-term.

(DDJ 19, 57.)

**Plain words** Cut out cleverness and profit-scheming; abandon contrived benevolence — the machinations, not the kindness. Simple beats cunning long-term, because cunning has to keep outsmarting a world that's taking notes. Scheming is a treadmill with a view.

*For example:* The office politician who wins the quarter and loses the decade — everyone eventually updates their model of them.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Machiavellianism|Machiavellianism]]**, correlates with lower long-term well-being and unstable relationships
- **Generous strategies win iterated games** (Axelrod, 1984)

## Philosophy parallels

- **[[The categorical imperative|The categorical imperative forbids manipulation]]** (Kant, Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals)
- **[[The corruption of amour-propre|The corruption of amour-propre]]** (Rousseau, Discourse on the Origin of Inequality)
