> **Principle 172** — Value silence

Cultivate periods without speech, without input: let the mind hear itself. Silence is not empty; it is where the noise becomes audible.

(Zen tradition; MN 122.)

**Plain words** Cultivate periods without speech, without input — let the mind hear itself. Silence is not empty; it's where the noise becomes audible. The first ten minutes are unbearable; that's the noise, leaving.

*For example:* The first silent car ride without the radio — twitchy for ten minutes, then something settles that hasn't settled in years.

## Psychology parallels

- **[[Restorative environments|Restorative environments]]**, quiet and natural settings aid recovery from attentional load (Kaplan, 1995)

## Philosophy parallels

- **Silence and the limits of language** (Wittgenstein, Tractatus 7)
