> **Principle 5** — Carry the ten modes lightly, as spot-checks

The tradition's ten modes of inducing suspension (differences of animal, person, senses, circumstances, positions, mixtures, quantities, frequency, relation, and custom) are a catalogue of the relativity of perception. The distinct use: spot-checking any strong impression against the modes (would this look different from another position? to another perceiver? in another quantity?). Practically, take your current strongest impression and run it through three of the modes in one minute.

(Sextus Empiricus, Outlines, Book I, the ten modes.)

**Plain words** Keep the old list of ten ways perception shifts — different animal, different person, different position, different quantity — as spot-checks, not homework. When an impression grabs you, pick three: would this look different from across the street? To a stranger? In a smaller dose? Usually, yes.

*For example:* The insult that stings in the meeting shrinks when run through the modes — from the back of the room it was barely audible, and the person who said it was running on three hours' sleep.
