> **Principle 116** — Understand fear (phobos)

Fear is a pain or disturbance caused by the imagination of future evil. Fear involves a judgment about danger: that something bad is coming and that you are vulnerable. Examine your fears. Are the imagined evils real? Is your vulnerability as great as you think?

(Rhetoric II.5; NE III.6.)

**Plain words** Fear is distress about imagined future harm. It judges that something bad is coming and that you're vulnerable. So check the picture. Is the harm real? Are you as exposed as it feels?

*For example:* Dreading a talk, you picture the room laughing. Asking what's most likely to happen puts the fear in proportion.
