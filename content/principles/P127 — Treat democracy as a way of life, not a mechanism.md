> **Principle 127** — Treat democracy as a way of life, not a mechanism

Democracy is not primarily voting; it is a mode of associated living in which experience is freely shared and each counts as one. Practice democracy in your kitchen, team, and friendships.

(Dewey, Democracy and Education, 1916; The Public and Its Problems, 1927.)

**Plain words** Democracy is not primarily voting — it's a mode of associated living in which experience is freely shared and each counts as one. Practice democracy in your kitchen, team, and friendships. The ballot box is the tail; the dinner table is the dog.

*For example:* The family where the kids get a real vote on the move — not a fake vote, a real one, with their interests counted. That's democracy as a way of life, not a field trip.
