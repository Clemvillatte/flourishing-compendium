> **Principle 188** — Return good for the world's bad faith

Expect the world to sometimes misuse your goodness; give well anyway: the alternative is becoming what you opposed.

(DDJ 49, 63.)

**Plain words** Cynicism is letting the worst actors set your curriculum. Guarded generosity still beats armored bitterness.

*For example:* The volunteer burned once by the grifter who keeps helping — smarter about it now, but still helping. That's the whole art.
