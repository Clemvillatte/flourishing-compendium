> **Principle 114** — Give (dana)

Generosity is the first practice: it breaks the grip of grasping and connects you to others before any technique is learned. Give first: money, time, attention.

(Dhammapada 224; AN 5.35.)

**Plain words** Generosity is the first practice — it breaks the grip of grasping and connects you to others before any technique is learned. Give first: money, time, attention. The giving loosens the hand, and the loosened hand is the whole beginning.

*For example:* The new volunteer who arrives to help others and discovers their own loneliness dissolving — the gift was always two-way.
