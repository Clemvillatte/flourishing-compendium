> **Principle 370** — Practise deliberative community

Participate in collective deliberation about the common good. Do not leave public life to politicians and experts. Engage in the deliberative practices of your community: town meetings, community organisations, public forums. Your voice matters.

(Habermas, 1996, Between Facts and Norms; Sandel, 2009, Justice; Bohman, 1996, Public Deliberation.)

**Plain words** Don't leave public life to the professionals. Join the deliberation: town meetings, community organisations, forums — the places where the common good gets worked out. Your voice is a load-bearing part of it.

*For example:* The school parents' night where the same four voices always decide — until the quiet parent who "doesn't do politics" speaks once about the bus route, and changes the year for forty kids.
