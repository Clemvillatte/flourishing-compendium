> **Principle 19** — Awakening is available in this life

Liberation is not postponed to another existence: a shift of seeing: immediate, embodied, ordinary — is possible now. Practice toward what is already near.

(Platform Sutra; Batchelor, After Buddhism.)

**Plain words** Liberation isn't postponed to another existence — a shift of seeing, immediate, embodied, ordinary, is possible now. Practice toward what is already near. The exotic version of awakening is often just procrastination in ritual costume.

*For example:* The moment of ordinary presence while washing dishes — no bells, no lights, just full contact with warm water. That's the near thing.
