> **Principle 19** — Awakening is available in this life

The Platform Sutra locates the material of awakening inside the practitioner: "In the self-nature of each of you there is limitless wisdom. Maha Prajna Paramita is originally fully present within your self-nature. You need not seek it outside."

> You should enlighten yourself and not seek outside. If you hear the Sudden Teaching you may become enlightened right away.
>
> (Platform Sutra, Prajna chapter)

The compendium's reading is that "not seek outside" and "originally fully present" are claims about location: the seeing is near rather than remote. The Sudden Teaching passage adds that hearing it rightly can be the occasion. Practice is toward what is already at hand.

(Platform Sutra.)

**Plain words**
The claim is about location, not speed: nothing has to be fetched from elsewhere. The exercise: take one ordinary task today and give it your full attention. That is practicing toward what the text says is already at hand.

*For example:* The moment of ordinary presence while washing dishes — no bells, no lights, just full contact with warm water. That's the near thing.
