> **Principle 144** — Let cognition extend into the environment

Mind leaks into the world: notebooks, calendars, kitchens, teammates are parts of the thinking system. Design your environment as cognitive architecture.

(The 4E turn; James, The Principles of Psychology — habits as environment-cued.)

**Plain words** Mind leaks into the world — notebooks, calendars, kitchens, teammates are parts of the thinking system. Design your environment as cognitive architecture. Your mind is not the brain; it's the brain plus the desk, the whiteboard, and the friend who gets it.

*For example:* The shopping list on the fridge — that's not a memory aid, that's your mind, extending into the kitchen. The person with no systems is trying to think with a smaller mind than they own.
