> **Principle 99** — Hold success and failure level

Evenness (samatva) is defined as yoga itself: the mind held level in gain and loss, pleasant and painful. The tradition's reason is structural: the valuation that swings with outcomes is the swing. Practically, choose a levelness exercise for the day's swings: one sentence spoken to yourself at each peak and each valley, the same sentence.

(BG 2.48.)

**Plain words** Evenness is defined as yoga itself: the mind held level in gain and loss, pleasant and painful. The reason is structural — the valuation that swings with outcomes is the swing. Choose a levelness exercise for the day's swings: one sentence spoken at the top, one at the bottom.

*For example:* Someone writes the same three-word note on the day of the triumphant launch and the day of the layoff announcement — "this too, level" — and the note does what the note is for: reminding the swing that there's a floor and a ceiling, both held.
