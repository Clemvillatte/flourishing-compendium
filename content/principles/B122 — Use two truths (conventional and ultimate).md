> **Principle 122** — Use two truths (conventional and ultimate)

Conventionally, the self and the world work as usual; ultimately, nothing has independent essence. Don't confuse the registers: use each where it functions.

(Nagarjuna, MMK 24.8-10.)

**Plain words** Conventionally, the self and the world work as usual; ultimately, nothing has independent essence. Don't confuse the registers — use each where it functions. You need the convention to catch the bus; you need the emptiness to not grieve the bus.

*For example:* Money is 'real' at the checkout and 'empty' under analysis — both true, both useful, in their respective lanes.
