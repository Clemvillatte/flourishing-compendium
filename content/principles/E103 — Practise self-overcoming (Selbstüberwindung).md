> **Principle 495** — Practise self-overcoming (Selbstüberwindung)

Recognise that the fundamental drive of life is self-overcoming — the surpassing of what you are toward what you can become. Nietzsche's concept of self-overcoming is not mere self-improvement but the transcendence of the self through the creation of new values. Do not be content with who you are. Overcome yourself.

(Nietzsche, 1883/1961, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "Of Self-Overcoming"; Nietzsche, 1886/2001, Beyond Good and Evil, §259; Nietzsche, 1882/1974, The Gay Science, §289.)

**Plain words** Selbstüberwindung: the fundamental drive of life is self-overcoming — surpassing what you are toward what you can become. Not mere self-improvement (better versions of the same self) but transcendence through the creation of new values, new works, new ways. Climb past yourself.

*For example:* The musician who masters the style and then has to break it — the next album isn't a better version, it's a surpassing. The tradition says the same drive runs through every living thing: not to remain, but to exceed.
