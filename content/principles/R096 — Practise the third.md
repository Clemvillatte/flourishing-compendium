> **Principle 358** — Practise the third

Recognise that the ethical relation is not only between two (I and Thou) but also involves the third — all the other persons who also demand justice. Justice is the movement from the face-to-face encounter to the question of how to distribute responsibility among all who make claims on you.

(Levinas, 1969, Otherwise Than Being; Levinas, 1961/1969, Totality and Infinity.)

**Plain words** It's not just you and the other — there's always a third: everyone else who also makes claims on you. Justice is the move from the face-to-face encounter to the question of how responsibility gets distributed among all who call on you. Love your neighbour; adjudicate among neighbours.

*For example:* The manager who'd give anything for the struggling team member — but has eleven others with claims too — and has to turn care into a fair system, which is what the third demands.
