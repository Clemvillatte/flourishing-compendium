> **Principle 47** — Learn focused attention meditation: concentration on a single object

Focused attention (FA) meditation involves concentrating on a single object (breath, body sensation, visual object, mantra) and returning attention to it when it wanders. This trains concentration, reduces mind-wandering, and builds attentional stability.

**Plain words** Focused-attention meditation is gym work: one object (breath), and every time the mind leaves, bring it back — that's the rep. It's not about staying; it's about returning. Boredom and frustration are the burn, not the failure.

*For example:* Ten minutes on the breath, mind off to the holiday forty times, forty returns — that's not a bad session. That's forty reps. The session was perfect.
## Psychology parallels

- **Focused attention meditation improves concentration**, regular practice improves sustained attention and reduces distractibility (Lutz et al., 2008; Tang et al., 2015)
- **FA meditation reduces mind-wandering**, training the return from distraction reduces spontaneous mind-wandering (Mrazek et al., 2013; Hasenkamp et al., 2012)

## Practice

- Practise focused attention: 10-20 minutes daily, attending to breath. When attention wanders, notice and gently return. The return itself is the training. Build gradually.
