> **Principle 392** — Practise the more-than-human relational self

Expand your sense of self to include the more-than-human world. You are not only in relationship with other humans; you are in relationship with animals, plants, landscapes, waters, and weather. These relationships are not metaphorical; they are real, and they shape who you are.

(Abram, 1996, The Spell of the Sensuous; Plumwood, 1993, Feminism and the Mastery of Nature.)

**Plain words** Your relationships aren't only with humans — animals, plants, landscapes, waters, weather: real relationships that shape who you are. Expand the relational self past the species line. They're not metaphors; they're kin.

*For example:* The old man and the river he's fished for fifty years — knows its moods, defends it at council meetings, and would say with a straight face that it's the steadiest relationship of his life.
