> **Principle 120** — Meet people where they are

The Gita's instruction to the wise: do not disturb the minds of those attached to action and its rewards; let them work and rejoice while you act with discipline. The principle is pedagogical humility: teaching beyond readiness hardens rather than opens. Practically, with one person you want to fix, stop advancing the argument for a week, model instead, and let the model do the slow work.

(BG 3.26.)

**Plain words** Do not disturb the minds of those attached to action and its rewards — let them work and rejoice while you act with discipline. Pedagogical humility: teaching beyond readiness hardens rather than opens. With one person in your life, practise saying less.

*For example:* The convert who lectures their whole family at every dinner — versus the one who just lives differently and answers questions when asked. The tradition's rule: the second one is the teacher; the first is just another agitation.
