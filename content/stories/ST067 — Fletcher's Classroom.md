*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2014 · [Damien Chazelle, Whiplash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whiplash_%282014_film%29)*

Andrew Neiman, a jazz drummer at an elite conservatory, is pushed by conductor Terence Fletcher past every limit: humiliation, bleeding hands, a car crash answered with a demand to play. Fletcher's theory is explicit: there are no two words more harmful than "good job." Andrew achieves ferocious technical brilliance, and the final scene, in which he seizes control of the performance, can be read as triumph or as the complete destruction of a person, and the film refuses to decide.

Read against the lute-string sutta, the film is the counter-example. Every string is wound past breaking, and the movie's achievement is that the audience feels the cost inside the exhilaration.
