*Biographical/film narrative, cross-cultural · Ireland, 1954/1989 · [My Left Foot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Left_Foot_%28film%29)*

Christy Brown, born in 1932 into a working-class Dublin family, has cerebral palsy so severe that he can control only his left foot. The family's story turns on the day his mother, alone with him, sees the boy everyone has written off take a piece of chalk between his toes and scrape the letter A on the kitchen floor. He goes on to write and paint with that foot, producing an autobiography, novels, and a body of work.

The story is the [[The lived body|lived body]]'s purest case study. Brown's achievement is not described as triumph over his body, which he did not defeat, but as the constitution of a body-subject around the one limb that answered. The A on the floor is the moment the world, for one family, reorganised around a different body's possibilities, and the memoir and film preserve it as such.
