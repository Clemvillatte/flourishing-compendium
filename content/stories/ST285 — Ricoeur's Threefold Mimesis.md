*Philosophical metaphor · France, 1983-1985 · [Paul Ricoeur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Ricoeur)*

Ricoeur, in Time and Narrative, describes three levels of the relation between life and story. Mimesis1: the pre-understanding of the world of action, its goals, motives, agents. Mimesis2: the configuration of events into a plot, the emplotment that makes a story. Mimesis3: the refiguration, the intersection of the world of the text and the world of the reader, where the story changes the life. Narrative identity, the self as the story one tells and lives, is the fruit of the whole circuit.

The theory is the most complete account of the narrative self, and its practical content is the discovery that a life and a story are not two things: the living is shaped by the telling, which is shaped by the living. The clinical descendants, narrative therapy and McAdams's research programme, are the operationalisations of the circuit.
