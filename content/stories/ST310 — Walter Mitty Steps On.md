*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2013 · [The Secret Life of Walter Mitty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Secret_Life_of_Walter_Mitty_(2013_film))*


Walter Mitty is a negative assets manager at Life magazine, and his daydreams, the secret life of the title, are elaborate heroic fantasies he experiences while his actual life is one of quiet competence and no visible adventure. The magazine is going digital, and the film's plot, his search for a missing negative from a legendary photographer, takes him to Greenland, Iceland, and the Himalayas, and the daydreams stop, the film notes, when the life starts.

The film is usually filed under adventure, and its content is the engagement literature's most direct claim: the zone, the broadened attention, the absorbed state, is available in the actual world, and the daydream is its cheap substitute. Walter's competence, the film shows, was always real, and the risk was only the step.
