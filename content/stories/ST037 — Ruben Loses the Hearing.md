*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2019 · [Sound of Metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_of_Metal)*

Ruben is a metal drummer who begins to lose his hearing, rapidly and irreversibly. His first instinct is to fix it: cochlear implants, expensive, urgent, funded by whatever he can sell. Deaf friends who have made their lives in the Deaf community show him something else: a world with its own language, its own pace, not a diminished version of hearing life. The film ends with Ruben sitting in stillness, having chosen to stop grasping at the restoration.

The film's honesty is in refusing either pole. His grief is real, the loss is real, and the community is not presented as a consolation prize. The freedom he reaches is the same one the Zhuangzi describes at the bottom of the well: the willingness to live the life that is actually available.
