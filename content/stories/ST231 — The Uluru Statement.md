*Public/civic narrative · Australia, 2017 · [Uluru Statement from the Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uluru_Statement_from_the_Heart)*

In 2017, after a process of regional dialogues, First Nations delegates from across Australia meet at Uluru and issue a one-page statement. It is not a petition or a demand. It is an invitation: to the Australian people, to walk with them toward a First Nations Voice to Parliament and a Makarrata Commission for treaty-making and truth-telling. The statement's prose is the shortest in Australian public life, and its structure is an argument about relation: "Our Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander tribes were the first sovereign Nations of this continent," it begins, and it ends with a request to be heard.

The document's form is its content. Where a petition asserts claims against an authority, an invitation constitutes a relationship, and the delegates chose the form deliberately. The subsequent history, including the 2023 referendum's failure, is part of the story the statement continues to tell.
