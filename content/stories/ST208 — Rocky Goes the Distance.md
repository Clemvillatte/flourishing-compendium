*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1976 · [Rocky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky)*

Rocky Balboa, a club fighter, is given a million-to-one shot at the heavyweight championship as a publicity stunt. He tells Adrian his real goal: he cannot win, but if he can go the distance, be standing at the end of the fifteenth round, he will know, for the first time, that he is not just another bum from the neighbourhood. He loses the decision. He is still standing. In the ring, he does not wait for the announcement; he is calling for Adrian.

The film's structure is the dichotomy of control in boxing gloves. The title, the judges, the odds: not his. The distance, his training, his ability to remain standing: his, and the film's entire emotional payoff is built on the audience learning, along with Rocky, that these were the only stakes in the fight.
