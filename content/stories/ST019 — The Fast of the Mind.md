*Zhuangzi parable · China, 4th century BCE · [Zhuangzi, ch. 4, "In the World of Men"](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/in-the-world-of-men)*

Yan Hui wants to go reform the tyrant of Wei. Confucius demolishes each plan he offers: all of them are strategies laid over an agitated mind. Before Yan Hui can act, he must fast, but not the kind that abstains from food. "Do not listen with your ears; listen with your mind. Do not listen with your mind; listen with qi." The mind, emptied of plans and proofs, becomes still and ready.

The dialogue is the oldest systematic account of what preparation for a hard conversation actually involves. It is not the gathering of arguments. It is the quieting of the agitated strategist who wants to win.
