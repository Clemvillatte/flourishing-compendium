*Biographical narrative, cross-cultural · India/Burma, 20th century · [Dipa Ma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipa_Ma)*

Dipa Ma, a Bengali housewife, loses her husband and two of her three children, and develops severe heart trouble. At 53, told she had months to live, she begins intensive meditation practice in Burma. She recovers, and within years she is recognised as a master of the deepest concentration states. She returns to raising her surviving daughter and, famously, to teaching householders: she trained mothers to practise in the interstices of child care, telling students that anger could be watched while stirring rice.

She is the modern tradition's clearest case against the excuse of circumstance. No monastery, no leisure, a damaged heart, and a child. The practice, she demonstrated, fits inside an ordinary life because an ordinary life is made of moments, and moments are all it requires.
