*Sporting/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · Kenya/Austria, 2019 · [Eliud Kipchoge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliud_Kipchoge)*

In Vienna, Kipchoge runs a marathon in 1:59:40, the first time a human has covered the distance in under two hours. It is not a record; the conditions were engineered. Kipchoge lives in a spartan training camp in Kaptagat, shares chores with younger runners, and famously smiles in the late stages of races. Afterwards he said: "No human is limited."

The effort is enormous; the resistance is minimal. Watching him, commentators reach for the word "effortless" because there is no visible fight anywhere in his form. His own account is religious in tone but operationally precise: he does not battle the distance, he aligns with it, metre by metre.
