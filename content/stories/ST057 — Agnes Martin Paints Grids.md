*Artistic/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · Canada/United States, 1960s-1990s · [Agnes Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnes_Martin)*

Martin painted nothing but grids for decades: hand-drawn pencil lines and pale bands of colour on square canvases, in solitude in the New Mexico desert, living without most conveniences, hearing voices she described as inspiration and schizophrenia in the same breath. Her titles are the explanation: "Innocence," "Gratitude," "Happiness." She wrote that her paintings were about "what we know almost nothing of," the subtle emotions of innocence and responsiveness, and that the work was not about the grid at all. The grid was the trap; the meaning was the fish.

Her refinement is of a different order from technique. She spent fifty years narrowing art down to the state of mind a perfectly quiet surface could induce in a still observer, and asked nothing else of painting.
