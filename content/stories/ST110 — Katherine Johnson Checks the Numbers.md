*Historical/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1960s · [Katherine Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Johnson)*

Katherine Johnson, a Black mathematician at NASA, calculates the trajectory for John Glenn's orbital flight. The new electronic computer has produced a result, and Glenn, before boarding, asks that Johnson verify it by hand: if she says the numbers are good, he says, he is ready to go. She checks the output against her own computations, including the calculations for the exact point of re-entry.

The episode is preserved because of Glenn's line, but the substance is Johnson's method. Verification here is not ceremony; it is the last and most important step of inquiry, the submission of the machine's answer to a human check the machine cannot perform on itself.
