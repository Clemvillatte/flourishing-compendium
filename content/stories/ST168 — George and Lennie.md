*Literary narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1937 · [John Steinbeck, Of Mice and Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Of_Mice_and_Men)*

George Milton and Lennie Small, itinerant ranch hands in the Depression, share a dream: a small farm, rabbits Lennie will tend. Lennie is mentally disabled and immensely strong, and George, exhausted, sometimes dreams of the free life he could have alone. When Lennie accidentally kills a rancher's wife and a lynch mob forms, George finds him at their meeting place, tells him the story of the farm one last time, and shoots him.

The novel's Aristotle is in the framing, not the ending: the friendship of utility and the friendship of virtue are constantly contrasted in the bunkhouse, and George's final act is the terrible price of the second kind. The novel asks what it costs to love someone for their own sake when they cannot reciprocate, and it answers honestly.
