*Literary/film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1996/2007 · [Into the Wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Into_the_Wild)*

Christopher McCandless graduates, gives away his savings, and walks into the Alaskan wilderness with minimal supplies, idealising solitude and self-sufficiency. He is found dead four months later in an abandoned bus. His journal's last legible line: "Happiness only real when shared."

The story is the negative image of [[Yangsheng (cultivating vitality)|yangsheng]], the cultivation of vitality. The body McCandless brought to the wilderness was untrained, unsupported, and alone, and the ideal could not survive contact with actual conditions. His final line, written as he was dying, is the correction the whole tradition would have offered him at the start.
