*Literary/film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1996/2007 · [Into the Wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Into_the_Wild)*

Christopher McCandless graduates, gives away his savings, and walks into the Alaskan wilderness with minimal supplies, idealising solitude and self-sufficiency. He is found dead four months later in an abandoned bus, his remains discovered by hunters on 6 September 1992. His journal's final written entry, Day 107, read only "BEAUTIFUL BLUE BERRIES"; the days after it were marked with slashes until they stopped. Beside a passage in his copy of Doctor Zhivago he wrote "HAPPINESS ONLY REAL WHEN SHARED."

The story is the negative image of [[Yangsheng (cultivating vitality)|yangsheng]], the cultivation of vitality. The body McCandless brought to the wilderness was untrained, unsupported, and alone, and the ideal could not survive contact with actual conditions. The note he pencilled in the margin as he weakened is the correction the whole tradition would have offered him at the start.
