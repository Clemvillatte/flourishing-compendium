*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1998 · [The Truman Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Truman_Show)*

Truman begins to withhold assent from his world. A studio light falls from the sky; the radio narrates his movements; the traffic loops. He starts running experiments: he bursts into a shop he has passed a thousand times, he drives through the fire, he demands an answer from his wife, whose product-placement speech breaks the illusion further. The tests culminate in the boat, the storm, and the hand on the wall of the world.

The film is popularly read as a media allegory, but its mechanics are Epictetus'. The discipline of assent is exactly this: the refusal to automatically ratify an impression, the active testing of it, and the willingness to follow the test wherever it leads, including out the door of the only world one has known.
