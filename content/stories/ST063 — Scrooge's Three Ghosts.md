*Literary narrative, cross-cultural · England, 1843 · [Charles Dickens, A Christmas Carol](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/46/46-h/46-h.htm)*

Scrooge is shown his past (what he has lost), his present (what he refuses to see), and his future (what will happen if nothing changes). He wakes on Christmas Day and transforms, becoming generous and warm overnight. The story has been criticised for exactly this, and the criticism misses the mechanism: Dickens understood that character is built through repeated action, and the ghosts supply something no sermon could, the direct re-experiencing of consequence.

The story remains the most complete popular treatment of the claim that a life is a pattern, not a substance. Scrooge does not learn a lesson. He revisits his own causality, and the pattern changes.
