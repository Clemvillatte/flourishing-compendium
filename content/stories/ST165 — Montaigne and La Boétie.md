*Historical/biographical narrative · France, 16th century · [Michel de Montaigne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_de_Montaigne)*

Montaigne's friendship with Étienne de La Boétie ended with La Boétie's death at 32, and Montaigne wrote that if pressed to say why he loved him, he could only answer: because it was he, because it was I. The friendship, he says, had no reason, no occasion, no purpose beyond itself; it was not built by argument but found, complete, like a kinship.

The essay "On Friendship" is the classic statement of Aristotle's third and highest kind of friendship, the one grounded in virtue rather than utility or pleasure. Montaigne's added claim is stronger and stranger: that such a friendship is not chosen but discovered, and that he never recovered it, writing about it for the rest of his life.
