*Biographical/cultural narrative, cross-cultural · Japan, 2011-2019 · [Marie Kondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Kondo)*

Kondo's method is famous for one question. Hold each object you own and ask whether it sparks joy. If not, thank it and let it go. Behind the meme is a serious claim about desire: most of what we own was acquired by impulse, guilt, or aspiration rather than need, and each object requires ongoing attention that could go elsewhere.

The thanking is the part people find strange, and it is the part that matters. It treats the relationship with possessions as a relationship, one that can be ended well rather than abandoned. Knowing what is enough turns out to be a skill, practised object by object.
