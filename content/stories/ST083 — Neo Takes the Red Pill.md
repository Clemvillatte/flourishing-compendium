*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1999 · [The Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Matrix)*

Neo is offered the blue pill, which returns him to comfortable ignorance, or the red pill, which shows him that his entire world is a simulation. He takes the red pill. The rest of the film is the cost of the seeing: the body he never knew he had, the muscles rebuilt, the food that is gruel, and the responsibility he cannot give back.

The film is popularly read as Plato's cave, and it is, but its structure is the Buddhist one. The world as commonly conceived is a construction; seeing through it is not a consolation but a disruption, and the tradition's texts are unusually honest about this. Liberation, in both, is initially experienced as loss.
