*Film/historical narrative, cross-cultural · Poland/Germany, 1943 · [Schindler's List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schindler%27s_List)*

Oskar Schindler arrives in Kraków as a profiteer, hiring Jewish workers because they are cheap and bribing his way to wealth. Something crosses over: the bribes reverse direction, from buying influence to buying lives, until his entire fortune is spent and he is negotiating with Auschwitz for names. At the end, surrounded by the people he saved, he breaks down: I could have got more out, this watch, this car.

The film's centre of gravity is that scene. Schindler is not depicted as having become good; he is depicted as having discovered, too late to complete it, what the money had been for all along. The list is the exact record of the discovery.
