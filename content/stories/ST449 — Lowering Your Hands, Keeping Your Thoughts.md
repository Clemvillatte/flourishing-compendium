Contemporary ACT · experiential metaphor · Russ Harris

Harris asks a person to imagine their hands as thoughts and feelings. Raised in front of the eyes, the hands obscure the room and make ordinary tasks awkward. Lowered into the lap, they leave the room visible again. The hands remain, available for use ([author's exercise](https://www.actmindfully.com.au/upimages/Hands_as_Thoughts_and_feelings_Exercise_-_2017_version.pdf)).

> They’re still here.
>
> Harris, final paragraph ([author's exercise](https://www.actmindfully.com.au/upimages/Hands_as_Thoughts_and_feelings_Exercise_-_2017_version.pdf)).

The author includes two qualifications. Painful thoughts and feelings may contain useful information. And the demonstration explains a reason for learning defusion; it does not itself give someone that skill ([opening caution and final paragraph](https://www.actmindfully.com.au/upimages/Hands_as_Thoughts_and_feelings_Exercise_-_2017_version.pdf)).

An editorial application for a writer is to choose one concrete revision task, such as checking a citation. Notice self-critical commentary, then try examining the cited passage while allowing that commentary to remain. Afterwards, ask whether the source check became more accurate or merely more hurried. Retain the adjustment if it helped; change it if it interfered. Attention and overt behaviour provide the observations.

This application does not require dismissing criticism. A thought that a claim is unsupported may be pointing to work that needs doing. The image invites a change in how the writer attends to that thought while staying available to the page, the evidence and the next decision.

**Principles:**
- [[P159 — Defuse — see thoughts as thoughts]]: the hands remain while their position changes.
- [[P161 — Return to the present — contact what is here]]: uncovering the room represents renewed contact with the immediate situation.
- [[P162 — Take committed action — behavior is where values become real]]: the editorial application translates care for accuracy into a specific source check.
