Philosophical anecdote · Zhuangzi, chapter 6, in Robert Eno's translation, cross-read with Legge's.

Ziyu and Zisang are friends. After ten days of steady rain, Ziyu says, "I bet Zisang is in trouble!" and wraps up a packet of food to bring him. Legge's Ziyu says, "I fear that Zi-sang may be in distress" ([Eno, §6.9](https://scholarworks.iu.edu/iuswrrest/api/core/bitstreams/5dc89a23-c6bf-4190-a1e4-6762dc5359cc/content); [Legge, ch. 6](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)).

At Zisang's gate he hears something between singing and sobbing, with a zither being plucked, and the words "Oh, Father! Oh, Mother! Oh, Heaven! Oh, Humanity!" Ziyu goes in and asks why he sings that way. Zisang says he has been trying to work out who brought him to this pass. His parents could not have wished him poor, and heaven and earth cover and bear everything without bias. "Seek though I may for the one who has done this, I cannot find him." He concludes: "What has brought me to this pass – it must simply be fate (ming)!" Legge's wording: "I was trying to find out who had done it, and I could not do so," and "it is what was appointed for me!" ([Eno](https://scholarworks.iu.edu/iuswrrest/api/core/bitstreams/5dc89a23-c6bf-4190-a1e4-6762dc5359cc/content); [Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)).

The compendium reads this beside D097 and D098. Zisang does not claim to feel fine. He is singing and wailing at the same time, and the conclusion comes after he has looked for someone to blame and found no one. The story shows the friend who turned up with food, and does not say what happened next.

Limits: the story ends at the conclusion, and gives no sign that Zisang was comforted or that Ziyu said anything. Eno notes that the passage parallels earlier ones in the chapter, with poverty as its theme. Poverty that has a human cause, such as exploitation or neglect, is not "fate," and this page does not offer the story as a reason to stop looking for one.

**Principles:**
- [[D097 — Don't fear what you can't act on]]: the search for a culprit that ends without one.
- [[D098 — Accept what destiny brings (li ming)]]: "it must simply be fate."
