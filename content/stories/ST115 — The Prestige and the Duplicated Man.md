*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States/UK, 2006 · [Christopher Nolan, The Prestige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prestige_%28film%29)*

Two magicians ruin each other over a trick. Angier, desperate to copy Borden's teleportation, uses Tesla's machine, which creates a duplicate. Every night, one copy drowns in a tank below the stage; the other takes the bow. The film ends with Angier's death and Borden's reunion with his daughter, the rivalry having consumed everything both men had.

The film's philosophical question is usually posed as identity: is the man who steps off the stage the one who entered? The process reading is more unsettling and more useful. If a person is a continuous performance, then the trick is not a metaphysical anomaly; it is every life, and the only real question is what one is willing to drown nightly to keep the show running.
