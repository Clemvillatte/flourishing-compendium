*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2016 · [Jim Jarmusch, Paterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paterson_(film))*

Paterson drives a bus in Paterson, New Jersey. His weeks are identical in shape: wake without an alarm beside his wife, drive the route, listen to passengers talk, walk the dog to the bar, write a few lines in his secret notebook. He publishes nothing. A Japanese poet he meets by chance asks if he is a poet, and he says no, though he writes.

The film is a week in a life with no career arc whatsoever, and it is one of the most contented portraits in cinema. Jarmusch's wager is that a life of rhythm, attention, and small private creation is not a failed artistic life. It is a complete one.
