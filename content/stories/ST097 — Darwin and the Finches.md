*Historical/biographical narrative · England, 1859 · [Charles Darwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Darwin)*

Returning from the Beagle voyage, Darwin works for years on the collections: the Galápagos finches, whose beaks vary by island and food source; the barnacles, dissected by the thousand. The conclusion, published after two decades of caution, is that species are not fixed kinds but populations in motion, shaped by what the environment rewards. There is no plan in it, and no endpoint.

The insight mattered to the pragmatists, several of whom read Darwin as a young man, more than any philosophy. It is process metaphysics with evidence: the categories are snapshots of a moving thing, and the moving thing is all there is.
