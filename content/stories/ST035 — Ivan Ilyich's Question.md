*Literary narrative, cross-cultural · Russia, 1886 · [Leo Tolstoy, The Death of Ivan Ilyich](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/861/861-h/861-h.htm)*

Ivan Ilyich, a judge, has lived the life that everyone around him considers correct: career, propriety, a well-furnished house. He falls from a ladder, develops a fatal illness, and in his long dying discovers something unbearable. His life, the whole respectable edifice of it, was "not the real thing." He had lived it as others lived theirs, without once asking whether it was his.

Tolstoy's horror is precise. Ivan Ilyich is not punished for villainy. He simply never examined the life he was assembling while he assembled it, and death is the deadline that forces the audit.
