ACT metaphor · ACT for Psychosis Recovery, prompt sheet A3; later commentary by Russ Harris

In the prompt sheet's bus, the driver travels with pleasant, neutral and unwelcome passengers representing inner experiences. Fighting with the frightening ones stops the journey. Bargaining for their silence lets them determine the route. The sheet leaves the driver with a question about other ways of responding ([A3 prompt sheet](http://www.actforpsychosis.com/pdfs/A3_Passengers_on_the_bus.pdf)).

> There can be different ways to respond to the passengers
>
> ACT for Psychosis Recovery, A3, final sentence ([A3 prompt sheet](http://www.actforpsychosis.com/pdfs/A3_Passengers_on_the_bus.pdf)).

Harris's later commentary offers another angle: explore how a passenger may be trying to help and whether something useful can be taken from its contribution. He presents the language of inner parts as metaphorical, referring to patterns of cognition and emotion ([ACT and Working with Parts, opening and Conflict Between Parts](https://contextualconsulting.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/ACT-and-working-with-parts-free-eBook-by-Russ-Harris-December-2022-final-version-updated.pdf)).

For an editorial application, consider a commitment interrupted by worry. Ask whether the worry identifies a practical matter, such as a missing preparation or a need for support. Address what is useful, then try one manageable action toward the commitment. Review whether it helped and revise the plan accordingly. Cognition and overt behaviour supply the observations.

The passengers need not all be cast as enemies. Equally, a driving image cannot establish that illness or difficult circumstances yield to willpower. The prompt sheet reports no successful journey, and this application promises neither symptom disappearance nor complete personal control.

**Principles:**
- [[P156 — Cultivate psychological flexibility — the master skill]]: consider a response beyond fighting or bargaining, including a useful concern.
- [[P157 — Accept what cannot be changed while committing to what can]]: an empty bus is not made a prerequisite for considering the next action.
- [[P162 — Take committed action — behavior is where values become real]]: the editorial application examines an observable step toward a chosen commitment.
