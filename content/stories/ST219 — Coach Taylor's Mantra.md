*Television narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2006-2011 · [Friday Night Lights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_Night_Lights)*

Coach Eric Taylor runs a high-school football programme in a West Texas town where the team is the town's identity. His mantra, "Clear eyes, full hearts, can't lose," is a Stoic formula in coaching language: the clarity is the discipline of assent, the full heart is the commitment of the whole self to the work, and the "can't lose" is not a prediction about the scoreboard. Over five seasons the show tests the mantra against losses, scandals, and the ordinary disasters of its characters' lives.

The series' achievement is that it never lets the mantra become a slogan. Taylor's teams lose, his players fail, his family strains, and the show's argument, sustained across every episode, is that the mantra was never about football: it is a description of the only orientation that is fully in one's power, applied to a town that measures everything by the scoreboard.
