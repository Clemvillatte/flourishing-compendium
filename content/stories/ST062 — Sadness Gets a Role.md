*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2015 · [Pete Docter, Inside Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_Out_%282015_film%29)*

In Riley's mind, Joy runs operations and has been keeping Sadness contained. The family's move to San Francisco destabilises everything, and Joy and Sadness are ejected from headquarters. On the long journey back, Joy discovers that the moment she had been most proud of, Riley winning a hockey game, only happened because Sadness had first made her cry out for help and her parents and team had come.

The film's science consultants, including Paul Ekman, built it around a correction: the attempt to suppress an emotion does not remove it, it removes its information. Sadness turns out to be the system's signal for needed support. Joy's arc is learning to hand her the controls.
