*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1998 · [The Truman Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Truman_Show)*

Truman Burbank's entire life, unknown to him, is a television set, broadcast to the world. The cracks show gradually: a light falls from the sky, a radio channel narrates his movements, the same people cycle past daily. When he finally sails to the edge of the set and confronts the creator, he is offered safety and comfort to stay. He bows, says his catchphrase, and steps through the door into the dark.

The film's premise is a technology-age parable, but the choice it dramatises is ancient: the given world versus the examined one. What Truman refuses is not comfort. It is comfort purchased with the assumption that he would never check.
