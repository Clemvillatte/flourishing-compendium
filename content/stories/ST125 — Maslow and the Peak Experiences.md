*Scientific/biographical narrative · United States, 1962 · [Abraham Maslow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Maslow)*

Maslow studies people he judges to be psychologically healthy and finds they report experiences of profound unity, absorption, and rightness: moments of complete being. He calls these peak experiences and finds them not confined to mystics; they occur in artists, athletes, parents, and lovers, and his interviewees describe them as more real than ordinary experience, not less.

Toward a Psychology of Being is the hinge between humanistic psychology and the later study of optimal experience. Its method was unfashionable then and now: Maslow studied the upper end of the distribution rather than the clinic, on the argument that a psychology built only on damage could tell you nothing about growth.
