*Biographical/cultural narrative · United States, 1983-1994 · [Bob Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Ross)*

Bob Ross, a former US Air Force master sergeant who found painting therapeutic after leaving the military, hosts The Joy of Painting on PBS from 1983 to 1994, painting a complete landscape in thirty minutes while speaking in a near-whisper. His signature phrases, "happy little trees," "we don't make mistakes, just happy little accidents," became the show's cultural signature, and the series, after his death, became one of the most-watched art programmes in history, with a second life on streaming.

The show's content for the play and creativity literature is the relationship it models between the self and the work: the refusal to judge, the willingness to let the unexpected become the material, and the patience with process. Ross's whisper, which viewers found hypnotic, is the pedagogy: the calm is not the aesthetic but the condition of the making, and the show's millions of viewers were, in the main, not painting along, but watching a man be unafraid of a canvas.
