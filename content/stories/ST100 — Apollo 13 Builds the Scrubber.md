*Historical narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1970 · [Apollo 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_13)*

An oxygen tank explodes on Apollo 13, 200,000 miles from Earth. The crew retreats to the lunar module, where the carbon dioxide scrubbers are failing: square cartridges, round sockets, and time measured in hours. Engineers on the ground are given the exact materials aboard, duct tape, cardboard, plastic bags, a sock, and told to build a working adapter. They do, in a day, and talk the crew through building it in flight.

The episode is the pragmatist theory of knowledge in mission-control form. No principle applied from outside, no appeal to what should work; the materials and the constraints were the argument, and the test was the only reviewer that counted.
