*Scientific/biographical narrative · United States, 2011 · [Martin Seligman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Seligman)*

Seligman, who had launched positive psychology around the measurement of happiness, announces in Flourish that happiness was the wrong target, too thin and too dependent on mood. The proposed replacement is PERMA: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, accomplishment, each measurable separately, none reducible to the others. Well-being, he argues, is a construct, not a single thing.

The book is explicit that the shift is a correction of his own earlier work, which is unusual in psychology and worth noting. Its philosophical shape is pluralist and, as critics observed, broadly Aristotelian: the good life is not a feeling but a pattern of activity, and the components can be pursued and measured one at a time.
