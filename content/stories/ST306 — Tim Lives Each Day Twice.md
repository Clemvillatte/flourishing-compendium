*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United Kingdom, 2013 · [About Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/About_Time_(2013_film))*


The men in Tim's family can travel in time, and the film's premise is used, in its second half, for an unusually precise purpose. Tim's father, dying, shares his secret for happiness: live each day once, with the tension and distraction of ordinary life, then go back and live it again, attending to what was missed. The film's ending is Tim's refinement: he no longer goes back at all, because he has learned to live each day as if he had come back to it, which is the same thing.

The film is a romantic comedy with a [[Savoring|savoring]] protocol at its centre, and its mechanism is the field's: the day is the same, the attention is different, and the difference is the entire content of the well-being. The father's deathbed, which the film handles without sentimentality, is the reason the practice cannot be deferred.
