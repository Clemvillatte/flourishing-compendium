*Television narrative, cross-cultural · United Kingdom, 2016-2019 · [Fleabag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleabag)*

Fleabag narrates her own life straight to camera, a device the show uses as diagnosis: the performance is a defence, a way of managing the audience, her family, and her grief before anyone else can. In the second season she falls for a priest who sees through it. He notices the glances. "Where did you just go?" he asks, and the question, asked with love, begins to dissolve the apparatus.

The show's structural joke is that the fourth wall is the mind's commentary turned into architecture. What ends it is not suppression or insight but being seen by someone attentive enough to notice the machinery running.
