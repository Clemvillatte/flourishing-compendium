*Historical/biographical narrative · United States, 1979 · [Richard Rorty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Rorty)*

Rorty argues that Western philosophy spent centuries trying to be the mirror of nature, and that the mirror does not exist. What we have are vocabularies, inherited sets of descriptions, and vocabularies are tools: they help some projects and obstruct others. The question to ask of a vocabulary is not whether it is the true one but what it makes easy, and what it makes impossible to say.

The position sounds corrosive until applied to oneself. Rorty's later work is explicit about this: redescribing your own situation in different words is not relativism; it is the main lever people actually have, and therapists, poets, and philosophers are all in the business of handing over better tools.
