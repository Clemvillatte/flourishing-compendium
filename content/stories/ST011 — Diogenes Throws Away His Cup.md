*Historical/biographical, cross-cultural · Greece, 4th century BCE · [Diogenes of Sinope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diogenes_of_Sinope)*

Diogenes the Cynic lives in a storage jar in Athens, owning almost nothing. Seeing a child drink from cupped hands, he throws away his own cup, saying a child has beaten him in the economy of living. The anecdote is recorded by Diogenes Laertius among the stock stories of his life.

The gesture is deliberately comic rather than ascetic. He is not renouncing comfort for its own sake; he is testing each possession against actual use. Whatever survives that test, he keeps. The rest was never his, only clutter he was maintaining.
