*Mythological/literary narrative · Greece, 8th century BCE · [Homer, Iliad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iliad)*

Agamemnon takes Briseis, and Achilles' anger is not wrong in its origin: the violation of honour calls for response. It is wrong in its measure. He withdraws from the war, refuses every embassy and every offer of restitution, prays for the deaths of his own comrades, and returns only when Patroclus is killed, at which point the anger exceeds every limit again, desecrating Hector's body.

The Iliad is the founding study of a virtue and its ruin having the same root. Anger at injustice is necessary to a warrior; anger without measure destroys everything it was supposed to protect, including the honour it was invoked to defend.
