*Historical/biographical narrative · United States, 1889 · [Jane Addams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Addams)*

Addams and Ellen Gates Starr rent a mansion on Halsted Street in a Chicago immigrant neighbourhood and call it Hull-House. She does not arrive with a programme; she arrives with a residence. The settlement house model meant living among the neighbours, and the programmes, kindergarten, art gallery, employment aid, came from what the community itself asked for. Twenty Years at Hull-House records the method.

The move is the inquiry principle in urban form. Addams explicitly argued against the philanthropist's distance: the reformer who does not live with the facts will design solutions for a neighbourhood that exists only in the office. Hull-House became the template for hundreds of settlement houses and, eventually, for community-based research.
