*Literary/film narrative, cross-cultural · Sweden, 2012/2015 · [A Man Called Ove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Man_Called_Ove)*

Ove, 59, widowed, has decided to die and keeps being interrupted: a pregnant Iranian neighbour reverses a trailer into his flowerbed, a stray cat appears, a friend's son needs a father's care. Each suicide attempt, played as farce, is foiled by someone needing him. He dies, years later, of natural causes, surrounded by the family that accumulated while he was trying to leave.

The novel's structure is an argument about the contemplative life that Aristotle would recognise. Ove's retreat from the world is not evil or wrong; it is incomplete, and what completes it is not solitude but use, the discovery that a person's remaining years have takers. The book is funny, and it is using the comedy to make exactly this case.
