*Historical/biographical narrative · South Africa, 1990-1994 · [Nelson Mandela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Mandela)*

On the day of his inauguration as president, Nelson Mandela invites one of his former Robben Island warders, Christo Brand, and other former guards to attend as honoured guests. The gesture, repeated in smaller forms throughout his presidency, was deliberate: a public statement that the new South Africa would be built on recognition rather than revenge, and that the men who had administered the old order were part of the new one's raw material.

The act is the practical content of ubuntu, the southern African concept that a person is a person through other persons, and that the self is constituted in relation rather than against it. Mandela's refusal to emerge from prison as an accuser is often attributed to political calculation, and it was, and it was also the philosophy, worked out in a cell, that no one is extractable from the web that damaged them.
