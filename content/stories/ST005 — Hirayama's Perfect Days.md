*Film narrative, cross-cultural · Japan, 2023 · [Wim Wenders, Perfect Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_Days)*

Hirayama cleans public toilets in Tokyo. Each morning follows the same shape: he waters his plants, buys coffee from a vending machine, drives to work listening to cassette tapes, photographs the light through the trees, and reads paperbacks before sleep. When a colleague asks why he is so content, he does not explain. There is nothing to explain. The days are the answer.

The film works because it refuses to reveal a backstory that would "account for" his peace. His contentment is not a result. It is a manner of inhabiting a life, and the ordinary turns out to be inexhaustible.
