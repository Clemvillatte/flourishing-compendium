*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1993 · [Groundhog Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groundhog_Day)*

Phil Connors, a cynical weatherman, is trapped in a repeating day in Punxsutawney. He runs the full repertoire: indulgence, then manipulation, then despair, then repeated suicide. Nothing works; the day resets. Only after the exhaustion of every strategy does he begin to practise: piano, French, ice sculpture, small kindnesses done with no audience to impress. One morning the day moves on.

The film is often described as a Buddhist story, and it is, but its mechanics are Daoist. Every attempt to force the loop fails; the loop only releases its grip when he stops contending with it and starts inhabiting it.
