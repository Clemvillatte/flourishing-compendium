*Tham Luang cave, Chiang Rai, Thailand · June–July 2018 · the Wild Boars youth football team*

On 23 June 2018, twelve boys of the Wild Boars football team, aged 11 to 16, walked into the Tham Luang cave system in northern Thailand after practice, with their 25-year-old assistant coach. Monsoon floodwaters cut off the way out, and the group spent more than two weeks underground before the rescue ([Vox](https://www.vox.com/2018/7/9/17548512/thai-cave-rescue-soccer-boys-meditation-buddhism)).

The coach, Ekapol Chanthawong, had gone to live in a monastery at 12 after he was orphaned, and trained there to be a monk for ten years at a monastery in Mae Sai before leaving to care for a sick grandmother and taking the assistant-coach job. When the cave flooded, the decade came with him. According to multiple news sources, he taught the boys to meditate in the cave to keep them calm and preserve their energy through the ordeal ([Vox](https://www.vox.com/2018/7/9/17548512/thai-cave-rescue-soccer-boys-meditation-buddhism)). When British divers found the group, the boys were reportedly meditating. The mother of one of them, watching the widely shared video of the moment they were found, told the AP: "Look at how calm they were sitting there waiting. No one was crying or anything. It was astonishing." ([AP, via Vox](https://www.vox.com/2018/7/9/17548512/thai-cave-rescue-soccer-boys-meditation-buddhism)) His aunt put it more plainly: "He could meditate up to an hour. It has definitely helped him and probably helps the boys to stay calm." ([AP, via Vox](https://www.vox.com/2018/7/9/17548512/thai-cave-rescue-soccer-boys-meditation-buddhism))

After the rescue, the Guardian reported that eleven of the twelve boys were to spend nine days at a temple as part of a novice ordination, to offer gratitude ([Vox, update of 25 July](https://www.vox.com/2018/7/9/17548512/thai-cave-rescue-soccer-boys-meditation-buddhism)).

Vox reports that the coach taught meditation to help the boys remain calm and conserve energy, without specifying the technique ([Vox](https://www.vox.com/2018/7/9/17548512/thai-cave-rescue-soccer-boys-meditation-buddhism)). The compendium pairs that reported use with developing calm and with trusting that the mind can be trained: a skill built over ten years in one person, handed to twelve others in an emergency.
