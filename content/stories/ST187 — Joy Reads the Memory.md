*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2015 · [Inside Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_Out_%282015_film%29)*

The film's key scene, easy to miss under the comedy: Joy, examining a sad core memory, rewinds it and watches the part after the loss, when Riley's parents and teammates came onto the ice and comforted her. Joy realises that the memory she had been protecting was made by Sadness. The memory was always both: grief first, then love, and Joy's whole project of keeping Sadness away from the core memories had been a mistake about how memories work.

The scene is the Aristotelian claim that emotions are not opposed to reason but are themselves cognitive, dramatised for children. Joy's discovery is not that sadness is sometimes necessary; it is that the best moments of Riley's life were constituted by sadness doing its job, and the film's science advisers built the whole plot on that correction.
