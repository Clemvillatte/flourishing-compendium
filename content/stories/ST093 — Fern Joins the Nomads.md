*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2020 · [Chloé Zhao, Nomadland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomadland)*

Fern loses her husband and, when the US Gypsum plant closes, her town itself. She buys a van and joins the community of older Americans travelling the West for seasonal work. The film is unsparing about the precarity: injuries, cold, the loss of friends. And it is equally clear that what Fern is doing is not failure but a form of freedom she has chosen against the alternative of a storage unit and a warehouse job.

The film's Buddhism is quiet and literal: the community it depicts practices mutual aid and impermanence as a daily discipline, and the real nomads it cast play themselves. Nothing is permanent, the film keeps showing, including the grief, and Fern keeps moving.
