*Historical/legendary narrative · Greece, 4th century BCE · [Damon and Pythias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damon_and_Pythias)*

Pythias, condemned to death by Dionysius of Syracuse, asks to settle his affairs first. Damon offers himself as hostage: if Pythias does not return, Damon will die in his place. Pythias is delayed, and Damon is led to execution. Pythias arrives at the last moment, delayed by weather and pursuit. Dionysius, struck by the loyalty of both, spares them and asks to be admitted as a third friend.

The story, preserved in Cicero and Valerius Maximus, became the Western tradition's emblem of the perfect friendship. Its philosophical interest is the hostage's calm: Damon does not trust that Pythias will return; he trusts Pythias completely, and these are different, the second being the condition Aristotle calls loving the friend for the friend's own sake.
