*Artistic/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · France, 1899-1926 · [Claude Monet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Monet)*

Monet paints the pond at Giverny for the last twenty-seven years of his life: the same water, the same lilies, thousands of canvases. What he is painting is not the water lilies. He is painting the light on the water, which never repeats. As his cataracts worsen, the paintings become less representational and more atmospheric, and he keeps painting what he sees, which is no longer what others see.

The late water lilies are a study in attention at the edge of its own dissolution. Nothing about the subject changed in twenty-seven years. What changed, and what the paintings record, is the refinement of the seeing.
