*Mythological narrative · Greece, 5th century BCE · [The Choice of Hercules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choice_of_Hercules)*

The young Hercules, deliberating on his path, is visited by two women. One, beautiful and self-adorned, offers the easy road: pleasure now, without labour. The other, modest and serious, offers the steep road: struggle, toil, and the lasting glory of deeds worth doing. Hercules chooses the second, and the choice becomes the emblem of virtue ethics in the Western imagination.

The story is best known through Prodicus as Xenophon retells it, and its survival for twenty-four centuries rests on its simplicity. The choice is not between good and evil; it is between easy goods and excellent ones, which is the actual choice most lives present, and the harder one to get right.
