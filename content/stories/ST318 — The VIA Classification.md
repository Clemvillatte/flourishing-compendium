*Scientific/biographical narrative · United States, 2004 · [VIA Character](https://www.viacharacter.org/character-strengths)*

Peterson and Seligman's Character Strengths and Virtues, the product of a three-year project, catalogues 24 character strengths under six virtues, wisdom, courage, humanity, justice, temperance, and transcendence, drawing on the traditions surveyed in this volume's earlier Parts and on cross-cultural historical sources. The classification was built to be measurable, and the VIA Inventory, the self-report instrument, was the result.

The project's content is a claim about the virtues that is unusual in modern psychology: that they are real, classifiable, and trainable, and that the earlier traditions' catalogues, Aristotle's, Confucius's, the Stoics', converge on recognisably similar lists. The instrument's limitations, including self-report bias and the flat structure of the strengths, are documented, and the classification remains the field's common vocabulary.
