*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1957 · [Sidney Lumet, 12 Angry Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12_Angry_Men)*

A jury retires to deliberate a murder case. Eleven vote guilty. Juror 8 votes not guilty, and when pressed says only that he does not know, that he just wants to talk. Through the deliberation, the evidence is re-examined: the knife, the footsteps, the eyewitness's timing. Each certainty turns out to have been borrowed. The vote becomes unanimous for acquittal, and the men, who never learn each other's names, disperse.

The film is the single best dramatisation of the pragmatist theory of inquiry as a social process. Truth here is not declared; it is approached through the willingness of one man to be the friction in the machine, and the film's title is its argument: the group, not the individual, is the unit of correction.
