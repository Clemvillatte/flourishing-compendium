*Mythological/literary narrative · Greece, 8th century BCE · [Homer, Iliad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iliad)*

Patroclus wears Achilles' armour to rally the Greeks, and is killed by Hector. Achilles' grief is not proportional to the loss of a companion; the funeral games, the refusal to eat, the chase of Hector three times around the walls, the mingling of their ashes in the same urn. Homer makes the relationship the engine of the entire final movement.

The Iliad does not name what the two are to each other, and twenty-seven centuries of readers have argued about it. What is not arguable is the structure: the loss of this friend is the one thing that moves Achilles where honour, kings, and death could not, and the poem locates a person's deepest self in exactly that place.
