*Philosophical narrative · United States, 1896 · [John Dewey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dewey)*

The stimulus-response model, Dewey argues, is a fiction of the laboratory. A child reaches for a candle flame: that is not stimulus then response. The seeing is already a looking, shaped by previous reaching; the burn reshapes the next seeing. There is a circuit, not an arc, and the "stimulus" and "response" are points we mark on a continuous loop for convenience.

"The Reflex Arc Concept in Psychology" is one of the most influential papers in American psychology. Its claim, that action and perception are one transaction with the environment, anticipated ecological psychology and embodied cognition by a century, and it is the reason Dewey's name appears in every account of habit: habits are the circuit's established channels.
