*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2004 · [Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_Sunshine_of_the_Spotless_Mind)*

Joel and Clementine, a failed couple, each hire a company, Lacuna Inc., to erase the other from memory. The film's structure follows Joel's erasure from the inside, as the memories are destroyed in reverse order, and Joel, inside his own mind, fights the procedure, hiding Clementine in memories she does not belong to. The two meet again after the procedure, with no memory of each other, and are drawn together, and hear their own erased tape recordings warning them of the failure, and choose to try again.

The film is the narrative-identity thesis in its most ingenious form, and its content is the status of memory in the self: if the self is a story, and the story can be edited, what remains is not a truer self but a discontinuous one. The film's ending, the choice to repeat the relationship knowing its history, is its answer: identity is not the memory but the choosing, and the choosing can survive the editing.
