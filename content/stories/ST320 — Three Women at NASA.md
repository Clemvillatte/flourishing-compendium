*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2016 · [Hidden Figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Figures)*

Katherine Johnson, Dorothy Vaughan, and Mary Jackson, Black women mathematicians at NASA's Langley in the early 1960s, work in a segregated West Area computing group. Johnson calculates the trajectory for John Glenn's orbital flight, and is assigned to the Space Task Group, where she works in a building with no coloured women's bathroom, and runs half a mile in heels to the other building. Vaughan teaches herself FORTRAN and becomes the first Black supervisor. Jackson fights a court battle to attend an all-white engineering school.

The film's content for the strengths literature is the exercise of virtues under conditions of injustice: courage, perseverance, and justice are not traits the women possess in the abstract, but capacities exercised in specific, hostile contexts. The film's honesty, and the book's more so, is that the context was not a neutral stage for the virtues but the very thing the virtues were for.
