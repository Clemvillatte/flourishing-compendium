*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1984 · [The Karate Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Karate_Kid)*

Daniel LaRusso wants to learn karate. Mr. Miyagi has him wash cars, sand floors, and paint fences, for days, saying nothing. Daniel finally explodes that he is being used as free labour. Miyagi then demonstrates: wax on, wax off is a block. Paint the fence is a strike. The chores were the training; his body already knows the movements.

The film is the most popular retelling of a very old idea, that skill is built into the body before the mind is consulted about it. Daniel's fury is the natural reaction of anyone who believes learning must look like explanation. It doesn't.
