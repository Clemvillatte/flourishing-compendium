*Artistic/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · France, 1940s · [Henri Matisse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Matisse)*

Following surgery in 1941, Matisse, in his seventies, could no longer paint at an easel. Bedridden, he had assistants prepare sheets of gouache-painted paper and, with scissors, he cut shapes directly: leaves, birds, swimmers, the famous Blue Nudes. He called the cut-outs "drawing with scissors" and considered the last decade's work among the most important of his life. The chapel at Vence, designed in this period, is the fruit of a man who could not stand for long, working at a table.

The scissors story is the [[Yangsheng (cultivating vitality)|yangsheng]] principle in colour. The body that could no longer hold a brush at a canvas was not the end of the work; it was the occasion for the work's transformation into something that required exactly the body he had.
