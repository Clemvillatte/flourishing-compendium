*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1999 · [David Lynch, The Straight Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Straight_Story)*

Alvin Straight, 73, learns his estranged brother has had a stroke. His eyes are too bad to drive, his hips too bad to walk, and he refuses to be driven. He hitches a trailer to his riding lawnmower and travels 240 miles across Iowa at five miles an hour, the journey taking six weeks. Along the way he tells a runaway girl about family, and a priest holds a service for two old men who fought once too often.

The film's slowness is its argument. The distance is coverable in hours; Alvin needs the weeks, because the reconciliation he is travelling toward requires the person he becomes on the way. Speed would defeat the whole errand.
