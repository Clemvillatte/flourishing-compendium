*Television documentary narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2015-present · [Chef's Table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chef%27s_Table)*

The first episode profiles Massimo Bottura of Osteria Francescana in Modena. The arc the show returns to across its seasons: Bottura, a slow starter who was rejected by his father's expectations and by Italian culinary traditionalism, spent years working in other kitchens before returning to Modena, where critics initially mocked his deconstructions of tradition. He kept iterating, plate by plate, until the restaurant was named the world's best.

The series' consistent claim, across chefs, is Aristotelian: none of the profiles is a story of talent discovered. Each is a story of ten thousand repetitions of the same act under conditions of feedback, and the show is popular precisely because it makes the grinding nature of excellence visible, which the talent myth hides.
