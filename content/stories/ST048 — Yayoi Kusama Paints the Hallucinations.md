*Artistic/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · Japan/United States, 1960s-present · [Yayoi Kusama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yayoi_Kusama)*

Kusama has experienced hallucinations of fields of dots since childhood. Since 1977 she has lived, voluntarily, in a psychiatric hospital in Tokyo, walking daily to her studio to paint. She does not cure the hallucinations. She paints them: the Infinity Mirror Rooms are her inner experience, externalised at architectural scale, and millions of people queue to stand inside them.

The case is usually framed as art defeating illness, which is not quite what happens. Nothing is defeated. What changes is the relationship: the symptom becomes the material, and a life that could not exclude the dots becomes a life organised around them. Vitality, here, is not the absence of the condition but the fit between the condition and the life.
