*Literary/film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2011/2015 · [The Martian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Martian_%28Weir_novel%29)*

Stranded on Mars, presumed dead, astronaut Mark Watney works the problem. He inventories resources, calculates calories, and faces the gap: he can grow food, but he needs soil bacteria, which Mars does not have, until he identifies his own waste as the source. The novel is structured as a series of catastrophic failures, each answered by a technical improvisation, each documented in his log.

The story's pedagogy is its rhythm. Watney's signature line, "I'm going to have to science the shit out of this," is the inquiry cycle as personality. Nothing is solved by insight; everything is solved by attempting, measuring the failure, and adjusting, and the story makes that loop feel like heroism, which it is.
