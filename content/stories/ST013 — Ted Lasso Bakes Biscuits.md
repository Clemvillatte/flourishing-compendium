*Television narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2020-2023 · [Ted Lasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso)*

Ted Lasso, an American football coach hired to manage a Premier League soccer club he knows nothing about, is not worried about winning. He brings homemade biscuits to his hostile boss, learns the name of the kit man, and asks about people's lives before their performance. Asked if he is worried, he says worrying is like a rocking chair: it gives you something to do but gets you nowhere.

The show is a slow argument that the conditions for success are relational, and that chasing the outcome directly is the best way to miss it. The comedy is that everyone around him thinks this is naïve, right up until it starts working.
