*Pacific cultural practice narrative · Fiji, tradition; global stage, 2017 · [Talanoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talanoa)*

Talanoa is the Pacific practice of story-sharing dialogue: to talanoa is to sit together and talk without a hidden agenda, sharing stories that build understanding rather than testing positions. In 2017, the Fijian presidency of the UN climate summit made the Talanoa Dialogue the official format of the negotiations, replacing a portion of the confrontational bargaining with three questions: where are we, where do we want to go, and how do we get there. The sessions were conducted in the talanoa manner, seated, story-first, without the usual protocol of adversarial statements.

The episode is a narrative device travelling from village meeting houses to global diplomacy, and its result was mixed, as honest accounts note. Its interest is the demonstration that the format is not decoration: the change in structure changed what could be said, and delegations that had years of prepared positions found themselves telling stories instead.
