*Literary/film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1961/2008 · [Richard Yates, Revolutionary Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Road)*

Frank and April Wheeler, young suburbanites in 1950s Connecticut, believe they are different from their neighbours, and they have a plan: sell the house, move to Paris, where April will work and Frank will find himself. April becomes pregnant. Frank takes the promotion he had scorned. The Paris plan is abandoned, and April, performing a self-abortion, dies.

The novel is the most unsparing study of bad faith in American literature, and its mechanism is precise: the fantasy of the different life is not a plan but a device, the place where the meaning is stored while the actual life is lived. Yates's insight, which the film preserves, is that the couple's tragedy is not that the plan failed but that they had it, and that the having was the substitute for the doing.
