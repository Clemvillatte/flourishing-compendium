*Animated short film narrative, cross-cultural · Norway/Canada, 2006 · [Torill Kove, The Danish Poet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Danish_Poet)*

A Danish poet with writer's block travels to Norway to visit his hero, Kierkegaard's grave. He does not find inspiration there. He finds it when a farmer's daughter lends him an umbrella and their lives, across a chain of accidents including a slipped cow and a determined postman, braid together across two generations. The film's narrator concludes that everything hinges on the small chances, and that we owe our existence to a borrowed umbrella.

The film is a meditation on conditionality told as romantic comedy: no single cause, an assembly of tiny ones, and the assembly is the story. It won the Academy Award for animated short.
