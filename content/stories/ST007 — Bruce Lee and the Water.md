*Biographical, cross-cultural · United States/Hong Kong, 20th century · [Bruce Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Lee)*

In a 1971 interview, Bruce Lee describes his martial philosophy: "Empty your mind, be formless, shapeless, like water. You put water into a cup, it becomes the cup. You put water into a bottle, it becomes the bottle. Water can flow, or it can crash. Be water, my friend." The line became his epitaph.

Lee had trained in Wing Chun and later abandoned fixed styles altogether, developing an approach he called "the art of fighting without fighting." The water metaphor is not decorative. It is a worked-out position on rigidity: a system that insists on its form breaks against circumstances that no longer fit it.
