*Television narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2020 · [The Queen's Gambit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen%27s_Gambit_(miniseries))*

Beth Harmon, an orphan in a Kentucky basement, learns chess from the janitor and becomes a prodigy. The series is honest about the costs: she wins on talent until she meets the Soviets, who win on preparation, and her losses, to Borgov, coincide with her drinking. The turnaround requires the things talent had let her skip: study, sobriety, and, in the final match, the help of the friends she had outgrown.

The series' Aristotelian structure is unusually clean. The first half is a study of natural gift unsupported by habit; the second is the habituation catching up to the gift, and the ending, in which she plays a casual game with old men in a Moscow park, is the show's statement about what the mastery was for.
