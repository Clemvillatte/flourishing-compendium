*Musical/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · Canada/United States, 1955 and 1981 · [Glenn Gould](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Gould)*

In 1955, aged 22, Gould records Bach's Goldberg Variations at dazzling speed, and it announces him. In 1981, a year before his death, he records them again: slow, spacious, almost motionless in places. The notes are identical. The music is unrecognisable.

The two recordings are a natural experiment in what attention does to an object. Nothing in the score changed. What changed is the quality of mind meeting it, and any listener can hear the difference directly. Gould himself said the early version showed off, and the late version listened.
