*Television narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2008-2013 · [Breaking Bad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad)*

Walter White, a chemistry teacher with cancer, begins manufacturing methamphetamine, telling everyone, including himself, that it is for his family. Each decision makes the next easier: the lie, the murder, the escalation. In the final season he finally says it: "I did it for me. I liked it. I was good at it. And I was really... I was alive."

The series is the most complete study of habit ever put on television, and its mechanism is Dewey's circuit. Walter does not decide to become Heisenberg; he becomes him through accumulated action, each of which reshaped the perception available for the next. The confession scene matters because it is the moment the channel, long since carved, finally becomes visible to the man flowing through it.
