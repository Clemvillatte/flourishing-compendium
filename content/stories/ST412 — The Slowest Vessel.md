Biographical account · Henri Matisse, French painter, late work from 1941 · [Grand Palais](https://www.grandpalais.fr/en/node/2336)

In 1941, Henri Matisse underwent a serious operation ([Grand Palais](https://www.grandpalais.fr/en/node/2336)). In 1942 he wrote: "J'avais tellement préparé ma sortie de la vie, qu'il me semble être dans une seconde vie." Translated here: "I had so prepared my departure from life that I seem to be in a second life." ([Grand Palais](https://www.grandpalais.fr/en/node/2336)) A museum exhibition later took his phrase as its name for the years that followed, and they were years of "immense energy and productivity" despite "physical weakness and advanced age" ([Louisiana Museum](https://www.absolutearts.com/artsnews/2005/08/12/33222.html)).

In the late 1940s he turned almost exclusively to cut paper as his primary medium ([MoMA](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/1429)). In *Jazz* (1947), he described the process: "Drawing with scissors. Cutting directly into vivid colour reminds me of the direct carving of sculptors" ([Christie's](https://www.christies.com/zh-cn/lot/lot-5650351)).

Matisse described the technique in terms of simplification ([Christie's](https://www.christies.com/zh-cn/lot/lot-5650351)):

> "Paper cut-outs allow me to draw in colour. For me it is a question of simplification. Instead of drawing the outline and establishing colour within it, I draw directly in the colour, which is more exact for not being transposed."

([Christie's](https://www.christies.com/zh-cn/lot/lot-5650351))

The Louisiana Museum's exhibition text describes this period as "the culmination of half a century of work" and quotes Matisse: "I have needed all that time to reach the stage where I can say what I want to say" ([Louisiana Museum](https://www.absolutearts.com/artsnews/2005/08/12/33222.html)). That is the connection worth keeping: apparent simplicity can contain decades of practice.

### Action

When your progress looks like drawing back, take the long view on purpose. Ask what your slow years were building, and judge the vessel by what it is becoming rather than by how finished it looks today.

**Principles:**
- [[D160 — Be patient with your own maturation|160. Be patient with your own maturation]] — the late cut-outs as a vessel slow in the making, carried by his own words on needing all that time
