*Musical/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · Germany, 1975 · [The Köln Concert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_K%C3%B6ln_Concert)*

Keith Jarrett arrives at the Cologne Opera House to find the piano unusable: weak bass, a tinny upper register, pedals that stick. He nearly refuses to play. The producer, a teenager who organized the concert, stands outside in the rain pleading with him. He plays. He works around the weak bass by stomping pedal bass notes, avoids the dead upper register, and leans into the middle. The recording became the best-selling solo piano album in history.

The concert is often told as a triumph of adaptability, which it is, but the deeper detail is the venue: an opera house that had given him a club piano. What he played was not the instrument he chose. It was the one that was there.
