*Film narrative, cross-cultural · Japan/Germany, 2023 · [Wim Wenders, Perfect Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_Days)*

Each day, Hirayama photographs the komorebi, the light falling through leaves, with a film camera. He never checks the photographs, never prints them, and keeps them in a tin. One day a roll is found and developed: the pictures are beautiful, and the person who took them is unbothered either way. The act, not the artefact, was the practice.

The detail of the film camera is Wenders' argument in one prop: no screen, no feedback loop, no audience. It is the clearest cinematic image of meditation embedded in daily work rather than performed on a cushion.
