*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United Kingdom/Italy/South Africa, 2004 · [Hotel Rwanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Rwanda)*

During the 1994 genocide, Paul Rusesabagina, the manager of the Hôtel des Mille Collines in Kigali, shelters over 1,200 refugees. He is not a soldier or a saint; the film's honesty is that his tools are managerial: he negotiates, bribes, flatters, calls in favours, and uses his knowledge of how powerful men like to be treated to buy hours. He cannot stop the genocide. He keeps the hotel standing.

The film's Stoic content is the discipline of action within a situation that is almost entirely not in one's power. Rusesabagina's competence is the performance of his role, hotel manager, under conditions where the role's meaning has been transformed, and the film's refusal to make him larger than that is what makes it credible.
