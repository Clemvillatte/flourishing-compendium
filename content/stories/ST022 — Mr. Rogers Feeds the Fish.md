*Biographical/cultural narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1968-2001 · [Mister Rogers' Neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mister_Rogers%27_Neighborhood)*

In episode after episode, Fred Rogers feeds the fish on camera, saying out loud that he is feeding them, because a child had written worried the fish might be hungry. The pace of the show is unnatural for television: minutes pass without event. Rogers speaks to children as if they are the only person in the room.

The slowness is the content. It models a quality of attention in which nothing is rushed past and no worry is too small to be answered. For a child watching, it was often the only half-hour of the day that moved at the speed of care.
