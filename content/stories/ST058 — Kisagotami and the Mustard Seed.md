*Buddhist parable · India, 5th century BCE · [Kisagotami, Therīgāthā 10.1](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/thig/thig.10.01.than.html)*

Kisagotami's only child dies. Carrying the body, she begs everyone for medicine. The Buddha tells her to bring a mustard seed from a house where no one has died. Every house she visits has known death. She lays the child down in the forest and returns, and the story says she became a follower. The Therīgāthā preserves her own verses about it.

The teaching is not a trick; it is a field study. Grief had told her that she alone had been struck. One afternoon of doors, each one opening onto the same loss, did what no consolation could.
