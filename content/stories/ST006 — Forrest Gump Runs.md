*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1994 · [Robert Zemeckis, Forrest Gump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forrest_Gump)*

After Jenny leaves, Forrest starts running. He runs for three years, two months, fourteen days, and sixteen hours. Asked why, he says he just felt like running. When he is done, he stops, says he is tired, and goes home.

The passage is a comic inversion of purpose-driven culture. Nothing is achieved, no cause is advanced, and yet the running has its own completeness. Forrest does not impose a plan on his life; he follows what arises, and what arises has its own intelligence. The joke lands because we suspect the joke is on us.
