*Zen biographical narrative · China, 7th century CE · [Platform Sutra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platform_Sutra)*

Two monks argue before a shrine: the flag is moving; no, the wind is moving. Huineng, the future Sixth Patriarch, then a labourer in the kitchen, says: it is not the wind or the flag; your minds are moving.

The koan is the Chan tradition's sharpest single statement on where events happen. The flag and the wind are both real; what is added, and what can be dropped, is the contest about which one to be right about. It is no accident that the scene is an argument.
