*Biographical narrative · Norway, 20th century · [Arne Næss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arne_N%C3%A6ss)*

Arne Næss, Norway's most prominent philosopher and a serious mountaineer, who led the 1950 expedition that first summited Tirich Mir, publishes in 1973 a short paper distinguishing shallow from deep ecology. The shallow movement, he writes, fights pollution and resource depletion for the sake of the health and affluence of people in developed countries. The deep movement asks whether we can change our relation to the non-human world, and it answers that the environment is not a resource to be managed but a community to which we belong.

Næss spent much of his later life at his mountain hut, Tvergastein, high above the tree line, and his philosophy is explicitly continuous with the practice: the relational claim, that a self is constituted by its relations including its relations to the living world, was for him not a metaphor but an arrangement of a life.
