*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2016 · [Denis Villeneuve, Arrival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrival_%28film%29)*

Louise Banks, a linguist, is asked to decode the language of aliens whose writing is circular, all at once, with no tense. As she learns it, her experience of time begins to reorganise: she finds herself remembering, or foreseeing, her daughter's life and death. Knowing the loss, she chooses to have the child anyway, and tells her husband the choice knowing it will end the marriage.

The film is popularly read as science fiction, and is more exactly a phenomenology experiment: if time is a structure of the language you think in, then learning a new one changes what is available to perceive. Her final choice is the film's whole argument, that freedom is not the ability to avoid the foreseen, but the ability to affirm it.
