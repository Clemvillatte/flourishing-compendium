*Scientific/biographical narrative · United States, 2007 · [Savoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savoring)*

Fred Bryant's research on savoring distinguishes it from positive emotion itself: savoring is the capacity to attend to, appreciate, and enhance positive experience, and it is a skill with individual differences. His studies find that savoring predicts well-being independently of the frequency of positive events, and that the deliberate prolongation of enjoyment, through attention, gratitude, and sharing, is teachable.

The concept is the field's most direct operationalisation of the contemplative traditions' claims about attention, and its findings converge with them from the other direction: the meditative emphasis on presence in pleasant experience is here measured as a predictor of well-being, and the mechanism, the attending, is the same one the traditions prescribe.
