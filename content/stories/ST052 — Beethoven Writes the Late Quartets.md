*Musical/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · Austria, 1825-1826 · [Ludwig van Beethoven, late period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_van_Beethoven#Late_period)*

Beethoven, completely deaf, writes the final string quartets, Opp. 127 to 135. They are unlike anything he or anyone else had written: the Op. 131 runs seven movements without a break; the Cavatina of Op. 130 is marked beklemmt, "oppressed, suffocating." He reportedly wept when he wrote it. The quartets confused his publishers and were not popular. He wrote them anyway, at a level of interior detail that could not be reached by anyone listening for the market.

The story is not that deafness was a gift. It is that with reception physically removed, the work turned inward, and something unprecedented appeared. The refinement had no audience but the work itself.
