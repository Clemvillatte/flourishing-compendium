Biographical narrative, in his own words · Kabat-Zinn founding the Stress Reduction and Relaxation Program, 1979; two of the four foundations named in his account of the clinical teaching

Kabat-Zinn starts his account with the founding: "I started what was originally called the Stress Reduction and Relaxation Program in September 1979. It didn't come out of a vacuum . . . there were many years of pondering and meditating and inward and outward wandering before it arose as a possibility in my mind." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf)) MBSR became the generic name as other programmes formed: "Later, as more and more programmes started forming based on our work, we came to speak of our work in a more generic form as MBSR, or mindfulness-based stress reduction." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf)) He names the motive: "From the start, it was motivated by a strong impulse on my part ... to bring my dharma practice together with my work life into one unified whole, as an expression of right livelihood and in the service of something useful that felt very much needed in the world." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf)) And the first patients came by referral: "And so, MBSR came into being in the Fall of 1979, and those first three very forward thinking clinic directors, Tom Winters, Bob Burney and John J. Monahan exclusively referred the first few cycles of patients until word spread further into the medical community within the hospital and then out into the larger medical community as well." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf))

He gives two working definitions of mindfulness: "(a) paying attention in a particular way: on purpose, in the present moment, and non-judgmentally (Kabat-Zinn 1994); (b) the awareness that arises from paying attention, on purpose, in the present moment, and non-judgmentally (Kabat-Zinn 2005e)." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf))

Two of the four foundations are in the account, in his own words. On the feeling tone: "Rather, it points out that there will be many many judgments and opinions arising from moment to moment, but that we do not have to judge or evaluate or react to any of what arises, other than perhaps recognizing it in the moment of arising as pleasant, unpleasant, or neutral (the second foundation or establishment of mindfulness)." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf)) On the mental states: "Thus we become intimate with the nature of thoughts and emotions, and mental states such as aversion, frustration, restlessness, greed, doubt, sloth and torpor, and boredom, to name a few, which constitutes the territory of the third foundation of mindfulness, without ever having to mention the classical map of the four foundations of mindfulness, nor the five hindrances, nor the seven factors of enlightenment." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf))

**Principles:**
- [[B039 — Practice the four foundations of mindfulness (satipatthana)|B039 Practice the four foundations of mindfulness (satipatthana)]]: Kabat-Zinn's own account identifies the second foundation (feeling tone: pleasant, unpleasant, neutral) and the third (mental states: aversion, frustration, restlessness, greed, doubt, sloth and torpor, boredom) as the working territory of the clinical teaching, and states that the classical map of the four foundations need not be mentioned for the domains to be the practice; the first and fourth foundations are not illustrated by the selected passages, and the page does not treat the founding or the definitions as evidence of clinical or soteriological outcomes. ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf))
