*Literary narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1993 · [Octavia Butler, Parable of the Sower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parable_of_the_Sower_%28novel%29)*

Lauren Olamina, a teenager in a collapsing near-future California, suffers hyperempathy, feeling the pain she witnesses. She develops a philosophy she calls Earthseed, whose first and only doctrine is: God is Change. She does not deny the destruction around her; she rejects the idea that it is an exception to manage rather than a condition to organise within. The novel follows her building a community on that premise.

Butler's formulation is the process view with a survival edge. The community that treats change as the adversary spends its strength on defence; the one that treats it as the medium, Lauren's, puts the same strength into adaptation and movement.
