*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2015 · [Bridge of Spies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_of_Spies)*

Rudolf Abel, a Soviet spy on trial for his life, is asked by his lawyer, Jim Donovan, whether he is worried. Abel answers: "Would it help?" The line recurs through the film, including in the final exchange on the bridge, and it functions as the character's whole philosophy: worry is not a response to danger, it is an additional suffering added to danger, and it purchases nothing.

The film is a rare case of Stoicism stated in a single repeated line and dramatised without speeches. Abel is not indifferent to his fate; he is calm about it, having sorted what is his, his conduct, his dignity, from what is not, the verdict, the Cold War, and the sorting is the character.
