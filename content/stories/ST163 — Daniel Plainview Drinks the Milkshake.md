*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2007 · [There Will Be Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There_Will_Be_Blood)*

Daniel Plainview, an oil prospector, delivers the speech the film is remembered by: I have a straw that reaches across the room and into your milkshake, and I drink your milkshake. It is the boast of a man describing drainage, the legal doctrine that lets him take the oil under another man's land. His competence is total. He has a deaf son he uses as a prop, a false brother he murders, and a final scene in which he kills a preacher with a bowling pin.

The film is the purest study of competence without virtue in American cinema. Plainview's skill is never in question; every capability he has works. What is missing is any ergon beyond acquisition, and the film's title is its thesis: the result of a life with one good is blood, and only blood.
