*Historical/biographical narrative · Greece, 4th century BCE · [Diogenes of Sinope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diogenes_of_Sinope)*

Asked where he is from, Diogenes says: I am a citizen of the world, kosmopolitēs. The word was a joke and a provocation; the Cynics had no polis. The Stoics adopted it, and the claim hardened into doctrine: the sage's city is the cosmos, and local citizenships are roles within a larger commonwealth of reason.

The idea's history runs through Cicero's natural law, the Roman ius gentium, and into modern human rights. Its practical content is a relocation of the primary reference group: the duties one owes to family and city are real, and they are nested inside duties owed to every rational being, which the tradition treats as the city's actual charter.
