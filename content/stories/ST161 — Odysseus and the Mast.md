*Mythological/literary narrative · Greece, 8th century BCE · [Homer, Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odyssey)*

Odysseus wants to hear the Sirens' song, which no mortal has heard and lived. His solution is a pre-commitment: he has his crew plug their ears with wax and bind him to the mast with orders to add ropes, not remove them, no matter what he says. He hears the song, begs to be released, is held, and survives.

The passage is the founding text of commitment devices, now a standard tool in behavioural science. Its precision is worth noting: Odysseus does not overcome his desire; he arranges in advance to be unable to act on it, which is the strategy of the person who knows their future self cannot be trusted.
