*Film narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 2000 · [Cast Away](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cast_Away)*

Chuck Noland, a FedEx executive whose life is run by clocks and pagers, survives a crash onto an uninhabited island. Four years later he is rescued, having learned to make fire, crack coconuts, and survive the teeth of a dentist's parcel, and having lost Wilson, the volleyball friend, in a scene that reduces audiences to tears. Back in the world, he no longer carries a beeper. The film ends with him at a rural crossroads, literally, with a choice of directions.

The film's Stoicism is in the inventory. Everything the island strips away, the schedule, the convenience, the job's urgencies, turns out to have been not necessary, and the one thing the island gives, the friendship with Wilson, is revealed as the thing the old life had no slot for. The beeper's absence at the end is the moral.
