*Scientific/biographical narrative · United States, 2019 · [Steven C. Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_C._Hayes)*

Hayes, the founder of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, describes the core of his approach as psychological flexibility: the capacity to contact the present moment and, depending on what the situation affords, to persist or change behaviour in the service of chosen values. The truth of a thought is not the criterion; workability is. A Liberated Mind recounts the method and its origins, including his own panic disorder.

ACT is the most explicit operationalisation of pragmatism in clinical psychology. The move that makes it one is the replacement of the correspondence test with the functional test: does holding this thought, in this context, move you toward what you value? It is James's cash value, applied six days a week in clinics.
