*Literary narrative, cross-cultural · Russia, 1877 · [Anna Karenina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Karenina)*

Anna, in a cold marriage, falls in love with Vronsky. She leaves her husband and son, and is punished for it by a society that forgives Vronsky the same act. The love that promised liberation becomes its own prison: her world narrows to Vronsky, his world does not narrow to her, and she reads jealousy and diminishment into everything. She throws herself under a train.

Tolstoy's design is careful: Anna's fate is produced not by the passion but by the absence of everything else, the loss of work, son, son, and standing that left her with a single point of value. The novel is the study of a life in which one good has displaced all others, which is the Aristotelian definition of a ruined one.
