*Mythological/literary narrative · Greece, 8th century BCE · [Homer, Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odyssey)*

Odysseus' scouts reach a land where the people eat the lotus, and those who taste it forget home and want only to stay, eating more. Odysseus drags them weeping to the ships and ties them under the benches. They had to be tied down: the men themselves, once tasted, no longer wanted to leave.

The episode is the oldest portrait of pleasure as a threat to the project of a life. Its detail is the worth: the lotus does not harm the body or the mind; it removes the wanting of anything else, which Homer, with the whole Odyssey still to travel, treats as the most dangerous thing in the world.
