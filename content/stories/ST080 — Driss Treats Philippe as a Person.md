*Film narrative, cross-cultural · France, 2011 · [The Intouchables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Intouchables)*

Philippe, a wealthy quadriplegic aristocrat, hires Driss, a young man from the banlieue with a prison record, as his caregiver. Driss does not treat Philippe with pity or professional distance. He forgets, mislabels, and jokes about the disability; he takes him paragliding, listens to Earth Wind and Fire against Philippe's classical preferences, and addresses his loneliness directly. Philippe's life, and Driss's, reorganise around the friendship.

The film is sentimental and was criticised for it, but its mechanism is exactly the Buddhist claim about compassion's true form. Pity maintains distance and confirms hierarchy. Driss's friendliness is careless in the best sense: it declines to make the condition the centre of every interaction.
