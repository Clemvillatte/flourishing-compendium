### Boundaries to carry into the lesson

The recording presents alpha = .05 as a convention, not a law, and says
a p-value near .05 offers only weak evidence against the null; a module
that retains the Woolley worked example should keep its existing
caution about overlapping intervals and magnitude, which the recording
does not replace. The recording's "sociology of science" chapter is
brief and does not substitute for the reading's fuller treatment of
science as a social activity. Its final chapter states the Type
I/Type II power tradeoff compactly; the module's own faded-practice
appraisal still supplies the applied training.

The transcript states that the ASA's 2016 statement "we'll engage with
directly in the next lecture": a Week 9B recording on the ASA statement
appears to exist on the same channel and has NOT been checked here.
The M1 module already cites the ASA statement PDF directly, so this
matters only if the owner wants a video companion for that source; it
would need its own bounded check.
