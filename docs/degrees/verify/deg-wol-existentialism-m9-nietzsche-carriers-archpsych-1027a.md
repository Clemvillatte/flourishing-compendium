- **Article**: Terao T and Satoh M (2022), "The Present State of Existential Interventions Within Palliative Care", Frontiers in Psychiatry 12:811612, doi 10.3389/fpsyt.2021.811612 — full-text page and PDF both opened this session (https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2021.811612/full).
