  established from the materials opened: the uploader description and the Harvard Justice

  consequentialism treatment. The pilot should treat AR-01 as covering outcome 2 (why

### AR-04: Aristotle (Edith Hall, Gresham College)

- Identity and access: the Gresham watch-now page
  (https://www.gresham.ac.uk/watch-now/aristotle) hosts the recording, free, consistent
  with Gresham College's free public lecture mission. The official Gresham College
  YouTube upload of the same lecture was opened on its watch page
  (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ido9HCquCi0), free, no paywall.
- Duration: the Gresham web pages print no duration for this lecture. The official
  Gresham College YouTube upload prints 0:44:17 (opened on the watch page 2026-10-25,
  upload date 10 March 2021). The degree owner should attach 44:17 with this provenance.
- Instructor credentials: Edith Hall, Visiting Gresham Professor in Classics; Professor
  in the Department of Classics and Centre for Hellenic Studies at King's College
  London; a scholar of ancient Greek literature and cultural history (stated on the
  Gresham lecture pages).
- Language: English. Gresham publishes a transcript of the lecture on its
  lectures-and-events page, which was opened and read.
- Content coverage (from the Gresham transcript page,
  https://www.gresham.ac.uk/lectures-and-events/aristotle): the lecture works through
  Aristotle's method, then his practical works. The transcript states that the
  Nicomachean Ethics and Politics are his two great works of practical knowledge, that
  the human telos is "happiness or eudaimonia", that the precise meaning of eudaimonia
  is controversial and can be rendered as flourishing or living well, that happiness for
  Aristotle is an activity rather than a state, and that virtue and vice sit around the
  mean (meson), with an excess/mean/deficiency table. This is direct transcript
  evidence that the talk introduces eudaimonia, so it serves outcomes 1 and 2 as the
  definitional lecture.
- Relevant segment: assigned as a whole talk, 44:17 (duration provenance above). The
  transcript page prints no timestamps, so no finer segmentation is available from
  opened sources.
- Verification status: verified-video.
