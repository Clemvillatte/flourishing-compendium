## Bounded reading residuals and safeguards

1. **Autonomy continuum:** keep Figure 1 and the adjacent definitions
   of amotivation and integrated regulation from Ryan/Deci 2020.
   Intrinsic interest is not the same as value-endorsed extrinsic
   motivation; integration concerns congruence with the person's
   other values/identity, not a new reward schedule. The reopened
   preprint supports these distinctions. Do not retain the entire
   school-practice review merely because the video omits one label.
   [Opened preprint](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/2020_RyanDeci_CEP_PrePrint.pdf).
2. **Eudaimonic goals:** keep the 2024 review's *Data extraction and
   synthesis* paragraph that defines the authors' three goal
   categories, its finding that none of the reviewed models
   explicitly prioritises eudaimonic goals, and its sentence that
   empirical studies must consolidate the theoretically derived
   proposal. Do not call this classification a settled universal
   taxonomy, equate all instrumental aims with unpleasant emotion
   or present virtue as a clinical health test. Tamir supplies the
   more basic hedonic/instrumental distinction; Pekrun supplies
   control-value appraisals, neither establishes that proposal.
   [Opened systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11479914/).
3. **2025 adherence evidence:** retain the abstract, the paragraph
   immediately before *Study 3*, *Study 4*'s opening and final
   Results paragraph, and the *General Discussion* paragraph
   beginning “Several open questions remain.” Studies 1–2 are
   correlational/self-report; Study 3 uses step-count records;
   Study 4 randomly assigns intrinsic/extrinsic app framing and
   supports a **short-term, 24-hour** causal claim. Do not label
   the whole four-study paper correlational or extrapolate the
   app experiment into a year-long intervention guarantee.
   Generalisation beyond selected online pools remains open.
   Do not assign all tables or imply external motives are bad.
   [Opened Woolley et al. study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13428958/).
4. **Application boundary:** classify the reasons for a chosen action,
   not the learner's character. Multiple reasons may coexist.
   Autonomy support does not require ignoring poverty, disability,
   work requirements or other material constraints.
   [Ryan/Deci discussion](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/2020_RyanDeci_CEP_PrePrint.pdf).
