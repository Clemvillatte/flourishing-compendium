### D-T01: Daodejing, Chinese Text Project (Legge 1891 translation)

- URL: https://ctext.org/dao-de-jing (chapter URLs of the form
  https://ctext.org/dao-de-jing/<chapter-slug>, the form the wiki already
  cites 5+ times).
- Identity: the received 81-chapter Daodejing in parallel Chinese and
  English; the English is James Legge's 1891 translation (The Tao Te
  Ching), identified on the page itself. Base text is the Wang Bi
  recension from the Zhengtong Daozang (正統道藏本王弼註道德真經),
  labelled Warring States; textual notes record variant readings
  (Heshanggong, Mawangdui, Guyi congshu editions) on chapters including
  3, 32 and 52.
- Accessibility: opened anonymously, no paywall, no login; the full
  database is browsable and searchable on line.
- Content coverage: all 81 chapters present in both languages; a module
  can assign exact chapters by URL.
- Translator standing: Legge's 1891 translation is a public-domain
  standard; Legge was Oxford's first Professor of Chinese (institutional
  standing is historical and documented; not re-opened this session).
- Language: Chinese (simplified and traditional) and English, parallel.
- Status: verified (text). Caveat for module design: Legge 1891 is a
  period translation; the owner may prefer pairing it with a modern
  lecture video (the Ziporyn and Van Norden videos are separately
  re-checked in verify/deg-wol-daoism-two-anchor-recheck.md).
