## Primary-text carriers (proactive pass, 2026-10-26)

### D-T01: Daodejing, Chinese Text Project (Legge 1891 translation)

- URL: https://ctext.org/dao-de-jing (chapter URLs of the form
  https://ctext.org/dao-de-jing/<chapter-slug>, the form the wiki already
  cites 5+ times).
- Identity: the received 81-chapter Daodejing in parallel Chinese and
  English; the English is James Legge's 1891 translation (The Tao Te
  Ching), identified on the page itself. Base text is the Wang Bi
  recension from the Zhengtong Daozang (正統道藏本王弼註道德真經),
  labelled Warring States; textual notes record variant readings
  (Heshanggong, Mawangdui, Guyi congshu editions) on chapters including
  3, 32 and 52.
- Accessibility: opened anonymously, no paywall, no login; the full
  database is browsable and searchable on line.
- Content coverage: all 81 chapters present in both languages; a module
  can assign exact chapters by URL.
- Translator standing: Legge's 1891 translation is a public-domain
  standard; Legge was Oxford's first Professor of Chinese (institutional
  standing is historical and documented; not re-opened this session).
- Language: Chinese (simplified and traditional) and English, parallel.
- Status: verified (text). Caveat for module design: Legge 1891 is a
  period translation; the owner may prefer pairing it with a modern
  lecture video (the Ziporyn and Van Norden videos are separately
  re-checked in verify/deg-wol-daoism-two-anchor-recheck.md).

### D-T02: Zhuangzi, Chinese Text Project (Legge 1891 translation)

- URL: https://ctext.org/zhuangzi (chapter URLs of the form
  https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/<chapter-slug>; the wiki already cites 40+
  such URLs, e.g. /zhuangzi/enjoyment-in-untroubled-ease).
- Identity: the received 33-chapter Zhuangzi (Inner, Outer and Mixed
  chapters; 內篇 Inner Chapters listed on the index); English is Legge's
  1891 translation (The Writings of Chuang Tzu). Digital base text the
  Xugu congshu (續古逸叢書) edition of the Nanhai zhenjing, with a
  scanned source in the Chinese Text Project library.
- Accessibility: opened anonymously, no paywall, no login.
- Content coverage: full text browsable chapter by chapter; the Inner
  Chapters 1-7 the scope names as core are individually addressable.
- Language: Chinese and English.
- Status: verified (text). Same period-translation caveat as D-T01.

### D-T03: Neiye (Guanzi ch. 49), Chinese Text Project

- URL: https://ctext.org/guanzi/nei-ye/zh
- Identity: the Neiye (內業, Inward Training) chapter of the Guanzi,
  10 numbered sections, Chinese only.
- Accessibility: opened anonymously, no paywall.
- Content coverage: Chinese text complete; NO English translation is
  offered on the page for this chapter (ctext carries public-domain
  translations only, and none exists for the Guanzi).
- Status: verified (Chinese text); NOT an English carrier. Flag for the
  owner: the walk's Neiye elective needs an open English translation
  carrier; see D-T04 and the finding below.
