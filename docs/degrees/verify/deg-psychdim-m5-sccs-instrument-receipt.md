document attributed to Campbell and colleagues' 1996 article,
Self-concept clarity: Measurement, personality correlates, and cultural

([Archived SCCS instrument](https://dlab.sauder.ubc.ca/sjdm/dmidi/files/Campbell_et_al._(1996)_Self-Concept_Clarity.doc).)
This receipt verifies the document itself, not the full 1996 validation
paper, which was not opened in this pass.

The archived form contains 12 items with response anchors 1, strongly
disagree, through 5, strongly agree.
([Instrument and response anchors](https://dlab.sauder.ubc.ca/sjdm/dmidi/files/Campbell_et_al._(1996)_Self-Concept_Clarity.doc).)
Items 1–5, 7–10 and 12 are marked reverse-keyed; items 6 and 11 are not.
([Instrument's item markers and key](https://dlab.sauder.ubc.ca/sjdm/dmidi/files/Campbell_et_al._(1996)_Self-Concept_Clarity.doc).)
The complete item wording is not reproduced here.

For that five-point form, reversing a marked response is the arithmetic
operation \(6-r\). With all 12 valid responses present, a sum consequently
ranges from 12 to 60 and a mean from 1 to 5. These are arithmetic
consequences of the opened anchors and key, not diagnostic bands,
population norms or a missing-response rule.

## Why the version must be recorded

Vartanian, Hayward and Carter's article, published online in 2022 and
in a 2023 issue, uses the same 12-item SCCS with five response points
for its student sample and seven for its community sample.
([Open UNSW copy, Study 1 Measures, printed p. 185](https://www2.psy.unsw.edu.au/Users/lvartanian/Publications/Vartanian,%20Hayward,%20&%20Carter%20(2023).pdf).)
The authors compute mean scores after reverse coding and use each
scale's midpoint to group participants for presenting their analyses.
([Study 1 Measures and Table 1 note, printed pp. 185 and 187](https://www2.psy.unsw.edu.au/Users/lvartanian/Publications/Vartanian,%20Hayward,%20&%20Carter%20(2023).pdf).)
That is an analytic grouping choice in this study, not evidence of a
validated clinical threshold for a learner.

A later authorized use should therefore identify the actual form,
anchors, reverse key and aggregation rule before displaying a score.
Do not compare a five-point mean directly with a seven-point mean,
reuse \(6-r\) on a seven-point form, or borrow a research midpoint as
a verdict about who the learner is.

Only the instrument-handling passage of the UNSW paper serves this
receipt. Its body-image research question, models and findings are
not proposed for the degree, and the whole paper is not added to
required reading.

## Interpretation stays narrower than identity

The selected 2026 review distinguishes perceived clarity and
consistency of self-beliefs from self-esteem, self-knowledge accuracy
and the content of those beliefs.
([Manchiraju, related-constructs section and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13350471/).)
A properly keyed score is not thereby proof that a self-description is
accurate, ethically admirable or fixed.

The review also cautions that self-report dominates the evidence,
cross-national measurement invariance is insufficiently established,
and rigid clarity need not be adaptive.
([Measurement appraisal, cultural perspectives and optimal-clarity sections](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13350471/).)
For this degree's existing Walk-of-Life purpose, those limits belong
with any later reflection on commitments or changing self-descriptions.
No claim that this instrument causes flourishing or measures wisdom
is supported here.
