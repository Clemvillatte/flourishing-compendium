## Retained evidence, narrowly assigned

### Habit science

Keep the **Carden/Wood 2018 opening three mechanism paragraphs**
beginning “Habits form as people pursue goals…” and the portion on
habit/self-control relations. These distinguish learned cue–response
associations from an intention, repeated behaviour and momentary
effort. Do not turn Wood's spoken “no way…except repetition” into an
exhaustive law: the paper also discusses environmental change.
([Opened author-hosted review](https://dornsife.usc.edu/wendy-wood/wp-content/uploads/sites/183/2023/10/Carden.Wood_.2018.pdf).)

Keep **Singh 2024 abstract**, rather than the full paper, for the
duration/evidence caveat: 20 studies, 2,601 participants, 11 high-risk
studies; only four studies reported formation time, with substantial
individual variability. The pre/post score meta-analysis is not a
universal “days until automatic” estimate and is not necessarily a
randomised between-group effect. Neither “21 days” nor “66 days”
is a promise to a learner.
([Opened review, abstract and methods 2.7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11641623/).)

Bouton's **reinforcement/punishment vocabulary**, if needed after the
clips, is a short precision aid: reinforcement increases subsequent
behaviour; negative means removal, not “bad.” Extinction is not simply
erasing the original association. Do not require the whole Noba
module on top of clips covering the same introductory definitions.
([Opened Noba module](https://nobaproject.com/modules/conditioning-and-learning).)

### Self-regulation, measurement and competing claims

Keep **Werner/Berkman 2024 “Self-Control is a Flexible and Dynamic
Process”**, the four paragraphs starting “Exactly how and when…”:
identification, selection, implementation, monitoring. The short
Berkman video does not name all four stages. Read this as a
conceptual account, not a primary experiment proving one strategy
works for every person; use a fictional low-stakes goal conflict.
([Opened review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11423838/).)

Keep **Reed 2020 abstract plus “Participants”** for the three
measurement families and sample: executive function, HRV and subjective
self-regulation were only modestly correlated in 149 adults aged
60–93, followed semi-annually for five years. They are **not three
interchangeable personal scores or universal diagnostic components**.
This is observational older-adult evidence, not proof changing HRV
causes better regulation or health.
([Opened study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7250393/).)

Keep **Alarcón-Espinoza 2022 abstract plus eligibility criterion
(d)** only if the module explicitly contrasts ecological measurement:
it reviews **under-18s** in natural contexts, not a general adult
effectiveness review. Otherwise use it as an optional comparison,
not core adult self-regulation instruction.
([Opened review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9171140/).)

For numerical appraisal retain **Dang's Table 2 and opening Discussion
paragraphs**, alongside the **Vohs 2021 abstract and Methods:
Participants**. Dang's all-task estimate changes from unadjusted
g = 0.38 to trim/fill-adjusted g = 0.24; task choices, heterogeneity
and adjustment assumptions matter. The later preregistered 36-lab
project, N = 3,531 before exclusions, reports confirmatory
d = 0.06 nonsignificant, versus exploratory full-sample d = 0.08.
Do not blur Hedges' g with Cohen's d, preplanned with exploratory
analysis, or “not significant” with proof of no conceivable effect.
([Opened Dang meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6013521/),
[opened Vohs author manuscript](https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/bitstream/2438/28246/1/FullText.pdf).)

The 2019 interview predates that 2021 project; the latter is required
for an honest update rather than presenting the speaker's confidence
as the final result. Its null does not establish that tiredness is
imaginary, effort never matters or people can overcome every
environmental barrier.
([2019 interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fuA1l7bL954),
[2021 primary manuscript](https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/bitstream/2438/28246/1/FullText.pdf).)
