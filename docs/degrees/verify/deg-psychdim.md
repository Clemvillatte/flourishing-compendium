## PD-V01: EEMM orientation and context-sensitive change

- **Opened:** 2026-10-26, verifier-96108df3. Search identified the
  [publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39118646/);
  verification used the full, unprompted
  [PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11303929/).
- **Identity and access:** Stefan G. Hofmann and Steven C. Hayes,
  *A Process-Based Approach to Transtheoretical Clinical Research and
  Training*, Clinical Psychology in Europe 6, special issue, e11987,
  published 26 April 2024, DOI 10.32872/cpe.11987; anonymous full text,
  CC BY 4.0, as printed in the
  [article header](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11303929/).
- **Credentials:** the separate
  [Marburg institutional profile](https://www.uni-marburg.de/en/fb04/team-hofmann/team/prof-dr-stefan-g-hofmann)
  lists Hofmann in the psychology department as Head, Alexander von
  Humboldt Professor and LOEWE Spitzenprofessor.
- **Content checked:** main text from Abstract through Concluding
  Thoughts, with the reference list inspected for attribution; the paper
  describes affect, cognition, self, attention, motivation and overt
  behaviour as psychological dimensions, alongside biophysiological and
  sociocultural levels, and organises change through context-sensitive
  variation, selection and retention
  ([full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11303929/)).
- **Exact proposed assignment:** the introductory paragraphs beginning
  “PBT has a characteristic target” through the paragraph immediately
  before Figure 1, then the section *Functional Analysis in an Idionomic
  Approach*. These passages explain how a person's goals, circumstances
  and interacting processes guide an analysis
  ([assigned text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11303929/)).
- **Walk of Life fit:** retain as a short M1 orientation anchor. A
  production exercise can ask the learner to describe one recurring
  everyday difficulty across the six dimensions, identify a contextual
  constraint and state what they would need to observe before choosing a
  response. This exercise is a curriculum proposal, not a tested
  intervention from the paper.
- **Evidence type and limit:** conceptual and methodological argument,
  with discussion of earlier research. This article reports no new
  randomised trial or pooled treatment effect; it cannot establish that
  the proposed educational exercise relieves suffering
  ([article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11303929/)).
- **Scope caution:** the authors explicitly allow other useful
  dimensions and do not claim hard divisions between them. Teach the
  six as this degree's chosen organising frame; avoid describing them
  as an exhaustive natural taxonomy
  ([EEMM discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11303929/)).
- **Status:** verified reading, English; source identity, anonymous
  access, bounded content and independent institutional credential
  checked. Placement and instructional effectiveness remain separate
  decisions for the owner and independent reviewers.
