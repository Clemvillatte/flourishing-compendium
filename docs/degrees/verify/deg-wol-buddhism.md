### B-T01: Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta (SN 56.11), Access to Insight

- URL: https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn56/sn56.011.than.html
- Identity: SN 56.11 (PTS S v 420; CDB ii 1843), "Setting the Wheel of
  Dhamma in Motion", translated from the Pali by Thanissaro Bhikkhu
  (© 1993; BCBS Edition, last revised for Access to Insight
  30 November 2013). The discourse runs from the opening ("I have
  heard...") through Kondañña and the devas proclaiming the wheel set
  in motion; alternate translations (Ñanamoli, Harvey, Piyadassi) are
  listed on the page.
- Accessibility: opened anonymously, no paywall, no login. License
  stated on the page: Creative Commons BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial) —
  usable in a free non-commercial Academy quoted with credit.
- Content coverage: the four noble truths and their tasks are in the
  discourse body; a module can assign the whole sutta. This is the same
  text the deg-verify-b four-truths receipt
  (verify/deg-wol-buddhism-four-truths-source-boundaries.md) treats as
  the finite primary-text anchor for the truths; that receipt also
  bounds the Dreyfus video's narrower coverage (first session covers
  dukkha only).
- Language: English.
- Status: verified (text). Caveat: the page itself cautions the
  displayed version might be out of date relative to the translator's
  current text; the page also lists the SuttaCentral Sujato/Bodhi
  translations the wiki cites, which are fetcher-blocked from this
  session's environment (environment limitation, not a paywall
  finding).

### B-T02: Five Mindfulness Trainings, Plum Village

- URL: https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/the-5-mindfulness-trainings
- Identity: the full text of all five trainings (Reverence For Life,
  True Happiness, True Love, Loving Speech and Deep Listening,
  Nourishment and Healing), presented as Thich Nhat Hanh's translation
  of the precepts for modern times ("I have translated these precepts
  for modern times" — page's introductory quotation); the page states
  the trainings have their root in the Five Precepts offered by the
  Buddha and were last revised 15 June 2022.
- Accessibility: opened anonymously, no paywall, no login; a 2022 PDF
  of the trainings is also linked on the page.
- Content coverage: all five trainings in full on one page; the
  ethics-first module can assign the whole page. The page's framing
  matches the walk's ethics-first exit capability (precepts, harmlessness,
  compassion) and the HKU anchor's Five Mindfulness Trainings lesson.
- Instructor credentials: Thich Nhat Hanh's standing is documented on
  Plum Village's own biographical pages (the site's
  /about/thich-nhat-hanh/ section; the wiki already cites pages there).
  Not re-opened this session; the owner should attach the specific
  biography page used.
- Language: English.
- Status: verified (text).
