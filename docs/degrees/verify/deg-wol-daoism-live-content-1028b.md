Daoism I: the outline "Laozi the Person? / The Text / Concepts / Discussion on Selected
Extracts / Daoism vs Confucianism"; the dao glossed as "A mode of conducting affairs, living
one's life, or organising of the state"; key concepts "Dao", "Uncarved wood", "Non-Action";
discussion questions keyed to named chapters.

[2.0]", "Emptiness"; it asks "What implications does this have for how we are to act (or
not) in it?"
