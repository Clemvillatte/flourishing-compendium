### Boundaries to carry into the lesson

The video names the dimensions but does not replace the beginner-friendly
definitions of each dimension or the module's worked personal case.
Its brief research-project description also supplies no warrant to claim
that a framework alone establishes treatment efficacy or guarantees
improvement for a particular person.
([Assigned transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sDGpXQLHLfY).)

Keep the distinction between a psychological dimension and a level of
analysis explicit: sociocultural context and biophysiology are the
additional levels in Hayes's explanation.
([01:08:12 onward](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sDGpXQLHLfY&t=4092).)

The video has no lesson on regression coefficients, confidence intervals,
the ASA p-value correction, or the Woolley study. It therefore replaces
only the framework excerpts, not M1's research-literacy instruction or
worked appraisal.
([Inspected segment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sDGpXQLHLfY&t=3797).)
