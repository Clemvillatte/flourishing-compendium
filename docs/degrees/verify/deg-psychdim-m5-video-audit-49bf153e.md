## Accuracy repairs before production uses these videos

- **Do not assign the full dissonance chapter as currently planned.**
  It makes categorical claims that therapy without medication or
  cognitive-behavioural treatment is useless and that free therapy is
  useless. These are unsupported generalisations in this lecture, not
  acceptable mental-health guidance. It also includes painful hazing
  and cult examples unnecessary for this module. The **27:00–28:41**
  excerpt stops before the experimental survey; teach a fictional
  everyday mismatch and label it an illustration, not treatment.
  ([Complete chapter 4](https://oyc.yale.edu/psychology/psyc-110/lecture-16).)
- Lecture 16's self-evaluation chapter calls exaggerated positive
  self-views “normal, healthy.” Treat it as a historical lecture
  claim/example, not a universal prescription to distort evidence or a
  diagnostic contrast with depression. No learner should be assessed
  on having high self-esteem.
  ([Chapter 3](https://oyc.yale.edu/psychology/psyc-110/lecture-16).)
- The attribution chapter includes 2007 political, relationship and
  cultural examples. Use them to practise considering situational
  alternatives, not to teach that a bias occurs in every person,
  dismiss competence differences or diagnose another person's motives.
  ([Chapter 5](https://oyc.yale.edu/psychology/psyc-110/lecture-16).)
- Do not repeat lecture 13's “personality stabilizes at about 30” as a
  hard boundary. The reopened Noba text describes tendencies over time
  and situations, continuous distributions, context and aggregation;
  a single act is not a trait diagnosis. Retain the introduction's
  continuous-distribution paragraph and the **last three paragraphs
  of “The Person-Situation Debate”** as the short correction.
  ([Lecture 13](https://oyc.yale.edu/psychology/psyc-110/lecture-13),
  [Noba Personality Traits](https://nobaproject.com/modules/personality-traits).)
- The McAdams talk later discusses US presidents, national redemption
  narratives and individual case histories; none is assigned. The
  short clip teaches a concept, not “redemptive stories cause health.”
  Narrative identity, exploration and commitment are not synonyms.
  ([Full McAdams transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=49FCgaZRn_M),
  [identity review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9298910/).)
- The Little clip is an illustration, not a licence for trait-based
  stereotyping. The unassigned talk contains caffeine, sexuality,
  gender and communication generalisations not needed for this
  lesson; the selected excerpt ends before the bathroom anecdote.
  ([Complete TED transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYvXk_bqlBk).)

## Self-concept clarity and identity: bounded reading fallback

The reopened self-concept-clarity review defines SCC as clearly and
confidently defined, internally consistent and temporally stable
self-beliefs. It distinguishes **structure** from the positive or
negative evaluation of self-esteem, gives the 12-item SCCS's role,
and explicitly warns about correlational, self-report and cultural
limitations.
([Chen et al., conceptualisation, Table 1 and critical-appraisal sections](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13350471/).)

Retain those definition/contrast, measurement and limitation spans,
not the entire health-outcomes survey. The selected narrative and
trait videos do not teach SCCS scoring, cultural invariance or justify
a clinical cutoff. The degree's SCCS receipt remains a separate
instrument-verification task, not an invitation to invent a score
threshold.
([SCC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13350471/).)

For identity formation, retain only the review's **Exploration and
commitment** explanation and distinction from **Life narratives**:
the former concerns examining/choosing commitments, the latter concerns
meaning-making in stories. Adolescent evidence is not automatically
adult causal evidence.
([Branje et al., sections 1.1–1.2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9298910/).)

These are outcome-specific fallbacks after actual searches and
candidate checks below, not a claim that no relevant video exists.
