## Psychdim quoted-phrase locators

44 quoted phrases and headings used in the published psychdim locators (for example "Over the years, different models of ER", "Learning depends on such a surprise", "The alerting effect was calculated", "Does ego depletion exist?") were searched for in the full text of the 18 cited sources. 41 were found by the script. The remaining 3, all in PMC articles that returned a bot-check page to the script (PD-M7-08 Dang 2018 and PD-M7-07 Reed et al. 2020), were confirmed on the live pages through the fetch tool: PD-M7-08 has the heading "Does ego depletion exist?", the sentence "Therefore, taken together I suggest that it is not adequate to draw a strong conclusion from the current analysis that the ego depletion effect exists." and "Table 2. Meta-analytic results for the overall effect."; PD-M7-07 has a Methods subsection headed "Participants". Result: 44 of 44 verified.

Not recounted: the approximate word counts for PD-M1-03 (about 3,200), PD-M1-04 (about 300) and PD-M2-04 (about 2,794).
