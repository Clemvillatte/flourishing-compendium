## What changed

- **Lived transfer:** the final assessment now requires a safely chosen
  actual low-stakes episode, rather than allowing the fictional Jo task
  to stand in for all lived application. A 150–220-word private note
  connects the description, emotion function, two possible regulation
  families, a personally relevant choice, the accounts' different
  questions, another dimension and a remaining uncertainty.
- **Privacy and missing observations:** identifying details may remain
  private; a redacted outline and confirmation of an actual episode can
  support feedback. “Not noticed/not recalled” is acceptable for bodily
  sensation, and missing observations must not be guessed. Unsafe recall
  is not required; transfer can be deferred. Fiction remains available
  for the earlier practice.
- **Rubric calibration:** all five criteria now have concrete strong
  and rejection specimens. The reflection specimen is explicitly
  hypothetical and cannot be relabelled as a genuine learner record.
  The strong regulation specimen names and applies all five families.
- **Alignment:** the reflection outcome, mandatory instructions,
  criterion and alignment map now require the same private lived work.
  A fictional-only submission does not meet the complete assessment.
- **Workload:** teaching increases from 35 to 45 minutes and assessment
