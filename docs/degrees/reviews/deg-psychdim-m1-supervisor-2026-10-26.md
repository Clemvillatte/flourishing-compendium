## Independent source checks

These checks concern the inherited module, not any later video revision.
All sources below were fetched in this review session. They do not
stand in for a media-standard check of a new video.

- **Framework:** the opened Hofmann and Hayes paper presents a proposed,
  multidimensional and multilevel framework, discusses context-sensitive
  variation, selection and retention, and does not claim the six
  psychological dimensions are exhaustive or sharply separated. M1's
  proposal/non-exhaustive framing is appropriate; keep the distinction
  between dimensions and the biophysiological/sociocultural levels.
  ([Hofmann and Hayes, full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11303929/))
- **Maitland framework passage:** the opened article's EEMM discussion
  supports the variation-selection-retention-context explanation. Its
  relational/clinical application is not evidence that M1's fictional
  Jordan exercise produces a particular benefit.
  ([Maitland, full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12392214/))
- **Worked observational appraisal:** Study 1 reports the four survey
  counts as 2,000, 1,046, 709 and 577; the first adherence measurement
  occurs at the second wave. Its intrinsic-motivation paths and 95%
  intervals match M1: 0.216 [0.13, 0.30], 0.099 [0.04, 0.20] and
  0.080 [0.01, 0.15].
  ([Woolley, Giurge and Fishbach, Study 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13428958/))
- **Inference and missingness:** the same opened Study 1 states that
  missingness was handled with full-information maximum likelihood and
  that a completers-only check gave similar results. It also describes
  the association as primarily between people. M1 correctly does not
  convert this into a within-person causal prediction or claim that the
  analysis eliminates attrition bias.
  ([Study 1 analysis and robustness discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13428958/))
- **Noba limitation:** the opened Smith module does contain the
  problematic account of “significant at a p < .05 level” that M1
  identifies. The correction must accompany any retained assignment,
  not appear only in an internal verification record.
  ([Smith, research-literacy module](https://nobaproject.com/modules/thinking-like-a-psychological-scientist))
- **Statistical correction:** the opened ASA statement's principles 2,
  3 and 5 support distinguishing a p-value from a hypothesis probability,
  avoiding a single threshold as a decision rule, and separating
  statistical significance from effect magnitude or importance.
  ([ASA statement, PDF](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Real_World/ASA_statement.pdf))
- **Interval comparison:** Greenland and colleagues' misinterpretation
  21 explicitly says overlapping intervals do not establish the absence
  of a difference, and calls for direct estimation/testing of the
  difference. M1 appropriately declines to infer unchanged strength from
  overlapping intervals.
  ([Greenland and colleagues, item 21](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4877414/))
