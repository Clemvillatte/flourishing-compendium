## Required repair (bounded; route to the production owner)

The outcomes promise that the learner can "distinguish all six
positions on the SDT autonomy continuum", but the module's
feedback-bearing practice never asks the learner to distinguish
amotivation from an intentional form, and the final task's
"Motivation" criterion ("all-six map understood") is not an
observable pass/revise distinction — it lets a strong answer pass
without ever handling amotivation. One of:

1. add a short completion item with feedback that contrasts an
   amotivated statement (for example "I see no point and don't know
   how to begin") with an introjected or identified statement of the
   same activity, asking which evidence would distinguish them; or
2. with owner approval, narrow the outcome to the forms the module
   actually teaches and assesses with feedback.

Everything else in the module can stand as written.
