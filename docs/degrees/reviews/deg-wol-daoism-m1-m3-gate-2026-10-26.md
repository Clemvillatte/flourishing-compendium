## Centering-clause verdict per module

- **m1 (orientation):** PASS. The module is built around the owner's
  value proposition in its own opening ("suffer less, choose more
  wisely and flourish even when uncertain or overwhelmed"). The
  four-terms deliverable requires examples from the learner's own
  life with the three-part reflection (what matters / what the term
  helps with / what remains uncertain). The exclusions contract
  (Step 6) is exemplary: cosmology, ritual/liturgy, political
  readings and contemplative doctrine each named with an owner, and
  the finite-scope admission test stated in the module itself.
- **m2 (Daodejing as practice manual):** PASS. Passage-versus-
  application discipline is taught explicitly and assessed (the
  chapter "says nothing about jobs"); the two-translations
  comparison (Eno vs Ziporyn on chapter 28's closing line) is a
  genuine substantiated disagreement carried into a graded
  deliverable. Workload arithmetic labelled; sources free and
  verified (Eno educator-licensed text; Ziporyn video with
  transcript-verified coverage).
- **m3 (Zhuangzi: skill, wu wei, self):** PASS. The Walk-of-Life
  question (self-monitoring as avoidable strain vs useful vigilance)
  is the module's spine and is assessed with a limit the learner
  must name. Skill-as-story vs skill-as-advice distinction prevents
  the classic misuse. The refusal to assign the Van Norden martial-
  arts chapter (transcript service prints no timestamps beside
  speaker words — no verified placement claimable) is exactly the
  no-hallucination discipline the project requires.
