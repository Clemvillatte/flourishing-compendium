## Centering-clause assessment

The scope states a bounded personal-learning purpose: the intended learner is
"walking the positive psychology path" with the science "applied to their own
life", entry assumes no psychology background but a willingness to "run
personal experiments and keep records", and the middle exit capability is
application with recorded outcomes. That is a Walk-of-Life shape. The value
proposition is plausibly served: recorded personal experiments are the
proposed application route for flourishing work (the route itself is not
proof that the degree produces flourishing or alleviates suffering, and is
not claimed as such here), and the first exit capability (evaluating
wellbeing claims against their evidence) is wisdom work. On the retrieved
scope text, centering is defensible. This is an assessment, not a gate
award.

## Findings against the skill's coverage rules

1. **Criticisms-of-the-field exit capability: UNRESOLVED COVERAGE ITEM.**
   The scope's third exit capability is "Can state the field's criticisms
   and where it overreaches." The retrieved excerpts do not establish that
   any anchor teaches a criticisms-of-positive-psychology unit: Warwick's
   outline offers PP as a critical lens on psychology and its outcomes
   require critical evaluation of the field's methods and research (closest
   support); Penn and the retrieved Michigan excerpts name none. The scope
   honestly records this as a divergence. Under the skill's coverage rules
   this row needs a verified teaching resource or a justified scope revision
   before curriculum-coverage approval; the degree's gap register already
   carries a criticism-source item (per the positive-1026p inventory).
2. **Effect-size emphasis: UNRESOLVED COVERAGE ITEM.** The first exit
   capability includes "effect size"; the scope itself records that explicit
   effect-size instruction is not established in the opened extracts and
   remains a proposed emphasis. Anchor it with a verified resource or trim
   the emphasis at module design; it cannot pass silently.
3. **Flow pillar: partial anchor support.** Penn explicitly teaches a full
   flow class with a personal flow exercise ("Write your previous Flow
   Experiences"); a flow unit is not established by the retrieved Warwick or
   Michigan excerpts. The scope records this honestly; the pillar is also
   carried by the deg-wellbeing legacy material named as raw material.
   Noted for module design.
4. **Practice-with-recorded-outcomes: well anchored.** All three anchors
   norm it (Penn's experiential activities and strength project; Michigan's
   four-week intervention study and learning journal; Warwick's reflections
   and assessed intervention). The scope's middle exit capability sits
   squarely inside the anchor range, as the scope claims.
5. **Sociocultural flourishing: anchored.** Penn's cross-cultural class and
   Warwick's positive institutions and society topic support the scope's
   sociocultural pillar. (The Michigan syllabus's hosting is not used as
   evidence here.)
6. **Elective branches: finite-scope assessment owed.** Positive education
   and positive organisations are listed as elective branches. Elective
   status or curriculum topicality alone does not qualify them under the
   centering clause: each needs a stated contribution to this learner's
   Walk-of-Life purpose, or the recommendation is to exclude them from this
   degree. This review recommends exclusion unless the degree owner states
   the contribution in the scope.
7. **Exclusions.** The clinical and basic-mechanisms exclusions are
   consistent with the retrieved anchor content; the retrieved excerpts do
   not establish that any anchor devotes core time to treating disorder.
   (Michigan's biological approaches appear in the retrieved excerpts as
   applications framing; the precise framing is not fully established by
   them.)
