## Required source-fidelity repairs

- **Place the traditional frame honestly.** The assigned full AN 5.179
  opens with rebirth, stream-entry and future awakening, and Khp 9 ends
  with not returning to a womb. The module's blanket statement that its
  source set has no rebirth-dependent doctrine is false. Bound the
  actual assigned precepts passage, or mark the traditional frame as
  context without teaching cosmology. This does not reopen the scope's
  distinction between cosmology and a passing mention.
  ([AN 5.179](https://suttacentral.net/an5.179/en/sujato);
  [Khp 9](https://suttacentral.net/kp9/en/sujato))
- **Repair the composite quotation.** “May all beings be happy!” occurs
  before “With love for the whole world, unfold a boundless heart.”
  The module joins them in reverse order with an ellipsis. Quote them
  separately in source order or paraphrase. Also remove “true” from the
  alleged “true state of peace” quotation; this translation says “state
  of peace.”
  ([Actual Sujato text](https://suttacentral.net/kp9/en/sujato))
- **Correct the negative transcript claim.** The claim that Brahm does
  not enumerate the precepts is contradicted around 07:52–09:10: the
  transcript discusses lying, stealing, alcohol, the five precepts,
  killing and adultery. This does not mean the talk alone teaches every
  needed application; it means the verification record must not deny
  content that is present.
  ([Opened Brahm transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TpbWVaXBV4c))
- **Separate the two biographies.** The booklet attributes establishing
  Lokanta Vihara in 2019 to Sujato, not to this Dhammadipa. Use the
  proper Dhammadipa biography for his credentials and remove the
  conflated detail; his actual biography supports training/ordination
  in 1985 and international teaching since 1997.
  ([BSWA booklet, p. 3](https://bswa.org/bswp/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Ajahn-Brahm-and-His-Merry-Monks-updated-10-March-2026-Final.pdf);
  [Dhammadipa biography](https://www.buddha-haus.de/bhante-dhammadipa/))
