## Required repairs (bounded; route to the live production owner)

1. **Transparency needs a feedback-bearing application.** The
   outcomes list transparency alongside spotlight, self-serving
   attribution, dissonance and person/situation attribution, and the
   teaching defines it, but no exercise or assessment line requires
   applying transparency specifically; the final task allows
   choosing one self/other concept. Add one short micro-case with
   feedback (for example: a person assumes their nervousness is
   obvious to everyone; state the hypothesis, one observation that
   would test it, and what the spotlight hypothesis would predict
   instead), or narrow the outcome wording with owner approval.

   The outcomes promise comparing trait and personal-project
   explanations of one behaviour, but the independent task's
   "Comparison" bullet lets the learner choose either the two
   development approaches or the trait/project explanations. Make the
   trait/project comparison explicitly required in the assessed work
   (the development comparison can stay optional), or obtain an
   approved narrowing of the outcome.
