## Sources opened (the D1 corpus — 3, all fetched and read this session)

1. **Wesleyan University, "CIS 135Z / CSPL 135Z Introduction to Mindfulness, Winter 2024, Instructor: David Leipziger Teva, MHL, Rabbi"** — https://www.wesleyan.edu/wintersession/pdfs/1240/CSPL135Z-Teva-syl — full 10-day schedule read this session: introduction and course overview (awareness of breath, sound, posture, yoga); framing and naming mindfulness — historical perspective and cultural milieu; somatic awareness (mindful walking, body scan); awareness of thoughts and gratitude (naming/labeling thoughts, past/present/future); minding technology (unplugging); awareness of words — prayer, metta and gratitude (loving-kindness practice); awareness of emotions — RAIN practice; mindful communication (listening, difficult communication exercise); issues, context and ethics (paying attention to your stress response); "I'm mindful, now what?" — setting intentions, building community, implementation strategy. Course description verbatim includes parsing "critiques of mindfulness" and examining "the drive to lift the secular practices from their religious roots."
2. **Brown University, "Certificate in Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction Teaching" curriculum page** — https://professional.brown.edu/academics/mindfulness-education/stress-reduction/curriculum — read this session. Prerequisites: personal meditation practice of at least two years, an 8-week MBSR course, a qualifying silent retreat (one 5-7 day or two 3-day). Pathway: six courses and a final review — MBSR Foundations and Facilitator Training (10 weeks), Teacher Advancement Intensive (three three-day sessions), Curriculum Study Group (10 weeks, 2.5h/week), Individual Mentoring (10 weekly hours while teaching), Ethos of Mindfulness-Based Programs (two six-week hybrid courses covering contemplative roots and teaching fundamentals; mindfulness science, trauma sensitivity, diversity and inclusive teaching), Dialogue and Inquiry (five weeks, 2h/week), Certification Review after teaching six 8-week programs.
3. **Mindfulness and Health Institute, "MBSR" course page** — https://mindfulnessandhealthinstitute.org/mindfulness-based-stress-reduction — the full 8-week class-by-class curriculum read this session: Orientation; Class 1 building a mindfulness foundation (mindful eating, focused attention meditation, body scan); Class 2 perception and creative responding (reactivity vs responsiveness); Class 3 the power of being present; Class 4 the "unwanted" (stress reactivity physiology, personal stress patterns); Class 5 being with discomfort ("turning toward"); Class 6 communication and interpersonal relating; All-Day 8-hour silent retreat; Class 7 integrating mindfulness into daily life (self-nourishing vs maladaptive habits); Class 8 making the practice your own.
