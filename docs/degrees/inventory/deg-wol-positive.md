## Centering clause (owner 2026-10-26)

Every topic and every resource below must serve the value proposition — alleviate suffering, become wiser, genuinely flourish even when uncertain or overwhelmed — through the Walk of Life / Philosophy of Life frame. Positive psychology is the discipline MOST directly aligned to this mission (flourishing is its own vocabulary), so the finite-scope risk runs the other way: the temptation is to admit everything pleasant. The exclusion rule applies hardest here: sports-psychology detail, consumer-brand "positive-organisation" applications and self-help without a research base are OUT unless carried by a teaching layer that shows evidence of alleviating suffering / building wisdom.

## Known gaps (declared, not hidden)

- **Primary-text access is the field's own problem**: Seligman's Flourish, Peterson's Character Strengths and Virtues, Csikszentmihalyi's Flow, Fredrickson's Positivity are paywalled books (the research articles behind them are partially open-access). The D2 pool must lean on open-access journal articles (Journal of Positive Psychology has open archives), the existing enriched doc library (which carries flourishing, gratitude, hope, resilience, meaning, wisdom dossiers — all validated), Laurie Santos's free Coursera "The Science of Well-Being" (Yale, free audit), the Greater Good Science Center open resources, and the PositivePsychology.com open toolkit.
- **Laurie Santos's Science of Well-Being** is the single strongest open teaching-layer candidate (Yale Coursera, free audit, ~20 hours of video, directly on-topic for the walk) and is already in the owner's resource-pool guidance; D2 verification is a formality but must still be logged.
