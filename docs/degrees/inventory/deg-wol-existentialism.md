## Topic map (union of the three syllabi, organized as a coherent walk)

The three syllabi triangulate: UCLA gives the philosophy-first canon (Kierkegaard → Nietzsche → Heidegger → Sartre → Beauvoir/Fanon) with the deepest Heidegger arc; Stanford gives the hands-on literary-woven sequence with Camus and Tolstoy's death-of-Ivan-Ilyich as lived-application carrier; UVic gives the widest extension set (Fanon, Ellison, Mills on race; Beauvoir's ethics; Camus on the absurd). The union below is the existential-walk-of-life curriculum. It is split into a philosophy half (the walk's spine) and an applied half (existential therapy + meaning work), matching the scope's two-half design.

### A. Orientation and the precursors
- What is existentialism; existence precedes essence; the individual against the abstract subject [UCLA L1; UVic wk 1; UCCS 8/24]
- Dostoevsky's underground man as the anti-rationalist voice [UVic wk 2; UNO]
- Job, Socrates' Apology, Augustine's Confessions as pre-figurations [UCCS 8/24 — contextual only]

### B. Kierkegaard — anxiety, despair, faith
- Fear and Trembling: the Abraham problem, the leap of faith, the Knight of Faith [UCLA L2-3; Stanford wk 1; UVic wk 3-4]
- The Sickness unto Death: despair as the self's misrelation [UCLA L3; Stanford wk 2; UNO]
- Concluding Unscientific Postscript: subjective truth, "that individual" [UCLA L4; Stanford wk 2; UVic wk 3]
- Anxiety (Begrebet Angest) as the dizziness of freedom [UCLA theme; UNO]

### C. Nietzsche — the death of God and revaluation
- The Gay Science: the death of God, the will to truth [UVic wk 5-6; Stanford wk 4]
- Thus Spoke Zarathustra: the overman as an ideal of self-overcoming [UVic wk 5]
- On the Genealogy of Morals: slave vs master morality, ressentiment [Stanford wk 3; UNO]
- Twilight of the Idols, The Will to Power (as posthumous compilation — use with the standard caveats) [UCLA L4; UCCS 9/21]

### D. Heidegger — Dasein, Angst, being-toward-death
- Being and Time Division One: Dasein, being-in-the-world, readiness-to-hand [UCLA L6-7; Stanford wk 5]
- "What is Metaphysics?": Angst as revelation, the Nothing [UVic wk 7]
- Being and Time Division Two: being-toward-death, temporality, authenticity vs das Man [UCLA L7-9]
- Tolstoy's "The Death of Ivan Ilyich" as the lived-application carrier for being-toward-death [Stanford wk 5]

### E. Sartre — freedom, bad faith, the Other
- Existentialism is a Humanism: radical freedom, responsibility, anguish, abandonment, despair [UCLA L10; Stanford wk 7; UVic wk 8]
- Being and Nothingness: bad faith, the look / the keyhole, the being-for-others [UCLA L11; Stanford wk 8; UVic wk 9]
- No Exit, The Flies, The Wall as literary carriers [Stanford wk 8; UVic wk 8]

### F. Camus — the absurd and revolt
- The Myth of Sisyphus: the absurd, suicide as the one philosophical problem, revolt [Stanford wk 6; UVic wk 13]
- The Plague / The Stranger as lived-absurd carriers [Stanford wk 10; UCCS 11/9]

### G. Beauvoir, Fanon and the extension voices
- The Ethics of Ambiguity: freedom as ambiguous, ethics without ground [Stanford wk 9; UVic wk 10]
- The Second Sex: the situated self, gender as construction [UCCS 10/26]
- Fanon, Black Skin, White Masks; Ellison, Invisible Man; Mills' "Non-Cartesian Sums" — existentialism applied to race and social ontology [UVic wk 12-13; UCLA "if time permits"]
- Kafka, Atwood, Buber as elective literary carriers [UCCS 10/19; UVic wk 4]

### H. Existential therapy (the applied half — anchored by the existing scope's USFQ + Hebrew University lines)
- Yalom's four givens: death, freedom, isolation, meaninglessness [Hebrew University 3975]
- Frankl's logotherapy: meaning as the primary drive, the search for meaning, unavoidable suffering [USFQ PSI 3114E]
- May's The Discovery of Being; Rollo May on anxiety and creativity [Hebrew University 3975]
- Existential therapy's evidence base and limits — the scope's explicit evidence-and-limits clause, still the declared open gap (see ledger; candidates recorded, statuses UNSET)

### I. The walk's capstone
- A personal synthesis of the walk against one serious objection (e.g. the charge that existentialism collapses into relativism or nihilism), per the walk's exit capability.
