## Topic map (union of the three syllabi, organized as a coherent walk)

The three syllabi agree on the classical skeleton: Presocratics → Sophists and Socrates → Plato → Aristotle → Hellenistic schools. Yale and Utah both extend into the Hellenistic (Epicurus, Stoics, Skeptics); Moravian keeps it as stretch. The union below is the Greek-walk-of-life curriculum.

### A. Origins and the Presocratics
- The Milesians: Thales, Anaximander, Anaximenes (naturalistic philosophy) [Moravian; Utah wk 1-2]
- Heraclitus and Parmenides (flux vs being); Zeno's paradoxes [Utah wk 1-2; Yale wk 4 — CONFIRMED for Parmenides and Zeno of Elea (wk 4 Wednesday) against the directly-read week table; Heraclitus NOT named in the Yale week table — his bracket is corroborated by Utah wk 1-2 only, UNRESOLVED at Yale week level]
- Empedocles, Anaxagoras, Democritus and Leucippus (atomism) [Yale wk 4-5 — CORRECTED against the directly-read week table: Democritus/Leucippus are wk 4 Monday and Empedocles is wk 4 Friday; Anaxagoras is a wk 1 Monday reading; nothing Presocratic appears in wk 5]
- Pythagoreanism (number, soul, immortality) [Yale wk 3]

### B. Sophists and Socrates
- The Sophists: Protagoras, Gorgias, relativism vs rhetoric [Yale wk 1; Moravian]
- The life and death of Socrates: Apology, Euthyphro, Crito [Yale wk 1; Moravian wk 1/25-2/1 — the "1/2" bracket in the v1 version was a transposed typo, corrected per the deep-audit receipt deg-arch-d1-greek-deep-audit-archpsych-1027a.md; Utah wk 2]
- Socratic method and Socratic psychology (Protagoras 351B-358E) [Utah wk 2]
- Socratic views of virtue (Laches) [Utah wk 3]
- Socratic ethics: virtue is knowledge; the unexamined life [Moravian objectives]

### C. Plato
- Early dialogues: Euthyphro, Apology, Crito, Meno [Yale wk 1-2; Moravian; Utah wk 3-5]
- Phaedo: soul, forms, recollection, immortality [Yale wk 3; Utah wk 4-5; Moravian]
- Republic: justice (Book I-III), psychology (Book IV), philosopher-kings (Book V), Sun/Line/Cave (Books VI-VII), critique of poetry (Book X) [Yale wk 2; Utah wk 5-7; Moravian wk 2/22, 2/29 and 3/14 — the week of 3/21 is Re-enactments/Review for Test Unit Two, corrected per the deep-audit receipt deg-arch-d1-greek-deep-audit-archpsych-1027a.md]
- Theaetetus: knowledge [Utah wk 3-4]
- Symposium and Phaedrus: love, beauty, rhetoric [Utah wk 9; Moravian stretch]
- Parmenides: problems for the Forms in late Plato [Yale wk 4; Utah wk 9]
- Timaeus: cosmology [Yale wk 5]

### D. Aristotle
- Aristotelian logic and the Organon (Categories, Posterior Analytics) [Yale — CORRECTED against the directly-read week table: Posterior Analytics I.1-4 is a wk 2 Monday reading and Categories 1-5 is a wk 5 Friday reading; Moravian wk 4/11]
- Physics: nature, causes, change [Yale wk 4-5; Utah wk 9-10]
- Metaphysics: being, substance, the four causes, God (Book XII) [Yale wk 5; Utah wk 10; Moravian wk 4/4]
- De Anima: soul, perception, intellect [Yale wk 3-5; Utah wk 10; Moravian wk 3/28]
- Nicomachean Ethics: happiness (eudaimonia), moral and intellectual virtue, the good life [Yale wk 3; Utah wk 11-12; Moravian wk 4/18]
- Politics and Poetics (as extensions) [research-queue item — not in the three opened syllabi; flagged for the D2 reviewer to source or drop]

### E. Hellenistic schools
- Epicureanism: pleasure, atomism, the Letter to Menoeceus, Principal Doctrines [Yale wk 4; Utah wk 13]
- Stoicism: physics, ethics, Epictetus's Handbook [Yale wk 4 (Seneca); Utah wk 14]
- Ancient Skepticism: Pyrrhonism, the modes [Utah wk 15-16]
