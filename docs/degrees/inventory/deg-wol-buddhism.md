## Topic map (union of the three syllabi, organized as a coherent walk)

The three syllabi triangulate the discipline cleanly: UF gives the historical-religious survey (Harvey spine), UCF gives the five-vehicle structure (Foundations / Theravada / Mahayana / Vajrayana / West), UVic gives the philosophical depth (Nagarjuna, Yogacara, Huayan, Zen, Kyoto School). The union below is the Buddhist-walk-of-life curriculum.

### A. Origins and orientation
- What is Buddhism / Buddhist philosophy; the "philosophy vs religion" question [UVic wk 1; UF wk 1]
- Ancient Indian context: Vedic renunciate culture, Upanishads, sramana movements [UCF wk 2; UF wk 2]
- Life of the Buddha; the three refuges [UF wk 2; UCF wk 2]
- The Buddhist Saṅgha and early canon formation [UF wk 3; UTexas syllabus surfaced but not opened]

### B. Core teachings (the Four Noble Truths + Eightfold Path)
- Dukkha, non-self, the Four Noble Truths [UVic wk 2; UF wk 5]
- The Eightfold Path; Magga-vibhanga Sutta [UVic wk 2]
- Karma, rebirth, cosmology, three marks of existence [UF wk 4]
- Conditioned arising / dependent origination [UF wk 4]
- The five precepts, three refuges, lay practice [UF wk 11]

### C. Abhidharma and early philosophical psychology
- Pali Abhidhamma; mental factors; consciousness and time [UVic wk 3]
- Asanga and Vasubandhu commentaries [UVic wk 3]

### D. Mahayana
- Mahayana origins, the bodhisattva ideal, perfections and stages [UF wk 7; UCF M3]
- Perfection of Wisdom: Vimalakirti, Heart, Diamond sutras [UVic wk 4]
- Nagarjuna and Madhyamaka; emptiness; two truths [UVic wk 5]
- Yogacara; Vasubandhu, three natures, consciousness-only [UVic wk 6]
- Huayan / Avatamsaka; totality metaphysics [UVic wk 8]
- Santideva, Bodhicaryavatara, bodhisattva ethics [UVic wk 9]

### E. Vajrayana / Tantra
- Tantric Buddhism; symbols, practices, four classes of Tantra [UF wk 8; UCF M4]
- Tibetan Buddhism as the living expression [UCF M4]

### F. East Asian traditions
- Chinese Buddhism: introduction, Sinification, canon, major schools [UF wk 9]
- Chan/Zen: Bodhidharma, Huineng, Platform Sutra, koans [UVic wk 10]
- Japanese Zen: Dogen (Shobogenzo), Hakuin, koan training [UVic wk 11]
- Kyoto School and modern Japanese Buddhist philosophy (Nishida, Nishitani) [UVic wk 12]

### G. Meditation and practice
- Calm (samatha) and insight (vipassana) meditation; hindrances; mindfulness [UF wk 12]
- Meditation in East Asian Buddhism [UF wk 12]
- Monasticism, ordination, discipline, monastic economy [UF wk 11]

### H. Modern Buddhism
- Buddhism in modern China; revival, Cultural Revolution, Taiwan [UF wk 13]
- Buddhist modernism; mindfulness and psychology [UCF M5 wk 15]
- Buddhism and the Western world; European philosophy; Buddhism in America [UF wk 14; UCF M5]
- Asian American Buddhism and racial justice [UCF M5 wk 14]
