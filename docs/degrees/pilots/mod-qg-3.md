# Pilot (practice): mod-qg-3 Standing: strength without movement

Phase 0 pilot applying the curriculum-design skill to one practice module
(the qigong practicum's Standing module; qigong is a practicum, not a
degree, per the owner directive 2026-10-25). Purpose: test the standard
on a training-plan style module.
STATUS: DRAFT, NOT CLEARED: the advisor's round-2 review found open items
(recorded at the bottom).

## Audit of the current module (facts from fcAcademyData)

- 3 outcomes, 1 required medium (QG-M03-01), 1 wiki reading (K114 standing
  meditation), activity and rubric present, 4 hours claimed, capstone false.
- One single medium for a module whose core outcome is a 15-minute
  capability built over weeks: the media layer is thin for a practice
  module, which the skill flags as more damaging in practice degrees.
- Prerequisites list only "mod-qg-2" with no observable competency named.
- The rubric grades "posture improvement" and "endurance gained" with no
  observable criteria and no self-assessment instrument.
- The module description contains its own progression rule (five minutes,
  add one minute a week toward fifteen), which is good design that the
  outcomes and rubric do not yet reinforce.

## Redesign to the skill

Outcomes (exit criteria, L2 for a practice module):

1. Stand in zhan zhuang for 15 minutes with the posture checkpoints in
   place (recorded across at least four weekly sessions in your practice
   log).
2. Name each posture checkpoint and correct a partner's or your own
   photographed posture against them.
3. Adjust your standing time and structure from your own log data (weekly
   minutes, how the sessions felt) with reasons.

Prerequisites rewritten as observable competencies:

- From mod-qg-2: can breathe with the practice's baseline rhythm for five
  minutes without counting breaths (verified by the prior module's log).

Instruction (each outcome taught):

- Outcome 1: the single verified medium (QG-M03-01) as the guided
  instruction. Per fcAcademyData it is already verified-video with a
  printed 5-minute duration (YoQi VOD, free trial access, so its access
  condition must be labelled), which fills the 5-minute rung only.
  Graduated guided standing sessions drawn from the verified practice
gallery would cover the later rungs, so the learner has a guided option
at shorter standing times (this is the practice degree's version of
fading guidance). The verification band has since selected one candidate
  per tier (2026-10-25, see verify/deg-cmp.md), and their actual durations
do not all match the tier labels: 5 minutes, YOQI "5 Minutes of Zhan Zhuang
  Self Practice" (5:00, Marisa Cranfill; the candidate page does not state
its access condition and the platform carries a free-trial condition, so
unrestricted free access is NOT established and the candidate stays
unassigned until access is confirmed or the owner rules otherwise); 8
  minutes, Susan Thompson "Find Peace: Easy Standing Meditation (Zhan
Zhuang)" (16:12 total, the 8-minute standing practice is
  uploader-stated and not confirmed against the recording); 12 minutes,
Lee Holden "Postures of Power" (10:29 total, two foundational standing
  postures). These are instructional CANDIDATES, not tier-matched guided
sessions: Holden at 10:29 cannot supply a 12-minute guided session, and
none of the candidates guides a 15-minute stand. The design must say so:
the ladder's upper rungs are completed as SELF-TIMED standing without
  guidance, using the checkpoint vocabulary from outcome 2 and the log
discipline from outcome 3, and the module page teaches the learner to
run their own countdown (kitchen timer or watch) for the self-timed
sessions. Workload arithmetic counts the video totals (16:12, 10:29),
not the practice length inside them, and the guided options are for the
lower rungs only.
- Outcome 2: K114 (wiki kernel) for the checkpoint vocabulary, read once at
  module start and again mid-module (spaced retrieval). The module page
  carries the checkpoints themselves, taken from K114's protocol (the
  assigned reading), so the learner can check a posture without leaving
  the module: (1) feet roughly shoulder-width or a little narrower, knees
  soft, never locked; (2) posture built from the ground up: tailbone
  tucked slightly toward the heels, hips relaxed and settling, shoulders
  low, crown of the head lifted as if hung by a thread; (3) arms raised
  to chest height embracing an imaginary ball, palms facing inward,
  elbows lower than the shoulders, wrists relaxed; (4) breathing low
  into the abdomen, calm and even, a head-to-toe scan dropping whatever
  tension is found; (5) begin at five minutes and build slowly.
- Worked example (outcome 3, on the module page): a four-row sample log
  is printed in full, for example: week 2, three sessions, rungs 5/6/6
  minutes, notes "shoulders rose in minute 4"; week 3, three sessions,
  6/7/7, notes "trembling at minute 5, stopped at 6"; week 4, three
  sessions, 7/7/8, notes "calmer, breathing settled"; then one printed
  adjustment with its reasoning: "minute 6 kept the tremble, so week 5
  holds 8 minutes for two sessions and drops to 7 for the third, because
  the log shows the tremble starts after minute 6 of continuous
  standing". The learner is told this sample IS the format: minutes,
  sessions, one observation, one adjustment with a reason.

Practice (the module's spine, with an explicit schedule):

- The weekly ladder: one rung per week, 5 minutes in week 1, adding one
  minute per week to 15 minutes in week 11, then three CONSOLIDATION
  weeks (12 to 14) held at 15 minutes. That is 14 weekly rungs, and at
  least one logged standing session per week is required, with three
  sessions per week recommended (the practice-degree norm). Weeks 11 to
  14 are the four assessed 15-minute sessions outcome 1 requires: the
  ladder REACHES 15 minutes in week 11, and outcome 1's "at least four
  logged sessions at 15 minutes" is met by one or more logged 15-minute
  stands in each of weeks 11, 12, 13 and 14.
- Recommended practice arithmetic, summed: three sessions per week at the
  current rung gives 3 x (5 + 6 + ... + 15) = 3 x 110 = 330 minutes for
  weeks 1 to 11, plus 3 x 3 x 15 = 135 minutes for weeks 12 to 14, so
  about 465 minutes (7.75 hours) of standing across the 14 weeks. This is
  an explicit schedule the learner can follow; the earlier draft's
  four-week evidence window is dropped in favour of the full ladder. The
  guided sessions ARE practice sessions: standing along a guided video
  counts as one of the week's sessions, so guided-video standing time is
  inside the 465 minutes and is counted once (never twice).
- Week 1 and week 14 posture photograph or sketch (kept from the current
  design, now feeding outcome 2's self-correction and the log; week 14
  replaces the earlier week 11 photograph so the comparison spans the
  consolidation period).
- Two minutes of recall at each session start: name the checkpoints before
  standing (retrieval practice).

Assessment (rubric rewritten to observable criteria):

- Practice log shows at least one logged session per rung-week with
  minutes recorded (pass or redo), INCLUDING a logged 15-minute stand in
  each of weeks 11, 12, 13 and 14 (the four assessed sessions outcome 1
  names; one per week at minimum, any of the week's sessions may carry
  the 15 minutes).
- The week 1 and week 14 posture photographs are annotated with each
  checkpoint and a correction (the learner marks what changed and why).
- The final log entry states one adjustment made from log data with a
  reason.
- Self-assessment step: the learner rates their own 15-minute stand against
  the checkpoints before module close (L2 into early L3 independence).

Workload (honest arithmetic, components summed, measured and estimated
kept distinct):

- Guided instruction (measured, INSIDE the practice allocation): QG-M03-01
  is 5 minutes (printed on its page, checked 2026-10-25) and is the
  week-1 guided session; the learner performs it as one of the week's
  practice sessions, so its minutes are inside the practice allocation
  below and are NOT added again. The tier candidates (YOQI 5:00,
  Thompson 16:12, Holden 10:29) are OPTIONAL guided options for the
  lower rungs, not required, and remain unassigned pending the access
  and recording checks recorded below. No required recording beyond
  QG-M03-01.
- Practice (PLANNED PRACTICE ALLOCATION, recommended schedule): 465
  minutes of standing across 14 weeks as computed above. This is an
  allocation the learner schedules, not measured learner time; the log
  measures what actually happens. Minimum schedule, one session per
  week, is 110 + 45 = 155 minutes and still completes the ladder and the
  four assessed 15-minute sessions (one per week in weeks 11 to 14).
- Recall (estimated): two minutes at each session start; at three
  sessions per week for 14 weeks, about 84 minutes.
- Log writing (estimated): about 15 minutes per week for 14 weeks,
  about 3.5 hours.
- Reading (measured text, estimated time): K114's assigned body is 320
  words (measured 2026-10-25); read twice at the skill's purpose-adjusted
  study rate of 130 wpm that is about 2.5 minutes per read, about 5
  minutes total.
- Assessment (estimated): two posture photographs or sketches about 10
  minutes each, plus the closing self-assessment about 15 minutes, about
  35 minutes.
- Recommended total: 465 + 84 + 210 + 5 + 35 = 799 minutes, about 13.3
  hours, printed as components. The current 4-hour field is left as the
  module's legacy claim; the redesigned total is computed and printed as
  separate components per the skill.

## What this pilot demonstrates

The skill catches practice-degree-specific defects: media thinness against
a graduated capability, prerequisites without observable competencies, and
a rubric that cannot be self-checked. It also preserves what the original
design got right: the weekly ladder and the posture photographs.
