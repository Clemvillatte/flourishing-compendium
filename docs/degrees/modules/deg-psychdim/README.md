This degree uses six psychological lenses to examine ordinary life:
affect, cognition, attention, self, motivation and overt behaviour.
Its purpose is to help a learner understand avoidable suffering,
make wiser choices and pursue a genuinely worthwhile life without
pretending that uncertainty has disappeared. It is not clinical
training or a comprehensive introductory psychology survey.

## How the degree works

The route starts with explanations and worked examples, adds guided
application to unused cases, then asks for independently justified
inquiry and an executed capstone. M6 returns to guided application
for a new theoretical framework rather than assume that independent
work in M5 makes every subsequent topic familiar.

Videos replace the introductory explanation they actually cover.
Short reading residuals retain definitions, methods, disputes and
uncertainty that the selected windows do not establish. English
transcripts provide an alternative to listening; no paywalled reading
or paid course is required.

Each module provides outcomes, a bounded source route, teaching,
practice with feedback, assessment criteria, delayed retrieval and
an explicit workload estimate. Private or redacted lived examples
are permitted, and neither disclosure nor mood/productivity improvement
is graded. An honest counterexample or justified stop can show
better understanding than an invented success.
