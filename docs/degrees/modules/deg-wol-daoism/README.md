# WoL Daoism: A Walk of Life

Five-module Walk of Life degree on Daoist practice, published to the
Academy on 2026-10-26. All five modules hold independent supervisor
module-QC passes; the whole-degree gate passed at the QC-recorded
hashes (reviews/deg-wol-daoism-whole-degree-gate-1026h.md), and the
integration data in site/assets/app.js uses the canonical module
files below.

The degree centres the Walk of Life value proposition: alleviate
suffering, become wiser, and genuinely flourish even when uncertain or
overwhelmed. It is a walk through Daoist practice, not a philology
course — the finite-scope clause applies throughout.
