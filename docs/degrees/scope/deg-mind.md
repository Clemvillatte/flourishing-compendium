# Scope: deg-mind — Mind & Adaptive Behaviour

Status: PROPOSAL by the strategist (2026-10-25), derived largely from the
existing module titles and the owner's mission; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula and frameworks before
the scope gate. An exclusion that merely points at a neighbouring degree
covers that ground only if the prerequisite or elective route is actually
specified there.

## Intended learner

A learner who wants the science of mind and behaviour: emotion, attention, self, motivation and their physiology, read critically.

## Entry competencies

- No neuroscience or psychology background assumed.
- Comfortable reading summaries of studies; statistics literacy built inside the modules.

## Exit capabilities

- Can explain the main mechanisms of affect, attention, identity and motivation from the literature.
- Can read a study critically: design, evidence type, effect size, what it cannot support.
- Can apply self-regulation findings to their own behaviour with documented practice.

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Affect and emotion science (current mod-mind-1).
- Cognition and attention.
- Self and identity.
- Motivation, behaviour and biophysiology.
- A synthesis capstone on adaptive behaviour.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Clinical intervention and its evidence (owned by deg-therapeutic).
- Wellbeing science and its measurement (owned by deg-wellbeing).
- Contemplative practice as practice (owned by deg-cmp); meditation research appears here only as evidence about mechanisms.

Elective branches:
- Computational models of cognition; cultural psychology; contemplative neuroscience.

## Neighbouring degrees and the wiki

The proposed boundary (strategist, pending owner): deg-mind owns mechanisms of mind and behaviour, deg-wellbeing owns flourishing outcomes and their cultivation, deg-therapeutic owns clinical approaches as objects of critical study. All three feed each other through prerequisites, not duplication.
