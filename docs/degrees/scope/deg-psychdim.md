# Scope: deg-psychdim — Psychological Dimensions (PBT EEMM)

WALK OF LIFE series (owner directive, 2026-10-25). Status: PROPOSAL by the
strategist, mapped onto existing module material; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula before the scope gate.

## Intended learner

A learner who wants a deep, evidence-based understanding of what we currently know about the mind across the six Process-Based Therapy EEMM dimensions, and who values recency because the field moves fast. (Wording corrected from "five" on 2026-10-25: the owner's degree list and the opened EEMM papers both give six dimensions; see the ledger.)

## Entry competencies

- No psychology background assumed; research literacy is built inside the modules.

## Exit capabilities

- Can explain state-of-the-art knowledge on each dimension: affective, cognitive, attentional, self, motivational and overt behavioural.
- Can read current research critically (design, evidence type, effect size, replication status) and knows which findings are stable and which are moving.
- Can apply the dimensions to real cases and behaviour, distinguishing mechanisms from outcomes.

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- The six owner-named dimensions of the EEMM: affective, cognitive, attentional, self, motivational, overt behavioural.
- State-of-the-art reviews and current debates per dimension.
- A capstone integrating the dimensions on one real behavioural pattern.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Clinical protocols as treatment (legacy deg-therapeutic material stays legacy).
- Wellbeing science as outcomes (owned by A Walk of Life: Positive Psychology).
- Contemplative doctrine (practica); contemplative science appears only as attentional evidence.

Elective branches:
- Interpersonal dimension material; computational psychiatry primers.

## Raw material available (measured 2026-10-25)

deg-mind's four teaching modules (affect and emotion, cognition and attention, self and identity, motivation and behaviour) plus its capstone; attached sources: 31 attachments, 25 unique ids (to re-verify or harvest).

## Benchmark against opened authoritative curricula (2026-10-25, degree owner)

Benchmarked against three anchors opened this session (URLs in
ledgers/deg-psychdim.md): Hofmann and Hayes 2024 and Maitland 2024 on the
EEMM and process-based therapy, Yale Open Courses PSYC 110, and OpenStax
Psychology 2e. Findings:

- Six dimensions confirmed. Both opened EEMM papers list the psychological
dimensions as affect, cognition, self, attention, motivation and overt
behaviour. The previous "five" wording in this doc was a counting error;
  corrected. The EEMM also names socio-cultural and biophysiological levels
  of analysis; this scope covers only the psychological dimensions, with
  interpersonal material as an elective branch. Whether the two non-
  psychological levels should appear as context material is a strategist or
  owner decision, flagged here rather than silently decided.
- Exclusions hold. The PBT papers are treatment-framework papers; this degree
  studies the processes themselves, so excluding clinical protocols as
  treatment is consistent. Wellbeing as outcome science stays with deg-wol-
  positive. Contemplative doctrine stays in the practica; contemplative
  science appears in this degree only as attentional evidence (the opened
  meditation review is used for its attention findings).
- Curriculum anchor fit. Yale PSYC 110 and OpenStax Psychology 2e cover
  emotion, motivation, self and cognition at introductory breadth; they
  anchor entry-level coverage while the dimension reviews (2018 to 2026)
  supply the state-of-the-art depth the owner asked for.
- Recency gaps carried to the ledger's gap register: the attentional
  dimension's reviews are 2019 and 2014, and the motivational (2020) and
  overt behavioural (2018) anchors predate 2021. Fresh open-access reviews
  and current talks are required during module production.
