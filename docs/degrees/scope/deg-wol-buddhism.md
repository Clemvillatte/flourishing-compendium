# Scope: deg-wol-buddhism — A Walk of Life: Buddhism

WALK OF LIFE series (owner directive, 2026-10-25). Status: PROPOSAL by the
strategist, mapped onto existing module material; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula before the scope gate.

## Intended learner

A learner walking the Buddhist path in its practical, living schools, focused on ethics and practice.

## Entry competencies

- No Buddhist background assumed; open to practice instruction.

## Exit capabilities

- Can describe the ethical core (sila, the precepts, the four immeasurables) and the practical path in the Secular, Zen, Insight/Vipassana, Theravada and Thai Forest schools.
- Can practise the foundational meditations with guidance (bridging to the cmp practicum).
- Can apply Buddhist ethics to concrete situations and state where schools disagree on practice.

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Ethics first: the precepts, harmlessness, compassion.
- The practical path per school: Secular, Zen, Insight/Vipassana, Theravada, Thai Forest.
- Core practice instructions and their living teachers' guidance.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Metaphysical and arcane material (owner rule: skip it; rebirth cosmology, abhidharma scholastics and tantric ritual are out of scope except where ethics or practice requires a passing mention).
- Academic Buddhist studies as a discipline.

Elective branches:
- Buddhist psychology of the mind (practical portions); monastic life accounts (Thai Forest lineage stories).

## Raw material available (measured 2026-10-25)

deg-contemplative module 3 (Buddhist principles) and the Buddhist portions of modules 4 and 5; the cmp practicum supplies the sitting practice; wiki Buddhist dossiers and kernels supply readings.
