# Scope: deg-cmp — Contemplative & Mental Practices — A Year of Practice

Status: PROPOSAL by the strategist (2026-10-25), derived largely from the
existing module titles and the owner's mission; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula and frameworks before
the scope gate. An exclusion that merely points at a neighbouring degree
covers that ground only if the prerequisite or elective route is actually
specified there.

## Intended learner

A person building a daily mental practice across the main contemplative families, from sitting to compassion to daily-life mindfulness.

## Entry competencies

- No meditation background assumed.
- Can sit still for short periods; stamina is built across the year.

## Exit capabilities

- Can practise a 30 to 45 minute sit unguided in at least two families (for example focused attention and loving-kindness).
- Can work with thoughts and difficulty inside practice.
- Can design a personal sustained practice with records (L4 capstone, current mod-cmp-12).

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- The current 12-module arc: the daily sit, body as anchor, awareness, regulation, acceptance, loving-kindness, self-compassion, compassion in action, daily-life mindfulness, working with thoughts, concentration and release, integration.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Doctrine and history (owned by deg-contemplative; this degree points there).
- Clinical applications (owned by deg-therapeutic).
- Qi-based movement (owned by deg-qigong; cross-linked in walking practice).

Elective branches:
- Extended retreat-style sessions; specific lineages as deep dives.

## Neighbouring degrees and the wiki

The practice gallery in academy/CMP1 is the verified session library; deg-contemplative supplies doctrine; the wiki's meditation dossiers supply readings.
