# Scope: deg-therapeutic — Therapeutic Approaches — Critical Study

Status: PROPOSAL by the strategist (2026-10-25), derived largely from the
existing module titles and the owner's mission; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula and frameworks before
the scope gate. An exclusion that merely points at a neighbouring degree
covers that ground only if the prerequisite or elective route is actually
specified there.

## Intended learner

A learner who wants to understand what therapy is and what the evidence says, for themselves or to help others wisely; explicitly not a therapist training.

## Entry competencies

- No clinical background assumed or required.
- Can read outcome research summaries with support built into the modules.

## Exit capabilities

- Can describe the main approaches (motivational interviewing, compassion-focused therapy, CBT and MBCT) with their mechanisms and evidence.
- Can separate trial evidence from theory and from advocacy.
- Can judge when a claim about therapy outruns its evidence, and state the evidence-based safety limits of each approach where the literature requires it.

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Motivational interviewing (current mod-ther-1).
- Compassion-focused therapy.
- CBT and MBCT.
- A capstone comparing approaches on a chosen problem, evidence-first.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Training to deliver therapy or practising on others (the degree studies approaches; it does not license practice).
- Psychopharmacology and psychiatry as a system (pointer only).
- Wellbeing science (owned by deg-wellbeing) and basic mechanisms (owned by deg-mind).

Elective branches:
- Third-wave approaches beyond MBCT; common factors research; ethics of care in clinical settings.

Draws on deg-mind for mechanisms and deg-wellbeing for outcome science; the wiki's therapy-adjacent dossiers supply readings. Safety language ruling is ITEM 3 on OWNER-DECISIONS.md.
