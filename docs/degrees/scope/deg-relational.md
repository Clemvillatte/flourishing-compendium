# Scope: deg-relational — Relational & Decolonial Thought

Status: PROPOSAL by the strategist (2026-10-25), derived largely from the
existing module titles and the owner's mission; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula and frameworks before
the scope gate. An exclusion that merely points at a neighbouring degree
covers that ground only if the prerequisite or elective route is actually
specified there.

## Intended learner

A learner interested in how selves are constituted in relationship and in non-Western and decolonial frames; no prior training assumed.

## Entry competencies

- Openness to traditions outside the Euro-American canon.
- Can read structured argument.

## Exit capabilities

- Can explain ubuntu, care ethics, Confucian role ethics, Levinasian ethics and Indigenous relationality in their own terms.
- Can analyse a concrete situation through a relational lens against an individualist one.
- Can write a synthesis on relational flourishing that faces the strongest objection.

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Ubuntu and African relational thought.
- Care ethics and Confucian role ethics.
- Levinas, Indigenous relationality and nepantla (current mod-rel-3).
- A relational synthesis capstone.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Full decolonial theory as a literature (only what bears on relational flourishing).
- Political economy of colonialism beyond the framing each module needs.
- Continental ethics owned by deg-philosophy (existentialism, phenomenology).

Elective branches:
- An ethics of community practice; dialogue and restorative circles as applied electives.

## Neighbouring degrees and the wiki

Overlaps care ethics with deg-philosophy (boundary: relational application stays here); the wiki's ubuntu and care dossiers supply readings; deg-wellbeing's sociocultural module cross-links.
