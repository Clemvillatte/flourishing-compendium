# Scope: deg-philosophy — Practical Philosophy & Ethical Life

Status: draft by the strategist (2026-10-25), pending verification by the
degree owner against opened sources and the scope gate.

## Intended learner

An adult self-directed learner with no formal philosophy training, comfortable reading a book chapter a week, who wants philosophy as a practice for living rather than as an academic credential.

## Entry competencies

- Reads English at an adult level; no logic or history of philosophy assumed.
- Can write a short structured essay or journal entry (no formal training assumed).

## Exit capabilities

- Can read a primary philosophical text with guidance and map its central claims.
- Can place the major practical traditions (Aristotelian, Stoic, Hellenistic, pragmatist, existentialist) in relation to each other.
- Can apply a framework to a live personal decision and state what the framework leaves out.
- Can write a defended synthesis of their own practical philosophy against one serious objection (L4 capstone).

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Virtue ethics from the ground up: virtue, vice, eudaimonia, phronesis (current mod-phil-1, to be deepened).
- Stoicism ancient and modern, including its psychology and its critics.
- Hellenistic alternatives: Epicureanism, Pyrrhonian skepticism, and their modern echoes.
- Pragmatism and process thought as practical philosophies.
- Existentialism and phenomenology as lived orientations.
- Practical judgement as the integrative capstone.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Formal logic and symbolic methods (instrumental, not practical-ethical; elective pointer only).
- Academic metaphysics and epistemology beyond what practical judgement needs.
- Non-Western traditions as traditions (owned by deg-contemplative and deg-relational); cross-links only.

Elective branches:
- Nietzsche and the art of living; Buddhist ethics as a comparative mirror; care ethics bridging into deg-relational.

## Neighbouring degrees and the wiki

Reads from the wiki's philosophy and Stoicism dossiers and kernels; deg-contemplative supplies the practice side of the examined life; deg-relational supplies the communal side. The wiki supplies depth, the degree supplies sequence and assessment.
