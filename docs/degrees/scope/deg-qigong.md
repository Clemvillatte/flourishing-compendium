# Scope: deg-qigong — Qi Gong — A Year of Practice

Status: PROPOSAL by the strategist (2026-10-25), derived largely from the
existing module titles and the owner's mission; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula and frameworks before
the scope gate. An exclusion that merely points at a neighbouring degree
covers that ground only if the prerequisite or elective route is actually
specified there.

## Intended learner

A person who wants a body-based contemplative practice they can sustain for a year and beyond, with graduated guidance.

## Entry competencies

- No fitness or meditation background assumed.
- Able to stand and move gently (adaptations are part of the curriculum).

## Exit capabilities

- Can perform a complete daily qigong set unguided.
- Can read their own practice (energy, posture, mood) and adjust it.
- Can design a personal sustained practice with records and self-assessment (L4 capstone, current mod-qg-12).

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- The current 12-module training arc: habit, breath, standing, routines, body awareness, emotional work, flow sequences, the tai chi bridge, walking, seasonal practice, depth forms, and integration (own practice).

Excludes (with reasons):
- Tai chi as a martial art beyond the bridge module.
- TCM theory as a clinical system (only what the practice needs).
- Clinical evidence claims beyond what is attached and verified.

Elective branches:
- Longer forms and live teaching (current mod-qg-11 extends here); walking practice in nature.

## Neighbouring degrees and the wiki

Reads from the wiki's qigong-adjacent kernels; deg-contemplative supplies the Daoist doctrinal background; the practice gallery records in academy/QG1 are the verified session library this degree schedules from.
