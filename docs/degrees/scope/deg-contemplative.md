# Scope: deg-contemplative — Contemplative Traditions

Status: draft by the strategist (2026-10-25), pending verification by the
degree owner against opened sources and the scope gate.

## Intended learner

A learner drawn to contemplative practice who wants the doctrinal and historical map, not only technique; comfortable with translated primary texts.

## Entry competencies

- Curiosity about meditation, prayer or contemplative practice; no training assumed.
- Can read translated classical texts with guidance.

## Exit capabilities

- Can describe the core doctrines and practices of the major contemplative traditions accurately.
- Can compare traditions on a given question (what is mind, what is practice for) without flattening them.
- Can choose and sustain a practice orientation with reasons (bridging into deg-cmp or deg-qigong for the practice year).

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Daoist foundations and advanced practice (current mod-cont-1 and 2).
- Buddhist principles across the main vehicles.
- Yoga-Vedanta, Kyoto School and Tibetan lines (current mod-cont-4).
- Mesoamerican and cross-tradition contemplation (current mod-cont-5).
- A comparative synthesis capstone.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Clinical or secularised mindfulness instruction (owned by deg-cmp as practice; deg-therapeutic as clinical study).
- Neuroscience of contemplation (pointer into deg-mind as an elective).
- Learning any given language or liturgy as a skill.

Elective branches:
- Christian contemplative (apophatic) line; Sufi practice; contemplative ethics bridging into deg-philosophy.

## Neighbouring degrees and the wiki

Feeds deg-cmp (the practice year) with doctrine; deg-philosophy shares Stoic and existential material; the wiki's contemplative dossiers supply readings.
