# Scope: deg-wol-positive — A Walk of Life: Positive Psychology

WALK OF LIFE series (owner directive, 2026-10-25). Status: PROPOSAL by the
strategist, mapped onto existing module material; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula before the scope gate.

## Intended learner

A learner walking the positive psychology path: the science of flourishing, strengths, flow and meaning, applied to their own life.

## Entry competencies

- No psychology background; willing to run personal experiments and keep records.

## Exit capabilities

- Can evaluate wellbeing claims against their evidence (measurement, design, effect size).
- Can apply the field's core practices (strengths, flow, gratitude, meaning) with recorded outcomes.
- Can state the field's criticisms and where it overreaches.

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Foundations of positive psychology and their critics.
- Flow and optimal experience.
- Strengths, meaning and sociocultural flourishing.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Clinical treatment of disorder (legacy material).
- Basic mechanisms of mind (owned by Psychological Dimensions).

Elective branches:
- Positive education; positive organisations.

## Raw material available (measured 2026-10-25)

deg-wellbeing's three teaching modules (foundations and critique, flow, sociocultural) plus their attachments; wiki flow, happiness and meaning dossiers.
