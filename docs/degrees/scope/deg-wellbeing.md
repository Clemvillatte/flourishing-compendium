# Scope: deg-wellbeing — Wellbeing, Creativity & Community

Status: PROPOSAL by the strategist (2026-10-25), derived largely from the
existing module titles and the owner's mission; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula and frameworks before
the scope gate. An exclusion that merely points at a neighbouring degree
covers that ground only if the prerequisite or elective route is actually
specified there.

## Intended learner

A learner who wants the science and craft of a flourishing life: what wellbeing is, how it is measured, and how it is built in persons and communities.

## Entry competencies

- No psychology background assumed.
- Willing to run small personal experiments and keep records.

## Exit capabilities

- Can evaluate wellbeing claims against their evidence (measurement, design, effect size).
- Can design and sustain a personal wellbeing practice with recorded outcomes.
- Can describe how communities and cultures support or undermine flourishing.

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Positive psychology foundations and their critics (current mod-wb-1).
- Flow and optimal experience.
- Sociocultural flourishing, creativity and community.
- A capstone integrating personal practice with community application.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Clinical treatment of disorder (owned by deg-therapeutic; wellbeing here is flourishing-oriented, not therapy).
- Mechanistic affect and cognition science (owned by deg-mind; this degree consumes it).
- Contemplative techniques as a practice path (owned by deg-cmp; used here as one evidence-based means among others).

Elective branches:
- Creativity and the arts in flourishing; nature and wellbeing; workplace design.

## Neighbouring degrees and the wiki

Shares the positive psychology literature with deg-therapeutic (boundary: wellbeing science vs clinical approaches) and deg-mind (boundary: outcomes vs mechanisms). Wiki dossiers on flow, happiness and meaning supply readings.
