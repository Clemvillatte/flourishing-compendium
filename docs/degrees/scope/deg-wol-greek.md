# Scope: deg-wol-greek — A Walk of Life: Greek Philosophy

WALK OF LIFE series (owner directive, 2026-10-25). Status: PROPOSAL by the
strategist, mapped onto existing module material; the degree owner must
benchmark it against opened authoritative curricula before the scope gate.

## Intended learner

A learner walking the Greek path to the examined life: Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, the Stoics and the Hellenistic schools as guides for living.

## Entry competencies

- No philosophy background; reads translated texts with guidance.

## Exit capabilities

- Can read the core Greek texts with guidance and map their practical claims.
- Can live the examination: apply Socratic questioning, Aristotelian virtue and Stoic practice to daily decisions.
- Can write a defended personal synthesis against one serious objection.

## Bounded domain

Covers:
- Socrates and the examined life; Plato's practical dialogues.
- Aristotle: virtue, eudaimonia, phronesis.
- Stoicism ancient and modern as a lived practice.
- Hellenistic alternatives: Epicureanism and Skepticism as practical rivals.

Excludes (with reasons):
- Formal logic; academic metaphysics and epistemology beyond practical needs.
- Non-Greek traditions (their own walks).

Elective branches:
- Cynics as a radical elective; Greek political thought where it bears on the lived polity.

## Raw material available (measured 2026-10-25)

deg-philosophy modules 1 to 3 (virtue, Stoicism, Hellenistic) plus their 3+4+3 attachments; wiki philosophy dossiers and kernels.
