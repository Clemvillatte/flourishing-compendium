# Creation

> Why does making things (objects, connections, children, a life) feel like it means something, and what kind of meaning is that?

A life spends itself making things: meals, sentences, friendships, children, institutions, a character. Frankl counts creating a work or doing a deed among the three ways a life finds meaning. Bergson goes further and reads joy itself as the sign that creation has happened. Arendt gives the maker a world to erect. The accounts do not agree on what the making is for, and that disagreement runs through everything below.

## Nearby terms

The family around creation overlaps easily, so I keep apart what can be kept apart:

- Creativity: the capacity to produce work that is both novel and appropriate, with its measurement debates and its psychology. The capacity and what making means for a life are different things; the capacity has its own dossier, and this one follows the meaning.
- Generativity: Erikson's name for the adult concern for guiding and providing for the next generation, set against stagnation. The nearest research neighbour to creating children and legacies.
- Procreation: little of the material below treats having children as making. The nearest things to it are generativity and legacy research, and I say so where the gap shows.
- Meaning-making: the process tradition on how people reinterpret events that violate their wants and beliefs. It works on the meaning of events rather than on making things, though Ricoeur's narrative work joins the two.

## The map: the entries on creation

### Making as a route to meaning

[Creative values](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Creative%20values): Frankl's first of the three routes to meaning, what a person gives to the world through a work or a deed, and the only route a person exercises while creating.

> "According to logotherapy, we can discover this meaning in life in three different ways: (1) by creating a work or doing a deed; (2) by experiencing something or encountering someone; and (3) by the attitude we take toward unavoidable suffering." ([Frankl, Man's Search for Meaning](https://antilogicalism.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/mans-search-for-meaning.pdf))

[E062 — Practise meaning through creative work](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=E062%20%E2%80%94%20Practise%20meaning%20through%20creative%20work): the principle built on that route: work as an ongoing contribution to others, with what calling research says about how people get drawn into it.

[The will to meaning](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20will%20to%20meaning): Frankl's primary motivational force, built against Freud's pleasure principle and Adler's striving for superiority: the striving to find meaning, not to restore equilibrium.

[Meaning-centred therapy](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Meaning-centred%20therapy): Breitbart's short-term treatment for people with advanced cancer, rooted in Frankl's writings, working with the sources of meaning rather than with symptoms.

[Everyday creative activities](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Everyday%20creative%20activities) and [Creative activities](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Creative%20activities): Conner, DeYoung and Silvia's 13-day diary study of 658 young adults. What a day's creative time did to the next day:

> "Lagged multilevel models revealed that people felt higher activated PA and flourishing following days when they reported more creative activity than usual. The other direction – PA predicting next-day creative activity – was not supported, suggesting that the cross-day effect was specific to creative activity predicting well-being." ([Conner, DeYoung and Silvia 2018, abstract](https://doi.org/10.1080/17439760.2016.1257049))

[BE059 — Engage in creative behaviour — making is well-being](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=BE059%20%E2%80%94%20Engage%20in%20creative%20behaviour%20%E2%80%94%20making%20is%20well-being): the principle carrying that diary finding into practice: making is well-being, on the evidence of the one study, whose own limits the principle states.

[Meaning-making](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Meaning-making) and [Narrative as meaning-making](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Narrative%20as%20meaning-making): the boundary markers. Park's process tradition revises the meaning of events; Ricoeur's narrative work composes a life. Neither makes anything in the ordinary sense, and the two senses of making meaning are easy to run together.

### Life itself as creation

[Creative advance](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Creative%20advance): Whitehead's name for the universe's ongoing production of novelty, the becoming by which "the many become one and are increased by one."

[P006 — Creativity is the ultimate principle](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=P006%20%E2%80%94%20Creativity%20is%20the%20ultimate%20principle): the principle carrying the Process and Reality category of the ultimate:

> "'Creativity' is the universal of universals characterizing ultimate matter of fact. It is that ultimate principle by which the many, which are the universe disjunctively, enter into the unity which is the universe conjunctively." ([Whitehead, Process and Reality, quoted in the Garland Encyclopedia of Whiteheadian Thought](https://encyclopedia.whiteheadresearch.org/entries/thematic/metaphysics/the-mystery-of-creativity/))

[Creative evolution](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Creative%20evolution): Bergson's 1907 account of life as genuinely new. The élan vital is a shared thrust, and mechanism and finalism both make real creation impossible: the first says everything is given in advance, the second that the future is pulled along a single line. The universe creates, no one is its ultimate author, and freedom lives in that gap.

[P102 — Trust the creative thrust of life](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=P102%20%E2%80%94%20Trust%20the%20creative%20thrust%20of%20life): the principle carrying the Bergsonian point that making modifies the maker. The passage, from Creative Evolution:

> "It is then right to say that what we do depends on what we are; but it is necessary to add also that we are, to a certain extent, what we do, and that we are creating ourselves continually." ([Bergson, Creative Evolution, ch. 1, Mitchell trans., Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/26163/26163-h/26163-h.htm))

[Creative selection](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Creative%20selection): the label this project gives to Bergson on self-remaking: "for a conscious being, to exist is to change, to change is to mature, to mature is to go on creating oneself endlessly."

[P191 — Joy is the felt signature of creation; distinct from pleasure](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=P191%20%E2%80%94%20Joy%20is%20the%20felt%20signature%20of%20creation%3B%20distinct%20from%20pleasure): the principle carrying the joy passage quoted in the section on what making is for.

[P195 — Creative emotion (émotion créatrice) is generative of thought, not a byproduct of it](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=P195%20%E2%80%94%20Creative%20emotion%20%28%C3%A9motion%20cr%C3%A9atrice%29%20is%20generative%20of%20thought%2C%20not%20a%20byproduct%20of%20it): Bergson's claim that an emotion can generate representations rather than only follow them, worked through the musician whose emotion gives rise to the symphony.

[Flower and song](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Flower%20and%20song): the Nahua difrasismo in xochitl in cuicatl, art and metaphor as the world's own activity, on a metaphysics in which the cosmos is the ongoing self-expression of teotl, nature and art the same activity.

[Creative destruction](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Creative%20destruction): Schumpeter's term for capitalism renewing itself by displacing the old from within. Included for the boundary it marks: renewal that destroys, and the creation here is the system's, not the person's.

### The maker and the made world

[Homo faber — man the maker](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Homo%20faber%20%E2%80%94%20man%20the%20maker): Arendt's toolmaker, who invents tools and implements to erect a world and cannot help but consider himself the creator of the human artifice, not primarily of life. His world exists beyond all organic life cycles, and man is also part of that world, even though he is an organic entity: a maker of things, living in the world of the made.

> "homo faber, the toolmaker, invented tools and implements in order to erect a world — not, strictly speaking, in order to help the life process. He is also the one who assists the process of nature, but he assists it in such a way that his assistance, being only an extension and acceleration of the natural process, never becomes its rival." ([Arendt, The Human Condition, excerpt](https://kortina.nyc/notes/the-human-condition/))

[The pontifex, bridge-maker](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20pontifex%2C%20bridge-maker): the Roman high priest's title, pons plus facere, the one who makes the bridge between the community and the gods. The word holds a whole sociology of mediation: connection as something built, and the person who builds it, with a social status to go with it, even though ancient scholars contested the etymology from the start.

[Value creation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Value%20creation): Nietzsche's account of valuing as creating: "Values did man only assign to things in order to maintain himself," and through valuation only is there value.

[E107 — Practise the creation of values](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=E107%20%E2%80%94%20Practise%20the%20creation%20of%20values): the principle carrying that claim: a value was once given and bestowed, and it was we who gave and bestowed it, with the demand to become what we are, making laws for ourselves and creating ourselves.

[A035 — Cultivate art and craft (techne)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=A035%20%E2%80%94%20Cultivate%20art%20and%20craft%20%28techne%29): Aristotle's techne, a rational quality concerned with making that reasons truly. The maker knows why the method works, so the method can be varied, repaired and taught.

[Craftsmanship research](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Craftsmanship%20research) and [PP022 — Pursue craftsmanship](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=PP022%20%E2%80%94%20Pursue%20craftsmanship): the line of work around Sennett's The Craftsman, with its charter definition:

> "Craftsmanship names an enduring, basic human impulse, the desire to do a job well for its own sake. Craftsmanship cuts a far wider swath than skilled manual labour; it serves the computer programmer, the doctor, and the artist; parenting improves when it is practices as a skilled craft, as does citizenship. In all these domains, craftsmanship focuses on objective standards, on the thing itself." ([Sennett 2008, quoted in Jones et al. 2021](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13549839.2021.1939289))

[The quiet craftsman’s life](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20quiet%20craftsman%27s%20life): the Zhuangzi ideal of skilled, quiet work away from display. Qing's bell-stands look like the work of spirits because his fasting loses thought of reward by the third day, praise by the fifth, and his own body by the seventh.

[The craft of attention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20craft%20of%20attention): Weil's attention as the trained suspension of one's own thought so the thing or person in front of one can appear, the maker's craft aimed at what is made, and at whom.

### Creating people and what outlasts you

[Generativity theory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Generativity%20theory): Erikson's generativity turned by McAdams and de St. Aubin into a measurable research programme, the seven-facet arrangement behind the tool used in ageing studies:

> "McAdams and de St. Aubin suggest approaching generativity as an arrangement of seven facets of this psychological strength; these are (1) cultural demand, (2) inner desire, (3) generative concern, (4) belief in the species, (5) generative commitments, (6) generative actions, and (7) narration of generativity narratives in the life story." ([Rubinstein et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4542587/))

[Legacy motivation research](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Legacy%20motivation%20research): Fox, Tost and Wade-Benzoni on the legacy motive as a link between life-meaning and prosocial motivation, developed from intergenerational decision making, generativity and terror management theory, and measured by what participants endorse across a list of ends beyond themselves.

[K224 — Legacy letter](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K224%20%E2%80%94%20Legacy%20letter): the practice kernel: write a letter to be read after your death, to one named person, covering what you learned, what you love and what you left unfinished. The value is in the writing.

[K225 — Service and creation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K225%20%E2%80%94%20Service%20and%20creation): the weekly kernel: one act of service or creation that outlasts the week. Plant, help, make, teach, and apply the test of whether a trace of it will still exist next month.

### Creating as identity

[Creative identity](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Creative%20identity): Beghetto's line of work on seeing oneself as someone who makes things, with what the 2006 study of two middle schools actually measured: creative self-efficacy, self-judgments of creative ability.

[Creative self-efficacy](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Creative%20self-efficacy): Tierney and Farmer's construct, "the belief one has the ability to produce creative outcomes," extended from Bandura's self-efficacy to creative work.

[The creative life](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20creative%20life): Nietzsche's creative life as a mode of existence rather than a career: one must still have chaos in oneself to give birth to a dancing star, and the spirit's three metamorphoses end in the child, a new beginning, a sacred Yes.

[The dignity of self-expression](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20dignity%20of%20self-expression): Kant's application to speech: saying what one needs and where one stands treats the speaker as an end, not a mere means. Whatever has value can be replaced; what is above value has dignity.

[P114 — Create as growth — creation is the evidence of growth](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=P114%20%E2%80%94%20Create%20as%20growth%20%E2%80%94%20creation%20is%20the%20evidence%20of%20growth): Maslow's creativeness of self-actualizing people, an open, spontaneous way of approaching ordinary activities, with his own caution that producing something does not by itself prove psychological health.

[P149 — Use instability creatively](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=P149%20%E2%80%94%20Use%20instability%20creatively): Prigogine's non-equilibrium as a source of order rather than decay, with the analogy drawn carefully: unstable periods of a life as the raw material for reorganization.

## What is the making for?

When a person makes something, where does the meaning land? The traditions above answer the same question differently, and each answer has been worked out in a source I opened.

The first answer: on the work and the ones who receive it. Frankl's creative values route puts the meaning in what the work gives, and the generativity researchers put the adult's concern in "establishing, guiding, and contributing to the welfare of future generations." The legacy researchers ask people to weigh ends that reach beyond their own lifetime. On this answer, a life that made nothing anyone received has missed the route.

The second answer: on the maker. Bergson's joy passage:

> "Pleasure is only a contrivance devised by nature to obtain for the creature the preservation of its life, it does not indicate the direction in which life is thrusting. But joy always announces that life has succeeded, gained ground, conquered." ([Bergson, "Life and Consciousness"](https://www.anthologialitt.com/post/henri-bergson-life-and-consciousness))

> "wherever there is joy, there is creation; the richer the creation, the deeper the joy. The mother beholding her child is joyous, because she is conscious of having created it, physically and morally." ([Bergson, "Life and Consciousness"](https://www.anthologialitt.com/post/henri-bergson-life-and-consciousness))

The diary study agrees in its register: creative activity predicted next-day activated affect and flourishing, and the reverse direction did not hold. And the making changes the maker: "we are, to a certain extent, what we do, and that we are creating ourselves continually."

The third answer: on the work itself. Sennett's craftsman wants "to do a job well for its own sake," with objective standards, on the thing itself. Zhuangzi's bell-stand maker loses thought of reward and praise before he touches the tree. Arendt's homo faber erects a world that outlasts organic life cycles, and considers himself its creator, not life's assistant.

What remains unresolved is whether the three ends compose or compete. A life that makes only for the maker risks building nothing anyone can receive; a life that makes only for the work or the world can spend the maker, and the generativity researchers' own measure of stagnation sits close beside it; a life that makes only for the ones who come after can keep nothing of the joy that, on Bergson's account, is the sign it worked. No source I opened settles the ranking, and the stories below carry the tension rather than resolving it.

## Practice: variation, selection, retention

::Variation:: What tends to need varying on this theme is the direction of the energy: consuming without contributing, waiting to feel like making before making, making only for display, or treating the finished thing as the only point, so that the maker is left out of the account and either collapses or inflates. The adaptive direction is making that is small, regular, connected to someone beyond the maker, and allowed to be imperfect. The accounts above disagree about what making is for, and they agree on this: making is a place a life can grow from.

::Selection:: The kernels I have selected as sources of that variation:

1. [K324 — Constraint-based creation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K324%20%E2%80%94%20Constraint-based%20creation): choose a creative task and impose a constraint, 100 words, one colour, three ingredients, one hour. In the rhyme experiments, constrained versions were rated more creative, and the Dr. Seuss wager shows a constraint doing generative work.

2. [E062 — Practise meaning through creative work](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=E062%20%E2%80%94%20Practise%20meaning%20through%20creative%20work): put one of Frankl's three routes into the week deliberately: a work, an encounter, or an attitude, with the calling research's account of how people get drawn into work they experience as an ongoing contribution.

3. [K225 — Service and creation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K225%20%E2%80%94%20Service%20and%20creation): one act of service or creation a week that outlasts the week, with the trace-next-month test; the research tradition behind it is generativity.

4. [K224 — Legacy letter](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K224%20%E2%80%94%20Legacy%20letter): write the letter to be read after your death; the value is in the writing, and the unfinished things belong in it.

::Retention:: Retention is what people keep because it works for them. The constraint someone keeps using, the weekly act that survives its month, the letter that gets reread: what stays is a matter of fit between the practice and the life it enters.

## Story: the stories that carry the theme

### Making as forgetting oneself

[ST026 — Woodcarver Qing Makes a Bell-Stand](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST026%20%E2%80%94%20Woodcarver%20Qing%20Makes%20a%20Bell-Stand): asked how his bell-stands look like the work of spirits, Qing describes a fasting that loses thought of reward by the third day, praise by the fifth, and his own body by the seventh; only then does he enter the forest, where the right tree already holds the finished stand.

### Making under constraint, for a village

[ST247 — William Kamkwamba Builds the Windmill](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST247%20%E2%80%94%20William%20Kamkwamba%20Builds%20the%20Windmill): forced out of school by the 2001 Malawi famine, a 14-year-old finds a library book on energy and builds a windmill from junkyard parts, a bicycle dynamo and a tractor fan, that first powers lights and later a water pump.

### A lifetime of making for the work

[ST309 — Jiro Makes Sushi](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST309%20%E2%80%94%20Jiro%20Makes%20Sushi): Jiro Ono, 85, makes sushi in a ten-seat Tokyo subway restaurant with three Michelin stars, doing the same thing over and over, improving bit by bit; the film's quiet ache is his son's fifty years of preparation to take over.

### Deciding for the not-yet-born

[ST256 — The Seventh Generation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST256%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Seventh%20Generation): the Haudenosaunee Great Law instructs the confederacy's lords to weigh every deliberation on the seventh generation to come, extending the web of affected people through time and not only through space.

### Making a teacher out of a connection

[ST456 — Salzberg, Goldstein, and Kornfield Make One Teacher](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST456%20%E2%80%94%20Salzberg%2C%20Goldstein%2C%20and%20Kornfield%20Make%20One%20Teacher): three students back from years of training in Asia meet at Naropa in 1974, and in 1976 found the Insight Meditation Society, building a home in the west for the practice they each carried home.
