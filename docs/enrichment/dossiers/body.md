# Body

> What does the body know, and how does it participate in suffering and flourishing?

The body is more than the shell the mind rides in. The scientific literatures give it three jobs: the signals the body sends upward are part of how emotion is made, the rhythms of breath and heart are part of how calm is made, and the wear of chronic stress is written into the organs. The phenomenologists give the claim its strongest form: the body is the medium through which a world is had. The map below holds both literatures side by side, and the divergences are left where they stand.

## Nearby terms

Embodiment: the philosophical wing of the theme; the map's first group holds the lived body, the body schema and situated embodiment.

Interoception: the scientific wing; perception of the body's internal states, in the map's second group.

Physiology: the stress systems and their measures, in the third group.

Breath: the theme's most practical entry, in the fourth group; the classical training traditions and the modern trials meet there.

## The map: the entries on body

### The body as lived

[The lived body](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20lived%20body): the body one is, not only the body one has; the SEP entry the card quotes puts Husserl's distinction plainly:

> "It is necessary to distinguish the body as it is subjectively lived through (Leib), and the body as an object among others (Körper)" ([SEP, Husserl](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/husserl/))

[Embodiment](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Embodiment): Merleau-Ponty's version; the body's mode of existence as being-toward-the-world, pre-objective and explicable neither as third-person causation nor as representation.

[Situated embodiment](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Situated%20embodiment): Beauvoir's move; the body as a situation the subject lives.

[Body schema](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Body%20schema): Head and Holmes's unconscious postural standard; an on-line, real-time plastic representation of one's own body in space.

[Body representation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Body%20representation): Gallagher's term for the internal structures that carry body information.

[Body memory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Body%20memory): Fuchs's totality of implicit dispositions of perception and behaviour sedimented in earlier experience; it shows in what we have become able to do.

[Embodied cognition](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Embodied%20cognition): Barsalou's grounded cognition; modal simulations, bodily states and situated action underlie cognition.

[Somatic marker hypothesis (Damasio)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Somatic%20marker%20hypothesis%20%28Damasio%29): decision-making influenced by marker signals arising in bioregulatory processes, some conscious, some not.

[T044 — Merleau-Ponty, Maurice](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=T044%20%E2%80%94%20Merleau-Ponty%2C%20Maurice): perception as the living action of an embodied being already caught up in the world.

[The habit body](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20habit%20body): habit as knowledge in the hands; the organist and the typist, from the same tradition.

### Interoception and the feeling of the body

[Interoception](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Interoception): the general term for the signals; the card carries the James-Lange lineage in brief.

[Interoceptive awareness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Interoceptive%20awareness): the perception of internal states and the meta-awareness of them as information rather than noise; the Frontiers definition the card quotes treats interoception as a process that regulates life at basic levels while modulating emotional experience at the most complex.

[Interoceptive inference](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Interoceptive%20inference): Seth's predictive account; feelings not read off raw signals but inferred from generative models of them.

[Interoceptive exposure (Barlow)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Interoceptive%20exposure%20%28Barlow%29): deliberately bringing on a racing heart or dizziness in a safe setting and staying, to learn the sensation does not lead to the expected disaster.

[Somatic psychotherapy](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Somatic%20psychotherapy): the umbrella for therapies that use body techniques as part of the treatment; the review the card quotes names neurophysiological regulation as the aim.

[Somatic experiencing (Levine)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Somatic%20experiencing%20%28Levine%29): pendulation, the figure-eight movement between activation and a settled resource state, held to complete interrupted defensive responses.

### The stress systems

[Autonomic nervous system (sympathetic vs parasympathetic)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Autonomic%20nervous%20system%20%28sympathetic%20vs%20parasympathetic%29): heart rate, blood pressure, respiration, digestion, involuntarily regulated.

[HPA axis theory stress](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=HPA%20axis%20theory%20stress): the hypothalamus-pituitary-adrenocortical cascade; the review the card quotes puts its function as stress adaptation and the redirection of energy to meet real or anticipated demand.

[Cortisol awakening response theory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Cortisol%20awakening%20response%20theory): the sharp rise after waking as an index of the system's health and load.

[Allostatic load](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Allostatic%20load): McEwen's wear and tear from chronic over- or under-activity of systems built for change; the price of accommodation to stress.

[Polyvagal theory (Porges)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Polyvagal%20theory%20%28Porges%29): distinct dorsal and ventral vagal branches proposed to map to emotional states; the card is thin on carriers and the theory is held as theory, not verdict.

[Heart rate variability theory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Heart%20rate%20variability%20theory): beat-to-beat variation as an index of self-regulation; Thayer and Lane's neurovisceral integration account.

[Heart-rate-variability biofeedback](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Heart-rate-variability%20biofeedback): training the variability itself.

### Breath

[Anapanasati (mindfulness of breathing)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Anapanasati%20%28mindfulness%20of%20breathing%29): the MN 118 instruction; sitting upright in a quiet place, attending to inhalation and exhalation, discerning long from short.

[ST068 — The Sixteen Steps of the Breath](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST068%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Sixteen%20Steps%20of%20the%20Breath): the full manual in four tetrads; the card calls it the most detailed in the early canon and the root text of every breath meditation taught today.

[The rhythm of breath](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20rhythm%20of%20breath): the Daoist image of whole-body breathing, from the Zhuangzi's true men who breathe from their heels.

[Slow-paced breathing](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Slow-paced%20breathing): the parasympathetic effect of slowed breathing, and the caveats the card carries on the popularised claims.

[Physiological sigh (Balban et al., Stanford)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Physiological%20sigh%20%28Balban%20et%20al.%2C%20Stanford%29): the double inhale and long exhale the body uses on its own; the 2023 trial of five minutes a day.

[Pranayama](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Pranayama): the Indian branch of the same family.

[Qi](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Qi): the Chinese word for the breath-like, moving stuff early thinkers took everything to be made of; the card notes Legge rendered it as breath.

[Yoga — union of body, breath, and mind](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Yoga%20%E2%80%94%20union%20of%20body%2C%20breath%2C%20and%20mind): the practical synthesis of posture, breath and attention; the card is precise that the posture-centred presentation is the young part.

[Yangsheng (cultivating vitality)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Yangsheng%20%28cultivating%20vitality%29): the Chinese art of nurturing life.

[B038 — Practice mindfulness of breathing (anapanasati)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=B038%20%E2%80%94%20Practice%20mindfulness%20of%20breathing%20%28anapanasati%29): the principle form of the MN 118 posture and instruction.

[B040 — Know the body (body contemplation)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=B040%20%E2%80%94%20Know%20the%20body%20%28body%20contemplation%29): the Satipatthana Sutta lists the body first among its frames.

[Y048 — Use the breath to steady attention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Y048%20%E2%80%94%20Use%20the%20breath%20to%20steady%20attention): Yoga Sutra 1.34, expulsion and retention of breath as one means of steadying the mind.

## Practice: variation, selection, retention

The practice side of this theme is unusually concrete. [Breath anchoring](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K100%20%E2%80%94%20Breath%20anchoring): rest attention on the sensation of breathing, note where attention went, return; the return is the practice. [Body scan](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K102%20%E2%80%94%20Body%20scan): move attention slowly through the body, noticing sensation where there is some and absence where there is none. [Interoceptive awareness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K111%20%E2%80%94%20Interoceptive%20awareness): a brief daily check-in on heartbeat, breathing, hunger, tension, temperature. [Slow breathing (5 to 6 breaths per minute)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K194%20%E2%80%94%20Slow%20breathing%20%285%20to%206%20breaths%20per%20minute%29): five to six breaths a minute for five minutes; the kernel states the frequency maximizes heart-rate variability and activates the parasympathetic nervous system, and that claim is the kernel's own. [Interoceptive exposure](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K118%20%E2%80%94%20Interoceptive%20exposure) for the panic side. [Body-first emotion work](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K172%20%E2%80%94%20Body-first%20emotion%20work) for the naming side. [Lived-body description](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K304%20%E2%80%94%20Lived-body%20description) and [Body-schema noticing](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K307%20%E2%80%94%20Body-schema%20noticing) for the phenomenological wing.

This is an editorial combination, not a validated recipe. The trial is: pick one body practice, run it daily for two weeks, and judge by what changes in the body's felt presence in ordinary situations. Retain what steadies; drop what does not.

## Story: the stories that carry the theme

[ST045 — The True Man Breathes from His Heels](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST045%20%E2%80%94%20The%20True%20Man%20Breathes%20from%20His%20Heels): Zhuangzi's true man of old breathes from his heels; everyone else from the throat.

[ST046 — The Yellow Emperor Climbs Kongtong](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST046%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Yellow%20Emperor%20Climbs%20Kongtong): the sage refuses the emperor's question, sends him to sit in stillness for three months, then teaches him to guard the inner and quiet the hearing and sight.

[ST044 — The Tortoise in the Mud](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST044%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Tortoise%20in%20the%20Mud): Zhuangzi declines a ministry; the sacred tortoise would rather be alive dragging its tail in the mud.

[ST263 — The Phantom Hand](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST263%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Phantom%20Hand): Merleau-Ponty's patient who still feels the lost hand; the body schema does not dissolve when a part is lost.

[ST264 — Bauby Blinks](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST264%20%E2%80%94%20Bauby%20Blinks): locked-in syndrome; a memoir dictated by blinking, the butterfly of consciousness not shrinking with the diving bell.

[ST265 — Christy Brown's A](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST265%20%E2%80%94%20Christy%20Brown%27s%20A): cerebral palsy, one controllable foot, a letter A scraped on the kitchen floor; a body-subject constituted around the limb that answered.

[ST048 — Yayoi Kusama Paints the Hallucinations](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST048%20%E2%80%94%20Yayoi%20Kusama%20Paints%20the%20Hallucinations): Kusama has seen fields of dots since childhood, has lived voluntarily in a Tokyo psychiatric hospital since 1977, and paints the hallucinations at architectural scale in the Infinity Mirror Rooms.

[ST047 — McCandless and the Bus](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST047%20%E2%80%94%20McCandless%20and%20the%20Bus): the Alaskan wilderness, four months, a journal's last legible line.

[ST049 — The Pipes of Heaven](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST049%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Pipes%20of%20Heaven): the Zhuangzi's opening image of breath and wind.
