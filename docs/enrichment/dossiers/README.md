# Dossiers index

A dossier is a research desk for one theme: what the compendium holds on it, where
its traditions genuinely disagree, and how the theme is practiced. Each dossier
serves the essays directly, with every quoted passage checked against a source
that was opened for it.

## Structure (binding for every dossier)

1. **Theme-Based Compilation** — 8 to 12 selected entries from the wiki, grouped by
   the questions they help answer. Each entry names what it contributes to the
   theme and carries its own evidence status. A checked quotation certifies only
   the quoted passage, never the whole linked page.
2. **Theme-Based Areas of divergence** — two disagreements developed properly,
   framed as research questions. Each must first show that the traditions are
   answering the same question, then quote each position from an opened carrier,
   then state what remains unresolved. Contrasts presented as disagreements by
   mere juxtaposition are not admitted.
3. **Theme-Based practice** — an editorial sequence of 3 to 5 existing practice
   kernels, each with a situation, a source-faithful action and its evidence
   limits. The ordering is an editorial arrangement, not a tested progression,
   and the page says so.

Reader-facing evidence phrases (never workflow terms): "Primary text; quoted
passage checked", "Scholarly interpretation; cited passage checked", "Study
abstract; quoted passage checked", "Further reading; not assessed".

## Proposed next dossiers

An editorial proposal, ordered by service to the mission (alleviating suffering,
detaching from the self-as-object, cultivating wisdom, genuine flourishing):

1. **Suffering and the second wound** — dukkha, the second arrow, rumination,
   pain versus suffering. Question: what exactly is added to pain by the mind?
2. **Attention in an overwhelming world** — attention residue, restoration,
   single-tasking, the economy of the gaze. Question: where does attention go,
   and who decides?
3. **Desire, craving and enough** — tanha, the insatiability of craving,
   desire-as-spanda, preference fasting. Question: which desires are worth
   keeping?
4. **Agency, effort and non-forcing** — wu wei versus discipline, goal setting
   versus letting happen. Question: when does effort help and when does it
   obstruct?
5. **Living with uncertainty** — fallibilism, tolerance, probabilistic
   thinking, control of the controllable. Question: how to act without false
   certainty?
6. **Anger, boundaries and repair** — the anger dissection, de-escalation,
   forgiveness research. Question: how to hold anger without being held by it?
7. **Compassion without self-abandonment** — self-compassion, care ethics,
   compassion fatigue. Question: how to stay open without being consumed?
8. **Love and nonattachment** — nonattachment versus bonded love, attachment
   styles, love as practice. Question: can love be held lightly?
9. **Impermanence, grief and loss** — anicca, grief research, memento mori.
   Question: how to love what does not last?
10. **Habit and returning after failure** — habit formation, the returning
    practice, lapse research. Question: how does change actually stick?
11. **Wisdom and practical judgement** — phronesis, wise reasoning research,
    the mean. Question: what does wisdom do in the moment of decision?
12. **Meaning, mortality and contribution** — meaning-in-life research, legacy,
    mortality salience. Question: what is worth building given finitude?

## Location and status

Dossiers stage in this folder, in the wiki's card-link format so every entry
is one click from the live site. The owner's stated destination is the
Academy, as a Dossiers view beside MORE VIDEOS and DISCOVERY. That view is
wired in this pass (site/assets/app.js, window.fcDossiersData + renderers)
and verified live in a browser on the Pages deploy: tab, grid, detail,
quotes with attributions, card links opening wiki cards, back
navigation. The markdown of record for each dossier stays in this folder,
and the view's data is a manually maintained transcription of it, updated
when the markdown changes.
