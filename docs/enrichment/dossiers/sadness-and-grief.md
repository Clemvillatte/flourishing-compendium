# Sadness & Grief

## Nearby terms

- Sadness: the state itself, felt toward anything lost or absent; the family's cleanest treatment of it as a working state rather than a defect is [Sorrow's fruit](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Sorrow%27s%20fruit), and the film case is [ST187 — Joy Reads the Memory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST187%20%E2%80%94%20Joy%20Reads%20the%20Memory).
- Sorrow: the deep register of sadness; Gibran's chapter treats it as constitutive, the well the laughter rises from.
- Mourning: grief's public and ritual form; the classical arguments about its limits sit on [S062 — Do not grieve excessively](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=S062%20%E2%80%94%20Do%20not%20grieve%20excessively).
- Bereavement: the objective situation of loss; grief is the process it starts.
- Lament: voiced, formalised mourning; the Aztec songs that hold grief and enjoyment in one breath are on [M038 — Grieve without pretending, and without drowning](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=M038%20%E2%80%94%20Grieve%20without%20pretending%2C%20and%20without%20drowning).
- Depression: the clinical condition this dossier must keep distinct from grief; the boundary work is [AF051 — Recognise the difference between sadness and depression](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=AF051%20%E2%80%94%20Recognise%20the%20difference%20between%20sadness%20and%20depression) and [Complicated grief research](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Complicated%20grief%20research).

## The map: the entries on sadness and grief

### The boundary the band must test

This dossier was minted as an editorial grouping, and its first task is to test the boundary between sadness as a state and grief as a process, from opened sources, and to flag any split proposal.

The clinical material separates grief from depression rather than sadness from grief, and it does so with two observations:

> "In grief, painful feelings come in waves, often intermixed with positive memories of the deceased. In major depression, mood and/or interest (pleasure) are decreased for most of two weeks." ([APA, What Is Depression](https://www.psychiatry.org/patients-families/depression/what-is-depression))

> "In grief, self-esteem is usually maintained. In major depression, feelings of worthlessness and self-loathing are common." ([APA, What Is Depression](https://www.psychiatry.org/patients-families/depression/what-is-depression))

The research tradition gives grief its structure as a process rather than a state: [Grief's oscillation model](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Grief%27s%20oscillation%20model) defines it as alternation between two kinds of work, and [AF052 — Learn to grieve — grief is a process, not a problem to solve](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=AF052%20%E2%80%94%20Learn%20to%20grieve%20%E2%80%94%20grief%20is%20a%20process%2C%20not%20a%20problem%20to%20solve) states this family's own reading of that structure.

Verdict: the boundary holds, and no split is proposed. Sadness as a momentary state is real but thin in this family's material; the family's sadness entries ([Sorrow's fruit](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Sorrow%27s%20fruit), [ST187 — Joy Reads the Memory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST187%20%E2%80%94%20Joy%20Reads%20the%20Memory)) treat sorrow as deep and constitutive, which is grief's register rather than a passing mood's. The state/process distinction is respected rather than violated by the sources, and the grouping keeps both sides legible. The register note is recorded in the boundary matrix.

### Grief as work

[Grief's oscillation model](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Grief%27s%20oscillation%20model): the model's own summary sentence:

> "This model identifies two types of stressors, loss- and restoration-oriented, and a dynamic, regulatory coping process of oscillation, whereby the grieving individual at times confronts, at other times avoids, the different tasks of grieving." ([Stroebe & Schut 1999](https://wendyvanmieghem.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/dual-process-model-by-M.-Stroebe-.pdf))

The practice built on it is [K319 — Non-avoidant grief work](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K319%20%E2%80%94%20Non-avoidant%20grief%20work): a deliberate, time-limited sitting with the loss, then a return to the restoration side, with respite treated as part of adaptive coping rather than as failure. [AF052 — Learn to grieve — grief is a process, not a problem to solve](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=AF052%20%E2%80%94%20Learn%20to%20grieve%20%E2%80%94%20grief%20is%20a%20process%2C%20not%20a%20problem%20to%20solve) carries the same point as a principle rather than a model. [M038 — Grieve without pretending, and without drowning](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=M038%20%E2%80%94%20Grieve%20without%20pretending%2C%20and%20without%20drowning) adds the paired observation from the Cantares Mexicanos: the songs hold grief and enjoyment side by side inside one stanza, which is oscillation in an older dress.

[Continuing bonds](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Continuing%20bonds) records the finding that softened the older clinical picture: the bereaved often keep an inner relationship with the dead, and the 2012 longitudinal study found the combination of unexpected loss and strong retained bonds the least well adapted of its groups. The material is summarised on the card; the conditionality is the finding, not a verdict against bonds as such.

### How long to grieve: the classical prescriptions

Zhuangzi, on his wife's death, gives grief both its due and its stop:

> "When she first died, was it possible for me to be singular and not affected by the event? ... The relation between these things is like the procession of the four seasons from spring to autumn, from winter to summer. ... I therefore restrained myself!" ([Zhuangzi 18, Legge, ctext](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/perfect-enjoyment))

Seneca's letter on the death of Flaccus draws the line at proportion rather than at absence:

> "I am grieved to hear that your friend Flaccus is dead, but I would not have you sorrow more than is fitting." ([Seneca, Letter 63](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Moral_letters_to_Lucilius/Letter_63))

> "Let not the eyes be dry when we have lost a friend, nor let them overflow. ... Let us see to it that the recollection of those whom we have lost becomes a pleasant memory to us." ([Seneca, Letter 63](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Moral_letters_to_Lucilius/Letter_63))

> "For I have had them as if I should one day lose them; I have lost them as if I have them still." ([Seneca, Letter 63](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Moral_letters_to_Lucilius/Letter_63))

[S070 — Do not let grief become your identity](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=S070%20%E2%80%94%20Do%20not%20let%20grief%20become%20your%20identity) carries the identity limit: grief may be kept and kept company with, but it is not to become the person. [S114 — Handle grief without excess](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=S114%20%E2%80%94%20Handle%20grief%20without%20excess) adds Epictetus' instruction for the companion: accommodate the griever, groan with him if need be, but do not groan inwardly. [D089 — Grief without destruction](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=D089%20%E2%80%94%20Grief%20without%20destruction) and [D090 — Accept death as transformation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=D090%20%E2%80%94%20Accept%20death%20as%20transformation) hold the Zhuangzian side of the same limit, and [S018 — Accept loss without devastation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=S018%20%E2%80%94%20Accept%20loss%20without%20devastation) holds the Stoic verb change: what was received is restored, not lost.

The clinical thresholds on the same question sit on [S062 — Do not grieve excessively](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=S062%20%E2%80%94%20Do%20not%20grieve%20excessively) and [Complicated grief research](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Complicated%20grief%20research): the card records prolonged grief disorder as grief that persists beyond roughly six months (ICD-11) or twelve months (DSM-5-TR, adults), beyond cultural norms, with impaired functioning. Seneca's proportion is a letter-writer's judgement; the clinic's is a duration and an impairment. The two do not compete; they answer different questions.

### Sorrow and its fruit

Gibran's chapter is the family's statement of what sorrow is made of:

> "Your joy is your sorrow unmasked. And the selfsame well from which your laughter rises was oftentimes filled with your tears. ... The deeper that sorrow carves into your being, the more joy you can contain." ([Gibran, The Prophet](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/58585/58585-h/58585-h.htm))

> "When you are sorrowful look again in your heart, and you shall see that in truth you are weeping for that which has been your delight." ([Gibran, The Prophet](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/58585/58585-h/58585-h.htm))

[ST187 — Joy Reads the Memory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST187%20%E2%80%94%20Joy%20Reads%20the%20Memory) is the same claim dramatised: Joy rewinds the sad core memory and finds that the comfort Riley received after the loss was made by Sadness. [B108 — Grieve with presence](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=B108%20%E2%80%94%20Grieve%20with%20presence) turns the state into an attention practice: notice the empty chair at breakfast, stay with the memory or tell someone, set no deadline for feeling better. The Buddhist note it carries is the other edge of the same cloth: the Dhammapada verse on its card ties grief to endearment itself, which is a claim about the root, not a deadline for the feeling.

### Grief beyond death

[Grief for what passes](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Grief%20for%20what%20passes): Zhuangzi extends mourning past the funeral to the youth and relationships that pass while the person lives; the grief has the same structure and the same stop. [Ecological grief](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Ecological%20grief) extends it to places: ecological grief is associated with the physical disappearance, degradation and death of species, ecosystems and landscapes, driven by climate change ([Cunsolo & Ellis 2018](https://www.uwosh.edu/sirt/wp-content/uploads/sites/86/2020/04/Cunsolo-and-Ellis-2018.pdf)).

The same article gives this extension its social reading:

> "grief and mourning have 'we-creating' capacities, exposing our known, unknown and unacknowledged connections to others" ([Cunsolo & Ellis 2018](https://www.uwosh.edu/sirt/wp-content/uploads/sites/86/2020/04/Cunsolo-and-Ellis-2018.pdf))

> "grief and mourning can also question fundamental assumptions about what we choose to value — and what we choose to grieve and mourn — including climate change-induced ecological loss and degradation." ([Cunsolo & Ellis 2018](https://www.uwosh.edu/sirt/wp-content/uploads/sites/86/2020/04/Cunsolo-and-Ellis-2018.pdf))

[R124 — Practise ecological grief work](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=R124%20%E2%80%94%20Practise%20ecological%20grief%20work) and [K348 — Name the loss](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K348%20%E2%80%94%20Name%20the%20loss) carry the practice: name the loss in a plain sentence, first person, specific, and let the naming be said or seen.

### When grief goes wrong

[Complicated grief research](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Complicated%20grief%20research) holds the research on the failure modes: grief that persists past the clinical thresholds, grief that forecloses the restoration side of life, and the rumination that [K319 — Non-avoidant grief work](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K319%20%E2%80%94%20Non-avoidant%20grief%20work)'s container is built to catch. The dual-process reading on [S062 — Do not grieve excessively](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=S062%20%E2%80%94%20Do%20not%20grieve%20excessively) treats dosage, not suppression, as the operative variable: the model argues for respite from grieving, which is a different instruction from either wallowing or cutting the loss off.

## Retention

A reader who keeps one thing from this family might keep the oscillation. The classical prescriptions and the research tradition agree on more than either side's summaries usually admit: Zhuangzi grieves first and stops where the tracing stops; Seneca allows the eyes to fill and not to overflow; the dual-process model alternates confrontation with restoration and calls the respite adaptive. The disagreement is about who sets the stop. Zhuangzi sets it at the pattern of transformation, Seneca at proportion and self-seeking, the clinic at duration and impairment. The reader's own grief is not obliged to choose between them.

What the practice entries in this family share is quieter: [K319 — Non-avoidant grief work](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K319%20%E2%80%94%20Non-avoidant%20grief%20work), [K348 — Name the loss](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K348%20%E2%80%94%20Name%20the%20loss) and [B108 — Grieve with presence](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=B108%20%E2%80%94%20Grieve%20with%20presence) all assume that grief is work with two sides, and that the work includes returning to the side of life that loss has reshaped.
