## The picture the survey started from

Registry state, recounted from registry.json: 15 published dossiers and 16 planned
(agency, behaviour, others, body, the eight Eightfold Path factors, gratitude, fear,
sadness-and-grief, shame-and-guilt), across six groups. Sixteen further candidates sit

## Method

Title sweeps across content/ for about thirty candidate terms; full-text sweeps for
stragglers; then opening and reading the cards behind the hits. For every retained
proposal I then tested coverage against the actual dossier drafts staged in this
folder, including the planned drafts (agency.md, the right-* dossiers, gratitude.md,
fear.md, sadness-and-grief.md, shame-and-guilt.md), in two passes: a keyword pass,
then an exact-target audit that decodes every #card= target in those files and
compares it with each candidate's supporting cards, because keyword absence alone
does not establish coverage absence and a shared card is not the same as a mapped
family. One limit of that audit, stated here: a card's absence among the decoded
links is evidence it is not placed, not proof that no passage discusses the
construct; where the distinction mattered I read the relevant passages as well.
Where the coverage test failed, I dropped or demoted the proposal rather than
padding the list. After the Others dossier draft was staged by its band late in
this session, I re-ran the same target check against it (122 decoded targets):
no overlap with any proposed family.

Evidence status, stated once for the whole document: the card descriptions below
summarize what the linked cards themselves say with their own opened carriers (each
card carries its source links; the writer of any minted dossier re-verifies every
carrier they rely on). Separately, I opened and verified six external carriers
directly this session, and those claims carry their links inline:
[Bratman et al. 2015, PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4507237/),
[Litman and Mussel 2013](https://drjlitman.net/papers/Litman-Mussel-2013.pdf),
[Hares et al. 2006](https://anxietyinstitute.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Hayes-et-al.-2006.pdf),
[Seligman et al. 2005](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/images/uploads/Seligman-PosPsychProgress.pdf),
the Zhuangzi ch. 22 passage at
[26reads](https://www.26reads.com/library/92138-zhuangzi/22), and the Sartre entry
at [the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sartre/).

### 2. Acceptance (slug: acceptance)

changes in a life when what cannot be changed stops being fought. The wiki holds both
the clinical programme (ACT, chronic pain) and the classical material (amor fati,

Coverage test (exact-target audit): [affect.md](affect.md) and
[uncertainty.md](uncertainty.md) contain no acceptance targets. The Body dossier is

### 5. Meaning & Mortality (slug: meaning-and-mortality)

wisdom and the flourishing pillars: the classical memento mori traditions, the
existential analyses, and a real research programme (terror management, death
reflection) all hold that facing death changes how a life is lived. The registry
already holds this family in its backlog; backlog presence makes it neither
published nor planned, and the card base is strong enough to develop.

Coverage test (full-registry audit: targets decoded from all 30 staged registry drafts):
the family's pieces are already scattered without an owner. The fear-of-death wing is

also maps Death anxiety research); Impermanence sits in uncertainty.md and

Mortality salience (the terror-management account), Finitude,
Death acceptance, the deathbed test kernel. So this is a promotion case, stated as
such: the material exists, is scattered across at least five dossiers, and no
dossier owns the question. The distinct question survives: Fear holds the affective
response to dying; this holds death as the shaper of meaning, gratitude and choice.
