# Right Mindfulness

> What does it take to know what is happening, while it is happening?

This dossier is a map, not an essay. The aim is that a reader can say "I want to think about right mindfulness", open this page, and see the spread of what the compendium holds on the seventh factor of the Eightfold Path: the entries that bear on trained awareness, grouped by the question they answer, each with a card link. The lines are short on purpose; the dense arguments live on the cards. This is a pass-1 draft: the core map is here, and an exhaustiveness sweep over the attention-family cards remains for the next pass.

How to read this page. Each line gives the entry's own opening claim, compressed from the entry itself. Evidence notes in this dossier describe the quoted passages only; a checked quotation certifies the passage, not the whole linked page.

## Nearby terms

- Mindfulness: the modern practice family, MBSR and its relatives; this page holds the path factor itself and its root text, and points to the family.
- Attention: the general science of how experience is selected; this page holds the training of it toward clear seeing.
- Right concentration: the neighbouring factor that steadies the mind this one keeps honest; each has its own page.
- Meta-awareness: knowing that one is aware; the compendium pairs the capacity with sati, and the map below holds both.

## The map: entries the wiki holds on right mindfulness

### What the factor is

[Right mindfulness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Right%20mindfulness): the seventh factor of the Eightfold Path; its root text, the Satipatthana Sutta, calls the four establishments the direct path.

[Eightfold Path](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Eightfold%20Path): the fourth Noble Truth, the Buddha's summary of the training; the factor's umbrella hub.

[The three trainings](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20three%20trainings): the path in its most compressed form, virtue, concentration and wisdom; every practice placed under one of the three.

[Sati](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Sati): the Pali word for mindfulness, keeping something in mind vividly, without commentary.

[Anapanasati (mindfulness of breathing)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Anapanasati%20%28mindfulness%20of%20breathing%29): the flagship practice, mindfulness established on the breath.

[The returning practice](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20returning%20practice): the compendium's name for the central move of meditation, noticing that the mind has wandered and bringing it back, until the returning rather than unbroken attention is understood as the practice.

[Vipassana (insight)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Vipassana%20%28insight%29): clear-seeing, treated by Thanissaro as a quality of mind rather than a technique, the ability to see events clearly.

The Satipatthana Sutta opens by naming what the practice is for:

> "This is the direct path for the purification of beings, for the overcoming of sorrow & lamentation, for the disappearance of pain & distress, for the attainment of the right method, & for the realization of Unbinding — in other words, the four frames of reference." ([MN 10, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.010.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

### The refrain that does the work

What makes the practice right is less the objects than the posture toward them; for each of the four frames the instruction returns to the same refrain:

> "There is the case where a monk remains focused on the body in & of itself — ardent, alert, & mindful — putting aside greed & distress with reference to the world." ([MN 10, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.010.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

The observation is not detached watching; it is effortful and free of grasping. The map holds the capacities the refrain trains:

[Bare attention in mindfulness practice](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Bare%20attention%20in%20mindfulness%20practice): the attending to what arises before commentary begins.

[Meta-awareness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Meta-awareness): knowing that one is aware, the capacity the compendium pairs with sati.

[Metacognitive awareness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Metacognitive%20awareness): the part of metacognition that can be reported and measured, what a person knows about their own thinking and how they manage it.

[Mindful attention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Mindful%20attention): attention deliberately trained on present experience, Kabat-Zinn's operational definition.

[Mindful presence](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Mindful%20presence): the compendium's label for the attended presence itself; Kabat-Zinn, 1990.

[Present-moment awareness theory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Present-moment%20awareness%20theory): the claim that contact with the current moment, rather than the remembered past or imagined future, underpins wellbeing.

[Detached mindfulness theory Wells](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Detached%20mindfulness%20theory%20Wells): the clinical cousin, watching thoughts without engaging them.

[Decentering](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Decentering): the capacity to observe thoughts and feelings as mental events rather than as the world or the self.

[Cognitive defusion](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Cognitive%20defusion): the ACT practice of seeing thoughts as thoughts, constructions passing through awareness, rather than as commands or facts.

[Acceptance](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Acceptance): the willing embrace of unwanted inner experience, without trying to change its frequency or form.

[Beginner's mind](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Beginner%27s%20mind): the Zen practice of meeting experience as if for the first time, many possibilities where the expert's mind holds few.

[Clarity of mind](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Clarity%20of%20mind): the compendium's label for the unclouded mental state the contemplative traditions describe, Stoic assent, Daoist purity-and-stillness, Buddhist clear seeing.

[The lived body](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20lived%20body): the body as experienced from within, the body one is rather than only the body one has; the first frame's home.

### The modern family

[Mindfulness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Mindfulness): the compendium's entry for the modern practice family.

[Mindfulness-based stress reduction](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Mindfulness-based%20stress%20reduction): Kabat-Zinn's clinical programme.

[Mindfulness-based cognitive therapy](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Mindfulness-based%20cognitive%20therapy): the programme developed for relapse prevention in depression.

[Dispositional mindfulness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Dispositional%20mindfulness): the trait version of the capacity, as it varies between people.

[Mindfulness's clarity mechanism](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Mindfulness%27s%20clarity%20mechanism): seeing mental events accurately rather than dimly, the Bishop et al. operational definition.

[Mindful self-compassion programme](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Mindful%20self-compassion%20programme): the practice family's kindness branch.

[Body scan in MBSR (Kabat-Zinn)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Body%20scan%20in%20MBSR%20%28Kabat-Zinn%29): the first formal practice taught in MBSR, attention moved systematically through the body.

[Walking meditation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Walking%20meditation): deliberate awareness brought to walking itself, the contact of feet with ground, the movement of the body, the flow of breath.

### The attention traditions

[The craft of attention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20craft%20of%20attention) and [The discipline of attention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20discipline%20of%20attention): Weil's account of attention as the trained capacity to suspend one's own thought so that what is in front of one can appear.

[The effort of attention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20effort%20of%20attention): James's phrase from the Principles of Psychology, attention doing the work where a thought prevails with effort.

[Effortless attention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Effortless%20attention): attention or action experienced as effortless, in Bruya's working definition.

[Focused-attention meditation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Focused-attention%20meditation): directing and sustaining attention on a chosen object, noticing when it wanders, returning it; Lutz et al.'s account.

[Attention management](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Attention%20management): the practice of governing where attention goes, choosing what receives it and protecting it from capture.

[Epoché](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Epoch%C3%A9): suspension, the deliberate withholding of an automatic mental act, in Husserl's tradition the trained posture of attention.

[Delight in the ordinary](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Delight%20in%20the%20ordinary): the capacity to find sufficiency and freshness in plain, repeated things, the food, clothes, work and people already at hand.

## Related questions and parallels

### How do these definitions differ?

The clinical definition that the modern practice family works from, Kabat-Zinn's, reads:

> "the awareness that emerges through paying attention, on purpose, in the present moment, and non-judgmentally to the unfolding of experience moment by moment." ([Wikipedia, "Mindfulness", quoting Jon Kabat-Zinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindfulness)): Reference article quoting the primary definition; quoted passage checked.

Thanissaro, reading the Pali sati, holds that the functions the clinical definition names are not automatically part of sati:

> "Directions such as “bring bare attention to the breath,” or “accept the breath,” or whatever else modern teachers tell us that mindfulness is supposed to do, are actually functions for other qualities in the mind. They're not automatically a part of sati, but you should bring them along wherever they're appropriate." ([Thanissaro Bhikkhu, "Mindfulness Defined"](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/thanissaro/mindfulnessdefined.html)): Scholarly essay by a monastic translator; quoted passages checked.

From the same essay:

> "meditation isn't just a passive process of being nonjudgmentally present with whatever's there and not changing it at all." ([Thanissaro Bhikkhu, "Mindfulness Defined"](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/thanissaro/mindfulnessdefined.html)): Scholarly essay by a monastic translator; quoted passages checked.

These excerpts emphasize different aspects of mindfulness: purposeful, nonjudgmental awareness in the clinical definition, and keeping the practice and its purpose in mind in Thanissaro's account. They do not, by themselves, establish a contradiction. The wiki holds both, the clinical family's cards on one side and the sati cards and this essay on the other, and records the question as open.

## Practice: variation, selection, retention

::Variation:: What tends to need varying on this theme is the default setting of the unattended life: experience passing unexamined, the body noticed only when it complains, attention captured by whatever arrives loudest. The adaptive direction is the factor's own shape, awareness established on some frame of the present, breath, body, feeling or thought, and returned to without drama when it wanders.

::Selection:: The kernels the compendium holds on this theme, each a selected source of that variation:

1. [K102 — Body scan](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K102%20%E2%80%94%20Body%20scan): attention moved deliberately through the body, frame by frame.

2. [K104 — Mindful walking](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K104%20%E2%80%94%20Mindful%20walking): the walk itself taken as the frame of reference.

3. [K415 — Mindful eating](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K415%20%E2%80%94%20Mindful%20eating): one meal attended; the look, smell and changing taste of the food and the body's hunger and fullness signals, noticed.

4. [K398 — Mindfulness bells](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K398%20%E2%80%94%20Mindfulness%20bells): two or three ordinary activities from the day turned into bells, pre-set reminders to stop and attend.

5. [K108 — Meta-awareness check](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K108%20%E2%80%94%20Meta-awareness%20check): a standing question through the day, what is the mind doing right now.

6. [K111 — Interoceptive awareness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K111%20%E2%80%94%20Interoceptive%20awareness): subtle body signals, heartbeat, breathing, hunger, tension, temperature, tuned into.

::Retention:: Retention is what people keep because it works for them. A reader who keeps the bell, or the evening scan, or the question at the red light has what the theme offered; what stays is a matter of fit between the practice and the life it enters.
