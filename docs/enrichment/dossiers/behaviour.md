# Behaviour

> How do repeated actions become a life, and how does a stuck pattern change?

A life is mostly not deliberated. Most of what a person does today was done yesterday, in the same place, triggered by the same cues, without a decision being made. The sciences that study this are old enough to have left several distinct answers lying side by side: consequences shaping actions, contexts triggering them, deliberate repetition automatizing them. The classical traditions add a sharper hope: that what is repeated hardens not only into routine but into character, and that the hardening can be aimed. The question is where the aim is best placed, and the honest answer runs through environment and design more often than through willpower.

## Nearby terms

Habit: the biggest share of this dossier's material; the umbrella card holds the family, and the map below separates formation, maintenance, discontinuity and the body that carries it.

Action and conduct: the same territory seen from the choosing side; the Agency dossier (not yet minted as a card) holds the say a person has over their doing, this page what the doing does to the person. [Autonomy](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Autonomy) is the need that pairs them.

Practice: the deliberate, designed repetition side of the same process, in the map's classical group.

Behaviour change: the applied science of moving stuck patterns; its frameworks and techniques hold their own groups below.

## The map: the entries on behaviour

### What the science says a habit is

[Habit](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Habit): the umbrella card; its gloss runs the associations and carries the family's map.

[Habit formation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Habit%20formation): the acquisition question; its principle links cite Lally's 18-to-254-day range for automaticity and Wood and Neal's context-cuing model.

[Habit maintenance](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Habit%20maintenance): the stage after rooting; its principle cites the systematic review's sobering opening that maintenance is rarely attained.

[Habituation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Habituation): the classical meaning, learning by repetition; Aristotle's Book II line that we become just by doing just acts, in the Ross translation.

[Second nature](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Second%20nature): the idea that a settled habit works like a natural disposition; the phrase's absence from the opened Ross passages is recorded on the card.

[Context-dependent behaviour (Wood & Neal)](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Context-dependent%20behaviour%20%28Wood%20%26%20Neal%29): the model that redefined habit as context-cued responding:

> "Habits emerge from the gradual learning of associations between responses and the features of performance contexts that have historically covaried with them... Once a habit is formed, perception of contexts triggers the associated response without a mediating goal." (Wood & Neal, "A New Look at Habits and the Habit-Goal Interface", Psychological Review, 2007, p. 843)

[Goal-directed behavior theory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Goal-directed%20behavior%20theory): the boundary marker; Dickinson and Balleine's two tests, action-outcome contingency and current motivational value, for what counts as goal-directed rather than habitual.

[Effortless action through long effort](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Effortless%20action%20through%20long%20effort): the Daoist resolution of the paradox; wu wei as the far end of training, Cook Ding's years of oxen.

[Ego depletion](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Ego%20depletion): the willpower-as-resource account and its contested history.

[Self-control training](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Self-control%20training): the card quotes Aldao cautioning against the trait-and-identity framing and the bathroom-scale claim.

[Plasticity of human nature](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Plasticity%20of%20human%20nature): Dewey's claim that habits are the material education works on; the card carries his line that the real existence of habits is different in old and young.

### Related questions and parallels

Is habit the enemy of freedom or its form? The traditions split. James's flywheel is society's conservative agent and the individual's; Dewey's habits are the material of education and the stuff that obstruction interrupts. The meditative traditions treat automaticity as the very thing to be seen through. The dossier holds all three; the disagreement is real and the card material does not resolve it.

What is a body in all this? Merleau-Ponty's answer, knowledge in the hands, runs against both the consequence account and the context account; none of the three cites the others. The habit body is the phenomenological corrective to the laboratory's habit, and the two literatures have not been reconciled here.

Why does maintenance fail so often? The systematic review's opening line, that maintenance is rarely attained, is the field's honest verdict; the techniques above are answers, not guarantees.

## Practice: variation, selection, retention

The practice material clusters into design and review. [Environment design](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K177%20%E2%80%94%20Environment%20design) makes the desired behaviour the easy choice. [Habit stacking](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K176%20%E2%80%94%20Habit%20stacking) anchors it to a cue that already fires. [Implementation intentions](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K153%20%E2%80%94%20Implementation%20intentions) pre-decides the moment. [Habituation by design](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K257%20%E2%80%94%20Habituation%20by%20design) runs a thirty-day plan of micro-actions for one virtue. The review side: [Weekly habit review](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K193%20%E2%80%94%20Weekly%20habit%20review) reads the week as a design review, and [Deimplementation](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K181%20%E2%80%94%20Deimplementation) asks what to stop. The unlearning side: [The Habit Releaser](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K393%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Habit%20Releaser) breaks a small pattern to see the autopilot.

What stays is what fits. A person who keeps one anchor, one review and one environment change has what the theme offered.
