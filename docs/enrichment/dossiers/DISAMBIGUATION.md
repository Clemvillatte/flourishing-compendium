# Dossier disambiguation policy

Written 2026-10-24 under the dossiers baton, following the owner's
question: the wiki could hold dossiers on Anxiety, Stress, Worry and
Angst, but the overlaps are heavy — what is best?

## The rule (binding for dossier writers)

A dossier is minted per **construct family**, not per synonym. One
dossier carries the family's umbrella construct; the near-synonyms are
handled inside it through a **Nearby terms** section, not by silent
absorption and not by alias redirects.

A nearby term earns its own dossier only when the inventory shows all
three of these:

1. A distinct central question the family umbrella does not already
   answer.
2. Substantial distinct material in the wiki (its own concept cards,
   kernels and principles, not just shared citations).
3. A useful distinct treatment — a map that reads differently, not the
   same map under another title.

Until a term passes that test it stays a nearby term. Editorial
convenience never becomes a conceptual claim: do not assert that
Stress *is* a form of Anxiety or that Worry *is* cognitive Anxiety
unless an opened source is quoted for it.

## The Nearby terms section (required in every new dossier)

Each dossier opens its map with a short **Nearby terms** block that:

- lists the family's near-synonyms and close neighbours;
- says, in one line each, what the wiki actually holds on the term
  (with card links) and where the reader should go if their question
  is really about the neighbour;
- separates what is settled in the sources from what is an editorial
  grouping, and uses plain reader-facing phrasing (no workflow
  language).

The published Anger dossier's handling of irritation, resentment and
contempt is the pattern to follow.

## The scope matrix (working document)

Each family gets a row here as its first dossier is built. The matrix
records: the term, the wiki cards found for it (title sweep first,
then full-text sweep), the source-backed distinctions found in opened
carriers, the proposed home, and links to neighbouring dossiers. A
split into a new dossier is proposed to the supervisor with the
matrix row as evidence.

## First task of the Anxiety writer

Before writing the map, build the matrix row for the anxiety family:
sweep the wiki for anxiety, stress, worry, angst, fear and dread
cards; open the primary or scholarly carriers that distinguish them
(e.g. the stress literature's demand-and-resource account of stress
versus the anxiety literature's threat-anticipation account); record
the distinctions with verbatim-checked quotes; then propose the home
for each term. The dossier's Nearby terms section is written from
that matrix. A future split (say, a standalone Stress dossier) is
proposed with the matrix row as evidence, not assumed now.

## Boundaries that are already set

These are EDITORIAL STARTING SCOPES, not settled scholarly
classifications: each writer tests them against their opened sources
and flags any correction for the supervisor.

- Happiness vs Genuine Flourishing: as a starting scope, hedonic
  material and subjective well-being sit in Happiness; eudaimonic
  material and the flourishing-versus-counterfeit question sit in
  Genuine Flourishing. Each dossier states the boundary in its
  intro, and each writer may refine it from what the sources
  actually support.
- Goals vs Motivation vs Agency: three dossiers by owner direction.
  Motivation holds the umbrella (why anything moves a person), Goals
  holds pursuit and disengagement, Agency holds autonomy and
  competence as felt power to act.
- Uncertainty and Overload & Overwhelm are cross-cutting (Life
  Challenges group), not forced into a single EEMM band; their
  EEMM-relevant material still links into Affect, Cognition and Body
  dossiers as nearby terms.
