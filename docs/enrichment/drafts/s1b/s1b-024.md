ACT teaching analogy · Bethay, Weinstein and Stubbs, VA strategies guide

A VA handout imagines a trip from Chicago to Las Vegas. In its simplified travel analogy, the city is a destination and west is a direction. Arrival completes the goal; the direction remains available for further travel ([Introduction to Values, pp. 50-51](https://www.mirecc.va.gov/visn16/docs/act-strategies-guide.pdf)).

> West doesn’t really have an end point.
>
> Bethay, Weinstein and Stubbs, Introduction to Values Handout for Veterans ([guide](https://www.mirecc.va.gov/visn16/docs/act-strategies-guide.pdf)).

The same handout uses fitness to show why this distinction matters. A goal can be reached while the value continues. Age or injury may require a change in the activities through which someone expresses it ([same handout](https://www.mirecc.va.gov/visn16/docs/act-strategies-guide.pdf)).

For an editorial application, take care for accuracy as the direction and checking one disputed quotation as today's goal. Completing the check does not finish accuracy. It gives that concern a particular action. If the text cannot be obtained, another suitable goal might be flagging the uncertainty or removing the unsupported quotation.

At the next revision, examine whether those actions served accuracy and what still needs attention. Retain a useful checking method and revise one that failed. Motivation supplies the concern, while overt behaviour supplies evidence of what was done.

The direction must remain open to the person's choice; the VA's Whole Health account explicitly describes values as freely chosen. The metaphor does not select them for the reader or settle the moral merits of every possible direction ([Values, p. 1](https://www.va.gov/WHOLEHEALTHLIBRARY/docs/Values.pdf)).

**Principles:**
- [[P158 — Choose values as directions, not destinations]]: a completed waypoint leaves the ongoing quality of action available.
- [[P162 — Take committed action — behavior is where values become real]]: a particular source check makes care for accuracy observable and revisable.
