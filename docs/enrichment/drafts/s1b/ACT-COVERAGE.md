# ACT metaphor coverage register

This is an integration register, not a claim that every item has a finished story. Names and locators below were checked against the opened [Big Book of ACT Metaphors full text, index, and Appendix J](https://mimhtraining.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/The-Big-Book-of-Act-Metaphors.pdf). The register reproduces bibliographic metadata, not clinical scripts.

The main-text list includes brief named comparisons as well as fully developed metaphors. Appendix J is explicitly a selected list of work published elsewhere; listing an item there does not establish that its full narrative is present in this book. Proposed principle connections are editorial routing suggestions, not assertions of full principle coverage.

## Publication and rights gates

- Existing ST IDs are duplicate checks, not claims that their present sources, prose or backlinks have been revalidated.
- Only a ready draft with its own evidence and full coverage matrix should enter story_register.py. Do not generate a story from an unresearched table row.
- Gillanders' author page expressly permits using and copying clinical handouts subject to a non-profit condition. Confirm that the intended website and essay use meets it; the present drafts do not claim a commercial-reuse license ([author's conditions](https://contextualscience.org/david_gillanders_training_page)).
- Appendix K records permissions granted for this book. It is not a blanket downstream license for the compendium ([book, Appendix K](https://mimhtraining.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/The-Big-Book-of-Act-Metaphors.pdf)).
- A complete set of substantive retellings could become a substitute for the collection. Use the register as a reading and routing aid; obtain appropriate permission before systematic reproduction or adaptation of the full work.

## Evidence quality correction

The initial prompted PDF extraction produced an inaccurate inventory and five incorrect page locators. It was discarded. The five locators were repaired against the unprompted full source and index before those drafts were marked ready. This register was built from the raw text and checked against actual index rows and Appendix J, not the earlier generated extraction.

Independent advisor review confirmed all 38 main-index rows and all 37 Appendix J metaphor rows. Removing nine title overlaps yields 66 normalized unique metaphor names; this count excludes the separate exercise table.
