- **Validation:** run the published-context story checker and link checks after registration; the draft-context check does not test assigned ST IDs or completed backlinks.

## Permissions and cumulative use

Confirm whether the intended website and essay use meets that condition; do not convert the offer into a blanket commercial license ([author's training-page conditions](https://contextualscience.org/david_gillanders_training_page)).

The present batch uses short accounts, short verified quotations and original commentary, rather than reproducing full clinical scripts.
