Documented case, reported in the sources · Sabato (Simon) Rodia, an Italian immigrant tile-setter, built the Watts Towers in Los Angeles between 1921 and 1954, working alone after his day jobs, with no commission and no crew ([PBS SoCal, Lost LA](https://www.pbssocal.org/shows/lost-la/how-the-watts-towers-escaped-demolition)).

The action ran at the size of a week. "One week I buy cement, next week I buy iron. Every week I use up all my money, buy things to keep working, keep working" ([Los Angeles Times, July 28, 1985](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-07-28-mn-5388-story.html)). He had no plan he could hand to anyone: "I had no help. I couldn't tell no one what to do because a million times I didn't know what to do myself" ([Los Angeles Times, July 28, 1985](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-07-28-mn-5388-story.html)). He called the work Nuestro Pueblo, our town, and when it was done with him he deeded it away and left for Martinez, never to return ([PBS SoCal, Lost LA](https://www.pbssocal.org/shows/lost-la/how-the-watts-towers-escaped-demolition)). Asked why, he answered: "I had in mind to do something big. And I did" ([Los Angeles Times, July 28, 1985](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-07-28-mn-5388-story.html)).

Read beside P162, the case shows the doing half of the principle, and the ACT framing is this page's editorial link, not Rodia's. His own account of the value is one sentence long; the rest is weekly behavior, money spent on materials as it came in, evenings given to the work. There was no audience, no buyer, no encouragement; the city would later move to condemn the towers, and engineering tests found them sound, and the commission decided for preservation in 1959 ([PBS SoCal, Lost LA](https://www.pbssocal.org/shows/lost-la/how-the-watts-towers-escaped-demolition)). What the story does not carry is the ACT structure around committed action, the homework and the goal ladder; it shows the action itself, sustained and unwitnessed, week after week for thirty-three years.

**Principles:**
- [[P162 — Take committed action — behavior is where values become real]]: Rodia's value lived in weekly purchases and evening labor, a goal-sized doing sustained with no witness and no reward; the case shows the behavior the principle names, while the protocol structure stays with the principle's own text.
