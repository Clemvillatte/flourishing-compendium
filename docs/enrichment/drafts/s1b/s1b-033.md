Documented case, reported in the sources · Sabato (Simon) Rodia, an Italian immigrant, one-time telephone lineman and stone mason, built the Watts Towers in Los Angeles between 1921 and 1954, working without cranes, scaffolds or assistants, in the spare moments and with the spare dimes of his construction job ([PBS SoCal, Lost LA](https://www.pbssocal.org/shows/lost-la/how-the-watts-towers-escaped-demolition); [Los Angeles Times, July 28, 1985](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-07-28-mn-5388-story.html)).

The action ran at the size of a week. "One week I buy cement, next week I buy iron," he told an interviewer, and in the same account: "Every week I use up all my money, buy things to keep working, keep working" ([Los Angeles Times, July 28, 1985](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-07-28-mn-5388-story.html)). He had no plan he could hand to anyone: "I had no help. I couldn't tell no one what to do because a million times I didn't know what to do myself" ([Los Angeles Times, July 28, 1985](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-07-28-mn-5388-story.html)). He called the work Nuestro Pueblo, our town, and when it was done with him he sold the property to his neighbor and left for Martinez, never to return ([PBS SoCal, Lost LA](https://www.pbssocal.org/shows/lost-la/how-the-watts-towers-escaped-demolition)). "I had in mind to do something big," he said of the 33 years he devoted to building the towers. "And I did" ([Los Angeles Times, July 28, 1985](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-07-28-mn-5388-story.html)).

Read beside P162, the case shows the doing half of the principle, and the ACT framing is this page's editorial link, not Rodia's. His own account of the value is one sentence long; the rest is weekly behavior, spare dimes spent on materials as the money came in, spare moments given to the work. The record the sources keep is of a man working without cranes, scaffolds or assistants; against the towers stood weather, vandals and a city government order that they be ripped down, until the hearings found them sound from an engineering standpoint and fit to be strengthened and preserved ([PBS SoCal, Lost LA](https://www.pbssocal.org/shows/lost-la/how-the-watts-towers-escaped-demolition)). What the story does not carry is the ACT structure around committed action, the homework and the goal ladder; it shows the action itself, sustained without help, week after week for thirty-three years.

**Principles:**
- [[P162 — Take committed action — behavior is where values become real]]: Rodia's value lived in weekly purchases and spare-moment labor, a goal-sized doing sustained without help, cranes, scaffolds or assistants; the case shows the behavior the principle names, while the protocol structure stays with the principle's own text.
