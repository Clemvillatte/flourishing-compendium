Daoist literary narrative · Zhuangzi, Book IV (Renjian shi), in James Legge's 1891 translation.

Yan Hui comes to Confucius asking leave to depart for Wei, where the ruler is young and headstrong: "I have heard that the ruler of Wei is in the vigour of his years, and consults none but himself as to his course." ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)) The ruler deals with his state lightly and the dead lie thick in the countryside; Yan Hui wants to bring the state to order ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)). Before the teacher will discuss whether he has the way to do it, the instruction turns to preparation, and the preparation is an emptying. The fasting of the mind is described: "But the spirit is free from all preoccupation and so waits for (the appearance of) things." ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)) The passage's line for it: "Where the (proper) course is, there is freedom from all pre-occupation; such freedom is the fasting of the mind." ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)) The instruction closes on the image:

> "Look at that aperture (left in the wall); the empty apartment is filled with light through it." ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

Hui's report of the method's effect is the student's side of the emptying: once he can employ it, "the Hui that I was has passed away" ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)).

For D045, the compendium reads the dialogue as the narrative behind the principle's abstract line. The page's carrier says the spirit waits for things free of preoccupation; the dialogue shows the emptying being taught to a man with a plan, before he walks into a situation that will not match it. The principle's plain words put it as carrying the skill and dropping the expectation; here the plan is not confiscated, it is fasted from, and what fills the emptied apartment is light.

**Principles:**
- [[D045 — Keep the empty cup]]: before Yan Hui goes to reform the ruler of Wei, he is taught the fasting of the mind, the spirit's freedom from preoccupation, and the empty apartment that is filled with light through the aperture; the emptying is what lets things appear rather than the memory of them.
