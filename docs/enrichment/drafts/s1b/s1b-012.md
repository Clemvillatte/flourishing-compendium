Daoist literary narrative · Zhuangzi, Book IV (Renjian shi), in James Legge's 1891 translation ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)).

Yan Hui comes to Confucius asking leave to depart for Wei, where the ruler is young and headstrong: "I have heard that the ruler of Wei is in the vigour of his years, and consults none but himself as to his course." ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)) The ruler deals with his state lightly and the dead lie thick in the countryside; Yan Hui wants to bring the state to order ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)). The instruction that follows describes a preparation that is an emptying. The fasting of the mind: "But the spirit is free from all pre-occupation and so waits for (the appearance of) things." And the line for it: "Where the (proper) course is, there is freedom from all pre-occupation; such freedom is the fasting of the mind." ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

Hui then reports the effect on himself: once he can employ the method, "the Hui that I was has passed away" ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)). The Master's reply continues with the image:

> "Look at that aperture (left in the wall); the empty apartment is filled with light through it." ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

For D045, the compendium reads the dialogue as an image of receptivity: the fasting instruction describes freedom from preoccupation, and the later empty-apartment image gives that openness a visible form ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)). The dialogue stops before Yan Hui departs for Wei; the emptying is taught, and the reform is not narrated. The image of light entering an empty space is the dialogue's own figure, read here without any physical claim about illumination.

**Principles:**
- [[D045 — Keep the empty cup]]: the fasting of the mind frees the spirit from preoccupation to wait for the appearance of things, and the later image of the empty apartment filled with light through the aperture gives that openness a visible form ([Zhuangzi 4.1, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)).
