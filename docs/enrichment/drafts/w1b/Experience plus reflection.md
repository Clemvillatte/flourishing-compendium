"Experience plus reflection" is John Dewey's formula for how experience becomes educative. His definition of reflective thinking: "Active, persistent, and careful consideration of any belief or supposed form of knowledge in the light of the grounds that support it, and the further conclusions to which it tends" ([How We Think, 1910](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/37423/37423-h/37423-h.htm)). Raw experience, as the data of reflection, is "isolated, fragmentary, and discrepant"; thinking organizes it into a whole in which the fragments find their proper place ([How We Think](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/37423/37423-h/37423-h.htm)).

### Trying and undergoing

Democracy and Education gives the dual structure: "experience is trying... on the passive, it is undergoing... The connection of these two phases of experience measures the fruitfulness or value of the experience" ([Democracy and Education, 1916](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/852/852-h/852-h.htm)). Thinking is what makes the connection: "the intentional endeavor to discover specific connections between something which we do and the consequences which result, so that the two become continuous" ([Democracy and Education](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/852/852-h/852-h.htm)).

### Education as emancipation

Dewey's definition of education follows directly: "that reconstruction or reorganization of experience which adds to the meaning of experience, and which increases ability to direct the course of subsequent experience" ([Democracy and Education](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/852/852-h/852-h.htm)). And the same book of How We Think closes the circle: "experience also includes the reflection that sets us free from the limiting influence of sense, appetite, and tradition... the business of education might be defined as just such an emancipation and enlargement of experience" ([How We Think](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/37423/37423-h/37423-h.htm)).

### In the compendium

The principles make the loop practical: P109 pairs every experience with reflection, P048 turns experience over, P049 closes the loop with single- and double-loop learning, P179 ends the day with savoring and review, S065 and S120 make self-examination daily. P042's learning by doing and A039's valuing experience over theory keep the experiential half honest. Reflection without experience is empty; experience without reflection is drift.
