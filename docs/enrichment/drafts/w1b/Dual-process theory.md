Dual-process theory holds that cognition runs on two kinds of processing. The canonical review's summary: "Our preferred theoretical approach is one in which rapid autonomous processes (Type 1) are assumed to yield default responses unless intervened on by distinctive higher order reasoning processes (Type 2). What defines the difference is that Type 2 processing supports hypothetical thinking and load heavily on working memory" ([Evans & Stanovich 2013, Perspectives on Psychological Science](http://keithstanovich.com/Site/Research_on_Reasoning_files/Evans_Stanovich_PoPS13.pdf)).

### What defines each

"The defining characteristic of Type 1 processes is their autonomy. They do not required 'controlled attention,' which is another way of saying that they make minimal demands on working memory resources" ([Evans & Stanovich 2013](http://keithstanovich.com/Site/Research_on_Reasoning_files/Evans_Stanovich_PoPS13.pdf)). Autonomous execution "tends to be rapid, they do not put a heavy load on central processing capacity, they tend to be associative," though "these correlated features are not defining" ([Evans & Stanovich 2013](http://keithstanovich.com/Site/Research_on_Reasoning_files/Evans_Stanovich_PoPS13.pdf)). Type 2 processing requires working memory and is marked by "cognitive decoupling; mental simulation": "In order to reason hypothetically, we must be able to prevent our representations of the real world from becoming confused with representations of imaginary situations" ([Evans & Stanovich 2013](http://keithstanovich.com/Site/Research_on_Reasoning_files/Evans_Stanovich_PoPS13.pdf)).

### The architecture and its cautions

The architecture is default-interventionist: "fast Type 1 processing generates intuitive default responses on which subsequent reflective Type 2 processing may or may not intervene" ([Evans & Stanovich 2013](http://keithstanovich.com/Site/Research_on_Reasoning_files/Evans_Stanovich_PoPS13.pdf)). One caution travels with the theory's fame: the authors "have recently discontinued and discouraged the use of the labels System 1 and 2," since System 1 is really a plural, "a set of systems in the brain" ([Evans & Stanovich 2013](http://keithstanovich.com/Site/Research_on_Reasoning_files/Evans_Stanovich_PoPS13.pdf)). Kahneman's Thinking, Fast and Slow made the system names famous; the careful form is Type 1 and Type 2 processing.

### The family of dual processes

The compendium's file lists the family. Grief: in the dual process model of coping, "healthy people oscillate between focusing on loss-related stressors... and restoration-related stressors... and at other times are simply engaged in everyday life experience" ([O'Connor 2019 review, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6844541/)). The file also pairs dual-process accounts of moral judgment (Greene) and of pleasure (Berridge & Kringelbach).

### In the compendium

CO002 names the two modes for practice: fast intuition and slow analysis. The compendium pairs the theory with Cognitive defusion (decoupling's clinical cousin), Metacognition (Type 2's self-awareness), and Cognitive reappraisal (Type 2's emotional application).
