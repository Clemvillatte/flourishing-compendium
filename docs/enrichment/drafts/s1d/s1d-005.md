France, 1800-1828 · biographical account from Jean Itard's Mémoire (1801) and Rapport (1806), with the UQAM/psychaanalyse French text, CYC-Net's English account and Le Monde diplomatique

In 1800 a boy was found in the mountains of the Aveyron, mute and wild, and brought to Paris for examination ([Popelard](https://www.monde-diplomatique.fr/mav/131/POPELARD/54353)). The psychiatrist Philippe Pinel pronounced him "an incurable idiot, a hopeless case" ([CYC-Net, after Shattuck 1980](https://cyc-net.org/cyc-online/cycol-0103-mcdermott.html)). A young physician named Itard saw the causes differently: "Loin de les imputer à une quelconque arriération biologique, Itard y voit le résultat de la complète solitude dans laquelle a vécu l'enfant depuis sa naissance", instead of biological retardation, the complete solitude the child had lived in since birth ([Popelard](https://www.monde-diplomatique.fr/mav/131/POPELARD/54353); translations of the French are mine). In Itard's own text the boy's habits bore "l'empreinte d'une vie errante et solitaire", the imprint of a wandering and solitary life ([Mémoire 1801](https://www.psychaanalyse.com/pdf/memoire_et_rapport_sur_victor_aveyron.pdf)), and the Institute for Deaf Mutes confided him to Itard's care ([Mémoire 1801](https://www.psychaanalyse.com/pdf/memoire_et_rapport_sur_victor_aveyron.pdf)).

Itard's program, in his own five-point statement, opened with the social: "L'attacher à la vie sociale", attach him to social life, and "Réveiller la sensibilité nerveuse par les stimulants les plus énergiques et quelquefois par les vives affections de l'âme", awaken nervous sensibility by the most energetic stimulants and sometimes by strong emotions ([Itard, Mémoire 1801](https://www.psychaanalyse.com/pdf/memoire_et_rapport_sur_victor_aveyron.pdf)). The education worked through named people. Mme Guérin was given the boy's care, and Victor's affection "is to be noticed above all in his relations with Madame Guerin" ([CYC-Net, after Itard 1962, p. 91](https://cyc-net.org/cyc-online/cycol-0103-mcdermott.html)). An exclamation of joy was borrowed from her: "C'est l'exclamation Oh Dieu ! qu'il a prise de Mme Guérin, et qu'il laisse fréquemment échapper dans ses grandes joies" ([Itard, Mémoire 1801, IV](https://www.psychaanalyse.com/pdf/memoire_et_rapport_sur_victor_aveyron.pdf)).

In his 1806 report, Itard recorded gains and limits. The affective faculties had begun to give rise to "quelques-uns de ces sentiments généreux", some of the generous sentiments, even as he found them subordinated to "un profond sentiment d'égoïsme", a deep sentiment of selfishness ([Rapport 1806, XLII and conclusion](https://www.psychaanalyse.com/pdf/memoire_et_rapport_sur_victor_aveyron.pdf)). The speech never came: "Mon espérance fut entièrement déçue", my hopes were completely dashed, "et j'abandonnai mon élève à un mutisme incurable", and I abandoned my pupil to an incurable mutism ([Rapport 1806, XLI](https://www.psychaanalyse.com/pdf/memoire_et_rapport_sur_victor_aveyron.pdf)). The education "doit être à jamais incomplète", he concluded, must remain forever incomplete, and the generous passions "l'heureux fruit de son éducation", the happy fruit of education ([Rapport 1806, conclusion](https://www.psychaanalyse.com/pdf/memoire_et_rapport_sur_victor_aveyron.pdf)). Victor stayed with Mme Guérin, who cared for him, until his death in 1828 ([CYC-Net](https://cyc-net.org/cyc-online/cycol-0103-mcdermott.html); [Popelard](https://www.monde-diplomatique.fr/mav/131/POPELARD/54353)).

The compendium's reading for P057: the expression Victor took from Mme Guérin and used in moments of joy shows another person's words becoming part of his own expression, and Itard's reports of the attachment illustrate learning within relationships; they do not establish the cause of his speech difficulties, and Mead's role-taking mechanism is not carried by the story. Itard's credo, the hypothesis that drove the education: "l'homme n'est que ce qu'on le fait être", man is only what others make of him ([Mémoire 1801](https://www.psychaanalyse.com/pdf/memoire_et_rapport_sur_victor_aveyron.pdf)).
