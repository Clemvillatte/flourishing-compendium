Right livelihood (samma ajiva) is the fifth factor of the Noble Eightfold Path: "right view, right resolve, right speech, right conduct, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right samadhi" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noble_Eightfold_Path)). The early canonical texts "state right livelihood as avoiding and abstaining from wrong livelihood"; for lay Buddhists "this precept requires that the livelihood avoid causing suffering to sentient beings by cheating them, or harming or killing them in any way".

### The five trades

The prohibition has a canonical list. The Vanijja Sutta: "A lay follower should not engage in five types of business... Business in weapons, business in human beings, business in meat, business in intoxicants, and business in poison" ([AN 5.177, trans. Thanissaro Bhikkhu](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/an/an05/an05.177.than.html)). Wikipedia's summary: "The Anguttara Nikaya III.208 asserts that the right livelihood does not trade in weapons, living beings, meat, alcoholic drink or poison", and section V.177 "asserts that this applies to lay Buddhists".

The placement is instructive: the path groups right livelihood with right speech and right action in the moral-virtue division (sila), not with wisdom. Work is an ethical practice field before it is a question of insight.

### In the compendium

The compendium's principles read livelihood as identity work. B152, "Align your work with your values (right livelihood, revisited)," and B025, "Practice right livelihood," carry the concept by name; MO021, "Learn to connect goals to identity — I am a person who...," and BE017's valued-activity scheduling are the modern mechanisms of the same alignment; MO061's meaning-as-motivation and BE061's helping behaviour mark the contribution side. The attention cluster, AT059's attention economy, AT062's infinite scroll and AT066's deliberate technology, extends the precept to the digital trades: what one's attention is sold to. AT046's digital sabbath is the boundary practice.

The file's media and technology facets invite the sharpest reading: a livelihood that captures attention is trading in a refined intoxicant. That extension is interpretation, not canon.
