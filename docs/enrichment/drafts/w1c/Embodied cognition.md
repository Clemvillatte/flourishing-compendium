Embodied cognition is the research programme that moves thinking out of the amodal symbol system and into the body. Its clearest manifesto is Barsalou's: "Grounded cognition rejects traditional views that cognition is computation on amodal symbols in a modular system, independent of the brain's modal systems for perception, action, and introspection. Instead, grounded cognition proposes that modal simulations, bodily states, and situated action underlie cognition" ([Barsalou, 2008](https://www.barsaloulab.org/Online_Articles/2008-Barsalou-ARP-grounded_cognition.pdf)).

### Three themes

The Stanford Encyclopedia organizes the field into three claims. Conceptualization: "The properties of an organism's body limit or constrain the concepts an organism can acquire". Replacement: computational concepts like symbol and representation "must be abandoned in favor of others" better suited to bodily-informed systems. Constitution: the body "does more than merely contribute causally to cognitive processes: it plays a constitutive role in cognition, literally as a part of a cognitive system" ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/embodied-cognition/)).

The Conceptualization theme has its classic defense in Lakoff and Johnson: basic concepts derive from the "direct physical experience" of moving a human body, and "the peculiar nature of our bodies shapes our very possibilities for conceptualization and categorization" (1999, 19). Gibson's ecological psychology supplies the perception side: visual processes "are not exclusive to the eye or even the brain, but involve the whole organism as it moves about its environment". Enactivism, in the encyclopedia's summary, is "the view that cognition emerges from or is constituted by sensorimotor activity".

### Posture reaching mood

The body-first claims reach emotion too. In a randomized trial, 74 participants were strapped with physiotherapy tape into slumped or upright seated postures and put through a stressful speech task. "Upright participants reported higher self-esteem, more arousal, better mood, and lower fear, compared to slumped participants", and the authors conclude the work "is consistent with embodied cognition theories that muscular and autonomic states influence emotional responding" ([Nair et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25222091/)).

### In the compendium

The compendium's embodiment principles state the programme directly. CO005, "Understand that thinking is embodied — the body shapes the mind," and P139, "Know that cognition is embodied sense-making," carry the constitution claim; D142, "Align the body," E017 and E020 turn it into practice; E013, "Practise the lived body (Leib)," is the phenomenological arm. The kernels are the applied wing: K102's body scan trains attention on bodily states, K172 runs emotion work through the body first, and K195's posture reset is the compendium's own posture trial. The pairing of practice and research is a comparison, not a documented influence.
