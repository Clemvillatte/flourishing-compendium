> **Principle 11** — Choose the soft over the hard

The Daodejing makes the case from four angles. Chapter 36 gives the axiom in its plainest form: the soft overcomes the hard, and the weak the strong (DDJ 36, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt)). Chapter 78 presents water as unsurpassed at attacking the hard and strong. Chapter 76 widens the case to life itself: living things begin supple and weak and end dry and stiff, and the strong tree is the one that grows big enough to invite the feller (DDJ 76, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt)).

> Thus it is that firmness and strength are the concomitants of death; softness and weakness, the concomitants of life. (DDJ 76, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt))

> There is nothing in the world more soft and weak than water, and yet for attacking things that are firm and strong there is nothing that can take precedence of it;--for there is nothing (so effectual) for which it can be changed. (DDJ 78, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt))

Chapter 43 adds the image of penetration: what has no (substantial) existence enters where there is no crevice, and the chapter pairs softness with the advantage of doing nothing with a purpose, the wider practice of [[Wu wei]]. Chapter 78 also observes that everyone knows this lesson and no one manages to act on it. The principle is that knowledge carried into the choice: where force and yield are both open, the yield is the default, following the Dao's [[The water metaphor|water model]].

(DDJ 36, 43, 76, 78.)

**Plain words** Picture two people carrying a sofa down a narrow staircase. One locks their arms and fights the geometry; the other twists and yields to the shape of the turn. That is the principle's logic in miniature: the locked posture meets the stair edge-on, the twisting one finds the angle the stair allows. When a forceful option and a yielding option both look workable, the principle says take the bending one.

*For example:* In a stalled argument, the soft move is to ask what the other person actually needs before restating your position, and to give ground where the ground costs you nothing.
