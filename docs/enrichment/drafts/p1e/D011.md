> **Principle 11** — Choose the soft over the hard

The Daodejing makes the case from four angles. Chapter 36 states the axiom outright: "The soft overcomes the hard; and the weak the strong." Chapter 78 sets the image: water, the softest and weakest thing there is, and yet, for attacking what is firm and strong, the chapter gives nothing precedence over it. Chapter 76 widens it to life itself: living things begin supple and weak and end dry and stiff, so "firmness and strength are the concomitants of death; softness and weakness, the concomitants of life" (DDJ 76, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt)). The strong tree is the one that grows big enough to invite the feller.

> There is nothing in the world more soft and weak than water, and yet for attacking things that are firm and strong there is nothing that can take precedence of it;--for there is nothing (so effectual) for which it can be changed. (DDJ 78, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt))

Chapter 43 adds the image of penetration: "that which has no (substantial) existence enters where there is no crevice." The same chapter pairs softness with the advantage of doing nothing with a purpose, which is the wider practice of [[Wu wei]]. Chapter 78 also names the catch: "Every one in the world knows that the soft overcomes the hard, and the weak the strong, but no one is able to carry it out in practice." The principle is that knowledge carried into the choice: where force and yield are both open, the yield is the default, following the Dao's [[The water metaphor|water model]].

(DDJ 36, 43, 76, 78.)

**Plain words** Picture two people carrying a sofa down a narrow staircase. The one who stiffens and fights the geometry gets pinched; the one who twists and yields to the shape of the turn keeps moving. Hard postures meet resistance head-on and take the damage; soft postures adapt. When a forceful option and a yielding option both look workable, the principle says take the bending one.

*For example:* In a stalled argument, the soft move is to ask what the other person actually needs before restating your position, and to give ground where the ground costs you nothing.
