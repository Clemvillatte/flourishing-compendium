Lifestyle pillars of mental health are the daily behaviours with trial evidence for antidepressant effect: exercise, omega-3 intake, sunlight exposure, sleep, social connection and anti-ruminative activity. The evidence base is Stephen Ilardi's Therapeutic Lifestyle Change (TLC) programme, the trial arm of the [[Behavioral activation|behavioral activation]] family's lifestyle components, developed at the University of Kansas from the insight that "human beings were never designed for the sedentary, indoor, socially isolated, sleep-deprived, fast-food-laden, frenzied pace of 21st-century American life" ([The Depression Cure, publisher page](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/stephen-s-ilardi-phd/the-depression-cure/9780306832925/)).

### The six elements

TLC prescribes the hunter-gatherer lifestyle's antidepressant elements: aerobic exercise, "elevate your pulse between 120 to 160 beats per minute for 35 minutes three times weekly"; seven to eight hours of sleep; sunlight, "the brain needs 2,500 lux for 30 to 60 minutes per day"; roughly 1,000 milligrams daily of omega-3 EPA; social support against isolation; and engaging activity against rumination, "learn to redirect attention to more engaging activities" ([Lawrence Journal-World, the six TLC essentials](https://www2.ljworld.com/news/2005/jun/01/depression/)). A Springer review lists the elements as "physical exercise, healthy sleep behaviour, supplementation with omega-3 fatty acids, sun-light exposure, engaging activity, and the fostering of social connections," each individually associated with antidepressive effects ([Psychological Reports framework paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12144-025-08842-4)).

### The evidence, honestly weighed

The outcome data are encouraging but small. An early report from the 12-week programme counted 26 adults with major depression through TLC, with "81 percent achieved a favorable response" against a 47 percent favorable response cited for citalopram ([Journal-World](https://www2.ljworld.com/news/2006/jan/26/depression_therapy_results_cheered/)); a later report gave 31 treated adults, "86 percent recovered fully or had a significant reduction in symptoms" ([VC Star](https://archive.vcstar.com/lifestyle/psychologist-examines-human-history-for-solutions-to-depression-ep-375810991-352913161.html/)). These are small, uncontrolled trials rather than the randomized comparisons the medication and psychotherapy literatures rest on; the programme's claim to effectiveness is best read with that limit attached.

### In the compendium

The principles carry each pillar as a virtue. BE040 and BI023 make exercise an antidepressant behaviour; BE049 makes social connection a basic need and health behaviour; Y058, K064 and SP062 supply the classical moderation that the table and the daily round require; P140 treats mind and life as continuous, the frame the whole programme assumes; K006 guards the mind as the root of everything downstream; and SO048 notes that the pillars are not equally available to everyone, discrimination being itself a health determinant.
