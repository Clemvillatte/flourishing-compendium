## Final page body (published)

Behavior analysis is the tradition founded on B. F. Skinner's experimental analysis of behavior. Its explanatory core is the three-term contingency: "a stimulus (the light) is the occasion upon which a response (stretching the neck) is followed by reinforcement (with food)"; Skinner adds, "We must specify all three terms" ([Skinner, Science and Human Behavior, ch. VII, p. 108](https://www.bfskinner.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/ScienceHumanBehavior.pdf)). Behavior is read as a function of environment and history rather than of inner states ([Graham, SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/behaviorism/)).

### The operant

The unit of analysis is not a single act but the operant, a class of responses defined by its consequences: "The word 'operant' will be used to describe this class. The term emphasizes the fact that the behavior operates upon the environment to generate consequences" ([Skinner 1953, ch. V, p. 65](https://www.bfskinner.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/ScienceHumanBehavior.pdf)). Reinforcement cannot change a response that has already occurred; what changes is "the future probability of responses in the same class" ([Skinner 1953, ch. V, p. 87](https://www.bfskinner.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/ScienceHumanBehavior.pdf)). The consequence works like selection in evolution: "Operants grow strong because they are followed by important consequences in the life of the individual" ([Skinner 1953, ch. V, p. 90](https://www.bfskinner.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/ScienceHumanBehavior.pdf)).

### Readings and criticisms

Psychological behaviorism aims to explain human and animal behavior in terms of external physical stimuli, responses, learning histories and reinforcements, and the encyclopedia entry names Skinner's work on schedules of reinforcement as its fullest expression ([Graham, SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/behaviorism/)). The entry records the standing objections. Chomsky's 1959 review of Verbal Behavior argued that children's rapid acquisition of language is underdetermined by reinforcement history ([Graham, SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/behaviorism/)). Other critics hold that behavior depends on how the individual represents or interprets the environment, and U. T. Place argued that qualia resist behavioral analysis ([Graham, SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/behaviorism/)). The tradition is no longer dominant; its living descendants include behavior therapy and animal learning research ([Graham, SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/behaviorism/)).

### In the compendium

The compendium reads behavior analysis mainly through its contextual reading of action. P073 "See conduct as interaction" (Dewey) treats action as impulse, habit and environment together, a structural cousin of the three-term analysis. P078 "Read action in its context: always" cites functional contextualism, the tradition's philosophical heir in ACT. P012 "An idea's meaning is its practical effects" (Peirce) states the pragmatist test the tradition applies to concepts: what difference would its truth make in practice. Y125 "Use the limbs as a diagnostic grid" reads the body's actions as evidence. A048 and SP076 ask that action fit its occasion, a comparison offered here rather than a claim of influence. P002 "Treat the world as events and doings, not things and substances" shares the process reading of the world the analysis presupposes.
