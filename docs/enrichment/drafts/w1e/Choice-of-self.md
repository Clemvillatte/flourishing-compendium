## Verified quotes (EIH, Kaufman translation)

- "Man is nothing else but that which he makes of himself. That is the first principle of existentialism."
- "We mean that man first of all exists, encounters himself, surges up in the world — and defines himself afterwards." (preceded by "What do we mean by saying that existence precedes essence?")
- "He will not be anything until later, and then he will be what he makes of himself."
- "When we say that man chooses himself, we do mean that every one of us must choose himself; but by that we also mean that in choosing for himself he chooses for all men."
- "man is condemned to be free. Condemned, because he did not create himself, yet is nevertheless at liberty, and from the moment that he is thrown into this world he is responsible for everything he does."
- "You are free, therefore choose that is to say, invent. No rule of general morality can show you what you ought to do: no signs are vouchsafed in this world." (end of the pupil case)
- "there is no reality except in action"
- The pupil case: young man choosing between the Free French Forces and staying with his mother; Sartre's reply is the "You are free, therefore choose" passage. Usable as the For-example.

## Verified quotes/claims (SEP Sartre)

- BN gloss: the for-itself is "a 'being which is what it is not and is not what it is' (Sartre 1943 [1956: 79])" (Barnes translation p. 79).
- From Sartre's Baudelaire: "the free choice which a man makes of himself is completely identified with what is called his destiny" (Sartre 1947a [1967: 192]).
- Fundamental project: existential psychoanalysis "describes human subjects as synthetic totalities in which every attitude, conduct, or behaviour finds its meaning in relation to the unity of a primary choice, which all of the subject's behaviour expresses in its own way."
- "the impact of past events is determined in relation to one's present choice"; determinist explanations "proceed from a kind of self-delusion that operates by concealing one's free project, and thus contributes to the obliteration of responsibility."
- Facticity/transcendence: "Any conduct can be seen from two perspectives – transcendence and facticity, being-for-itself and being-for-others." Bad faith = "the exclusive affirmation of one or the others of these".
- Café waiter: "I am a waiter in the mode of being what I am not."

## Concept identity (IS-IT-A-CONCEPT check)

- "Choice of self" is not a verbatim EIH phrase; it tracks BN Part IV ch. 2 (Existential
  Psychoanalysis: the fundamental project as one's free choice of self) — SEP section 5
  uses "fundamental free choice of themselves" and "Baudelaire's choice of himself".
  The compendium principle E039 cites BN Part IV ch. 2. Treat the page as the Sartrean
  thesis: the self is not given but chosen (existence precedes essence; the fundamental
  project). Distinct from [[Authenticity]] (what is chosen is not sincerity but the
  project) and from [[Bad faith]] (the flight from choosing).
