Verbatim DDJ 42 (Legge, Gutenberg pg216.txt, ch. 42 §1):

> The Tao produced One; One produced Two; Two produced Three; Three produced All
> things. All things leave behind them the Obscurity (out of which they have come),

> are harmonised by the Breath of Vacancy.

My validated draft body (superseded plain words/example still replicate per advisor;
rewrite before publishing):

Chapter 42 of the Daodejing gives Laozi's account of how anything exists: the Dao
produced the One, the One split into Two, and the interaction of the two produced
everything else. Legge's translation reads, "The Tao produced One; One produced Two;
Two produced Three; Three produced All things." [quote as standalone blockquote]
Refusing one pole breaks the whole, because the pair is the mechanism and not a menu.
Effort without rest, giving without receiving, speaking without listening: each is
half a machine running alone. (DDJ 42.)
