Traditional Daoist parable · from Zhuangzi, chapter 2, "The Adjustment of Controversies", in Legge's translation as printed on the Chinese Text Project.

A keeper of monkeys tells them: "In the morning I will give you three (measures) and in the evening four." This makes them all angry, and he says: "Very well. In the morning I will give you four and in the evening three." The monkeys were all pleased. The two proposals were substantially the same, "but the result of the one was to make the creatures angry, and of the other to make them pleased - an illustration of the point I am insisting on" ([Zhuangzi 2, Legge, ctext](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/adjustment-of-controversies)).

The connection with D124 is offered as a bounded reading, and the draft says where it stops. What the passage shows is a keeper accommodating another response to the same allocation: the total does not change, the arrangement does, and the monkeys' anger changes completely. The chapter's frame names what the monkeys' anger and pleasure both miss: "When we toil our spirits and intelligence, obstinately determined (to establish our own view), and do not know the agreement (which underlies it and the views of others), we have what is called 'In the morning three'" ([Zhuangzi 2, Legge, ctext](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/adjustment-of-controversies)). I read the keeper's re-arrangement as an illustration of the agreement that underlies views: the same substance, stated in the form the other side can receive. The passage does not show the keeper identifying what is true or right in an opposing argument, which is D124's central instruction, and the draft does not claim that it does; whether the illustration is close enough to the instruction is routed to the supervisor.

Not carried: D124's central instruction, stating the opposing position's strongest point in your own words before answering; the parable shows accommodation of a preference, not that operation, and the page's exercise keeps to the principle page.

**Principles:**
- [[D124 — Look for the right in the other side]]: offered as a bounded illustration of the agreement underlying views, with the passage's accommodation of a preference named and the central instruction marked as not carried; target fit routed to the supervisor.
