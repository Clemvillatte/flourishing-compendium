Literary/biographical narrative, cross-cultural · United States, 1854 · [Henry David Thoreau, Walden](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/205/205-h/205-h.htm)

Thoreau built his own house at Walden Pond, ten feet by fifteen, and paid for it himself: "The exact cost of my house, paying the usual price for such materials as I used, but not counting the work, all of which was done by myself, was as follows" ([Walden, Gutenberg #205](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/205/205-h/205-h.htm)) — the itemised table runs from boards at $8.03½ down to chalk at one cent, "In all, $28.12½" ([Walden, Gutenberg #205](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/205/205-h/205-h.htm)). Next to the house stands the bean field: seven miles of rows, hoed early and late.

In the passage where he chooses a house site, he lets the matter drop with the sentence: "I let it lie, fallow perchance, for a man is rich in proportion to the number of things which he can afford to let alone" ([Walden, Gutenberg #205](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/205/205-h/205-h.htm)).

And the bean field itself: "I came to love my rows, my beans, though so many more than I wanted. They attached me to the earth, and so I got strength like Antæus. But why should I raise them? Only Heaven knows. This was my curious labor all summer,—to make this portion of the earth’s surface, which had yielded only cinquefoil, blackberries, johnswort, and the like, before, sweet wild fruits and pleasant flowers, produce instead this pulse. What shall I learn of beans or beans of me? I cherish them, I hoe them, early and late I have an eye to them; and this is my day’s work" ([Walden, Gutenberg #205](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/205/205-h/205-h.htm)).

My reading: Thoreau is not against comfort; he is against the confusion of means with ends, the way a lifetime can be spent maintaining a life no one has time to live. And the bean-field passage complicates the tidy reading: he does not know why he raises them ("Only Heaven knows") and he questions his own right to oust the wild flowers for his pulse. The economy is deliberate; the crop is almost incidental. The wealth is in what he can let alone, and the day's work is something he has an eye to, early and late.
