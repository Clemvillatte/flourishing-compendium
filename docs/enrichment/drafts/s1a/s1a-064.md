Biographical account · Alfred Nobel (1833-1896), inventor and businessman, via NobelPrize.org's biography and will pages and a 2017 retrospective in the Rambam Maimonides Medical Journal.

Nobel built one of the era's great fortunes on controlled explosion and then, in a will signed in Paris on 27 November 1895, directed that the fortune's interest be distributed as prizes "to those who, during the preceding year, have conferred the greatest benefit to humankind" ([NobelPrize.org will](https://www.nobelprize.org/alfred-nobel/alfred-nobels-will/)). The fortune's source is documented: he held 355 patents, invented dynamite, and within ten years sixteen explosives factories had been founded in fourteen countries; he also bought the Bofors-Gullspång machine works at Karlskoga ([NobelPrize.org biography](https://www.nobelprize.org/alfred-nobel/)). The coverage of his career highlighted "the manufacturing of explosive chemicals and devices and the development of armaments", and he "was portrayed as an arms merchant" ([Lichtman 2017](https://www.rmmj.org.il/issues/34/748/manuscript)).

The will's terms are on record. It was his third and last, signed at the Swedish-Norwegian Club, and it expressly disregarded nationality in the awards ([NobelPrize.org will](https://www.nobelprize.org/alfred-nobel/alfred-nobels-will/)). His family opposed the establishment of the prize, the awarders he named at first refused, and five years passed before the first award in 1901 ([NobelPrize.org will](https://www.nobelprize.org/alfred-nobel/alfred-nobels-will/)).

I read the two facts against each other, as a pairing of origin and instruction: the money came out of explosives, and its final stated use was benefit to humankind. That is my reading; what the opened sources establish is the fortune's line of work, the world's arms-merchant account of it, and the instruction in the will. The sources opened here do not establish why Nobel made the bequest, and they do not show him changing his trade.

The fit with B025 is therefore a question rather than a settled mapping: the bequest redirected proceeds, and right livelihood concerns the work itself. The draft is retained as a source-backed bequest narrative and the target fit is routed to the supervisor for reassignment if needed.
