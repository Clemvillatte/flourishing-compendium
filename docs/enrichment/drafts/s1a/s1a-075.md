Traditional Buddhist commentarial narrative · the Rohini water dispute, from the Dhammapada Commentary in E. W. Burlingame's translation (Access to Insight), attributed there to Dhammapada verses 197-199.

The Sakiyas and the Koliyas confined the waters of the river Rohini "by a single dam between the city of Kapilavatthu and the city of Koliya and cultivated the fields on both sides of the river" ([Burlingame](https://accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/burlingame/wheel324.html)). In the month Jetthamula the crops drooped, and the quarrel over a single watering grew until "one arose and struck another a blow", a general fight ensuing with aspersions on the origins of the two royal families ([Burlingame](https://accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/burlingame/wheel324.html)). Surveying the world at dawn, the Teacher thought, "If I refrain from going to them, these men will destroy each other. It is clearly my duty to go to them", and flew alone to the spot, seating himself cross-legged in the air over the middle of the river; his kinsmen threw away their weapons and did reverence ([Burlingame](https://accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/burlingame/wheel324.html)).

The connection with D083 is the question chain that re-prices the quarrel. The Buddha works down the ranks asking what the quarrel is about, and the slave-laborers answer, "The quarrel is about water, reverend sir". Then the questions turn on the price of things: "How much is water worth, great king?" - "Very little, reverend sir." - "How much are Khattiyas worth, great king?" - "Khattiyas are beyond price, reverend sir." - "It is not fitting that because of a little water you should destroy khattiyas who are beyond price" ([Burlingame](https://accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/burlingame/wheel324.html)). The blow and the blood feud had been priced in water; the questions set the same facts in the currency of lives, and the kings "were silent", after which the Teacher addressed them: "Were I not here present today, you would set flowing a river of blood" ([Burlingame](https://accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/burlingame/wheel324.html)).
