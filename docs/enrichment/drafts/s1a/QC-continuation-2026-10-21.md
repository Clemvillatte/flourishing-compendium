## Opened-source checks

### Meghiya

The [complete Udana 4.1 translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/ud/ud.4.01.than.html) contains the mango grove, three requests, permission after the third, sensual/hostile/harmful thoughts, return to the Buddha, five supports and four further practices. The account ends with teaching, not a narrated subsequent awakening. The draft keeps these features and does not confuse four practices with the four efforts.

Retained verbatim quotation: “He should develop good will so as to abandon ill will.” The page title is Meghiya Sutta: About Meghiya; translator Thanissaro Bhikkhu; PTS Ud 34.

### Dewey textile lessons

The [complete chapter at Brock University's Mead Project](https://brocku.ca/MeadProject/Dewey/Dewey_1907/Dewey_1907a.html), pp. 34-37, reports one group's thirty-minute cotton-fibre-removal trial yielding less than an ounce, then fibre comparison, teacher questions, carding boards, weighted spinning, a floor-spun top and further inventions towards the loom. The account is a sequence of lessons, not an invented single afternoon.

Retained verbatim quotation: “The children worked this out for themselves with the actual material, aided by questions and suggestions from the teacher.” The educational aim, “the development of social power and insight,” occurs earlier at p. 32. The draft omits precise fibre-length figures and sweeping historical claims rather than certifying them as modern scientific or historical facts.

The narrative is distinct from ST349's sand-table farm. It supplies a non-injury example of action, consequences and reflection.

### Tree and goose

The [complete Zhuangzi chapter 20](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/tree-on-the-mountain), section 1, contains both incidents, the disciples' question, rejection of the supposedly safe midpoint, changing with the times, and the final exhortation to dwell in Dao and its Attributes. All remain in the draft.

Retained verbatim quotation: “he is transformed with the (changing) character of the time, and is not willing to addict himself to any one thing”. The [Watson translation in China Heritage](https://chinaheritage.net/journal/the-use-of-the-useless-zhuangzi/), Three Trees & a Gourd III, was opened as a cross-check; its wording is not mixed into Legge quotations. This is not the chapter 4 shrine-oak story.
