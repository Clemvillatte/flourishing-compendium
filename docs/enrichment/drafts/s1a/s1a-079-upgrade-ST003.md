Parable · Warring States China · Zhuangzi, Man in the World, Associated with Other Men, section 5 · quotations from the James Legge translation (1891), opened at [METIS ETH Zurich](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)

The story opens with the judgment: "A (master) mechanic, called Shi, on his way to Qi, came to Qu-yuan, where he saw an oak-tree, which was used as the altar for the spirits of the land" ([Zhuangzi IV §5, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)). The mechanic walks past without stopping, and gives his verdict: "It is quite useless. A boat made from its wood would sink; a coffin or shell would quickly rot; an article of furniture would soon go to pieces; a door would be covered with the exuding sap; a pillar would be riddled by insects; the material of it is good for nothing, and hence it is that it has attained to so great an age" ([Zhuangzi IV §5, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)).

That night the tree answers in the mechanic's dream. Of the fruit trees it says: "their productive ability makes their lives bitter to them; they do not complete their natural term of existence, but come to a premature end in the middle of their time, bringing on themselves the destructive treatment which they ordinarily receive" ([Zhuangzi IV §5, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)). And of itself: "I have sought to discover how it was that I was so useless; I had long done so, till (the effort) nearly caused my death; and now I have learned it - it has been of the greatest use to me. Suppose that I had possessed useful properties, should I have become of the great size that I am?" ([Zhuangzi IV §5, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)). The dream ends with the tree turning the judgment back on the judge: "how is it that you a useless man know all this about me a useless tree?" ([Zhuangzi IV §5, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

My reading: the story inverts the calculator's ledger. What looks like failure from the standpoint of the market — the tree is good for nothing a carpenter can price — is the very thing that lets it live out its full term. The tree's question to the mechanic is the transferable part: the judgment of usefulness and the judgment of a life are being made by different standards, and only one of them is standing at the end of the story.
