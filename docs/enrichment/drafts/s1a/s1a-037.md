Between 1906 and 1915, Hilma af Klint produced The Paintings for the Temple, which eventually comprised 193 works ([Guggenheim education resource](https://www.guggenheim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/guggenheim-education-hilma-af-klint-teacher-resource-unit-10-5.pdf)). According to the Guggenheim, she kept her experimental paintings largely private and rarely exhibited them ([Guggenheim](https://www.guggenheim.org/exhibition/hilma-af-klint)). In 1932 she wrote in one of her notebooks that she did not want her work to be seen for twenty years after her death ([Guggenheim education resource](https://www.guggenheim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/guggenheim-education-hilma-af-klint-teacher-resource-unit-10-5.pdf)). The Guggenheim's account says she was "convinced the world was not yet ready to understand her work", and that the work was all but unseen until 1986 ([Guggenheim](https://www.guggenheim.org/exhibition/hilma-af-klint)).

What she was keeping private was not minor work. The Guggenheim describes the Temple paintings as her first major group of largely nonobjective work ([Guggenheim](https://www.guggenheim.org/exhibition/hilma-af-klint)). Of one of the series, painted in 1907, she said ([Guggenheim education resource](https://www.guggenheim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/guggenheim-education-hilma-af-klint-teacher-resource-unit-10-5.pdf)):

> "The pictures were painted directly through me, without any preliminary drawings and with great force. I had no idea what the paintings were supposed to depict; nevertheless, I worked swiftly and surely, without changing a single brushstroke."

([Guggenheim education resource](https://www.guggenheim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/guggenheim-education-hilma-af-klint-teacher-resource-unit-10-5.pdf))

The Daodejing has an image for a person like this, in James Legge's translation ([Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/216/pg216.txt)): "It is thus that the sage wears (a poor garb of) hair cloth, while he carries his (signet of) jade in his bosom." The value is real, and it is carried close, and it is not put in the surface where the world can appraise it.

### Action

Choose a period for working without announcing progress. At the end, decide what the work needs from an audience, rather than treating visibility as a measure of its worth.

**Principles:**
- [[D102 — Shine without displaying|102. Shine without displaying]] — real value carried close and kept out of the surface, per Daodejing 70's cloth and jade
