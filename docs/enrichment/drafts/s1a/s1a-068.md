Canonical verse narrative · from the Padhana Sutta, Sn 3.2, in Thanissaro Bhikkhu's translation (Access to Insight).

The discourse opens with the striving itself: "To me — resolute in exertion near the river Nerañjara, making a great effort, doing jhana to attain rest from the yoke" ([Sn 3.2, Thanissaro](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/snp/snp.3.02.than.html)). Mara comes to petition the striver to live, and the reply the sutta gives the Blessed One is a roll call: "Sensual passions are your first army. Your second is called Discontent. Your third is Hunger & Thirst. Your fourth is called Craving. Fifth is Sloth & Drowsiness. Sixth is called Terror. Your seventh is Uncertainty. Hypocrisy & Stubbornness, your eighth. Gains, Offerings, Fame, & Status wrongly gained, and whoever would praise self & disparage others" ([Sn 3.2, Thanissaro](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/snp/snp.3.02.than.html)). The passage closes with the armies as a single force: "That, Namuci, is your army, the Dark One's commando force. A coward can't defeat it, but one having defeated it gains bliss" ([Sn 3.2, Thanissaro](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/snp/snp.3.02.than.html)).

The connection with B034 is the roll call meeting the hindrance list the principle page takes from MN 10: sensual desire, ill will, sloth and drowsiness, restlessness and anxiety, uncertainty ([MN 10, Thanissaro, cited on the B034 page](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.010.than.html)). The correspondences the opened source directly supports are sensual passions, sloth and drowsiness, and uncertainty; the sutta's other entries, Discontent, Hunger and Thirst, Craving, Terror, Hypocrisy and Stubbornness, and wrongly gained gains, offerings, fame and status, are kept as parts of Mara's army without any claimed equivalence to the remaining hindrances. The defeat, in the sutta's own terms, does not come from the naming alone: the resolve continues with discernment, mastered resolve and established mindfulness, "I will go about, from kingdom to kingdom, training many disciples" ([Sn 3.2, Thanissaro](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/snp/snp.3.02.than.html)).

Not carried: the page's MN 10 practice of discerning each hindrance's presence, absence, arising and prevention; the sutta keeps to the battlefield image and the list.

**Principles:**
- [[B034 — Know the five hindrances]]: the armies named in a roll call, with the correspondences to the MN 10 hindrances kept to the three the opened source directly supports and the rest left unclaimed; the fit is offered as an illustration and routed to the supervisor.
