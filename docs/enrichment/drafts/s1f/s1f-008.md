Biographical narrative, in his own words · Kabat-Zinn founding the Stress Reduction and Relaxation Program at a Massachusetts medical school, 1979; the four foundations put to work in a hospital clinic

Kabat-Zinn starts his account with the founding: "I started what was originally called the Stress Reduction and Relaxation Program in September 1979. It didn't come out of a vacuum . . . there were many years of pondering and meditating and inward and outward wandering before it arose as a possibility in my mind." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf)) The program was renamed as it grew: "Later, as more and more programmes started forming based on our work, we came to speak of our work in a more generic form as MBSR, or mindfulness-based stress reduction." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf)) He names the motive: "From the start, it was motivated by a strong impulse on my part ... to bring my dharma practice together with my work life into one unified whole, as an expression of right livelihood and in the service of something useful that felt very much needed in the world." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf)) And the first patients came by referral: "And so, MBSR came into being in the Fall of 1979, and those first three very forward thinking clinic directors, Tom Winters, Bob Burney and John J. Monahan exclusively referred the first few cycles of patients until word spread further into the medical community within the hospital and then out into the larger medical community as well." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf))

The working definition he gives is the practice's instruction: "(a) paying attention in a particular way: on purpose, in the present moment, and non-judgmentally (Kabat-Zinn 1994); (b) the awareness that arises from paying attention, on purpose, in the present moment, and non-judgmentally (Kabat-Zinn 2005e)." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf))

The four foundations are in the account, and he names two of them in his own words. On the feeling tone: "Rather, it points out that there will be many many judgments and opinions arising from moment to moment, but that we do not have to judge or evaluate or react to any of what arises, other than perhaps recognizing it in the moment of arising as pleasant, unpleasant, or neutral (the second foundation or establishment of mindfulness)." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf)) On the mental states: "Thus we become intimate with the nature of thoughts and emotions, and mental states such as aversion, frustration, restlessness, greed, doubt, sloth and torpor, and boredom, to name a few, which constitutes the territory of the third foundation of mindfulness, without ever having to mention the classical map of the four foundations of mindfulness, nor the five hindrances, nor the seven factors of enlightenment." ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf))

The last clause is the point the story carries for B039: the domains of the satipatthana map are in the practice, taught and worked with, whether or not the map is mentioned.

**Principles:**
- [[B039 — Practice the four foundations of mindfulness (satipatthana)|B039 Practice the four foundations of mindfulness (satipatthana)]]: Kabat-Zinn's own account names the second foundation (feeling tone: pleasant, unpleasant, neutral) and the third (mental states: aversion, frustration, restlessness, greed, doubt, sloth and torpor, boredom) as the working territory of the MBSR teaching, and states that the classical map of the four foundations need not be mentioned for the domains to be the practice; the page does not supply a body or mental-patterns passage the text does not give, and does not treat the founding or the definitions as evidence of clinical or soteriological outcomes. ([Kabat-Zinn](https://jonkabat-zinn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JonKabat-Zinn_SomeReflectionsOriginsMBSR.pdf))
