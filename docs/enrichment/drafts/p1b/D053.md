> **Principle 53** — Balance alertness and ease

The manual names the two failure modes together: "The two mistakes of laziness and distraction must be combated by quiet work that is carried on daily without interruption" (Section 4, trans. Wilhelm/Baynes, [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/stream/TheSecretOfTheGoldenFlowerByRichardWilhelmAndCarlJung/The+Secret+of+the+Golden+Flower+by+Richard+Wilhelm+and+Carl+Jung_djvu.txt)). Laziness shows up in the sitting as sleepiness; distraction as the spirit wandering off. The state asked for instead is quiet light:

> "The Light of the eyes must shine quietly, and, for a long time, neither sleepiness nor distraction must set in." (Secret of the Golden Flower, Section 3, trans. Wilhelm/Baynes, [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/stream/TheSecretOfTheGoldenFlowerByRichardWilhelmAndCarlJung/The+Secret+of+the+Golden+Flower+by+Richard+Wilhelm+and+Carl+Jung_djvu.txt))

The same instructions guard against the opposite error, forcing. When the sitting begins, "care must be taken to see that everything can proceed in a comfortable, easy manner. Too much must not be demanded of the heart" (Section 5, trans. Wilhelm/Baynes, [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/stream/TheSecretOfTheGoldenFlowerByRichardWilhelmAndCarlJung/The+Secret+of+the+Golden+Flower+by+Richard+Wilhelm+and+Carl+Jung_djvu.txt)). The body is to sit "comfortable and harmonious" while the light stays quietly lit; the state the compendium calls relaxed alertness is this pairing.

(Secret of the Golden Flower.)

### Plain words

The two ways to ruin a sitting are opposites. One is to go dull: the eyes droop, the breath gets heavy, you slide toward sleep. The other is to grip: you stare inward, hold the breath, demand the quiet right now. The manual's image for the right state is quiet light, eyes lit but soft, body settled. It also says the noticing is itself the correction: becoming conscious of inattention is the mechanism that does away with inattention. When you catch either failure, return to the easy, lit sitting without demanding more of the heart.

### For example

Long-haul drivers hold the wheel with relaxed hands and keep the eyes moving: nothing gripped, nothing drooping.

## Interpretation limits (for the ledger)

- The compendium's "relaxed alertness" is a gloss; the text's own pairing is quiet light
  (eyes shine quietly) against two named mistakes (laziness, distraction), plus the ease
  instruction ("comfortable, easy manner", "too much must not be demanded of the heart").
- "The noticing is itself the correction" is supported: "To become conscious of the
  inattention is the mechanism by which to do away with inattention" (Section 4).
- The driver example is an everyday illustration, not a claim from the text.
- The old For example's "alert, not clenched. That's the state." closer and the old Plain
  words' "trainable skill" claim had no opened source and were dropped.
