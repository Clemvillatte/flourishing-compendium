# P155 review draft

## Proposed body

Workability needs a purpose: working towards what? In Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), chosen values supply that direction; a response is assessed by what it helps a person do in context. Hayes connects this approach to functional contextualism, which evaluates scientific analyses by their capacity to predict and influence events ([Hayes, 2006, “Functional Contextualism”](https://contextualscience.org/book/export/html/839); [Hayes, 2008, pp. 287–288](https://contextualscience.org/files/Hayes,2008.pdf)).

James's pragmatism also asks what difference an idea makes in experience, but his account of truth includes verification and consistency with other truths ([James, Pragmatism, lectures II and VI](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5116/5116-h/5116-h.htm)).

> "What difference would it practically make to anyone if this notion rather than that notion were true?" ([James, lecture II](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5116/5116-h/5116-h.htm)).

These are related uses of consequences, with different tasks: James is discussing the meaning and truth of ideas; ACT uses workability to examine action in relation to values ([James, lectures II and VI](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5116/5116-h/5116-h.htm); [Hayes, 2006](https://contextualscience.org/book/export/html/839)).

(Hayes et al., 2011, ACT, pp. 19-27; Hayes, 2019, A Liberated Mind; James, 1907, Pragmatism, Lecture II.)

### Plain words

Ask what following a thought leads you to do, and whether that action serves something you care about. Keep the question about usefulness separate from checking a factual claim: James's account requires beliefs to bear scrutiny in experience ([Hayes, 2006](https://contextualscience.org/book/export/html/839); [James, lecture VI](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5116/5116-h/5116-h.htm)).

### For example

Suppose you value being a reliable friend but think you need to feel cheerful before replying to a message. Try a brief, honest reply while feeling low, then check whether you followed through on that friendship. This is an illustration of testing a rule against a chosen value, not a reported treatment result.
