# Right View

> What is it to see rightly, and what does that seeing do to a life?

This dossier is a map, not an essay. The aim is that a reader can say "I want to think about right view", open this page, and see the working spread of what the compendium currently holds on it: the entries that bear on the first factor of the Eightfold Path, grouped by the question they answer, each with a card link; the spread is a working map, and gaps are expected. The lines are short on purpose; the dense arguments live on the cards. The editorial aim running through the compendium also runs through this page: to loosen the grip of views held as possessions, without pretending there is no seeing to be done.

How to read this page. Each line gives the entry's own opening claim, compressed from the entry itself. Evidence notes in this dossier describe the quoted passages only; a checked quotation certifies the passage, not the whole linked page. Every concept card carries onward to the practice principles that cite it, so the map needs no separate principle index.

## Nearby terms

- Right understanding: another rendering of the same factor (Pali sammā diṭṭhi); the wiki's card is [Right view](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Right%20view), and nothing is split between the two names.
- Discernment: MN 117's word for the transcendent form of the factor, the faculty of discernment in one developing the noble path; the wiki's dedicated card on discernment is Ignatian rather than Buddhist ([Discernment of spirits](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Discernment%20of%20spirits)), a cousin practice in another tradition.
- View, perspective, standpoint: the general material on perspectivality, where seeing always happens from somewhere, is on-theme and lives in the map below ([The standpoint determines what appears](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20standpoint%20determines%20what%20appears), [The coloured lens](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20coloured%20lens)); it is not a separate family.
- Wisdom: the bigger question of what seeing well amounts to across traditions has its own published dossier (Wisdom); the boundary is that this page holds the path's own account of the view, not wisdom in general.

## The map: a working map of what the wiki holds on right view

### What the view is

[Right view](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Right%20view): the first factor, defined in the Sammādiṭṭhi Sutta by what a person of right view discerns: stress, its origination, its cessation and the way of practice leading to its cessation.

[Four Noble Truths](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Four%20Noble%20Truths): the teaching the view is a view of, each truth is paired with a task: comprehension, abandonment, realization and development.

[Eightfold Path](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Eightfold%20Path): the umbrella hub; right view is the first of eight factors, grouped with intention in the wisdom training.

[The three trainings](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20three%20trainings): the grouping that places view and intention together under wisdom, alongside ethics and concentration.

[Distinguishing practice from theory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Distinguishing%20practice%20from%20theory): the raft simile of MN 22; the Dhamma is a crossing device, and the view is held for the crossing, not the carrying.

The Sammādiṭṭhi Sutta defines the view by the discernment itself:

> "When a disciple of the noble ones discerns stress, the origination of stress, the cessation of stress, and the way of practice leading to the cessation of stress, then he is a person of right view... who has arrived at this true Dhamma." ([MN 9, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.009.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

### The two forms of the view

[Right view](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Right%20view) (two forms section): the Mahācattārīsaka Sutta separates an everyday form, a set of convictions siding with merit, from a transcendent form that is a factor of the path.

> "There is right view with effluents, siding with merit, resulting in acquisitions [of becoming]; there is right view that is noble, without effluents, transcendent, a factor of the path." ([MN 117, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.117.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

The everyday form is spelled out as convictions, there is what is given, offered and sacrificed, fruits and results of good and bad actions, this world and the next, and the transcendent form as the discernment of one developing the noble path.

### The middle way between views

[Dependent origination](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Dependent%20origination): the twelve-link chain of SN 12.1, the conditionality the view tracks; arising and cessation both.

[The middle path](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20middle%20path): the way of practice that avoids the two extremes; a path with its own logic, not a midpoint averaged between vices.

The Kaccānagotta Sutta sets the view between the two existence extremes:

> "'Everything exists': That is one extreme. 'Everything doesn't exist': That is a second extreme. Avoiding these two extremes, the Tathagata teaches the Dhamma via the middle: From ignorance as a requisite condition come fabrications." ([SN 12.15, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn12/sn12.015.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

And the same sutta gives the view its concrete test, watching stress arise and pass without a self fastened to it:

> "He has no uncertainty or doubt that just stress, when arising, is arising; stress, when passing away, is passing away. In this, his knowledge is independent of others. It's to this extent, Kaccayana, that there is right view." ([SN 12.15, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn12/sn12.015.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

### Wrong view and the distortion family

[Cognitive distortions](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Cognitive%20distortions): Beck's systematic errors in the thinking of depressed patients, arbitrary inference, selective abstraction, overgeneralization, with the re-examination method that checks them.

[The coloured lens](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20coloured%20lens): the image of moods as tinted glass; Emerson's beads, each lens showing only what lies in its focus, with Gallegos's caution that the metaphor misleads in ways.

[First impressions bias](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=First%20impressions%20bias): how fast an impression forms, a hundred milliseconds (Willis and Todorov), and how much weight the first information carries (Asch's order effects).

[Appraisal theory](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Appraisal%20theory): the family of theories in which emotions arise from evaluations of what an event means, Lazarus's stakes and coping; the same event yields different affect under different appraisals.

MN 9 names the roots of what is unskillful (the passage covers unskillful qualities generally, not wrong view alone):

> "And what are the roots of what is unskillful? Greed is a root of what is unskillful, aversion is a root of what is unskillful, delusion is a root of what is unskillful." ([MN 9, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.009.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

### The view as forerunner

[Action over intention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Action%20over%20intention): the right action factor, defined by abstentions rather than states of mind; the path names conduct its own factor beside the resolve that precedes it.

[Right intention](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Right%20intention): the factor that follows view in the list; the resolve toward renunciation, freedom from ill will and harmlessness.

> "Of those, right view is the forerunner. And how is right view the forerunner? In one of right view, wrong view is abolished. The many evil, unskillful qualities that come into play with wrong view as their condition are also abolished, while the many skillful qualities that have right view as their condition go to the culmination of their development." ([MN 117, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.117.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

The compendium's saying for the same dependence: Right action serves right view.

The modern cards in this subtheme are editorial connections the compendium draws between the path's wrong view and the bias research; the tradition does not equate them, and neither does this page.

### The view of the self

[The standpoint determines what appears](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20standpoint%20determines%20what%20appears): Husserl's account of embodied seeing; things appear from one side, at a distance, from an angle, and there is no view from nowhere.

[Right view](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Right%20view) (in the compendium section): the attentional-bias principles file their Buddhist parallel under the coloured lens and right view: anxiety and depression distort what you notice, threat captures attention, negative bias and rumination narrow it.

### Views held lightly

[The four views](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20four%20views): the Diamond Sutra's four ways a mind reifies itself, views of a self, a person, a being and a life, usable coordinates that become toxic when mistaken for the territory.

[Shunyata](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Shunyata): the Madhyamaka doctrine that all phenomena lack inherent existence; emptiness is always the emptiness of something, and is itself empty.

[Buddhist emptiness of inherent nature](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Buddhist%20emptiness%20of%20inherent%20nature): the claim that no phenomenon possesses svabhāva, a nature it has independently; the rejected svabhāva is a superimposition projected onto objects, a cognitive default.

[The emptiness of emptiness](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20emptiness%20of%20emptiness): Nagarjuna's chapter thirteen turning the analysis on its own central term; the empty is defined only against the non-empty, so emptiness cannot stand as an independent metaphysical item.

[Ends-in-view](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Ends-in-view): Dewey's name for the foreseen consequence that guides present action; an end belongs inside activity as a tool for directing it, not outside it as a fixed destination.

[Fixed views of self](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Fixed%20views%20of%20self): the belief that who you are is a set quantity; Dweck's and Crocker's research lines and the fragility prediction, with its failed-premises tests honestly recorded.

[Partial truths in every opinion](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Partial%20truths%20in%20every%20opinion): Mill's argument that a prevailing opinion usually holds part of the truth and the missing part sits in the opinion it opposes.

[Steelmanning the opposing view](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=Steelmanning%20the%20opposing%20view): the practice of addressing the strongest form of the other person's argument, with Mill's ground that knowing only one's own side of the case is knowing little of that.

[The view from above](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=The%20view%20from%20above): the Stoic exercise of picturing human affairs from a great height until their scale changes; Marcus Aurelius's herds and solemnities seen from above.

## Where the traditions genuinely disagree

No same-question disagreement between traditions is yet grounded on this theme; the candidates below are recorded as open research questions, each missing either an opposed position quoted from an opened carrier or the demonstration that the two positions answer the same question.

### Is the work with views a cultivation or a non-establishment?

The strongest candidate pairs two passages that may not oppose each other. The early discourses cultivate right view as forerunner:

> "Of those, right view is the forerunner. And how is right view the forerunner? In one of right view, wrong view is abolished. The many evil, unskillful qualities that come into play with wrong view as their condition are also abolished, while the many skillful qualities that have right view as their condition go to the culmination of their development." ([MN 117, Thanissaro translation](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.117.than.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

The Mahāyāna passage that grounds the candidate instructs knowing and viewing dharmas without setting up the perception of a dharma:

> "That which has been taught by the Tathagata as 'view of self', as a no-view has that been taught by the Tathagata. Therefore is it called 'view of self'... someone who has set out in the Bodhisattva-vehicle should know all dharmas, view them, be intent on them. And he should know, view and be intent on them in such a way that he does not set up the perception of a dharma... 'Perception of dharma, perception of dharma,' Subhuti, as no-perception has this been taught by the Tathagata. Therefore is it called 'perception of dharma'." ([Diamond Sutra, section 31, Conze rendering, full text](http://www.nyx.net/~dbachman/texts/Diamond.txt)): Primary text; quoted passages checked against the raw text.

The Stanford Encyclopedia frames the Madhyamaka move as a corrective rather than a new position, which is context for the comparison, not one of its poles: emptiness is "not some kind of primordial reality ante rem but a corrective to a mistaken view of how the world exists" ([SEP, Nāgārjuna](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nagarjuna/)): Scholarly interpretation; cited passages checked.

Whether this is a disagreement at all is what remains unresolved: MN 117 affirms a view whose holding culminates skillful qualities; the Diamond Sutra instructs knowing and viewing dharmas while not setting up the perception of a dharma. The Diamond Sutra's own wording both directs the viewing and dissolves the view's reification, so the two texts may be levels of one training, a view held for cultivation but not clung to, or genuinely opposed stances toward views. Until a carrier is opened in which a tradition reads the early right view under this dissolution, the compendium holds both texts without certifying an opposition.

### Is the view a set of convictions or a direct discernment?

MN 117 presents the two forms within one discourse, which is a grading rather than a disagreement between traditions; the two-forms material stays in the map above. The open question is whether any tradition actually reads the conviction set as sufficient without the discernment, and no carrier taking that position has been opened yet.

### Are the truths tasks or beliefs?

The sutta's own presentation assigns each truth a task:

> "This noble truth of stress is to be comprehended... This noble truth of the origination of stress is to be abandoned... This noble truth of the cessation of stress is to be realized... This noble truth of the way of practice leading to the cessation of stress is to be developed." ([SN 56.11, Thanissaro translation](https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/SN/SN56_11.html)): Primary text; quoted passages checked.

The propositional reading exists in summary sources, but no carrier advocating a beliefs-only reading has been opened; the compendium's tasks-not-beliefs principle is an editorial reading of the verbs, not a settled scholarly classification. Next pass: open the summary carriers and the tasks-reading scholarship, quote both positions, or drop the divergence.

## Practice: variation, selection, retention

::Variation:: What tends to need varying on this theme is the way views are held: a fixed view of where happiness comes from, a view held as mine, a first impression taken for the whole person. The adaptive direction is the one the suttas give the view itself: watching stress arise and pass without a self fastened to it, and checking, before effort, what the plan assumes.

::Selection:: The kernels the compendium holds on this theme, each a selected source of that variation:

1. [Two-truths drill](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K134%20%E2%80%94%20Two-truths%20drill): argue the strongest case for your position, then the strongest case against it; the consider-the-opposite studies behind the kernel found the structural act of constructing the opposing case had greater corrective effect than instructions to be fair, and did not test effects on belief strength or well-being over time.

2. [Perspective-taking](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K131%20%E2%80%94%20Perspective-taking): when locked in a view, step into another position: the other person's, a neutral observer's, a future self's; each position reveals what the others conceal.

3. [Perspective-getting](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K218%20%E2%80%94%20Perspective-getting): when you assume you know what someone thinks, ask instead; the research favours asking over guessing, with the caution that the advantage is real but not automatic, and most people ask less than they could.

4. [Kill-the-Buddha drill](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K238%20%E2%80%94%20Kill-the-Buddha%20drill): for one belief you hold, argue from your own experience against your favorite authority; the drill tests whether the belief survives contact with lived evidence or is held on authority alone.

5. ["I am becoming" reframes](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K147%20%E2%80%94%20I%20am%20becoming%20reframes): for a stuck identity, catch the fixed label and ask what it forecloses; the reframe works on the view of self as a set quantity, and the mindset literature behind it carries its own failed-premises caution.

6. [The pragmatic maxim](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K132%20%E2%80%94%20The%20pragmatic%20maxim): take the belief doing the most work in your thinking and ask what conceivable practical difference it makes against its rival; if no difference can be found, the dispute is about a word, not a world.

7. [The chariot analysis](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K144%20%E2%80%94%20The%20chariot%20analysis): when a self-image starts speaking as the whole self, the wounded child, the achiever, the critic, give it the chariot treatment; ask, part by part, where the designation lands, and watch it fail to land on any of them.

8. [Evidence-versus-interpretation drill](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K270%20%E2%80%94%20Evidence-versus-interpretation%20drill): lay a distressing thought out and write the evidence for it, the evidence against it, and an alternative reading; the written version of Epictetus's question, which is the thing and which the notion.

9. [The view from above](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=K273%20%E2%80%94%20The%20view%20from%20above): picture your concerns from a great height, the city, then the country, then the planet; the self-distancing research trains a similar observer's stance and reports less distress, though it does not test the ascent itself.

::Retention:: Retention is what people keep because it works for them. A reader who keeps the question put to a plan before acting, or the opposite case written once, has what the theme offered; what stays is a matter of fit between the check and the life it enters.

## Story: the stories that carry the theme

Three of the compendium's view-family stories carry the theme: the view of the self, the view of effort and the view of mastery.

### The view of the self

- [ST065 — Nagasena and the Chariot](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST065%20%E2%80%94%20Nagasena%20and%20the%20Chariot): King Milinda pounces on the monk's name, and Nagasena dismantles the chariot part by part until the king concedes that "chariot" is a convention for an assembly in working order, and so is Nagasena.

### The view of effort

- [ST064 — The Lute String](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST064%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Lute%20String): a monk's feet bleed from straining in meditation, and the Buddha asks the former lute player whether the strings sounded best too tight or too slack; neither, and so with the energy.

### The view of mastery

- [ST067 — Fletcher's Classroom](https://clemvillatte.github.io/flourishing-compendium/#card=ST067%20%E2%80%94%20Fletcher%27s%20Classroom): the drummer practices until his hands bleed, arrives bloodied from a car crash and is dismissed mid-performance; in the final scene he reclaims the drum kit and cuts the conductor off by cueing the band himself.
