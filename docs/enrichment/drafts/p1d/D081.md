> **Principle 81** — Recognize the relativity of perspectives

In the Zhuangzi's seventeenth chapter, the river god walks east along his course till he reaches the North Sea, looks out and cannot see where its waters begin, and sighs that the saying about one who has learned a hundred points of the Dao and thinks no one equals him was surely spoken of him ([Zhuangzi 17, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)).

> "A frog in a well cannot be talked with about the sea - he is confined to the limits of his hole. An insect of the summer cannot be talked with about ice - it knows nothing beyond its own season. A scholar of limited views cannot be talked with about the Dao - he is bound by the teaching (which he has received)." (Zhuangzi 17, [Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf))

The sea god's reply turns the rebuke into an opening: having come out from between his banks and beheld the great sea, the river god has come to know his own ignorance and inferiority, and is thereby fitted to be talked with about great principles ([Zhuangzi 17, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)).

The relativity runs all the way up. Ruo continues that between heaven and earth he is but as a small stone or a small tree on a great hill, and that he has never made much of himself because he compares his own form with heaven and earth ([Zhuangzi 17, Legge](https://metis.ethz.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Zhuangzi_EnglishLegge.pdf)). Every view in the scene is a view from somewhere. What separates Ruo from the river god is the habit of comparing his own position with something larger.

(Zhuangzi 17.)

**Plain words**
The river god's boast was not a character flaw; it was an accurate reading of his river. Standing on his banks, he really was the biggest thing around. The correction came only from travel: seeing the sea did what argument could not. Your confidence tracks the size of your pond, so the correction is exposure rather than argument.

*For example:*
The strongest player in a small-town club joins an academy squad and spends the first week discovering that the local ceiling was a regional floor.
