> **Principle 29** — Watch how words work, not what they name

The Philosophical Investigations makes the point in a carefully hedged sentence:

> "For a large class of cases—though not for all—in which we employ the word "meaning" it can be defined thus: the meaning of a word is its use in the language." (Philosophical Investigations §43, [Anscombe translation, Internet Archive](https://ia800704.us.archive.org/30/items/OurFaith/Ludwig.Wittgenstein.-.Philosophical.Investigations.pdf))

The hedge matters: this is a working rule for a large class of cases, not a universal theory of meaning ([PI 43](https://ia800704.us.archive.org/30/items/OurFaith/Ludwig.Wittgenstein.-.Philosophical.Investigations.pdf)). The same book names the unit of analysis: the language-game, a term chosen to stress that the speaking of language is part of an activity, or of a form of life, with examples running from giving orders to asking, thanking, cursing, greeting and praying ([PI 23](https://ia800704.us.archive.org/30/items/OurFaith/Ludwig.Wittgenstein.-.Philosophical.Investigations.pdf)). And it names the repair for jammed talk: bringing words back from their metaphysical to their everyday use ([PI 116](https://ia800704.us.archive.org/30/items/OurFaith/Ludwig.Wittgenstein.-.Philosophical.Investigations.pdf)).

Applied to a jammed conversation, the rule is a troubleshooting order: before disputing what a word really means, look at what each speaker is doing with it.

(Later Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations, 1953.)

**Plain words**
A word is not a container with a fixed content; it is a tool with a job, and the job is visible in the sentence and the situation. When two people fight over one word, they are usually holding two tools with the same handle. The fix is not a better dictionary; it is asking each person to show their use: an example, a sentence, a case where the word did work they approved of.

*For example:*
"We need to be more professional": she means punctual, he means formal dress. Asking each for a sentence where "professional" was violated turns the argument into a comparison of jobs.
