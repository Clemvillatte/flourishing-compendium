> **Principle 12** — Model yourself on water

Chapter 8 gives the model and the reason attached to it:

> "The highest excellence is like (that of) water. The excellence of water appears in its benefiting all things, and in its occupying, without striving (to the contrary), the low place which all men dislike. Hence (its way) is near to (that of) the Tao." (Daodejing 8, [Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm))

The other three chapters rotate the image onto different stages. Chapter 66 explains why the rivers and seas are kings of all the valley streams: their skill in being lower than they; the sage who wishes to lead puts himself below others by his words and behind them with his person ([DDJ 66, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)). Chapter 61 applies the same geometry to states: a great state is like a low-lying, down-flowing stream to which the small states tend ([DDJ 61, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)). Chapter 78 adds the counterintuitive payoff: nothing in the world is softer or weaker than water, yet for attacking things firm and strong nothing takes precedence of it; everyone knows the soft overcomes the hard, though no one carries it out in practice ([DDJ 78, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)).

The strategy the compendium takes from this: usefulness without contention, position at the low place, adaptability and persistence. Chapter 8 closes with the social mechanism: the person of highest excellence does not wrangle about a low position, so no one finds fault with him ([DDJ 8, Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/216/216-h/216-h.htm)).

(DDJ 8, 61, 66, 78.)

**Plain words**
Water does four things at once: it serves everything it touches, it runs to the bottom where nobody wants to be, it fits whatever container it lands in, and it does not stop. Any one of them costs something in a world where people compete for the visible top spots. The chapter's claim is that the combination is not weakness: it is the closest thing to the Tao's own way of operating, and it ends up running the valley.

*For example:*
Someone takes the tasks nobody wants, does them without competing for credit, and changes shape as the project changes. Chapter 66's logic, in office clothes: the streams go to what is lower.
