## Carriers opened and quoted

  https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.6.vi.html. Quotes: sophia as intuitive reason plus
  scientific knowledge (VI.7); "what is wise is the same but what is practically wise is different" (VI.7);
  cleverness and the mark (VI.12); no goodness without practical wisdom, no practical wisdom without
  moral virtue (VI.13); "practical wisdom is a virtue and not an art" (VI.5). Matched verbatim.

## Open points, left open on purpose

- Whether a person who reasons wisely by the wise-reasoning measures but chooses badly would count as wise: not settled by the abstracts.
- Whether the contemplative wisdom (sophia) steadies or competes with practical wisdom: not settled by the sources held.
- The age finding in Grossmann and Kross 2014 concerns reasoning about personal conflicts only; the dossier says so.
- Character formation over years and a short reasoning intervention are different outcomes; the dossier does not claim one refutes the other.
