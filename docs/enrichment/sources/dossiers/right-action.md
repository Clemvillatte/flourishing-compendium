  https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn45/sn45.008.than.html —
  quoted: "And what, monks, is right action? Abstaining from taking life,
  abstaining from stealing, abstaining from unchastity: This, monks, is

  first draft quoted the definition from memory with "illicit sex"; the
  raw page reads "unchastity", and the dossier was corrected to the
  page's wording before this verification record was written.
- Bhikkhu Bodhi, The Way to the End of Suffering — carrier
  https://www.accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/bodhi/waytoend.html —
  quoted three passages: the positive counterpart to abstaining from
  taking life ("kindness and compassion for other beings... desiring the
  welfare of all beings"), the marital-fidelity counterpart ("The
  positive virtue corresponding to the abstinence is, for laypeople,
  marital fidelity..."), and the non-stealing counterpart ("The positive
  counterpart to abstaining from stealing is honesty... The most eminent
  opposite virtue is generosity..."). All verified against the fetched
  page. The fidelity passage is Bodhi's lay instruction and is kept
  distinct from any monastic celibacy reading of the precept.
- Wang Yangming doctrine of the unity of knowledge and action — carrier
  https://iep.utm.edu/wangyang/ (Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy) —

  Verified against the fetched page.
