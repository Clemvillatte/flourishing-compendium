## Carriers opened and verified (pass 1)

1. MN 9, Sammādiṭṭhi Sutta — Thanissaro Bhikkhu translation,
   https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.009.than.html
   - Verified present: "When a disciple of the noble ones discerns stress,
     the origination of stress, the cessation of stress, and the way of
     practice leading to the cessation of stress, then he is a person of
     right view... who has arrived at this true Dhamma."
   - Verified present: "And what are the roots of what is unskillful? Greed
     is a root of what is unskillful, aversion is a root of what is
     unskillful, delusion is a root of what is unskillful."
   - Verified present: the path list "right view, right resolve, right
     speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right
     mindfulness, right concentration."
   - NOT on this page: any "forerunner" passage (that is MN 117; the Right
     view card cites the two suttas separately and correctly).

2. SN 12.15, Kaccānagotta Sutta — Thanissaro Bhikkhu translation,
   https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn12/sn12.015.than.html
   - Verified present: "By & large, Kaccayana, this world is in bondage to
     attachments, clingings (sustenances), & biases. But one such as this
     does not get involved with or cling to these attachments, clingings,
     fixations of awareness, biases, or obsessions; nor is he resolved on
     'my self.' He has no uncertainty or doubt that just stress, when
     arising, is arising; stress, when passing away, is passing away. In
     this, his knowledge is independent of others. It's to this extent,
     Kaccayana, that there is right view."
   - Verified present: "'Everything exists': That is one extreme.
     'Everything doesn't exist': That is a second extreme. Avoiding these
     two extremes, the Tathagata teaches the Dhamma via the middle..."
