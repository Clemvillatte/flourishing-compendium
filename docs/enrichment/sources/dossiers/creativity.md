## Verification method

Every block quotation in rev 1 was verified word-for-word against raw text:
raw curl + pdftotext with whitespace-normalized grep (Hennessey & Amabile
2010; Guilford 1950; Schumpeter ch. VII; Cambridge International chapter 4;

Gutenberg text files (Dewey; Bergson), or raw curl of PubMed eutils
efetch abstract text with no LLM extraction (Wang et al. 2017; Kounios &
Beeman 2014). Three quotes were taken from fetch-tool page reads and then
cross-checked against the wiki's own previously PDF-verified card wording,
matching word-for-word (Conner et al. 2018 abstract via the Taylor & Francis
abstract page, cross-checked against BE059's PDF-verified quote; Sio & Ormerod
2009 abstract via the ERIC record, cross-checked against AT036; Haase et al.

disagreed anywhere, the quote would have been dropped; no disagreement was
found. Map-entry glosses rest on card bodies opened and read this session
