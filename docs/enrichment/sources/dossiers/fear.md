## Fear-versus-anxiety boundary matrix (the band's first task, per DISAMBIGUATION.md)

- Fear: response to a specific danger, near and great — Aristotle's
  definition ("pain or disturbance due to a mental picture of some
  destructive or painful evil in the future", with the nearness clause and
  the "fear sets us thinking what can be done" clause), verified at
  https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/rhetoric.2.ii.html . The evolved
  fear module (Öhman & Mineka 2001, four characteristics, verified at
  http://people.whitman.edu/~herbrawt/classes/390/Ohman.pdf ) gives fear
  the automatic, partly impenetrable defense system.
- Anxiety: response to possibility without a definite object — the
  Kierkegaard line held on the Anxiety dossier's map ("Anxiety about the
  future" card); the Anxiety dossier's own divergence treats the same
  boundary.
- Homes: threat-anticipation, worry, panic-of-fear, stress and
  metacognitive-model material stays with Anxiety; the defense emotion
  (fear proper), the fear-of-death cluster, the courage cluster and the
  exposure cluster live here. Panic: no dedicated card exists; fear.md
  points to Anxiety about the future and the interoceptive-exposure
  material. Dread: no dedicated card; objectless dread is anxiety-family.
  The DISAMBIGUATION.md matrix row has been updated to record this.
- Courage: the registry lists it as fear's nearby term; by the
