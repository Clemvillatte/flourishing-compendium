# A005 — Choose the mean, not the extreme (p1b, genuineflourishing36, 2026-10-19)

- Opened: https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.2.ii.html — NE Book II (Internet Classics Archive, Ross translation; URL used by the owner's update). Six quoted fragments verified mechanically against the raw HTML (curl + tag-strip):
  - "Virtue, then, is a state of character concerned with choice, lying in a mean, i.e. the mean relative to us, this being determined by a rational principle, and by that principle by which the man of practical wisdom would determine it."
  - "to feel them at the right times, with reference to the right objects, towards the right people, with the right motive, and in the right way, is what is both intermediate and best, and this is characteristic of virtue."
