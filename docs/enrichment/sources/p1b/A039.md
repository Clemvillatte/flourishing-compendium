# A039: source ledger

## Opened sources (2026-10-19, genuineflourishing27)

- Nicomachean Ethics, trans. D. P. Chase (Project Gutenberg #8438):
  https://www.gutenberg.org/files/8438/8438-h/8438-h.htm
  - Supported (Book VI, practical wisdom): "Nor again does Practical Wisdom consist in a
    knowledge of general principles only, but it is necessary that one should know also the
    particular details, because it is apt to act, and action is concerned with details: for
    which reason sometimes men who have not much knowledge are more practical than others who
    have; among others, they who derive all they know from actual experience."
  - Supported (light-meats example): "suppose a man to know, for instance, that light meats
    are easy of digestion and wholesome, but not what kinds of meat are light, he will not
    produce a healthy state; that man will have a much better chance of doing so, who knows
    that the flesh of birds is light and wholesome."
  - Supported (young men): "...the young come to be geometricians, and mathematicians... but
    it is not thought that a young man can come to be possessed of Practical Wisdom: now the
    reason is, that this Wisdom has for its object particular facts, which come to be known
    from experience, which a young man has not because it is produced only by length of
    time." (not used in the body; kept here for reuse)
  - Batch note: same ebook serves A004 (NE II), A005 (NE II), A010 (NE I), A065 (NE VIII, IX),
    A068 (NE VIII, IX) later in this band.
- Metaphysics I.1, trans. John H. M'Mahon (1896, Internet Archive full text):
  https://archive.org/stream/metaphysicsaris00arisgoog/metaphysicsaris00arisgoog_djvu.txt
  - Supported: "As regards, indeed, practical purposes, therefore, experience seems in no wise
    to differ from art; nay, even we see the experienced compassing their objects more

  - Supported (the caveat, quoted in body): "For the experienced, indeed, know that a thing is
