# B119 — The self is a chariot (p1b, genuineflourishing42, 2026-10-20)
- Opened: https://suttacentral.net/mil3.1.1/en/tw_rhysdavids (Rhys Davids trans., SBE 35, ed. Sujato; PTS En 40-45 markers visible). Verbatim: "only a generally understood term, a designation in common use. For there is no permanent individuality (no soul) involved in the matter"; the chariot questions (En 43-44); "Chariot is a mere empty sound"; "on account of its having all these things"; Vajirā verse (En 45). "(no soul)" is Rhys Davids' bracket.
- Opened: https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn05/sn05.010.than.html (Vajira Sutta, Thanissaro): "Just as when, with an assemblage of parts, there's the word, chariot, even so when aggregates are present, there's the convention of living being."
- Opened: https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn22/sn22.059.than.html (Thanissaro): "If form were the self, this form would not lend itself to dis-ease..." Confirmed that SN 22.59 contains NO chariot; old locator implied it did.
- Removed: "the driving is the driver" (no source). McAdams 2013, Dennett 1991, Hume, Metzinger inherited and unopened.
