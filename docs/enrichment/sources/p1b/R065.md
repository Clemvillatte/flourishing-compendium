# R065: source ledger

## Opened sources (2026-10-19, genuineflourishing27)

- Legge's Great Learning, Chinese Text Project: https://ctext.org/liji/da-xue
  - Supported: the full chain passage ("The ancients who wished to illustrate illustrious
    virtue... first extended to the utmost their knowledge. Such extension of knowledge lay
    in the investigation of things. Things being investigated, knowledge became complete.
    Their knowledge being complete, their thoughts were sincere...").
  - Locator: the ctext Daxue has no numbered divisions in Legge's text; the quote is from
    the opening passage beginning "The ancients who wished...". No numbering invented.
  - Limit: the passage connects investigation, completed knowledge, sincere thought and
    self-cultivation. It does not rank experience above books; it does not prescribe
    experimental testing. The first draft's "contact with things first, books second" reading
    was removed as unsupported.
- Legge's Analects 15.3, Chinese Text Project: https://ctext.org/analects/wei-ling-gong
  - Supported: "Ci, you think, I suppose, that I am one who learns many things and keeps them
    in memory?" ... "No, ... I seek a unity all pervading."
  - Direction: Confucius asks Zigong; Zigong replies yes; Confucius rejects the description.
    (The first draft wrongly had Zigong asking Confucius; corrected.)
  - Limit: the passage concerns unity in Confucius's learning; it is presented without any
    bridge to zhizhi.
