## Interpretation limits

- Dewey's test is about ideas and inquiry generally; the compendium's application to life
  decisions is the principle's own framing and is stated as such ("the test the principle
  works by").
- James's essay argues for the right to believe under genuine uncertainty (live, forced,
  momentous options); the body uses it for the permission-to-act point, not for the
  essay's religious examples.
- The psychology parallel (behavioral activation, Dimidjian et al.) is an inherited link
  line, untouched.
