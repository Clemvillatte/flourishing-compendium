VII, “Some Early Undertakings at Hull House”.** Opened: https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/jane-addams/twenty-years-at-hull-house/text/chapter-7 . Primary autobiographical text. Opening public-kitchen paragraphs and the following coffeehouse discussion support the mixed reception of food, increasing soup/stew sales, clubs and social gatherings, and revising undertakings as residents learned what neighbours would accept. Exact excerpt: “We did not reckon, however, with the wide diversity in nationality and inherited tastes”. It is Addams's narrative voice, not a quotation attributed to a neighbour.

University-hosted primary text supports taking others' standpoints towards oneself and the effect of anticipated responses on one's conduct. Related social account, not identical to Addams's ethics and not an intervention-outcome claim.
