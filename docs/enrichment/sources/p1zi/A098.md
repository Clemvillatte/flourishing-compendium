# A098 — Recognise natural justice (physikon dikaion) — evidence (p1zi)

- Twenty-seventh item of the p1zi band. Original page: no verbatim quotes;
  the seed's glosses ("Some things are unjust regardless of what any law
  says") dropped Aristotle's own qualification; "Learn to distinguish..."
  was an unlabelled prescription; the psychology parallels carried
  claim-bearing clauses (Haidt 2012; Curry, Mullins and Whitehouse
  2019).
- Opened 2026-10-20: NE Book V fetched raw (no prompt) from the MIT
  Classics Ross translation
  (https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.5.v.html); chapter 7
  inspected directly and the quoted fragments confirmed
  character-for-character: the blockquote "Of political justice part is
  natural, part legal, natural, that which everywhere has the same force
  and does not exist by people's thinking this or that; legal, that
  which is originally indifferent, but when it has been laid down is not
  indifferent, e.g. that a prisoner's ransom shall be a mina, or that a
  goat and not two sheep shall be sacrificed, and again all the laws
  that are passed for particular cases, e.g. that sacrifice shall be made
  in honour of Brasidas, and the provisions of decrees."; "as fire burns
  both here and in Persia"; "while with us there is something that is
  just even by nature, yet all of it is changeable; but still some is by
  nature, some not by nature."; "by nature the right hand is stronger,
  yet it is possible that all men should come to be ambidextrous.";
  "exists not by nature but by law (nomos)" (V.5).
- Repairs: quotes added; the seed's unqualified "unjust regardless of
  any law" replaced by the text's own qualified account (natural justice
  is not made true by say-so, yet with us all of it is changeable); a
  labelled compendium exercise added; the For example made imagined and
  action-only; the psychology parallels reduced to bare references. The
  seed's V.10 and Politics I.5 locators not quoted from (not fetched);
  the page cites the opened V.5 and V.7.
