## Quotes used (verified verbatim against the opened text)
- Blockquote: "Deliberation is a dramatic rehearsal (in imagination) of various competing possible lines of action... Deliberation is an experiment in finding out what the various lines of possible action are really like. It is an experiment in making various combinations of selected elements of habits and impulses, to see what the resultant action would be like if it were entered upon. But the trial is in imagination, not in overt fact." (ellipses mark omitted sentences).
- Body: "takes its turn in being tried out"; "deliberation is a dramatic rehearsal (in imagination) of various competing possible lines of action"; "what the various lines of possible action are really like"; "Thought runs ahead and foresees outcomes, and thereby avoids having to await the instruction of actual failure and disaster"; "is not final or fatal. It is retrievable"; "activity is turned from execution into intra-organic channels, resulting in dramatic rehearsal"; "supplies an adequate stimulus and releases definitive action"; "All deliberation is a search for a _way_ to act, not for a final terminus" (italic markup in source); extension note's "overrides all competitors".
