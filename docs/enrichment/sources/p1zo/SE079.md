# SE079 — The self is not the goal (p1zo item 9)

- Source opened: Alagaddupama Sutta (MN 22), trans. Thanissaro Bhikkhu, Access to Insight:
  https://accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.022.than.html (BCBS edition).
- Supported passages (verified verbatim against the opened page):
  - Body fragment: "for the purpose of crossing over, not for the purpose of holding onto"
    (verbatim substring, appears twice in the passage).
  - Page blockquote: "In the same way, monks, I have taught the Dhamma compared to a raft,
    for the purpose of crossing over, not for the purpose of holding onto. Understanding
    the Dhamma as taught compared to a raft, you should let go even of Dhammas, to say
    nothing of non-Dhammas." (verbatim, contiguous closing sentences of the simile).
  - Body paraphrase of the hoisting-the-raft-on-the-head detail matches the opened text:
    "having hoisted it on my head or carrying it on my back, go wherever I like" followed
    by the Buddha's "No, lord" verdict that this would not be doing what should be done.
- Limitations: the raft simile is about the Dhamma/teachings; its application to the self
  is the compendium's own extension (flagged in state). Ryan & Deci 2001, Seligman 2011
  and Aristotle NE parallels are inherited and NOT certified this session (flagged).
