- Supported passages (verified verbatim against the opened pages):
  - SN 22.59: "Is it fitting to regard what is inconstant, stressful, subject to change as:
    'This is mine. This is my self. This is what I am'?" and the summary line "every form
    is to be seen as it actually is with right discernment as: 'This is not mine. This is
    not my self. This is not what I am.'" (page blockquote).
  - Treatise I.iv.6: the mind is "nothing but a bundle or collection of different
    perceptions, which succeed each other with an inconceivable rapidity, and are in a
    perpetual flux and movement"; the identity we ascribe to the mind "is only a
    fictitious one, and of a like kind with that which we ascribe to vegetables and animal
    bodies." (fragments quoted inline on the page).
