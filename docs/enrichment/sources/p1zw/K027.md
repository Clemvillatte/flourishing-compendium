# K027 — In post-meditation, be a child of illusion (p1zw, 2026-10-20)

## Evidence ledger

**Claim set on the page (all quotes verbatim from opened sources):**
- Kongtrül commentary blockquote: "After meditation, do not allow the experience of resting evenly to dissipate, no matter what form of activity you engage in. Continually foster the feeling of knowing that all appearances, yourself, others, animate or inanimate, appear though they seem to be nothing - be like a child of illusion." VERIFIED verbatim on the freshly opened Tibetan Buddhist Encyclopedia page (Lojong - Tonglen), which carries the McLeod translation of The Great Path of Awakening commentary.
- Pema Chödrön commentary blockquote: "When you finish sitting meditation, if things become heavy and solid, be fully present and realize that everything is actually pliable, open, and workable. This is instruction for meditation in action, realizing that you don't have to feel claustrophobic because there is always lots of room, lots of space." VERIFIED verbatim in the freshly opened Shambhala Compassion Cards PDF (commentary (c) 2003 Pema Chödrön; slogan translation (c) 1993 Diana J. Mukpo and the Nalanda Translation Committee).
- Root slogan wording "In postmeditation, be a child of illusion" verified in the same Shambhala PDF (Nalanda Translation Committee). Multiple standard renderings noted (Adhisthana interleaved translations PDF: McLeod "In daily life, be a child of illusion"; Trungpa "In post-meditation, be a child of illusion").
