## Repairs and scope

Removed “One reply is nothing; the thousandth reply is a person”, which both trivialised the single action and turned repetition into an identity verdict. The new reading attends to intentional action, another person's experience and repair. Its colleague scene is explicitly invented.

Removed the blanket empirical claim that every repeated mental pattern strengthens a track. Automaticity research is a bounded comparison about chosen behaviours; it does not establish that every passing thought is a moral vote or define the canonical meaning of kamma.

All original wikilinks, metadata, principle statement and reflection comment preserved. Inherited reflections retain the track/vote metaphors; they are outside this body repair.
