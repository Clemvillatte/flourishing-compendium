  Verbatim quotes used: abstract opening ("The psychological principles that

  Verbatim quotes used (their description of McNeil et al. 1982): "preferences
  of physicians and patients between hypothetical therapies for lung cancer

  an explicit truncation colon and attributed as (McNeil, Pauker, Sox, & Tversky,
