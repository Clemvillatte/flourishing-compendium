# AT047 — Focused attention meditation (p1z)

- Source opened: Lutz, Slagter, Dunne & Davidson, "Attention regulation and
  monitoring in meditation", Trends in Cognitive Sciences 12(4), 163-169 (2008),
  full text via https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2693206/ (curl-extracted
  and verified against the displayed article with fetch_url during AT005).
- Quote 1 (Box 1, "Focused Attention meditation"): "Thus, while cultivating the
  acuity and stability of sustained attention on a chosen object, this practice
  also develops three skills regulative of attention: the first is the monitoring
  faculty that remains vigilant to distractions without destabilizing the intended
  focus. The next skill is the ability to disengage from a distracting object
  without further involvement. The last consists in the ability to redirect focus
  promptly to the chosen object." Character-for-character match (verified in the
  fetch_url read during AT005, same session, same source).
- Quote 2 (Box 1): "Progress in this form of meditation is measured in part by the

  focus on localized sensations around the nostril caused by breathing, one may
  notice that the focus has shifted to the pain in one's knee. One then 'releases'
  this distraction, and returns to the intended object.").
