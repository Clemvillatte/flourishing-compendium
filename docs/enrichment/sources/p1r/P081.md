  - Raw text fetched; chapter X ("Experience, Value and Criticism") read at the immediate-value passages (running-header pages 322 and 326). Verbatim:
    - "Of immediate values as such, values which occur and which are possessed and enjoyed, there is no theory at all; they just occur, are enjoyed, possessed; and that is all." (p. 326)

Thought goes beyond immediate existence to its relationships, the conditions which mediate it and the things to which it is in turn mediatory. And such a procedure is criticism." (p. 322)
