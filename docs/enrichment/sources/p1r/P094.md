## Primary sources opened this session

  - "The first implies that we move round the object; the second that we enter into it."

  - "For we do not obtain an intuition from reality - that is, an intellectual sympathy with the most intimate part of it - unless we have won its confidence by a long fellowship with its superficial manifestations."
