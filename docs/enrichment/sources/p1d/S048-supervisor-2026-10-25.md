## Opened sources

- [Epictetus, Discourses I.28, George Long translation](https://www.stoicsource.com/epictetus/discourses/1.28/), full chapter read. The sentence beginning "Because this is the nature" is exact and includes suspension in uncertain matters. This is Epictetus' philosophical account, not an experimentally demonstrated universal default policy of minds.

The chapter distinguishes quick bathing and much wine from bathing/drinking badly, asking for understanding of the actor's principle. It concerns evaluative inference about conduct. The revision no longer presents it as a command to defer every action.
- [Dugas et al. (1998), Generalized anxiety disorder: a preliminary test of a conceptual model, PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9613027/), opened and read. The abstract specifies 24 GAD patients and 20 nonclinical controls, a discriminant analysis and intolerance of uncertainty's role. It does not test Stoic training or show that simply deferring a reply treats anxiety.
