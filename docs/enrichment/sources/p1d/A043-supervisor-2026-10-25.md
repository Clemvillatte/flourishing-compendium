## Sources opened

- [Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics VI, W. D. Ross translation, Internet Classics Archive](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.6.vi.html), complete Book VI read. Chapter 5 contains the short retained definition exactly. Chapter 7 supplies the food-rule/particular-food illustration; this is presented as Aristotle's example, not current nutritional advice. Chapter 8 associates familiarity with particulars with experience. Chapters 12–13 distinguish cleverness serving any given end from practical wisdom in conjunction with moral virtue.
- [Baltes and Staudinger (2000), Wisdom, PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11392856/?dopt=Abstract), opened and read. Five evaluation criteria are listed: rich factual knowledge, procedural knowledge, lifespan contextualism, relativism of values/priorities, recognition/management of uncertainty. The body groups the first two under knowledge without claiming that the paradigm has only four criteria.
