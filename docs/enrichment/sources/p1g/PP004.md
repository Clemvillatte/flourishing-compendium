# PP004 — Treat flourishing as multi-dimensional (item 39, worker-p1g-5e8c3a21)

- Opened sources:
  - Seligman 2018, "PERMA and the building blocks of well-being", The Journal of Positive
    Psychology, author PDF hosted at Penn (https://ppc.sas.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/permawellbeing.pdf).
    Verbatim blockquote: "In 2011, I suggested that PERMA (Positive Emotion, Engagement,
    Relationships, Meaning and Accomplishment) are five measurable elements that make up
    well-being (Seligman, 2011, pp 16-25)." Also verified: each element contributes to well-being,
    is pursued for its own sake, and "can be defined and measured independently of the other
    elements" (paraphrased in body).
  - Huppert & So 2013, Social Indicators Research 110(3) 837-861, PMC full text
    (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3545194/). Verbatim blockquote: "Flourishing refers
    to the experience of life going well. It is a combination of feeling good and functioning
    effectively." Also verified abstract: ten features from the opposite of DSM/ICD depression
    and anxiety criteria (competence, emotional stability, engagement, meaning, optimism,
    positive emotion, positive relationships, resilience, self-esteem, vitality); 43,000
    Europeans; 41% Denmark to under 10% Slovakia/Russia/Portugal.
  - Aristotle NE I.7, Ross, MIT Classics (https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.1.i.html).
    Verified verbatim: "human good turns out to be activity of soul in accordance with virtue,
    and if there are more than one virtue, in accordance with the best and most complete";
    the state-vs-activity passage ("as in a man who is asleep... but the activity cannot").
