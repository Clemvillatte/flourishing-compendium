## Opened sources (2026-10-19, genuineflourishing34)

- Brewer, Cook and Bird (2016), "Alexithymia: a general deficit of interoception", Royal
  Society Open Science 3(10):150664; open-access PDF via City Research Online (URL in page
  Sources). Fetched and quoted verbatim:
  - "Interoception refers to the perception of a wide range of physical states beyond
    emotions, including heart rate, respiratory effort, temperature, fatigue, hunger,
    thirst, satiety, muscle ache, pain and itch"
  - "Those with alexithymia therefore not only struggle to recognize their own emotions
    (affective interoception) but also confuse these states with non-affective
    interoceptive states, such as hunger, tiredness and arousal."
  - "Alexithymia may, therefore, be characterized better by a general failure of
    interoception, rather than one of affective interoception alone."
  - Limitation: the study is one paper (n = 191 typical development participants); the
    meta-analytic picture is broader (Van Bael et al. 2024, PLOS ONE, surfaced in search
    but not opened; not cited on the page).
