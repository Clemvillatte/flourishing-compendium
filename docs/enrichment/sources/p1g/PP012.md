# PP012 — Savor positive experience (item 40, worker-p1g-5e8c3a21)

- Opened sources:
  - Jose, Lim & Bryant 2012, J Positive Psychology 7(3) 176-187, author-copy PDF
    (http://moodwatchers.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/DOES-SAVOURING-INCREASE-HAPPINESS.pdf).
    Verbatim blockquote: "These results provide support for the hypothesis that savoring is an
    important mechanism through which people derive happiness from positive events." Also
    verified: 101 participants, 30 days ESM; momentary savoring mediated and moderated the impact
    of daily positive events on momentary happy mood.
  - Smith & Hanni (Loyola eCommons PDF, https://ecommons.luc.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1093&context=psychology_facpubs).
    Verbatim: savoring is "the capacity 'to attend to, appreciate, and enhance the positive
    experience in one's life' (Bryant & Veroff, 2007, p. xi)"; "Training on momentary positive
    emotion regulation (i.e., memory building, expressing positive emotions) resulted in
    decreased self-reported depression symptoms and negative affect (but not positive affect)
    when compared to a control group after two weeks (Hurley & Kwon, 2012)."
