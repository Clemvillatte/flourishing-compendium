# S073 — Love the people you live with (item 52, worker-p1g-5e8c3a21)

- Opened source: Marcus Aurelius, Meditations II.1, George Long translation, Wikisource
  (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Thoughts_of_the_Emperor_Marcus_Aurelius_Antoninus/Book_II).
  Verified verbatim: the morning-rehearsal opening ("Begin the morning by saying to thyself, I
  shall meet with the busybody, the ungrateful, arrogant, deceitful, envious, unsocial...");
  the kinship passage ("participates in [the same] intelligence and [the same] portion of the
  divinity, I can neither be injured by any of them... nor can I be angry with my kinsman, nor
  hate him"); the blockquote (co-operation like feet/hands/eyelids/teeth; "to be vexed and to
  turn away").
- The old body's "to be angry at them is to be angry at your own body" was a paraphrase not in
  the opened chapter (the chapter's body metaphor is the co-operation one, which I use as the
  source gives it); replaced. The old locator's other chapters (II.13, VII.13, VII.22, IX.1,
  IX.4, IX.11; Disc. III.24) were not opened; citations dropped.
- Example kept from the original page (concrete, no outcome claim).
