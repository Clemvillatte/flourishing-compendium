# Y015: source ledger

## Opened sources (2026-10-20, genuineflourishing34)

- The Yoga Sutras of Patanjali, trans. Charles Johnston (1912), Project Gutenberg ebook 2526
  (https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2526/pg2526.txt). Downloaded and read directly.
  - Supported (1.5, verbatim): "The psychic activities are five; they are either subject
    or not subject to the five hindrances (Book II, 3)."
  - Supported (1.6, verbatim): "These activities are: Sound intellection, unsound
    intellection, predication, sleep, memory."
  - Supported (1.9, verbatim): "Predication is carried on through words or thoughts not
    resting on an object perceived."
  - Also read in the opened text: the Johnston commentary on 1.9 (predication may be an
    interplay of thoughts without the presence of the object) and on 1.10/1.11.
- Yoga Sutras of Patanjali: Interpretive Translation (Swami Jnaneshvara Bharati),
  archive.org PDF (fetched directly):
  - Supported (1.6, verbatim): "The five varieties of thought patterns to witness are:
    1) knowing correctly (pramana), 2) incorrect knowing (viparyaya), 3) fantasy or
    imagination (vikalpa), 4) the object of void-ness that is deep sleep (nidra), and
    5) recollection or memory (smriti)."
  - Supported (1.9, verbatim): "Fantasy or imagination (vikalpa) is a thought pattern
    that has verbal expression and knowledge, but for which there is no such object or
    reality in existence."
- Translation note (kept here, not asserted on the page): Johnston renders vikalpa as
  "predication"; the page's "imagination" follows the other opened rendering. The
  Sanskrit-term mapping is verified from the interpretive translation's printed text,
  not from Johnston (his Gutenberg text carries no Sanskrit).
