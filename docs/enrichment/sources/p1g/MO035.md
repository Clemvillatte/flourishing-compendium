# MO035 — Understand that habits are the foundation of sustainable motivation (item 35, worker-p1g-5e8c3a21)

- Opened sources (all fetched this session, quotes verbatim from the fetched page text):
  - Gardner, Lally & Wardle 2015, BJGP (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3505409/).
    Verbatim blockquote used: "Once initiation of the action is 'transferred' to external cues,
    dependence on conscious attention or motivational processes is reduced." Also verified:
    "habits are likely to persist even after conscious motivation or interest dissipates" (used as
    paraphrase, unquoted); "automaticity plateaued on average around 66 days after the first daily
    performance, although there was considerable variation across participants and behaviours"
    (paraphrased); "The effects are typically short-lived because motivation and attention wane".
  - Lally et al. 2010, EJSP (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ejsp.674). Verified:
    96 volunteers, daily behaviour in the same context, 12 weeks; asymptotic curve; 18-254 days;
    "Missing one opportunity to perform the behaviour did not materially affect the habit
    formation process." The 66-day figure is not in the fetched abstract text; the bullet and
    body say "plateaus on average around 66 days" following Gardner et al. 2015's opened wording
    ("plateaued on average around 66 days"), not "median".
  - Wood & Neal 2007 via ERIC record (https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ776723): "Once a habit is formed,
    perception of contexts triggers the associated response without a mediating goal." Paraphrased
    in body, not quoted.
  - Aristotle NE II.1, Ross, MIT Classics (https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.2.ii.html):
    "moral virtue comes about as a result of habit" — verified verbatim; paraphrased in body,
    quoted in the Sources entry.
