## Verbatim quote used
- "These exercises encompassed all of those practices still associated with
  philosophical teaching and study: reading, listening, dialogue, inquiry, and
  research. However, they also included practices deliberately aimed at addressing the
  student's larger way of life, and demanding daily or continuous repetition: practices
  of attention (prosoche), meditations (meletai), memorizations of dogmata,
  self-mastery (enkrateia), the therapy of the passions, the remembrance of good
  things, the accomplishment of duties, and the cultivation of indifference towards
  indifferent things (PWL 84)."
