# Creative prompts: authoring ledger

Offline record behind `site/assets/prompts-bank.json`. Nothing in this file ships to readers. Method: docs/enrichment/CREATIVE-PROMPT-GENERATION.md (anchor, preclusive and promotive constraints, constraint test, three-directions sketch, nine-point QC checklist). Humanised per docs/enrichment/HUMANIZER-SKILL.md. Every principle named was opened and read in the repo before the prompt was written; every dossier named is published in `docs/enrichment/dossiers/registry.json`. These are desk tests of structure, not measured outcomes for writers.

Bank-wide caps enforced by `scripts/tests/test_creative_prompts.py`: word bans in at most 30 percent of prompts, at most two prompts per principle, no repeated anchor, no two prompts opening with the same three words, 25 to 90 words per prompt.

Status: PILOT of 12 (3 per genre). The owner reads these before the bank is scaled.

Checklist notes. (1) Where a prompt bans a move it also gives a constructive route (CP001 two layers, CP004 return through a physical detail, CP006 room-body-room). (2) No answer examples are embedded. (3) One anchor each. (4) Constraints each open at least two directions. (5) Incubation hooks are not used in the pilot. (6) Frames carry nothing evaluative. (7) Each states a start and a done-when condition. (8) The sketches above diverge in situation and stance, not only in scenery. (9) Each fits its target and genre.

Safety. Memoir prompts that could touch painful memory offer a character route and the sensitive flag adds a line in the interface. Abuse, self-harm and acute trauma are excluded from v1.
