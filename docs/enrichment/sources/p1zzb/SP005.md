# SP005 — Treat yourself as part of nature (band p1zzb)

## Opened sources
1. Spinoza, B. (c. 1677). Ethics, in the clear-language translation by Jonathan
   Bennett (earlymoderntexts.com). Full PDF opened (139 pages):
   https://www.earlymoderntexts.com/assets/pdfs/spinoza1665.pdf

## Verbatim quotes (with locators)
- "Most of those who have written about the affects and men's way of living write as
  though their topic was not natural things that follow the common laws of Nature but
  rather things that are outside Nature. Indeed they seem to think of man in Nature
  as a kingdom within a kingdom." (Ethics III, Preface; PDF p. 51)
- "Nothing that happens in Nature can be attributed to any defect in it; for Nature
  is always the same—the laws and rules of Nature that govern all events are the
  same at every place and every time. So the affects of hate, anger, envy etc.,
  considered in themselves, follow from the same necessity and force of Nature as
  other particular things." (Ethics III, Preface; PDF p. 51)
- "approaching human actions and appetites in the way I would approach questions
  about lines, planes, and bodies" (Ethics III, Preface; PDF pp. 51-52)
- "Whatever exists is in God, and nothing can exist or be conceived without God."
  (Ethics I, P15; PDF p. 8)
