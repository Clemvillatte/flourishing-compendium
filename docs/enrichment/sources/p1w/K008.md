# K008 source ledger

**Item**: K008 — Expect the collapse of meaning; do not waste it
**Primary source**: Nishitani, *Religion and Nothingness*, trans. Jan Van Bragt, University of California Press, 1982.
**Access**: archive.org full-text PDF (https://dn720002.ca.archive.org/0/items/important-publications-in-philosophy/Important%20Publications%20in%20Philosophy/Contemporary%20Philosophical%20Texts/Chinese%20and%20Japanese%20Thought/Religion%20and%20Nothingness.pdf); printed p. 3 = PDF page 53.

## Verbatim passages (verified character-for-character against the opened PDF)

1. "The point at which the ordinarily necessary things of life, including learning and the arts, all lose their necessity and utility is found at those times when death, nihility, or sin-or any of those situations that entail a fundamental negation of our life, existence, and ideals, that undermine the roothold of our existence and bring the meaning of life into question- become pressing personal problems for us." (printed p. 3)
2. "[A] void appears here that nothing in the world can fill; a gaping abyss opens up at the very ground on which one stands." (printed p. 3; original reads "A void", bracket marks ellipsis of preceding clause)
3. "that abyss is always just underfoot" (printed pp. 3-4)
4. "Nihility refers to that which renders meaningless the meaning of life." (printed p. 4)

## Notes
