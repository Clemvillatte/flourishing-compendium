## Verbatim passages (verified)

1. Blockquote, §23 (H.139): "De-severing amounts to making the farness vanish—that is, making the remoteness of something disappear, bringing it close. Dasein is essentially de-severant: it lets any entity be encountered close by as the entity which it is." — verified in the opened archive.org text AND in the Galloway handout PDF (https://cultureandcommunication.org/galloway/pdf/Heidegger,%20Being%20and%20Time--HANDOUT.pdf), which quotes it with the same wording and page (139).
2. §23 (H.140): "In Dasein there lies an essential tendency towards closeness" — verified in both the archive.org text and the Galloway handout (140).
3. §22: "a different closeness, which is not to be ascertained by measuring distances" — from the opened archive.org text (OCR line: "Every entity that is 'to hand' has a diffe.ent closeness, which is not to be ascertained by measuring distances."; the two OCR glitches ["cquipment"→equipment, "diffe.ent"→different] sit outside the quoted span; the quoted fragment is OCR-clean).
4. §23 (H.140): "conquest of remoteness" (short fragment of "push us on towards the conquest of remoteness"); "speed things up" (fragment of "All the ways in which we speed things up, as we are more or less compelled to do today") — from the opened archive.org text.
