## Application note

The compendium's application is a labelled writing exercise (describe a familiar custom for someone who does not share it; write the assumed purpose; write one question an unfamiliar reader might ask) — borrowed from Ricoeur's use of texts to explore how distance can contribute to understanding. It claims no outside-of-culture viewpoint and no guaranteed benefit.
