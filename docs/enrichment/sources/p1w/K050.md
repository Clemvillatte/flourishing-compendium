# K050 source ledger

**Item**: K050 — Reflect on this life's rarity as urgency, not as dread
**Source (opened this session)**: Chiggaḷa Sutta (SN 56.48), trans. Ṭhānissaro Bhikkhu — dhammatalks.org (https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/SN/SN56_48.html), Thanissaro's own site; page fetched this session.

## Verbatim passages (verified against the fetched page text)

1. "It would be a sheer coincidence, lord, that the blind sea turtle, coming to the surface once every one hundred years, would stick his neck into the yoke with a single hole." — the monks' reply.
2. "It's likewise a sheer coincidence that one obtains the human state. It's likewise a sheer coincidence that a Tathāgata, worthy & rightly self-awakened, arises in the world. It's likewise a sheer coincidence that a Dhamma & Vinaya expounded by a Tathāgata appears in the world. Now, this human state has been obtained. A Tathāgata, worthy & rightly self-awakened, has arisen in the world. A Dhamma & Vinaya expounded by a Tathāgata appears in the world." — the Buddha's reply.
3. "Therefore your duty is the contemplation, 'This is stress … This is the origination of stress … This is the cessation of stress.' Your duty is the contemplation, 'This is the path of practice leading to the cessation of stress.'" — the closing duty passage.
