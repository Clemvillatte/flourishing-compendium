## Opened sources and verbatim passages

1. [Shields, *Critical Buddhism*, 2020, PDF at archive.org](https://ia902905.us.archive.org/30/items/criticalbuddhismengagingwithmodernjapanesebuddhistthoughtjamesmarkshields_202003_771_Y/Critical%20Buddhism%20Engaging%20with%20Modern%20Japanese%20Buddhist%20Thought%20James%20Mark%20Shields.pdf) — quotes Nishida, *An Inquiry into the Good* (New Haven, 1987), pp. 3-4: "To experience means to know facts just as they are, to know in accordance with facts by completely relinquishing one's own fabrications. What we usually refer to as experience is adulterated with some sort of thought, so by pure I am referring to experience just as it is without the least addition of deliberative discrimination … In this regard, pure experience is identical with direct experience. When one experiences one's own state of consciousness, there is not yet a subject or an object, and knowing and its object are completely unified. This is the most refined type of experience." — verified character-for-character via pdftotext at the cited PDF.

## Blockquote used in the body

The body's long quotation is Nishida's own words as reproduced by Shields (Critical Buddhism), with the primary locator given on the Shields page as *An Inquiry into the Good* (New Haven, 1987), pp. 3-4. The ellipsis in the body's quotation marks a cut between "deliberative discrimination" and "When one experiences" (the Shields quote continues "In this regard, pure experience is identical with direct experience." between them; this intervening sentence is present in the Shields quotation and was elided, not added, in the body). Cited as Shields quoting Nishida, not as a direct Nishida sentence.
