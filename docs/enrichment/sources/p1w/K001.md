**Item**: K001 — Return to experience before the labels
**Primary source**: Nishida, *An Inquiry into the Good*, trans. Masao Abe and Christopher Ives, Yale University Press, 1990 (Part I, ch. 1).
**Access**: The 1990 Yale translation was NOT independently opened (no archive.org full-text found). The verbatim wording was obtained through opened secondary sources that reproduce the primary text directly with page locators.

[Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Nishida Kitarō (Winter 2023 archive)](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/win2023/entries/nishida-kitaro/) — the quoted sentence containing "The moment of seeing a color or hearing a sound" is the SEP's own sentence quoting Nishida's opening phrase; locator "Nishida 1990a, 3" given on the page.
