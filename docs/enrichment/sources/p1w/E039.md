## Verbatim passages

1. EH (Mairet): "Man is nothing else but that which he makes of himself. That is the first principle of existentialism." — verified in the opened PDF text. NOTE: the 2007 Macomber translation (which the seed cited) reads "what he makes of himself"; the page quotes the Mairet text as opened and cites it as such.
2. EH (Mairet): "man first of all exists, encounters himself, surges up in the world — and defines himself afterwards" — verified in the opened PDF text.
3. BN (Barnes, Part IV ch. 1, p. 480): "Since freedom is a being-without-support and without-a-springboard, the project in order to be must be constantly renewed. I choose myself perpetually and can never be merely by virtue of having-been-chosen; otherwise I should fall into the pure and simple existence of the in-itself. The necessity of perpetually choosing myself is one with the pursued-pursuit which I am." — verified in the opened PDF text AND on the rendered page image (PDF page 536; printed p. 480 confirmed; hyphenated phrases "without-a-springboard", "having-been-chosen", "pursued-pursuit" confirmed character-for-character on the image).

## Citation correction

The seed's citation line said "Being and Nothingness, Part IV, ch. 2". The p. 480 passage sits in Part IV, ch. 1 ("Being and Doing: Freedom"), section I (Freedom: The First Condition of Action, pp. 433-481), per the book's own table of contents in the opened scan (ch. 1 §II begins p. 481). Corrected to ch. 1.
