### B018: Kālāma (AN 3.65, Thanissaro, © 1994, ATI revision 30 Nov 2013)
- The acceptance-formula quote matches the opened text exactly ("When you know for yourselves that, 'These qualities are skillful; ... then you should enter & remain in them.").
- The translator is correct.
- The opening dialogue about teachers who glorify their own doctrines and disparage others is accurate.
- The greed/aversion/delusion test through harmful acts (killing, taking what is not given, etc.) is accurate.
- The translator's note is attributed correctly as Thanissaro's own text, separate from the discourse. It says one's preferences "are not to be followed simply because..." and that results "must further be checked against the experience of people who are wise".

- The AOT claim is supported by the Stanovich & Toplak 2023 abstract (J. Intelligence). The abstract covers "sensitivity to evidence contradictory to current beliefs" and states that AOT scales "do not predict the avoidance of myside thinking". The limitation is stated. PASS.
