## 1. Method scope: substring probes, not full-quote comparisons

The receipts report substring probes against selected spans inside
each quoted quotation, on carriers opened in this environment. A hit
establishes that the probed words appear on the carrier; it does not
establish that the complete quoted passage was compared word-for-word.
The receipts' "confirmed verbatim" phrasing is therefore narrowed to:
"probed spans confirmed present on opened carriers". Sampling is
legitimate QC practice; a blanket all-clear for every word of every
quotation was NOT performed and is not claimed.
