## Request discipline

Agents may request books beyond the catalogue when a real evidence gap requires them.
Record title, author/translator, edition, exact item or passage served, and unsuccessful lawful
access attempts. Do not call a request an order placed. Do not substitute another thinker's
agreement for evidence of a claim attributed to the requested author.

Paid purchases require approval of the exact vendor, edition, format, total and delivery
terms before checkout. This page records no such approval or purchase.
