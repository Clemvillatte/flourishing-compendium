## Supported passages (verbatim, with locators)

- Blockquote (VI.5, 1140a, contiguous first sentence): "Now it is thought to be a mark of a man of practical wisdom to be able to deliberate well about what is good and expedient for himself, not in some particular respect, e.g. about what sorts of thing conduce to health or to strength, but about what sorts of thing conduce to the good life in general."
- Definition (VI.5, 1140b, paraphrased): "Practical wisdom, then, must be a reasoned and true state of capacity to act with regard to human goods."
- Particulars (VI.7, 1141b, paraphrased): "Nor is practical wisdom concerned with universals only — it must also recognize the particulars; for it is practical, and practice is concerned with particulars... the man who knows that chicken is wholesome is more likely to produce health."
- Young men (VI.8, 1142a, paraphrased): "while young men become geometricians and mathematicians and wise in matters like these, it is thought that a young man of practical wisdom cannot be found. The cause is that such wisdom is concerned not only with universals but with particulars, which become familiar from experience, but a young man has no experience..."
