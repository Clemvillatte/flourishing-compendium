# B166 — Chop wood, carry water (p1d, genuineflourishing30)

## Sources opened

- Layman Pang (Pang Yun), Encounter Dialogues and Verses of Layman Pangyun, compiled by Satyavayu, Touching Earth Sangha, via the Terebess Zen archive: https://terebess.hu/zen/pang.html (page carries multiple English translations of the Shitou exchange and the daily-activity verse)

## Supported passages (verbatim, with locators)

- Shitou exchange (Satyavayu compilation, used as the standalone blockquote, en dash preserved): Shitou: "Since seeing me what have your daily activities been?" Pang: "When you ask about my daily activities I can't open my mouth." Shitou: "Because I know you're like that, I'm asking you." Pang: "How wondrous, how miraculous – chopping wood and carrying water."
- Daily-activity verse (paraphrased in body; Satyavayu version): "My daily activity is not unusual; I just remain in spontaneous harmony. Not grasping or rejecting... Supernatural powers and miraculous activity: fetching water and carrying firewood"
- Page also carries van Schaik ("I use my mysterious spiritual powers / To carry water and haul firewood") and Whitfield (Records of the Transmission of the Lamp) translations of the verse; the Chodang chip introduction carries the Mazu West-River exchange.
