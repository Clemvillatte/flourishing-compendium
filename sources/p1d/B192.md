## Supported passages (with locators)

- MN 61, before/while/after reflection on bodily, verbal and mental actions (paraphrased in body): "Whenever you want to do a bodily action, you should reflect on it: 'This bodily action I want to do — would it lead to self-affliction, to the affliction of others, or to both?...'" (same structure for while/after, and for verbal/mental actions).
- MN 61, training formula (used as the standalone blockquote, verbatim): "Thus, Rahula, you should train yourself: 'I will purify my bodily actions through repeated reflection. I will purify my verbal actions through repeated reflection. I will purify my mental actions through repeated reflection.'"
- MN 61, after-action branches (paraphrased per advisor): faulty bodily/verbal → confess, reveal, lay it open to the Teacher or a knowledgeable companion, exercise restraint in the future; faulty mental → "you should feel distressed, ashamed, & disgusted with it... you should exercise restraint in the future"; clean → "stay mentally refreshed & joyful, training day & night in skillful mental qualities."
- Dhp 239 (paraphrased per advisor): "One by one, little by little, moment by moment, a wise man should remove his own impurities, as a smith removes his dross from silver."
