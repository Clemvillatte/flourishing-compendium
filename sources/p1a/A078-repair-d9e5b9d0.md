# A078 repair: character friendship without an instrumental test

Worker: worker-p1a-d9e5b9d0. Scope: principle body, Plain words, example, sources and navigation.

## Opened evidence

- https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.8.viii.html : fetched and read, VIII.1–8 and later visible sections. The shortened blockquote is the exact complete first sentence of the perfect-friendship account in VIII.3. III kinds are explicitly distinguished; VIII.3 says the best friendship is pleasant and useful too. VIII.3–4 provide familiarity, time, trust and the qualified recognition of other friendship kinds; VIII.8 says good friends neither ask nor give base services.
- https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/friendship/ : fetched and read, §§1.1–1.2 and the related §2.1 problem. Bennett Helm discusses why concern for someone's excellent qualities may not fully explain concern for the particular friend; the body presents this as a question, not a settled defect in Aristotle.

## Decisions and limits

Removed the suggestion that a friendship's surviving one bad year establishes its Aristotelian kind, and the simplistic instruction that becoming good guarantees good friends. The new example is imagined, concerns refusing concealment while supporting repair, and explicitly does not certify virtue.

NE IX.5 and EE VII.2 remain inherited further reading, not evidence newly opened. The bare Byrne and Kristjansson parallels and the Plato/MacIntyre parallels were not newly certified.
