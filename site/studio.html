Cognitive Neuroscience & ADHD Attention Architecture

Baddeley's Phonological Loop: Essay writing requires formulating complex syntax through internal speech. Lyrics or vocal-like formants cause immediate linguistic interference (Irrelevant Sound Effect). Lyricless felt piano, nylon guitar, and calm handpan protect verbal working memory.

Moderate Brain Arousal (MAM) & Stochastic Resonance: ADHD is characterized by low tonic prefrontal dopamine. The brain craves stimulation and wanders when environments are too quiet. A continuous brown ($1/f^2$) or pink ($1/f$) noise bed introduces beneficial stochastic resonance—elevating faint thoughts above the neural firing threshold without distraction.

14Hz Beta Sensorimotor Entrainment: 14Hz binaural beats entrain the sensorimotor rhythm (SMR) and dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, reducing motor restlessness and sustaining long-form focus.

Zero-Mismatch Transition Continuity: Abrupt song transitions trigger mismatch negativity (MMN) and involuntary P300 attentional resets. Our 5.0s equal-power crossfade ensures acoustic energy remains constant, keeping the writer locked in effortless Flow.
